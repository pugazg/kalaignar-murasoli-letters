## Objective

Create a clear, faithful English translation that carries Kalaignar's argument, political judgement, irony, direct address, evidence and emotional emphasis into English without turning the letters into literary adaptations or academic prose.

The audited Tamil page files remain authoritative. English is an interpretive layer and must never be used to silently correct the source.

## Mandatory translator’s note and bilingual order

Every translated letter must place the following note immediately below the English title:

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This translation is intended to carry Kalaignar's voice into clear, contemporary English rather than recast the letter as literary or academic prose. It preserves the source's argument, political directness, rhetorical questions, repetition, irony, factual detail, and paragraph order. Names, dates, figures, quotations, and intentional English expressions are retained. Where Tamil idiom cannot be reproduced literally without sounding unnatural, the English follows its sense and rhetorical force without adding claims absent from the source. The original Tamil is reproduced in full below the translation and remains the authoritative text. `Udanpirappē` is retained in Tamil transliteration rather than flattened into “brother,” “sister,” or “comrade.” Literally evoking “one born alongside me,” Kalaignar uses it as a distinctive address of shared identity, equality, affection, and solidarity within the movement.

Required order in every letter file:

1. English title
2. standard translator’s note
3. source links, date and page range
4. complete English translation
5. necessary letter-specific notes
6. **Original Tamil — மூலத் தமிழ்**, reproduced in full from the canonical page files

## Translation principles

- Preserve thought and argument order before literary elegance.
- Translate every substantive sentence, list item, quotation and rhetorical question.
- Use clear contemporary English without rewriting the letters as commentary.
- Preserve accusation, irony, repetition, sarcasm and emotional emphasis.
- Keep allegations and reported claims explicitly attributed.
- Retain `lakh` and `crore`.
- Use established English names/institutions where certain; otherwise transliterate without guessing.
- Add no historical explanation inside the body unless the source contains it.
- Use minimal notes only for source anomalies, ambiguity, institutional terminology or culturally difficult idiom.
- Reproduce the complete audited Tamil under the English translation.
