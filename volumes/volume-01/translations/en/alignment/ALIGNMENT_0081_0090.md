## Scope

This batch covers canonical English letters **0081–0090**, corresponding to source PDF / printed pages **298–325**.

The English records had already completed canonical migration/source checking. This gate compared each English translation directly with the complete canonical Tamil witness embedded in the record. The canonical Tamil layer had already passed the full scan-based visual/textual-fidelity gate; this batch does not claim a new independent scan transcription pass.

## Alignment method

For every record, the review checked argument and narrative sequence; substantive coverage and unsupported additions; paragraph, verse and quotation correspondence; names, dates, figures and political references; lists, metaphors, wordplay and rhetorical questions; repetition, irony and political intensity; movement vocabulary and the established `Udanpirappē` convention; and closing/sign-off integrity.

Only demonstrable English/Tamil correspondence errors would be corrected at this gate. Stylistic polishing remains deferred to the later editorial-consistency gate.

## Batch result

### 0081 — `தெள்ளிய ஆலின் சிறு பழத்தொரு விதை!`

PASS. The Rajamannar Committee / Sezhiyan–Maran committee sequence, Anna's *Home Rule* argument, the **8,226-word** report, executive-committee debate from the morning through **2 a.m.**, and the banyan-seed image all correspond to the Tamil witness. The formulation **“Autonomy in the State, federalism at the Centre”**, Jayaprakash Narayan, Uma Shankar Dikshit and the closing `லட்சியச் சுவடி` argument remain source-ordered and complete.

### 0082 — `பொங்கல் உறுதி!`

PASS. The Pongal stove/pot analogy remains complete: hostile flame imagines that it scorches the pot, while the pot bears and uses the heat to produce milk Pongal. Anna's call for a heart capable of bearing anything, the shield / bow-and-arrow warning, patient continuation of work and final pledge not to be cowardly if the bow and arrow must be used all correspond without softening the rhetoric.

### 0083 — `காண வருகிறேன் உன்னை!`

PASS. The five campaign-headline items, Congress-rule counterexamples, repeated `ஊழல்` rhetoric, black-money questions, Russia/Hitler comparison and the cadre's martial progression remain in source order. The lineated `வீரனே! / தீரனே! / வெற்றிச் சரிதம் / படைக்கும் என் / அன்பு உடன்பிறப்பே!`, the `படைக்கருவி` image, victory-flag exhortation and intimate Chennai departure / face-to-face close are all represented.

### 0084 — `‘தொய்வு இல்லை! தொடர்க பயணம்!’`

PASS. The budget argument preserves the tax-relief thresholds and rates, public-ownership measures, Thiru Arooran land action, dearness allowance, electricity and transport controls, and the Delhi railway/kerosene/postal counterexamples. The welfare list, including `திருவள்ளுவர் ஆலயம்` as **a Thiruvalluvar shrine**, remains complete. The flour/sweet, rain-cloud, sun and storm-boat images lead correctly to the no-weariness journey close.

### 0085 — `“எல்லா மலர்களும் ரோஜா மலர்களே!”`

PASS. The branch/area/union election sequence, Dindigul–Coimbatore–Puducherry references, Legislative Council / Council of States passage and the full principles block correspond. The English preserves the central distinction that these contests are not clashes of policy, ideals or doctrine, the warning that two camps must not persist after an election, and the closing declaration that every flower in the movement's garden is a rose.

### 0086 — `மே தின வாழ்த்து!`

PASS. Chicago, **1 May 1886**, the eight-hour-day struggle, Periyar and Anna's May Day lineage, the holiday declaration and the catalogue of labour measures all align. Figures including **more than two lakh**, **more than thirty-five thousand** and **six lakh rupees** are retained. The mocking `சமதர்ம சங்கீதம்` / `சங்கீத பூஷணம்` passage and the pledge **“We shall extend a hand for friendship; we shall raise our voice for rights”** preserve the source's argument and tone.

### 0087 — `‘நமது நிலை’!`

PASS. The railway strike, Labour Progressive Union position, George Fernandes arrest during negotiations, insistence on peaceful action, Centre/State asymmetry and the limited authority of Chief Ministers all correspond. The hundreds of arrests, warning against sabotage, opposition-leaders meeting, proposed Prime Ministerial assurance — attend to the problems, withdraw the strike, no victimisation — and the final peace appeal remain complete and source-ordered.

### 0088 — `என் பிறந்த நாளில்...!`

PASS. Kalaignar's refusal of an elaborate fiftieth-birthday celebration, recollection of leaving Chennai during the Anna-led celebration, and the three earlier funds — Beggars' Rehabilitation, Eyesight and abolition of hand-pulled rickshaws — all align. The `கவியரங்கம் / பாட்டரங்கம் / கலையரங்கம் / கூத்தரங்கம்` sequence, movement-flag and *Kazhagak Kural* directives, feeding a poor person and making that smile endure are preserved without converting the appeal into personal celebration.

### 0089 — `“மறவாதே!”`

PASS. The organisational-election chronology, headquarters workload, hostile-pressure catalogue and Anna's earthen-lamp image all correspond. The equal-pearls analogy preserves the point that losing does not make a cadre defective or rejected; the balance-needle image preserves neutral leadership; democratic competition, work/ability/sacrifice criteria and the `சிந்தாமல் சிதறாமல்` responsibility remain direct. The closing callback **“Every flower is a rose to me”** is retained.

### 0090 — `கோடையிலே...!`

PASS. The birthday gratitude and grief sequence, `மாமருந்து` image, sleepless burden and cyclone / simple `கட்டுமரம்` metaphor remain intact. The letter explicitly condemns Tamil–Malayali / Tamil–other-language violence, records the **forty arrests and cases**, and preserves the Cumbum/Kerala and Kanyakumari references. The Sivaji–M. G. R., *Alai Osai*, Kamaraj birthday and Bhaktavatsalam material remain in sequence, culminating in Kamaraj's quoted attack, the sarcastic summer **“cool breeze”**, and the lineated Congress-split counter-question.
