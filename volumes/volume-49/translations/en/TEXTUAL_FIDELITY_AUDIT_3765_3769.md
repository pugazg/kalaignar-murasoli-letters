# Textual-Fidelity Audit — Letters 3765–3769

## Scope

- Letters: **3765–3769**
- Canonical Markdown pages: **PDF 32–63**
- Total pages visually compared: **32**
- Source PDF: `TVA_BOK_0065839_கலைஞரின்_கடிதங்கள்_தொகுதி_49.pdf`

## Method

Before translating the five letters, every canonical Markdown page was rendered beside its corresponding PDF scan and visually compared page by page. The comparison covered:

- letter numbers and titles;
- salutations, paragraph order and page boundaries;
- names, dates, figures and quoted wording;
- punctuation and intentional English expressions;
- signatures and printed dates on closing pages.

Tamil-plus-English OCR was used only as a secondary discrepancy detector. The scan remained authoritative in every case.
