# 3791. Heartfelt Greetings to the Islamic Community!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This translation is intended to carry Kalaignar's voice into clear, contemporary English rather than recast the letter as literary or academic prose. It preserves the source's argument, political directness, rhetorical questions, repetition, irony, factual detail, and paragraph order. Names, dates, figures, quotations, and intentional English expressions are retained. Where Tamil idiom cannot be reproduced literally without sounding unnatural, the English follows its sense and rhetorical force without adding claims absent from the source. The original Tamil is reproduced in full below the translation and remains the authoritative text. `Udanpirappē` is retained in Tamil transliteration rather than flattened into “brother,” “sister,” or “comrade.” Literally evoking “one born alongside me,” Kalaignar uses it as a distinctive address of shared identity, equality, affection, and solidarity within the movement.

**Udanpirappē,**

Tomorrow, 9 August 2013, is the Ramadan festival of the Islamic community. As I convey my heartfelt good wishes to everyone, I wish also to recall briefly the achievements of the DMK Government for the welfare and advancement of that minority community.

In a press release issued on 4 August 2013, the Information Department of the Tamil Nadu Government stated that Chief Minister Jayalalithaa had ordered the grant to the Haj Committee to be increased to thirty lakh rupees. At the beginning of that release, it also referred to an increase in the number of beneficiaries under the Ulama Pension Scheme and to the monthly pension having been raised to one thousand rupees.

At a time when these achievements for the Islamic community under the AIADMK Government have been publicised, I wish to recall the many concessions given to that community during DMK rule. At the Iftar event held in Chennai on 25 July 2013, I did not merely send a message of greetings; I attended in person and, mindful of the available time, mentioned only a few of those measures. Having now seen the Government’s press release, I wish to recall the DMK Government’s other achievements for Muslims as well.

To improve the educational, social and economic conditions of minority communities and to implement welfare programmes effectively, the Directorate of Minorities Welfare was created under the DMK Government in 2007 and has functioned since 1 August 2007. To implement and monitor these schemes in the districts, thirty posts each of superintendent, junior assistant and typist were created in all districts except Tiruppur.

From the thirty per cent reservation for Backward Classes, a separate 3.5 per cent reservation was provided for Backward Class Muslims under Bill No. 9 of 2008. It was introduced by the DMK Government and has been implemented since 15 September 2007.

Accepting a longstanding demand of the Islamic community, the Urdu Academy was established in Tamil Nadu with an initial investment of fifteen lakh rupees. I inaugurated it under the DMK Government on 21 July 2000.

Prizes for first, second and third places have been awarded to three boys and three girls who studied Urdu as a subject and secured the highest marks at the State level in the Class 10 and Class 12 public examinations. Thirty-seven students benefited during the DMK Government.

Five new hostels benefiting 250 Muslim school and college students were opened under the DMK Government in 2008 in Tiruchi, Dindigul, Tirunelveli, Coimbatore and Vellore districts. Twenty-five computers and printers were supplied at a cost of ₹10.13 lakh so that the students staying in these hostels could learn computing. In 2009–10, colour television sets were provided to all five hostels at a cost of one lakh rupees.

With financial assistance from the Union Government, scholarships have been provided since 2007–08 to minority students pursuing vocational or technical education. Under this scheme, ₹18.53 crore was distributed to 7,090 students.

Again with Union Government assistance, scholarships for students from Class 11 through research studies were introduced under the DMK Government in 2007–08. Under this scheme, ₹22.22 crore was provided to 67,683 students.

With funding shared by the Union and State Governments in the ratio of 75:25, scholarships for minority students from Class 1 to Class 10 were introduced by the DMK Government in 2008–09. In the first phase, scholarships were obtained for Tamil Nadu and distributed to 15,340 students. As the Tamil Nadu Government created widespread awareness of the scheme among minority communities, ₹18.90 crore was distributed to 1,54,264 students in 2009–10 and ₹28.62 crore to 2,42,301 minority students in 2010–11.

Chief Minister Jayalalithaa’s order refers to Muslim Women’s Aid Societies. From 2007, the DMK Government established such a society in every district, with the District Collector as ex officio chairperson, to assist destitute Muslim women and those abandoned by their husbands. It was the DMK Government that provided each society with thirty lakh rupees as seed money. Orders were also issued to provide a matching grant, subject to a maximum of ten lakh rupees, equal to the funds each society raised for its development. During DMK rule, ₹44.37 lakh was provided to fourteen districts.

To help unemployed minority students obtain jobs in the private sector and improve their skills, specialised technical training in information technology, garment design and manufacture, footwear design and related fields was implemented for women and young people from 2007–08 with Tamil Nadu Government funding of ₹2.50 crore. Over four and a half years, 41,316 people benefited at a total expenditure of ten crore rupees.

The report containing Chief Minister Jayalalithaa’s order proudly speaks of raising the monthly pension of the ulama to one thousand rupees. But it was under the DMK Government that the Ulama and Employees Welfare Board was established on 24 August 2009 with an allocation of one crore rupees. Identity cards were issued to 11,274 members, and welfare assistance costing ₹12.93 lakh was given to 704 beneficiaries. Members of the Board and their families were enabled to obtain life-saving advanced medical treatment. In 2010–11, ₹3.18 crore was allocated to provide free bicycles to ulama and employees, and I inaugurated the scheme in Chennai on 28 February 2011 under the DMK Government.

The pension under the Ulama Pension Scheme, which had been ₹200, was increased to ₹400 by the DMK Government in 2006 and to ₹500 a month from 1 December 2010. The AIADMK Government subsequently raised that amount further. The number of ulama receiving pension, which was 2,000 in 1998–99, was raised to 2,200 and then to 2,400 in 2007–08 under DMK rule. The Ulama Pension Scheme was also extended by the DMK Government to mujawars serving at dargahs.

To safeguard the welfare and protect the rights of religious and linguistic minorities, a State Minorities Commission was established for the first time under the DMK Government on 13 December 1989. It began functioning on 14 March 1990.

With the worthy objective of protecting minority rights, statutory recognition was given to the Tamil Nadu Minorities Commission through the Tamil Nadu State Minorities Commission Act, 2010, enacted under the DMK Government. A Government Order was issued enabling it to function with statutory authority from 1 August 2010. The Commission operated under the chairmanship of the Reverend Vincent Chinnadurai with five members.

As soon as the DMK Government assumed office in 2006, it repealed the anti-conversion law enacted by the preceding AIADMK Government. It also returned the one day’s salary that the previous Government had withheld from all teachers who had participated in the protest against that law.

To advance minorities economically, the Tamil Nadu Minorities Economic Development Corporation was established under the DMK Government and has functioned since 27 December 1999. During the last DMK administration, it granted individual loans totalling ₹29.68 crore to 7,208 people from minority communities. Over five years, vehicle loans totalling ₹10,67,85,000 were given to 966 people for purchasing autorickshaws. Loans totalling ₹27.94 lakh were given to 146 people over five years to purchase milch cattle. Small-business loans totalling ₹46,46,90,000 were given to 34,589 people.

Jayalalithaa’s statement also discusses the Tamil Nadu Haj Committee at length. Saudi Arabia fixes each country’s Haj quota according to its Muslim population. Within India’s allocation, State quotas are fixed according to the Muslim population of each State as recorded in the 2001 Census. During DMK rule, applications exceeded Tamil Nadu’s quota. The DMK Government therefore requested the Prime Minister of India to grant an additional allocation. As a result, over five years the Union Government enabled 5,611 additional Muslims from Tamil Nadu to undertake the Haj, beyond the fixed quota of 13,626.

Mr M.S.A. Siddiq, Chairperson of the Union Government’s National Commission for Minority Educational Institutions, and the delegation that visited under his leadership examined the Tamil Nadu Government’s minority-welfare programmes. Fully satisfied with the DMK Government’s schemes, they praised it as a “Pro-Minority” Government.

Are these the only things the DMK Government did for the Islamic community? In 1969, Milad-un-Nabi was declared a Government holiday for the first time. The holiday, which the earlier AIADMK Government had withdrawn in 2001, was restored through an order dated 15 November 2006.

It was also under a DMK Government, in 1973, that Urdu-speaking Muslims were included in the Backward Classes list.

In 1974, the Government Women’s College on Anna Salai in Chennai was named the Quaid-e-Millath Government College for Women.

On 25 February 2001, under the DMK Government, the foundation stone was laid in Chennai for the Quaid-e-Millath memorial hall, to be built at a cost of ₹58.69 lakh, and arrangements were made for its completion and opening.

All these stand as evidence of the DMK Government’s achievements for the Islamic community. At this time, as the community celebrates Iftar, I take pride in recalling the work undertaken for it by the DMK.

Anna said, “Islam is a way of life.” The Holy Prophet taught principles of life that apply not only to Muslims but to all humanity. He said that everyone must treat others with affection and nurture relationships. Teaching that we must abandon the tendency to find fault with others, the Prophet said: “Do not go searching for the faults of others; do not lower others in order to raise yourself; do not envy others; do not speak ill of others behind their backs; and do not turn your face away from them.”

May the precious teachings of the Prophet remain engraved in our minds. Once again, I convey my heartfelt good wishes to all my Islamic Udanpirappugal who give to the poor and celebrate Ramadan as a festival of generosity, surrounded by their children, families and friends.

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**

8 August 2013

கல்லூரிக்கு “காயிதே மில்லத் அரசு மகளிர் கல்லூரி” எனப் பெயர்
சூட்டப்பட்டது.

2001இல் “காயிதே மில்லத் மணிமண்டபம்” 58 இலட்சத்து
69 ஆயிரம் ரூபாய் செலவில் சென்னையில் அமைத்திட 25.2.2001
அன்று தி.மு.கழக ஆட்சியிலே அடிக்கல் நாட்டி, திறக்கப்பட ஆவன
செய்யப்பட்டது.
இவை எல்லாம் தி.மு.கழக ஆட்சி இஸ்லாமிய சமுதாயத்திற்கு
செய்த சாதனைகளுக்கான சான்றுகளாகும். இஸ்லாமிய சமுதாயம்
“இப்தார்” நிகழ்ச்சியைக் கொண்டாடிக் கொண்டிருக்கும் இந்த
நேரத்தில் அந்தச் சமுதாயத்திற்காகத் தி.மு.கழகம் மேற்கொண்ட
இந்தப் பணிகளை நினைவுபடுத்துவதில் பெருமை கொள்கிறேன்.
“இஸ்லாம் என்பது ஒரு வாழ்க்கை நெறி” என்றார் அண்ணா.
இஸ்லாமியர்களுக்கு மட்டுமல்லாமல் மனித சமுதாயம்
அனைத்திற்கும் பொதுவாகப் பொருந்தும் வாழ்க்கை நெறிகளைப்
போதித்த அண்ணல் நபிகளார், ஒவ்வொருவரும் மற்றவர்களுடன்
அன்புடன் பழகி, உறவை வளர்க்க வேண்டும் என்றார்.
மற்றவர்களைப் பற்றிக் குற்றம் குறைகள் கூறும் மனப்பான்மையை
விட்டுவிட வேண்டும் என்று போதித்த அண்ணல் நபிகளார்,
“பிறருடைய குற்றங்களைத் தேடி அலையாதீர்கள்; நீங்கள்
உயர்ந்த நிலை அடைவதற்காகப் பிறரைத் தாழ்த்தி விடாதீர்கள்;
பிறர்மீது பொறாமை கொள்ளாதீர்கள்; பிறரைப் பற்றிப் புறம்
பேசாதீர்கள்; பிறரிடம் முகம் சுளிக்காதீர்கள்” என்கிறார்.
நபிகள் பெருமானின் மணிவாசகங்களை மனதில் பதித்து,
எளியோர்க்கு ஈந்து - ஈகைத் திருநாளாக இரமலான் திருநாளை
குழந்தைகள் உள்ளிட்ட குடும்பத்தார், நண்பர்கள் சூழக்
கொண்டாடி மகிழும் இஸ்லாமிய உடன்பிறப்புகள் அனைவருக்கும்
எனது உளமார்ந்த நல்வாழ்த்துகளை மீண்டும் உரித்தாக்குகிறேன்.
