## Review method

Each complete English record was compared against its complete audited Tamil source, including the Tamil appendix reproduced in physical PDF-page order. The review checked:

- title, salutation, continuation/conclusion marker, closing and date;
- paragraph and argumentative sequence;
- complete substantive coverage without summarising away source material;
- names, institutions, dates, figures, percentages, monetary amounts and units;
- lists, statutory references, reservation categories and historical sequences;
- quotations, rhetorical questions, accusation, sarcasm, repetition and source imagery;
- printed source English and documented source anomalies;
- absence of English additions that would introduce claims not present in the Tamil.

This gate is distinct from the later volume-level English editorial consistency review and final release verification.
