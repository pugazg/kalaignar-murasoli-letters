# 3494. Is It Fair? You Tell Me!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This translation follows the audited canonical Tamil and the locked Volume 44 conventions. The letter’s contrast between the Dravidian movement’s `அறப்போராட்டம்` tradition and the “Fort siege” agitation, its criticism of Communist parties, and the complete list of Nutritious Meal/Anganwadi employee concessions are retained without compression. Source anomalies remain visible in the Tamil appendix and are not silently repaired. The complete audited Tamil is reproduced below and remains authoritative.

**Tamil source:** [Letter 3494](../../../chapters/3494-niyaayam-thaanaa-neengale-sollungal.md)  
**Source pages:** [PDF 99](../../../pages/page-099.md)–[PDF 104](../../../pages/page-104.md)  
**Date:** 31 August 2010

**Udanpirappē,**

Whether the Dravidian movement was engaged in struggles as the Dravidar Kazhagam, or whether it was engaged in struggles as the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam, at no time did it gather the public with a call to “come to the battlefront” merely to advertise itself or deliberately create difficulty for a government; nor did it summon its party comrades in that fashion. Whenever it raised a demand on an issue, it was accustomed to conduct the related agitations peacefully and on the path of righteousness. That is why those struggles themselves came to be called `arapporattam` — moral, non-violent struggles.

Even when opposing Hindi domination at a time when the Tamil language appeared to face grave danger, around 1938 the volunteers in the moral agitation stood in formation at the entrance of the Tondaimandalam High School in Chennai. The members of the first contingent were named; on a specified day, at a specified school entrance, only the specified comrades peacefully picketed and were arrested. The party leadership did not announce that the language-war volunteers would besiege the school, or besiege the Fort of the rulers who had made Hindi compulsory; nor did it engage in such a struggle.

But I want to say one thing so that the general public — people who believe in democracy — may compare.

You know our Communists. Whatever agitation they conduct, they will not leave it without stamping it with a battlefield mark. First they call it a procession; next, a protest demonstration; after that, picketing; and then, a siege of the Secretariat — the Fort. They expect a government to stand watching all this, beat the drum in welcome for the force marching in formation, and offer greetings and a reception to the commanders of the siege. This is how Communist agitations have grown from demand-processions to the level of a “Fort siege.”

The fire they lit has flared up in some States of India to the level of “Maoist” attacks, lawlessness and loss of life. In Tamil Nadu too, they draw up plans and pass resolutions in the expectation that only by unleashing such lawlessness, violent acts and murderous attacks, and thereby placing at least some small stain on the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam government, can they stand in the election or enable their alliance leader, the “Lady of Siruthavur,” to wear the garland of victory and rule again.

Look at this! What have they said in substance in one of their statements? “In accordance with the decision of the State Executive Committee of the Nutritious Meal Employees’ Association, a Fort-siege agitation had been planned in Chennai on Monday, 30-8-2010, but the government stopped it.” That is the accusation against this government found in their leaflet.

They say they will march toward the Fort. They say they will conduct a siege. And the government and the police of this government are apparently expected simply to watch and happily feast them all.

What is their demand?

As their own statement says, their demand is that Nutritious Meal and Anganwadi employees who have worked for twenty-seven years on consolidated pay should be made permanent.

How old is this demand? They themselves say it is a twenty-seven-year demand. During these twenty-seven years, whether or not they were called Nutritious Meal workers, have thousands of employees on consolidated pay worked under governments or not? Were all of them made permanent under any government?

Has any government — even Communist governments such as West Bengal or Kerala — ever come forward to convert employees on consolidated pay and part-time employees into permanent employees? Yet, like the saying about the temple mendicant being the easiest person in town to push around, only in Tamil Nadu they say they will agitate demanding permanency, that they will even besiege the Fort, and that if the police stop them they will magnify the clash and go from town to town crying “the Tamil Nadu government’s ultimate lawlessness.” I do not know what kind of democracy it is to speak and write like this.

Let that be. Did this government ignore even this demand? It is precisely because we paid attention to them that, though we could not make them full-time government employees, we have granted under this government concessions that the previous AIADMK government did not grant. To give a few examples:

- Under AIADMK rule, Nutritious Meal and Anganwadi employees were not given even a single increase in their pay scale. Under the DMK government, their pay scale has been increased three times.
- For cooks, cooking assistants and Anganwadi assistants working in Anganwadi and Nutritious Meal centres, it was during this government that a decision was taken to provide **special time-scale pay** instead of consolidated pay.
- It was under the DMK government that, for the first time, Nutritious Meal and Anganwadi employees began receiving House Rent Allowance, City Compensatory Allowance, Medical Allowance and festival advance.
- It was under the DMK government that they began receiving a monthly pension for the first time.
- It is under the DMK government that they are being given promotions.
- It is under the DMK government that they are provided the General Provident Fund scheme, Special Savings Welfare Fund scheme, Family Welfare Scheme, new medical-insurance scheme, maternity leave, pay during the summer-vacation period and travelling allowance.
- The level of pay now received by Nutritious Meal organisers and Anganwadi employees in Tamil Nadu is higher than in any other State in India.

When the DMK government is granting concessions to them to this extent, is it fair for them to oppose this government and speak of a siege in front of the Fort? What has happened in the present Nutritious Meal employees’ issue that warrants besieging the Fort? Have laws such as `ESMA` and `TESMA`, as under AIADMK rule, been brought in now? Under that rule, government employees were arrested in the dead of night and the situation was such that no one even knew where they had been taken. Is there any such situation now? Or has an order been issued, as happened then, dismissing lakhs of government employees in a single day? Why a siege agitation?

Is this one association the only association for Nutritious Meal employees? On 21-11-2009, on behalf of various associations of Nutritious Meal employees, people such as M. Palaninathan and Varadarasan gathered thousands of Nutritious Meal employees at Valluvar Kottam and held a thanksgiving and felicitation function for me. Do those employees not know the truth? When I announced, one after another, fifteen concessions before them, did they not applaud until the hall itself echoed? Not only did they speak at that function; Palaninathan and Suriyamurthy issued statements thanking us for those concessions, and those statements remain in the newspapers even today. Why, when a pay increase was announced for Nutritious Meal employees within a few months of the DMK government taking office in 2006, members belonging to both Communist parties, the Congress and the PMK spoke in the Legislative Assembly itself expressing appreciation and thanks; it is recorded in the proceedings dated 1-9-2006!

Udanpirappē, to tell the truth, the Nutritious Meal employees have no grievance at all against this government. They are all quite happy. It is only a few association leaders who try to deceive them by saying they are taking them on a tour and that they can visit a few temples, and thus attempt to bring them to Chennai. Associations of other employees that genuinely want solutions to such demands approach me or the Ministers, present their demands and obtain concessions. It is those who deliberately want somehow to give this government a bad name who engage in such actions.

Should not the members of these associations recognise their true character and act after considering whether such concessions were ever available under any other government or in any other State? Is it fair to find fault with this government that follows the Kural principle of “ruling while embracing the people” — `குடிதழீஇக் கோலோச்சும்`? You tell me!

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**31-08-2010**

## Letter-specific notes

1. `சத்துணவு` is rendered **Nutritious Meal** in the institutional scheme/employee context; `அங்கன்வாடி` is retained as **Anganwadi**.
2. Administrative benefit terms are translated directly from the source, including **special time-scale pay**, House Rent Allowance, City Compensatory Allowance and the named welfare-fund schemes.
3. The canonical Tamil prints `அரசம்` in the PDF-101 sentence; the English renders the sentence function conservatively as “the government” without changing the Tamil source.
4. The final Kural phrase `குடிதழீஇக் கோலோச்சும்` is translated for its immediate source sense as “ruling while embracing the people”; no outside verse material is added.
