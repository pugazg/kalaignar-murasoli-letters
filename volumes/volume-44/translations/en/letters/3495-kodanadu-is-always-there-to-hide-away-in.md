# 3495. Kodanadu Is Always There to Hide Away In!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This translation follows the audited canonical Tamil and the locked Volume 44 conventions. The letter’s political sarcasm, MGR/Nutritious Meal Scheme argument, the *Vanavasam* / `சகவாசம்` wordplay, and both quoted employee-leader statements are retained in source order. Apparent tensions inside the source’s claims about employee status are preserved rather than reconciled from outside material. The complete audited Tamil is reproduced below and remains authoritative.

**Tamil source:** [Letter 3495](../../../chapters/3495-pathungik-kolla-irukkave-irukkirathu-kodanaadu.md)  
**Source pages:** [PDF 105](../../../pages/page-105.md)–[PDF 111](../../../pages/page-111.md)  
**Date:** 2 September 2010

**Udanpirappē,**

An argument is sometimes made that it is wrong to cite things that happened earlier and compare some of them with what is happening now. The question is raised: if those who were in office before committed a mistake, may we commit the same mistake? But the issue I raise between these two positions is this: when those who were in power earlier handled certain problems according to the circumstances of their time, and when we now handle a similar problem amid similar circumstances — but approach it more gently and skilfully than before — they act with political motive and ulterior intent, portray our present gentle approach as though it were some brutal approach, and warn that a storm is about to break. They lament as though an earthquake has already struck.

You may wonder what all this preamble is for! I am writing so that you and the public may understand the agitation by employees working in the government-run Nutritious Meal Scheme begun by our dear friend MGR — and the motive of those who have incited it.

You know that Jayalalithaa stands foremost among those who dream that if even a little stir arises anywhere against our DMK government, they can turn it into a great deluge, topple this government and come sit in its place themselves!

Recently, Nutritious Meal employees raised certain demands. Without even considering it necessary to discuss them with me or with the Minister concerned, they roared that they would leap straight into agitation and besiege the Secretariat Fort itself. They gathered forces from many towns with the dream that, if this government took any defensive countermeasure, they could magnify it and raise a storm. When that dream of some parties failed, they gathered every word available in the dictionary of abuse and have been showering them as offerings upon me and upon this government under my leadership.

At least our friends in the Marxist Communist Party have some reason to be upset that an agitation they began ended this way. They have the right, on that account, to spit fire at us. But what we cannot understand is why Jayalalithaa should jump into the middle of it and raise such a hue and cry.

Apparently I am taking revenge on all the Nutritious Meal employees! Today she speaks sweetly as though during her rule she had held a coronation for all of them, as though she had paid them full salary for half a day’s work to such an extent that they were in ecstasy!

“Ah! Is Karunanidhi taking revenge on the Nutritious Meal employees merely because our MGR introduced the scheme?” she cries, shedding tears.

Udanpirappē! There is a kind of tears called “Neeli tears.” That name suits Jayalalithaa’s tears!

What are the Nutritious Meal employees asking now? They work half a day — that is, part time — and ask for full-time pay. When they joined the service, did they not join accepting that their work was “part-time work”? When they were recruited, did the government give any assurance that they would later be made full-time employees? It was precisely because they were part-time workers that people from the respective localities were appointed to these posts. Is Jayalalithaa saying that the demand to make them full-time employees can simply be accepted as it stands?

Jayalalithaa was in power for ten years! Could she not have fulfilled this demand then? Even then the Nutritious Meal employees were making this demand!

Unable and unfit to answer any of this, Jayalalithaa went to the Tiruchi meeting in search of “*Vanavasam*”! And that too the *Vanavasam* written by Kannadasan! Did Kannadasan write only about *Vanavasam*? Did he not also write page after page about what the `சகவாசம்` — the “company” — of people like her was like?

Why stop there? Even more extensively, even more interestingly, letters written in her own hand still stand today as evidence of that “company,” do they not?

MGR introduced the Nutritious Meal Scheme, they say; and therefore we are taking revenge on Nutritious Meal employees!

What is the truth, Udanpirappē? Who expanded MGR’s Nutritious Meal Scheme further and made it truly nutritious — providing three eggs a week to the boys and girls who eat the meal, and bananas to those who do not eat eggs? Was it Jayalalithaa’s government, or the present Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam government?

Let Jayalalithaa obtain and read the present demands of the Nutritious Meal employees. Let her herself say which demands can be fulfilled!

Mr. Ko. Suriyamurthy, former State president who served for a long time as president of the Tamil Nadu Government Officials’ Union, a major organisation working for the welfare of government employees, and who recently retired, issued a press statement two days ago about these Nutritious Meal employees. He said:

> “Tamil Nadu has not experienced administrative deficiencies or adverse conditions in dearness allowance and pay such as those in States like West Bengal, Kerala and Tripura, where Communist parties rule. Nutritious Meal and Anganwadi employees and cooking assistants have obtained pay increases, promotions and several other concessions through smooth negotiations with the government. In the same way, they should remain united and try to obtain the concessions still due to them without becoming victims of political intrigue.
>
> “Nothing great can be achieved either by politically motivated action that hurts the minds of rulers who have humanely granted many concessions, or by issuing threats that ‘we will struggle.’ Since a large proportion of Nutritious Meal and Anganwadi employees and cooking assistants come from rural areas, and since they belong to Scheduled Caste, Backward and Most Backward communities, Honourable Chief Minister Kalaignar, with class concern and humanitarian feeling, on 15-9-2008 made these employees permanent, increased their pay, provided pension, and announced in his Independence Day address that higher pay would be given according to seniority. Following requests for a few more concessions, at the thanksgiving conference held on behalf of Nutritious Meal and Anganwadi employees at Valluvar Kottam, Chennai, on 21-11-2009, he participated and announced fulfilment of fifteen demands. When the government and the Chief Minister who guides it act with humanity and provide pay increases and increases in dearness allowance every six months, it is wrong to listen to ‘what someone else says’ and struggle against the welfare of the employees.
>
> “As one who worked for these employees and helped secure many concessions, I appeal that Nutritious Meal and Anganwadi employees should not become victims of political intrigue in the name of agitation. All Nutritious Meal and Anganwadi employees and cooking assistants have understood the true position. If anyone incites these employees thinking they will become puppets in the hands of whoever picks them up, it will not succeed — I state this firmly.”

Mr. Mu. Varadarasan, State president of the Tamil Nadu Integrated Nutritious Meal Employees’ Welfare Association, wrote to me:

> “In your golden rule, at a time when tens of thousands of Nutritious Meal and Anganwadi employees who had been yearning, ‘When will the flower bloom, when will dawn break?’ have been showered with many concessions and immersed in an ocean of happiness, an association, at the instigation of a political party, is attempting to gain political advantage by announcing a Fort siege on the coming 30th of August. At such a time, tens of thousands of employees who believe it is wrong to forget gratitude will gather from across the State and hold a thanksgiving procession for you in Chennai on the coming 17-9-2010.”

That statement comforts me by showing that not all Nutritious Meal employees are ungrateful.

Does Jayalalithaa accept the demand that people who work part time should be paid full-time salaries? If so, to how many lakhs of people would that pay increase have to be given? Has she looked at that calculation? If, without looking at any of it, she says, “If I come to power, I will make all of them full-time employees,” is that not an act of deceiving the people? Why did she not fulfil that demand during the ten years she was in power? The only calculation she wants is when the Fort’s gate will open for her! Not even a calculation — a dream! To make that dream come true, she dances every dance she has not danced, sings every song she has not sung and wears every disguise she has not worn!

She goes to Srirangam for darshan! A group waits to place a three-legged stool wherever she grows tired and stands! So she will say anything and do anything! If necessary, I will again remind you of how she made government employees suffer during her rule and of all the criticisms judges made in the courts about her government at that time.

For questions like these that she cannot answer, Kodanadu is always there for her to hide away in!

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**2-9-2010**

## Letter-specific notes

1. *Vanavasam* is retained as the source-named work; the following `சகவாசம்` wordplay is rendered as “company” while keeping the Tamil term visible in the note and appendix.
2. `நீலிக் கண்ணீர்` is kept as **Neeli tears** rather than replaced by a different English idiom.
3. The quoted Suriyamurthy statement is translated as printed, including its claim about employee permanency; it is not reconciled with other passages in the letter.
4. `சத்துணவு` / `அங்கன்வாடி` follow the batch convention **Nutritious Meal / Anganwadi**.
