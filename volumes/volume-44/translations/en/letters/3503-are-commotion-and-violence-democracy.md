# 3503. Are Commotion and Violence Democracy?

> **Translator’s note**
>

**Source pages:** [PDF 159](../../../pages/page-159.md)–[PDF 162](../../../pages/page-162.md)  
**Date:** 6 October 2010

**Udanpirappē,**

No one can deny that in any democracy the ruling party and the opposition are essential necessities. The ruling party and the opposition are like the two sides of a coin. Only then will the dignity of democracy gain greater and greater lustre. It is an accepted democratic practice for the opposition constructively to point out shortcomings that arise from time to time in administration, and for the ruling party, without any other ulterior motive, to remove shortcomings pointed out with sincerity of heart and keep the wheel of government moving.

But what do we see in Tamil Nadu politics? The DMK government is accomplishing achievements of a kind that history will celebrate, on a scale not seen before. It is a government that safeguards the welfare of every section — the poor and simple, working people, teachers, government employees, farmers and traders. With the lofty objective of providing every person living in Tamil Nadu with food security, medical security and shelter security, it has devised various schemes; as a result, lakhs upon lakhs of people have benefited and are living with fulfilment and peace. Good people, neutral observers, economists and organisations unaffiliated with political parties have been praising the DMK government’s schemes and actions.

Some opposition parties, unable to bear the praise that the DMK government receives day after day and the growing public support for it, spend their time and thought turning even a splinter found somewhere into a pillar and blowing upon even a tiny spark until it becomes a great fire. Any number of examples could be given. Let us look at the incident that took place in Chennai yesterday.

At 9 p.m. on 3-10-2010, a Metropolitan Transport Corporation bus left T. Nagar, Chennai, for Avadi. When the bus was travelling near the Padi Pudur bus stop, a Scorpio car attempted to overtake it. The car contained driver Udayakumar, Senthil Suresh, Stalin and Murugan.

Because the bus driver did not give way to the car, a contest and rubbing between the bus and car occurred. In the continuation of this, a verbal quarrel between the two groups intensified into a physical clash. In that clash, Kumaravel, Kumar, Umapathi, Dinakaran, Pappaiya and Subramaniam of the Metropolitan Transport Corporation were injured. All six injured persons were taken to Kilpauk Government Hospital and, after receiving the required treatment there, were sent back. But some persons belonging to the CITU union took Kumaravel, Kumar and Dinakaran and admitted the three to Chennai General Hospital. The trade unionists deliberately spread the false rumour that “one of the injured persons has died,” causing tension in the transport corporation depots.

As a result, on the morning of 4-10-2010, drivers and conductors belonging to the Anna Nagar, Avadi and Ambattur depots refused to operate buses. They blocked drivers and conductors who had taken buses out and made them stop the buses criss-cross on the roads themselves.

Every day 3,151 buses operate on Chennai city roads. But yesterday only 1,907 buses ran. Seven hundred and thirteen buses did not run. The reason was trade unions belonging to some opposition parties. All the newspapers have reported in detail how much hardship the public suffered because the buses did not operate.

The police registered cases under various sections, including attempted murder and damage to public property, against the four persons Senthil Suresh, Stalin, Murugan and Udayakumar; they arrested them and lodged them in Puzhal prison.

Do you know the true position in this? Senthil Suresh, who travelled in the car, is the son of Anbu, DMK councillor for Ward 37 of Ambattur Municipality. Among the drivers and conductors injured in the disturbance caused by the clash and admitted to hospital, Umapathi, Pappaiya and Subramaniam are members of the DMK-affiliated Labour Progressive Federation. Because they were DMK members or DMK supporters, the police did not refrain from arresting them, did not refrain from remanding them, and did not simply release them; on the contrary, they too were lodged in Puzhal prison. When this government’s police have taken all these steps without partiality and without showing favour, I ask that one think neutrally about how, by preventing buses from operating and drawing worker comrades into agitation, transport was paralysed and the public was made to suffer so greatly.

I am one who has proved not merely today or yesterday but from my youth onward that I do not wish any action hostile to a worker struggling for justice. Yet whatever anyone’s anger or grievance may be, how can one bear the loss caused when the public or public property is struck as a consequence?

Citing a clash between two groups, trade unions belonging to opposition parties went on strike and caused the public hardships of various kinds.

This is how opposition parties in Tamil Nadu function: even when there are no problems, they create problems, divert those concerned, and, without the slightest concern that the problems they create will cause hardship and distress to the public, act only on the basis of whether some issue can be found and used to bring disrepute upon the government. This incident too is evidence of that.

The people of Tamil Nadu are watching the condition in which opposition parties here are wholly intent, not on rendering constructive public service, but on using anything at all to conduct politics and seek advantage for the party to which they belong. I lovingly ask those who engage in such acts that disturb peace not to forget this.

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**6-10-2010**

## Letter-specific notes

1. The transport-corporation incident, union affiliations, arrests and hospital sequence are retained as reported by the letter and are not independently reconciled.
2. The source prints both `3 ஆயிரத்து 151` buses operating daily, `ஆயிரத்து 907` buses operating that day and `713` buses not operating. All three figures are preserved without arithmetic repair.
3. `தொழிலாளர் முன்னேற்ற சங்கம்` is rendered descriptively as the **DMK-affiliated Labour Progressive Federation** in this immediate context.

சிறையில் அடைக்கப்பட்டுமிருக்கிறார்கள். இத்தனை நடவடிக்கைகளையும் பாரபட்சமில்லாமல் - தயவு தாட்சண்யம் பாராமல்;
இந்த அரசின் காவல் துறை எடுத்திருக்கும்போது, பேருந்துகளை
இயக்கிடாமல்; தொழிலாளத் தோழர்கள் கிளர்ச்சியில்
ஈடுபடுவதால், போக்குவரத்து ஸ்தம்பித்துப் போய், பொதுமக்கள்
எவ்வளவு பெரும் பாதிப்புக்கு ஆளாகிட நேரிட்டது என்பதை
நடுநிலையோடு சிந்தித்துப் பார்த்திட வேண்டுகிறேன்.

என்பதை; இன்று நேற்றல்ல; இளமைக் காலம் முதலே நிரூபித்து
வருபவன் என்ற முறையில்; யாருடைய கோபதாபமானாலும்,
அதன் விளைவாக பொது மக்களோ, பொதுச் சொத்துக்களோ
தாக்குண்டு - ஏற்படும் இழப்பை எப்படித் தாங்கிக் கொள்ள
முடியும்?
இரு குழுவினருக்கிடையே ஏற்பட்ட மோதலை காரணமாகக்
காட்டி, எதிர்க் கட்சிகளைச் சேர்ந்த தொழிலாளர் சங்கங்கள் வேலை
நிறுத்தத்தில் ஈடுபட்டு, பொதுமக்களுக்கு பல்வேறு வகையிலும்
இன்னல்களை ஏற்படுத்தியிருக்கின்றன.
இப்படித்தான், தமிழகத்திலே இருக்கின்ற எதிர்க்கட்சிகள்
பிரச்சினைகளே இல்லை என்றாலும்கூட, பிரச்சினைகளை
உருவாக்கி, தொடர்புடையவர்களைத் திசைதிருப்பியும்,
உருவாக்குகின்ற பிரச்சினைகளின் மூலமாக பொதுமக்களுக்கு
அவதியும், இன்னலும் ஏற்படும் என்றாலும், அதைப் பற்றியெல்லாம்
கொஞ்சமும் கவலை கொள்ளாமல், எந்தப் பிரச்சினையாவது
கிடைக்காதா, அதை வைத்து அரசுக்கு அவப்பெயர் ஏற்படுத்த
முடியாதா என்ற அடிப்படையிலேயே செயல்பட்டு வருகிறார்கள்
என்பதற்கு இந்த நிகழ்ச்சியும் ஒரு சான்றாகும்.
தமிழகத்தில் உள்ள எதிர்க்கட்சியினர்க்கு ஆக்கப்பூர்வமான
முறையில் பொதுப்பணி ஆற்றிட வேண்டும் என்ற
நோக்கத்தைவிட; எதையாவது வைத்து அரசியல் நடத்திட
வேண்டும்; தாங்கள் சார்ந்திருக்கும் கட்சிக்கு ஆதாயம் தேடிட
வேண்டும்; என்பதிலேயே கண்ணுங்கருத்துமாக இருக்கிறார்கள்
என்ற நிலையைத் தமிழக மக்கள் கவனித்துக் கொண்டுதான்
இருக்கிறார்கள் என்பதை; இத்தகைய அமைதி குலைக்கும்

கேட்டுக்கொள்கிறேன்.
