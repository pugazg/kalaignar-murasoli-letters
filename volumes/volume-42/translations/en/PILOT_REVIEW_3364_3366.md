## Volume 42 conventions locked

1. **Udanpirappē** is retained without flattening it to “brother”, “sister” or “comrade”.
2. The plural `உடன்பிறப்புகள்` / `உடன்பிறப்புக்கள்` may be retained as **Udanpirappugal** where it carries the movement’s political-familial vocabulary.
3. Standard `அன்புள்ள, / மு.க.` closes as **With affection, M.K.**; the printed date remains visible.
4. Ordinary party references use **DMK**, **AIADMK** and **MDMK** where the Tamil abbreviations identify them clearly; the full **Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam** may be used where the source’s institutional framing benefits from it.
5. `மத்திய அரசு` is **Union Government** in institutional prose and `மாநில அரசு` is **State government**.
6. `இலங்கைத் தமிழர் நல உரிமைப் பேரவை` is provisionally **Sri Lankan Tamils’ Welfare Rights Council** for this volume.
7. Source-supplied English is preserved verbatim when it functions as printed source wording.
8. Political allegations, irony, repetition, rhetorical questions and slogans are preserved as source-framed material rather than neutralised or externally corrected.
9. The source’s “minority” wordplay in Letter 3364 is retained rather than regularised.
10. The Letter 3365 scissors image and the Letter 3366 `உள்ளம் / பள்ளம்` contrast are preserved as images rather than replaced by abstract paraphrase.
11. Every English record includes the complete audited Tamil under **Original Tamil — மூலத் தமிழ்**, with physical source-page markers retained.
