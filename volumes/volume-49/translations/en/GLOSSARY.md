# English Translation Glossary — Volume 49

| Tamil / source form | English treatment | Status | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| உடன்பிறப்பே | **Udanpirappē** | approved | Kalaignar's distinctive form of address, joining siblinghood with political fellowship. Retained rather than reduced to “comrade” or “dear brother”. |
| அன்புள்ள, மு.க. | **With affection, M.K.** | approved | Preserves the personal closing without making it formal. |
| திராவிட முன்னேற்றக் கழகம் / தி.மு.க. | **Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam (DMK)**; thereafter **DMK** | approved | Full form on first contextual need; abbreviation thereafter. |
| அனைத்திந்திய அண்ணா திராவிட முன்னேற்றக் கழகம் / அ.தி.மு.க. | **All India Anna Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam (AIADMK)**; thereafter **AIADMK** | approved | Full form on first contextual need; abbreviation thereafter. |
| கழகம் | **the DMK** / **the movement** | context-dependent | Chosen by reference and rhetorical force; not mechanically rendered “organisation”. |
| ஆட்சி | **government**, **rule**, or **regime** | context-dependent | `regime` is used only where the Tamil sentence itself is strongly critical. |
| லட்சம் | **lakh** | approved | Indian numbering unit: 100,000. |
| கோடி | **crore** | approved | Indian numbering unit: 10,000,000. |

| ரவுடி | **rowdy** | approved | Retained as an Indian policing and press term rather than broadened to “criminal”. |
| பேரறிஞர் அண்ணா | **Perarignar Anna** | approved | Established movement honorific retained; a generic replacement would lose the political relationship invoked by Kalaignar. |
| பேராசிரியர் | **Perasiriyar** when used as the established personal honorific | context-dependent | Translated as “Professor” only when the source uses it descriptively rather than as a recognised form of address. |

| கச்சத் தீவு | **Katchatheevu** | approved | Established Tamil place-name retained. |

| சமச்சீர் கல்வி | **Samacheer Kalvi** / **Uniform System of School Education** | approved | Tamil policy name retained on first reference with a brief English identification. |
| மாநிலங்களவை | **Rajya Sabha** / **Council of States** | approved | `Rajya Sabha` is used in normal reference; the institutional meaning is explained once. |
| 13வது அரசியல் சட்டத் திருத்தம் | **Sri Lanka's Thirteenth Amendment** | approved | Refers to the constitutional arrangement arising from the 1987 Indo–Sri Lanka Accord. |

| வழக்காடு மொழி | **language of court proceedings** | approved | Covers oral argument, judgments, orders and official court use; not reduced to “spoken language”. |

| பெருந்தலைவர் | **Perunthalaivar** | approved | “Great Leader”; retained as Kamarajar’s established honorific. |
| கர்ம வீரர் | **Karma Veerar** | approved | “Hero of action”; retained as an established honorific for Kamarajar. |
