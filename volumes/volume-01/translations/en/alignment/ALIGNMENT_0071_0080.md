# Volume 1 — Bilingual Alignment: Letters 0071–0080

Status: **PASS — no English prose/quotation correction required.**

## Scope

This batch covers canonical English letters **0071–0080**, corresponding to source PDF / printed pages **277–297**.

The English records had already completed canonical migration/source checking. This gate compared each English translation directly with the complete canonical Tamil witness embedded in the record. The canonical Tamil layer had already passed the full scan-based visual/textual-fidelity gate; this batch does not claim a new independent scan transcription pass.

## Alignment method

For every record, the review checked argument and narrative sequence; substantive coverage and unsupported additions; paragraph, verse and quotation correspondence; names, dates, figures and political references; lists, metaphors, wordplay and rhetorical questions; repetition, irony and political intensity; movement vocabulary and the established `Udanpirappē` convention; and closing/sign-off integrity.

Only demonstrable English/Tamil correspondence errors would be corrected at this gate. Stylistic polishing remains deferred to the later editorial-consistency gate.

## Batch result

**All ten records PASS bilingual alignment. No English prose/quotation correction was required.**

### 0071 — `காலக் கருவூலம்!`

PASS. The Becosules / B-Complex capsule explanation, printed English ingredient list, container/label details, disputed Delhi Time Capsule, alleged omissions and inaccuracies, Sezhiyan's parliamentary intervention and closing question about politics entering a historical record for descendants all correspond to the Tamil witness. The medicinal-capsule / `காலக் கருவூலம்` wordplay remains intact without normalising the mixed Tamil-English source forms.

### 0072 — `ஒன்று சொல்க!`

PASS. Bharathidasan's 1942 poem remains in source-controlled sequence; the Tiruvarur student movement, anti-Hindi / self-respect / rationalist explanation of the `பகைக் கூட்டம்`, students-as-medicine image, thirty-year recollection and final appeal to the young student all correspond. The abuse-as-music, stones-as-flowers, effigies-as-torches sequence and `நீர் இடித்து நீர் விலகாது` close remain unsoftened.

### 0073 — `புன்னகையும் பெருமூச்சும்!`

PASS. The Madras Corporation financial-misconduct action, equality before law, pain of arrests among former mayors and councillors, 1969 mayoral-election labour, Anna's ring, the same hand celebrating victory and signing dissolution, and the bird / nest / snake / chicks allegory all remain in source order. The final **sigh / smile** juxtaposition that gives the letter its title is preserved.

### 0074 — `குறள் மறவோம்!`

PASS. Bhaktavatsalam's statement praising impartial action, his earlier Assembly observation that Corporation wrongdoing crossed party lines, Kalaignar's willingness to welcome both praise and criticism, readiness to correct faults, and the closing Thirukkural all correspond. The English renders the Kural's meaning while the canonical Tamil preserves the source couplet rather than substituting text from another edition.

### 0075 — `உன் எதிரே!`

PASS. The direct second-person cadre address, Madurai station and Tamukkam sequence, reunion of estranged comrades, Anna Arivalayam fundraising, Ramanathapuram rain, Tiruchirappalli weariness, Salem/Namakkal gathering and the **₹1 lakh / ₹1.20 lakh-plus / ₹1.30 lakh** progression remain aligned. The closing declarations preserve the repeated `நீ`, the falsehood/betrayal/intimidation sequence and the movement's rising rhetorical force.

### 0076 — `தட்டிக் கேட்கலாமா?`

PASS. The three-sons analogy remains structurally complete: genuine physical weakness deserves support; unused ability should not be rewarded as helplessness; and limited strength joined to effort must not become grounds for deprivation. The Sixth Finance Commission argument then maps this distinction onto genuinely resource-poor states, administratively underperforming states and states such as Tamil Nadu that use available resources effectively. The source-controlled `உடல் வலிவு பெறும்` and the final `தட்டிக் கேட்கும்` demand retain their force.

### 0077 — `பெரியாரின் வெற்றி!`

PASS. The mother/child analogy, caste/religious hatred/superstition sequence, distinction between Periyar's objective and debate over his methods, hostility while he lived, respectful posthumous journal and national-leader tributes, openness to considered advice and the closing `வெற்றி அறுவடை` / **harvest of victory** image all correspond without flattening the argument.

### 0078 — `நம்மை வென்றாரா?`

PASS. The letter's deliberate self-critical turn remains intact. It distinguishes the Kazhagam's practical difference with Periyar on belief while acknowledging agreement on uprooting caste, religious divisions and superstition; then enumerates caste's persistence in political, social, office, employment, recommendation, kinship and `கொள்வன கொடுப்பன` thought. The full-length-mirror test and painful answer **“No! He did not win us over!”** remain direct.

### 0079 — `இருபதாயிரம் பாடி வீடுகள்!`

PASS. The branch-election structure, more than twenty thousand branches and more than one million members, discipline across leadership and cadre ranks, severed-lizard-tail image, blood-vessel / convulsive-disease contrast, internal contest and post-election unity, Anna's sibling formulation, hand-lamp imagery and closing military metaphor all correspond. `பாடி வீடுகள்` remains **military camps**, with members as soldiers guarding Anna's movement and flag, rather than being flattened into administrative branch offices.

### 0080 — `கொள்கை மலர்கள்`

PASS. The criteria for office, self-questioning before seeking responsibility, office as labour rather than ornament, endurance of opposition and ridicule, patience before obstacles, firmness before betrayal, and `தன்னலமற்ற-கண்ணயராத` labour all remain represented. The closing image is preserved: office-bearers blooming from twenty thousand branches become flowers of ideals/principle gathered into a victory garland for Anna.
