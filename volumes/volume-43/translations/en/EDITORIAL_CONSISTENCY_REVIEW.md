## Method

This is the separate volume-level consistency gate required after source-check drafting and complete bilingual meaning-level alignment. The review used the English README/index, locked `GLOSSARY.md`, `TRANSLATION_MANIFEST.csv`, all six completed `BILINGUAL_ALIGNMENT_REVIEW_*.md` reports and the aligned bilingual records for Letters 3428–3483. Those alignment reports had already compared each complete English record against its complete audited Tamil source, including the `Original Tamil — மூலத் தமிழ்` appendix in physical PDF-page order.

The editorial pass therefore concentrated on cross-record consistency rather than retranslating or re-adjudicating source meaning. It checked title/front-matter/manifest agreement, dates and PDF ranges, translator-note conventions, names and honorifics, place-name and transliteration forms, institutions and schemes, legal/political/reservation terminology, Indian public-language quantities, capitalization, punctuation and quotation treatment, source-supplied English, source anomalies, facsimile handling, stale QA wording and complete appended Tamil.

The controlling scan remains the ultimate textual authority. No possible residual Tamil defect was exposed by this editorial pass, so no new scan comparison or Tamil canonical change was required.

### Salutation, closing and facsimile exception

The locked forms remain `Udanpirappē` and `With affection, M.K.` where the source has the ordinary letter structure. No ASCII/wrong-accent `Udanpirappe` variant was found.

Letter **3467** is intentionally different and was not normalised: it is a facsimile-bearing archival record. Its printed contents date remains blank, while the record separately displays `Record-level handwritten date: 2 November 1974` and `Printed contents date: blank`. Its photographic handwriting remains unguessed and untranslated.

### Terminology and quantities

The locked recurring treatments remain coherent across the corpus, including **State autonomy**, **internal reservation**, **World Classical Tamil Conference**, **copper-plate record**, **persons with disabilities**, and **differently abled persons / differently abled people** in the source-defined rights context. No `State self-rule`, `sub-reservation`, hyphenated `differently-abled`, or incorrect `Udanpirappē` variant remained.

One English-body inconsistency was demonstrated in Letter **3481**. The canonical Tamil rendering of the *Wall Street Journal* article says `ஐம்பது இலட்சம் மக்கள் தொகை`, while the English body had converted that to `five million people`. Volume 43 locks Indian public-language units `lakh` / `crore`, and the surrounding 3481 translation already follows that convention (`15 lakh cars`, `two lakh people`). The editorial pass therefore corrected **`five million people` → `50 lakh people`**. This is an English consistency correction only; canonical Tamil is unchanged.

### Union/State terminology

Volume 43 locks `மத்திய அரசு` as **Union Government** in institutional/political prose. Letter **3483** correctly used `Union Government` around its price-rise argument but one translated Tamil newspaper quotation alone used `Central Government`. Because that quotation is translated from the Tamil rendering printed in the source, not protected source-supplied English, the editorial pass corrected **`Central Government` → `Union Government`**. Meaning and attribution are unchanged.

### Source-supplied English and documented anomalies

Genuinely printed English remains untouched. The audit specifically reconfirmed protected strings including the Kerala Supreme Court English in 3430; the disability-law English in 3463; `Don’t fail the Women of India` in 3464; `and/ or` and `ourinterference` in 3468; `Dear Chief Justice,` in 3475; and Letter 3481’s exact *Wall Street Journal* headline, `THE ASIAN WALL STREET JOURNAL`, `Advertisement - Advantage Tamil Nadu-`, `Further to our telecon...`, and `Rs. 3,88,62,225`.

Documented source anomalies also remain intact: Letter 3477 retains **85 surviving / 86 inscribed / 86 invoked** copper plates; Letter 3482 preserves canonical `3.54 மெட்ரிக் டன்` in the Tamil appendix while documenting the contextual English unit; and the final genuine printed-contents / actual-title distinctions remain independent.

### Quotations, rhetoric and source voice

The completed alignment phase had already verified quotations, rhetorical questions, repetition, irony, accusation, political force, figures and source order. This editorial pass found no cross-record convention that justified weakening, broadening, modernising or reattributing those passages. Apart from the two consistency corrections above, no bilingual body was edited.
