## English translation

**Udanpirappē,**

Today is 1-8-2012. There are only ten days in between. On Sunday, 12-8-2012, on behalf of our “TESO” organisation, we are conducting the Conference for the Protection of the Right to Life of Eelam Tamils. In connection with that conference, at about 10 in the morning, at the “Accord” Hotel situated near the Kalaivanar statue in T. Nagar, Chennai, a conclave will be held under my chairmanship. There will be an exchange of views at the conclave. How the resolutions to be adopted at the public conference in the evening should be framed, and what our demands and appeals regarding the issue of Eelam Tamils should be — all these will be discussed and decided there.

At about 4 in the evening, the public conference will be held at the YMCA grounds in Royapettah. It is at that conference that I will be able to meet you and our Udanpirappugal who come with you. At both the conclave and the evening public conference, our dear friend, Honourable Sharad Pawar, Union Minister for Agriculture and one of India’s senior political leaders; Honourable Farooq Abdullah, son of my old friend, the “Lion of Kashmir” Sheikh Abdullah, and Union Minister for New and Renewable Energy; friend Shri Sharad Yadav, leader of the Janata Dal (United) and Member of Parliament; Shri Ram Vilas Paswan, leader of the Lok Janshakti Party, my long-time friend and Member of Parliament; and Professor Shri Ram Gopal Yadav, General Secretary of the Samajwadi Party and Member of Parliament, will participate.

From Sri Lanka, on behalf of the Tamil National Alliance, Members of Parliament and friends Shri Mavai Senathirajah, Shri Sumanthiran, Shri Yogeswaran and Shri K. Saravanapavan; on behalf of the Tamil National People’s Front, friends Shri Gajendrakumar Ponnambalam, Shri Selvarajah Kajendren, Tmt. Padmini Sithamparanathan and advocate Shri Viswalingam Manivannan; Member of Parliament Shri E. Selvam Adaikalanathan; and Dr. Shri Wickramabahu Karunaratne, leader of the Nava Sama Samaja Party, are coming to express the feelings and inner concerns of Eelam Tamils.

Further, from Nigeria, Edo State Minister for Commerce and Industry Honourable Osagie-na Boy Donald; Member of Parliament Shri Abdul Razak Momo; Swedish parliamentarian Shri Nasim Malik; from Morocco, Shri Daida Mohammed and Shri Afekhomuyak; from Turkey, Dr. Shri Kemal Yildirim; from Malaysia, Member of Parliament Shri Yusmadi Yusoff; and Shri Anand Gurusamy will also come. Because they have fully understood the problems of Eelam Tamils, they will extend their support and speak.

Apart from them, Eelam Tamils living in different parts of the world are continuously asking through the Internet whether there is an opportunity to participate in our “TESO” conference. Many more have also expressed a desire to attend as observers.

Conference work is being discussed in detail morning and evening every day at Anna Arivalayam, and preliminary work and arrangements are proceeding accordingly. The General Secretary, our Inamaana Perasiriyar, who is a member of TESO on behalf of our Kazhagam, comes to Anna Arivalayam and offers his experienced suggestions for the conference work. Another member, sister Subbulakshmi Jagadeesan, is in Erode; though she cannot come to the Chennai office every day, whenever she finds time she continues to give suggestions for the conference over the telephone. One of the TESO members, Tamilar Thalaivar, younger brother Veeramani, had told me before leaving for America on work that he would return soon to attend to conference work. Because he fell ill in America, he is receiving treatment there. He has written to me from there saying that somehow he will come for the conference. Another TESO member, younger brother Thirumavalavan, leader of the Viduthalai Chiruthaigal movement, came to my house, asked what his duty was in the conference work, and also offered suggestions regarding the conference. Another member, younger brother Suba. Veerapandian, who had gone to London because of his work, has returned to Tamil Nadu specifically for this conference. Morning and evening he comes to Anna Arivalayam and has been of immense help to me in the work relating to this conference.

Apart from the TESO members, Kazhagam functionaries and younger brothers Treasurer M.K. Stalin, Deputy General Secretary Duraimurugan, Organisation Secretaries T.K.S. Elangovan, M.P., Kalyanasundaram, Dr. Ponmudy, advocates K.S. Radhakrishnan and Hasan Mohammed Jinnah are tirelessly attending to conference arrangements; E.V. Velu is attending to publicity and invitation work and similar tasks. Younger brother K.S. Radhakrishnan has been contacting Tamil leaders in Sri Lanka by telephone and inviting all of them to our conference. Parliamentary Party leader T.R. Baalu is attending to the work of speaking with North Indian leaders and inviting them to the conference. Union Minister of State Jagathrakshakan is making arrangements for the conclave and for accommodation for the guests. Chennai district Kazhagam secretary J. Anbazhagan is overseeing construction of the stage for the public conference.

A booklet explaining the central objectives of this conference was also prepared on behalf of “TESO” in English and Tamil and given to the newspapers as early as 16-7-2012. Rajya Sabha Member Tmt. Kanimozhi gathered and supplied the important notes required for this booklet. Younger brother Hasan Mohammed Jinnah played a major role in preparing it.

All the resolutions we are going to adopt at this conference are part of an effort to strengthen and improve the livelihoods of Eelam Tamils affected by war and living displaced, to enable them to live with dignity and democratic rights, and to emphasise in what ways we in mother Tamil Nadu and the Government of India can help them. Our Tamil people, unable to continue living in Eelam, are suffering in many countries of the world under the names of diaspora Tamils and refugees. At the conference conclave, we are also going to discuss and decide what can be done through international institutions such as the United Nations so that they may return to Sri Lanka and lead a peaceful life with rights.

When we announced this conference, you would have read how some newspapers in Tamil Nadu criticised it! They gave as much publicity as they could saying the conference would not take place, that the Union Government would not grant permission, that the State Government was going to ban it, that the Kazhagam front-rank leaders did not support the conference, and that it would not succeed. All those newspapers are still being published. Let time decide to what extent those newspapers work to safeguard Tamil ethnic feeling.

Have the problems of Eelam Tamils ended? In the 29-7-2012 issue of *Dinamalar*, an article appeared under the heading, “A New Danger Sprouts in the Sea — Sri Lanka Helps the Infiltrating China.” It reported that immediately after the war ended Sri Lanka asked China to undertake development works in its country and gave Chinese engineers a red-carpet welcome; that under the guise of engineers, engineers from the construction wing of the Chinese military entered Sri Lanka; that China is establishing, with Sri Lanka’s permission, a massive naval base at Hambantota at the southern tip of Sri Lanka; and that high-speed patrol boats manufactured by the Chinese navy have begun intruding up to the Indian maritime boundary and testing the limits.

The article continued that Rameswaram fishermen had raised a warning that Chinese personnel were also present with Sri Lankan naval personnel, and that the Union Government should monitor this and act immediately. At the end of that article, *Dinamalar* also wrote that, with the help of Chinese naval personnel, the Sri Lankan navy was rapidly carrying out work on a temporary naval camp in the seventh sandbank area along the international maritime boundary near Dhanushkodi; that DMK leader Karunanidhi had warned the Union Government about this a few days earlier; and that fishermen warned that continued silence by the Union Government on the Sri Lankan issue would not be favourable to India’s security.

Should we not bring all this to the attention of the Union Government and demand action against threats to the security of our Indian nation? It is to seek answers to all these questions that we are conducting the TESO conference on the 12th. “Let those who praise, praise; let those who fling dust and revile, revile; our duty is only to remain engaged in service to Tamils” — to the extent that we can, our voice will sound for Eelam Tamils, and will continue to sound loudly.

The TESO conference is a conference to apply medicine to the wounds of Eelam Tamils who have been grievously affected in the violence caused by Sinhala domination, and to the suffering and sorrow that have entered their lives! Understanding the central objective of this conference, your elder brother calls upon everyone to participate.

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**02-08-2012**

உடன்பிறப்பே,

இன்று 1-8-2012 இன்னும் இடையில் பத்தே நாட்கள் தான் உள்ளன. 12-8-2012 ஞாயிற்றுக் கிழமை அன்று நமது “டெசோ” அமைப்பின் சார்பில் நாம் நடத்தும் ஈழத் தமிழர் வாழ்வுரிமைப் பாதுகாப்பு மாநாடு. அந்த மாநாட்டினையொட்டி காலை 10 மணியளவில் சென்னை, தியாகராய நகரில் கலைவாணர் சிலை அருகில் அமைந்துள்ள “அக்கார்ட்” ஓட்டலில் ஆய்வரங்கம் (கான்கிளேவ்) என் தலைமையிலே நடைபெறவுள்ளது. ஆய்வரங்கத்தில் கருத்துக்கள் பரிமாற்றம் நடைபெறும். மாலையில் நடைபெறவுள்ள பொது மாநாட்டில் நிறைவேற்றப்பட வேண்டிய தீர்மானங்கள் எவ்வாறு வடிவமைக்கப்பட வேண்டும், ஈழத் தமிழர்கள் பிரச்சினையில் நம்முடைய கோரிக்கைகள், வேண்டுகோள்கள் என்னென்ன என்பதைப் பற்றியெல்லாம் அந்த ஆய்வரங்கில் கலந்து பேசித் தீர்மானிக்கப்படும்.

சென்று, இந்தியத் திருநாட்டின் பாதுகாப்புக்கெதிரான அச்சுறுத்தல்கள் மீது நடவடிக்கை எடுக்க வேண்டுமென்று நாம் கோர வேண்டாமா? இதற்கெல்லாம் விடை காணுவதற்காகத்தான் 12ஆம் தேதி டெசோ மாநாட்டினை நாம் நடத்துகிறோம். - “போற்றுவோர் போற்றட்டும், புழுதி வாரித் தூற்றுவோர் தூற்றட்டும், நம் கடன் தமிழர்க்கு பணி செய்து கிடப்பதே” என்று, நம்மால் முடிந்த வரை, நம்முடைய குரல் ஈழத் தமிழர்களுக்காக ஒலிக்கும், ஓங்கி ஒலித்துக் கொண்டே இருக்கும்.

இலங்கையில் சிங்களவர்களின் ஆதிக்க வெறியினால் ஏற்பட்ட கலவரங்களில் பெரிதும் பாதிக்கப்பட்ட ஈழத் தமிழர்களின் காயங்களுக்கும் - அவர்கள் வாழ்வில் ஏற்பட்ட துன்ப துயரங்களுக்கும் மருந்து போடுகின்ற மாநாடு தான் டெசோ மாநாடு! இந்த மாநாட்டிற்கான மைய நோக்கத்தைப் புரிந்து கொண்டு அனைவரும் பங்கேற்க வேண்டுமென உன் அண்ணன் அழைக்கின்றேன்.
