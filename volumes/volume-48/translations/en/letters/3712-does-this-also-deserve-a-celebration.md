# 3712. A Felicitation Ceremony for This Too?

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This translation is intended to carry Kalaignar's voice into clear, contemporary English rather than recast the letter as literary or academic prose. It preserves the source's argument, political directness, rhetorical questions, repetition, irony, factual detail, and paragraph order. Names, dates, figures, quotations, and intentional English expressions are retained. Where Tamil idiom cannot be reproduced literally without sounding unnatural, the English follows its sense and rhetorical force without adding claims absent from the source. The original Tamil is reproduced in full below the translation and remains the authoritative text. `Udanpirappē` is retained in Tamil transliteration rather than flattened into “brother,” “sister,” or “comrade.” Literally evoking “one born alongside me,” Kalaignar uses it as a distinctive address of shared identity, equality, affection, and solidarity within the movement.

**Date:** 3 March 2013

**Udanpirappē,**

It is said that farmers in Thanjavur are going to thank Chief Minister Jayalalithaa and hold a felicitation ceremony for her because the Cauvery Tribunal's final award has been published in the Central Gazette! Because Karnataka did not release Cauvery water for cultivation in the Delta districts, the Tamil Nadu Chief Minister filed a case in the Supreme Court. While hearing that case, the judge—without any plea from the Tamil Nadu government on this point—asked why the Tribunal's final award had not been published and directed the Union Government to publish it immediately. They now say Jayalalithaa is to be felicitated for an order the Supreme Court itself issued on its own initiative. Very well; let them praise her.

But I have a doubt! This year, because Cauvery water was not received, cultivation failed for farmers throughout the Cauvery Delta, leaving them immersed in suffering after losses running into many crores of rupees. Seventeen farmers have so far died by suicide. Perhaps a felicitation ceremony can be held for Jayalalithaa for that too!

There were protests demanding compensation of ₹10 lakh for each of the families of the seventeen farmers who died by suicide, along with a government job for one member of each family. Yet the Tamil Nadu government reduced even the number of the dead, saying that only nine were farmers, and provided merely ₹3 lakh each to those families. Jayalalithaa can be praised for that too!

Instead of the demanded relief of ₹25,000 per acre, only ₹15,000 has been announced. Even that relief is not being given to every affected farmer; the Chief Minister has announced it only for farmers whose yield was affected by more than 50 per cent. Though there were demands to cancel farmers' cooperative loans, and repeated appeals to give agricultural labourers ₹10,000 each, nothing has been given; none of these pleas has been heeded. Jayalalithaa can be praised for giving no answer to the continuing cries of anguish from farmers!

What did the *Dinamalar* report dated 26 February 2013 say under the headline **“Confusion in Paying Crop Compensation to Delta Farmers”**?

> “Because confusion has arisen in the distribution of crop-loss compensation, farmers' associations in the Delta districts are once again preparing for protest. A demand has arisen that the Chief Minister intervene directly and take steps to resolve the problem.”

The paper went on to publish in detail the criticism expressed by Keezhvelur Dhanapalan, General Secretary of the Cauvery Farmers Protection Association. Why not praise Jayalalithaa for this news too!

Although Chief Minister Jayalalithaa had already declared the other 31 districts apart from Chennai as drought-affected districts, relief was announced only for farmers whose crops were affected in the Delta districts. Now accusations are being raised in the Delta districts that even this relief has not been distributed properly. Protests and road blockades have begun. Jayalalithaa can surely be praised in many ways for creating disparities and disappointment among farmers like this!

The Communist Party of India under Tha. Pandian seems to regard supporting the AIADMK government—and especially the Chief Minister—as the very purpose of its birth. Yet do you know what the leading news in its newspaper *Janasakthi* was on the 26th and 27th?

On the first day, accompanied by photographs, it carried the headline **“Farmers and Agricultural Labourers Continue Road Blockades—All Transport Halted.”** The report said:

> “The Tamil Nadu Farmers Association and the Tamil State Agricultural Labourers Association are conducting continuous road-blockade protests across Tiruvarur district, pressing demands including that the Tamil Nadu government provide appropriate relief to agricultural labourers who lost employment during the kuruvai and samba seasons, and that full relief be given, in accordance with the Chief Minister's announcement, to all affected farmers.”

It then reported where the road blockades had taken place and under whose leadership. On the following day too, farmers and agricultural labourers held road blockades in Tiruvarur district for a second day. Even those accustomed to praising the AIADMK government have come forward to list grievances and raise their voice. Why not rejoice at that and praise Jayalalithaa!

Jayalalithaa now tells reporters that **“publication of the Cauvery Tribunal's final award in the Gazette has provided a guarantee for the future of Tamil Nadu's Delta farmers.”** But when the Tribunal's final award was delivered on 5 February 2007, did she accept it as a good award? Did she not issue a statement calling it **“an award adverse to Tamil Nadu”**? For the marvel by which an adverse award has become a beneficial award, why not praise Jayalalithaa over and over again!

On 21 March 1991 Jayalalithaa said:

> “Even if the Tribunal gives an award, Cauvery water will come to Tamil Nadu only if they themselves open the dam in Karnataka. The Tribunal's award can be useful only if Karnataka agrees and adopts a fair and favourable decision.”

Even after the Tribunal issued its interim award, Jayalalithaa was the one who said:

> “Even if the Tribunal gives an award, Karnataka Chief Minister Bangarappa is under no compulsion to implement it.”

Yet she now calls publication of the Tribunal's award in the Gazette a twenty-two-year victory and a thirty-year achievement. Why not offer a full-throated tribute to this contradictory position!

Speaking about remarks made by Thanjavur district minister Vaithilingam at a function, Sami Natarajan, Thanjavur District Secretary of the Tamil Nadu Farmers Association affiliated with the Communist Party of India (Marxist), said:

> “They have said only 12,300 acres were affected in Thanjavur district. Even that survey has been completed in a way that allows only ruling-party people to benefit. Farmers are enraged. In this situation Minister Vaithilingam spoke to the farmers asking, ‘Have you no sense? Are you emotionless lumps? Will you not applaud what I say?’ This is irresponsible. We condemn it on behalf of the farmers.”

Several other farmers' association leaders too condemned the minister's remarks. Why not glorify and praise Jayalalithaa even for an AIADMK minister speaking of farmers as people without sense—as emotionless lumps!

P.R. Pandian, Tiruvarur District Deputy Secretary of the Tamil Nadu Farmers Association, said: **“What was announced was ₹15,000 per acre, but what is being given is only ₹3,000.”** For the trick of announcing one thing and giving another, Jayalalithaa can be praised as many times as one likes!

The Chief Minister's announcement that relief in the Delta districts would cover 1.75 lakh farmers and 3.61 lakh acres of crops was not based on a properly conducted survey, it is being said; the Chief Minister simply announced some figures that were written down and handed to her. Demands and complaints continue to emerge one after another: that the relief amount is insufficient and must be increased; that agricultural labourers too must receive relief; that affected farmers in other districts must receive relief; and that assistance must also be given to farmers in the Delta districts whose yields were affected by less than 50 per cent. Why not praise Jayalalithaa without limit for making this half-hearted relief announcement that has caused such cries of anguish!

In accordance with agricultural scientists' advice that farmers should not depend on paddy cultivation alone, several lakhs of farmers cultivated alternative crops such as sugarcane, black gram, banana, groundnut, coconut and sesame, and they too have been badly affected. Demands continue that relief be given to them as well, both to encourage alternative cultivation and protect farmers who adopt it. Since the rulers have paid not the slightest attention to this demand, why not offer Jayalalithaa our praise for that too?

Therefore, even while the present relief is being distributed, a fresh survey must be conducted of affected farmers throughout Tamil Nadu. These details cannot be learned by sending only a few ministers. A committee of senior IAS officers should be constituted to obtain complete, unbiased statistics about all those affected. Relief should then be announced accordingly, and the money should be distributed properly in the presence of an all-party committee formed for that purpose.

Like the saying about **“showing the head to the fish and the tail to the snake,”** *Janasakthi*, which maintains great friendship with the AIADMK government, publishes every day reports of road blockades because relief assistance is not being received properly, yet does not hesitate at the same time to welcome and praise the Tamil Nadu government's actions. Even that paper has written:

> “It is clear that the high-level committee did not bring the true facts to attention. In many affected villages, affected lands were left out of the survey. A large section of the affected farmers too has been omitted. The figures now announced—3.61 lakh acres affected and relief to 1.75 lakh farmers—are not a correct count either of the affected area or of the number of farmers. Anger and agitation have arisen among farmers. In rain-fed districts too, a complete study and survey of the damage must be conducted and relief provided without omissions.”

It also noted that several further demands had been written directly to the Chief Minister.

Casting all these warnings aside, concentrating instead on panegyrics, songs of praise and congratulatory speeches, putting up roadside boards calling her **“Mother of Justice”** and **“Mother Cauvery,”** and continuing to divert Tamil Nadu's farmers—who are already struggling through many forms of suffering—into one more “felicitation ceremony” and “farmers' thanksgiving function”: people will regard all this only as an arrangement to deceive the farmers.

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**3 March 2013**

## Letter-specific notes

1. The repeated invitation to “praise” or “felicitate” the Chief Minister is sustained irony in the source and is preserved rather than compressed.
2. `kuruvai` and `samba` remain the established names of the Cauvery Delta paddy seasons.
3. **“Showing the head to the fish and the tail to the snake”** is retained close to the Tamil proverb; in context it criticises taking accommodating positions toward opposing sides.
4. All acreage, compensation and suicide counts are translated as stated in the audited source, without external reconciliation.
