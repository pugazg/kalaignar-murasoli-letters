# 3587. What the Mother-in-Law Broke; What the Daughter-in-Law Broke!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This translation is intended to carry Kalaignar's voice into clear, contemporary English rather than recast the letter as literary or academic prose. It preserves the source's argument, political directness, rhetorical questions, repetition, irony, factual detail, and paragraph order. Names, dates, figures, quotations, and intentional English expressions are retained. Where Tamil idiom cannot be reproduced literally without sounding unnatural, the English follows its sense and rhetorical force without adding claims absent from the source. The original Tamil is reproduced in full below the translation and remains the authoritative text. `Udanpirappē` is retained in Tamil transliteration rather than flattened into “brother,” “sister,” or “comrade.” Literally evoking “one born alongside me,” Kalaignar uses it as a distinctive address of shared identity, equality, affection, and solidarity within the movement.

**Date:** 13 September 2011

**Udanpirappē,**

> “Housing on 311 acres at Thirumazhisai—satellite town near Chennai—Jayalalithaa's announcement in the Assembly.”

That was the headline carried by all the newspapers in Tamil Nadu on 9-9-2011. As usual, Chief Minister Jayalalithaa herself read the announcement in the Assembly under Rule 110. And, as usual, once she finished reading, speeches of praise were delivered on behalf of all the parties in the House.

As far as we are concerned, we are happy that a satellite town is to come up at Thirumazhisai. Why? Because the land on which they now propose to establish the satellite town at Thirumazhisai was acquired by the Housing Board during the DMK Government!

In the statement she read, Amma said that Chennai was expanding very rapidly, that the prices of housing plots and flats in Chennai had risen enormously, and that, taking this situation into account, the Government proposed to establish a satellite town. Jayalalithaa announced in the Assembly on 9-9-2011 that the Thirumazhisai satellite town would be created over 311 acres, and members of all parties immediately stood up and praised it. Yet the scheme had already been announced in the policy explanatory note placed before the Assembly on 25-8-2011 by Housing Minister R. Vaithilingam. Poor man—who would take notice if he announced it? Only when “J” announces it does it acquire great prestige!

On page 8 of the policy-note book placed by Minister Vaithilingam at that time itself, it says:

> “Because of the growing population in Chennai Metropolitan City, there is a need to avoid buildings arising in an unregulated manner and to create a self-contained integrated new township with basic amenities such as planned roads, streetlights, storm-water drains, small bridges, sewerage disposal and drinking-water facilities. It is proposed to develop an integrated modern township on approximately 311 acres of land already acquired by the Board near Thirumazhisai in Tiruvallur district. The small extent of 12.87 acres required for an approach road will be acquired during this year.”

Thus, a scheme already announced in the policy explanatory note the previous month was presented by Chief Minister Jayalalithaa under Rule 110 on 9-9-2011 as though she were announcing it for the first time that day.

The idea of creating a satellite town adjoining Chennai is not new. It arose during the DMK Government too. In an Assembly announcement in 2006, it was stated:

> “South of the Vandalur–Kelambakkam Road, between the Old Mamallapuram Road and National Highway No. 45, there are 1 lakh 40 thousand acres of land. Of this, the Chennai Metropolitan Development Authority has tentatively selected 30 thousand acres. Thirteen thousand families live in this area. This 270-square-kilometre tract is in a pollution-free and good environment and is suitable for creating a new town. Most of the lands in this region are uncultivated and lie fallow. When land is acquired, water-resource areas, residential areas and forest areas will be excluded, and only fallow and dry lands will be acquired. It has been decided that landowners will be paid a fair market price when lands are acquired. The people living in this area will not be displaced. They may continue to live in their respective places. The employment opportunities created when the new town is established will provide good benefits to the people living in the area and enable them to improve economically.”

No sooner had this report been read in the Assembly under the DMK Government than, the very next day, Jayalalithaa hurriedly issued a statement. In it she said:

> “Because governing power has fallen into the hands of arrogant and petty creatures, anti-democratic and anti-people disorder has become a recurring event in tradition-rich Tamil Nadu. The announcement of a satellite town near Chennai by Karunanidhi, Chief Minister of the minority DMK Government, has become another example.
>
> “When a Chief Minister lacks farsightedness, such arbitrary acts become degrading deeds that make the heart tremble, and Karunanidhi and his party will have to pay the price for them.
>
> “Karunanidhi appears to be very fond of the word ‘companion’ (*thunai*); perhaps that is why he is looking for a ‘companion’ for Chennai city too. Reports keep coming that the Chennai satellite-town scheme is being designed and created only for the benefit of his family members, relatives and grandsons Dayanidhi Maran and Kalanithi Maran. Karunanidhi says that the satellite town is being created to reduce congestion among the people of Chennai. First let the wife and children in his household, his grandsons and granddaughters, the relatives of his late first wife and their descendants, the relatives of his present wife, and the relations through the companion he acquired leave Gopalapuram and C.I.T. Colony and reduce the population congestion in those areas.”

That is what Jayalalithaa said in that statement. Yet now she herself has announced that a satellite town is to be created at Thirumazhisai.

There is an even more amusing part. Even before Jayalalithaa issued her opposing statement, Dr. Ramadoss of the PMK, which was then allied with us, had expressed in a civil manner the concern that the satellite town would affect agricultural lands and cause difficulty to residents there. Within just two days, in my statement dated 2-9-2006, I said:

> “The scheme, announced in the DMK election manifesto, the Governor's Address and the Budget, for establishing a satellite town adjoining Chennai, which I had said would be created, has been abandoned on the basis of goodwill, after considering the advice given by an allied party, and with the precautionary concern that innocent members of the public should not become victims of the open frenzy of violence planned by certain selfish evil forces.”

About Jayalalithaa's statement, the PMK's official newspaper *Tamil Osai* carried an article on 3-9-2006 under the headline, “Jayalalithaa's Statement—the Peak of Indecency.”

And *Theekkathir*, the newspaper of the Marxist Communist Party, which is now allied with the AIADMK, wrote in its editorial of 4-9-2006:

> “Chief Minister Karunanidhi has announced that the scheme to establish a satellite town near Chennai is being cancelled. The Chief Minister has said that this decision was taken in goodwill and respecting the sentiments of allied parties. But when the previous AIADMK Government tried to demolish Queen Mary's College and build the Secretariat there, the students protested. People of every section opposed it. Yet the Jayalalithaa Government arrogantly and obstinately refused to abandon that scheme. It is impossible not to compare that conduct with the present Chief Minister's announcement.
>
> “Jayalalithaa's statement opposing the satellite-town scheme causes pain because it represents the height of political indecency. Opposition parties have every right to oppose a Government scheme, but political civility must be maintained. In her statement Jayalalithaa has used repulsive and indecent words about Chief Minister Karunanidhi's family. The statement reveals only her spite and perversity. Perhaps this is why, when the Chief Minister (Kalaignar) read this statement out in the Assembly, even AIADMK members seemed unable to tolerate it. The Legislative Assembly and the political arena are places for civilised debate, not places to display rage and slander. Their words and actions prove that Jayalalithaa and her party have still not learned from the verdict of the people and reformed themselves.”

At this moment one cannot help recalling what they wrote then and the fact that the same Marxists now find themselves having to conduct themselves in a spirit of alliance.

Because I have mentioned all this, Jayalalithaa may immediately say: “I opposed only a satellite town near Mamallapuram; now I have announced one at Thirumazhisai.” But during the DMK Government itself, after we assumed office in 2006, we began efforts to create a satellite town around Thirumazhisai by acquiring 33.66 acres in Vellavedu village, 68.31 acres in Narasingapuram village, 141.68 acres in Pallavarajapuram village, 112.20 acres in Kuthambakkam village and 97.63 acres in Chembarambakkam village.

It was proposed that 1,695 acres be acquired for the project. When 467 acres had been acquired, the people and farmers of the area expressed their opposition. Accepting their demands, a press release issued on behalf of the Tamil Nadu Government on 21-12-2006 stated that wet and dry agricultural lands would not be acquired for the satellite-town scheme in areas including Kuthambakkam, Vellavedu and Parvatharajapuram in Tiruvallur district.

Had Jayalalithaa not opposed the DMK's satellite-town scheme when it was announced in 2006, the satellite town would have come up by now and Chennai's congestion would have been reduced. Yet although opposition was raised when the DMK Government announced a satellite town then, the moment Amma Jayalalithaa announced the Thirumazhisai satellite town in the Assembly under Rule 110 now, members belonging to every party—including the Marxist Communist Party and the PMK—welcomed it.

Is this what they mean by the saying, “If the mother-in-law breaks it, it was only an earthen pot; if the daughter-in-law breaks it, it was a golden pot”?

**With affection, M.K.**  
13-9-2011

## 3587. மாமியார் உடைத்ததும்; மருமகள் உடைத்ததும்!

உடன்பிறப்பே,

“திருமழிசையில் 311 ஏக்கரில் குடியிருப்புகள் - சென்னை அருகே துணை நகர் - சட்ட சபையில் ஜெயலலிதா அறிவிப்பு”

இதுதான் தமிழகத்தின் அனைத்து ஏடுகளிலும் 9-9-2011 அன்று தலைப்புச் செய்தி. இந்த அறிவிப்பினை முதலமைச்சர் ஜெயலலிதாதான் பேரவையில் வழக்கம்போல 110வது விதியின் கீழ் படித்திருக்கிறார். அவர் படித்து முடித்தவுடன் வழக்கம்போல அவையிலே உள்ள அனைத்துக் கட்சிகளின் சார்பில் பாராட்டுரைகள் வழங்கப்பட்டுள்ளன. நம்மைப் பொறுத்த வரையில் திருமழிசையில் துணை நகரம் வரப் போவதில் நமக்கு மகிழ்ச்சிதான். ஏனென்றால் திருமழிசையில் இவர்கள் துணை நகரம் அமைக்கவுள்ள இடம் தி.மு.கழக ஆட்சிக் காலத்தில் வீட்டு வசதி வாரியத்தினால் கையகப்படுத்தப்பட்ட இடம்!

அம்மையார் அவர்கள் படித்த அறிக்கையில் “சென்னை மாநகரம் வெகு வேகமாக விரிவடைந்து வருகிறது, சென்னையில் வீட்டு மனை மற்றும் அடுக்கு மாடிக் குடியிருப்புகளின் விலை பெருமளவில் உயர்ந்திருக்கிறது, இந்த நிலையை கருத்திலே கொண்டு துணை நகரம் அமைக்கப் போவதாக” அவர் தெரிவித்திருக்கிறார். திருமழிசை துணை நகரத்தை 311 ஏக்கரில் உருவாக்கப் போவதாக ஜெயலலிதா பேரவையில் 9-9-2011 அன்று அறிவித்து அனைத்துக் கட்சியினரும் உடனடியாக எழுந்து அதைப் பாராட்டியிருக்கிறார்கள் என்ற போதிலும், அதற்கு முன்பே 25-8-2011 அன்று பேரவையில் வீட்டு வசதித் துறை அமைச்சர் ஆர். வைத்திலிங்கம் வைத்த கொள்கை விளக்கக் குறிப்பிலேயே இத்திட்டம் அறிவிக்கப்பட்டுள்ளது. பாவம்; அவர் அறிவித்தால் அதை யார் பொருட்படுத்துவார்கள்? “ஜெ” அறிவித்தவுடன்தான் அதற்கு பெரிய மவுசே ஏற்படுகிறது!

9-9-2011 அன்று முதலமைச்சர் ஜெயலலிதா ஏதோ புதிதாக அன்றுதான் அத்திட்டத்தை அறிவிப்பதைப்போல பேரவையில் 110வது விதியின் கீழ் அறிவித்திருக்கிறார்.

நான் இதையெல்லாம் குறிப்பிட்டதால், ஜெயலலிதா உடனே நான் மாமல்லபுரம் அருகே துணை நகரம் அமைப்பைத்தான் எதிர்த்தேன், தற்போது திருமழிசையில்தானே அறிவித்திருக்கிறேன் என்று சொல்லக்கூடும்! ஆனால் தி.மு. கழக ஆட்சிக் காலத்திலேயே திருமழிசையைச் சுற்றியுள்ள வெள்ளவேடு கிராமத்தில் 33.66 ஏக்கரும், நரசிங்கபுரம் கிராமத்தில் 68.31 ஏக்கரும், பல்லவ ராஜபுரம் கிராமத்தில் 141.68 ஏக்கரும், குத்தம் பாக்கம் கிராமத்தில் 112.20 ஏக்கரும், செம்பரம்பாக்கம் கிராமத்தில் 97.63 ஏக்கரும் கையகப்படுத்தி அங்கே ஒரு துணை நகரம் அமைப்பதற்கான முயற்சிகளில் 2006ஆம் ஆண்டு பொறுப்புக்கு வந்ததும் தொடங்கினோம். 1695 ஏக்கர் நிலம் அதற்காக கையகப்படுத்த உத்தேசிக்கப்பட்டு, 467 ஏக்கர் கையகப்படுத்தப்பட்ட நிலையிலே அந்தப் பகுதி மக்களும், விவசாயிகளும் தங்கள் எதிர்ப்பினைத் தெரிவித்தார்கள். எனவே அவர்களின் கோரிக்கைகளை ஏற்று, 21-12-2006 அன்று தமிழக அரசின் சார்பில் வெளியிடப்பட்ட செய்திக் குறிப்பில், திருவள்ளூர் மாவட்டம் - குத்தம்பாக்கம், வெள்ளவேடு, பர்வதராஜபுரம் உள்ளிட்ட பகுதிகளில் துணை நகரம் அமைக்கும் திட்டத்திற்காக நஞ்செய், புஞ்செய் நிலங்களைக் கையகப்படுத்துவதில்லை என்று தெரிவிக்கப்பட்டது.

இவ்வாறு 2006ஆம் ஆண்டு தி.மு. கழகம்; துணை நகரத் திட்டத்தை அறிவித்தபோது இந்த ஜெயலலிதா அதனை எதிர்க்காமல் இருந்திருந்தால் இந்நேரம் துணை நகரமும் வந்திருக்கும், சென்னையின் இட நெரிசலும் குறைந்திருக்கும். ஆனால் அன்றைக்கு தி.மு.கழக அரசு துணை நகரத்தை அறிவித்தபோது எதிர்ப்பு கூறப்பட்டாலும், தற்போது ஜெயலலிதா அம்மையார் பேரவையில் 110வது விதியில் திருமழிசை துணை நகரத்தை அறிவித்தவுடன் மார்க்சிஸ்ட் கம்யூனிஸ்ட் கட்சி
