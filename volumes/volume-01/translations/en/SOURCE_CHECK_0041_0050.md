## Letter-level notes

### 0041 — `நான் கேட்கும் பிச்சை!`

Preserves the birthday appeal's deliberate `பிச்சை` paradox, the Anna Trust, assistance to artists, the Beggars' Rehabilitation Fund, fifty-paise cards, lottery-ticket appeal, ten-rupee garland condition, and printed date **26-05-1971**.

### 0042 — `ஜூலை 15!`

Preserves the anti-violence argument through the Anna-statue and *Murasoli* incidents, the 1965 anti-Hindi reference, farmers' agitation examples, the `வெற்றிச் சிந்து / வெறிச் சிந்து` wordplay, and the call for the statewide **15 July Day of Condemnation of Violence**.

### 0043 — `பெருமை எது?`

Preserves the continuation of the anti-violence theme, Anna's `கத்தியைத் தீட்டாதே! புத்தியைத் தீட்டு!` injunction, the distinction between answering violence with violence and defeating it through `நன்முறை`, the cadre-centred direct address, and the printed date **17-07-1972**.

### 0044 — `வழக்கு ஜோடனை!`

Preserves the Gospel epigraph, the complete accusation list from the Indira Congress resolution, references to the farmers' agitation and Union taxation demands, the Puducherry/Krishnagiri political contrast, the Fernandes passage, and the closing `கொக்கென்று நினைத்தாயோ - கொங்கணவா?` image.

### 0045 — `மதுரை மாட்சி (1)`

Preserves the first Madurai-conference instalment's procession chronology, distinction between Madurai Muthu and Kalaignar's son Muthu, thousand-plus bullock carts, Chera/Pandya/Chola historical imagery, state-autonomy slogans, the state-autonomy palace and four-armed Anna-statue imagery, and source sign-off `மு. க.`.

### 0046 — `மதுரை மாட்சி (2)`

Preserves the Race Course refusal under the earlier Congress government, later permission for Indira Congress and Communist conferences, organiser names, the hundred-flag campaign and violence surrounding it, the “grandfather and two grandsons” quotation, Ganesan/Maran passages, and source sign-off `மு. க.`.

### 0047 — `“தாயே எட்டடி என்றால்...”`

Preserves the `நாடு + அகம் = நாடகம்` wordplay, the *Silappathikaram* reference, Kamarajar criticism together with Kalaignar's explicit wish for his health and longevity, the Silver Jubilee passage, and the closing `தாய் ஒரு அடி / குட்டி எட்டடி` proverb reversal.

### 0048 — `பாவம்; பண்பாடு, படும் பாடு!`

Preserves the account of Navalar Nedunchezhiyan, MGR and the 1972 Executive Committee episode, including **25 of 33** members seeking action and the Chepauk Guest House announcement. The printed `சென்னை. / 10.10.1972` is retained exactly as source evidence. Because the body also states that the AIADMK has existed for eight years, no unsupported later composition date has been inferred or silently substituted.

### 0049 — `அன்பகம்! அறிவகம்!`

Preserves the 1961 Sampath–Kannadasan controversy, the accusations concerning accounts and dictatorship, Thangamuthu's request, Anna's full quoted reply with its musical, procession and vehicle analogies, and the closing `நெஞ்சகம் / அன்பகம் / அறிவகம்` wordplay.

### 0050 — `நமக்குப் புரியவில்லை!`

Preserves the *Navasakthi* September/October chronology, Madurai conference collection figures of **₹5.10 lakh / ₹3 lakh / ₹2.10 lakh**, the 6 August crowd exchange about bribery and corruption, the contrary 8 October allegation sixty days later, and the elephant-and-concealed-pit image.
