## English corrections made during pilot review

### Letter 3484

1. **TANSI judgment attribution restored.** The draft had detached the statement about Jayalalithaa denying the signature in the land document from the Supreme Court judges' reported observations. The corrected English keeps the source attribution and sequence together: the judges are reported as observing that the land should be returned, that she had gone so far as to deny the signature in an attempt to escape the case, and that she should look to her conscience and seek atonement.
2. **Tender sentence de-overtranslated.** `தன்னிச்சையாக ஒருவருக்கு வழங்கப்பட்டுவிடவில்லை` is now rendered as **“It was not arbitrarily awarded to anyone.”** The unsupported drafting addition “of our choosing” was removed.

### Letter 3485

1. **`அறிக்கை என்ன கேடு?` rhetoric restored.** The sentence now keeps the source's sarcastic rhetorical force as **“When they have no such ‘masala,’ what harm is there in issuing a statement?”** rather than the weaker drafting paraphrase.
2. **Vehicle action kept source-generic.** `453 வாகனங்களின் உரிமத்தை ரத்து` is now **“cancel the licences of 453 of those vehicles”** rather than the more specific and unsupported “registration”.
3. **Written-sheet accusation restored.** `எழுதிக் கொடுத்தவன் ஏட்டைக் கொடுத்தான்` now explicitly retains that someone had **written it down and handed her the sheet**, instead of reducing the image to merely putting a paper in her hands.

### Letter 3486

No omission, mistranslation, overstatement, figure mismatch, quotation loss or metadata/source-boundary defect requiring correction was found.

## Volume 44 conventions locked

1. **Udanpirappē** is retained without flattening it to “brother”, “sister” or “comrade”.
2. Standard `அன்புள்ள, / மு.க.` closes as **With affection, M.K.**; the printed date remains visible in the bilingual record.
3. **lakh** and **crore** are retained as Indian public-language units; source anomalies in figures are not silently repaired.
4. Ordinary political references use **DMK** and **AIADMK**; a party's full English name may be used where the Tamil itself gives the full name or where the immediate sentence requires it.
5. `மத்திய அரசு` is rendered **Union Government** and `மாநில அரசு` **State government** in institutional/legal passages unless the source context requires another natural construction.
6. In reservation/legal contexts, **Backward Classes**, **Most Backward Classes**, **Scheduled Castes** and **Scheduled Tribes** are used for the corresponding source categories; the translation does not import an outside classification to repair the source.
7. Public/political terms and names such as **Samathuvapuram**, *Kudiyarasu*, *Vairam* and **bandh** may remain transliterated when translation would erase source identity or rhetoric.
8. Printed source English is preserved where it functions as source wording, including examples in this pilot such as the Supreme Court sentence, `(Creamy Layer)`, `(Street House)` and `(SLPs)`.
9. Quotations, accusations, sarcasm, rhetorical questions, repetition and political imagery are translated for meaning and force rather than softened into neutral academic prose.
10. Dense figures, lists, dates, statutory references and named schemes remain in source order; they are not summarized or reconciled against outside material.
11. Source anomalies are preserved or explicitly noted. The audited canonical Tamil remains the immediate translation source, and the controlling scan remains the ultimate Tamil authority.
12. Every bilingual record includes the complete audited Tamil under **Original Tamil — மூலத் தமிழ்**, with physical source-page markers retained.
13. `(தொடரும்)` is rendered **(To be continued)** and `(தொடர்ச்சி நாளை)` **(To be continued tomorrow)** when those exact continuation markers occur.
