# 3453. There Is a ‘Leader’ — Do Not Be Deluded!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This long letter first surveys resolutions, laws and notable events associated with the Legislative Assembly chamber at Fort St George and then gives the source author’s detailed rebuttal to statements attributed to Opposition Leader Jayalalithaa during the Assembly sitting of 11 January 2010. Accusations, political characterisations, historical claims and financial comparisons are preserved as source-attributed material, not independently verified conclusions. Source-supplied English passages are retained as printed.

**Tamil source:** [Letter 3453](../../../chapters/3453-oru-thalaivan-irukkiraan-mayangaathe.md)  
**Source pages:** [PDF 212](../../../pages/page-212.md)–[PDF 234](../../../pages/page-234.md)  
**Date:** 13 January 2010

**Udanpirappē,**

This letter includes resolutions and laws, like copper-plate records, from the history of the Legislative Assembly at Fort St George.

Further, since this letter proves with evidence what a “truthful!” Opposition Leader our historically renowned Legislative Assembly has obtained, is that too not a copper-plate record in the history of the Assembly?

Therefore read this letter in full!

**Udanpirappē,**

11.1.2010 was the last day on which the Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly would conduct its work in the hall in which it was then meeting. It was also the day on which the Chief Minister was to reply to the Governor’s Address. But at midnight on the 10th, I received information that an attempt was being made to create disorder in the Assembly. I immediately asked Ministers Duraimurugan and Ponmudy to come to my house and spoke with them until two in the morning. We decided that no matter how much slander was spoken against the DMK government or against me, DMK members must show patience and must not give room for disorder; that no untoward incident of any kind should occur on the last day; that Deputy Chief Minister M. K. Stalin could reply to the Governor’s Address; and that afterwards I could speak for a short time since it was the Assembly’s final day. Only after two o’clock did we go to sleep.

Exactly as the information that came to me at midnight had indicated, the Opposition Leader brought her speech to the Assembly prepared like a book and spoke — no, ranted. I shall later give detailed replies to every accusation she levelled against the DMK government. First, I wish to continue in this letter the thoughts about that Assembly which I had intended to speak in the House but stopped halfway, and which I had wanted to place on record there. You will already have read what I spoke in the Assembly!

I began in the Assembly with the history of Fort St George. I continue here the details of the resolutions passed and decisions taken in the Legislative Assembly chamber situated there.

On 29.7.1953 there was the resolution concerning implementation of the hereditary education scheme, and an amendment that the scheme should not be implemented. In the vote, 138 votes were cast for the amendment and 138 against it; because the Speaker voted against the amendment, it was defeated. Even so, on 18.5.1954 the government decided to abandon the hereditary education scheme.

After Rajaji resigned as Chief Minister, Perunthalaivar Kamaraj became Chief Minister on 13.4.1954. Later, the ministry headed by Kamaraj resigned and a ministry headed by Bhaktavatsalam was formed and continued its work in the present Legislative Assembly chamber.

Madurai University was established in 1965 and renamed Madurai Kamaraj University in 1978.

On 6.3.1967, a nine-member DMK ministry headed by Anna took office. Adithanar became Speaker and Pulavar Govindan Deputy Speaker.

From 24.11.1967, the practice began of the Speaker opening proceedings by reciting a Tirukkural.

Perarignar Anna passed the historic separate resolution naming Madras State “Tamil Nadu” on 18.7.1967.

A law was enacted validating Self-Respect and reform marriages.

Tamil was made the official language; the three-language formula was rejected and the two-language formula implemented in Tamil Nadu on 23.1.1968.

After I took office as Chief Minister in 1969, the Land Reform (Reduction of Ceiling) Act was enacted.

A law was made enabling fair wages to be fixed for agricultural labourers for various kinds of work.

May Day was made a government holiday in order to protect labour welfare.

On 31.3.1969, marking the completion of eighty years by G. Gomathi Sankara Dikshithar, a long-serving Congress member, I honoured him with a shawl.

Following my visit to Punjab at the invitation of that State’s Chief Minister Gurnam Singh, and my visit to the university there, a law was passed in 1971 to establish an Agricultural University in Tamil Nadu.

The Slum Clearance Board Act was passed under the DMK government in 1971. The law granting rights to occupants was passed in 1971.

On 8.7.1971 a resolution was adopted seeking establishment of a tribunal for the Cauvery issue.

On 17.12.1971 a resolution was adopted congratulating the newly created country of Bangladesh.

To mark the silver jubilee of Indian Independence, a special sitting of the Assembly was held in the chamber at midnight on 14.8.1972 under Governor K. K. Shah. K. A. Mathiazhagan was then Speaker and P. Srinivasan Deputy Speaker.

On 15.4.1974 a unanimous resolution urged the Union Government to make it possible for people of all sections to become temple priests.

On 16.4.1974 I moved the resolution for State autonomy and the Assembly adopted it.

On 21.8.1974 there was a resolution concerning Katchatheevu.

A law was passed enabling the government to take over buses and contract carriages.

On 30.6.1977 an AIADMK ministry headed by M.G.R. was formed.

The Anna University of Technology Act was passed in 1978.

Resolutions concerning the Sri Lankan issue were adopted on 21.8.1981 and 25.10.1983.

The Mother Teresa Women’s University Act was passed in 1984.

After regaining his health and returning to the country, M.G.R. formed a ministry on 14.2.1985.

A law was enacted to establish a separate university for the Tamil language at Tanjavur.

The Alagappa University Act was passed in 1985.

Under the AIADMK government, on 14.5.1986 a resolution was adopted to abolish the Tamil Nadu Legislative Council with effect from 1.11.1986. Silambuch Selvar M. P. Sivagnanam was then Chairman of the Council. I too was a member of the Council.

It was in this very Assembly that ten DMK legislators, including Professor K. Anbazhagan, were stripped of their membership on the ground that they had engaged in unconstitutional acts by burning a provision of the Constitution, following Anna’s path in the language agitation. (22.12.1986)

The Tamil Nadu Dr M.G.R. Medical University Act was passed in 1987.

On 9.8.1987 a special sitting was held to celebrate the completion of forty years of Indian Independence.

Following M.G.R.’s death on 24.12.1987, his wife Mrs Janaki Ammal became Chief Minister — for only a few months.

On 27.1.1989, a government was again formed under my leadership.

On 5.5.1989 the Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly celebrated its golden jubilee. At that function I presented commemorative gifts to former Minister and former Kerala Governor Mrs Jothi Venkatachalam and freedom fighter L. Krishnaswami Bharathi. In particular, golden-jubilee awards were presented to former Legislative Council Chairmen Manickavelu and Silambuch Selvar M. P. Sivagnanam and to former Legislative Assembly Speakers such as Pulavar Govindan.

On 12.5.1989 a resolution was adopted asking the Union Government to implement the Mandal Commission recommendations.

On 24.4.1990 a resolution was adopted asking the Union Government to inform the Supreme Court of the position that the Cauvery issue should be referred for adjudication by a tribunal.

On 21.8.1990 a resolution of thanks was adopted for implementation of the Mandal Commission recommendations.

A law was enacted giving women rights in property.

In 1991 an AIADMK ministry was again formed and Jayalalithaa became Chief Minister.

It was in this same Assembly that Murasoli editor Selvam was placed in a cage in the middle of the House over a privilege issue. (21.9.1992)

A Bill providing that the Chief Minister should be Chancellor of the universities did not receive the Governor’s assent before the Assembly’s term ended.

On 5.6.1993 an All-India Conference of Legislative Speakers and Secretaries was held in this very House.

On 20.4.1994 a resolution was passed asking the Government of India to take steps to declare the Liberation Tigers of Tamil Eelam in Tamil Nadu an unlawful organisation.

On 26.4.1995 a resolution was adopted that the views of State Chief Ministers should be sought before appointment of a State Governor.

On 13.5.1996 a ministry under my leadership once again took office.

A separate ministry for Tamil development was created and a Minister appointed exclusively for it. He was Tamilkudimagan.

The Bill for the Ambedkar Law University was passed.

On 28.4.1997 a law was enacted to establish a university in the name of Thanthai Periyar.

On 14.7.1997 the platinum jubilee of the Legislature and diamond jubilee of the Legislative Assembly were celebrated.

The constituency development fund for MLAs, introduced for the first time in 1997, allocated ₹25 lakh to each constituency.

In the 2001 general election the AIADMK won and Jayalalithaa assumed office as Chief Minister. Because of a court judgment she had to leave office, and for a few months O. Panneerselvam served as Chief Minister in the interim.

In May 2006 the DMK government was formed and I assumed office as Chief Minister for the fifth time.

A law was passed making it compulsory for all students in schools to study Tamil from Standard 1 through Standard 10.

A Bill was passed repealing the anti-conversion law.

A law was passed declaring the first day of the month of Thai as Tamil New Year’s Day.

A Bill was passed granting a separate three per cent reservation in education and employment to Arunthathiyars.

A law granted 3.5 per cent reservation to the Muslim minority.

It was in this House that action was taken to enable persons of all castes to become temple priests.

A separate law was enacted for Kamaraj’s birthday to be celebrated throughout Tamil Nadu as Education Development Day.

It was in this Legislature that the Periyar Memorial Samathuvapuram scheme was enabled to take shape.

On 11.5.2007, a special sitting marking the golden jubilee of my Legislative Assembly service was held in this House. Governor Surjit Singh Barnala participated. At the second day’s special programme on 12.5.2007, Lok Sabha Speaker Somnath Chatterjee attended as chief guest, presided over the function and offered felicitations.

I wish to recall a few debates that took place in the Legislative Assembly chamber.

**Rama. Arangannal:** The family of Vanchinathan Iyer of the Tirunelveli region, one of the old-time martyrs, had applied for a freedom-fighters’ grant. The previous government did not attend to it. What is its present position?

**Honourable Anna:** In Vanchinathan Iyer’s case, his widow submitted a petition. Although Vanchinathan Iyer became very well known through his participation in the freedom struggle, the previous government had some hesitation in accepting his final act as an act of martyrdom. The earlier decision had been to give a pension to those who lost their lives, became disabled, or fell into hardship after sacrifice. Since Vanchinathan Iyer shot and killed Collector Ashe and then took his own life, there was hesitation over whether to grant it because he was not regarded as having lost his life in the freedom struggle but as having ended his own life. When I happened to examine the matter last week, I ordered that even though he took his own life, because his underlying purpose was the freedom struggle, a pension should be given to his widow.

Udanpirappē, it is standing in the path of that broad-mindedness of Anna that I too have been running the government and safeguarding the party. Leaving aside what took place in the old Assembly chamber, I come now to the incident that occurred recently. From the very beginning of Opposition Leader Jayalalithaa’s speech, it was possible to understand with what intention she had come. For example, she said no one should interrupt her speech and that if anyone did, it would mean they lacked confidence in the Chief Minister’s ability to reply. Minister Duraimurugan immediately rose and asked: when the AIADMK was in office, Ministers interrupted opposition members while they spoke; did those Ministers interrupt because they thought you lacked the ability to answer? The reason he said so was precisely because the Opposition Leader had begun by saying that if anyone interrupted her it would mean the Chief Minister lacked the ability to reply. Had she instead begun simply by asking everyone to cooperate by not unnecessarily interrupting her speech, would there have been any need for Duraimurugan to interrupt?

Next, the Opposition Leader said that the medical-insurance scheme introduced by the DMK government had been given the Chief Minister’s name, whereas during her administration schemes had not been named after her. Is this a true statement? If false information is given in the Assembly, even a privilege issue may be raised. Jayalalithaa said that during her administration her name had not been given to schemes. Here are a few examples showing how greatly that statement differs from the truth, according to the source author:

An announcement that a new transport corporation for buses travelling from Tamil Nadu to other States would be started under the name “Puratchi Thalaivi Dr J. Jayalalithaa Transport Corporation.” (6.4.1993)

Udanpirappē, I do not come forward to say that it was wrong for her name to be given to many schemes during her government. The Opposition Leader next spoke about the financial condition of the Tamil Nadu Government, the State’s debt burden, and alleged that the debt burden had grown greatly only under the DMK government. The source gives the following figures. At the end of the 1996–2001 DMK government, on 31.3.2001, Tamil Nadu Government debt liabilities stood at ₹28,685 crore. At the end of the AIADMK government’s 2001–2006 tenure, on 31.3.2006, State government debt liabilities had risen to ₹57,457 crore. In short, the author argues that at the end of five years of AIADMK rule, Tamil Nadu’s debt liabilities had doubled compared with what the DMK government had left behind.

As far as the present government was concerned, the source says it was administering finances by borrowing, with Union Government permission, only to the extent recommended by the Twelfth Finance Commission and the Union Government to meet the fiscal deficit. Under those recommendations, the State government’s fiscal deficit should not exceed three per cent of Gross State Domestic Product. The Tamil Nadu Government followed that limit in the financial years 2006–2007, 2007–2008 and 2008–2009. The ₹57,457 crore debt liabilities at the end of the AIADMK government on 31.3.2006 rose to ₹60,170 crore at the end of 2006–2007, ₹64,655 crore at the end of 2007–2008, and ₹74,858 crore at the end of 2008–2009 on 31.3.2009. Funds raised through government borrowing, the source says, were used to implement welfare schemes and infrastructure facilities.

To rescue the economy from recession, the Union Government in 2009–2010 permitted State governments to raise their fiscal deficit to four per cent of Gross State Domestic Product by increasing State expenditure, thereby increasing people’s purchasing power and giving economic growth a stimulus. The Tamil Nadu Government was administering its 2009–2010 finances under that guidance. Therefore, the author says, the Opposition Leader’s statement that information was coming in that the debt burden was becoming extremely high was not correct information — “conspiratorial information!”

The Opposition Leader then said she was going to ask three important questions and that the Chief Minister must answer them. Having asked the three questions, the source says, she did not wish to wait in the House for the replies she had demanded, but hurried away as soon as she finished speaking. Even so, the author gives his answers.

**First question:** What is Tamil Nadu’s present debt burden in crores of rupees?

**Answer:** As already stated above, on 31.3.2009 the State Government’s total debt liabilities were ₹74,858 crore.

**Second question:** From where will you obtain the money to build 21 lakh houses free of cost for the poor and simple people?

**Answer:** Since this housing scheme is plan expenditure, the cost will be met from the resources for the State Government’s Annual Plan. ₹12,600 crore will be required to build 21 lakh houses. Since the 21 lakh houses will be built over six years, an average of ₹2,100 crore will be needed each year. In the coming 2010–2011 financial year, three lakh houses are to be built, and at an estimate of ₹60,000 per house, ₹1,800 crore will be allocated.

**Third question:** Why has there been this fall in the rate of Gross State economic growth?

**Answer:** In 2006–2007, the first financial year after the DMK government assumed office, the overall growth rate of the Tamil Nadu economy was 11.29 per cent. For 2008–2009, Tamil Nadu’s overall economic growth was estimated by the Central Statistical Organisation at 4.55 per cent. Among thirty-two States and Union Territories, estimates were then available for only eighteen States and Union Territories. Developed States such as Tamil Nadu, Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh had annual growth rates only in the range of 4.5 to 5.5 per cent. Andhra Pradesh’s growth rate in 2008–2009 was 5.53 per cent; Karnataka’s 5.08 per cent; Tamil Nadu’s 4.55 per cent. Because of the global economic downturn and the resulting recession in India’s industrial sector, particularly exports, leading industrial States such as Tamil Nadu, Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh attained only low growth rates. Statistical figures for the growth rates of other leading industrial States such as Gujarat and Maharashtra had not yet been received. The principal reason for Tamil Nadu’s growth rate falling to 4.55 per cent in 2008–2009, the source argues, was the impact of the global economic downturn.

The Opposition Leader next asked a major question about the 2006 promise that 55 lakh acres of wasteland would be given to poor landless agricultural families, saying that only 2,11,366 acres had been distributed. The author says he replied: “Fifty-six lakh acres was the wording stated in the AIADMK budget. Believing it to be true, we too published the figure of fifty-six lakh acres.” Jayalalithaa immediately replied: “In the budget issued during our government, we announced that 55 lakh acres of wasteland would be developed; we did not say that all those 55 lakh acres belonged to the government. If we said so, let him show it.” The author says he replied: “Trusting your statement in the budget that 55 lakh acres would be developed, we announced that if it was so developed, it could be given to poor and simple people.”

The source then cites paragraph 18 of the 2001–2002 Budget read by Finance Minister Ponnaiyan on 18.8.2001:

> “Honourable Chief Minister Selvi J. Jayalalithaa has already announced a massive long-term scheme called the Wasteland Development Scheme to develop within the coming five years twenty lakh hectares (50 lakh acres) of wasteland suitable for cultivation.”

The author says it was on the basis of this announcement that the 2006–2007 DMK Budget referred to fifty lakh acres. He adds that this explanation was not being made only now: paragraph 14 of the 2006 Budget itself stated that the previous government’s 2001–2002 Budget had announced that Tamil Nadu had a total of fifty lakh acres of wasteland, and that the DMK election manifesto accordingly said these wastelands would be improved and given by the government to poor landless agricultural families. Jayalalithaa had challenged in the House, “Let him show it.” The author says he has now shown the words contained in the Budget itself, and asks: “What is Jayalalithaa going to do?”

Finally, Jayalalithaa spoke about building 21 lakh free houses over six years, calling it a scheme to deceive the people and saying the people could not be deceived; she asked where the ₹1,800 crore would come from. The author says that within a few weeks Jayalalithaa would learn from the forthcoming Budget whether funds were being allocated for the houses, and the people of the country would understand who was deceiving them.

நிறைவேறியது.

உடல் நலம் பெற்று நாடு திரும்பிய எம்.ஜி.ஆர். தலைமையில்
14-2-1985 அன்று அமைச்சரவை அமைந்தது.

தமிழக மேலவை 1-11-1986 முதல் ஒழிக்கப்படுவதற்கான
தீர்மானம் அ.தி.மு.க. ஆட்சியில் 14-5-1986 அன்று நிறைவேற்றப்
பட்டது. மேலவைத் தலைவராக அப்போது சிலம்புச் செல்வர்
ம.பொ.சி. இருந்தார். மேலவை உறுப்பினராக நானும் இருந்தேன்.

மொழிப் போரில் அண்ணா வழியில் அரசியல் சட்டப் பிரிவை
எரித்தமைக்காக பேராசிரியர் க. அன்பழகனார் உள்ளிட்ட கழகச்
சட்டமன்ற உறுப்பினர்கள் பத்து பேரை அரசியல் சட்டத்திற்கு
விரோதமான நடவடிக்கைகளிலே ஈடுபட்டார்கள் என்று கூறி
அவர்களின் பதவி பறிக்கப்பட்டதும் இந்தச் சட்ட மன்றத்திலேதான்.
(22.12.1986)

தமிழ்நாடு எம்.ஜி.ஆர். மருத்துவப் பல்கலைக் கழகச் சட்டம்

9-8-1987 அன்று இந்தியா விடுதலை அடைந்து
நாற்பதாண்டுகள் நிறைவுப் பெற்றதைக் கொண்டாடும் வகையில்
சிறப்புக் கூட்டம் நடைபெற்றது.

24-12-1987இல் எம்.ஜி.ஆர். மறைந்ததை யொட்டி அவரது
துணைவியார் திருமதி ஜானகி அம்மாள் முதலமைச்சராகப்
பொறுப்பேற்றார். (சில மாதங்கள் மட்டும்)

27-1-1989இல் மீண்டும் என் தலைமையில் அரசு
அமைந்தது.

5-5-1989இல் தமிழகச் சட்டமன்றப் பேரவை தனது பொன்
விழாவினைக் கொண்டாடியது. இந்த விழாவில் முன்னாள்
அமைச்சரும், முன்னாள் கேரள ஆளுநருமான திருமதி
ஜோதி வெங்கடாசலத்திற்கும், சுதந்திரப் போராட்ட வீரரான
எல். கிருஷ்ணசாமி பாரதிக்கும் நினைவுப் பரிசுகளை நான்
வழங்கினேன். குறிப்பாக - சட்ட மேலவை முன்னாள் தலைவர்கள்
மாணிக்கவேலு, சிலம்புச் செல்வர் ம.பொ.சி., முன்னாள் சட்டப்
பேரவைத் தலைவர்கள் புலவர் கோவிந்தன் போன்றவர்களுக்கு
பொன்விழா விருதுகள் வழங்கப்பட்டன.

24-4-1990 அன்று காவேரி பிரச்சினையை நடுவர் மன்றத்
தீர்ப்புக்கு விட வேண்டுமென்ற நிலையை மத்திய அரசு உச்ச
நீதிமன்றத்திற்கு எடுத்துரைக்க வேண்டுமென்று கோரி தீர்மானம்
நிறைவேற்றப்பட்டது.

21-8-1990 அன்று மண்டல் கமிஷன் பரிந்துரைகளை
அமல்படுத்தியதற்காக நன்றி தெரிவிக்கும் தீர்மானம்.

பெண்களுக்கு சொத்தில் உரிமை வழங்கிட சட்டம் கண்டது.

1991ஆம் ஆண்டு மீண்டும் அ.தி.மு.க. அமைச்சரவை
அமைந்து, ஜெயலலிதா அவர்கள் முதல் அமைச்சரானார்.

முரசொலி ஆசிரியர் செல்வத்தை உரிமை பிரச்சினை
காரணமாக பேரவை நடுவே கூண்டிலே ஏற்றியதும் இதே
சட்டமன்றத்திலே தான். (21-9-1992)

பல்கலைக் கழகங்களுக்கு முதல் அமைச்சரே வேந்தராக
இருப்பதற்கு வகை செய்யும் சட்ட முன் வடிவுக்கு ஆளுநரின்
ஒப்புதல் சட்டமன்றத்தின் காலம் முடியும் வரை கிடைக்கப்
பெறவில்லை.

5-6-1993இல் அனைத்திந்திய சட்டமன்றத் தலைவர்கள்
மற்றும் செயலாளர்கள் மாநாடு இதே அவையில் நடைபெற்றது.

20-4-1994 அன்று தமிழக விடுதலைப் புலிகள் இயக்கம்
ஒரு சட்ட விரோதமான அமைப்பு என அறிவிக்கப்படுவதற்கான
நடவடிக்கைகளை மேற்கொள்ளுமாறு இந்திய அரசைக் கேட்டுக்
கொள்ளும் தீர்மானம் நிறைவேற்றப்பட்டது.

26-4-1995 அன்று மாநில ஆளுநர் நியமிக்கப்படுவதற்கு
முன்பு மாநில முதல் அமைச்சர்களின் கருத்துக்களை கேட்க
வேண்டுமென்ற தீர்மானம் நிறைவேற்றப்பட்டது.

13-5-1996இல் மீண்டும் என் தலைமையிலான
அமைச்சரவை பதவியேற்றது.

தமிழ் வளர்ச்சிக்கென தனி அமைச்சகம் கண்டு அமைச்சர்
ஒருவர் தனியாக நியமிக்கப்பட்டார். அவர்தான் தமிழ்க்குடிமகன்.

அம்பேத்கர் சட்டப் பல்கலைக் கழகத்திற்கான சட்ட முன்
வடிவு நிறைவேற்றப்பட்டது.

28-4-1997 அன்று தந்தை பெரியார் பெயரில் பல்கலைக்
கழகம் அமைய சட்டம் இயற்றப்பட்டது.

14-7-1997 அன்று சட்டமன்றத்தின் பவள விழாவும்,
சட்டமன்றப் பேரவையின் வைர விழாவும் நடைபெற்றன.

1997இல் முதன் முறையாக அறிமுகப்படுத்தப்பட்ட
கிட்டப்பார்வை சட்டமன்ற உறுப்பினர் தொகுதி மேம்பாட்டு நிதி
ஒவ்வொரு தொகுதிக்கும் 25 இலட்சம் என ஒதுக்கப்பட்டது.

2001ஆம் ஆண்டு நடைபெற்ற பொதுத் தேர்தலில் அ.தி.மு.க.
வெற்றி பெற்று ஜெயலலிதா அவர்கள் முதல் அமைச்சராகப்
பொறுப்பேற்றார். நீதிமன்றத் தீர்ப்பு காரணமாக ஜெயலலிதா பதவி
விலக நேரிட, இடையில் ஒரு சில மாதங்கள் ஓ. பன்னீர்செல்வம்
முதலமைச்சராக பதவி வகித்தார்.

2006ஆம் மே திங்களில் தி.மு. கழக ஆட்சி அமைந்து
முதலமைச்சராக ஐந்தாவது முறையாக நான் பொறுப்பேற்றேன்.

தமிழ் மொழியை 1 முதல் 10ஆம் வகுப்பு வரையில் பள்ளிகளில்
அனைத்து மாணவர்களும் பயில்வதை கட்டாயமாக்கும் சட்டம்
நிறைவேற்றப்பட்டது.

மத மாற்றத் தடைச் சட்டத்தை நீக்குவதற்கான சட்ட முன்
வடிவு நிறைவேற்றப்பட்டது.

தைத் திங்கள் முதல் நாளை தமிழ்ப் புத்தாண்டு தினமாக
அறிவிக்கும் சட்டம் நிறைவேற்றப்பட்டது.

அருந்ததியர்களுக்கு கல்வியிலும் வேலை வாய்ப்பிலும் தனி
ஒதுக்கீடு செய்ய வகை செய்யும் சட்ட முன் வடிவு மூன்று சதவிகித
இட ஒதுக்கீடு வழங்கி நிறைவேற்றப்பட்டது.

சிறுபான்மையினரான முஸ்லீம்களுக்கு 3.5 சதவிகிதம் இட
ஒதுக்கீட்டுச் சட்டம்.

அனைத்துச் சாதியினரும் அர்ச்சகராக வழி செய்யும்
நடவடிக்கை மேற்கொள்ளப்பட்டதும் இந்த அவையிலேதான்.

காமராஜர் பிறந்த நாளை கல்வி வளர்ச்சி நாளாக தமிழகம்
எங்கும் கொண்டாட தனிச் சட்டம்.

பெரியார் நினைவு சமத்துவபுரத் திட்டம் நிறைவேற வழி
வகுத்ததும் இந்தச் சட்டமன்றத்தில்தான்.

11-5-2007 அன்று இந்தச் சட்டப் பேரவையில் என்னுடைய
சட்டப் பேரவை பணிகளுக்கான பொன் விழா சிறப்புக் கூட்டம்
நடைபெற்றது. மேதகு ஆளுநர் சுர்ஜித்சிங் பர்னாலா கலந்து
கொண்டார். 12-5-2007 அன்று நடைபெற்ற இரண்டாம்
நாள் சிறப்பு நிகழ்ச்சியில் சிறப்பு விருந்தினராக மக்களவைத்
தலைவர் மாண்புமிகு சோம்நாத் சட்டர்ஜி அவர்கள் விழாவிற்கு
தலைமையேற்றுப் பாராட்டினார்கள்.

சட்டமன்றப் பேரவை மண்டபத்தில் நடந்த விவாதங்களில்
ஒருசிலவற்றை நினைவூட்டிட விரும்புகிறேன்.

இராம. அரங்கண்ணல்:- பழம் பெரும் தியாகிகளில்
ஒருவரான நெல்லைச் சீமை வாஞ்சிநாத ஐயரின் குடும்பத்தினர்
தியாகிகள் மானியம் கோரி விண்ணப்பித்திருந்தார்கள். அதைக்
கடந்த அரசாங்கம் கவனிக்காமல் இருந்தது. அது இப்போது எந்த
நிலையில் உள்ளது?

மாண்புமிகு அண்ணா:- வாஞ்சிநாத ஐயர் அவர்களுடைய
பிரச்சினையில் அவருடைய விதவையார் அவர்கள் மனு செய்து
கொண்டார்கள். வாஞ்சிநாத அய்யர் அவர்கள் சுதந்திரப்
போராட்டத்தில் ஈடுபட்டு மிகவும் பெயர் பெற்றவர் என்றாலும்,
அவர் இறுதியாகச் செய்த காரியத்தை தியாகிகளின் காரியம்
என்று முன்னால் இருந்த அரசாங்கம் ஒப்புக் கொள்வதற்குக்
கொஞ்சம் தயக்கம் இருந்தது. ஏனெனில் உயிர் இழந்தவர்கள்
அல்லது அங்ககீனமானவர்கள் அல்லது தியாகத்திற்குப் பிறகு
நலிவுற்றவர்கள் ஆகியோருக்கு பென்ஷன் தருவது என்று

சொல்லப் போனால் திராவிட முன்னேற்றக் கழகத்தின் கட்சித்
தேர்தல் நடைபெற்றபோது - தலைவராக நான்தான் வர
வேண்டுமென்று எண்ணி அதற்காகப் பாடுபட்டவர், அதற்காக
ஆட்களைச் சேர்த்தவர். அந்த நன்றி எனக்குண்டு, சாகின்ற
வரையிலே உண்டு. அதைப் போல இந்தப் பொறுப்புக்கு நான்
வர வேண்டுமென்று விரும்பியவர்களில் அவரும் ஒருவர்.
ஆகவே நான் அவருக்கும் எனக்கும் உள்ள நாற்பதாண்டு கால
நட்பின் காரணமாக சொல்லுகிறேன். இங்கே அவர் இறுதியில்
ஒரு பாடலைச் சொன்னாலுங்கூட - அதனை நான் இப்படித்தான்
கருதிக் கொள்கிறேன்.

என்ன தான் நடக்கும், நடக்கட்டுமே!
இருட்டினில் நீதி மறையட்டுமே!
தன்னாலே வெளி வரும் தயங்காதே!
ஒரு “தலைவன்” இருக்கிறான் மயங்காதே!

அதாவது ஒரு தலைவன் இருக்கின்றான் மயங்காதே
என்றுதான் - என்னைக் குறிப்பிட்டுத் தான் சொல்கிறார் -
ஒரு தலைவி இருக்கிறார் மயங்காதே என்று சொல்லவில்லை
(மேஜையைத் தட்டும் ஒலி). இதை இந்த அவையிலே உள்ள
உறுப்பினர்கள் எம்.ஜி.ஆருடைய அந்த நம்பிக்கையான
வார்த்தையை ஏற்றுக் கொண்டு, இந்த அவையை சிறப்பாக நடத்த
வேண்டுமென்று கேட்டுக் கொள்கிறேன்.”
