**Date:** 27 June 2012

**Udanpirappē,**

In the resolution adopted at the meeting of the Kazhagam Executive Committee on 22 June 2012, it was stated in detail that, beginning on the morning of 4 July, a jail-filling agitation would be conducted and what the reasons for conducting it were. It was also decided that, as a prelude to the agitation, public meetings explaining the *arappor* would be held in district headquarters, circles and major towns, and it was said that the headquarters would soon announce the places where those explanatory public meetings would be held and the details of those participating in them.

There are about 500 headquarters speakers in the Kazhagam. There are more than thirty district Kazhagam organisations. Apart from them, there are metropolitan districts. After the Executive Committee resolution, our propaganda secretaries, Thambis A. Raja and Tiruchi N. Siva, separately contacted and consulted the district secretaries as well as the Kazhagam President, General Secretary and Treasurer and announced the dates and speakers for the public meetings. As for the other speakers, they also published in *Murasoli* a list showing which speakers were assigned to each district. On seeing it, I myself contacted the propaganda secretaries and pointed out that the published list did not clearly and fully say which speakers were to be in which towns on which dates.

By then, some speakers had become angry that their names had not been mentioned—out of anxiety that they might be left without a place in the ranks of the *arappor*!

Yesterday evening (25 June 2012), the General Secretary and I sat at the headquarters, spoke with the propaganda secretaries, contacted the district secretaries and continued arranging for details of where the *arappor* explanatory public meetings would be held to be published. That list too has appeared in today's *Murasoli*.

Although there is such an upsurge and enthusiasm among the Kazhagam front-rankers and cadres about this agitation, two newspapers that lie waiting to advertise the ruling party have been unable to bear this upsurge and speed and have tried to soothe their irritation by publishing false reports. In particular, *Dinamalar* of 24 June 2012, as a report from “our correspondent,” carried the headline “Voice of Dissatisfaction in the DMK — Battle Flag Raised over Agitation Announcement,” and displayed its “royal loyalty” by claiming that questions were being asked such as whether cadres should go to prison not for the party’s welfare but for private gains. Seeing that, *Dinamani*, again citing information from “our correspondent,” happily displayed its “Amma devotion” under the headline “DMK District Secretaries in a Challenge — Cadres in Confusion,” saying that cadres did not welcome the agitation.

Though we understand the malice and heartburn of such people, after seeing these reports every Kazhagam comrade is even more determined to participate in this agitation, somehow make it a magnificent success, and certainly go to prison. Whenever they meet me, even those in the Kazhagam’s front ranks tell me eagerly that, more than my cadres, they themselves are ready to go to prison.

Yet some are upset: “They left me out of the speakers’ meetings! They did not call me to the field of agitation! They did not mention my name!” Though a handful who can be counted on one’s fingers have become angry in this way, it gives me joy when district secretaries and front-rankers keep contacting me and telling me of the overall upsurge among Kazhagam comrades for this agitation.

For two or three days continuously from the day after the Executive Committee resolution, open *Murasoli* and there are details from every district and area—district Kazhagam meetings, area Kazhagam meetings, emergency meetings of office-bearers! In today’s *Murasoli*, for instance, the announcement issued by Villupuram District DMK Secretary Thambi Ponmudi contains a long list of the eight blockade points in his district for 4 July, who will lead at each place and who will stand in the forefront.

Tiruvarur District Kazhagam Secretary Thambi Kalaivanan has divided the period from 10 in the morning to 4 in the evening into one contingent for each hour, planned under whose leadership each contingent will participate, and sent me the list.

Not only that: from some districts, heartening reports are arriving that Kazhagam members have forgotten the small differences that existed among them earlier and are working together with feeling in this field of agitation. One example. Yesterday, Kazhagam Member of Parliament Thambi Rishi sent me a letter.

When the Kazhagam Executive Committee met, that district’s secretary, Thambi Suba. Thangavelan, said that everyone in their district would take part in this agitation in unity. Referring to that when I spoke, I had said: “The very announcement that Kazhagam comrades in Ramanathapuram district will fight unitedly tells us that this agitation will succeed.” Writing about this, Thambi Rishi said: “When you expressed your distress about our district in the Executive Committee, I felt deeply pained. It is the duty of cadres like us to keep you happy. Hereafter, nothing will happen in our district that causes you distress. If anything has happened that made you grieve, I humbly ask you to forgive us.” I firmly believe that the agitation of 4 July will unite the factional quarrels that arose from local anger and resentment in a few districts. If water strikes water, will the water separate?

Seeing the cadres’ enthusiasm and upsurge, there is no doubt that this agitation will achieve a very great victory. Nor are we conducting this agitation without cause. We are not conducting it merely because we want to agitate. How many acts of lawlessness have the AIADMK rulers committed during the past year? How many false cases against Kazhagam members? How many have been arrested? Against how many have complaint after complaint been piled up and action taken through the police? Against how many has the Goondas Act been invoked? In even one case has it been said that imposing those Goondas Act detentions was correct? What kinds of condemnations have judges expressed against these false cases? Do these rulers give any answer to that?

Do the AIADMK rulers take revenge only upon the Opposition? Do the people of Tamil Nadu not know that they take action against everyone they dislike, everyone they think must be subdued and ruled? Take, for example, six cases against Natarajan, husband of Sasikala—their inseparable sister who even today is with her, in her very house. Ramalingam of Vilar complained that Natarajan’s side had grabbed 15,000 square feet of land belonging to him. Vincent Albert complained that Natarajan’s people had demolished and occupied a 4,200-square-foot house and shops standing in his wife Mary’s name. Rangarajan, a retired Block Development Officer from Anbu Nagar, Thanjavur, complained that his 25 cents of land at Vilar had been occupied. Varadarajan of Kottapattu, Tiruchi, complained of monetary fraud in the sale of land. There were also complaints that a house belonging to Sakunthala near Thanjavur Medical College had been demolished and occupied, and that, at Natarajan’s instigation, relatives had threatened the owner and registered documents for two plots totalling 4,800 square feet belonging to Amalapushpa Mary in Vilar village. Cases were registered against Natarajan and others on these complaints, and he was arrested because of those cases. What became of all those cases?

The very person who complained that a house had been demolished and occupied suddenly withdrew the complaint. How did he withdraw it? Why did he lodge the complaint in the first place? How much of the Court’s time was wasted? If it was a false complaint, how did the police act upon it believing it? Was it a false complaint or a true complaint? Should they not have inquired whether there was any prima facie evidence? If it was not known to be a genuine complaint, could an arrest be made? Police who do not come forward to act on so many genuine complaints—how did they hurry to act on a false complaint? And how did complaints suddenly arise immediately after Jayalalithaa took action against Sasikala and issued statements to the newspapers? Is it because Sasikala has returned that those who gave the complaints are withdrawing them, or are they being made to withdraw them? What is the price for that?

What did that Natarajan say in the interview he gave on 12 May 2012 after coming out of prison?

> “All the cases filed against me are false cases. Of the six cases filed against me, two complainants have now withdrawn. The land-grabbing law has been fraudulently used against me. I have filed three writ petitions in the Chennai High Court seeking action against officials including the Chief Secretary, Principal Secretary, Home Secretary, DGP, Tiruchi IG and DIG. It has been twenty years since I left J.’s house. She issued a statement saying I had been removed from the party. I was never even a member of the party. Can she prove that I was? Whatever they did, I remained quiet. I will not remain so any more. I will enter politics directly. I was the one who prepared the 2011 election manifesto. Ponnarj and Panneerselvam IAS and I were the ones who added the laptop and other freebies. The first copy of that manifesto is still with me. The statement that appeared in her name before Sasikala was taken back was not a statement given by her. My wife does not know how to prepare a statement. Even someone who has been Chief Minister three times cannot prepare a statement. An engineer like me has to ready it for her. Even then, she cannot speak without even a note. You arrested me for land grabbing—how many acres did you recover? Can you at least file a charge sheet? One can drive from Chennai to Thanjavur in five hours. After arresting me they dragged me around for twelve hours. DGG Amalraj stopped the vehicle in a forested place near Sengipatti on the Thanjavur side. Why? To kill me in an encounter? Tiruchi IG Alexander Mohan, sitting in a police station in Thanjavur, is said to have summoned all the inspectors and ordered them to go and put some case or other on me.”

Was not such an interview published in all the newspapers? What answer does this AIADMK Government give to it? Is he the only one? What became of the case against Divakaran? What became of the case against Ravanan? Is it a crime to ask this? Must *Dinamani* and *Dinamalar* rage at us?

The people have voted them in for five years; let them govern freely and go. But merely because authority is in their hands, may they do whatever they please? That is why the agitation on 4 July. Today is Wednesday. The jail-filling agitation is next Wednesday. Only one week remains. Prepare yourselves today itself to engage in vigorous efforts for it! Conduct the *arappor* explanatory meetings peacefully throughout Tamil Nadu! As I said on the 22nd amid the sea-like gathering at Purasawalkam, prove that **“The Tamil bows to none—and lowers none!”**

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**27 June 2012**

நேற்று (25-6-2012) மாலையில் நானும், பொதுச் செயலாளரும் தலைமைக் கழகத்திலே அமர்ந்து கொள்கைப் பரப்புச் செயலாளர்களோடு கலந்துபேசி மாவட்டக் கழகச் செயலாளர்களோடு தொடர்புகொண்டு அறப்போர் விளக்கப் பொதுக் கூட்டங்கள் எங்கெங்கே நடைபெறும் என்ற விவரங்களைத் தொடர்ந்து வெளியிடவும் செய்து, அந்தப் பட்டியலும் இன்று - “முரசொலி”யிலே வெளிவந்துள்ளது.
