## Review method

Each English translation was compared directly with the complete audited Tamil source reproduced in the bilingual letter and the canonical page sequence. The review checked title/date, salutation/closing, paragraph and argument sequence, substantive claims and attribution, quotations and intentional source-English, names and initials, dates, figures, lists, rhetorical force, institutional titles, and English wording that could broaden, narrow, soften or silently normalise the source.

The Tamil source had already passed the scan-based textual-fidelity audit. **No canonical Tamil page was modified in this alignment pass.**
