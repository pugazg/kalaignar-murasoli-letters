# 3407. Not Just a World Wonder; a Great World Wonder!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This polemical letter compares positions the author attributes to Jayalalithaa and the AIADMK on the Sethusamudram project and the Sri Lankan Tamil issue, and sets out relief and rehabilitation measures the author attributes to his government and the Union Government. Sarcasm, accusations, historical claims and quoted political positions are preserved strictly as source framing; no present-day judgment is added. The complete audited Tamil is reproduced below and remains authoritative.

**Source pages:** [PDF 284](../../../pages/page-284.md)–[PDF 288](../../../pages/page-288.md)  
**Date:** 11 June 2009

**Udanpirappē,**

If one wishes to see or hear the greatest of the world’s wonders, there is no need to travel far or take much trouble, the author says. One need only repeatedly read the political and social statements, vows and challenges issued by former Chief Minister Jayalalithaa; he says a different “world wonder” can be found in each.

He identifies three important election manifestos issued under her signature on behalf of the AIADMK.

In the manifesto released for the 2004 parliamentary election, he says, it declared with great force:

> “We shall certainly implement the Sethusamudram project!”

It then described benefits the project was said to bring Tamil Nadu:

- The Sethusamudram project is an excellent project.
- It is a distinguished project.
- It is a project that will make Tamil Nadu prosperous.
- It is a project the world will praise.
- Whatever obstacle arises, we shall oppose it and certainly implement the project.

The author stresses that the person who issued that manifesto was Jayalalithaa herself.

Next came the 2006 Assembly general election, for which she issued another manifesto. Again, he says, it emphatically declared that the Sethusamudram project would certainly be implemented.

Then came the 2009 parliamentary general election. Another manifesto was issued. This time, the author says, there was no discussion of the Sethusamudram project. More than that, he says she declared that if a government supported by the AIADMK alliance came to power at the Centre, the Sethusamudram project would be cancelled.

One manifesto saying they would work for the project.

Another saying that if a government supported by them came to power at the Centre, that same project would be cancelled.

The author says he does not know what the people of Tamil Nadu thought on reading the two positions. He sarcastically depicts her followers as marvelling at the “supreme wonder” of declaring that a ₹2,467-crore Sethusamudram project should be cancelled when, according to the source, more than sixty per cent of the work had already been completed.

He then turns to the Sri Lankan Tamil issue and argues that Jayalalithaa’s words and actions had again collided with one another.

According to the author, she had earlier remained silent over the issue and had said that Rajapaksa was waging war against the LTTE, not against Tamils, and that she could not accept the claim by political parties in Tamil Nadu that it was a war against Tamils.

He further attributes to her the statement:

> “When a war takes place, it is natural that innocent people are killed.”

The author then contrasts that with her election-period demand for Tamil Eelam and her campaign rhetoric, asking sarcastically where she had now gone and saying she was challenging him from what he calls the “Kodanadu cave.”

The letter quotes another statement attributed to her:

> “Karunanidhi, Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu, who beats his own drum by calling himself a leader of the Tamil people, has no concern at all for Tamils; Karunanidhi has no interest in finding out about the safety of Sri Lankan Tamils or the relief assistance that should be given to them.”

Responding to that allegation, the author lists relief measures.

He says he appealed for funds on behalf of the Tamil Nadu government for affected Sri Lankan Tamils and that the people of Tamil Nadu contributed more than ₹50 crore.

Of that amount, he says, clothing, food, medicines and other goods worth ₹25 crore were purchased and supplied through the Red Cross, and the Union Government informed the State that they had been distributed properly.

He says the remaining ₹25 crore would be sent through the Union Government for the rehabilitation of Sri Lankan Tamils.

He further says DMK ministers and Members of Parliament at the Centre met Mrs. Sonia Gandhi and Prime Minister Dr. Manmohan Singh on his behalf and requested expanded rehabilitation work and increased relief. He says he too continued sending requests through letters and telegrams, and that Sonia Gandhi and Manmohan Singh assured him they would consider and act on them.

The author attributes to these continuing appeals the Union Government’s decision to provide ₹500 crore in assistance for the rehabilitation of Sri Lankan Tamils, for which he says thanks had been expressed.

Against this, he criticises Jayalalithaa’s allegation that “Karunanidhi will not open his mouth about Sri Lankan Tamils.”

He recalls that she had moved an Assembly resolution calling for Prabhakaran to be arrested, brought to India and punished.

He also says she boycotted an all-party meeting convened by the Tamil Nadu government on the Sri Lankan issue, a large human chain and a delegation to Delhi to meet the Prime Minister, and then portrayed herself as concerned for Sri Lankan Tamils only during the election. The author says the people of Tamil Nadu understood this.

But, he concludes, the “world wonder” is that she wrote and issued a statement containing what he calls a falsehood she herself could not believe.

And if, he says, some people nevertheless believe it and claim that Jayalalithaa alone is concerned about Sri Lankan Tamils, that is not merely a world wonder:

**it is a great world wonder.**

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**11-6-2009**

செய்தியை மத்திய அரசின் வாயிலாக நாம் அதனைத் தெரிந்து கொண்டிருக்கிறோம். பொருள்கள் வாங்கியது போக மீதமிருந்த 25 கோடி ரூபாயை மத்திய அரசின் மூலமாக இலங்கைத் தமிழர்களின் மறு வாழ்வுக்காகவே அனுப்பி வைப்பதாகவும் உறுதியளித்திருக்கிறோம்.

அதுமட்டுமல்ல, மத்தியில் உள்ள தி.மு. கழக அமைச்சர்களும், தி.மு. கழக நாடாளுமன்ற உறுப்பினர்களும் திருமதி சோனியா காந்தி அம்மையாரையும், பிரதமர் டாக்டர் மன்மோகன் சிங் அவர்களையும் சந்தித்து இலங்கைத் தமிழர்களின் மறுவாழ்வுக்கான பணிகள் மேலும் விரிவுபடுத்தப்பட வேண்டுமென்றும், அவர்களுக்குக் கொடுக்கின்ற நிவாரணத் தொகை அதிகப்படுத்தப்பட வேண்டுமென்றும் என் சார்பில் வேண்டுகோள் விடுத்திருக்கிறார்கள். நானும் தொடர்ந்து கடிதங்கள் மூலமாகவும், தந்திகள் வாயிலாகவும் வேண்டுகோள் அனுப்பிக் கொண்டேயிருக்கிறேன். அவைகளையும் கவனித்து ஆவன செய்வோம் என்று அம்மையார் திருமதி சோனியா காந்தியும், பிரதமர் மன்மோகன் சிங்கும் உறுதியளித்து வருகிறார்கள். நமது தொடர் வேண்டுகோள்களின் காரணமாகத் தான் மத்திய அரசு இலங்கைத் தமிழர்களின் புனர் வாழ்விற்காக இந்திய அரசின் சார்பில் 500 கோடி ரூபாய் நிதியுதவியளிக்க முன்வந்து, அதற்காக நாம் நன்றியும் தெரிவித்திருக்கிறோம்.

ஆனால் இங்கேயுள்ள ஜெயலலிதா “கருணாநிதி, இலங்கைத் தமிழர்களைப் பற்றி வாய்திறக்க மாட்டேன் என்கிறார்” என்று ஒரு வடிகட்டிய பொய்யை தன்னுடைய அறிக்கையின் வாசகமாக ஆக்கியிருக்கிறார். பிரபாகரனை கைது செய்து, இந்தியாவிற்குக் கொண்டு வர வேண்டுமென்றும், அவருக்கு தண்டனை வழங்கப்பட வேண்டுமென்றும் சட்டமன்றத்திலே தீர்மானத்தை முன்மொழிந்தவரே இதே ஜெயலலிதாதான். அனைத்துக் கட்சிகளும் சேர்ந்து தமிழக அரசின் சார்பில் இலங்கைப் பிரச்சினைக்காக சென்னை தலைமைச் செயலகத்திலே அனைத்துக் கட்சிக் கூட்டம் நடத்திய போதும், மகத்தான மனித சங்கிலி ஒன்றை நடத்தியபோதும், டெல்லி சென்று பிரதமரைச் சந்தித்த போதும்; அதையெல்லாம் புறக்கணித்து விட்டு தேர்தல் நேரத்தில் மட்டும் இலங்கைத் தமிழர்களுக்காக அக்கறை கொண்டவர் போல செயல்பட்டதை தமிழ்நாட்டு மக்கள் நன்றாகப் புரிந்து கொண்டு தான் இருக்கிறார்கள்.
