# 3637. Go Today! Win Sweetly! — I Send You Forth with My Blessing!

>
> This is the first of two distinct consecutive source records both printed as letter number 3637. The source numbering anomaly is preserved; no 3636 is invented. The draft follows the locked Volume 46 conventions. Kalaignar’s `அறப்போர்` / jail-filling mobilisation language, first-person pledge to stand in the ranks, criticism of the AIADMK Government and hostile press, references to the Thiruverkadu mass marriages, official transfers, Delta distress, the V.K. Gurusamy case and the closing martial exhortation are retained in source order and rhetorical force. Political and legal claims remain source-attributed and are not externally reconciled. **Udanpirappē,**

At the emergency meeting of the DMK Executive Committee held under my chairmanship at Kalaignar Arangam, Anna Arivalayam, on 22 June 2012, we unanimously adopted a resolution stating that the time had come for the never-faltering jewels of the Kazhagam to gather and enter an *arappor*—to uphold justice under law and to confront and defeat authoritarian injustice. The resolution decided that the jail-filling agitation would begin on the morning of 4 July, and that, as a prelude, explanatory public meetings for the *arappor* would be held in district headquarters, circles and major towns. In accordance with that decision, headquarters speakers of the Kazhagam have addressed public meetings attended by thousands across Tamil Nadu and explained why the Kazhagam has come forward to conduct the jail-filling agitation on 4 July.

The AIADMK Government has been pleased to say that, because the DMK is not the ruling party, there will not be much response to the jail-filling agitation. Newspapers such as *Dinamani* and *Dinamalar* have been publishing artificial reports saying cadres have no enthusiasm to join it. But reports reaching headquarters from all the districts show, to an extent that makes us proud, how strongly enthusiasm and awakening for this *arappor* have surged among the cadres. Every district secretary has published in *Murasoli* the places where the agitation will take place, who will lead it and who will stand in the forefront.

I too have a great longing to take part in this *arappor*! I am not one accustomed to sending others into a protest and then standing aside to rest. In every struggle, I have taken part and have been sent away to prison; I have never been merely one who sends others. Even if I have not been formally announced as one participating in this jail-filling agitation, remember what I said at the Tambaram meeting: I shall stand in this line; if that line is stopped, in the next; if that too is stopped, in the last line. Karunanidhi will be there in that line. Even if I am not among the first line inside prison, you know well that my heart and feeling will be inside the prison, beating their wings and moving about.

This agitation itself arises from the Executive Committee resolution adopted when we could no longer bear the continuing acts of revenge by the AIADMK rulers against Kazhagam pioneers who organised and participated in earlier *arappor* struggles. Unable to answer the questions repeatedly raised by us and by other Opposition parties about their actions, these rulers have not even been able to answer the many accusations made against this Government and the Chief Minister by the husband of the Chief Minister’s close companion, who lives in her own house, regarding his arrest.

Somehow, the Chief Minister’s name has to appear in the newspapers every day. During the Assembly session, announcements were somehow found and read under Rule 110; now the same thing is done in the name of a “Government press release.”

How much publicity, noise and how many cut-outs were there for the “1,006 free marriages” at the Thiruverkadu temple! Even newspapers favourable to the Government mocked the pomp because it was enough to make anyone grimace. What happened next? The day after the ceremony, several of the supposed newly married couples appeared with their children, and weekly magazines published the photographs, showing the people what kind of marriages the Chief Minister had conducted with taxpayers’ money. Ministers and district secretaries of the ruling party had selected the couples and given the lists to officials; those names were entered, and Amma conducted the marriages. But when the weekly magazines exposed the whole affair beyond doubt, the Commissioner of the Hindu Religious and Charitable Endowments Department was hurriedly transferred without being given another posting. Four or five officials beneath him were also temporarily suspended.

Virudhunagar District Collector Balaji, who moved quickly to fill Anganwadi and nutritious-meal vacancies without recommendation or bribery, was transferred and placed on a waiting list without another posting. Whatever happens, Government officials are made sacrificial goats for the mistakes of these rulers. The burning resentment and wounded minds of those officials will one day certainly make the rulers answer. Without concern for these matters, the Chief Minister is resting in cool Kodanadu. Yet every day there is an announcement in her name. The State is being governed through press notes.

Had the same mistakes occurred when the DMK was in office, how fiercely would the other parties in Tamil Nadu have issued statements! Jayalalithaa would have sent out statement after statement demanding the Chief Minister’s immediate resignation, dismissal of the Government and Central intervention.

How much corruption and extortion are the ruling party’s Chennai Corporation councillors engaged in? This is not an allegation made by the Opposition. The Chief Minister of their own party has openly admitted it and spoken about it, and newspapers across Tamil Nadu have carried it page after page. If one says those councillors did no wrong, then it would mean the Chief Minister falsely accused them. Is it enough merely to scold them? Should not stern action be taken? Will taxpayers not ask? The ruling-party councillors themselves ask: “Are we alone the fools? Did ministers do no wrong? District secretaries? Ruling-party MLAs? Who has piled up the commission money collected in the name of giving it to the leadership?” What answer does this Government have to the questions the public asks without even having to ask aloud? Is the answer merely to rest in Kodanadu?

June has ended and July has begun. The Mettur Dam, which ought to have been opened on 12 June, has still not been opened. Farmers of the Thanjavur, Tiruvarur, Nagapattinam and Tiruchi Delta districts shed tears, wondering whether this year’s *kuruvai* crop is simply over. Who knows when electricity will come and when it will go? Is a Government functioning in Tamil Nadu, or is it sleeping and resting in Kodanadu?

Unable to answer any of this, the rulers search out Opposition members and people they dislike, obtain some half-baked false complaint, arrest them in the name of action, send them to prison, file another case when a court grants bail, and if they come out in that too, torment them under the Goondas Act. They carry on this lawlessness without hesitation.

A judgment delivered yesterday by the Madurai High Court itself is a fitting example of the extent to which this Government files false cases for revenge.

Selvaraj of Madurai Anna Nagar complained that, in February 2010, Madurai city Kazhagam functionary V.K. Gurusamy and his son-in-law, advocate S. Pandi, had grabbed his seven-cent property and the buildings on it. Without conducting any proper inquiry, the AIADMK Government’s police immediately arrested Gurusamy and Pandi. They approached the Madurai High Court seeking to quash the case, producing documents showing that S. Pandi had purchased the property for proper consideration as early as 2008 and arguing that the police had acted without investigation. Justice A. Selvam noted that the complaint regarding an alleged February 2010 incident was filed only on 29 July 2011, that the delay had not been explained, and that the petitioners’ contention that the case may have been registered for political reasons was capable of acceptance. The land-grab case against them was therefore quashed.

From this judgment one can clearly understand the extent to which the AIADMK Government receives false complaints, uses the police to take revenge on Kazhagam members, and arrests them.

It is precisely in search of dawn and an answer to all this that the DMK, as the genuine Opposition, has announced tomorrow’s jail-filling *arappor*. I ask the people of Tamil Nadu to give this struggle their wholehearted support. I ask cadres and front-rankers to work together in unity and conduct this *arappor* in a manner that makes it a magnificent success, teaches the rulers a good lesson and shows the way forward.

*Arappor* is not new to us. I wait with confidence that, as always, you will carry this one through splendidly too! Let the useless ones lament; let them babble whatever they wish! **Warrior! Resolute one! Set forth with sparks of fire in your eyes!** There are still some who abuse even the history of the language struggles you conducted. Kick aside that mean act—go to the announced field of *arappor* to remove the people’s grievances and their sufferings. **Go today! Win sweetly!** I bless you. I send you forth!

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**3 July 2012**

ஆண்டு பிப்ரவரி மாதம், அவரது 7 சென்ட் நிலத்தையும், அதில் இருந்த கட்டிடங்களையும், மதுரை மாநகராட்சி கிழக்கு மண்டலத் தலைவராக இருந்த வி.கே. குருசாமியும், அவரது மருமகன் வழக்கறிஞர் பாண்டியும் அபகரித்துக் கொண்டதாக கொடுத்த புகாரின் அடிப்படையில் அ.தி.மு.க. அரசின் காவல் துறை எந்த விதமான விசாரணையும் நடத்தாமல் உடனடியாக வி.கே.குருசாமியையும், பாண்டியையும் கைது செய்தார்கள். அந்தச் சொத்துக்களை 2008ஆம் ஆண்டிலேயே எஸ். பாண்டி உரிய விலை கொடுத்து வாங்கியதற்கான ஆவணங்கள் உள்ளன என்றும், காவல்துறை விசாரிக்காமல் நடவடிக்கை எடுத்திருப்பதாகவும், வழக்கினை ரத்து செய்ய வேண்டுமென்றும் மதுரை உயர் நீதி மன்றத்தில் மனு தாக்கல் செய்தனர். இந்த வழக்கை விசாரித்த மதுரை உயர்நீதி மன்ற நீதிபதி ஏ. செல்வம் அவர்கள், 2010ஆம் ஆண்டு பிப்ரவரி மாதம் நடந்ததாகக் கூறப்படும் சம்பவம் தொடர்பாக, 29-7-2011 அன்று புகார் கொடுக்கப்பட்டுள்ளது. இந்தத் தாமதத்துக்கான விளக்கம் அளிக்கப்படவில்லை. அரசியல் காரணங்களுக்காக இந்த வழக்கு பதிவு செய்யப்பட்டிருக்கலாம் என்ற மனுதாரரின் தரப்பு வாதம் ஏற்றுக் கொள்ளப்படும் நிலையில் உள்ளது. எனவே மனுதாரர்கள் மீதான இந்த நில அபகரிப்பு வழக்கை ரத்து செய்து உத்தரவிடப்படுகிறது என்று கூறியிருக்கிறார்.

இந்தத் தீர்ப்பிலிருந்து அ.தி.மு.க. அரசு எந்த அளவிற்குப் பொய்ப் புகார்களை கொடுத்து, காவல் துறையின் மூலம் கழகத்தினரை பழி வாங்குகிறது, கைது செய்கிறது என்பதை தெளிவாகப் புரிந்து கொள்ளலாம்.

இதற்கெல்லாம் விடிவையும், விடையையும் தேடித்தான் செல்லப்படும் உண்மையான எதிர்க்கட்சி என்ற முறையில் தி.மு. கழகம் நாளை சிறை நிரப்பும் அறப் போராட்டத்தை அறிவித்து நடத்துகின்றது. இந்தப் போராட்டத்திற்குத் தமிழ்நாட்டு மக்கள் தங்கள் இதயபூர்வமான ஆதரவினைத் தந்திட வேண்டுமென்று கேட்டுக் கொள்கிறேன். இந்த அறப்போரினை மிகப் பெரிய வெற்றியாக - ஆட்சியினருக்கு நல்ல பாடம் புகட்டுகின்ற அளவிற்கும் ஆக்க வழிகாட்டுகின்ற முறையிலும் நடத்திட

தொண்டர்களும், கழக முன்னணியினரும் ஒருங்கிணைந்து ஒற்றுமையோடு பாடுபட வேண்டுமென்று கேட்டுக் கொள்கிறேன். அறப்போர் நமக்கு புதிதல்ல; எப்போதும் போல இதனையும் சிறப்பாகச் செய்து முடிப்பாய் என்ற நம்பிக்கையோடு காத்திருக்கிறேன்! வீணர்கள் புலம்பட்டும்; வீணர்கள் ஏதாவது உளறிக் கொண்டிருக்கட்டும்! வீரனே! தீரனே! விழிக்கணல் வழியப் புறப்படுவாய்! மொழிப்போர் நடத்திய உன் வரலாற்றை இன்றும் பழிப்போர் சிலர் உளர்! அந்த இழி செயலை இடறிவிட்டு - மக்கள் குறை தீர்க்க - அவர்தம் இன்னல்களை - அறிவிக்கப்பட்டுள்ள அறப்போர்க்களத்திற்கு இனிதே செல்க! இனிதே வெல்க! என்று வாழ்த்துகிறேன்! வழியனுப்புகிறேன்!
