## Method

This was the separate volume-level consistency gate required after source-check drafting and all eleven bilingual-alignment batches. The review used the English README/index, glossary, translation controls and the complete set of bilingual-alignment reports for Letters 3537–3591 as the durable record-level QA evidence. Those alignment reports had already compared each complete bilingual record against its complete audited Tamil, including the `Original Tamil — மூலத் தமிழ்` appendix in physical PDF-page order.

The editorial pass therefore concentrated on cross-volume consistency rather than retranslating or re-adjudicating source meaning. It checked title/front-matter/index agreement, dates and page ranges, translator-note wording, names and honorifics, place-name and transliteration conventions, institutions and schemes, legal/political terminology, Indian public-language quantities, punctuation and quotation treatment, source-supplied English, source-anomaly handling, stale control-layer status language and the durable record of appended Tamil completeness.

The controlling scan remains the ultimate textual authority. No possible residual Tamil defect was exposed by this editorial pass, so no new scan comparison or Tamil canonical change was required.
