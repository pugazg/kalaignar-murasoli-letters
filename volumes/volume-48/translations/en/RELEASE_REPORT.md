## Manifest integrity

The release manifest contains exactly **58 rows**, one for each letter from **3706 through 3763**. Its source-page ranges are continuous from **PDF 24 through PDF 400** and from **printed page 23 through printed page 399**, with no gap or overlap. Every row is marked `verified` for both translation and bilingual alignment and is mapped to the applicable completed bilingual-alignment report.

The manifest records release-critical identity and provenance fields: letter number, date, English title, Tamil title, PDF and printed page spans, verification status, textual-fidelity status, translation file path and bilingual-alignment report. Unlike the earlier Volume 49 manifest, this manifest does not add a derived English word-count field; the release gate is based on source identity, completeness and verification state rather than a length metric.

## Editorial and archival policy

The released English is a clear, contemporary, non-literary translation. It preserves Kalaignar’s argument order, political judgement, criticism, irony, repetition, quoted voices, factual detail and rhetorical questions. The complete audited Tamil source remains appended to every letter and remains authoritative.

The release does not use editorial review to reconcile or silently correct source irregularities. Exact English already printed in the source remains unchanged where deliberately reproduced, including the Katchatheevu spelling variants, court and newspaper passages, and malformed **`By injectinga`** in letter 3763. Source-visible numerical, chronological and institutional tensions already documented during alignment remain preserved.

## QA summary

The completed editorial consistency pass made only six title-level typography corrections, in letters **3708, 3727, 3739, 3743, 3748 and 3752**. These standardised English title quotation marks and the apostrophe in **Kalaignar’s**. No prose-level source meaning, responsibility, attribution, uncertainty, figure, quotation or Tamil text was changed.

The complete release retains the established Volume 48 conventions: `Udanpirappē`, **With affection, M.K.**, `lakh`, `crore`, **Rule 110**, source-attributed allegations, source-specific transliteration and exact intentional source English.
