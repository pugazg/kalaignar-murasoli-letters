# Volume 1 — Bilingual Alignment: Letters 0011–0020

Status: **PASS — second bilingual-alignment batch reviewed after one correspondence correction.**

## Scope

This batch covers canonical English letters **0011–0020**, corresponding to source PDF pages **067–104**.

The English records had already completed canonical migration/source checking. This gate performs a fresh meaning-level comparison between each English translation and the complete canonical Tamil witness embedded in the record, with the earlier source-check report consulted for source-specific controls.

## Alignment method

For every record, the review checked:

1. argument and narrative sequence;
2. substantive coverage, omissions and unsupported additions;
3. paragraph order and transitions;
4. quotation boundaries, attribution and embedded verse;
5. names, dates, figures, offices and political references;
6. metaphors, wordplay and rhetorical questions;
7. repetition, irony, satire and political intensity;
8. movement vocabulary and the established `Udanpirappē` convention;
9. closing/sign-off integrity;
10. known source controls documented by the source-check gate.

Only demonstrable English/Tamil correspondence errors qualify for correction here. Stylistic polishing belongs to the later editorial-consistency gate.

## Batch result

**All ten records PASS bilingual alignment. One English prose correction was required, in letter 0014.**

Apart from that correction, the canonical Tamil and English remain in the same substantive order, with no material omission or unsupported addition identified.

### 0011 — `தென்றல்- தெம்பாங்கு- தேன்கீதம்!`

PASS. The fifty-six-day separation, Anna's return from America, the peacock/deer/flower-garden sequence, the September 10 anguish, the Buddha comparison, the extended praise of Anna's literary and political stature, and the final repeated welcome all correspond to the Tamil witness. The poetic repetitions and direct vocatives remain intact.

### 0012 — `“பூப்போட்ட கிளாசிலே போட்டய்யா ஒண்ணரை!”`

PASS. The Goa Congress resolution, C. Subramaniam's seven-year prohibition argument, Sushila Nayyar's whisky-bottle example, the law/propaganda comparison, Bhaktavatsalam's earlier Legislative Assembly answer, and the Gandhi-tomb/final-song satire all correspond in source order.

### 0013 — `மேயர் தேர்தல் நேரம் - நிலை - நேர்த்தியான முடிவு!`

PASS. The newspaper-created mayoral speculation, the domestic/idli comic scene, the contrast between names appearing in agitation lists and an office-seeker list, the distinction between service without office and office as an opportunity for service, and the appeal to leave the decision to Anna all align with the Tamil. The source-restored paragraph about pursuing office remains represented once, not duplicated.

### 0014 — `‘பகைமரம்’ தழைக்க விடோம்!`

PASS **after one alignment correction**. The political-assassination examples, DMK martyrs, argument that ideas must confront ideas, rejection of political violence, `பகைமரம்` imagery, and closing appeal for political culture all correspond.

The previous English compressed two consecutive Tamil statements into one sentence and thereby extended the source's strongest condemnation to the wrong items. Tamil first says that aggressive swagger (`முண்டா தட்டுதல்-மீசை முறுக்குதல்`) does not befit politics; it then separately says that raising a gun or drawing a knife is a political method even savages should fear to adopt. The English has been split into two matching statements so this distinction is restored without editorial embellishment.

### 0015 — `நம்பிக்கையில்லாத் தீர்மானம் - நாடாளுமன்ற நிகழ்ச்சி!`

PASS. The November 13 no-confidence uproar, the September 19 government-employees' strike and reprisals, the argument for democratic debate without disorder, the contrast with the Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly, and the closing appeal concerning affected employees and their families all correspond to the Tamil witness. The final move from writing to the friend to writing to the country retains its source function.

### 0016 — `யார் அந்த உணவு அமைச்சர்?`

PASS. The opening satire on Congress newspapers, rice procurement and landlord litigation, the argument about continuity from Congress rule, the *Navasakthi* headline/report distinction, the 1966 order and Food Minister Ramayya context, and the mother-in-law metaphor all align. The satire does not acquire claims absent from the Tamil.

### 0017 — `கிளம்பிற்றுக்காண் தமிழச் சிங்கக் கூட்டம்!`

PASS. The Student DMK proclamations, Rajendran and Sarangapani passages, the Anbagam memorial, Congress-era treatment of anti-Hindi students, the contrast with Anna and Kazhagam ministers, the statewide Student DMK organising decision under Raja Mohammed, and the closing revolutionary verse all preserve source sequence and political intensity.

### 0018 — `கழுதையும் - பன்றியும்!`

PASS. The response to Kannadasan's attack on DMK ministers, the Kamaraj/Sivaji/Nehru examples, criticism of his earlier language, Congress comparison, and the king-turned-pig fable all correspond. The deliberate printed censorship ellipses in the Bhaktavatsalam/Alagesan passage remain unreconstructed in the Tamil witness and are not supplied in English.

### 0019 — `“பசுத்தோல் வேங்கை பாராய்!”`

PASS. Morarji Desai's Lucknow remarks, the contrast with Anna's handling of students, the bus-worker/student episode, the Assembly exchange about students, Morarji's Hindi statement, and the final cowhide/tiger metaphor align. The substantive material restored during source checking on PDF 099 — including Anna's `ரத்தத்தின் ரத்தம் / சதையின் சதை` reply and Morarji's five-line Hindi statement — remains fully represented.

### 0020 — `“தமிழ்த்தாயே அவர்களை மன்னித்துவிடு!”`

PASS. The December 1 Tamil Nadu naming celebration, household dialogue, twenty-year struggle, Sankaralinganar passage, Congress opposition to the name, appeal to Mother Tamil, and closing festival exhortations all remain in source order. Repetition of `தமிழ் நாடு`, festival language, and the praise of Anna retain their rhetorical force.
