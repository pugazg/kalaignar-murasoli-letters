# Textual-Fidelity Audit — Letters 3795–3799

## Scope

- Volume: **49**
- Letters: **3795–3799**
- Canonical Tamil Markdown pages: **PDF 242–276**
- Total pages visually compared with scans: **35/35**
- Method: each canonical page was compared against its corresponding rendered PDF scan before translation. Titles, letter numbers, paragraph order, quotations, English passages, figures, punctuation, signatures, dates and page boundaries were checked.

## Result

- All **35** pages were inspected.
- No paragraph or passage was missing.
- Seventeen scan-verified transcription artefacts were corrected in the canonical Tamil pages before the bilingual files were assembled.
- The corrections are limited to visible word boundaries, broken words, punctuation, a misread character and false paragraph breaks. They do not modernise, fact-check or rewrite the source.

## Source-exact features retained

- Letter 3795’s historical and political claims and its Lee Kuan Yew quotation are translated as printed, without external fact-checking or reconciliation.
- Letter 3796 preserves the source’s newspaper-based accounts of sexual violence, its names and sequence, and the visibly printed question mark in `நல்லெண்ணம் (?)`.
- The original English quotations in letter 3797 remain exactly as printed in the Tamil source, including `XI th`.
- The English *Express* quotation in letter 3798 remains as printed; the arrest, death and disappearance figures are not externally reconciled.
- Letter 3799 retains its source-era exchange rates, economic values and internal figures, including `170 லட்சம் கோடி` and `58700 கோடி டாலர்`.
- Political descriptions, institutional titles, dates, statistics and attributed statements are translated as presented in the volume.

## Translation assembly

Each English file contains:

1. the standard Translator’s note;
2. a complete thought-preserving English translation;
3. letter-specific source notes; and
4. the full corrected canonical Tamil text, divided by original PDF page.

This audit establishes **source-checked** status for letters **3795–3799**. Full bilingual line-by-line editorial review remains a later, separate stage.
