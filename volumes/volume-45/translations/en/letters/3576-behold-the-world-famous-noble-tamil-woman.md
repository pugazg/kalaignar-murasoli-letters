# 3576. Behold the World-Famous Noble Tamil Woman!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This translation is intended to carry Kalaignar's voice into clear, contemporary English rather than recast the letter as literary or academic prose. It preserves the source's argument, political directness, rhetorical questions, repetition, irony, factual detail, and paragraph order. Names, dates, figures, quotations, and intentional English expressions are retained. Where Tamil idiom cannot be reproduced literally without sounding unnatural, the English follows its sense and rhetorical force without adding claims absent from the source. The original Tamil is reproduced in full below the translation and remains the authoritative text. `Udanpirappē` is retained in Tamil transliteration rather than flattened into “brother,” “sister,” or “comrade.” Literally evoking “one born alongside me,” Kalaignar uses it as a distinctive address of shared identity, equality, affection, and solidarity within the movement.

**Udanpirappē,**

“Bowing at the golden feet of Amma, leader of Tamils across the world, who saved the Sri Lankan Tamils by making America impose economic sanctions on Sri Lanka”—advertisements like this, page after page! “Praise! Praise!” advertisements! Advertisements singing fabricated praise!

Next, our Finance Minister O. Panneerselvam has placed a half-page advertisement: “Thank you! Thank you!! Thank you!!! O Chanakya of politics who tirelessly worked and succeeded in making America impose economic sanctions on the Sri Lankan government! O rare ascetic praised by the world! O tireless leader honoured by Tamils across the world! O mighty power that showed the superpower the way! Amma, I bow at your golden feet.” Those are the words of the advertisement!

Next comes a Minister—K.A. Sengottaiyan, Minister for Agriculture—with another half-page advertisement. The words in it are the same: thank you! thank you! thank you! et cetera, et cetera.....

Why all these thanks? Has America imposed economic sanctions on the Sri Lankan government? Ministers themselves have placed advertisements, have they not? Would they do so if it had not happened? Everyone is left wondering!

Prakash M. Swamy is a journalist from Tamil Nadu who is now in New York. Whenever he comes to Tamil Nadu he asks for time to meet me, and has met me once or twice. Each time he would say: you must come to America; I will arrange for you to receive a doctorate; I will arrange an award; I will arrange for a street to be named after you, and so on. During the previous DMK government too, when he asked to meet me, I met him once; the next time I could not give him an appointment. During the DMK government he was even given the “Kalaimamani” award. At the time of the World Classical Tamil Conference he asked that he be given authority to coordinate and bring all the Tamils living in America. It is this same Prakash M. Swamy who has now praised Tamil Nadu Chief Minister Jayalalithaa in an interview to the weekly *Kumudam*.

In that praise, he reportedly said there is a college in New York called John Jay College of Criminal Justice; that its textbook devotes several pages to praising the all-women police stations created by Jayalalithaa's thinking; and that many such astonishing reports had so impressed U.S. Secretary of State Hillary Clinton that, after meeting the Prime Minister in Delhi, she came straight to Chennai.

He further shared with the *Kumudam* reporter, in wonder, that because the U.S. Government calculates that Jayalalithaa may have an opportunity to become Deputy Prime Minister after the next parliamentary election, Hillary may think it would be good to have someone close to America in Delhi; and that Hillary was greatly impressed by J.'s courage and resolve in passing a resolution in the Tamil Nadu Assembly against the Sri Lankan government.

The news that this same Prakash M. Swamy met me on 2-2-2010 appeared in the newspapers at the time. That report said: “Prakash M. Swamy, Secretary of the New York Tamil Sangam in America, met Chief Minister Kalaignar at his residence and spoke with him. Expressing the support of the New York Tamil Sangam for the Coimbatore Classical Tamil Conference, he said that 100 Tamil scholars from America would take part in the conference. Speaking later to reporters, Prakash M. Swamy said that, in appreciation of Chief Minister Kalaignar's social service, the highest award given on behalf of the American Congress—the ‘American Congressional Assembly Award’—would be presented to him. He said representatives of the New York Tamil Sangam would personally present this award, being given to an Indian leader for the first time, during the Coimbatore Classical Tamil Conference.” That was all!

It is this Prakash M. Swamy, who caused that report, who now says that J.'s courage and resolve in passing a resolution in the Tamil Nadu Assembly against the Sri Lankan government impressed Hillary. Is Jayalalithaa the only person ever to have passed a resolution in the Assembly against the Sri Lankan government? Were no resolutions against the Sri Lankan government passed in the Tamil Nadu Assembly before this?

Some others are practically saying that because Jayalalithaa passed a resolution in the Assembly, America has already imposed economic sanctions on Sri Lanka, and they are offering praise. A poster for an evening paper two days ago printed: “America imposes economic sanctions on Sri Lanka—success for Jayalalithaa's efforts.” This is an example of the “Bhagiratha effort” some people here are making to turn such an enormous, deliberately created falsehood into truth and deceive the public. Seeing reports like these, even friend Thirumavalavan, in a statement he issued, appears to have welcomed it, thinking perhaps it might be true. As soon as I saw that, I inquired with some Sri Lankan Tamils living in India, and they said America had so far made no such announcement.

The U.S. Congressional foreign-affairs committee has voted to limit American assistance to the Sri Lankan government in the wake of war-crime allegations. Even this action will not come into effect immediately. The committee's recommendation concerning suspension of aid to Sri Lanka in the financial year beginning in October 2012 must be placed before both houses of the American legislature—the Senate and Congress—and will take effect only after resolutions are passed there. This proposal has been under discussion among committee members for several weeks. It has no connection whatever with the U.S. Secretary of State's visit to India. A U.S. Secretary of State would discuss matters concerning other countries only with the Government of India, not with a State Government in India.

The official website of the U.S. State Department carried details of Hillary Clinton's India visit and her speeches. It listed only three events—the Anna Centenary Library, the Working Women's organisation, and the meeting at the Taj Hotel—and the details of what she said there. Even when a U.S. Assistant Secretary of State spoke to reporters about the meeting, he said only: “They talked about Sri Lanka, and I think they both agreed that we have concerns about the situation in Sri Lanka.” Even the Tamil Nadu Government's press release about the meeting says only that Jayalalithaa stated that internally displaced Tamils had still not been resettled after the war, and that Hillary said a new programme was being developed.

Yet people here have struggled desperately to write as though the U.S. Congressional committee took this economic-sanctions decision only after Jayalalithaa pressed for it during the Sri Lankan war, and as though Jayalalithaa had thereby risen to become the leader of Tamils across the world.

And meanwhile AIADMK cadres place page after page of congratulatory advertisements proclaiming “victory upon victory” for Amma's effort without even knowing what actually happened!

It was the same way during Jayalalithaa's previous rule. A report was circulated that the United Nations was going to confer an honorary award on Jayalalithaa. There was a grand function, page after page of advertisements, garlands of praise—the whole spectacle. But when it later became known that it was not true, they changed the address: it was not the United Nations, they said, but a consultative body of the UN, a human-rights protection committee. They said the very award given to Kofi Annan was going to be given to our Amma.

Only afterwards did R. Shanmugasundaram, MP, who had gone to address the United Nations as a representative of the Indian Parliament, along with Congress spokesperson Anand Sharma, four other MPs and Madhavan Kutty, editor of the well-known Malayalam paper *Mathrubhumi*, inquire about the award and announce that it had no connection with the United Nations. The claim that the International Human Rights Protection Committee giving an award to Jayalalithaa was an official UN body was an outright lie. As many as 2,531 non-governmental organisations across the world had been accredited to participate in the UN's consultative system; the organisation giving Jayalalithaa the award was not even in that list of 2,531. Yet it was advertised at the time as the UN's “Golden Star Award” and so on.

In the same manner now, they are trying to spread the story that because Jayalalithaa introduced a resolution against the Sri Lankan government, America became frightened and imposed economic sanctions on Sri Lanka. If senior Ministers in the AIADMK Cabinet themselves say such things in half-page newspaper advertisements, is it proper to advertise falsehoods about America in this fashion? Is that not a foreign-affairs matter?

Open the evening papers and page after page carries photographs of Jayalalithaa seated with the U.S. Secretary of State, dressed up with verbal fireworks. Even yesterday evening's front-page advertisement declared: “O lamp of Tamils across the world, Amma, who introduced the first Assembly resolution calling for economic sanctions on the racist Sri Lankan government and paved the way for America to impose economic sanctions on Sri Lanka, I bow at your flower-like feet with heartfelt thanks”—and there are advertisement after advertisement like this. Across Chennai there are cut-outs proclaiming “Future of Eelam” and “Dawn of Eelam.”

For the past three months, Tamil Nadu's schoolchildren have stood confused, not knowing which books they are to study. Their parents and teachers, not knowing when the books will come, are waiting for the Supreme Court's decision. In such a situation, while Jayalalithaa has subjected students to hardship because of her obstinacy, people publish all these nonexistent stories about her and derive pleasure from them. Should there not be some limit even to telling lies?

The very same Jayalalithaa who introduced and passed a resolution in the Assembly against the Eelam liberation fighters has today become the “world-famous noble Tamil woman” to whom page after page of advertising bhajans are sung!

**With affection, M.K.**  
31-7-2011

தமிழர்களின் ஒளி விளக்கு அம்மா அவர்களே, தங்களின் மலர்
பாதம் பணிந்து நெஞ்சம் நிறைந்த நன்றி” - இன்னும் இப்படி
அடுக்கடுக்கான விளம்பரங்கள் மட்டுமல்ல. சென்னை மாநகரம்
முழுவதும் “ஈழத்தின் எதிர்காலமே” என்றும் - “ஈழத்தின்
விடியலே” என்றும் கட்-அவுட் காட்சிகள். தமிழகத்து மாணவச்
செல்வங்கள் கடந்த மூன்று மாதங்களாக எந்தப் புத்தகத்தைப்
படிப்பது என்று தெரியாமல் கலங்கி நிற்கின்றார்கள். அவர்களின்
பெற்றோர்களும், ஆசிரியர்களும் எப்போது புத்தகம் வரும் என்று
தெரியாமல் உச்ச நீதி மன்றத் தீர்ப்பினை எதிர்பார்த்திருக் கிறார்கள்.
இந்த இலட்சணத்தில் மாணவர்களை தன்னுடைய பிடிவாதம்
காரணமாக இன்னலுக்கு ஆளாக்கிய ஜெயலலிதா அம்மையாரைப்
பற்றி இப்படி இல்லாத செய்திகளையெல்லாம் வெளியிட்டு,
அதிலே இன்பம் காணுகிறார்களே, பொய் சொல்வதற்கும் ஒரு
எல்லை வேண்டாமா? ஈழ விடுதலைப் போராளிகளுக்கு எதிராக
சட்டமன்றத்தில் தீர்மானத்தை முன்மொழிந்து நிறைவேற்றிய
சாட்சாத் அதே ஜெயலலிதாவேதான்: இன்று பக்கம் பக்கமாக

ஆகிவிட்டார்!
