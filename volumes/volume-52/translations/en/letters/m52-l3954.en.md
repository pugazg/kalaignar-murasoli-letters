## Translator's Note

This translation is intended to carry Kalaignar's voice into clear, contemporary English rather than recast the letter as literary or academic prose. It preserves the source's argument, political directness, rhetorical questions, repetition, irony, factual detail, and paragraph order. Names, dates, figures, quotations, and intentional English expressions are retained. Where Tamil idiom cannot be reproduced literally without sounding unnatural, the English follows its sense and rhetorical force without adding claims absent from the source. The Tamil below is a source-checked reading copy: the opening salutation omitted by OCR has been restored, list-marker corruption has been regularised, and clear OCR character and word-break errors in names and incidents have been repaired without changing the dated source chronology. The canonical source file remains unchanged and authoritative.

`Udanpirappē` is retained in Tamil transliteration rather than flattened into “brother,” “sister,” or “comrade.” Literally evoking “one born alongside me,” Kalaignar uses it as a distinctive address of shared identity, equality, affection, and solidarity within the movement.
