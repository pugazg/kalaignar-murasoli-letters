# 3516. An Elegant Hair-Bun, a Fragrant Screw-Pine Flower...!

**Udanpirappē,**

Today, 9-12-2010, AIADMK-supporting newspapers have carried prominent reports under the headline “Government Lands Being Looted under Karunanidhi Rule,” alleging that house sites were allotted to DMK MLAs and IAS officers. Though I gave a clear reply to this issue yesterday itself, the same accusation has been repeated. I shall therefore briefly say to whom such house sites were allotted during the AIADMK government.

During Jayalalithaa’s rule, the letter lists: former Chief Secretary N. Narayanan, IAS — 4,115 square feet in 1993; K. S. Karthesan, son of former Minister K. A. Sengottaiyan — 4,535 square feet in Besant Nagar in 1995; Noor Jameela, wife of former Minister Nagore Meeran — 2,559 square feet at Kottivakkam in 1993; Dr Bhanumathi Thambidurai, wife of Member of Parliament Thambidurai — seven grounds in Anna Nagar; the AIADMK Labour Federation — three grounds in Anna Nagar; Mrs Bhanumathi, wife of former AIADMK Minister S. M. Velusamy — a house in Coimbatore in 1993; and, in 2004, IPS officers including Devaram, K. Vijayakumar and R. Nataraj — 4,800 square feet each at Sholinganallur. Justice S. R. Singaravelu was allotted two plots at Sholinganallur in 2005. The source then continues its list with former MLA K. Selvaraj.

It says K. Selvaraj received 2,692 square feet at Kottivakkam in 1994; T. Natarajan, IAS, who had been Deputy Secretary to a former Chief Minister, received 6,784 square feet at Thiruvanmiyur in 1995; Adhi Rajaram received 3,101 square feet in 1995; Saraswathi, who was an MLA in 1993, received 880 square feet in Anna Nagar; former MLA Mallika received 950 square feet in Anna Nagar; MGR’s driver Boopathy received 3,600 square feet at Nandanam; and Plomina, wife of S. Andi Thevar, received 1,500 square feet at Madurai in 1994. The letter says there is also a long list of persons who were given two plots in 2005.

Thus, it argues, during AIADMK rule houses and plots were allotted in the names of ministers’ wives and sons, the AIADMK trade-union office, IAS and IPS officers, AIADMK legislators and Members of Parliament, the wife of an AIADMK district secretary and judges. I have cited only a few examples. If journalists who give great importance to allegations of wrongdoing in house-site allotments under DMK rule would investigate and publish the full details of who received such houses and plots under AIADMK rule, then, the letter says, their journalistic ethics could be appreciated.

The newspapers have also published a statement issued today by Jayalalithaa. We need not go anywhere else to find evidence of how confused Jaya is; merely read her statement. Do you know how the statement she issued on 6-12-2010 begins?

> “Just as Emperor Nero played the fiddle while the city of Rome was burning, while the people of Tamil Nadu are suffering because of the floods, Chief Minister Karunanidhi is spending his time merrily participating in the song-release function of his film ‘Ilaignan’.”

Again, on 8-12-2010, another Jayalalithaa statement appeared. Do you know how that one begins?

> “Just as Emperor Nero played the fiddle while the city of Rome was burning, while all of Tamil Nadu is submerged in floods because of heavy rain, Karunanidhi has spent his time happily at a film function with his Cabinet colleagues.”

The people of Tamil Nadu themselves must understand what difference there is between these two statements. If she begins the statement on the 8th without even remembering how she began the one on the 6th, what is the reason? Anger? Ignorance? Heartburn because I attended a film-industry function? Or jealousy that nobody invited her? And in this condition, this Kodanadu millionaire woman writes that it is I who issue clownish statements!

Do you know what Jayalalithaa says next? “The shocking truth in this is that the entire Tamil Nadu Cabinet participated in this event.” There is another bout of heartburn in that! During this lady’s government, every Minister in the Cabinet would attend every function she attended. Why, even now when she is not Chief Minister, when she attended events at Coimbatore, Tiruchi and Madurai, all her former Cabinet colleagues and today’s AIADMK front-line leaders went and participated.

But what was the actual situation now? There was a Cabinet meeting on the 7th. On the morning of the 5th there was a Pudukkottai district Kazhagam review meeting. In their party capacities, Finance Minister Professor as General Secretary, Deputy Chief Minister M. K. Stalin as Treasurer, Arcot Veerasamy as Principal Secretary, and Duraimurugan and Parithi Ilamvazhuthi as Deputy General Secretaries participated. Of these Ministers, all except the Professor — four Ministers — attended the evening event. In addition, Higher Education Minister Ponmudy and E. V. Velu, who had come to take Deputy Chief Minister Stalin to an event at Villupuram on the 6th, attended the film function. Out of a total of 29 Ministers, only six or seven attended. Yet Jayalalithaa states that the entire Tamil Nadu Cabinet attended. What name should be given to that?

Of even those six or seven, the Deputy Chief Minister and Ponmudy left Chennai after 9 p.m. when the function ended, travelled to Villupuram, attended various programmes, toured flood-affected areas in Cuddalore district and distributed relief assistance, and then returned in time to take part in the Cabinet meeting on the 7th.

Jayalalithaa further alleged that this government did not care about flood-relief work. Is even that true? Ministers, District Collectors and senior IAS officers were inspecting flood-affected areas daily and submitting reports to the government. On that basis, the entire Tamil Nadu Cabinet met, discussed the situation and decided on various forms of assistance. The Cabinet also decided to request Union Government assistance, and the necessary letter had already been sent. If Jayalalithaa nevertheless says that I showed no concern for flood relief, does the accusation have any substance?

When the anti-Hindi agitation was blazing, when bodies were falling in Tamil Nadu, the letter accuses, it was this same Jayalalithaa who was dancing on the Goa shore and singing a “duet.”

Jayalalithaa has also, as usual, rambled that I am threatening an alliance party. Does Jaya imagine the people of Tamil Nadu have forgotten that, when the BJP was in power, former Prime Minister Vajpayee said that Jayalalithaa had threatened them even on Independence Day and that the period of alliance with her was the worst period of his life?

Jayalalithaa says in her statement that the opposition parties’ struggle will not cease until a Joint Parliamentary Committee inquiry is accepted, imagining on her own that she is the sole leader of all opposition parties. There is a saying that one’s own imaginings can ruin one’s life. In the same way, she longs for something — anything — to happen somehow so that a dawn may come for her through it. Meanwhile, she weeps at the achievements and schemes of the DMK government and cannot bear it when others praise me. How could she tolerate Superstar Rajinikanth, epic poet Vaali and Kaviperarasu Vairamuthu attending that event and praising me? That is why, the letter says, she uses uncivil words in her statement.

Finally, Jayalalithaa closes her statement by writing: “Karunanidhi knows that the day when he must answer for all the sins he has committed is drawing near.” This Karunanidhi is not one to worry because Jayalalithaa — who, the letter says, is now experiencing the fruits of sins she herself committed — has declared in words that my day is approaching.

Next, Jayalalithaa says in her statement that I threatened the Congress and suggested that the Congress too was connected with the spectrum corruption. She is trying, on her own, to sow conflict between the DMK and Congress. When I spoke at the *Ilaignan* song-release function, I said: if someone says there was corruption of ₹1 lakh 76 thousand 352 crore, what an enormous amount that is — could an amount that large have been corruptly taken? Immediately Jayalalithaa, as usual, tries to tie a quarrel and says I am threatening the Congress. Her intention will not succeed.

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
10 December 2010

அடுத்து ஜெயலலிதா தனது அறிக்கையில் நான் காங்கிரசை மிரட்டுவதாகவும் ஸ்பெக்ட்ரம் ஊழலில் காங்கிரசுக்கும் தொடர்பு உண்டு என்பதைப்போல கருத்து தெரிவித்ததாகவும் அவராகவே சொல்லிக் கொண்டு தி.மு.க. விற்கும், காங்கிரசுக்கும் இடையே கலகம் ஊட்டுகின்ற முயற்சியில் ஈடுபட்டுள்ளார். நான் “இளைஞன்” பாடல்கள் வெளியீட்டு விழாவிலே பேசும்போது, ஒரு இலட்சத்து 76 ஆயிரத்து 352 கோடி ரூபாய் ஊழல் என்று சொன்னால், அது எவ்வளவு பெரிய தொகை, அந்தத் தொகையை ஊழல் செய்திருக்க முடியுமா? என்று பேசினேன். உடனே ஜெயலலிதா காங்கிரசை மிரட்டுவதாக வழக்கம் போல சிண்டு முடியப்பார்க்கிறார். அவரது எண்ணம் நிறைவேறாது.
