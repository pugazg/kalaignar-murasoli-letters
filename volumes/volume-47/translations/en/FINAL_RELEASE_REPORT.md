## Editorial release assessment

The completed volume-level review checked cross-letter consistency in:

- translator's notes and source-limitation notes;
- headings and title treatment;
- `Udanpirappē` and direct-address handling;
- personal names and transliteration;
- institutional, political and legal terminology;
- dates, figures and number formatting;
- quotation and attribution handling;
- recurring rhetoric and idiom;
- source-page presentation;
- sensitive-content framing; and
- closing conventions.

No unresolved editorial blocker was found. No English translation file required a source-aligned prose rewrite during the volume-level consistency pass.

The review deliberately preserved legitimate variation where the Tamil source varies rather than flattening the corpus into artificial uniformity.

## Mandatory exception — letter 3681

Letter **3681**, **“Let the Darkness Disappear! Come Quickly, Come!”**, remains the sole source-incomplete record.

Printed page **252** is absent from the only source PDF. Consequently, the unavailable continuation and closing are not present in either the authoritative audited Tamil transcription or the English translation. They were **not reconstructed**.

The letter's date is retained from the printed contents and is labelled accordingly in the English record.

The available portion of 3681 has completed bilingual alignment and the volume-level editorial review. Its incompleteness is therefore an explicit archival/source exception rather than a failed English-review gate.
