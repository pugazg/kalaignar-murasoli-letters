## Letter-level notes

### 0071 — `காலக் கருவூலம்!`

Preserves the Becosules / B-Complex capsule wordplay, the printed English ingredient list and mixed Tamil-English forms, and Kalaignar's contrast between the openly labelled medicinal capsule and the politically disputed Time Capsule.

### 0072 — `ஒன்று சொல்க!`

Preserves the Bharathidasan verse in its source-controlled sequence and the movement rhetoric surrounding it rather than smoothing the poem into ordinary prose.

### 0073 — `புன்னகையும் பெருமூச்சும்!`

Preserves the juxtaposition signalled by the title—smile and sigh—and the letter's original political and emotional movement without compressing the contrast.

### 0074 — `குறள் மறவோம்!`

Preserves Bhaktavatsalam's statement, Kalaignar's willingness to accept criticism, and the closing Thirukkural. The Tamil couplet remains in the full source witness while the English renders its meaning rather than substituting another edition.

### 0075 — `உன் எதிரே!`

Preserves the direct cadre address, the Madurai/Ramanathapuram/Tiruchirappalli/Salem fundraising sequence, the repeated second-person praise, and the lineated closing declarations of movement strength.

### 0076 — `தட்டிக் கேட்கலாமா?`

The three-sons analogy remains the controlling structure of the English: genuine weakness deserves support, unused capacity is not the same as disadvantage, and effort must not itself become grounds for deprivation. The Sixth Finance Commission argument is retained as a demand that Tamil Nadu not be denied its just share for having used its resources and administration effectively. Canonical PDF 287–288 controls over the older legacy reading, including `உடல் வலிவு பெறும்` and the source punctuation.

### 0077 — `பெரியாரின் வெற்றி!`

Preserves the mother/child analogy and Kalaignar's distinction between Periyar's objective and the methods used to pursue it. The posthumous praise from journals and national leaders remains contrasted with the abuse Periyar received while alive, and the closing `வெற்றி அறுவடை` image is retained as a “harvest of victory.”

### 0078 — `நம்மை வென்றாரா?`

Preserves the deliberate self-critical turn. Kalaignar asks whether Periyar has truly won over those who profess agreement with caste abolition while caste continues to shape political thought, office, employment, recommendations, kinship and `கொள்வன கொடுப்பன`. The full-length-mirror image and the painful answer `இல்லை! அவர் வென்றிடவில்லை!` remain unsoftened.

### 0079 — `இருபதாயிரம் பாடி வீடுகள்!`

`பாடி வீடுகள்` is retained as a governing martial image: more than twenty thousand branch organisations become military camps and more than one million members become soldiers guarding Anna's movement and flag. The translation does not reduce the title to administrative “branch offices.” Canonical PDF 294 controls the source forms `அறை கூவல்`, `நியாய நெஞ்சும்` and `வலிப்பு நோய் ஆவான்...`; the English also preserves the blood-vessel, convulsive-disease, fortress, hand-lamp and military-camp imagery.

### 0080 — `கொள்கை மலர்கள்`

Preserves the argument that movement office is labour rather than ornament, the requirements of endurance, patience, firmness and `தன்னலமற்ற-கண்ணயராத` labour, and the closing image in which the office-bearers blooming from twenty thousand branches become flowers gathered into a victory garland for Anna.
