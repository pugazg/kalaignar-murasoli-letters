## Review method

Each English translation was compared directly with the complete audited Tamil source reproduced in the bilingual letter and the canonical page sequence. The review checked title/date, salutation/closing, paragraph and argument sequence, substantive claims and attribution, quotations and intentional source-English, names, dates, figures, lists, rhetorical force, institutional terminology, and English wording that could broaden, narrow, soften or normalise the source.

The Tamil source had already passed the scan-based textual-fidelity audit. **No canonical Tamil page was modified in this alignment pass.**

### 3742 — *Unending Cruelties Against Eelam Tamils!*

- The follow-up on the 19 Dubai refugees, Antony's Lok Sabha answer, the 2008 fishermen agreement, Commonwealth/Human Rights Watch material, land-seizure claims and TESO resolutions were aligned against PDF 255–259.
- Two source-specific corrections were made:
  1. In Antony's answer, the Tamil says India advised the Sri Lankan government to instruct the **Sri Lankan military** (`இலங்கை ராணுவம்`) not to attack fishermen near the international maritime boundary and to act humanely. The earlier English had narrowed this to **“its Navy.”** It now follows the source term.
  2. In the quoted TESO resolution, `மாவட்ட ஆட்சியராகவும் அரசு நிர்வாகிகளாகவும்` is now rendered **“District Collectors and government administrators”** rather than the earlier generic **“district administrators and government officials.”**
- No Tamil-source change was made.

### 3743 — *Dream or Reality? Is This the Tale of “Donkey, Keep Quiet”?*

- Rule 110 procedure, the “Your Own Home” police-housing announcement, Assembly praise, power-project figures, Kudankulam passage, detailed substation list, village saying and Claudius dream story were aligned against PDF 260–266.
- Two English corrections were made:
  1. `போராட்டக்காரர்களை முதலில் உசுப்பி விட்டுவிட்டுப் பின்னர் அவர்களைப் பார்க்கக்கூட மறுத்த` is now rendered with its stronger source force as **“first stirring up those protesters and then later refusing even to meet them,”** replacing the softer “encouraged” / “not even granted a meeting.”
  2. The parenthetical Rule 110 jab about **not asking in which year the other projects will begin** has been moved back to its source position immediately after the Tiruvallur 400-kV substation announcement, before the Kayathar transmission-line item.
- The scan-proven title `கழுதையே` and the village saying remain unchanged.
- No Tamil-source change was made.

### 3744 — *Law and Order Turns to Dust in a State Ruled by a Woman!*

- The unusual source wording that local Malayali associations were consoling Jayalalithaa remains preserved rather than reassigned.
