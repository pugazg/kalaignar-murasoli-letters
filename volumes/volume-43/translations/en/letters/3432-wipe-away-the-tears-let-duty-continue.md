# 3432. Wipe Away the Tears! Let Duty Continue!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This translation follows the audited canonical Tamil and preserves the letter’s disaster-relief comparisons, historical recollections, quoted Anna passage, dates, figures, administrative sequence and closing exhortation. Claims and figures remain source-framed and are not reconciled with outside records.

**Tamil source:** [Letter 3432](../../../chapters/3432-kanneer-thudaithiduga-kadamai-thodarnthiduga.md)  
**Source pages:** [PDF 54](../../../pages/page-054.md)–[PDF 60](../../../pages/page-060.md)  
**Date:** 12 November 2009

**Udanpirappē,**

An accusation has been made against the DMK government that meetings are being held to discuss the relief work required for people affected by the heavy rain falling in Tamil Nadu, but that no action is being taken to carry out relief work.

Generally, when disasters such as cyclones, floods, droughts, earthquakes, tsunamis and tremors occur, human nature demands that all parties, setting aside political differences, work for the welfare of the people and to wipe away their suffering. One should not treat that as the moment to spend time finding fault with the ruling party and taking pleasure in doing so.

When the DMK was in opposition in Tamil Nadu, after the cyclone that struck Thanjavur district in 1952, the party resolved to collect funds for those affected, use the money to buy handloom dhotis and sarees and distribute them to the poor, and raise ₹25,000 for the purpose — a large sum at the time. The DMK also resolved that I should lead that work.

To raise the funds, my play *Thookku Medai* was staged in many places. In that play I acted as the schoolboy Pandian; Nadigar Thilagam Sivaji played Abhinayasundarar; friend C.V.M. Annamalai played Paramarthigan; and brother Arangannal and other DMK front-line members also acted. Nadigar Thilagam refused to receive any payment for acting in those performances held to raise party funds.

Although the DMK Executive Committee had decided to collect ₹25,000 for cyclone relief, more than ₹35,000 was collected. Handloom cloth was bought with that amount and, with a request that it be distributed by the government to the affected people, it was handed over on 18-6-1954 during Congress rule.

Here is a portion of a letter written by Anna in *Dravida Nadu* on 11-12-1955:

> “Thambi, no other issue comes to mind; sorrow pierces the whole heart.
>
> Whether the eyelids are open or closed, all that appears before us is torrential rain, cyclone wind, a raging sea, heaps of corpses, destruction, destruction! ******
>
> Thambi, our Chief Minister, Kamaraj, is among the affected people! That is where a responsible head of government ought to be! Yes, he is there surrounded by senior officials. When I think that he is intensely engaged in the task of wiping away the tears of the people who have suffered great devastation, I feel: O cruel sea that rose in fury, O demon wind that destroyed families, here our Chief Minister has come to give us comfort; he sees our tears — sheds his own tears — and pours consolation upon places surrounded by destruction. We are proud of Chief Minister Kamaraj’s sense of responsibility. Experts may say the scale is inadequate or the method deficient. But no one, ever, will forget that the Chief Minister’s heart is pure and that the concern he has shown at this time for the poor and humble is sincere.
>
> This is a stage at which everyone must work. We appeal: begin today itself! We assure you that whatever cooperation is asked of DMK comrades will be given.
>
> Comrades who are sobbing and bewildered! Our own kin! Set out saying, ‘Here is help, here, here!’ — and work!”

**Udanpirappē,** at that time the DMK was in opposition. I think about how Anna, instead of rejoicing at the destruction and throwing blame at the ruling party, wrote with such pride in praise of Chief Minister Kamaraj; how he asked DMK members, without regard to party differences, to engage in flood-relief work; and how DMK members accordingly cooperated with the Congress government and worked to wipe away suffering.

But what is the situation now?

They proudly issue statements saying that during the AIADMK government’s great flood in 2005, everyone was given ₹2,000 each as relief, as though nothing is being done under the DMK government. There is nothing to boast about in that. It was simply a government’s duty. Even so, the people of Tamil Nadu know very well how and for what that ₹2,000 was given then. In Chennai city, even those living on upper floors of multi-storeyed buildings who had not been affected in the slightest by the rain were then given ₹2,000 each. ₹148 crore was allocated as relief funds for Chennai city alone.

I also wish to compare this with whether relief was or was not provided after the DMK came to power in 2006, when Cyclone “Nisha” and heavy rains struck in 2008.

During the northeast monsoon in 2005, heavy rain occurred four times and 22 districts were affected. Likewise, because of excessive northeast-monsoon rain from 19.11.2008 to 28.11.2008 and Cyclone “Nisha,” 12 districts suffered severe damage. The DMK government immediately undertook relief work in those areas and provided the emergency assistance needed by the affected people.

To supervise this work, a Cabinet subcommittee headed by the then Minister for Rural Development and Local Administration, M.K. Stalin, was constituted. Tamil Nadu ministers personally visited the affected places and relief work was accelerated. On 7.12.2008, I convened a meeting of the Collectors of these 12 districts and reviewed the flood damage and relief work. It is noteworthy that in 2005, when even greater flood damage occurred, no meeting of District Collectors was convened for it.

During the flood under AIADMK rule in 2005, families of those who died were given relief of ₹50,000 each. Those who gave only ₹50,000 then are the very people who now recommend that ₹5 lakh each should be given under our government.

In 2005, 1,520 livestock deaths occurred and ₹86 lakh was paid as relief. By contrast, in 2008 under the DMK government, 5,982 livestock losses occurred and ₹1 crore 79 lakh was paid as relief.

For hut losses, ₹93 crore 7 lakh was paid as relief under the AIADMK government in 2005. Under the DMK government in 2008, ₹231 crore was paid.

As special relief assistance for houses affected by rain and surrounded by floodwater, ₹466 crore 97 lakh was paid in 22 districts under the AIADMK government in 2005. In 2008, under the DMK government, ₹402 crore was paid in just 12 districts.

For crop damage, ₹368 crore was paid under the AIADMK government in 2005, whereas ₹388 crore was paid under the DMK government in 2008. Adding all the relief assistance mentioned above, if ₹931 crore was paid under the AIADMK government in 2005 for losses across 22 districts, ₹1,027 crore was paid in 2008 for losses in only 12 districts. If this is kept in mind, one can understand that the DMK government has been second to none in flood-relief work.

Under the norms fixed by the Union Government, relief assistance to farmers affected by crop damage was ₹4,000 per hectare. In 2008, under the DMK government, this was raised to ₹7,500 per hectare. Further, under the crop-insurance scheme announced and put into practice by the DMK government in 2006–2007, registered farmers were given financial assistance of up to ₹10,000 for every affected hectare.

Therefore, without worrying about those who find fault with us and our government, we must continue the work of wiping away the people’s suffering.

When news came on 7-11-2009 that heavy rain had fallen in Chennai city and some parts of Tamil Nadu, although that day was a Saturday holiday, I contacted all the officials concerned by telephone, asked them to come to the Secretariat, and I too reached the office by 9.30 in the morning.

Before me, Deputy Chief Minister M.K. Stalin had already taken the Mayor, Corporation Commissioner and officials with him, personally visited all the rain-affected areas, noted the situation and returned to the Secretariat. The Chief Secretary had contacted the District Collectors through “video conferencing” and arrived with complete details. We all discussed the matter and directed that relief work be carried out on a war footing. Only after the lunch hour had passed did I return home.

It was not just that one day. As the rain continued in many areas, although 8-11-2009 was a Sunday, I went to the Secretariat, called all the officials concerned, discussed the progress of flood-relief work and also discussed what more should be done. In every district where damage had occurred, I instructed the respective district ministers to go there directly and engage in relief work.

Following reports that Nilgiris district had been severely affected by the cyclone rains, that transport had been cut off, landslides had occurred at more than a hundred places and more than 40 people had died, I went to the Secretariat on the morning of 10-11-2009 and discussed the matter with everyone. I then sent Agriculture Minister Veerapandi and Small Industries Minister Pongalur Palanisamy to hurry to Nilgiris district to inspect the affected places and accelerate relief work.

They went there accordingly. After inspecting the affected places, Minister Veerapandi Arumugam telephoned me at about 10 last night and explained the situation. District Minister Ramachandran, neighbouring-district Minister Vellakoil Saminathan, and Union Minister and Member of Parliament for that constituency A. Raja are staying there and taking part in relief work.

In accordance with the saying, “Let those who praise, praise; let those who heap dust and abuse, abuse; my debt is only to keep doing my work,” whatever anyone may say, we shall not worry about it and shall continue our work. The people understand us well. They are not concerned about the statements that keep blaming us. You too should not worry about them. Wipe away the tears of the suffering people! Let duty continue!

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**12-11-2009**

மாவட்டத்திற்கு பாதிக்கப்பட்ட பகுதிகளைப் பார்வையிடவும்,
நிவாரணப் பணிகளைத் துரிதப்படுத்தவும் விரைந்து சென்றிடுமாறு கூறி அனுப்பி வைத்தேன். அவர்களும் அவ்வாறே
சென்று பாதிக்கப்பட்ட பகுதிகளைப் பார்த்து விட்டு அமைச்சர்
வீரபாண்டி ஆறுமுகம் நேற்றிரவு 10 மணி அளவில் என்னோடு
தொலைபேசியில் தொடர்பு கொண்டு நிலவரங்களை எடுத்துக்
கூறினார். அந்த மாவட்ட அமைச்சர் ராமச் சந்திரனும், பக்கத்து
மாவட்ட அமைச்சரான வெள்ளக் கோவில் சாமிநாதனும், மத்திய
அமைச்சரும் அந்தத் தொகுதியின் நாடாளுமன்ற உறுப்பினருமான
ஆ.இராசாவும் அங்கேயே தங்கி நிவாரணப் பணிகளிலே
ஈடுபட்டுள்ளார்கள்.

“போற்றுவோர் போற்றட்டும், புழுதிவாரி தூற்றுவோர்
தூற்றட்டும், என் கடன் பணி செய்து கிடப்பதே” என்ற
மொழிக்கிணங்க யார் என்ன சொன்னபோதிலும், அதைப்பற்றி
கவலைப்படாமல் நம்முடைய பணிகளை தொடருவோம். மக்கள்
நம்மை நன்றாகப் புரிந்து கொண்டிருக்கிறார்கள். நம்மைப் பழி
கூறி வரும் அறிக்கைகளைப் பற்றி அவர்கள் கவலைப்படவில்லை;
நீயும் அதுபற்றிக் கவலைப்படாமல் துயர்படும் மக்களின் கண்ணீர்
துடைத்திடுக! கடமை தொடர்ந்திடுக!

அன்புள்ள,
