## Source-completeness review

For all three pilot letters:

- the English title, date and PDF/printed page range match the chapter record;
- the standard translator's note is reproduced without drift from the approved Volume 49 wording;
- `Udanpirappē` is retained;
- every substantive Tamil paragraph has an English counterpart;
- quoted statements, newspaper material, figures, dates and rhetorical questions are represented;
- the closing is rendered `With affection, M.K.`;
- the complete audited Tamil is reproduced beneath `Original Tamil — மூலத் தமிழ்` in PDF-page order;
- source anomalies are documented rather than silently repaired in English; and
- no canonical Tamil page was changed as a consequence of translation.

The three translation files are therefore marked **source-checked**. Their `bilingual_alignment_status` remains **pending** until the separate alignment QA stage.

## Style decisions confirmed

The Volume 49 style lock transfers cleanly to Volume 48:

1. **Thought before ornament.** English follows the sequence of the Tamil argument rather than rewriting for literary effect.
2. **Political force retained.** Sarcasm, accusation, repetition, direct questions and irony are not softened.
3. **Attribution retained.** Claims from fishermen, newspapers, interviewees and political speakers remain explicitly attributed.
4. **`Udanpirappē` retained.** It is not replaced with “brother”, “sister” or “comrade”.
5. **Closing fixed.** `அன்புள்ள, மு.க.` is rendered `With affection, M.K.`.
6. **Indian public-language units retained.** `lakh` and `crore` remain in English.
7. **Rule 110 retained.** The procedural label is not paraphrased away.
8. **Honorifics handled contextually.** `Perasiriyar` is retained where it functions as the established personal honorific; `Amma` is retained where the source wording or irony depends upon it.
9. **Minimal notes.** Notes explain only source anomalies, culturally difficult idiom or institutional terms that materially affect understanding.
10. **Tamil remains authoritative.** English never normalises a scan-visible source form or resolves a source inconsistency silently.

## Pilot-specific issues resolved

### Letter 3735

The source gives `13-5-2006 முதல் இதுவரை` for one statistical period but then gives an aggregate ending `21-12-2006`. The translation preserves both and records the inconsistency in a note rather than reconciling it.

### Letter 3743

The village saying `மலைக்கே வாழ்க்கைப்படப் போகிறேன்; கழுதையே சும்மா இரு` is translated close to the source image and explained minimally in a note. The source name `கிளாடியஸ்` is retained as **Claudius** without external identification or correction.

### Letter 3756

The source reports **100 acres** in the farmers' quoted objection and **65 acres** in the later newspaper account of the Vandalur proposal. Both are preserved and the difference is documented rather than harmonised.
