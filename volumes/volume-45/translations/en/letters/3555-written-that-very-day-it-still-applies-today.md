# 3555. Written That Very Day; It Still Applies Today!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This translation is intended to carry Kalaignar's voice into clear, contemporary English rather than recast the letter as literary or academic prose. It preserves the source's argument, political directness, rhetorical questions, repetition, irony, factual detail, and paragraph order. Names, dates, figures, quotations, and intentional English expressions are retained. Where Tamil idiom cannot be reproduced literally without sounding unnatural, the English follows its sense and rhetorical force without adding claims absent from the source. The original Tamil is reproduced in full below the translation and remains the authoritative text. `Udanpirappē` is retained in Tamil transliteration rather than flattened into “brother,” “sister,” or “comrade.” Literally evoking “one born alongside me,” Kalaignar uses it as a distinctive address of shared identity, equality, affection, and solidarity within the movement.

**Date:** 17 April 2011

**Udanpirappē,**

As soon as the election date was announced, on **3-3-2011** I wrote a letter under the title **“The Commission's Order Has Been Issued!”** In that letter I wrote:

> “For the next two months, this is a period in which each of us must work without a break! A time when we must labour without rest! The time has come to learn the verdict on how far the immeasurable and valuable work carried out by the DMK government for the people of Tamil Nadu over the past five years is regarded and appreciated by the people of Tamil Nadu. There are only **42 days** left before the people of Tamil Nadu go to vote! There are only **17 days** left before nominations are filed! Within that period, the number of constituencies for the friendly parties must be settled. Then it must be discussed and decided which constituencies go to each party. After that, each party must call and speak with all those who applied for nomination, decide whom to field as its candidates, and announce them. The election manifesto must be written. Those announced as candidates by the parties must file their nominations. After that, in their respective constituencies, they must meet the voters and ask them to vote. Party leaders and important campaigners must take part in election campaigning. There are only **17 days** for all this.”

I had written that. Just as I wrote, all the parties worked day and night, and the election polling itself has now been completed. But the Election Commission has given all of us an interval of another month before the counting of votes. I had referred to that too in my first letter. It was as follows:

> “After casting the votes, we must wait for one month before counting takes place. Why such an interval? That is the Election Commission's decision! The day for voting is **April 13**. The day on which those votes are to be counted is **May 13**. Why a one-month interval? We are told that because elections are being held in six phases in West Bengal, votes here too must be counted only after elections have been completed there; that is the election rule. But why leave a one-month interval and, in such haste, conduct the polling itself on April 13, putting everyone to difficulty? I do not know the reason! One might suspect that the Election Commission would have announced the election date only after consulting the State governments. Not so! Because the Election Commission is not under anyone's control! It is an independent body. It has no need to ask anyone.
>
> “According to the decision now announced, the election will be held on **April 13**. After that, the votes will be counted on **May 13**. If the votes are counted on May 13, it will take a day to complete the count. The results will be known only on **May 14**. But the present Legislative Assembly will come to an end, and the next Assembly must begin on **May 17**. That is because the term of the present Assembly ends on **May 16**.
>
> “Therefore, by May 17 a new Council of Ministers must have been formed in Tamil Nadu. On May 14 it will be announced who have been elected as Members of the Legislative Assembly; it will be determined which party has obtained a majority; the elected MLAs of that party must then meet and choose one among them as their legislative-party leader—that is, as Chief Minister. He must meet the Governor; the Governor must ask him to form the ministry; the ministers must then be announced; those ministers must take the oath of office before the Governor; and the new Legislative Assembly must be convened by May 17. The only days available for all this are **May 15 and May 16**—just two days. Can so many tasks be completed within that time? Yet the Election Commission has announced that they must be completed!
>
> “If one asks whether the Election Commission could have failed to think about all this, surely it would have thought about it. It is only after thinking about it that these election dates have been announced.
>
> “If the new Legislative Assembly need be convened only on May 17, why must the election be held in such haste on April 13 itself? Could not a little more time have been given to those contesting the election and to the political parties, with the election date announced for the end of April or the first week of May—as everyone had expected? What compulsion was there to fix the election date in such haste for April 13 itself? Even if the Election Commission is an institution that no one can question, should not Caesar's wife be above suspicion?”

Udanpirappē,

Just as I wrote then, the verdicts given by the people of Tamil Nadu have been securely locked away in rooms until **May 13**, with three-tier security arrangements. Until then, no important decisions will be taken on behalf of the government. No, no—they must not be taken. If a sudden emergency arises in which some important decision, some policy decision, has to be taken, what is to be done then? Only the Election Commission knows! What is the position of the officials? It is the very state that is called **Trishanku heaven**! Newspapers are already reporting that the Secretariat lies deserted and that officials are preparing to go on holiday with their families.

That is not all. This is also the time when important works that must be carried out in the country on behalf of the government have to be completed. Only if all those works are properly carried out will agricultural work be able to proceed in an orderly way during the southwest monsoon season due to begin in June. It is said that the Election Commission has advised officials that they should not even inspect maintenance works! Because of that, there is a condition in which even basic works cannot be completed.

A government elected by the people and its ministers are entitled to discharge their responsibilities for five years—that is, until they are again elected by the people and the result is announced. In what way, then, is it just to prevent them from carrying out their duties? Those who have studied law and the Election Commission itself must find a proper solution to all this!

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**17-4-2011**

அனைவரும் எதிர்பார்த்ததைப் போல தேர்தல் தேதியை
அறிவித்திருக்கலாம் அல்லவா? ஏப்ரல் மாதம் 13ஆம்
தேதியே அவசர அவசரமாக தேர்தல் தேதியை நிர்ணயம்
செய்ய வேண்டிய கட்டாயம் என்ன? தேர்தல் ஆணையம்
யாராலும் கேள்வி கேட்க முடியாத அமைப்பு என்றாலும்
- சீசரின் மனைவி சந்தேகத்திற்கு அப்பாற்பட்டவராக
இருக்க வேண்டும் அல்லவா?”

உடன்பிறப்பே,

நான் இவ்வாறு எழுதியவாறே, தமிழ்நாட்டு மக்கள் அளித்த
தீர்ப்புகள் - மே திங்கள் 13ஆம் தேதி வரை அறைகளிலே
பத்திரமாகப் பூட்டி வைக்கப்பட்டு மூன்றடுக்குப் பாதுகாப்பு
ஏற்பாடுகள் செய்யப்பட்டுள்ளன. அதுவரை அரசின் சார்பில்
எந்தவிதமான முக்கிய முடிவுகளும் எடுக்கப்பட மாட்டாது. அல்ல,
அல்ல, எடுக்கப்படக்கூடாது. திடீரென ஏதாவது ஒரு முக்கிய முடிவு,
கொள்கை முடிவு எடுக்கப்பட வேண்டிய நெருக்கடி ஏற்பட்டு
விட்டால் அப்போது என்ன செய்வது? தேர்தல் ஆணையத்திற்கே
வெளிச்சம்! அதிகாரிகளின் நிலை என்ன? திரிசங்கு சொர்க்கம்
என்பார்களே, அந்த நிலைதான்! இப்போதே பத்திரிகைகள் எல்லாம்
தலைமைச் செயலகம் வெறிச்சோடிக் கிடப்பதாகவும் - அதிகாரிகள்
எல்லாம் குடும்பத்துடன் விடுமுறையில் செல்லவிருப்பதாகவும்
செய்திகளை வெளியிட்டுள்ளன.

அது மாத்திரமல்ல, அரசின் சார்பில் நாட்டில் நடைபெற்றாக
வேண்டிய முக்கியமான பணிகள் எல்லாம் நிறைவேற்றப்பட
வேண்டிய நேரம் இது. அந்தப் பணிகள் எல்லாம் முறையாக
நடைபெற்றால்தான் ஜூன் மாதத்தில் தொடங்க விருக்கும்
தென்மேற்குப் பருவக் காற்று காலத்தின் போது வேளாண்மைப்
பணிகளை ஒழுங்காகச் செய்திட முடியும். அதிகாரிகள் பராமரிப்புப்
பணிகளை ஆய்வுகூட செய்யக்கூடாது என்று தேர்தல் ஆணையம்
அறிவுரை கூறியுள்ளதாம்! அதனால் அடிப்படைப் பணிகளைக்கூட
நிறைவேற்ற முடியாத நிலைமை உள்ளது.

அவர்களது பொறுப்புகளை நிறைவேற்ற தகுதி படைத்தவர்கள்.
ஆனால் அவர்களின் பணிகளை நிறைவேற்ற முடியாமல் தடுப்பது

ஆணையமும்தான் இதற்கெல்லாம் ஒரு முறையான தீர்வினைக்
காண வேண்டும்!
