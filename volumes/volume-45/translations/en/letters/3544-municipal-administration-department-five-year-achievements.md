# 3544. Five-Year Achievements of the Municipal Administration Department!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This translation is intended to carry Kalaignar's voice into clear, contemporary English rather than recast the letter as literary or academic prose. It preserves the source's argument, political directness, rhetorical questions, repetition, irony, factual detail, and paragraph order. Names, dates, figures, quotations, and intentional English expressions are retained. Where Tamil idiom cannot be reproduced literally without sounding unnatural, the English follows its sense and rhetorical force without adding claims absent from the source. The original Tamil is reproduced in full below the translation and remains the authoritative text. `Udanpirappē` is retained in Tamil transliteration rather than flattened into “brother,” “sister,” or “comrade.” Literally evoking “one born alongside me,” Kalaignar uses it as a distinctive address of shared identity, equality, affection, and solidarity within the movement.

**Udanpirappē,**

I continue to write about the achievements and schemes carried out by the DMK government in the Municipal Administration Department during the past five years.

### Thanjavur Big Temple — 1,000th anniversary; Thanjavur city development scheme

To mark the completion of 1,000 years since the construction of the Thanjavur Big Temple, the government organised the anniversary celebration and undertook many basic-facility works in Thanjavur city with an allocation of ₹25.19 crore.

### Directorate of Town Panchayats

### Town Panchayat administration

Tamil Nadu was the first State to create the category of local body called **Town Panchayats**.

### Increase in financial-authority limits

The authority limit for schemes sanctioned by Special Grade and Selection Grade Town Panchayat Councils was raised from ₹2 lakh to ₹8 lakh, and that for First Grade and Second Grade Town Panchayats from ₹1 lakh to ₹4 lakh. Similarly, the authority limit of the Assistant Director of Town Panchayats was raised from ₹5 lakh to ₹20 lakh; that of District Collectors from ₹10 lakh to ₹30 lakh; and that of the Director of Town Panchayats from ₹1 crore to ₹3 crore, with effect from 12 April 2008.

Technical-sanction limits were also increased: the Executive Engineer's limit from ₹30 lakh to ₹40 lakh, the Assistant Executive Engineer's from ₹15 lakh to ₹20 lakh, and the financial limit for technical approval by an Executive Engineer in Town Panchayats from ₹40 lakh to ₹50 lakh.

### Anaithu Peruratchi Anna Marumalarchi Thittam

A special scheme called the **Anaithu Peruratchi Anna Marumalarchi Thittam** was announced in the Legislative Assembly on 11 April 2007 for implementation over four years to strengthen the infrastructure required by Town Panchayats. Orders were issued on 6 July 2007, and the scheme was launched at Mamallapuram on 3 September 2007. Since then, it has been implemented in all Town Panchayats at an estimated cost of ₹280.50 crore and basic facilities have been improved.

Under the Urban Infrastructure Development Scheme for Small and Medium Towns, 479 road and drainage works were undertaken in 28 Town Panchayats at an estimated cost of ₹31.84 crore and have been completed.

### Basic Services for the Urban Poor and Integrated Housing and Slum Development Scheme

Under the Integrated Housing and Slum Development Scheme and the Basic Services for the Urban Poor scheme, 7,248 houses were taken up in 42 Town Panchayats—including Vilankurichi, Periyanaickenpalayam, No. 4 Veerapandi, Veerakolam, Kannampalayam, Idigarai, Madukkarai, Vettapatti, Savanampatti, Vellalur, Gangavalli, Mohanur, Sirapalli, Alampalayam, Tholur, Veeraganur, R. Pudupatti, Aval Poondurai, Uthukuli, Pallapalayam, Kodumudi, Lakkampatti, P. Mettupalayam, Koogalur, P.N. Patti and Karuppur—at an estimated cost of ₹81.44 crore. So far, 4,190 houses have been constructed; construction of the remaining houses is progressing at various stages.

### Urban poverty-eradication measures

### Swarna Jayanti Sahari Rozgar Yojana Scheme

The Swarna Jayanti Sahari Rozgar Yojana Scheme was introduced from 1 December 1997. With financial participation by the Union and State Governments, its sub-schemes—Urban Self-Employment Scheme, Urban Women's Self-Help Scheme, skill-development training to create employment for the urban poor, urban community-development infrastructure and Urban Wage Employment Scheme—have been implemented at an estimated cost of ₹55.32 crore.

### Tsunami relief and rehabilitation

### Tsunami Emergency Assistance Project

In 19 Town Panchayats in five coastal districts affected by the tsunami, road-repair and building-repair works were completed at a cost of ₹63.67 crore.

### Livelihood activities

To improve the livelihoods of tsunami-affected families, 3,387 self-help groups with 60,966 members were created in 19 Town Panchayats at a cost of ₹12.78 crore. Skill-development training was provided to group members and financial assistance given to create durable assets. In addition, 969 persons with disabilities received grants totalling ₹96.90 lakh to improve their livelihoods.

For 22,891 beneficiaries who received economic assistance and revolving funds under this scheme, the government allocated ₹24.33 lakh for issuing biometric identity cards; ₹11.42 lakh of that amount has been spent.

### Underground sewerage schemes completed from 2006 to 2011

Six underground sewerage schemes have been completed at an estimated total cost of ₹365.42 crore for the benefit of 16.71 lakh people. They include: Karur and Inam-Karur Municipality, ₹38.50 crore for 1.21 lakh people; Thanjavur Municipality, ₹77 crore for 2.16 lakh people; Mayiladuthurai Municipality, ₹46.20 crore for 84,290 people; Tiruchirappalli Municipal Corporation, ₹132 crore for 7.46 lakh people; Tirunelveli Municipal Corporation, ₹66 crore for 4.10 lakh people; and omitted areas of Udhagamandalam Municipality, ₹5.72 crore for **94 lakh people**, as printed in the audited source.

Apart from these, underground sewerage schemes have also been completed in Kumbakonam, Alandur, Valasaravakkam and Madurai.

In addition, 23 underground sewerage schemes costing ₹806.55 crore have been taken up for implementation in the current financial year.

### Underground sewerage schemes in small and medium towns

Under the Urban Infrastructure Development Scheme for Small and Medium Towns, underground sewerage schemes were sanctioned in four Municipalities at an estimated cost of ₹136.14 crore. The Maraimalai Nagar scheme is nearing completion. Schemes in Ariyalur and Udumalpet Municipalities, sanctioned at an estimated cost of ₹66.83 crore, are at an initial stage.

The Avadi Municipality underground sewerage scheme, sanctioned by this government at an estimated cost of ₹158.05 crore and now under way, is scheduled for completion in August 2011; the Tambaram Municipality scheme costing ₹160.97 crore in May 2012; the Maduravoyal Municipality scheme costing ₹60.01 crore in August 2012; the Thirumazhisai Town Panchayat scheme costing ₹20.47 crore in February 2012; and the Porur Town Panchayat scheme costing ₹38.29 crore in July 2012.

### Ambattur underground sewerage scheme

Under the second phase of the Ambattur Municipality underground sewerage scheme, work to construct a sewage-pumping station and force mains was begun in August 2007 at an estimated cost of ₹7.50 crore. The work was completed and the scheme brought into use in August 2009.

For areas omitted from the first-phase Ambattur underground sewerage scheme, a work order for ₹35.86 crore was issued on 22 June 2010. The work now in progress is scheduled for completion in July 2012.

### Tiruvottiyur underground sewerage scheme

Work to improve and expand the sewerage system in Tiruvottiyur Municipality is being carried out in five packages with assistance from the Tamil Nadu Urban Development Project, at a revised estimated cost of ₹87.63 crore. The work began in May 2007 and is scheduled for completion in December 2012.

### Pallavapuram underground sewerage scheme

The underground sewerage scheme for Pallavapuram Municipality has been completed at a cost of ₹72.10 crore.

### Madhavaram underground sewerage scheme

The underground sewerage scheme for Madhavaram Municipality is under way at an estimated cost of ₹37.81 crore and is planned for completion in March 2012.

Apart from these, the Ambattur Municipality underground sewerage scheme, Section 3, is under way at a revised estimate of ₹155.22 crore and is scheduled for completion in July 2012; the Ullagaram-Puzhuthivakkam Municipality scheme costing ₹28.08 crore in January 2013; the Perungudi Town Panchayat scheme costing ₹20.19 crore in November 2012; and the Pallikaranai Town Panchayat scheme costing ₹58.61 crore in January 2013.

Under the Jawaharlal Nehru National Urban Renewal Scheme, underground sewerage schemes are being implemented in Porur and Thirumazhisai Town Panchayats within the Chennai Metropolitan Development Authority area at an estimated cost of ₹58.76 crore, in Perungudi Town Panchayat at ₹20.19 crore, and in Pallikaranai Town Panchayat at ₹58.61 crore. Detailed project reports are also being prepared for implementation in Nandambakkam, Sholinganallur, Meenambakkam, Chinnasekkadu and Puzhal Town Panchayats.

Approval has been given and work begun on underground sewerage schemes at Mamallapuram Town Panchayat at an estimated cost of ₹6.08 crore and Tiruchendur Town Panchayat at ₹14.48 crore.

### Providing underground sewerage connections to houses

Through underground sewerage schemes completed so far, 1,70,477 houses have been provided underground sewerage connections.

### Tamil Nadu Urban Finance and Infrastructure Development Corporation

### Jawaharlal Nehru National Urban Renewal Scheme — Urban Infrastructure and Governance Scheme

Under this scheme, 47 projects costing ₹5,134.05 crore have been sanctioned for Chennai, Madurai and Coimbatore. The Union Government's share for these 47 projects is ₹2,162.72 crore and the State Government's share ₹881.78 crore. Of these shares, the Union Government has so far released ₹933.16 crore and the State Government ₹373.88 crore, a total of ₹1,307.04 crore. Of the 47 sanctioned projects, nine have so far been completed; the others are at various stages of progress.

### Urban Infrastructure Scheme for Small and Medium Towns

The State-Level Sanctioning Committee has sanctioned 122 projects at a project cost of ₹882.72 crore. The Union Government's share is ₹706.18 crore and the State Government's share ₹88.27 crore. So far, the Union Government has released ₹559.64 crore and the State Government ₹69.96 crore, a total of ₹629.60 crore. Of these 122 projects, 88 have been completed and the others are at various stages of progress.

### Metropolitan Infrastructure Development Fund Scheme

Under this scheme, 28 projects have been sanctioned for ₹662.42 crore. Of ₹198.88 crore released, five projects have been completed; the others are at various stages of progress.

### Chennai Corporation

### Flyovers

To reduce the growing traffic congestion in Chennai city, the flyover built at the junction of North Usman Road and Mahalingapuram Road at a cost of ₹9.72 crore was opened on 30 March 2008; the flyover at the junction of Usman Road and Duraisamy Road costing ₹19.80 crore on 14 August 2008; Kalaivanar Flyover at the junction of Gopathi Narayana Road and Thirumalai Road costing ₹16.50 crore on 29 December 2008; G.K. Moopanar Flyover at the junction of Turnbulls Road and Cenotaph Road costing ₹19.93 crore on 11 December 2009; Alandur Abraham Flyover across the Adyar River on Alandur Road costing ₹6.03 crore on 11 December 2009; and the C.B. Pavazhavan vehicular subway on Jones Road costing ₹4.33 crore on 11 December 2009. They are now in use.

### Murasoli Maran Flyover — Perambur

Although work on the Perambur railway flyover, a long-standing demand of the people of North Chennai, had begun in 1999, the work was halted during the previous AIADMK government and people living in the Perambur area suffered greatly. After this government was formed in 2006, construction of the bridge was resumed in June 2008 at a cost of ₹51 crore. The work was completed in 21 months and the bridge was opened on 28 March 2010.

In addition, work is under way on a flyover across the railway level crossing at Rangarajapuram at a cost of ₹15.75 crore; a vehicular subway at Villivakkam railway level crossing No. 2 at ₹13.39 crore; and a vehicular subway across the railway level crossing at Maniyakkara Chathira Street at ₹10.55 crore. These works will be completed shortly.

### Storm-water drains

During the past four years, the Chennai Corporation's Storm Water Drain Department has constructed 158.61 kilometres of storm-water drains at a cost of ₹89.68 crore in various areas where rainwater stagnates.

To spare Chennai residents the hardship caused by flooding during the rainy season, Union Government approval has been obtained under the Jawaharlal Nehru National Urban Renewal Scheme for a ₹1,447.91-crore project to construct small and large storm-water drains. Among the schemes sanctioned for implementation by Chennai Corporation to date, this is the largest.

Of the ₹814.88 crore allocated to Chennai Corporation under this project, work covers construction of 320.82 kilometres of new storm-water drains, renewal of 183.08 kilometres of damaged drains, and improvement of 28.64 kilometres of drains. The source records that **`ஒப்பங்கள்` have been received** for undertaking these works.

Work orders have been issued and work is now in progress in the twelve catchment areas included in the project: Kolathur, Velachery, Royapuram, Captain Cotton Canal, Cooum, Mambalam-Nandanam, North Buckingham Canal, Central Buckingham Canal, South Buckingham Canal, Otteri Nullah, Adyar and Virugambakkam-Arumbakkam.

Under the Marina beautification scheme, equipment has been purchased for cleaning the promenade: a machine for scrubbing away dirt, equipment for levelling lawns, a machine introduced for the first time on Marina Beach to sift and clean the sand, and two small ride-on mechanical sweepers for cleaning the promenade. Beautification work on the Marina Beach area is being carried out effectively.

### அம்பத்தூர் பாதாள சாக்கடைத் திட்டம்

அம்பத்தூர் நகராட்சியில் பாதாள சாக்கடைத் திட்டம், இரண்டாம் கட்டப் பணியின் கீழ் கழிவு நீரேற்று நிலையம் மற்றும் கழிவுநீர் கொண்டு செல்லும் விசைக் குழாய்கள் அமைக்கும் பணிகள் ரூ.7.50 கோடி மதிப்பீட்டில் ஆகஸ்ட் 2007ல் தொடங்கப்பட்டன. இப்பணிகள் முடிவடைந்து ஆகஸ்ட் 2009ல் திட்டம் பயன்பாட்டிற்கு கொண்டு வரப்பட்டது.

முதற்கட்ட அம்பத்தூர் பாதாள சாக்கடைத் திட்டத்தில் விடுபட்ட பகுதிகளுக்கு ரூ.35.86 கோடி மதிப்பீட்டில் 22.6.2010 அன்று பணி ஆணை வழங்கப்பட்டு நடைபெற்று வரும் பணிகள் 2012 ஜூலையில் முடிவடையும்.

### சென்னை மாநகராட்சி

### முரசொலி மாறன் மேம்பாலம் - பெரம்பூர்

வட சென்னை மக்களின் நீண்ட நாள் கோரிக்கையான பெரம்பூர் ரயில்வே மேம்பாலப் பணி 1999ஆம் ஆண்டில் தொடங்கப்பட்டிருந்த போதிலும், முந்தைய அ.தி.மு.க. ஆட்சி காலத்தில் இப்பணி முடக்கப்பட்டு, பெரம்பூர் பகுதி வாழ்மக்கள் பெரும் வேதனைகளுக்கு ஆட்பட்டு வந்தனர். 2006ஆம் ஆண்டில் இந்த அரசு அமைந்தபின் 51 கோடி ரூபாய்ச் செலவில் 2008 ஜூன் திங்களில் இந்தப் பாலத்தை மீண்டும் கட்டத் தொடங்கி 21 மாத காலத்தில் பணிகள் முடிந்து, 28.3.2010 அன்று திறந்து வைக்கப்பட்டது.

மேலும், ரூ.15.75 கோடி செலவில் ரங்கராஜபுரம் இரயில்வே சந்திக்கடவின் குறுக்கே மேம்பாலம், ரூ.13.39 கோடி செலவில் வில்லிவாக்கம் இரயில்வே சந்திக்கடவு - 2ல் வாகன சுரங்கப்பாதை, ரூ.10.55 கோடி செலவில் மணியக்கார சத்திரத் தெருவில் இரயில்வே சந்திக்கடவின் குறுக்கே வாகனச் சுரங்கப் பாதை ஆகிய பணிகள் நடைபெறுகின்றன; இப்பணிகள் விரைவில் முடிவடையும்.

### மழைநீர் வடிகால்வாய்

சென்னை மாநகராட்சியில் கடந்த நான்கு ஆண்டுகளில் மழைநீர் தேக்கம் உள்ள பல்வேறு பகுதிகளில் மழைநீர் வடிகால் துறை மூலம், 158.61 கி.மீ. நீளத்திற்கு ரூ.89.68 கோடியில் மழைநீர் வடிகால்வாய்கள் கட்டப்பட்டுள்ளன.

சென்னைவாழ் மக்களுக்கு மழைக்காலத்தில் உண்டாகும் வெள்ள பாதிப்பினால் ஏற்படும் துன்பத்தைத் தவிர்க்கும் விதமாக ஜவஹர்லால் நேரு தேசிய நகர்ப்புற புனரமைப்புத் திட்டத்தின்

மூலமாக சிறிய மற்றும் பெரிய மழைநீர் வடிகால்வாய்கள் அமைப்பதற்கு ரூ.1447.91 கோடி திட்டத்திற்கு மத்திய அரசின் ஒப்புதல் பெறப்பட்டுள்ளது. இந்நாள் வரை சென்னை மாநகராட்சி செயல்படுத்திட அனுமதிக்கப்பட்ட திட்டங்களில் இதுவே பெருந்திட்டமாகும்.

இத்திட்டத்தின்கீழ் சென்னை மாநகராட்சிக்கு ஒதுக்கப்பட்ட ரூ.814.88 கோடி மதிப்பீட்டுத் தொகையில் 320.82 கி.மீ. நீளத்திற்கு புதிய மழைநீர் வடிகால்வாய்கள் கட்டுதல், 183.08 கி.மீ. நீளத்திற்குப் பழுதடைந்த கால்வாய்களைப் புதுப்பித்தல், 28.64 கி.மீ. நீளத்திற்கு கால்வாய் மேம்பாட்டுப் பணிகள் ஆகியவற்றை மேற்கொள்ள ஒப்பங்கள் பெறப்பட்டுள்ளன.

இத்திட்டத்தில் அடங்கிய கொளத்தூர், வேளச்சேரி, இராயபுரம், கேப்டன் காட்டன் கால்வாய், கூவம், மாம்பலம் - நந்தனம், வடக்கு பக்கிங்காம் கால்வாய், மத்திய பக்கிங்காம் கால்வாய், தெற்கு பக்கிங்காம் கால்வாய், ஓட்டேரி நல்லா, அடையாறு, விருகம்பாக்கம், அரும்பாக்கம் ஆகிய 12 நீர்ப்பிடிப்புப் பகுதிகளுக்குப் பணி ஆணைகள் வழங்கப்பட்டுப் பணிகள் தற்போது நடைபெறுகின்றன.

மெரினா அழகுபடுத்தும் திட்டத்தின்கீழ் நடைபாதையைச் சுத்தம் செய்வதற்கு, அழுக்கைத் தேய்த்து சுத்தப்படுத்தும் இயந்திரம், புல்தரையைச் சமப்படுத்துவதற்கான உபகரணம், மெரினா கடற்கரையில் முதன்முதலாக மணற்பரப்பில் மணலை ஜலித்து சுத்தம் செய்வதற்கான இயந்திரம், மெரினா கடற்கரையின் நடைபாதையைச் சுத்தம் செய்வதற்கு அமர்ந்து இயக்கும் சிறிய வகையிலான 2 இயந்திரப் பெருக்கிகள் ஆகியன கொள்முதல் செய்யப்பட்டு மெரினா கடற்கரைப்பகுதி எழிலூட்டும் பணி சிறப்பாக மேற்கொள்ளப்பட்டு வருகிறது.
