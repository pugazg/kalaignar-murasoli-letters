## Review method

Each English translation was compared directly with the complete audited Tamil source, using the canonical page files and the Tamil reproduced in the bilingual letter file. The review checked:

1. title, salutation, closing and date;
2. paragraph and argumentative sequence;
3. every substantive claim and attribution;
4. quotations and reported speech;
5. names, dates, counts, monetary figures and institutional terms;
6. list completeness and item order;
7. rhetorical questions, irony, repetition, proverbs and political intensity; and
8. English wording that could accidentally broaden, narrow, normalise or change responsibility.

The Tamil source had already passed the scan-based textual-fidelity audit. **No canonical Tamil page was modified in this alignment pass.**

## Results by letter

### 3706 — *Should India Join the U.N.’s List—or Not?*

- The historical examples, death-penalty argument, TADA/Supreme Court sequence, nine-year imprisonment discussion, execution dates, DMK commutation references and U.N. figures were aligned against PDF 24–29.
- Two English phrases had strengthened the source beyond what it says. `குற்றச்சாட்டுக்கு உரியவர்கள்` was restored from **“being guilty”** to **“facing extremely grave charges”**, and `குற்றம் சாற்றப் பெற்ற ஒருவர்` was restored from **“convicted of an offence”** to **“accused of an offence.”**
- The correction preserves the source's distinction between accusation and proved guilt.

### 3707 — *Do All These Look Like Praise?*

- Every quoted response from Vijayakanth, Ramadoss, the CPI(M), the CPI, M.R. Sivasamy, P. Shanmugam, Durai Manickam, Kalaignar's own statement and the *Dinamalar* report was checked, including all acreage, relief and beneficiary figures.
- One silent normalisation was removed. In P. Shanmugam's statement the audited Tamil says `உற்பத்தி பலமடங்கு உயர்ந்துள்ள நிலையில்`; the earlier English supplied **“production costs”**. The translation now retains the source as **“production has increased several-fold.”**
- The repeated “is this praise?” irony and the final video-conferencing criticism remain aligned.

### 3708 — *Does Everything the Winner Says Become “Scripture”?*

- The *Dinamani* questions, Cauvery Tribunal chronology, 1991/1998/2002/2003 dates, Authority and Monitoring Committee meeting counts, Jayalalithaa quotations, exact 1998 English statement, hunger-strike material and final rhetorical conclusion were checked against PDF 36–43.

- The source's internally awkward legal phrasing is deliberately preserved rather than corrected from outside knowledge.
