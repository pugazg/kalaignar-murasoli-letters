**Udanpirappē,**

In the villages they say: “It is foolish to keep bending as one is struck; to keep striking because one keeps bending is still greater foolishness.” In the same way, today’s rulers think they can keep striking us and make us keep bending. Even patience has a limit. The people have voted for the AIADMK. Let them remain in office and govern freely. Democracy permits that. But if, merely because they have taken office, they think the Opposition must be suppressed, crushed and destroyed and that they may behave as dictators, their own party people may tolerate it. They must not imagine that everyone will tolerate it forever.

Thambi Veerapandi Arumugam, Secretary of the Salem District Kazhagam, is about seventy-five years old. He is one who works intensely for the Kazhagam, one who does not fear repression, one who personally faced even the cruelties of the Emergency. He is a heart patient. About a year ago, when he was in Apollo Hospital, I went personally two or three times to see him. He survived. How many cases have been put upon him?

No sooner had the AIADMK come to office than, on 15 July 2011, the first case was registered under eight sections alleging fraud concerning land in Angammal Colony. On 17 July 2011, another case was registered under nine sections alleging fraud concerning the Salem Premier Roller Mill land. On 29 July 2011, police registered a case under five sections alleging occupation of land belonging to one Balamohanraj of Dasanaickenpatti; on 30 July they arrested Veerapandi Arumugam and lodged him in Coimbatore prison.

On 4 September 2011, police registered a case under eight sections alleging occupation of land belonging to Coimbatore jewellery owners, and when he came out of Coimbatore prison they arrested him again for a second time. On 6 October 2011, an Agro Co-operative Society land-fraud case was registered under fourteen sections. In that case former MLA Veerapandi Rajendran was named as the first accused; Veerapandi Arumugam’s name was not in the First Information Report, but was added during the later investigation.

At 12.45 in the night on 2 June 2012, rowdies entered Angammal Colony, smashed huts and set them on fire. Police registered a case against Veerapandi Arumugam alleging that he had joined the rowdies in a conspiracy, arrested him in Chennai on June 3 and lodged him in Vellore prison. It is in this case that, on 18 June 2012, an order was issued to detain him under the Goondas Act.

In any one of these cases, has a trial taken place, have witnesses been examined and has a Court ruled that he is guilty? Why these cruelties before that? Let us even assume he committed a wrong. Let us agree that action according to law may be taken. But knowing that he is a heart patient, what cruelty is it to arrest him in Salem, put him in a van and take him from there to Coimbatore, bring him back by van to Salem, and then, after the Court grants bail, file case after case; to arrest him in Chennai just as he sits down to eat, not even allow him to take his medicines, drag him into a van and take him from Chennai to Salem; then bring him back again to Chennai and lodge him in Puzhal prison; and, the moment it is known that Stalin, Duraimurugan and I are to visit him in prison the next morning, transfer him before dawn from Puzhal prison to Vellore prison? Let those with human hearts think about it. Is not justice supposed to show compassion even to true offenders? Does not the old saying tell us, “Anger must cool”? Has Veerapandi Arumugam’s guilt been proved in these cases? Why, before that, this chain of cruelties?

What crime did K.P.P. Samy, born in the fisher community, commit? He was given a ministerial post for his sacrifice and labour in the Kazhagam. Was holding ministerial office his crime? The Goondas Act against him! His younger brother arrested! His children arrested! Police going to his house at midnight and, in the name of inquiry, attacking his wife and family! What is happening in this country? Are such cruelties found in any other State? If they committed offences, let action be taken under the law. Let there be a trial in Court. If judges find them at fault and impose punishment, let them accept it. Why this haste before that?

The Secretary of the Madurai City District Kazhagam, Thalapathi—how many times will they arrest him? They arrested him. The Court released him on bail. They did not even allow him to go home and see his wife and children. Arrested again. Could they not have waited one day and, after he had gone home, gone to his house and arrested him again the next day?

Another is Tiruvarur District Kazhagam Secretary Kalaivanan. How many times will you arrest him? What is the offence? Students took part in the moral protest supporting the Samacheer Kalvi scheme and boycotted schools. One student who had taken part, while returning home, died when the bus he was travelling in collided with a lorry. What connection did Kalaivanan have with that? Yet the accusation was that because he conducted the protest the student had to die! Kazhagam lawyer Aasan Mohamed argued that this was a case filed out of political vendetta; the judge accepted that argument and ordered bail. Before he could reach home, another case: at the very time Kalaivanan had gone to Tiruchi to receive the Kazhagam Treasurer, he was accused of having gone to a betel-and-nut shop in Thiruthuraipoondi and quarrelled there. For that, the Goondas Act! Is it not a spectacle? Is it not strange?

Chennai District Kazhagam Secretary and MLA J. Anbazhagan belongs to a family that has worked for the Kazhagam from his father’s time. They arrested him in Chennai and dragged him to Coimbatore. What kind of vengeance is this? What became of that case?

They went searching for complaints and arrested senior District Kazhagam Secretaries one after another: Ponmudi of Villupuram, K.N. Nehru of Tiruchi, I. Periyasamy of Dindigul, Pongalur Palanichamy of Coimbatore, Vellakoil Saminathan of Tiruppur, T.M. Anbarasan of Kanchipuram, K.K.S.S.R. Ramachandran of Virudhunagar, and Karuppasamy Pandian of Nellai. What became of all those cases? Searches in the homes of all those District Secretaries! What else did Dhanasekaran, who was an area Kazhagam secretary in Chennai, do apart from working for the party? The Goondas Act against him! In any other Government, within a single year, has the Goondas Act ever been invoked against so many people belonging to one political party?

What sin did former MLA Purasai Renganathan commit? He works actively in the party. Is that a fault? How many cases against him? The Goondas Act as well! What happened now? When police asked that he be placed in police custody for interrogation, a High Court judge said that police custody was unnecessary in that case and refused permission to interrogate him in police custody.

In Erode, N.K.K.P. Raja is District Kazhagam Secretary. At about two in the night, police climbed over the compound wall, entered the house and arrested him. The judge himself said that police had not stated any evidence or reasons for remanding him. Are arrests without any foundation, without any prima facie evidence, permissible under law?

Pottu Suresh, a Kazhagam comrade from Madurai—the Goondas Act against him. What happened? High Court Judges Mr Nagappan and Mr T. Sudandhiram, in their judgment, said: “There is no evidence that he caused such disturbance to public peace and public order as would justify detention under the Goondas Act. We therefore quash the order detaining him under the Goondas Act,” and released him. How many times have judges condemned this Government? Yet if such a frightening journey continues on the same path, what does it mean?

In Tiruchi, District Kazhagam Deputy Secretary Kudamurutti Sekar—the Goondas Act. What happened? How many more Kazhagam comrades face cases? The list of those arrested runs long:

Chandrasekar, Dr Navaneethakrishnan, Krishnapandi, Murali alias Padmanabhan, Pandi and Ochapalu.**

The police show their concern chiefly in fabricating false cases and taking action against former DMK ministers. In the case registered on a complaint against AIADMK MLA Paranjothi, the Court itself had directed police to file a charge sheet. But the police hesitated to do so. The complainant therefore again approached the Court. The Court ordered that the charge sheet against Paranjothi be filed by June 19. Yet when the case came before Tiruchi JM-4 Court on June 19, the police did not file it. When Magistrate Rajaram asked why no charge sheet had been filed against Paranjothi, the police replied: “MLA Paranjothi has not properly cooperated with police; moreover, verification of his signature is taking time.” The Magistrate adjourned the case to July 9 and imposed a deadline that police must definitely file the charge sheet against Paranjothi that day.

This cruelty and vengeance continue without end. The attitude is that anyone may be arrested whenever they please. It is not only against members of the Kazhagam. Against anyone they dislike, in order to achieve whatever they desire, these arrests are being carried out with police assistance. Can such acts take place while keeping Anna’s name in the name of the party?

That is why tomorrow morning, 22 June 2012, an emergency meeting of the Kazhagam Executive Committee will be held. We shall discuss in our Executive Committee what action can be taken, democratically and according to legal rules, to condemn this repression—and **the Executive Committee will take the appropriate decision!**

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**21 June 2012**
