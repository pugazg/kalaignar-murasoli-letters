## Scope and method

This audit verifies the complete canonical Tamil source for the first **15 consecutive letters**, 3706 through 3720.

The range contains **105 canonical letter pages** in total. PDF pages **024–025** had already passed the direct scan comparison in `TEXTUAL_FIDELITY_AUDIT_001_025.md`; PDF pages **026–128** were freshly compared page by page in this pass. Accordingly, all **105/105** pages belonging to letters 3706–3720 now have second-pass scan verification.

For every letter the audit checked:

- printed letter number and title;
- salutation;
- complete page and paragraph sequence;
- page-boundary continuations;
- quotations and deliberate English text;
- names, dates, figures and institutional references;
- numbered/list material and visible list markers;
- punctuation affecting the source reading;
- closing, signature and printed date; and
- omissions, duplicated passages and transcription artefacts.

No source typo, unusual spacing, awkward grammar or anomalous wording was silently normalised.

A follow-up verification of the completed first-15-letter range found four additional scan-proven defects on PDF **027, 080, 096 and 110**. Those defects were corrected and this audit record was amended accordingly. PDF 110 therefore contains two separate scan-proven corrections from the completed fidelity pass.

## Scan-proven canonical corrections

### PDF 027 — letter 3706

A Tamil word in the sentencing example was missing the doubled consonant visible in the scan.

- Before: `இதனை ஆண்டுக் காலச் சிறைவாசம்;`
- Corrected to source: `இத்தனை ஆண்டுக் காலச் சிறைவாசம்;`

### PDF 045 — letter 3709

The source prints four legislative-resolution items with solid circular bullets. The canonical transcription had converted those visible markers to Markdown hyphens.

- Before: `- ...`
- Corrected to source marker: `● ...`

All four item openings on PDF 045 now preserve the printed bullet form.

### PDF 046 — letter 3709

The fifth legislative-resolution item also begins with the same solid circular source marker.

- Before: `- ஸ்ரீலங்கா அரசினால் ...`
- Corrected to source: `● ஸ்ரீலங்கா அரசினால் ...`

### PDF 049 — letter 3709

The closing quotation mark after the cited Periyapuranam line was an ASCII straight quote in the canonical text. The scan shows a right curly quotation mark.

- Before: `பொய்தவ வேடம் கொண்டு புகுந்தனன் முத்தநாதன்"`
- Corrected to source: `பொய்தவ வேடம் கொண்டு புகுந்தனன் முத்தநாதன்”`

### PDF 073 — letter 3713

A personal name was missing the visible pulli in the scan.

- Before: `கலாநிதிநிமல்கா பெரனாண்டோ`
- Corrected to source: `கலாநிதிநிமல்கா பெர்னாண்டோ`

No attempt was made to regularise the surrounding printed name form beyond this scan-proven character correction.

### PDF 080 — letter 3714

A comma printed after the party abbreviation `ம.தி.மு.க.` was missing from the canonical transcription.

- Before: `... பா.ஜ.க., ம.தி.மு.க.`
- Corrected to source: `... பா.ஜ.க., ம.தி.மு.க.,`

The following line continues with `தே.மு.தி.க.,` as printed.

### PDF 096 — letter 3716

The scan prints the expression as two words.

- Before: `இந்தப் பொதுவேலை நிறுத்தத்திற்கு`
- Corrected to source: `இந்தப் பொது வேலை நிறுத்தத்திற்கு`

### PDF 110 — letter 3718

Two scan-proven discrepancies were corrected on this page.

First, the first-pass canonical text contained an em dash after `திரு. டேவிட் தெரிவித்திருக்கிறார்.`. No dash is printed there in the scan; the next sentence begins directly with `அமெரிக்கா`.

- Before: `திரு. டேவிட் தெரிவித்திருக்கிறார். —`
- Corrected to source: `திரு. டேவிட் தெரிவித்திருக்கிறார்.`

Second, the source uses the dative form `ஆகியவற்றிற்கு`.

- Before: `இனப் படுகொலை ஆகியவற்றுக்கு அதிபர் மகிந்தர் ராஜபக்சேவே`
- Corrected to source: `இனப் படுகொலை ஆகியவற்றிற்கு அதிபர் மகிந்தர் ராஜபக்சேவே`

## Source forms deliberately preserved

The audit retained source-specific material even where it is unusual or inconsistent, including:

- the printed English Supreme Court quotation in letter 3708;
- visible source line/page splits and unusual spacing;
- the `சொன்னதன்னியில்` form where printed;
- mixed Tamil/English institutional terminology and quotation styles;
- dates in their printed zero-padded or non-padded forms;
- the complete English quotation on PDF 090: `The Ramayana is not history or biography. It is a part of Hindu mythology`;
- `உண்மை விகாரங்களை` on PDF 050, `தூதுவரகத்திற்கு` on PDF 062, `சாராம் சத்தை` on PDF 125 and `அழுத்தம் தருவக சொன்னதன்னியில்;` on PDF 127;
- list structures, rhetorical repetition and emphatic passages in letters 3711 and 3716; and
- source-specific names and figures without external factual correction.

Page-boundary source splits were also retained, including `முடியாதவை` / `யெனினும்` across PDF 025–026, `மனித` / `நேயத்திற்குப்` across PDF 026–027, `நிவாரண` / `உதவி` across PDF 063–064, `இடம் பெயர` / `முடியும்` across PDF 077–078, `கட்டுமான` / `அமைப்பும்` across PDF 088–089, `கொல்லப்பட்ட` / `கொடூரத்தைக்` across PDF 094–095, `சமுதாயத்தின்` / `எல்லாப் பிரிவினர்க்கும்` across PDF 097–098, `எதிர்பார்த்` / `திருந்தோமோ` across PDF 108–109, `வேண்டிய` / `நன்றி அல்ல` across PDF 121–122 and `அவர்களுக்கு` / `ஆறுதல்` across PDF 127–128.
