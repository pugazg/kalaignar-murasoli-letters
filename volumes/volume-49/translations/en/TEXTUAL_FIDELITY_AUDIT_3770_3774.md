# Textual-Fidelity Audit — Letters 3770–3774

## Scope

- Letters: **3770–3774**
- Canonical Markdown pages: **PDF 64–100**
- Total pages visually compared: **37**
- Source PDF: `TVA_BOK_0065839_கலைஞரின்_கடிதங்கள்_தொகுதி_49.pdf`

## Method

Before translating the five letters, every canonical Markdown page was rendered beside its corresponding PDF scan and visually compared page by page. The comparison covered:

- letter numbers and titles;
- salutations, paragraph order and page boundaries;
- names, dates, figures and quoted wording;
- punctuation and intentional English expressions;
- signatures and printed dates on closing pages.

Tamil-plus-English OCR, where consulted, was used only as a secondary discrepancy detector. The scan remained authoritative in every case.

## Preserved source anomaly

The closing page of letter **3770** visibly prints the date **30-6-2016**, although the letter's content, cited events and placement between letters dated 28 June and 2 July belong to the volume's 2013 sequence. The anomalous printed date has been preserved in the canonical Tamil and in the translation metadata rather than silently corrected.

This audit verifies the Tamil source used for translation. It is distinct from the later bilingual alignment review of the English translation itself.
