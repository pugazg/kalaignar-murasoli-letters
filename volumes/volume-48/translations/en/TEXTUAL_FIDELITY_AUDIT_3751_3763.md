## Source forms and material deliberately preserved

The audit retained the printed source rather than copy-editing it, including:

- all English legal, newspaper and institutional passages in the remaining letters;
- the long English `The Hindu` passage on PDF 397 exactly as printed, including the malformed source form `By injectinga`;
- source-specific spacing and broken forms such as `வாழ்க் கைத்`, `பெறு\nவதற்காக`, `ஒப்பந்\nதப்புள்ளிப்`, `வேண்டு\nமென்பதற்காக`, `திட்டத் திற்கு`, `கிடைக் கப்`, and other scan-visible line/word behaviour where already accurately represented;
- source punctuation and quotation irregularities, including asymmetrical quotation marks and unusual spacing;
- names, initials, dates, monetary figures, percentages, court material, government-order references and project statistics as printed; and
- the final running header/blank page and back-cover material rather than omitting them as non-letter pages.

No external fact-checking or stylistic normalisation was used to alter the canonical text.
