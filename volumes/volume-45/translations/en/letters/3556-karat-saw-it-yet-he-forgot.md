# 3556. Karat Saw It, Yet He Forgot!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This translation is intended to carry Kalaignar's voice into clear, contemporary English rather than recast the letter as literary or academic prose. It preserves the source's argument, political directness, rhetorical questions, repetition, irony, factual detail, and paragraph order. Names, dates, figures, quotations, and intentional English expressions are retained. Where Tamil idiom cannot be reproduced literally without sounding unnatural, the English follows its sense and rhetorical force without adding claims absent from the source. The original Tamil is reproduced in full below the translation and remains the authoritative text. `Udanpirappē` is retained in Tamil transliteration rather than flattened into “brother,” “sister,” or “comrade.” Literally evoking “one born alongside me,” Kalaignar uses it as a distinctive address of shared identity, equality, affection, and solidarity within the movement.

**Tamil source:** [Letter 3556](../../../chapters/3556-paarthum-marandhaare-karat.md)  
**Source pages:** [PDF 160](../../../pages/page-160.md)–[PDF 163](../../../pages/page-163.md)  
**Date:** 21 April 2011

**Udanpirappē,**

From the time when a group that considered itself superior by caste worked fiercely to give effect to that degrading notion, drew out the sacred thread and raised a battle cry—and Ayya Periyar and Arignar Anna took up the weapons of righteous struggle against it, gathering thousands of young people into their ranks and expanding the Self-Respect Movement—the orthodox defenders consumed by varna-ashrama zeal rose in fury and hurled harsh arrows and scorching words, words unbearable even to the ear, at Periyar and Anna. I have watched this from that time onward; and I believe that people like you too cannot have failed to notice it.

Those who regard themselves as belonging to the upper classes have never stopped pouring disgusting and obscene abuse upon us—then or now. When we have written or spoken in reply and given them an explanation, we have never, at any stage, adopted a method of criticism that descended to their level.

Whenever the fiery spears of the opposite camp came flying, how respectfully, maturely and without losing culture—without allowing the old relationship to suffer even the slightest injury—I have conducted myself from the days when I was a young man full of restless energy until today. I have never abandoned, even by a fraction, the practice of respecting leaders of other parties and friends in other camps. That is not merely my personal nature; it is also the growth we have acquired by walking in the path of the two who shaped me—Periyar and Anna.

However much disappointment or anger I may have felt toward movements in the opposing ranks—particularly the Communist movements, and toward some of those called their leaders—when expressing those feelings I have never forgotten the affection I bear toward them or our old friendship.

I have associated with them with affectionate respect, calling one **Annan Jeevanandam** and another **Periyavar Manali**. Even now, people such as **Thiru Nallakannu** occupy that same place in my heart. Many friends in those parties know this truth. They also know that the principle called **“Communism,”** which we have accepted as one of our vital arteries, continues to flow within me like an undiminished current of blood that moves me. I have regarded the general secretary of that party, **Thiru Prakash Karat**, as one among such friends.

From the time he became known to me, differences of opinion that arose from time to time never affected our friendship.

We cannot forget the period when people such as Vibhuthi Veeramuthu, writing in a poisonous manner, criticised and attacked the Dravidian movement, Periyar and Anna. Even when I think about the way Pandiyans and Ramakrishnans speak of us with degradation and abuse in a filthy style, I sometimes choose not to take their taunts and low-grade attacks seriously because I remember that people such as Comrade Prakash Karat and Yechury are also in that camp.

I myself have stated from time to time that the reason is that there are many aspects of Communist ideology that have attracted me more strongly than these individuals. But I was astonished and my eyes were troubled when I read a report published in **Theekkathir** under the very name of Thiru Prakash Karat. I was stunned and startled.

It may be customary for **Theekkathir** to publish degrading language and taunts about me and about the DMK under my leadership. What startled me here, however, was that contrary to that usual pattern, the author of this statement was my friend Prakash Karat himself.

> “The DMK stood as a pioneer among the parties that raised their voice for State autonomy and Centre-State relations. It is deeply pathetic to see a party with such a tradition sink so low today, turn into a family advancement organisation, use looted money even in the election, and try to retain power.”

These are the words attributed to Thiru Prakash Karat.

I do not wish to begin answering that statement and enter into a tuft-grabbing quarrel with him. Even just three years ago, he and several Communist-party leaders came to my house in Chennai itself, and for hours we conversed and debated many matters based on policy, many matters concerning the Indian national framework, questions such as whether there was a relationship with the Congress party, whether it would continue, and matters such as the nuclear agreement with the United States.

Yet I cannot understand why that friend Karat, who at that time held a good place in my heart, has now become a person with such poisonous thoughts. Even so, I have maintained my relationship with that friend without allowing even one link in the chain of culture we protect—the path of Anna, the path of Periyar—to wear thin and snap. But after these words appeared in a Communist newspaper under his name, I have been left struggling, unable to find an answer to the question: **“Is this the same man?”**

As for his accusation that the DMK has become a **“family advancement organisation,”** when **Thirumathi Brinda Karat**, who works in the movement alongside him, herself stands as an answer and explanation to that charge, I will leave that accusation aside. I wish to answer only his other charge—that **“it is deeply pathetic to see the attempt to retain power.”**

That friend says it is deeply pathetic to see Karunanidhi trying to retain power. When I and the DMK under my leadership stood firm on principle—on the Sri Lankan issue and on Centre-State relations—and withstood even the cruelty of the Emergency to such an extent that Comrade **E. M. S. Namboodiripad** himself praised us at a meeting in Chennai, saying that **“Tamil Nadu is the place in all of India where the air of freedom can be breathed”**—Karat failed to see it then, or having seen it, has now forgotten it. It is when I think of that that **I** feel deeply sorry!

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**21-4-2011**

## Letter-specific notes

1. The translation preserves the source's personal criticism and ideological argument as Kalaignar's own political voice; it does not independently endorse the characterisations made in the letter.
2. The source's `குடுமி பிடி சண்டை` image is rendered as a “tuft-grabbing quarrel” to retain its deliberately physical rhetorical image rather than flattening it into a generic “argument”.

இந்த வரிகள் திரு. பிரகாஷ் காரத் அவர்களால் கூறப்பட்டவை.
அவரது இந்தக் கூற்றுக்குப் பதில் சொல்ல ஆரம்பித்து குடுமி

அமெரிக்காவுடன் அணு ஒப்பந்தம் போன்ற கருத்துக்களையும்
மணிக்கணக்கில் உரையாடி விவாதித்திருக்கிறோம்.

ஆனால் அந்தச் சமயங்களில் என் இதயத்தில் நல்லதோர்
இடத்தைப் பெற்றிருந்த அந்த நண்பர் காரத் இப்போது ஏன்
இத்தகைய நச்சு நினைப்பினர் ஆனார் என்பது தான் எனக்குப்
புரியவில்லை - என்றாலும் நாம் கட்டிக் காத்து வரும் அண்ணா
வழி - பெரியார் வழி - பண்பாட்டுச் சங்கிலியில் ஒரு கண்ணி

கூட தேய்ந்து அறுந்து விடாத அளவுக்கு அந்த நண்பரோடு பழகி
வந்திருக்கிறேன். ஆனால் கம்யூனிஸ்ட் ஏட்டில் இந்த வாசகங்கள்
அவர் பெயரால் வெளி வந்த பிறகு “அவரா இவர்?” என்ற
கேள்விக்கு பதில் காண முடியாமல் துடித்துத்தான் போகிறேன்.

அவர் எழுப்பியுள்ள “குடும்ப முன்னேற்றக் கழகம்” என்ற
குற்றச்சாட்டுக்கு அவரோடு இணைந்து இயக்கப் பணியாற்றும்
திருமதி பிருந்தாகாரத் அவர்களே விடையாகவும் விளக்கமாகவும்
விளங்கும்போது அந்தக் குற்றச்சாட்டைத் தவிர்த்து - “ஆட்சியைத்
தக்க வைத்துக் கொள்ள முயல்வதைப் பார்க்கும்போது மிகவும்
பரிதாபமாக இருக்கிறது” - என்று அவர் கூறியுள்ள குற்றச்சாட்டுக்கு
மாத்திரம் பதில் சொல்ல விரும்புகிறேன்.

ஆட்சியை தக்க வைத்துக் கொள்ள கருணாநிதி முயலுவதைப்
பார்க்கும் போது மிகவும் பரிதாபமாக இருக்கிறது என்கிறார் அந்த
நண்பர். கொள்கை அடிப்படையில் நானும், என் தலைமையில்
உள்ள கழகமும் விடாப்பிடியாக இருந்து இலங்கை பிரச்சினையிலும்
- மத்திய மாநில உறவுகள் பிரச்சினையிலும் - “நெருக்கடிக்
கொடுமைக்கு ஈடு கொடுத்து இந்தியாவிலேயே சுதந்திரக்
காற்றைச் சுவாசிக்கும் இடம் தமிழ்நாடுதான்” என்று தோழர் ஈ.எம்.
எஸ். நம்பூதிரிபாட் அவர்களே சென்னை கூட்டத்தில் பாராட்டிய
அளவுக்கு - நானும், என் தலைமையிலே உள்ள கழகமும் நிமிர்ந்து
நின்ற போது அதைப் பார்க்காமல் இருந்து விட்டாரே - அல்லது
பார்த்தும் இப்போது அதை மறந்து விட்டாரே “காரத்” என்பதை
நினைக்கும்போது தான் எனக்கு மிகவும் பரிதாபமாக இருக்கிறது!
