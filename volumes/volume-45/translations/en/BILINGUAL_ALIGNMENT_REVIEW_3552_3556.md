## Review method

Each complete English record was compared against its complete audited Tamil source, including the Tamil appendix reproduced in physical PDF-page order. The review checked title, salutation and closing; paragraph and argument order; every substantive claim and list item; names, places and institutions; dates, figures, percentages, monetary values and units; quotations and source-supplied English; rhetorical questions, repetition, imagery and political force; documented source anomalies; and whether the English broadened, narrowed, reassigned or accidentally changed the addressee of source meaning.

### 3553 — *“If One Looks for Faults, There Will Be No Circle of Kin!”*

**Result: ALIGNED — no English correction required.**

The alliance-party inventory, sea–wave–foam and silk-weaving metaphors, World Cup/eleven-player and 234-constituency comparison, fielding analogy, Vallalar/Anna passages, closing proverbs and final Anna quotation all preserve the Tamil source's sequence, rhetorical force and appeal for alliance unity. No outside proverb wording was substituted.

### 3554 — *What Is the Mark Worthy of Respect?*

**Result: ALIGNED AFTER ONE ENGLISH-ONLY CORRECTION.**

The election-day/mark metaphor, campaign journey, little-girl and grandfather episodes, Tiruvarur itinerary, Koradacheri meal, health/travel passage, campaign comparison, rice promise, Kalaignar Insurance anecdote and final appeal all align with the audited Tamil. Source-specific `பத்துகல்` remains `ten kal`, and `தள்ளு வண்டி` remains conservatively `thallu-vandi`.

One English addressee mismatch was corrected in the closing paragraph. Tamil `உங்களால் நிறைவேற்றப்பட்ட திட்டங்கள் என்று ஒவ்வொருவரும் கூறியபோது` reports what people were saying to Kalaignar—effectively, **“the schemes you implemented.”** The earlier English read, “when one person after another spoke of schemes **you had implemented**, I gave myself marks,” which could make the letter's reader the implementer. The aligned record now reads: **“when one person after another spoke to me of the schemes ‘you implemented,’ I gave myself marks.”** This is an English-only clarification of the source's speech relation; canonical Tamil is unchanged.

### 3555 — *Written That Very Day; It Still Applies Today!*
