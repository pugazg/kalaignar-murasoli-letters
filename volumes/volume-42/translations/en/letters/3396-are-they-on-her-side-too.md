# 3396. Are They on Her Side Too?

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This letter compares the DMK, PMK and AIADMK positions on the Sethusamudram project and argues that positions within the AIADMK alliance are contradictory. The source also links the issue to the BJP and to differing positions on the LTTE. All comparisons and inferences are preserved as the author’s political argument, not adopted as independent conclusions. The complete audited Tamil is reproduced below and remains authoritative.

**Source pages:** [PDF 227](../../../pages/page-227.md)–[PDF 231](../../../pages/page-231.md)  
**Date:** 19 April 2009

**Udanpirappē,**

At the AIADMK alliance’s candidate-introduction meeting held at the Island Grounds in Chennai on 16.4.2009, the AIADMK election manifesto was released by the alliance leader Jayalalithaa, and the first copy was received by Prakash Karat, General Secretary of the Communist Party of India (Marxist).

That AIADMK manifesto has appeared in the newspapers.

On behalf of the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam, I released our 2009 Parliamentary-election manifesto on 2.4.2009, and the first copy was received by our General Secretary, Professor.

On page 37, under the heading **“The Sethusamudram Canal Project Must Be Completed Quickly,”** the manifesto says:

> “The Sethusamudram Canal Project, a long-standing demand of Tamils, was launched on 2.7.2005 as a result of the continuous insistence of Tamil Nadu Chief Minister Kalaignar. When the works were nearing completion, the project was halted on religious grounds.
>
> “The DMK urges that the Union Government move quickly to conclude the case pending before the Supreme Court, complete the remaining works, and thereby promote the economic development of Tamil Nadu’s southern districts and strengthen India’s security.”

That, says the source, is the DMK’s position on the Sethusamudram project.

I also wish to bring to your attention a statement issued on 22 September 2007 by Dr. Ramadoss, founder-leader of the PMK, which is now part of the AIADMK alliance.

His statement, as reproduced in the source, says:

> “The Sethusamudram Canal Project is indispensable to the economic development of the southern States generally and Tamil Nadu in particular.
>
> “Many foreign forces are trying somehow to block this important project.
>
> “Some here are aiding those forces by inflaming religious sentiments, creating unnecessary controversies and attempting to stop the project completely.
>
> “They seek to block a project that will become a central pillar of Tamil Nadu’s development and the country’s security needs.
>
> “Therefore unnecessary controversies should be brought to an end, ways should be found to implement the project without delay, and immediate steps should be taken.”

From this, the author says, it is clear that the PMK too held the view that the Sethusamudram project must somehow be completed.

But what did the AIADMK manifesto released at the Island Grounds on the sixteenth say about Sethusamudram?

The source reproduces a long passage from the AIADMK manifesto, stating in substance that:

- the Sethusamudram Canal had been a dream of Tamil Nadu for more than 150 years, and the British had initially considered the project before abandoning it;
- leaders including Anna and M. G. R. had believed it would serve Tamil Nadu’s long-term needs;
- the DMK and UPA were presenting it as fulfilment of those leaders’ dream;
- but in the modern era of faster, heavier cargo ships, the project was allegedly outdated;
- fishermen’s livelihoods and rare marine plants and species would allegedly be harmed;
- the project would allegedly bring no significant economic benefit;
- it would allegedly cause irreversible environmental damage, threaten the employment of lakhs of people and hurt the religious sentiments of crores of people;
- and the manifesto further alleged that benefits would flow chiefly to Union Shipping Minister T. R. Baalu and the Karunanidhi family.

It concludes, as quoted by the source:

> “The All India Anna Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam will not support any project that benefits an individual or a single family while disregarding the interests of the public.
>
> “The All India Anna Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam will cancel this project.”

**Udanpirappē,**

The AIADMK manifesto says that if they come to power they will cancel the Sethusamudram project itself.

Even if a government including the AIADMK were, by accident, to be formed at the Centre, the source says we must remember that the AIADMK would raise its voice to cancel this project.

What I ask is this:

Is the view in Jayalalithaa’s manifesto also the view of the PMK, MDMK and Communist parties that are in the same alliance?

What is their position on abandoning the Sethusamudram project?

The voters of Tamil Nadu, says the author, are eager to know.

On the LTTE issue, Jayalalithaa’s position, as described by the source, is that its leader Prabhakaran should be arrested and brought to India for punishment.

Vaiko’s position is that the ban on the LTTE should itself be lifted.

Yet both are now in the same alliance.

The author argues that the same kind of contradiction appears over Sethusamudram.

Just as Jayalalithaa says Sethusamudram should not proceed, the source says the BJP expresses the same view.

The AIADMK manifesto, in the author’s argument, reflects the BJP’s position.

As an example, he says a voice in support of Jayalalithaa’s Sethusamudram position was heard even yesterday.

From where?

From BJP national president Rajnath Singh, who, while campaigning in Karnataka for BJP Lok Sabha candidates, echoed Jayalalithaa’s view on Sethusamudram, says the source.

Do you see now, asks the author, whose mask Jayalalithaa is wearing?

What further evidence is needed, in his argument, to show who shares policy with whom?

Is this not one example, he asks, of which side Jayalalithaa may lean toward after the election?

Vaiko, who once proclaimed across the country that he was the one who spoke in support of the Sethusamudram project, and Dr. Ramadoss, who proclaimed that the project must definitely be completed — what will they now say about Jayalalithaa’s manifesto declaring that the project will be cancelled?

Will they say, “Whatever our dear sister decides is our decision,” and move with her toward the BJP side while calling for the Sethusamudram project to be cancelled?

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**19-4-2009**

திராவிட முன்னேற்றக் கழகத்தின் சார்பில் 2009ஆம் ஆண்டிற்கான நாடாளுமன்றத் தேர்தல் அறிக்கையினை கடந்த 2-4-2009 அன்று நான் வெளியிட்டு, முதல் பிரதியை கழகத்தின் பொதுச் செயலாளர் பேராசிரியர் பெற்றுக் கொண்டார். அந்த அறிக்கையில் பக்கம் 37இல் ஒரு பத்தி - சேதுக் கால்வாய் திட்டத்தை விரைந்து நிறைவேற்ற வேண்டும் - என்ற தலைப்பில் வெளியாகியுள்ளது. அது இதோ :-
