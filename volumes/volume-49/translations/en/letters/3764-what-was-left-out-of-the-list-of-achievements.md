# 3764. What Was Left Out of the List of Achievements!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This translation is intended to carry Kalaignar's voice into clear, contemporary English rather than recast the letter as literary or academic prose. It preserves the source's argument, political directness, rhetorical questions, repetition, irony, factual detail, and paragraph order. Names, dates, figures, quotations, and intentional English expressions are retained. Where Tamil idiom cannot be reproduced literally without sounding unnatural, the English follows its sense and rhetorical force without adding claims absent from the source. The original Tamil is reproduced in full below the translation and remains the authoritative text. `Udanpirappē` is retained in Tamil transliteration rather than flattened into “brother,” “sister,” or “comrade.” Literally evoking “one born alongside me,” Kalaignar uses it as a distinctive address of shared identity, equality, affection, and solidarity within the movement.

**Udanpirappē,**

A report in the 29 May 2013 edition of *Dinamalar* carried the headline: **“May in Madurai—a month of murders! Five murders in three days—yesterday, a listed rowdy's windpipe cut out.”** The report published under that headline said:

> “Murders have been taking place one after another every day in Madurai since 26 May. Yesterday evening, in a densely populated residential area, a gang chased a rowdy, killed him, cut out the windpipe from his throat and escaped. The series of murders in Madurai began on 31 January this year with the killing of ‘Pottu’ Suresh, a member of the DMK executive committee, and has continued at an average of up to three murders a month. Selvam, the security guard at the State Bank ATM centre in Thanichiyam, was killed on 3 April; Murugesan, a daily-wage labourer from Madakulam, on 21 April; and Gunavathi of Mudakkathan on 18 May. No clue has yet been found in these cases. Three people were murdered in January this year, four in February, five in March, four in April and six up to the day before yesterday in May. Of them, four were killed one after another between 26 May and yesterday evening.”

So this is the state of law and order and peace under AIADMK rule!

Four months have passed since Pottu Suresh was murdered in Madurai. The all-powerful Tamil Nadu Police are searching, searching and continuing to search for Attack Pandi, who is said to be the principal accused. They have arrested and imprisoned his wife and father-in-law. But they have not been able to catch him. *Kumudam Reporter*, however, managed to interview the same Attack Pandi and publish that interview in its magazine. What a government this Jaya government is!

Meanwhile, newspapers have reported that Venkatesh, Sasikala's elder brother's son, has suddenly been arrested under this government and is shedding tears in prison. Under this government, no one must ask why a person is arrested at one moment or released at another. Sasikala's nephew has now been arrested. Last year, Sasikala's own husband was arrested and imprisoned. At that time, in an interview to the weekly *Ananda Vikatan*, he asked:

> “They arrested me on the allegation of land grabbing and kept me in prison for 82 days. They filed six cases in all. So many months have passed, but why has no charge sheet been filed in even one of them? I was the one who first placed Jayalalithaa in the Chief Minister's chair. It was because of us that the Two Leaves symbol was secured. It was because of us that the party headquarters was recovered. It was because of us that the political situation leading to the dismissal of the DMK government in 1991 was created. Jayalalithaa had written a letter saying, ‘I am going to resign.’ It was only because I took that letter away and hid it that she has been able to remain in politics until today. Can Jayalalithaa deny this?”

Neither this government nor its Chief Minister answered any of this, did they?

Whenever the Chief Minister wishes, she takes action against Sasikala, her husband and people close to them such as Ravanan and Divakaran. She files case after case. But if Ravanan, who was accused in one of those cases, is later summoned to Kodanadu for a conversation, whom are all these actions meant to deceive? What happened to all the cases filed against them? It was said that complaints had been made against Natarajan. Later, it was said that those complaints had been withdrawn. If so, what action was taken against those who made false complaints? Were those complaints false or true? Why did the police not conduct a proper preliminary inquiry? What was the reason for that wrongful action by the police? After the person involved in the case came out of prison, he accused this government of having attempted to kill him in an “encounter”. What answers did this government give?

After Sasikala's husband Natarajan came out of prison, what did he say in an interview on 12 May 2012?

> “Every case filed against me is false. Of the six cases filed against me, two complainants have now withdrawn their complaints. The land-grabbing law was fraudulently used against me. We have filed three writ petitions in the Madras High Court seeking action against officials including the Chief Secretary, Principal Secretary, Home Secretary, Director General of Police, Tiruchi Inspector General and Deputy Inspector General. It has been twenty years since I left Jayalalithaa's house. She issued a statement saying that I had been removed from the party. I was never a member of the party. Can they prove that I was? I remained silent whatever they did. I will not remain so any longer. I will enter politics directly.
>
> “I was the one who prepared the 2011 election manifesto. Ponraj, Panneerselvam, IAS, and I were the ones who included the freebies such as laptops. The first copy of that manifesto is still with me. The statement issued in Sasikala's name before she was taken back was not a statement given by her. My wife does not know how to prepare a statement. Even someone who has been Chief Minister three times cannot prepare one. Some gullible fool like me has to prepare it for her. Even then, she cannot speak without a written note.
>
> “You arrested me for land grabbing. How many acres did you recover? Can you at least file a charge sheet? One can drive from Chennai to Thanjavur in five hours. After arresting me, they made me wander around for twelve hours. Deputy Inspector General Amalraj stopped the vehicle in an isolated forest area at Sengipatti near Thanjavur. Why? Was it to kill me in an encounter? Tiruchi Inspector General Alexander Mohan was apparently sitting in a police station in Thanjavur, calling all the inspectors and ordering them to return after filing some case or other against me.”

Natarajan gave such an interview, and it appeared in all the newspapers. What answer did this AIADMK government give? It gave no answer at all. Does that mean everything said in that interview was true?

It is in this continuing sequence that Venkatesh has now been arrested. The most painful part is that some newspapers in Tamil Nadu are always ready to write editorials praising even this.

Meanwhile, a report spread that “Boss” alias Baskaran, the son of Sasikala's elder sister, had been arrested by the police. He himself gave an interview denying it. In that interview, he said that he was an ardent admirer of M.G.R.; that he had efficiently run a television channel in 1995; that he was driven out because of political intrigue and spent eight months in Palayamkottai prison; that he has now acted as the hero in a film; and that he is being threatened to prevent the film from being released.

Who is he? Sasikala's elder sister's son. The arrested Venkatesh is Sasikala's elder brother's son. The man who came out of prison and gave an interview accusing this government is Sasikala's husband. Yet who accompanied Chief Minister Jayalalithaa to the Srirangam temple yesterday? None other than Sasikala herself!

Those who watch all this are bound to ask: what is actually happening in this State? If arrest follows whenever they wish and release follows whenever they wish, are these actions being carried out under any legal rule or procedure? If anyone can be arrested at any time, the newspapers that ought to criticise it remain silent with their mouths shut, satisfied so long as they receive full-page advertisements. Why, even a few political parties act with the same calculation.

Do you know how many murders took place in Tamil Nadu in May alone—and these are only the murders reported in the newspapers?

**With affection,**

1. **Udanpirappē** is retained as Kalaignar's characteristic political-familial address. See the [glossary](../GLOSSARY.md).
