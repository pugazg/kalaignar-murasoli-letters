## Corrections made

**One English prose correction: 0043.**

Before:

> `Forget only this one thing:`

After:

> `Do not forget this one thing:`

Reason: canonical Tamil `ஒன்று மட்டும் மறவாதே` is a negative imperative meaning **do not forget**, not an instruction to forget. The earlier wording reversed Kalaignar's meaning at a key closing exhortation.
