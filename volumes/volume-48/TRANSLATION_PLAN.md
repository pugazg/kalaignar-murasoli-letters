## Mandatory translator’s note and bilingual order

Every translated letter uses the approved standard translator’s note immediately below the English title and follows this order:

1. English title
2. standard translator’s note
3. source links, date and page range
4. complete English translation
5. necessary letter-specific notes

## Translation principles

- Preserve thought and argument order before literary elegance.
- Translate every substantive sentence, list item, quotation and rhetorical question.
- Use clear contemporary English without rewriting the letters as commentary.
- Preserve accusation, irony, repetition, sarcasm and emotional emphasis.
- Keep allegations and reported claims explicitly attributed.
- Retain `lakh` and `crore`.
- Use established English names/institutions where certain; otherwise transliterate without guessing.
- Add no historical explanation inside the body unless the source contains it.
- Use minimal notes only for source anomalies, ambiguity, institutional terminology or culturally difficult idiom.
- Reproduce the complete audited Tamil under the English translation.
