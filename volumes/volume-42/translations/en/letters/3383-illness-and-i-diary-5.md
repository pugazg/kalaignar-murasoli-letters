# 3383. Illness and I — Diary (5)

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This fifth diary instalment covers 18–20 February 2009 and is dominated by the Madras High Court confrontation between lawyers and police, alongside Sri Lankan Tamil advocacy, alliance politics and continued work from hospital. Legal and political allegations are preserved as the author’s source framing. The complete audited Tamil is reproduced below and remains authoritative.

**Tamil source:** [Letter 3383](../../../chapters/3383-nalivum-naanum-naatkurippu-5.md)  
**Source pages:** [PDF 141](../../../pages/page-141.md)–[PDF 149](../../../pages/page-149.md)  
**Date:** 14 March 2009

*(Through this serial article I am bringing together various events from the time I was admitted to Ramachandra Hospital, through the surgery and the continuous treatments that followed.)*

★★

**Udanpirappē,**

## 18-2-2009

Many newspapers had published abundant praise for the government’s Financial Statement.

As for the opposition parties, because they had to say something critical, they searched with great difficulty and issued statements identifying one or two shortcomings.

Some, unable to find fault, said it was a Financial Statement prepared with the forthcoming election in mind — indirectly conceding that it was a good Financial Statement.

On behalf of the principal opposition, Jayalalithaa issued a long statement about it. She too said it had been prepared with the election in mind.

Under the title **“It Is the Silk Saree Bought for the Wedding!”**, I wrote an Udanpirappu letter explaining all the criticisms made by the opposition about the Financial Statement.

The subcommittee of the Sri Lankan Tamils’ Welfare Rights Council that had gone to Delhi under brother Duraimurugan met Sonia Gandhi, submitted a memorandum concerning Sri Lankan Tamils and telephoned me from there with the information.

The delegation that met Sonia Gandhi included Tamil leader K. Veeramani, Union Minister A. Raja, Rajya Sabha Member Kanimozhi, Cumbum Selvendran, retired Justices Mohan, P. R. Gokulakrishnan, Janarthanam, A. K. Rajan, Bhaskaran, Samidurai and Shanmugam, and Professor Dhanabalan.

I was also informed that, after Question Hour in the Lok Sabha today, Union External Affairs Minister Pranab Mukherjee had tabled a statement saying that more than about seventy thousand Tamils were trapped in the Mullaitivu area where fighting was taking place in Sri Lanka, that the Sri Lankan government should take proper steps to move them to a safe place, and that India was ready to provide assistance.

Regarding the assault on Janata Party leader Subramanian Swamy in court, I instructed Director General of Police K. P. Jain himself to go to the High Court, meet Acting Chief Justice S. J. Mukhopadhaya and convey regret over the incident.

When I called police officials and asked about the matter, they told me that the Law College police had registered cases against twenty lawyers in connection with the incident; that police were taking steps to arrest the lawyers involved; and that at around 10.30 p.m. a lawyer named Kinilio Emmanuel had been arrested.

I called the Inspector General of Intelligence and had him contact Subramanian Swamy and convey that I had expressed regret over the incident at the High Court and that appropriate action would be taken by the police.

I later learned that when the Intelligence Department telephoned Subramanian Swamy in Madurai and conveyed my message, he thanked me.

Because I had not been sleeping properly for several days and had a burning sensation in my right leg, neurologist Dr. A. V. Srinivasan was called. On his advice, some old medicines were changed and new medicines were prescribed.

Dr. Marthandam’s team examined the surgical wound and said it was healing well.

## 19-2-2009

My condition was good from the morning.

A spinal brace brought from Delhi was fitted and checked on my body. To test it, I was made to sit up for the first time today.

The news that came to the hospital in the afternoon was not good.

Yes — today was the day of the clash at the Madras High Court between police personnel and lawyers.

Reports continued to arrive by telephone.

Of the twenty lawyers connected with the case concerning the assault on Subramanian Swamy, one had been arrested. The remaining lawyers came to the police station saying they would surrender.

Those lawyers who were to be arrested told the police: “We are ready to be arrested, but at the same time you must act on the complaint we are giving against Subramanian Swamy and Radharajan for speaking disparagingly about lawyers.”

The police agreed, and lawyer Rajinikanth submitted the complaint petition.

Police received it and registered a case.

When the lawyers next asked for a copy of the case document, that too was accepted and supplied.

Police then said: “We have fulfilled your demands. Those who are to be arrested should now cooperate.”

At that point a new demand was made: they would permit themselves to be arrested only if Subramanian Swamy was first arrested under the Prevention of Atrocities Act on the basis of the complaint they had filed.

It was in this context that an argument between the two sides began and ended in a clash.

Because cases were proceeding over which side was at fault, I do not wish to say anything about responsibility.

When news came that a judge who had gone to pacify both sides had been injured, I contacted the police and instructed them to arrange for all judges to be taken home safely.

Reports kept coming that the police station inside the court complex had been set on fire and that vehicles were being burned.

When I realised that the situation was worsening, I considered whether I myself should go directly to the High Court.

The doctors firmly said that in my condition I must not go.

I immediately contacted the Chief Secretary and Home Secretary and told them to go at once to the court, meet the judge functioning as Acting Chief Justice and discuss the situation.

They left accordingly.

I similarly instructed the DGP and the Police Commissioner to go to the court. I was informed that the Commissioner was already there.

I tried to speak with the Chief Justice by telephone, but could not get through.

So I myself wrote a letter to the Acting Chief Justice. In it I wrote:

> “An unexpected incident has taken place. I have sent the Chief Secretary, Home Secretary and Tamil Nadu DGP to explain the incident to you.
>
> “Your cooperation is very necessary to determine whether the fault in this matter lies with the lawyers or with the police, and to establish the basic cause of the problem. I also look forward to your valuable views so that justice may be established in this matter.
>
> “Though I am in hospital, if you wish to see me, I am ready to come by ambulance and meet you.”

I had the letter faxed to the Acting Chief Justice.

The Chief Secretary and Home Secretary telephoned me from the court and said the Chief Justice was of the view that the matter should be sent for a CBI inquiry, and asked whether consent could be given.

I immediately told them to agree.

Once they conveyed this to the judge, the High Court ordered a CBI inquiry into the High Court violence.

By then it was 10 p.m.

Afterward, the Chief Secretary, Home Secretary, DGP, City Police Commissioner and Intelligence officials came to the hospital and explained the court events and the views expressed by the judges.

It was decided that the government would place a statement in the Legislative Assembly the next day under Rule 110 explaining the situation.

The statement was prepared that very night in the hospital, and it was decided that brother Duraimurugan, as Law Minister, would read it in the House.

## 20-2-2009

All the newspapers today gave extensive space to the court events.

They reported that the Chief Justice of India had telephoned the Acting Chief Justice of the Madras High Court and enquired about what had happened.

Opposition leaders issued statements condemning the incident.

In particular, the AIADMK General Secretary issued a statement demanding that Article 356 be invoked and that the Union Government take action to dismiss the State government.

The MDMK General Secretary asked that I accept responsibility for the incident and resign.

In the Legislative Assembly too, opposition parties raised the court events prominently.

They first demanded that Question Hour be postponed and the court issue taken up for debate.

Even after being told that the rules did not permit Question Hour to be postponed in that manner, they did not accept it.

The AIADMK walked out.

Because the PMK continued raising its voice, its Members were removed.

After Question Hour, the Law Minister read a detailed government statement under Rule 110 on the court events.

Even then, all the opposition parties refused to accept it and tried to create shouting and confusion, after which they were removed by the Speaker.

The newspapers of the twentieth carried another large banner headline.

It concerned a speech Jayalalithaa had made at a wedding function.

All the papers carried essentially the same headline: **“Jayalalithaa Invites Congress.”**

Until then Jayalalithaa had not been with Congress or its front.

The Communist Party of India and the Communist Party of India (Marxist) had said that because they opposed Congress they were leaving the DMK front and going to the AIADMK front, and had even gone to Jayalalithaa’s residence to hold talks with her.

After those talks, Jayalalithaa suddenly spoke at the wedding function in a manner that appeared to betray them: under the banner of an “invitation to Congress,” she said the AIADMK and Congress had a long relationship, that the AIADMK was going to win a magnificent election victory, and that whichever party joined the AIADMK would benefit.

That speech appeared in all the papers.

Some wondered what could be a more open betrayal.

The two Communist parties were left, as in the source proverb, like people who had lost their salted meat to a Saivite grand lady, unable to understand what was happening.

After the morning newspapers on the twentieth gave great prominence to this report, I wrote a letter that day.

I wrote first about Professor’s statement on my physical condition, then about the Sri Lankan Tamil issue, then about the undesirable events between lawyers and police at the Madras High Court.

At the end I referred to newspaper reports about Jayalalithaa inviting Congress, recalled the ways she had earlier spoken disparagingly of Congress and its leader Sonia Gandhi, and wrote that Congress members should remember all those things.

It took a long time to complete that letter.

After reading Jayalalithaa’s invitation, Congress spokesperson and my dear friend Veerappa Moily replied in the newspapers that the Congress alliance with the DMK was very firm and that Congress had no plan of the kind Jayalalithaa had suggested.

That evening, while still lying in my hospital bed, I inaugurated by video conference the new elevated rotary flyover built at the Padi junction in Chennai.

I inaugurated it from the Porur hospital.

At the function in Padi, Finance Minister Professor, Union Minister T. R. Baalu, Ministers Arcot Veerasamy and K. P. P. Samy, M.P. C. Kuppusamy, Mayor Subramanian, and MLAs Sivaji and P. Ranganathan participated and spoke.

Speaking through the video link, I said it was not right for some people to create trouble for a government administering well, and that without fearing the plans of conspirators who wished to topple the Kazhagam government, we would continue on our upright path.

Nine days had passed since the operation.

With the spinal brace fitted, I was seated in a wheelchair and moved around first inside the room and then around the seventh floor where I was staying.

Seated in the chair, I met a few of the visitors waiting there for me.

I called police officials and asked about the High Court situation that day.

They explained that tension continued; that, in accordance with a judge’s order, no police personnel had gone there; that four judges had inspected the damage; that lawyers continued to protest; that some lawyers had burned my effigy; and that journalists had been attacked on the allegation that they were not reporting events properly.

I spent the whole night wondering what wrong I had done to lawyers that they would burn my image.

The pain too began troubling me again.

When morning came and I saw in the newspapers that “lawyers” had burned my effigy, I recalled Anna’s phrase, “Long live those who abuse us,” and told myself in the same spirit:

**“Long live the lawyers!”**

This diary will continue tomorrow.

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**14-3-2009**

சட்டப் பேரவையிலும் நீதி மன்றச் சம்பவங்கள் தான் எதிர்க்கட்சியினரால் பெரிதாக எழுப்பப்பட்டது. முதலில் கேள்வி நேரத்தை ஒத்தி வைத்துவிட்டு, நீதி மன்றப் பிரச்சினையை விவாதிக்க வேண்டுமென்று கோரிக்கை வைத்தனர். கேள்வி நேரத்தினை ஒத்தி வைக்க விதி இடம் கொடுக்காது என்று கூறிய போதும் கேட்கவில்லை. அ.தி.மு.க.வினர் வெளிநடப்பு செய்தனர். பா.ம.க. தொடர்ந்து குரல் எழுப்பிய காரணத்தால் அவர்கள் வெளியேற்றப்பட்டனர். கேள்வி நேரம் முடிந்த பிறகு அரசின் சார்பில் 110வது விதியின்கீழ் சட்டத் துறை அமைச்சர் நீதிமன்ற சம்பவங்கள் குறித்து விரிவான அறிக்கை படித்த போதிலும், அதனை ஏற்காமல் அனைத்து எதிர்க்கட்சிகளும் கூச்சல், குழப்பம் ஏற்படுத்த முயற்சித்ததால் அவர்கள் பேரவைத் தலைவரால் வெளியேற்றப்பட்டனர்.

20ஆம் தேதிய அனைத்து ஏடுகளில் ஒரு பெரிய கொட்டை எழுத்து செய்தி வெளி வந்திருந்தது. அதாவது ஜெயலலிதா ஒரு திருமண விழாவில் பேசிய பேச்சுதான். அனைத்து ஏடுகளுமே அதே தலைப்பைத்தான் வெளியிட்டிருந்தன. “காங்கிரஸ் கட்சிக்கு ஜெயலலிதா அழைப்பு” என்பதுதான் அந்தத் தலைப்பு. ஜெயலலிதா இந்த நாள் வரை காங்கிரஸ் கட்சியுடனோ, அந்த அணியிலோ இல்லை. காங்கிரஸ் கட்சியைப் பிடிக்காமல் தான் இடது சாரி கட்சிகளான இந்தியக் கம்யூனிஸ்ட் கட்சியும், மார்க்சிஸ்ட் கம்யூனிஸ்ட் கட்சியும் தி.மு.க. அணியிலிருந்து பிரிந்து அ.தி.மு.க. அணிக்குச் செல்வதாகக் கூறி விட்டு, ஜெயலலிதாவின் வீட்டிற்கே சென்று அவருடன் பேச்சுவார்த்தை நடத்தி யிருக்கிறார்கள். அவர்களுடன் பேச்சுவார்த்தை நடத்தி முடித்த நிலையில், ஜெயலலிதா திடீரென்று அவர்களுக்கு துரோகம் செய்வதைப் போல - காங்கிரஸ் கட்சிக்கு அழைப்பு என்ற பெயரில் - அ.தி.மு.க.வுக்கும் காங்கிரஸ் கட்சிக்கும் நீண்ட கால தொடர்பு என்றும், அ.தி.மு.க. தேர்தலில் மகத்தான வெற்றியைப் பெற போகிறது என்றும், எந்தக் கட்சி அ.தி.மு.க.வோடு சேருகிறதோ அவர்களுக்குத்தான் இலாபம் என்றும் அந்தத் திருமண விழாவில் பேசியிருந்தார். அந்தப் பேச்சுதான் அனைத்து ஏடுகளிலும் வெளி வந்தது. இதைவிடப் பச்சைத் துரோகம் என்ன இருக்க முடியும் என்று சிலர் கருதினார்கள். சைவப் பெருமாட்டி உப்புக் கண்டத்தை பறி கொடுத்ததைப் போல இரண்டு கம்யூனிஸ்ட் காரர்களாலும் என்ன நடக்கிறது என்றே புரிந்து கொள்ள முடியாத நிலை.

20ஆம் தேதிய காலை ஏடுகள் இந்தச் செய்தியை பெரிதாக வெளியிட்ட பிறகு, அன்று நான் எழுதிய கடிதத்தில் என் உடல் நிலை குறித்து பேராசிரியர் வெளியிட்ட அறிக்கை பற்றியும், இலங்கைத் தமிழர் பிரச்சினை குறித்தும், சென்னை உயர் நீதி மன்றத்தில் வழக்கறிஞர்களுக்கும் காவல் துறையினருக்கும் இடையே நடைபெற்ற விரும்பத்தகாத சம்பவங்கள் குறித்து எழுதி முடித்த பிறகு ஜெயலலிதா காங்கிரஸ் கட்சிக்கு அழைப்பு விடுத்து பேசிய பேச்சு ஏடுகளிலே வெளி வந்ததை குறிப்பிட்டு - காங்கிரஸ் கட்சியையும், அதன் தலைவர் சோனியா காந்தியையும் எப்படியெல்லாம் ஜெயலலிதா இழிவுபடுத்தி பேசினார் என்பதை நினைவுபடுத்தி, காங்கிரஸ்காரர்கள் அதையெல்லாம்

நினைவுபடுத்திப் பார்த்துக் கொள்ள வேண்டுமென்று எழுதியிருந்தேன். அந்தக் கடிதத்தை முடிக்கவே நீண்ட நேரம் ஆகிவிட்டது.

ஜெயலலிதாவின் அழைப்பைப் படித்துப் பார்த்து விட்டு, காங்கிரஸ் கட்சியின் செய்தி தொடர்பாளரும், எனது அருமை நண்பருமான வீரப்ப மொய்லி அவர்கள் தி.மு.க.வுடன் காங்கிரஸ் கூட்டணி மிகவும் உறுதியாக இருக்கிறது. ஜெயலலிதா கூறியிருப்பதைப் போன்று காங்கிரஸ் கட்சியில் எந்தத் திட்டமும் தங்களிடம் இல்லை என்று ஏடுகளுக்கு பதிலளித்தார்.

மாலையில் மருத்துவமனையில் படுக்கையில் இருக்கின்ற நிலையிலே - தொலை ஒளி பரப்பு (வீடியோ கான்பரன்ஸ்) மூலமாக - சென்னை பாடி சந்திப்பில் கட்டப்பட்டுள்ள புதிய உயர் மட்ட சுற்று வட்ட மேம்பாலத்தினை திறந்து வைத்தேன். நான் போரூர் மருத்துவமனையில் இருந்து தொலை ஒளிபரப்பு மூலமாக பாலத்தை திறந்து வைக்க - நிதி அமைச்சர் பேராசிரியர், மத்திய அமைச்சர் டி.ஆர். பாலு, அமைச்சர்கள் ஆர்க்காடு வீராசாமி, கே.பி.பி. சாமி, செ. குப்புசாமி எம்.பி., மேயர் சுப்பிரமணியம், சட்டமன்ற உறுப்பினர்கள் சிவாஜி, ப.ரங்கநாதன் ஆகியோர் பாடியிலே நடைபெற்ற நிகழ்ச்சியிலே கலந்து கொண்டு உரையாற்றினார்கள். தொலை ஒளி பரப்பில் நான் பேசும்போது - நல்ல பரிபாலனம் செய்கின்ற அரசுக்கு தொல்லைகள் தர சிலர் நினைப்பது சரியல்ல, கழக அரசைக் கவிழ்க்க நினைக்கும் சில சதிகாரர்களின் திட்டத்திற்கு பயப்படாமல் நம்முடைய நேர்மையான பயணத்தைத் தொடர்ந்து நடத்துவோம் என்று கூறினேன்.

அறுவை சிகிச்சை முடிந்து ஒன்பது நாட்கள் ஆகிவிட்டது. முதுகு தண்டு கவசம் பொருத்தப்பட்டு, சக்கர நாற்காலியில் அமர்த்தப்பட்டு, அறையின் உள்ளேயே சிறிது நேரமும், பின்பு தங்கியிருந்த அந்த ஏழாவது தளத்தையும் சுற்றி வந்தேன். அங்கே எனக்காக காத்திருக்கும் பார்வையாளர்களில் ஒரு சிலரை நாற்காலியிலே அமர்ந்தவாறே சந்தித்தேன்.
