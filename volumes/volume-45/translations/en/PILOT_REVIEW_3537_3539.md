## Style decisions locked

1. **Udanpirappē** is retained without replacing it with “brother”, “sister” or “comrade”.
2. The closing is **With affection, M.K.**
3. The standard Volume 49 translator’s note is used verbatim.
4. English titles preserve serial markers such as `(1)`, `(2)`, `(3)` and continuation wording.
5. `lakh` and `crore` are retained rather than converted to Western million/billion units.
6. Department names are translated consistently as **Rural Development and Panchayat Raj Department** and **Health and Family Welfare Department**.
7. Proper scheme names may be retained in Tamil transliteration where that preserves identity; explanatory English is supplied only where needed.
8. Dense statistical passages are translated in the same order without compressing them into summaries.
9. Source anomalies are not “fixed” by outside knowledge. English follows the meaning supported by the audited Tamil and notes ambiguity only where it materially affects comprehension.
10. Every bilingual record reproduces the complete audited Tamil page-by-page under **Original Tamil — மூலத் தமிழ்**.
