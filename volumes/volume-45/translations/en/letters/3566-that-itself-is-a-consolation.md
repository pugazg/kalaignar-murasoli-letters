# 3566. That Itself Is a Consolation!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This translation is intended to carry Kalaignar's voice into clear, contemporary English rather than recast the letter as literary or academic prose. It preserves the source's argument, political directness, rhetorical questions, repetition, irony, factual detail, and paragraph order. Names, dates, figures, quotations, and intentional English expressions are retained. Where Tamil idiom cannot be reproduced literally without sounding unnatural, the English follows its sense and rhetorical force without adding claims absent from the source. The original Tamil is reproduced in full below the translation and remains the authoritative text. `Udanpirappē` is retained in Tamil transliteration rather than flattened into “brother,” “sister,” or “comrade.” Literally evoking “one born alongside me,” Kalaignar uses it as a distinctive address of shared identity, equality, affection, and solidarity within the movement.

**Date:** 13 June 2011

**Udanpirappē,**

A unanimous resolution has been passed in the Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly calling for **economic sanctions against Sri Lanka**. On behalf of the DMK, Thambi **Durai Murugan**, Deputy Leader of the DMK Legislature Party, spoke in the Assembly welcoming that resolution and asking all the parties in the House to unite in supporting it.

But some who spoke on that resolution used whatever words came to their mouths to make low-grade criticisms of the DMK and of me. Since, under the conventions of the Legislative Assembly, the DMK was not given an opportunity to reply, our members staged a walkout. Even newspapers in Tamil Nadu that describe themselves as neutral did not write to ask why those who brought the resolution, instead of criticising the Sri Lankan government that gave rise to it and President **Rajapaksa's war frenzy**, should needlessly attack the DMK and me, waste the Assembly's time, and divert the people of the country.

What need was there for the **DMDK leader**, while speaking on a resolution about Sri Lankan Tamils, deliberately to spew venom by saying that the DMK had betrayed Tamil Nadu on the Cauvery issue?

While the DMK was in office, for the sake of Sri Lankan Tamils we held an all-party meeting, a meeting of legislature-party leaders, passed resolutions in the Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly, organised a human-chain protest, sent a telegram to the Prime Minister, announced the resignation of Members of Parliament, received their resignation letters themselves, and issued a final warning. Yet Chief Minister Jayalalithaa has thundered in the Assembly that all of these were merely dramas staged by me. It is Jayalalithaa's usual practice, whatever we do, immediately to describe it as a **“deceptive drama.”**

When I decided to undertake a fast for Sri Lankan Tamils, I went all alone to the Anna memorial by **6 a.m.**, without even telling my family, and began the fast there. Yet if the person occupying the office of Chief Minister says falsely that I finished breakfast at home and went to sit on a fast while on my way to the Secretariat, one can only think that even after occupying the Chief Minister's chair she has not corrected herself in a manner worthy of that office. If I undertake a fast, is it a **“comic show,”** while if she undertakes one it becomes a fast like that of **Anna Hazare**?

Everything has become so unsettled that there is no longer any sense of who should speak about what! As early as **1956**, at the DMK General Council in Chidambaram, in the presence of Perarignar Anna, it was I who moved a resolution for Sri Lankan Tamils. Those who may or may not even have been born in that year now join hands and make low-grade criticisms that I staged a **“comic show”** and **“deceptive drama”** over the Sri Lankan Tamil issue!

On **24-8-1977**, with just one day's notice, the DMK gathered **five lakh people** in Chennai and conducted a massive procession for the Sri Lankan Tamil cause.

When newspapers carried reports in 1981 that Tamils in Sri Lanka were being affected, on **13-8-1981** I sent a telegram to the Prime Minister of India saying:

> “Reports coming from Colombo say that disturbances are spreading in the east and south of Sri Lanka. It appears that the government there itself is encouraging the unrest. Tamil homes are being attacked. Tamil passengers are being robbed and thrown out of trains. I request humanitarian assistance to resolve this problem.”

And now I am said to be staging a “drama”! Do you know who says so? The Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu and the Leader of the Opposition! For this very Sri Lankan Tamil issue, on **15 August 1981**, I was arrested by the AIADMK government then ruling Tamil Nadu. The present leader of the same AIADMK that arrested me then now thunders in the Assembly that I am staging a drama!

When, on **25-7-1983**, people entered Welikada prison and massacred **35 Tamils**, including **Thangathurai, Kuttimani and Jegan**, it was we who gathered **eight lakh people in Chennai within seven hours** and held a massive procession. To make the Union and State governments show concern for the Sri Lankan Tamil issue, on **10-8-1983** Perasiriyar, the General Secretary, and I resigned our seats as Members of the Legislative Assembly.

On **16-5-1985**, I took part in an agitation in Kanchipuram for Sri Lankan Tamils and was arrested. On **23 August of the same year**, following an order to deport **Chandrahassan, Balasingham and Sathyendra**, we held a procession in Chennai and announced that the agitation would continue unless the deportation order was withdrawn. In continuation of that struggle, the deportation order was made to be withdrawn.

In **May 1986**, who conducted the **Sri Lankan Tamils Protection Conference** in Madurai on behalf of **TESO**, in order to protect the welfare of Sri Lankan Tamils? I did. The list can go on like this; we repeatedly took action, one after another, without tiring.

In **December 1989**, wanting somehow to find a solution to the Sri Lankan issue, I consulted the then Prime Minister **V.P. Singh**. Acting on the suggestion he gave, I met the various militant groups separately and spoke with them. On **19-12-1989**, I met the Prime Minister in Delhi and explained the details.

In **June 1990**, I stayed in Delhi and pressed the matter with the Prime Minister. In that connection, on the evening of the **19th**, Prime Minister V.P. Singh convened an emergency consultation at his residence. Those present included then West Bengal Chief Minister **Jyoti Basu**, Kerala Chief Minister **Nayanar**, Orissa Chief Minister **Biju Patnaik**, Assam Chief Minister **Mahanta**, Himachal Pradesh Chief Minister **Shanta Kumar**, Sikkim Chief Minister **Bhandari**, Puducherry Chief Minister **T. Ramachandran**, Union Ministers **Arun Nehru, Mufti Mohammad Sayeed, Thambi Murasoli Maran, Gurupadaswamy, Dinesh Goswami, Upendra and Unnikrishnan**, and Indian leaders **Advani, E.M.S. Namboodiripad, Vajpayee, Chitta Basu MP, Samar Mukherjee MP, Indrajit Gupta and Farooqi**.

After I had explained all the details of the Sri Lankan issue to them at length and returned to Tamil Nadu House, a telephone call from Chennai brought the news that **Padmanabha of the EPRLF and ten others had been shot dead**.

Can we forget that this same Jayalalithaa, who has now moved a resolution in the Assembly for Sri Lankan Tamils, issued a condemnatory statement in **1997**, when the DMK was in office, saying: **“Karunanidhi is trying to disrupt law and order; the Liberation Tigers have again raised their heads in Tamil Nadu”**?

They now say that while the DMK was in office it did nothing to press the Union Government on behalf of Sri Lankan Tamils!

It is enough merely to look at the Assembly proceedings to know that on **23-4-2008**, while the DMK was in office, the Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly passed this resolution:

> “For the purpose of establishing peace in Sri Lanka, the Government of India should arrange talks between the two sides engaged in conflict there; this House urges the Union Government to come forward to arrange meaningful talks so that a proper political solution may be reached to establish peace in Sri Lanka.”

Nor did we stop after passing and sending the resolution. On **6-10-2008**, I contacted Prime Minister **Dr Manmohan Singh** by telephone and asked that the Union Government immediately summon the Sri Lankan ambassador in New Delhi in person and convey India's anguish over the killing of unarmed Sri Lankan Tamils, and that the Sri Lankan government's military action and ethnic massacre be brought to an immediate end. Whether it is right for Jayalalithaa now to level accusations after conveniently forgetting—or deliberately concealing—this is something the people of Tamil Nadu themselves must weigh!

When Rajapaksa's Sinhala forces were killing innocent Tamils in Sri Lanka and protest marches, public meetings and demonstrations were being held in Tamil Nadu, Jayalalithaa issued a statement without the slightest compassion saying: **“The Sri Lankan army did not intend to kill Sri Lankan Tamils; when there is a war, it is only natural that innocent civilians are killed.”**

Now, if she moves a resolution as though she has great concern for Eelam Tamils, some people here may come forward to praise it with an **“Aha!”** But will true Tamil hearts fail to understand who is staging the drama?

When an all-party meeting in Tamil Nadu unanimously passed a resolution condemning the ethnic massacre of Tamils in Sri Lanka and demanding an immediate ceasefire, Jayalalithaa issued a statement saying: **“A suspicion has arisen in the minds of the people of Tamil Nadu that Karunanidhi may be acting in support of the Liberation Tigers; the Government of India has no authority to stop the civil war now taking place in Sri Lanka.”** The person who then argued the Union Government's case in that manner is the one staging the comic show now; I have never at any time staged such a show. Sri Lankan Tamils know this well, and Tamils throughout the world understand it well.

She who then told us that the Government of India had no authority to stop the civil war in Sri Lanka has now made a recommendation to that very Government of India through a resolution in the Assembly. At least on seeing this, will Tamil Nadu not understand what is false and what is true?

After the DMK assumed office in **2006**, we passed Assembly resolutions on the Sri Lankan Tamil issue on **7-12-2006, 23-4-2008, 12-11-2008 and 23-1-2009**. In particular, after the DMK brought and passed the resolution on **23-4-2008**, **M.K. Narayanan** contacted me by telephone and said that the Government of India had summoned the Sri Lankan envoys and spoken with them that very afternoon, and that the Prime Minister had asked him to convey that information to me.

When **Tamilselvan**, who had been the head of the political wing of the Liberation Tigers, died, I wrote this condolence poem in *Murasoli* dated **4-11-2007**:

> A face that always smiled,
>
> a heart that burned away opposition!
>
> Youth, youth—yet the heart;
>
> the strength, the strength of the Himalaya!
>
> In the path of the old lion Balasingham,
>
> a seasoned commander ripened with age!
>
> A noble young man who offered even himself as nourishment
>
> for the struggle for rights—one as dear as life—
>
> one like an Udanpirappu;
>
> O Selva, who carved your fame into every land where Tamils live
>
> and into every one of their hearts—where have you gone?

Jayalalithaa could not tolerate even this expression of condolence for a person who had died. In the statement she issued, she said that this itself was evidence that Karunanidhi had secret links with the Tigers and that it deserved strong condemnation.

On **14.10.2008**, I convened a meeting of all-party leaders at the Secretariat on the Sri Lankan Tamil issue. On **15.10.2008**, I wrote to the Prime Minister about the issue. On **13.11.2008**, we sent food, clothing and medicines by ship for Sri Lankan Tamils.

When a resolution was brought in the Legislative Assembly in **2009** for Sri Lankan Tamils, it was I who declared that **if Tamil Eelam were to blossom in Sri Lanka, we were prepared even to lose power for it**.

Those who genuinely work for Sri Lankan Tamils will certainly understand this. That is why **Chandrahassan**, beloved son of **Eelam father Selva**, wrote to me at that time:

> “We know that the suffering of Eelam Tamils and the resulting turmoil in Tamil Nadu affect you. Yet it is because you are in office that Sinhala majoritarianism in Sri Lanka is restrained at least to some extent. Tamils throughout the world are able to express their feelings.”

From those words one can understand how deeply he had grasped the feelings at the very bottom of my heart.

Now that Jayalalithaa has taken office and passed a resolution for Sri Lankan Tamils, we have no objection at all to many people singing lullabies to it, welcoming it, praising it, speaking and issuing statements about it. But while praising Jayalalithaa in that way, is it right for them needlessly to fall upon us and claw at us in order to satisfy themselves?

I have collected and stated all these details here only to remind the world of what actually happened. If at least a few people with a conscience understand these truths, **that itself is a consolation to me!**

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**13-6-2011**
