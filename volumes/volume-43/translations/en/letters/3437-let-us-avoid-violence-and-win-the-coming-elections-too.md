# 3437. Let Us Avoid Violence and Win the Coming Elections Too!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This translation preserves the source’s detailed district-by-district campaign assignments, candidate descriptions, alliance framing, election rhetoric and the closing Rising Sun verses in source order. Names, offices and political claims are carried as printed without outside reconciliation.

**Tamil source:** [Letter 3437](../../../chapters/3437-vanmurai-thavirthu-varum-therthalgalilum-velvom.md)  
**Source pages:** [PDF 93](../../../pages/page-093.md)–[PDF 98](../../../pages/page-098.md)  
**Date:** 30 November 2009

**Udanpirappē,**

On the coming 19th of December, by-elections are to be held for the Tiruchendur Assembly constituency in Thoothukudi district and the Vandavasi Assembly constituency in Tiruvannamalai district. Preparatory work has been completed and the candidates are engaged in filing their nominations. With one constituency in the south and the other in the north, the arrangement makes it convenient for our DMK front-line workers to divide themselves and take part in the work. The party headquarters has decided which districts, under their respective district secretaries, should work in these by-elections, and the announcement has appeared in today’s *Murasoli*.

In particular, for the Tiruchendur Assembly constituency, the party headquarters has announced that the following district organisations should work under the leadership indicated: Thoothukudi district under district secretary brother N. Periyasamy; Kanyakumari district under district secretary brother Suresh Rajan; Tirunelveli district under district secretary brother Karuppasamy Pandian; Ramanathapuram district under brother Suba. Thangavelan; Sivaganga district under district secretary brother Periyakaruppan; Virudhunagar district under district secretary brother K.K.S.S.R. Ramachandran; Dindigul district under district secretary brother I. Periyasamy; Theni district under district secretary brother L. Mookkaiya; Madurai Suburban district under district secretary brother Moorthy; Madurai City district under district secretary brother Thalapathi; Tiruchi district under district secretary brother K.N. Nehru; Karur district under the person who will shortly be appointed as in-charge; Thanjavur district under district secretary brother Palanimanickam; Nagapattinam district under district secretary brother A.K.S. Vijayan; Tiruvarur district under district secretary brother Kalaivanan; and Pudukkottai district under district secretary brother Periyannan Arasu.

Likewise, in the Vandavasi (Reserved) Assembly constituency, the party headquarters has announced that the following should work: Tiruvannamalai district under district secretary brother E.V. Velu; Villupuram district under district secretary brother Dr K. Ponmudy; Vellore district under district secretary brother R. Gandhi; Cuddalore district under district secretary brother M.R.K. Panneerselvam; Krishnagiri district under district secretary brother Senguttuvan; Dharmapuri South district under district in-charge brother Mullaivendhan; Dharmapuri North district under district secretary brother Periyannan; Salem district under district secretary brother Veerapandi Arumugam; Namakkal district under district secretary brother Gandhiselvan; Tiruppur district under district secretary brother Vellakoil Saminathan; Perambalur district under district secretary brother Pa. Duraisamy; Ariyalur district under district secretary brother S. Sivasankar; Tiruvallur district under district secretary brother E.A.P. Sivaji; Kanchipuram district under district secretary brother T.M. Anbarasan; South Chennai district under district secretary brother J. Anbazhagan; and North Chennai district under district secretary brother V.S. Babu.

I request the DMK Members of Parliament, Members of the Legislative Assembly and front-line workers belonging to each district to join in the by-election work under their respective district secretaries. Tamil Nadu Congress Committee president and friend Mr Thangabalu, and the leaders of the other allied parties, have through statements and interviews asked that both candidates fielded with the support of the allied parties be supported and made victorious. We should obtain the assistance of friends belonging to the allied parties in those constituencies, bring them together with us and properly engage in election work.

Anitha Radhakrishnan has been fielded on behalf of our DMK in the Tiruchendur constituency. He had earlier contested there on behalf of the AIADMK and won. Had he wished, he could have continued in that office. But because he did not like that party’s policies or the course taken by its leadership, he left it, joined the DMK and has been carrying out party work fully.

Recognising on democratic grounds that it would not be proper to retain an office he had obtained on behalf of another party after leaving that party, he voluntarily resigned the position; that is why this by-election is being held. The DMK leadership recognised his integrity and, on the principle that it is only just to give that office again to a person who had treated the post as insignificant enough to give it up, has fielded him once more as the candidate for that constituency.

Although more than fifteen persons from the DMK had sought an opportunity to contest in that constituency, they accepted the decision of the party leadership, remained united and left after assuring me that all of them would work for Anitha Radhakrishnan’s victory. I appeal to all the respected voters of that constituency to make the news of that victory certain.

Likewise, J. Kamalakannan has been fielded as the DMK candidate for the Vandavasi (Reserved) constituency with the support of the allied parties. His special qualification is that he is the son of the late Jayaraman, who had already been elected Member of the Legislative Assembly from this constituency. The people of the constituency know very well how hard Jayaraman worked there. Had nature permitted, he would have continued in office. To fill his place, we have fielded his son there. He is a good young man, capable of working calmly. I appeal to all the respected voters of that constituency to make him victorious too.

When DMK comrades take part in election campaigning in these two constituencies, they should explain to the people the full list of achievements of the DMK government and ask them to support the DMK candidate backed by the allied parties so that the work being done may continue. Let us explain our achievements in the proper way! Let us avoid violence and win the coming elections too!

Those who stand against us said on the previous occasion that they would boycott the by-elections. But today they have come forward to contest. As Anna once said, “When a party’s influence is declining, for it to think that by accumulating money it can acquire new strength is like eating gold bhasma because desire will not subside — the vigour may rise rapidly, but even more rapidly the vigour will slacken — it may even break altogether.” In accordance with that, they must be taught a lesson in these by-elections.

**Udanpirappē,** the ideal we have undertaken is great. Our chief concern must be that these by-elections help us explain that ideal, strengthen it and enlarge the opportunity for it to succeed. The party headquarters has announced which districts are to work in which constituency. Your duty now is to labour for the victory of the Rising Sun. I wish to close this letter by writing again a few lines written by Anna:

> The Rising Sun is for those who desire a government of our people!
>
> The Rising Sun is for the liberation of Dravidam!
>
> The Rising Sun is the DMK’s election symbol!
>
> The Rising Sun is the fitting symbol on which to cast your vote!
>
> People of the land, praise the Rising Sun!
>
> The Rising Sun is the true friend of every living being!
>
> The Rising Sun makes the bud blossom!
>
> The Rising Sun gives light to everything!
>
> The Rising Sun has the strength to rule the whole world!
>
> The Rising Sun gives overflowing prosperity!

Let the country know this; let all good people become ours; and know, **Udanpirappē**, that it is your duty to make them give us abundant support in the work of removing the country’s decline.

I will continue to tell you the rest, my sibling who guards our tradition!

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**30-11-2009**

## Letter-specific notes

1. `உதயசூரியன்` is translated as **Rising Sun**, preserving the source’s explicit identification of it as the DMK election symbol.
2. The district-by-district campaign roster is retained without compression because the source uses the organisational list as part of its mobilisation argument.
3. `வந்தவாசி (தனி)` is rendered **Vandavasi (Reserved)**, preserving the source’s constituency designation without adding a category not stated there.
4. Anna’s closing verses are translated sense-first while keeping the repeated Rising Sun refrain and sequence.
