## Scope and method

This audit verifies the complete canonical Tamil source for five consecutive letters, **3721 through 3725**, comprising **33 canonical pages**. Every page from PDF 129 through PDF 161 was freshly rendered and visually compared against its corresponding Markdown file.

For every page and letter the audit checked:

- letter number and printed title;
- salutation;
- page and paragraph sequence;
- page-boundary continuations;
- quoted passages and intentional English text;
- personal names, dates, figures and institutional references;
- lists and visible list markers;
- punctuation affecting the source reading;
- closing, signature and printed date; and
- omissions, duplicated passages and transcription artefacts.

No source spelling, spacing, grammar, punctuation or apparent factual anomaly was silently normalised.
