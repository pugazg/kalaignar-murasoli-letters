# 3421. World Classical Tamil Conference — Now Joined by the Distinction of Classical Language!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This letter announces preparations for the 2010 World Classical Tamil Conference and then reproduces, almost in full, a radio address the author delivered before the Second World Tamil Conference held in Chennai in January 1968. The programme, institutional history, names, dates, numbers and literary quotations are preserved from the audited Volume 42 source. The complete Tamil source is reproduced below and remains authoritative.

**Source pages:** [PDF 365](../../../pages/page-365.md)–[PDF 373](../../../pages/page-373.md)  
**Date:** 20 October 2009

**Udanpirappē,**

Work on the **World Classical Tamil Conference** has begun.

The city and the venue have been selected.

The dates on which the conference will be held have been announced.

Even while people were celebrating Deepavali — indeed, even on the following Sunday evening — I remained at the Secretariat until eight at night, spending time on constituting the various committees for the World Tamil Conference and writing to the leaders of different parties in the Legislative Assembly with the intention that the conference should be conducted without party differences.

With me were Deputy Chief Minister younger brother M. K. Stalin, Higher Education Minister younger brother Ponmudy, the Chief Secretary, V. C. Kulandaisamy — Vice-Chairman of the Classical Tamil Institute’s Enperayam and Aimperumkuzhu — Rajya Sabha Member Kanimozhi, and the Vice-Chancellor of Tamil University.

I intend to write many more letters about this conference.

The purpose of this letter, however, is to remind you of the radio address I gave before the **Second World Tamil Conference**, conducted in January 1968 when Perarignar Anna was Chief Minister.

Here is that address.

---

Dearly beloved,  
respected elders,  
mothers who gave us birth,  
fine young lions,

I am happy to have received the great privilege of inviting all of you to the magnificent World Tamil Conference being held in the soil in which we were born.

Following the 26th meeting of the International Congress of Orientalists held in New Delhi in 1964, a number of scholars interested in Tamil research established the **International Association of Tamil Research**.

Among the leading members of that body were Professor Filliozat of France, Professor Emeneau of the United States, Professor Burrow of England, Professor Kuiper of the Netherlands and Professor Kamil Zvelebil of Czechoslovakia.

The first World Tamil Research Conference under the International Association of Tamil Research was held in Kuala Lumpur in April 1966.

The second conference is to be held in Chennai, capital of Tamil Nadu, from **3 January to 11 January 1968**.

Although the scholarly conference begins on the 3rd, on the very first day of January the ceremonial crown for the opening of the conference is placed upon Mother Tamil.

Yes.

On that day, Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu Arignar Anna will unveil his portrait-statue presented by Makkal Thilagam M. G. R., at a function presided over by the distinguished Sir A. Ramaswami Mudaliar.

On **2 January**, the President of India, His Excellency Dr. Zakir Husain, arrives to take part in the World Tamil Conference.

Tamil Nadu will welcome him at the airport at **6 p.m.**

At **6.30 p.m.** on the Marina, a distinguished ceremony will be held to erect statues for the sages and scholars who fostered Tamil.

The statue of Thiruvalluvar, who gave the world its universal scripture, will be presented by Nadigar Thilagam Sivaji Ganesan.

Under the chairmanship of Tamil Nadu Education Minister Navalar Mr. Nedunchezhiyan, it will be unveiled by Muthamizh Kavalar Mr. K. A. P. Viswanatham.

The statue of the venerable Avvai, praised by crowned kings, will be presented by the Corporation and unveiled by Mr. S. S. Vasan, MP, under the chairmanship of Information Minister Mrs. Sathyavani Muthu.

The statue of Kaviyarasar Kambar will be presented by the people of Coimbatore district and unveiled by former Chief Minister elder statesman Bhaktavatsalam under the chairmanship of Agriculture Minister Mr. A. Govindasamy.

The statue of G. U. Pope, the great English scholar who fostered refined Tamil, will be presented by the Church of South India.

With Speaker Mr. Adithanar presiding, Bishop Newbigin will unveil it.

The statue of Caldwell, the Irish-born English scholar who produced the comparative grammar of the Dravidian languages, will be presented by the Church of South India.

Under the chairmanship of Legislative Council Chairman Mr. Manickavelar, it will be unveiled by multilingual scholar Mr. K. Appaduraiyar.

The statue of Bharathi, who said:

> “Among the languages we know, nowhere do we see one as sweet as Tamil,”

will be presented by the Corporation and unveiled by Namakkal poet Ramalinganar under the chairmanship of Law Minister Mr. Madhavan.

The statue of revolutionary poet Bharathidasan, who proclaimed:

> “Our life and our prosperity are unfading Tamil,”

will be presented by the Corporation and unveiled by the distinguished Professor M. Varadarasanar under the chairmanship of Local Administration Minister Mr. Muthusamy.

The statue of V. O. C. — the Kappalottiya Tamizhan, who wrote commentary on the *Kural* and gave voice to freedom — will be presented by the Corporation and unveiled by Tamilavel Mr. P. T. Rajan under the chairmanship of Food Minister Mr. Mathiazhagan.

The statue of Veeramamunivar, the distinguished Italian who fostered delightful Tamil, will be presented by the Archbishop of Mylapore and unveiled by Archbishop Rev. Arulappa under the chairmanship of Public Welfare Minister Mr. Sadiq Batcha.

The statue of Kannagi — queen of chastity, who pleaded her case, straightened the Pandiyan’s bent sceptre and was made a statue at ancient Vanchi — will be unveiled by Silambu Selvar Mr. M. P. Sivagnanam.

I will preside.

Thus, during the first two days of January, the installation of statues of these distinguished figures in the capital will be a sight to behold.

But on the following day there will be a procession surpassing even these sights.

It will cause onlookers to wonder:

> “Did the earth become small because the army grew vast,  
> or did the army grow vast because the earth became small?”

Recalling the days when the Pandiyas, Cholas and Cheras ruled, a procession will move through the capital.

Twenty-six decorated floats will take part.

They will portray Tamil language, civilisation, art, culture and ways of life.

There will be images of Thiruvalluvar, Ilango, Andal who sang the *Tiruppavai*, Nakkirar and other great bearers of literary Tamil.

There will be scenes from Sangam literature: the siege of a fort; Avvai receiving the rare gooseberry from Adiyaman; and scenes evoking “beautiful southern Madurai where, when threshing with cattle was not enough for the abundance of red paddy, elephants were used.”

As these sights pass before the eyes, every Tamil will feel that we are walking through that ancient Tamil land enriched by Kurinji, Mullai, Marutham and Neithal.

The decorated procession is being organised by a committee of film artists under the chairmanship of Mr. S. S. Vasan, including Mr. A. V. M. Chettiar, Mr. A. L. Srinivasan, Mr. Nagi Reddy, Mr. A. P. Nagarajan, Mr. Vins Krishnamurthy and Mr. Sundarlal Nahata.

Between the decorated floats will march college students, higher-secondary students, male and female teachers, and Seerani volunteers in uniform carrying conference flags.

The number of those marching will be about **seven thousand**.

The procession, moving forward in this way, will be approximately **three-quarters of a mile long**.

The police are arranging facilities, order and security so that members of the public coming to witness it can stand along both sides of the road.

A large platform is being erected in front of Government Arts College for the President of India and the experts attending the conference to watch the procession.

The procession will begin at **2 p.m. on 3 January** from the Island Grounds in Chennai — yes, from the five grounds — and will end at the Marina at about **5.30 p.m.**

In a setting designed to surpass the procession itself, the opening ceremony of the World Tamil Conference will take place by the blue sea at **6.30 p.m.**

In a gathering where lakhs upon lakhs of people will assemble like a flood — making one wonder whether one is seeing the waves of the sea or the heads of those who have come to watch — President Dr. Zakir Husain will deliver the inaugural address of the World Tamil Conference.

Tamil Nadu Governor Mr. Ujjal Singh will release the conference souvenir.

Navalar Nedunchezhiyan, Vice-Chairman of the conference reception committee, will welcome the Tamil people who have come, saying:

> “Come, partake of rich Tamil and rejoice.”

On behalf of the souvenir committee, Makkal Thilagam M. G. R.; on behalf of the mother committee, Minister Mrs. Sathyavani Muthu; and on behalf of the International Association of Tamil Research, Professor Filliozat will deliver welcome addresses.

The Mayor will present, on behalf of the people of Chennai, the Corporation’s welcome to the President of India.

Under the Tamil Nadu government’s major programme for conducting research in universities into ancient Tamil art and culture through Tirukkural studies, Food Minister Mr. Mathiazhagan will present the grant for Annamalai University and Local Administration Minister Mr. Muthusamy the grant for Madurai University.

I have received the privilege of presenting the grant for the University of Madras.

The celebration will begin with Isaiarasu Dandapani Desikar’s Tamil Thai Vazhthu and end with Tagore’s national anthem.

Tamil Nadu Chief Minister Arignar Anna will preside.

Thus the third day’s celebration will crown itself with victory.

On the morning of the same day, **3 January**, an attractive exhibition connected with the World Tamil Conference will be opened by elder statesman Rajaji under the chairmanship of Dr. A. Lakshmanaswami Mudaliar.

At that time, a postage stamp approved by the Union Government for the conference will be released in the presence of Raja Muthiah Chettiar.

At **9.30 a.m. on 4 January**, the scholarly conference will begin at the University Centenary Auditorium.

The source lists the expected representatives as:

- **28** from Malaysia and Singapore;
- **12** from Sri Lanka;
- **150** from Tamil Nadu;
- **20** from Indian States other than Tamil Nadu;
- about **100** from foreign countries including the United States, England, the Netherlands, Germany and France.

Under Professor Filliozat’s chairmanship, from **9 a.m. to 5 p.m. each day through the 11th**, they will deliver papers on Tamil research, conduct discussions and provide explanations.

In connection with the scholarly sessions, evening cultural programmes will be held for the delegates at the University Centenary Auditorium and Annamalai Manram.

The overseas delegates will leave on the night of the 11th for a tour of various places in Tamil Nadu rich in art and will return to Chennai on the morning of the 18th.

The benefit arising from this World Tamil research conference, so far as Tamil research is concerned, will be of great value to the Tamil Nadu of today and of the future.

Yet the scholarly conference by itself cannot hold the attention of the members of the general public coming from different parts of Tamil Nadu merely to witness the World Tamil Conference events.

Recognising this, and following the Chief Minister’s advice, the conference committee decided that, alongside the scholarly sessions, a great seven-day Tamil festival open to the whole public should be conducted in Chennai.

Accordingly, at five grounds on Chennai’s Island Grounds, the external-programme committee has begun arrangements so that for seven days the fragrance of delightful Tamil may spread, literary taste may increase, poetic beauty may ripen, and programmes of art, music, literature, dance and related forms may flourish with beauty and inspiration.

In brief, one may say that the external-programme committee is building the city of Poompuhar across those five grounds.

An enthusiastic group led by Mr. Neelanarayanan and including Mr. Paramasamy, Mr. Muthuramalingam and Mr. Ganesan has undertaken this important work.

This public conference begins on **4 January**.

The source sets out the daily presiding and opening speakers:

- **4 January:** Professor Anbazhagan will preside; Mr. Karuthiruman will deliver the opening address.
- **5 January:** Kundrakudi Adigalar will deliver the opening address; Pulavar Govindan will preside.
- **6 January, Saturday:** Mr. Sa. Ganesan will inaugurate the conference; Chief Justice Mr. Ananthanarayanan will preside.
- **7 January, Sunday:** Nanjil Manoharan will preside; Mr. N. V. Natarajan will deliver the opening address.
- **8 January, Monday:** Mrs. Radha Thiagarajan will deliver the opening address; Mrs. Soundaram Kailasam will preside.
- **9 January, Tuesday:** Quaid-e-Millath Muhammad Ismail will preside; Mr. Era. Sezhiyan will inaugurate the conference.
- **10 January, Wednesday:** Avvai Duraisamy Pillai will preside; Professor Ma. Ki. Dasarathan will deliver the opening address.

The festival programme will include music by Isaiarasu Dandapani Desikar, Isai Sithar Chidambaram Jayaraman and Sirkazhi Govindarajan; the **“A Day in Puhar”** poetry competition; prize-giving; publication of a prize-poem book; and other events.

As though placing the crowning mark upon the World Tamil Conference, Tamil Nadu Chief Minister Arignar Anna himself will deliver the concluding festival address at the final stage.

To make it possible for college and higher-secondary teachers and students to see and hear all the distinguished events connected with this refined-Tamil festival in Chennai, holidays have been granted to Tamil Nadu colleges until **5 January** and to schools until **10 January**.

This conference is being held so that the distinction and richness of Tamil may rise higher; so that eminent people from around the world may witness the stature of Mother Tamil; and so that the greatness of Mother Tamil Nadu, which has seated Tamil upon a throne, may be made known.

The source names the organisers who call upon the people of Tamil Nadu to come so that Tamil may prosper:

- Director of Tamil Development Mr. M. R. Perumal Mudaliar;
- Organising Secretary Mr. V. Karthikeyan;
- General Secretary Mr. V. S. Thiagarasa Mudaliar;
- Treasurer Mr. A. Subbaiah;
- Convener of the World Tamil Research Conference Mr. K. Venkatasubramaniam;
- and the Special Officer.

On this day that honours Tamil, the Tamil Nadu government extends both hands and warmly welcomes, with all its heart, the people of Tamil Nadu coming towards the capital to drink the taste of Tamil, and the delegates from abroad.

To fulfil Bharathi’s words:

> “The learned treatises of scholars of other lands  
> must be translated into Tamil;
>
> new books of deathless fame  
> must be created in Tamil;
>
> there is no greatness  
> in telling old stories secretly among ourselves;
>
> if scholarship is truly strong,  
> people of foreign lands must bow to it,”

and to receive the feeling given by Bharathidasan:

> “Tamil is named nectar —  
> delightful Tamil is equal to our life,”

come — come — towards the capital!

Long live Tamil.

Greetings.

---

Udanpirappē,

The eight conferences held so far in different parts of the world were **World Tamil Conferences**.

What is now to be held at Coimbatore is the **World Classical Tamil Conference** — joined by the distinction our language has received as a **Classical Language**.

With that sweet feeling, I will continue to write.

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**20-10-2009**

செம்மொழி எனும் சிறப்பும் சேர்ந்தது!

உடன்பிறப்பே,

உலகத் தமிழ்ச் செம்மொழி மாநாட்டின் பணிகள் தொடங்கிவிட்டன. இடம் எந்த நகரத்தில் என்பதும், எந்த இடத்தில் என்பதும் தேர்வு செய்யப்பட்டுவிட்டன. மாநாடு நடைபெறும் தேதிகளும் அறிவிக்கப்பட்டு விட்டன. தீபாவளியை மக்கள் கொண்டாடிக் கொண்டிருந்த நேரத்தில், ஏன் அதற்கு அடுத்த நாள் ஞாயிற்றுக்கிழமை மாலையிலே கூட இரவு எட்டு மணி வரையில் தலைமைச் செயலகத்தில் உலகத் தமிழ் மாநாட்டிற்கான பல்வேறு குழுக்களை அமைப்பது குறித்தும் கட்சி வேறுபாடின்றி இந்த மாநாட்டினை நடத்திட வேண்டுமென்ற நோக்கத்தோடு சட்டமன்றத்தில் பல்வேறு கட்சிகளின் தலைவர்களுக்கு கடிதங்கள் எழுதுவதிலும் நேரத்தைச் செலவிட்டுக் கொண்டிருந்தேன். என்னுடன் துணை முதலவர் தம்பி மு.க. ஸ்டாலின், உயர் கல்வி அமைச்சர் தம்பி பொன்முடி, தலைமைச் செயலாளர், செம்மொழி நிறுவனத்தின் எண்பேராயம் - ஐம்பெரும் குழுவின் துணைத் தலைவர் வா.செ. குழந்தைசாமி, மாநிலங்களவை உறுப்பினர் கனிமொழி, தமிழ்ப் பல்கலைக் கழகத் துணை வேந்தர் ஆகியோர் கலந்து கொண்டனர்.

இந்த மாநாடு குறித்து தொடர்ந்து நான் பல கடிதங்கள் எழுதவிருக்கிறேன் என்ற போதிலும், இப்போது இந்தக் கடிதம் எழுதுவதின் நோக்கம், 1968 ஆம் ஆண்டு பேரறிஞர் அண்ணா அவர்கள் முதலமைச்சராக இருந்து 1968 ஜனவரி திங்களில் நடத்திய இரண்டாம் உலகத் தமிழ் மாநாட்டிற்கு முன்பு நான் ஆற்றிய வானொலி உரையை இந்தக் கடிதத்தின் மூலமாக உனக்கு நினைவூட்டிடலாம் என்பதுதான். இதோ அந்த உரை :-

பேரன்பு கொண்டோரே,  
பெரியோரே,

<!-- Source PDF page 366 -->

பெற்ற தாய்மாரே,  
நல் இளஞ் சிங்கங்களே,

நாம் பிறந்த மண்ணில் நடைபெறும் பெருமைமிகு உலகத் தமிழ் மாநாட்டுக்கு உம்மையெல்லாம் அழைக்கின்ற பெரும் பேறு பெற்றமைக்கு மகிழ்கின்றேன்.

தலைமையேற்கவும், பேராசிரியர் மா.கி. தசரதன் தொடக்கவுரை நிகழ்த்தவும் கேட்டுக் கொள்ளப்பட்டிருக்கிறார்கள்.

இசையரசு தண்டபாணி தேசிகர், இசைச் சித்தர் சிதம்பரம் ஜெயராமன், சீர்காழி கோவிந்தராஜன் இசை நிகழ்ச்சிகள் - “புகாரில் ஒரு நாள்” கவிதைப் போட்டி - அதற்கான பரிசளிப்பு - பரிசுப் பாடல் நூல் வெளியீடு - முதலிய நிகழ்ச்சிகள் கொண்ட உலகத் தமிழ் மாநாட்டிற்கு முத்தாய்ப்பு வைப்பது போல் விழா நிறைவு பேருரையை மாநாட்டின் இறுதிக் கட்டத்தில் தமிழக முதலமைச்சர் அறிஞர் அண்ணா அவர்களே ஆற்றுகிறார்.

இங்ஙனம் சென்னை மாநகரில் சிறப்புடன் நடக்கவிருக்கும் செந்தமிழ் விழாவினையொட்டிய சீர்மிகு நிகழ்ச்சிகளையெல்லாம் கண்டும் கேட்டும் களிக்க விரும்பும் கல்லூரி, உயர்நிலைப் பள்ளி ஆசிரியர்களுக்கும், மாணவர்களுக்கும் வசதி செய்யும் வகையில் தமிழகக் கல்லூரிகளுக்கு ஜனவரி 5ஆம் நாள் வரையிலும் பள்ளிகளுக்கு ஜனவரி 10ஆம் நாள் வரையிலும் விடுமுறை அளிக்கப்பட்டிருக்கிறது.

தமிழின் சிறப்பும், செழிப்பும் மேலோங்கிட அனைத்துலகப் பெருமக்களும் அன்னைத் தமிழ் மொழியின் மாண்பு கண்டிட தமிழை அரியாசனத்தில் ஏற்றியிருக்கும் தாய்த் தமிழகத்தின் பெருமை விளங்கிட நடைபெறும் இந்த மாநாட்டிற்கு தமிழ் வளர்ச்சித் துறை இயக்குநர், திரு. மு.ரா. பெருமாள் முதலியார் அமைப்புச் செயலாளர், திரு. வே. கார்த்திகேயன், பொதுச் செயலாளர், திரு. வி.எஸ். தியாகராச முதலியார் பொருளாளர், திரு. அ. சுப்பையா, உலகத் தமிழ் ஆராய்ச்சிக் கருத்தரங்கின் அழைக்குநர், திரு. கி. வேங்கடசுப்பிரமணியம், தனி அலுவலர் - இவர்கள் தமிழ் வளம் பெற, வகை காண, வாரீர் வாரீர் எனத் தமிழக மக்களை அழைக்கிறார்கள். தமிழ் போற்றும் இந்நாள் தமிழக அரசம் இரு கை நீட்டி, தலை நகரை நோக்கி தமிழ்ச் சுவை பருக வருகின்ற தமிழக மக்களையும், வெளிநாட்டுப் பிரதிநிதிகளையும் இரு கை நீட்டி இதயமார வரவேற்கின்றது.
