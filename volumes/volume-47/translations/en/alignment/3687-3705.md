## Scope

Final bilingual-alignment batch for Volume 47: letters **3687–3705**.

The review compares each English draft against the authoritative audited Tamil embedded in the corresponding translation record and checks:

- semantic coverage and paragraph progression;
- omissions and unsupported additions;
- names, dates, figures and institutional references;
- quotation and attribution integrity;
- rhetorical questions, repetition, irony and argumentative force;
- Tamil idiom rendered by sense rather than silently replaced with outside historical interpretation;
- salutation, closing and date integrity.

This is a source-alignment gate, not the later volume-level English editorial consistency pass. No outside historical corrections are introduced at this stage.
