# 3456. Humaneness and Unsullied Political Civility!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This letter moves from a list of DMK-government welfare measures to the continuing problem of caste oppression and untouchability. It recounts the Papapatti–Keeripatti local-body elections, removal of caste barriers at Uthapuram and Periyar Nagar, and the three-per-cent internal reservation for Arunthathiyars, while explicitly crediting opposition Left parties for their role. Political claims and attributions remain source-framed.

**Tamil source:** [Letter 3456](../../../chapters/3456-manitha-neyamum-maasatra-arasiyal-naagarigamum.md)  
**Source pages:** [PDF 246](../../../pages/page-246.md)–[PDF 249](../../../pages/page-249.md)  
**Date:** 11 February 2010

**Udanpirappē,**

During these three and a half years since the Tamil people placed us in office for the fifth time and I assumed responsibility as Chief Minister, the work carried out has been work that has served, one hundred per cent, to remove the poverty of people at the grassroots — the poor and simple people — and to secure their rights. Apart from this, it was during periods when the DMK held the responsibility of government that schemes such as the following were begun and have been implemented:

- one kilogram of rice for one rupee;
- financial assistance for poor women to marry;
- financial assistance for pregnant women;
- the Periyar Memorial Samathuvapuram Scheme;
- the Uzhavar Sandhai farmers’ market scheme;
- the Anna Renaissance Scheme;
- cancellation of cooperative loans;
- the Kalaignar Insurance Scheme for high-level treatment;
- the 108 ambulance service; and
- the Kalaignar Housing Scheme.

Even through the great service of Gandhiji, Vallalar Ramalinga Adigal and Thanthai Periyar, and through the sacrificial struggles they conducted, present-day events in the country continue to demonstrate that it has not been possible to wipe away completely, without leaving even a single drop, the deadly poison called untouchability.

Even though stringent laws exist to eradicate untouchability, complete victory has not been achieved in that struggle.

As far as Tamil Nadu is concerned, even under the governments of many parties, including the DMK, caste atrocities and the evils of untouchability — like reeds on a riverbank that bend in the wind and then stand upright again — continue here and there to raise their heads and dance defiantly.

No one can dismiss the fact that the great responsibility of bearing the whole burden of removing those cruelties has today fallen upon us — upon our government.

In earlier times, in villages such as Papapatti, Keeripatti, Nattarmangalam and Kottakachiyendal, no government had been able even to conduct panchayat elections; and if elections were conducted, because only Dalit brothers could become presidents of those panchayats, there was no assurance that they could remain alive. Because of that fear, none of them would contest the panchayat elections in those villages. Reflecting that we bore a great responsibility to remove this intolerable cruelty — and that, if we could not remove it, we would lose the right to call ourselves people who came in the path of Periyar and Anna — in 2006 I had panchayat elections conducted in those villages I mentioned above. I then invited all the victorious panchayat presidents to Kalaivanar Arangam in Chennai, honoured them in a manner that delighted the Dalit community, presented gifts and garlanded them, showering happiness upon them. I invited the leaders of the Congress, Communist parties, Viduthalai Chiruthaigal, Muslim League and Puratchi Bharatham to take part on the same platform, demonstrating that they too had a share in this victory. Thus we drove out the snakes of caste fanaticism that had entered the garden of democracy and sounded the conch of victory.

Next, at Uthapuram near Madurai, a wall had been built by dominant-caste people to prevent Dalits from crossing it. I spoke by telephone to the District Collector and the police, had that wall demolished and thereby subdued caste fanaticism.

Likewise, in Periyar Nagar in Coimbatore city, in an area where Arunthathiyars had lived for more than twenty years, a wall erected to prevent them from entering was removed. The Pillaiyar idol that had been placed there to protect the wall was also removed and, on behalf of this government, installed at another location.

To lift the cherished Arunthathiyar people — who had been subjected to humiliation in conditions even worse than those faced by Dalits — from the circumstances in which they lay at the very bottom, we granted them three per cent reservation and thereby implemented a resolution that had already been adopted at Self-Respect conferences held under Periyar’s leadership.

As a result, in this year alone, not only did more than a thousand Arunthathiyar students obtain places for higher education; 56 obtained places in medical colleges and 1,165 in engineering colleges.

With political propriety — yes, with political civility and with a sense of gratitude — I wish to point out that the Left parties sitting in the opposition ranks also have a fair share in all these achievements.

We have never thought of beating our own drum by claiming that we alone gave practical shape to these ideals; nor have we ever behaved that way. But there are some other parties for whom that seems to be a speciality. When we accept some demands made by those parties and implement them, the parties that made the demands congratulate themselves, while blacking out the action of the government that fulfilled those demands. They have made this one of their permanent “physiognomic characteristics.”

Which government came forward to conduct the panchayat elections in Papapatti, Keeripatti, Nattarmangalam and Kottakachiyendal — elections that had not been held for many years and at which, even when they were held, the elected could not remain alive? Which government demolished the evil walls that had stood as untouchability walls for twenty years? With whose assistance were these things done — with the assistance of those who made the demand, or through the officers of this government?

Let us bless and salute those who, concealing all this, fail to thank those who hosted the feast but offer thanks and praise to those who ate it — forgetting the Tirukkural, “There is no redemption for the one who destroys gratitude for a good deed” — and continue to go about as they do. Let us do so with the humaneness that never dries up within us, and with unsullied political civility!

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**11-02-2010**

## Letter-specific notes

1. The welfare-scheme list is retained item by item rather than compressed into a generic statement about social policy.
2. The account of caste violence, reserved panchayat leadership, walls described as instruments of untouchability, and the Arunthathiyar internal reservation is translated as the author’s political account and is not externally reconciled.
3. The figures **56** medical-college places and **1,165** engineering-college places are retained exactly as printed.
4. The closing irony about political credit and gratitude is preserved rather than neutralised. No canonical Tamil change was required during this source-check.

செயல் வடிவம் பெறச் செய்தோம் என்று தம்பட்டம் அடித்துக்
கொள்ள என்றைக்குமே நாம் கருதியதுமில்லை - அப்படி
நடந்து கொண்டதும் இல்லை. ஆனால் அதற்கென்று வேறு
சில கட்சிகள் இருக்கின்றன. நாம் அந்தக் கட்சிகள் சிலவற்றின்
கோரிக்கைகளை ஏற்று, அதனை நடைமுறைப்படுத்தினால்,
கோரிக்கைகளை வைத்த கட்சிகள் தங்களுக்குத் தாங்களே
நன்றி பாராட்டிக் கொள்வதும் - கோரிக்கைகளை நிறை
வேற்றிய இந்த அரசின் செயலை இருட்டடிப்புச் செய்வதும்
தங்களுடைய நிரந்தர “சாமுத்ரிகா இலட்சணங்”களில்
ஒன்றாகப் பதிய வைத்துக் கொண்டிருக்கின்றன.

பல ஆண்டு காலமாக நடைபெறாமல் இருந்த -
நடைபெற்றாலும் உயிரோடு இருக்க முடியாத ஊராட்சி மன்றத்
தேர்தல்களை பாப்பாப்பட்டி, கீரிப்பட்டி, நாட்டார்மங்கலம்,
கொட்டக்கச்சியேந்தல் ஆகிய ஊர்களில் நடத்த முன் வந்த ஆட்சி
எது - இருபதாண்டு காலமாக தீண்டாமைச் சுவர் என்று திகழ்ந்த
தீய சுவர்களை இடித்தெறிந்தது எந்த ஆட்சி - யார் துணையோடு
- கோரிக்கை வைத்தவர்களின் துணையோடா - அல்லது இந்த
ஆட்சியின் அதிகாரிகளைக் கொண்டு நடத்தப்பட்ட நிகழ்வா?

இதையெல்லாம் மறைத்துக் கொண்டு விருந்திட்டோர்க்கு
நன்றி கூறாமல் - விருந்தை அருந்தியவர்களுக்கு நன்றியும்
பாராட்டும் கூறி -

“உய்வில்லை செய்ந்நன்றி கொன்ற மகற்கு” என்ற
திருக்குறளை மறந்து - நடமாடிக் கொண்டிருப்பவர்களை
வாழ்த்துவோம் - வணங்குவோம் - நம்மிடம் வற்றாமல் இருக்
கின்ற மனித நேயத்தோடு - மாசற்ற அரசியல் நாகரிகத்தோடு!
