# 3461. The Film-World Celebration and the Dream Some Saw!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This letter looks back on film-industry celebrations held for the author across different periods, explains the immediate gratitude function following the grant of 90 acres of government land to film workers, and responds to the controversy surrounding actor Ajith’s remarks at that function. The named artists, dates, quoted speech, metaphors and political framing are preserved as printed; no outside interpretation is added.

**Tamil source:** [Letter 3461](../../../chapters/3461-kalaiyulaga-vizhaavum-silar-kanda-kanavum.md)  
**Source pages:** [PDF 266](../../../pages/page-266.md)–[PDF 270](../../../pages/page-270.md)  
**Date:** 26 February 2010

**Udanpirappē,**

At the root of many of the problems that remain unresolved in the film world are the evils called jealousy, deception and arrogance. People of the film world in general — artists and workers — have often held functions for me out of trust in me and in the government under my leadership, and out of a feeling of gratitude. Even during periods when I did not occupy a seat in the government, they held golden-jubilee and platinum-jubilee celebrations for my artistic life and encouraged me. With one foot in the art world and one in politics, I have planted both firmly without allowing either to slip. At the same time, I have provided welfare funds for those in distress in the film world; when in government, understood the hardships and losses of those in the industry and fulfilled their demands; created a situation in which films have no entertainment tax; given benefits in various forms that made them happy, such as concessions in shooting charges; and established a “Film Industry Workers Welfare Board.” Because I continue as one among them, friends in the film world have held so many functions for me wherever I have been — whether in government office or not — treating both roles as one because I am an artist, like holding a celebration for one of one’s own children or placing a ring on the hands that protect one.

Can I ever forget the grand golden-jubilee celebration for my film career held on the seashore on 14-4-1996 by younger brother Vijayakanth together with leading figures and producers of the film world, or the gift they gave me? Can I forget the golden jubilee for my fifty years of work in the film world and the platinum jubilee marking the beginning of my seventy-fifth year, held on 27-9-1998 at Chennai’s Nehru Stadium under the chairmanship of my dear friend Sivaji — memories that even now move my heart — along with the gifts and greetings mingled with tears?

While delivering the presidential address at that function, Nadigar Thilagam began his speech this way:

“**Tamil! Let your foremost son — my dear friend — this country’s outstanding intellectual — this eminent politician — live long. Tamil people, greet Kalaignar. I join you in doing so. The Tamil film world has arranged a splendid function for Kalaignar — my life-long friend — and, though there are so many great people, they asked me to preside over it. They could not have bestowed a greater honour on me. Even if crores upon crores were poured upon me, I could receive no greater honour than this. When will the film world hold a magnificent art celebration for Kalaignar? When will I preside over it? Such an opportunity may come to him — but will it come to me or not? By proudly giving a friend the opportunity to have Sivaji preside over this function, Kalaignar has honoured me; I welcome it wholeheartedly.**”

People say that an artist’s words do not prove false. After that function, many more celebrations were held for me, but, just as he said that day — wondering whether such an opportunity would come to him again — that great artist passed away.

Later came the appreciation function held jointly by the film world and the Producers’ Council on 23-9-2006 for the tax exemption that had been granted, and the function held on 9-10-2009 by film workers from across India to confer on me the award “World Art Creator.” These remain unfaded not only in my home, but also in my heart.

Even after I am gone, the halls in which those functions were held will continue forever to echo those loving words and sweet greetings that cannot disappear.

The present function held by the film world to express thanks to me was not conducted to publicise me, nor for anyone else to publicise themselves. It was a function jointly held by our artists and film workers in order to live by Valluvar’s dictum, “It is not good to forget a benefit received.”

There are some who cool their irritation by saying sarcastically, “They hold functions like this for every Chief Minister.” But, leaving aside that sarcasm, this was not one more in a series of functions held for every Chief Minister — not merely an eleventh added to ten. It was an outpouring of gratitude because, accepting the request of people in the film world — especially friends and comrades engaged in artistic work — so that their families could live without want, 90 acres of government land were duly allotted to them.

Realising what a major thing it is to grant 90 acres of land in a part of Chennai city, those who felt and rejoiced over it held that thanksgiving function to express their emotion.

We have seen a mother adorn a beautiful child still further before sending it to school, yet place a small black dot on the child’s cheek. It is true that something like such a dot was placed at that function, and that an episode arose like that of an uncomprehending child who magnifies the dot and smears its colour over the whole face.

Amid the flowers of affection showered on me in their greetings by the nationally renowned great artist Amitabh Bachchan, our Superstar younger brother Rajinikanth and Kalaignani younger brother Kamal Haasan, a *thumbai* flower named Ajith also fell upon me. Though it was a spotless flower, it raised the doubt whether it was a flower that had fallen against the very function being held for me. The applause and voices that followed were then seized upon by some who thought, “This is the moment,” and tried to divert the function held for me. There were, of course, certain newspapers ready at hand; when they began trying to make it into something monstrous, younger brother Ajith met me and explained matters as an effort to prevent it from reaching the stage after rehearsal itself and to put a full stop to it. He explained: “What I referred to was not this function. I was referring to the bitterness that arose in the film world at certain earlier events.”

Younger brothers such as Rajini showed magnanimity and remained calm, saying that the matter should not be magnified. Film-industry workers and artists cooperated. Thus the firework that some expected to burst spectacularly became the story of a dud firework. Those who imagined that this function could be used as a cause for turmoil in an already contentious film world now stand wringing their hands because the dream they saw has dissolved.

Udanpirappē, if not only those who attended the function but also younger brothers Ramanarayanan and Kuganathan, who stood in front and organised it, younger brothers Sarathkumar and Radharavi, and all those who worked alongside them understand the purpose of this letter and the thoughts expressed in it, and hereafter maintain discipline so that no one can create even a small disturbance in the film world, they themselves know that this would give me greater happiness than even the joy given by the function they conducted.

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**26-2-2010**

அவர்களுக்கு வழங்கியதற்காக - அவர்கள் காட்டிய நன்றிப்
பெருக்கு அந்த விழா!

சென்னை மாநகரத்தில் ஒரு பகுதியில் 90 ஏக்கர் நிலம்
வழங்குவது என்பது எவ்வளவு பெரிய காரியம் என்பதை உணர்ந்து
- மகிழ்ந்து - உணர்ச்சியுள்ளவர்கள் அதனைக் காட்டிக் கொள்ள
எடுத்த விழாதான் அந்த நன்றி தெரிவிக்கும் விழா.

அழகான குழந்தையை மேலும் அலங்கரித்து பள்ளிக்கு
அனுப்பும் தாய் - அதன் கன்னத்தில் ஒரு சிறிய கருப்புப் பொட்டு
வைத்து அனுப்புவதைப் பார்க்கிறோமே - அதைப் போன்றதொரு
பொட்டு - அந்த விழாவில் வைக்கப்பட்டதைப் பெரிதுபடுத்தி -
அந்தப் பொட்டின் வண்ணத்தை முகம் முழுதும் பூசிக்கொள்ளும்
புரியாத குழந்தையைப் போல ஒரு நிகழ்ச்சி அமைந்து விட்டது
உண்மைதான்.
அகில இந்தியப் புகழ் வாய்ந்த கலைஞர் பெருந்தகை அமிதா பச்சன் - நமது சூப்பர் ஸ்டார் தம்பி ரஜினிகாந்த் - கலைஞானி
தம்பி கமலஹாசன் - இவர்கள் எல்லாம் வாழ்த்துரைத்து அள்ளித்
தெளித்த அன்பு மலர்களுக்கிடையே அஜித் எனும் தும்பை
மலரும் என் மேல் விழுந்து - அது மாசற்ற மலர் எனினும் - அந்த
மன்றத்தில் எனக்கு நடத்திய விழாவிற்கு எதிராக விழுந்த மலரோ
என்ற ஐயப்பாட்டை எழுப்பி - அதைத் தொடர்ந்து எழுந்த
கையொலிகள், பேச்சொலிகள் இவற்றையெல்லாம் “இதுதான்
சமயம்” என்று சிலர் எனக்கு நடந்த விழாவினை திசை திருப்ப
முயன்று - “இருக்கவே இருக்கின்றனவே சில பத்திரிகைகள் -
அவைகள் அதனைப் பூதாகரமாக உருவாக்கிட முனைந்த போது
- அதனை மேடையேற விடாமல் ஒத்திகையிலேயே ஒரு வழி
செய்து, முற்றுப் புள்ளி வைத்திடும் முயற்சியாக - தம்பி அஜித்
என்னைச் சந்தித்து விளக்கமளித்தார். “நான் குறிப்பிட்டது இந்த
விழா பற்றியல்ல - இதற்கு முன்பு நடைபெற்ற சில நிகழ்வுகளில்
கலையுலகில் ஏற்பட்ட கசப்பு பற்றிதான் குறிப்பிட்டதாக” அவர்
விளக்கம் அளித்ததும் -
இதனைப் பெரிதுபடுத்தக் கூடாது என்று பெருந்தன்மை
பூண்டு தம்பி ரஜினி போன்றவர்கள் அமைதிகாத்ததும் -
திரையுலகத் தொழிலாளர்களும், கலைஞர்களும் அதற்கு
ஒத்துழைப்பு நல்கியதும் -
பெரிதாக வெடிக்கப் போகிறது என்று எதிர் பார்த்த வாணம்
- புஸ்வாணமாகி விட்ட கதையாயிற்று! கலகம் விளையும்
கலையுலகத்தில் - அதற்கு இந்த விழாவை ஒரு காரணம்
ஆக்கலாம் என்று கருதியோர் தாம் கண்ட கனவு கலைந்ததே என்று
கை பிசைந்து நிற்கின்றனர்.

உடன்பிறப்பே, இந்தக் கடிதத்தின் நோக்கத்தையும்
- இதில் இடம் பெற்றுள்ள கருத்துக்களையும் விழாவிலே
கலந்து கொண்டோர் மாத்திரமல்ல; விழாவினை முன் நின்று
நடத்திய தம்பி ராமநாராயணன், தம்பி குகநாதன், தம்பிகள்
சரத்குமார், ராதாரவி ஆகியோரும், அவர்களோடு உடனிருந்து
உழைத்தவர்களும் உணர்ந்து இனி எவர் ஒருவரும் கலையுலகில்
சிறு கலகம் விளைவித்திடவும் முடியாது என்று கட்டுப்பாடு
காப்பார்களேயானால் - அது அவர்கள் நடத்திய விழா தந்த
மகிழ்ச்சியை விட பெரு மகிழ்ச்சியாக எனக்கு அமையும் என்பதை
அவர்களே அறிவார்கள்!
