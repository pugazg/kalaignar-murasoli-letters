# 3573. Suffering Will End; Trouble Will Lessen!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This translation is intended to carry Kalaignar's voice into clear, contemporary English rather than recast the letter as literary or academic prose. It preserves the source's argument, political directness, rhetorical questions, repetition, irony, factual detail, and paragraph order. Names, dates, figures, quotations, and intentional English expressions are retained. Where Tamil idiom cannot be reproduced literally without sounding unnatural, the English follows its sense and rhetorical force without adding claims absent from the source. The original Tamil is reproduced in full below the translation and remains the authoritative text. `Udanpirappē` is retained in Tamil transliteration rather than flattened into “brother,” “sister,” or “comrade.” Literally evoking “one born alongside me,” Kalaignar uses it as a distinctive address of shared identity, equality, affection, and solidarity within the movement.

**Tamil source:** [Letter 3573](../../../chapters/3573-allal-neengum-thollai-kuraiyum.md)  
**Source pages:** [PDF 266](../../../pages/page-266.md)–[PDF 268](../../../pages/page-268.md)  
**Date:** 21 July 2011

**Udanpirappē,**

You would have heard a line of dialogue in the film **Manohara**:

> **“The exploits of the formless one have begun!”**

You would also have seen the outrages of **Vasanthasena** that follow!

You would have seen **King Purushothaman**, knowing nothing, lying intoxicated and declaring that her lap itself was his heavenly bed!

And you would have enjoyed the ending in which the characters in that story were destroyed and ruined in different ways—through **Manoharan**, and through the curse of his chaste, golden mother **Padmavathi**!

This is not merely something pictured in a play—in a screen epic. It is a new golden page from an old history written by **Pammal Sambandhanar**, placed in your heart as a lesson as well. As a sign that the page is turning back again, the new government that has spread through Tamil Nadu like a **cactus**, in keeping with the proverb **“old liquor in a new pot,”** has once more begun its lawless dance.

Crimes—  
Wrongdoing—  
Looting—  
Murders—

Wherever they happen, whoever commits them, we will never make the Himalayan mistake of saying that they deserve forgiveness.

Nor will we be prepared to forgive those who do such things.

Look! A party that climbed onto the shoulders of the people of Tamil Nadu, who were deceived a month or two ago, and came to power has made lawlessness its ornament and repression its sword of war. It has turned into a song the merciless cry of a Tsar: **“Say ‘hm’—prison; ask ‘why’—exile.”**

It bellows:

> “The opposition parties—especially those of the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam—whether they have committed an offence or have not even shown their face anywhere near that offence, I will indiscriminately put them all in the accused dock. In the name of law I will lash them with the whip. I will separate them from wife, children, kin, relatives and friends and isolate them—
>
> “If possible, I will even throw them into the fire and burn them.”

With that furious cry and booming roar, forgetting even to apply medicine to the wound on its own back, it turns the country itself into a cremation ground and declares that it will perform the dance of **Mahakali**; it torments every department under its feet, including the police, until foam rises at their mouths—

And every day we see the spectacle of it grinding its teeth, sticking out its tongue and declaring:

> **“Here I come—the Bhadrakali you cannot see anywhere else; I will demand even blood sacrifice!”**

Declaring that it will accomplish whatever it thinks of, announcing schemes exactly as it pleases, it watches schoolchildren and the young at their lively age laugh in ridicule as all those announcements are smashed to dust in the people's forum and in the courts. Growing ever more irritated, it asks:

> “Where is the opposition comrade? Where is his family? Where can they go without being caught in my treacherous net?”

Drawing a curtain of falsehood, darkening the eyes of those who live on this earth, how much longer does it think it can deceive the people here? That cry is heard wherever one turns! From all eight directions comes the cry: **“Your time is now ending!”**

This is the condition of Tamil Nadu today.

To change this condition—O sparks of fire!

Periyar and Anna, who once roused the youth of the Dravidian people saying that **you** are what is needed, now stand together in one rank and call you!

Elder brothers and younger brothers who have created a thousand achievements through righteous struggle!

Dear Udanpirappugal, dearer than life!

There—light can be seen.

Walk swiftly toward it!

**Suffering will end!**

**Trouble will lessen!**

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**21-7-2011**

இப்படி அந்தக் கதையில் வரும் பாத்திரங்கள் - மனோகரனாலும்

சம்பந்தனார் எழுதிய பழைய சரித்திரத்தின் ஒரு புதிய பொன்னேடு!
அந்த ஏடு - இதோ மீண்டும் திரும்புகிறது என்பதற்கு
அடையாளமாக தமிழகத்தில் “கள்ளி”யாகப் படர்ந்துள்ள
புதிய அரசு - “பழைய கள், புதிய மொந்தை” என்ற பழமொழிக்கேற்ப, தன்னுடைய அராஜக ஆட்டத்தை இதோ மீண்டும்
தொடர்ந்திருக்கின்றது.

தவறுகள் -
கொள்ளைகள் -
கொலைகள் -

எங்கே நடைபெற்றாலும் - அதை யார் நடத்தினாலும் -
அவர்கள் மன்னிக்கப்பட வேண்டியவர்கள் என்று சொல்லுகின்ற

கண்டு மேலும் மேலும் எரிச்சல் மிகக் கொண்டு - “எங்கிருக்கிறான்
எதிர்க்கட்சித் தோழன்? எங்கிருக்கிறது அவன் குடும்பம்? எங்கே

பொய்த் திரை போட்டு -

இந்தப் புவியில் வாழ்வோர் கண்களை இருட்டாக்கி - இன்னும்
எத்தனை காலம் இங்குள்ளவரை ஏமாற்ற நினைத்திடுவார் என்ற

“இனி தொலைவாய்” என்ற முழக்கமடா!

இதுதான் இன்றைய தமிழ்நாட்டு நிலை.

நீங்கள்தான் தேவையென்று திராவிட இன இளைஞர்களை
அன்று தட்டி எழுப்பிய பெரியாரும், அண்ணாவும் இதோ ஒன்றாக
ஓரணியில் நின்று உங்களை அழைக்கின்றார்கள்!
அறப்போர் குறித்து ஆயிரம் சாதனைகளைப் படைத்த
அண்ணன், தம்பிகளே!
ஆருயிர் உடன்பிறப்புகளே!

அதை நோக்கி விரைந்து நடந்து வாருங்கள்!
அல்லல் நீங்கும்!
தொல்லை குறையும்!
அன்புள்ள,
மு.க.
