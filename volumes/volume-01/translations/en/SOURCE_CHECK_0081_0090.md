## Letter-level notes

### 0081 — `தெள்ளிய ஆலின் சிறு பழத்தொரு விதை!`

Preserves the Rajamannar Committee / Sezhiyan–Maran committee sequence, the **8,226-word** report, the executive committee's debate extending into the early hours, and the banyan-seed image for the historically significant state-autonomy demand. The established formulation **“Autonomy in the State, federalism at the Centre”** is retained. Canonical PDF 299–300 controls source forms including `சட்டம் பயின்றோர்-அரசியல்`, `மாநிலத்தில் சுயாட்சி-மத்தியிலே கூட்டாட்சி`, `ராஜ மன்னார்` and `செழியன்-மாறன்`.

### 0082 — `பொங்கல் உறுதி!`

Preserves the Pongal stove-and-pot analogy: the movement bears hostile heat and turns it into achievement. Anna's “heart capable of bearing anything” and the shield / bow-and-arrow warning remain direct. Canonical PDF 301 controls the printed form `“நல்லவர்”(?)களுக்கும்`.

### 0083 — `காண வருகிறேன் உன்னை!`

Preserves the five-item campaign-headline sequence, the repeated `ஊழல்` rhetoric, the Russia/Hitler comparison, the cadre as warrior/hero and `படைக்கருவி`, and the intimate closing promise to leave Chennai and meet the Udanpirappē face to face. The source lineation of `வீரனே! / தீரனே! / வெற்றிச் சரிதம் / படைக்கும் என் / அன்பு உடன்பிறப்பே!` remains in the Tamil witness.

### 0084 — `‘தொய்வு இல்லை! தொடர்க பயணம்!’`

Preserves the budget's tax-relief, public-ownership and welfare argument together with Kalaignar's comparison to price increases imposed from Delhi. The closing flour/sweet, rain-cloud, sun and storm-boat images remain intact. The source's `திருவள்ளுவர் ஆலயம்` is rendered directly as **“a Thiruvalluvar shrine”** rather than silently substituting a later institutional name.

### 0085 — `“எல்லா மலர்களும் ரோஜா மலர்களே!”`

Preserves the internal-election argument that competition is not a clash of policy, ideals or doctrine and must not harden into permanent factions. The full lineated principles block and the closing image—**every flower in the movement's garden is a rose**—remain central.

### 0086 — `மே தின வாழ்த்து!`

Preserves Chicago and the eight-hour-day struggle, Periyar and Anna's May Day lineage, the catalogue of labour measures, the mocking `சமதர்ம சங்கீதம்` / `சங்கீத பூஷணம்` passage, and the pledge **“We shall extend a hand for friendship; we shall raise our voice for rights.”** Canonical PDFs 310–312 control the mixed source hyphenation and the colon after `தடைப்பட்டன:`.

### 0087 — `‘நமது நிலை’!`

Preserves the May 1974 railway-strike context, criticism of George Fernandes's arrest during negotiations, the Centre/State asymmetry in who may negotiate and who is merely instructed to enforce, the warning against sabotage, and the proposed no-victimisation settlement. The source's self-limiting line about the authority of Chief Ministers remains unsoftened.

### 0088 — `என் பிறந்த நாளில்...!`

Preserves Kalaignar's request to stop elaborate fiftieth-birthday celebrations and redirect affection into social and movement work. The three funds, the `கவியரங்கம் / பாட்டரங்கம் / கலையரங்கம் / கூத்தரங்கம்` sequence, `கழகக் குரல்`, feeding a poor person and making the poor person's smile endure are all retained.

### 0089 — `“மறவாதே!”`

Preserves the organisational-election argument through the earthen-lamp, equal-pearls and balance-needle images. Losing an internal contest is not rejection; neutrality must prevent factional rupture; and the closing command is to protect the movement's strength **`சிந்தாமல் சிதறாமல்`**. The callback to “Every flower is a rose to me” is retained.

### 0090 — `கோடையிலே...!`

Preserves the movement's affection as Kalaignar's `மாமருந்து`, the cyclone / simple `கட்டுமரம்` image, his explicit condemnation of Tamil–Malayali violence, the arrest/legal-action record, the *Alai Osai* sequence, the Kamaraj birthday comparison and the final sarcastic “cool breeze” / Congress-split counter-question. Canonical PDF 325 controls `காமராசரின்` and the lineated closing question.
