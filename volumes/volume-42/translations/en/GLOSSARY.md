## Locked method notes

- Preserve source-supplied English verbatim when printed as source wording.
- Preserve political accusation, irony, repetition, rhetorical questions and slogans as the author’s source framing.
- Retain Indian names, party abbreviations, dates and figures without importing outside corrections.
- Every English record carries the complete audited Tamil appendix with source-page markers.
