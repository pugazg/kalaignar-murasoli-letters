# 3502. Two Celebrations; One Comparison!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This translation follows the audited canonical Tamil and the locked Volume 44 conventions. Historical, archaeological, religious and legal assertions are translated as the author presents them and are not reconciled with outside scholarship. The Ayodhya judgment passage, the printed figure `17 இலட்சத்து 28 ஆயிரம் ஆண்டுகள்`, names and the final Dravidian/Aryan contrast are retained in source order. The complete audited Tamil is reproduced below and remains authoritative.

**Date:** 4 October 2010

**Udanpirappē,**

When I look together at two or three events that took place recently, I feel amazement and pride on one side, and surprise on the other. It is to share that with you that I write this letter.

On 20-9-2010, a spirited DMK Mupperum Vizha was held at Nagercoil with many thousands participating. At that function, the “Thanthai Periyar Award” was presented to younger brother Veerapandi Arumugam; the “Anna Award” to C. Kuppusamy; the “Pavendhar Bharathidasan Award” to Mrs Rajam John; and the “Kalaignar Award” to G. M. Shah. On behalf of the *Murasoli* Trust, prizes and certificates were given to students, certificates of merit, cash awards and medals to outstanding auto drivers, and financial assistance to students on behalf of the DMK Trust. In my speech I said:

> “The place from which I am speaking is Nagercoil. It is the place where the primordial Tamil appeared. This is not something said merely as imagination; it is in the region where the Lemurian continent existed that we have gathered today. That Lemurian continent changed and became Kanyakumari district, and this city of Nagercoil stands as an explanation of the evidence set out by our ancient poets and researchers — to the extent that the very name ‘Lemuria’ is uttered today!
>
> “Who are the Nagars? They are not some others — ‘Nagar’ refers to us. No one need think that Nagar means someone else, somewhere else. Even today we see the Nagercoil region as an example that the vast territory belonging by lineage to the Nagars was an abode of civilised culture. Nagapattinam and Nagercoil are all markers of the ancient land of the Tamils.
>
> “In those days when this place, including Kanyakumari, was called the Lemurian continent, even if we had not yet appeared, this was the place where our ancestors appeared. The civilisation sown by those ancestors gradually, little by little and step by step underwent the evolutionary growth that has become the civilisational development we experience today. The Nagercoil region is a land of such great fame and historical distinction.
>
> “On such an ancient land — a historically distinguished land — we are the descendants of people who appeared thousands, lakhs of years ago. Our culture is a culture of its own. Its name is Dravidian culture. When we say Dravidian culture, the important meaning in it is that there is another culture; there are people who can generally understand that distinction — that it is because another culture exists that we separately call this Dravidian culture. To distinguish Aryan culture, we have to speak of Dravidian culture. We are people steeped in and raised by Dravidian culture. That is why, if we have named this movement the ‘Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam’, the name of the organisation we created to hail Dravidian culture, cherish Dravidian culture and make Dravidian culture victorious is Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam.”

Thus I pointed to the evolutionary development of a Dravidian heritage that arose two thousand, three thousand or five thousand years ago. The saying that the Dravidian people are the “ancient people who appeared before stone and earth appeared” has come down generation after generation.

Likewise, as an example of Tamil art, tradition and culture, the thousandth-year celebration of Rajaraja’s Thanjavur Big Temple was held with great fervour in Thanjavur city, with lakhs upon lakhs of Tamil people participating. In my concluding address there, I described the many distinctions of the Chola period, a highly creative period in South Indian history; explained the nuances of the foreign trade, maritime trade and methods they employed; and, citing the views of researchers such as Nilakanta Sastri and Katyayana, set out evidence that the Chola empire endured in the South for 176 years. In that speech I said:

> “Rajaraja’s administration stood as an example for all kings. Guard forces on every frontier; his son Rajendra to move around the empire like a lion; old and trusted officials in newly conquered lands. Rajendra possessed the skill to keep with him officials endowed with political knowledge and sharp intelligence.”

I then explained the land-survey system, linear-measurement system, weighing system and the `kudavolai` method of local administration introduced in the time of Rajaraja Chola. This is the history of events that occurred a thousand years ago. Before our very eyes, the Thanjavur Big Temple built by Rajaraja himself and the inscriptions he had engraved remain enduring evidence for all these things.

On 30-9-2010, judgment was delivered in the case concerning Ayodhya, stating that the disputed land should be divided into three parts and distributed equally to two Hindu organisations and one Muslim organisation. Justice D. V. Sharma stated in his judgment:

> “The disputed place is indeed the birthplace of Rama. Rama is a god. He has been worshipped as one endowed with divinity. A structure was raised there by Babur. The year is not known with certainty. Idols were placed at the disputed site at midnight on December 22, 1949. Hindus have regarded the disputed place as Rama’s birthplace and worshipped there. From as far back as memory goes, they have regarded it as a sacred place and undertaken spiritual journeys there.”

Rama is said to have been born in the Krita Yuga. The Krita Yuga is said to comprise 17 lakh and 28 thousand years.

When we see a court judgment that determines with certainty that this was the place where Rama was born in an event said to have taken place such an unimaginably vast number of years ago, we can establish the place where Rama was born about 17 lakh years ago; yet we still cannot know how the great king Rajaraja Chola, who lived only a thousand years ago and built and ruled the South, died, where his tomb is, or where a memorial pillar for him stood. Can we not but feel pain and sorrow within?

Although the history of the Dravidian people has not been systematically written and arranged, through research on the Lemurian continent, research on the Indus civilisation and fundamental research on the Tamil language, archaeologists and linguists from foreign countries have expressed the view that Dravidian civilisation is at least three thousand years old.

On this basis, if the evidence for the history of our Dravidian people is compared with other histories, the world can clearly understand the true history of a Dravidian people who lived in a scientific spirit. It is enough if you understand that Aryan civilisation, which sought to push the Dravidian people aside, acted vigorously only to foster superstition among the people without basic evidence!

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**4-10-2010**

## Letter-specific notes

1. Statements about Lemuria, ancient chronology, Rajaraja Chola, the Ayodhya judgment and the Krita Yuga are translated strictly as the source frames them; this archival translation does not independently verify them.
2. The source figure `17 இலட்சத்து 28 ஆயிரம் ஆண்டுகள்` is retained as **17 lakh and 28 thousand years** rather than converted or reconciled.
3. `குடவோலை` is retained as **kudavolai**, with only its immediate administrative function indicated.

என்று குறிப்பிட்டு; இராஜராஜசோழன் காலத்தில் நிர்வாகத்தில்
அறிமுகம் செய்துவைத்த நில அளவை முறை, நீட்டல் அளவை
முறை, நிறுத்தல் அளவை முறை, ஊராட்சிக்கான குடவோலை
முறை ஆகியவற்றைப் பற்றியெல்லாம் எடுத்துரைத்தேன். இது
ஆயிரம் ஆண்டு காலத்திற்கு முன்னர் நடைபெற்ற நிகழ்ச்சிகளின்
சரித்திரம். இவையனைத்திற்கும் ஆதாரங்களாக இராஜராஜனே
எழுப்பியிருக்கும் தஞ்சைப் பெரியகோயிலும், பொறித்து
வைத்திருக்கும் கல்வெட்டுகளும் நீடித்து நிலைத்திருக்கும்
சான்றுகளாக நம் கண்முன்னே காட்சியளிக்கின்றன.
