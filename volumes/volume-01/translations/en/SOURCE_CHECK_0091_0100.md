## Letter-level notes

### 0091 — `நமது விழாக்கள்!`

Preserves Kalaignar's insistence that celebrations must produce political and social work rather than empty display. The dated sequence of Navalar's birthday, Anna's birthday, Periyar's birthday and the movement's silver jubilee remains intact, as does the closing image that the results of celebrations must become **supporting roots and seeds**.

### 0092 — `பயணம் தொடரட்டும்!`

Preserves the bitter sibling/betrayal analogies, the violent rhetoric Kalaignar quotes and answers with dark sarcasm, the contrast between former state-autonomy advocates and their present political conduct, and former Supreme Court judge Subba Rao's federalism/state-autonomy statement. The conclusion remains centred on historical continuity rather than personal survival.

### 0093 — `பிறந்த நாள் விழாவும் மறந்த சில விவரங்களும்!`

Preserves the deliberate opening praise of Kamaraj's seventy-second birthday celebration before Kalaignar develops his double-standard argument. The N. S. Krishnan, Periyar and Anna recollections, the Katchatheevu / *Navasakthi* passage, the lineated `கேலி / கிண்டல் / ஏசல் / ஏளனம் / ஏகடியம்` sequence, both *Dinamani* float quotations and the Kannadasan paragraph are retained.

### 0094 — `நேரமும் நினைப்பும்!`

Preserves the Police Commission / *Udhaya Suriyan* argument and the complete colloquial police-family lullaby, followed by the national prohibition argument. The flower-patterned-glass and matchbox satire, the repeated rabid-dog metaphor and the closing `நேரம் / நினைப்பு` turn remain direct rather than softened.

### 0095 — `‘மாம்பழ மங்கை!’`

Preserves the playful chain of maiden similes, the Ranipet newspaper report, satire of miracle-workers and credulity, the Vadakalai–Thenkalai elephant dispute, and the political comparison between a handless mango maiden and attractive parties without principles. Canonical PDF 342 controls the source-spaced `பகுத்தறிவு வாதிகளின்`.

### 0096 — `அதே தேதிகள்!`

Preserves the source's abrupt opening without inventing a salutation or missing line. Kattabomman's betrayal on **24 September 1799** is kept in parallel with Kalaignar's claim that betrayal of the movement began at the Chengalpattu meeting on **24 September 1972**. The reconstructed Kattabomman fort remains the final proof-image that history returns to view.

### 0097 — `இன்றே தயாராகு!`

Preserves the `முப்பெரும் விழா` mobilisation, the contrast between principle and merely capturing the Fort, the movement's scars/prisons/sacrifices, the weather-and-earthquake sequence, and the repeated summons to prepare immediately and gather in Chennai from hamlet, village, town and city.

### 0098 — `உயர்த்திடுவோம் தோள்!`

Preserves Anna's “double-barrelled gun” formulation for the DK and DMK, the joint anti-Hindi action, the Miran Sahib Street exchange, Anna's Legislative Assembly defence of Periyar, and the three-pronged-agitation reference. The governing closing metaphor remains the movement as a **thousand-year crop** threatened by weeds of betrayal, pests of opportunism and grazing enemies.

### 0099 — `வழி மேல் விழி...`

Preserves Kalaignar's movement tour amid Murasoli Selvam's health crisis, the Madurai and Kanchi emotional sequence, printed initials `ப. உ. ச.`, the cadres' affection as medicine for his wounded mind, and the closing invitation to join the 29 September Chennai procession while he waits **with eyes fixed on the road**.

### 0100 — `அழைக்கிறேன் - வா! அன்பே வா!`

Preserves the dense Dharmapuri–Salem–North Arcot travel chronology, delayed meals and roadside vadai, the `முக்கனி` comparison of DMK/Anna/Periyar, and the Chennai mobilisation. The *Kalingattuparani* verses, Kulothunga comparison and closing repeated `வா, வா!` summons remain central. Source wording in the canonical Tamil witness is preserved without speculative repair.
