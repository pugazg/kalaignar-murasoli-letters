## Scope and method

This audit covers five consecutive letters and **27/27 canonical pages**. Every page was visually compared with the rendered original scan at readable resolution. The review checked letter titles, salutations, page/paragraph continuity, quoted newspaper passages, deliberate English text, names, dates, figures, punctuation, quotation marks, closing/signature/date pages, and possible omissions or duplicated text.

The scan remained authoritative. Source-specific wording, spacing, spellings and typography were not silently modernised or fact-corrected. OCR was not used as the authority for this pass.

## Source forms and material deliberately preserved

The audit retained the scan's wording and presentation without external normalisation, including:

- the printed English headline on PDF 193: `Atrocities against Dalits touched their peak in 2012, finds study`;
- the quoted `இந்து`, `தினமலர்` and `தினத்தந்தி` material and their printed punctuation;
- district, village, institutional and personal names in the forms printed by the source;
- the large sets of dates, compensation figures, water-project figures and fisheries statistics across letters 3731, 3732 and 3735;
- the mixed Tamil/English political and technical expressions, including `ரன்-த்ரூ`, `சஸ்பென்ட்`, `அப்பீல்`, `ஜி.பி.எஸ்.` and other scan-visible forms;
- source-specific word spacing and line splits where they are part of the printed page presentation; and
- the ritual/superstition terminology in letter 3734 without editorial rewriting.

No lexical reading was changed in this batch. All five corrections are punctuation or quotation-spacing fidelity fixes.
