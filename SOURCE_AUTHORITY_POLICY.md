# Source Authority and No Outside Comparison Policy

**Status:** Mandatory repository-wide policy for canonical Tamil transcription and Tamil source-fidelity work.

## 1. Controlling authority

For every volume, the attached/original target PDF scan is the **sole textual authority** for canonical Tamil transcription.

Canonical Tamil may be accepted only when the reading is supported by the target scan itself.

## 2. Outside-source comparison is prohibited by default

During routine transcription, review, correction, boundary verification, textual-fidelity audit, or reconstruction, **do not use outside sources to supply, verify, correct, guess, or fill source wording**.

This prohibition includes, but is not limited to:

- web search;
- Google or other search engines;
- blogs and personal websites;
- Wikipedia or Wikisource;
- Internet Archive or other online mirrors;
- newspaper/archive reproductions;
- OCR corpora or searchable mirrors not produced from the controlling target scan;
- another edition of the same work;
- another volume or publication containing a parallel passage;
- quotations reproduced in secondary sources.

Do not search the web merely to resolve an unclear word, quotation, poem, name, figure, title, or passage.

## 3. What is allowed

- Direct visual reading of the controlling target scan.
- OCR or parsed text generated from the **same target scan** as a drafting/discrepancy aid only; the scan remains authoritative.
- Repository files used for workflow/state/navigation.
- The repository's shared-front-matter reuse workflow under `SERIES_FRONT_MATTER_POLICY.md`, but only after direct visual comparison proves that the target scan page matches the approved shared reference.

## 4. Unclear or missing text

If printed text exists but cannot be read confidently from the target scan, do not reconstruct it from another source. Use the repository-approved unresolved marker, normally:

`[தெளிவில்லை]`

If material is genuinely absent from the controlling source, record it as a source gap / `source-incomplete` condition under the existing repository rules. Do not import missing text from elsewhere.

## 5. Explicit user-approved comparison exception

Outside-source comparison is allowed **only when the user explicitly asks for it**.

When such a comparison is requested:

1. keep the outside evidence clearly labelled as non-canonical comparison material;
2. do not silently copy it into canonical Tamil;
3. do not change canonical Tamil unless the controlling target scan independently supports that reading;
4. if the target scan remains unclear, keep the canonical reading unresolved.

User approval for one comparison does not create a standing exception for later letters, pages, volumes, or audits.

## 6. Audit requirement

Every transcription/audit workflow must treat compliance with this policy as part of source fidelity.

A canonical Tamil reading that was supplied from an unapproved outside source must be rechecked against the controlling scan and removed or marked unresolved if the scan alone does not support it.
