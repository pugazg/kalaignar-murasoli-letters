The aligned English now reads: **“the Election Commission—the Brahma-rakshasa-like monster that charted an authoritarian ‘path’ for Tamil Nadu alone, using its own `ஜபர்தஸ்து` measures and anti-democratic actions as whips?”** The source form remains untouched in Tamil.
