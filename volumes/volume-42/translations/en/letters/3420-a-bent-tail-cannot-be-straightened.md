# 3420. A Bent Tail Cannot Be Straightened!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This letter responds to statements about meetings and permissions connected with the Mullaperiyar dispute. It reproduces two source-supplied English extracts from official proceedings verbatim and sets out an all-party resolution and the author’s chronology. All accusations and legal-administrative claims remain the author’s source framing. The complete audited Tamil is reproduced below and remains authoritative.

**Source pages:** [PDF 360](../../../pages/page-360.md)–[PDF 364](../../../pages/page-364.md)  
**Date:** 16 October 2009

**Udanpirappē,**

I have written many times about what I regard as Jayalalithaa’s lack of knowledge and haste.

Even so, the author says, her statements continue to show that she is not prepared to correct herself.

In her statement yesterday, she made what he calls a major accusation against me.

She said that on 29-11-2006 — several months after the DMK government led by Karunanidhi had taken over the administration — the Union Irrigation Minister convened a meeting in Delhi regarding the Mullaperiyar issue; that the Kerala Chief Minister attended; and that I did not attend.

When a Leader of the Opposition makes such a major accusation against a Chief Minister, should she not first find out whether it is true?

Although I do not wish to answer her statements, the author says, when falsehoods are repeated and newspapers publish them, if I remain silent will what she says not come to be treated as true?

There was sarcasm in the statement too.

She asked why Karunanidhi had time to seek prosperous portfolios in the Union Cabinet for his son, daughter and grandson, but did not have time to attend the meeting convened by the Union Irrigation Minister.

Here is my answer, the author says.

Jayalalithaa, the truth is that I, as Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu, **did attend** the meeting held in Delhi on 29-11-2006 in the presence of Union Water Resources Minister Mr. Saifuddin Soz.

Then Public Works Minister younger brother Duraimurugan, Electricity Minister Arcot Veerasamy, the Chief Secretary and senior officials also attended on behalf of Tamil Nadu.

I spoke at that meeting, and the speech was reported in newspapers at the time.

A photograph of me participating in the meeting was also published.

After the meeting I spoke in detail to journalists in Delhi and Chennai about the issue, and that too appeared in the newspapers.

Without even turning the pages of those national newspapers, the author says, Jayalalithaa issued her accusation in haste.

In my opening remarks at the meeting, I said the dam was strong, that there was no basis for fear or confusion about its safety, and that I hoped the Kerala government would wholeheartedly cooperate in implementing the Supreme Court’s order dated 27-2-2006.

Therefore, the author says, Jayalalithaa’s claim that I did not participate is a complete falsehood.

At that meeting, the Kerala Chief Minister said for the first time that building a new dam would be the solution to the issue.

In response, the Tamil Nadu Public Works Minister pointed out that construction of a new dam had been considered as early as 1979 and later abandoned after being regarded as infeasible.

The Union Government itself attached the minutes of that meeting to its letter dated 8-12-2006.

The source reproduces the following wording verbatim:

> “xxx. In so far as the proposal of constructing a new dam is concerned, this option was examined in 1979 and was ruled out at that time. xxx”

Later, when Kerala’s Irrigation Minister again insisted that construction of a new dam would be the solution, the Tamil Nadu Chief Secretary replied.

The source again reproduces the official English verbatim:

> “xxx. The Chief Secretary, Tamil Nadu also responded by saying that the proposal of constructing a new dam has already been deliberated earlier and the same has not been found feasible. xxx”

I will give further details, the author says.

Following the Supreme Court’s order dated 25-9-2006, concerning Tamil Nadu’s participation in talks between Tamil Nadu and Kerala ministers under the Union Water Resources Minister, I convened an all-party leaders’ meeting on **23-10-2006**.

On behalf of the AIADMK, Mr. O. Panneerselvam and Mr. K. A. Sengottaiyan attended.

If Jayalalithaa had any doubt, the author says, she could have asked them and learned the facts before issuing her statement.

That all-party meeting unanimously adopted a resolution.

In translation, it said:

> This meeting agrees, as a gesture of goodwill and on the basis of the Supreme Court’s observations, that the Tamil Nadu government may hold talks with the Kerala government in the presence of the Union Government, while pointing out Kerala’s refusal to implement the Supreme Court judgment that accepted the arguments advanced by Tamil Nadu on the Mullaperiyar issue.
>
> However, if those talks do not produce the proper result, the Tamil Nadu government should immediately approach the Supreme Court again and seek an order implementing the Court’s 27-2-2006 direction that the water level in the dam may be raised from **136 feet to 142 feet**.
>
> It should also seek implementation of the direction that Kerala must extend full cooperation when the remaining strengthening works are carried out and that Kerala government officials must not cause any obstruction to the Tamil Nadu government.
>
> The meeting further asks the Union Government to take action to implement such a Supreme Court order.

The author then turns to another claim in Jayalalithaa’s statement.

She said that on 16-9-2009 a meeting of the Standing Committee of the National Board for Wildlife was held under Union Minister of State for Environment and Forests Jairam Ramesh, that senior officials from several States attended, and that it was surprising no one attended on behalf of Tamil Nadu.

The author says this accusation too was made without first learning the facts.

The Standing Committee of the National Board for Wildlife was constituted under the Wildlife Protection Act, 1972.

The Union Environment and Forests Minister chairs it, and, according to the source, representatives only from Orissa and Uttarakhand were appointed as State representatives on a rotating basis.

No Tamil Nadu government representative was a member of the Standing Committee.

When a proposal is received from a State government recommending that forest land be used for a non-forest purpose, only the Chief Wildlife Warden of the concerned State is invited to the committee meeting so that the matter may be considered.

The meeting on 16-9-2009 was held to discuss a proposal sent by Kerala and proposals received from several other States.

Therefore no invitation was sent to Tamil Nadu’s Chief Wildlife Warden, and no one represented Tamil Nadu at that meeting.

But as soon as a newspaper report said that permission had been granted to Kerala at that meeting to conduct a survey, I wrote to Union Minister of State Jairam Ramesh on **22-9-2009**.

I asked that permission to conduct the survey not be granted without seeking the Tamil Nadu government’s view.

When information was received that, despite this, the Union Minister of State had approved Kerala’s survey on **6-10-2009**, I wrote in detail to the Prime Minister of India on **8-10-2009**.

Even before that, on **7-10-2009**, the Tamil Nadu government filed a petition in the Supreme Court seeking a stay.

That matter, the source says, was due to come before the Supreme Court on the 20th.

When these are the facts, the author says, Jayalalithaa has tried to sell bundles of falsehoods, and he asks the people of Tamil Nadu to understand this.

Udanpirappē,

This is the last letter I will write for people like Jayalalithaa, the author concludes.

No matter how much one says or writes, **a bent tail cannot be straightened**.

So I stop here.

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**16-10-2009**

அடுத்து, ஜெயலலிதா அவருடைய அறிக்கையிலே 16-9-2009 அன்று மீண்டும் மத்திய சுற்றுச் சூழல் மற்றும் வனத்துறை இணை அமைச்சர் ஜெய்ராம் ரமேஷ் தலைமையில் நடைபெற்ற தேசிய உயிரினங்கள் வாரியத்தின் நிலைக்குழு கூட்டத்தில் இந்தியாவின் பல்வேறு மாநிலங்களைச் சேர்ந்த மூத்த அதிகாரிகள் கலந்து கொண்டதாகவும், அந்தக் கூட்டத்தில் தமிழ்நாட்டின் சார்பிலே யாருமே கலந்து கொள்ளாதது வியப்பளிக்கிறது என்றும் கூறியிருக்கிறார். இதுவும் உண்மை என்ன என்பதை அறிந்து கொள்ளாமல் குற்றஞ்சாட்ட வேண்டும் என்ற ஒரே நோக்குடன் எழுதியிருக்கிறார்.

தேசிய வன விலங்கு வாரியத்தின் நிலைக்குழு என்பது 1972ஆம் ஆண்டைய வன விலங்கு சட்டத்தின் அடிப்படையில் ஏற்படுத்தப்பட்டுள்ளது. அந்த நிலைக்குழுவின் தலைவராக மத்திய சுற்றுச் சூழல் மற்றும் வன அமைச்சர் அவர்களும், சுழற்சி முறையில் ஒரிசா மற்றும் உத்தரகண்ட் மாநிலங்களின் பிரதிநிதிகள் மட்டுமே உறுப்பினர்களாக நியமிக்கப்பட்டுள்ளனர். நிலைக்குழு கூட்டத்தில் தமிழக அரசின் பிரதிநிதிகள் எவர் ஒருவரும் உறுப்பினராக இல்லை. ஏதாவது ஒரு மாநிலத்திலிருந்து வன நிலங்களை வனம் சாராத பிற பணிகளுக்கு பயன்படுத்த மாநில அரசின் பரிந்துரையுடன் கருத்துரு பெறப்பட்டால், சம்பந்தப்பட்ட மாநில தலைமை வன உயிரினக் காப்பாளரை மட்டுமே குழுவின் கூட்டத்திற்கு அழைத்து கலந்து முடிவெடுக்கப்படும். 16-9-2009 அன்று நடத்தப்பட்ட கூட்டமானது கேரள மாநிலம்

அனுப்பிய கருத்துரு குறித்தும், வேறு சில மாநிலங்களிலிருந்து பெறப்பட்ட கருத்துருக்கள் பற்றியும் விவாதிப்பதற்காக என்பதால், அந்தக் கூட்டத்திற்கு தமிழக அரசின் தலைமை வன உயிரினக் காப்பாளருக்கு அழைப்பிதழ் எதுவும் அனுப்பப்பட வில்லை. ஆகவே தமிழகத்தின் சார்பில் யாரும் அந்தக் கூட்டத்தில் கலந்து கொள்ளவில்லை.

ஆனால் அந்தக் கூட்டத்தில் கேரள அரசுக்கு ஆய்வு செய்ய அனுமதி வழங்கப்பட்டதாக பத்திரிகைகளில் ஒரு செய்தி வந்தவுடன் 22-9-2009 அன்று நான் மத்திய இணை அமைச்சர் திரு. ஜெய்ராம் ரமேஷ் அவர்களுக்கு கடிதம் எழுதினேன். தமிழக அரசின் கருத்தைக் கேட்காமல் ஆய்வு நடத்த அனுமதி வழங்கக் கூடாது என்றும் கேட்டுக் கொண்டேன். அதையும் மீறி 6-10-2009 அன்று ஆய்வு நடத்த கேரள அரசுக்கு மத்திய இணை அமைச்சர் ஒப்புதல் வழங்கிவிட்டார் என்ற செய்தி கிடைத்தவுடன் இந்தியப் பிரதமருக்கு விரிவாக 8-10-2009 அன்று கடிதம் எழுதினேன். அதற்கு முன்பாகவே 7-10-2009 அன்றே உச்ச நீதிமன்றத்திலும் தடையாணை வழங்க தமிழக அரசின் சார்பில் மனு தாக்கல் செய்தோம். அந்த விசாரணை வரும் 20ஆம் தேதி உச்ச நீதிமன்றத்திலே வரவுள்ளது.

உண்மை இவ்வாறிருக்க ஜெயலலிதா பொய்களை மூட்டையாகி விற்க முனைந்துள்ளார் என்பதை தமிழ்நாட்டு மக்கள் புரிந்து கொள்ள வேண்டுகிறேன்.
