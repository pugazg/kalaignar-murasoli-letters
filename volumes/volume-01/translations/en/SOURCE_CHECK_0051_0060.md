# Volume 1 — English Source Check: Letters 0051–0060

Status: **PASS — migrated and source-checked; bilingual alignment remains a later gate.**

## Scope

This batch covers canonical Volume 1 letters **0051–0060**, spanning PDF / printed pages **214–249**.

The preserved legacy bilingual records under `volumes/volume-1/translations/en/letters/` were used only as migration drafts and evidence. The fully fidelity-verified canonical Tamil layer and canonical chapter/page structure under `volumes/volume-01/` remained authoritative.

## Method

For each of the ten letters:

1. compared the legacy English draft with the complete source-checked Tamil reading copy and canonical chapter metadata;
2. preserved the source title, printed date, sign-off, names, figures, quotations, political terminology, rhetorical questions and thought order;
3. migrated the complete Tamil text into the canonical English record;
4. recorded canonical PDF/printed-page traceability;
5. marked the record `source-checked`, not `verified`;
6. deferred meaning-level bilingual alignment and volume-wide editorial consistency review to their dedicated later gates.

## Batch result

All ten records are now present under `translations/en/letters/` and source-checked. No new substantive English omission comparable to the earlier letter 0019 gap was identified in this batch. The legacy bilingual tree remains unchanged.

## Letter-level notes

### 0051 — `அகல்விளக்கு கையில் அண்ணன் மொழி நெஞ்சில்!`

Preserves the earthen-lamp journey metaphor, the deeply personal passage concerning Muthu and his late mother, Anna's extended quotation on reform without humiliation, the Sampath parallel, Nanjil Manoharan's house-wall line, and printed date **23-10-1972**.

### 0052 — `பிப்ரவரி மூன்றாம் நாள் !`

Preserves the Anna memorial appeal, the cadre's labour as the “ink” of their reply, the State-autonomy and anti-Hindi passages, the lineated praise of Anna, the memorial pledge, and the closing Kattabomman/Ettappan warning.

### 0053 — `நட வேகமாக! நமக்கே வெற்றி!!`

Preserves the Dindigul by-election context, the candidates' shifting affiliations, the social-policy examples, the hand-rickshaw and eyesight-programme arguments, and the rhythmic closing exhortation `நட வேகமாக; நமக்கே வெற்றி!`.

### 0054 — `ஐந்தாம் ஜார்ஜா? ஆறாம் ஜார்ஜா?`

Preserves the A. C. George satire, the serpent-and-Garuda anecdote, and the source-verified Chinnalapatti yarn-card figure **(6908)**. The canonical Tamil retains the unusual source-printed joined form `ஆறாயிரத்துத்தொள்ளாயிரத்து எட்டு` rather than normalising it to the stale legacy spacing.

### 0055 — `“அரிதாரம் கலையும்!”`

Preserves the post-Dindigul resignation argument, the Mujibur Rahman/Awami League comparison, the Banka and Kerala examples, the Bharathidasan references, and the theatrical `அரிதாரம்` / greasepaint closing image.

### 0056 — `சிந்தனைக்கு!`

Preserves the 1952 Assembly figures **166 / 152**, Rajaji's Aruppukkottai by-election and confidence-motion precedent, the critique of political forgetfulness, and the closing appeal to establish a shared grammar of political civility.

### 0057 — `“பிறந்த நாள் வேண்டுகோள்!”`

Preserves the fiftieth-birthday Rickshaw Fund appeal, the earlier rehabilitation programme for leprosy-afflicted beggars, the eyesight-restoration programme, the request to substitute contributions for travel, garlands and posters, and the source date **28-05-1973**.

### 0058 — `“நடக்கின்ற ஆச்சரியம்!”`

Preserves the lineated worker-centred verse, the intentionally impossible Rasputin/Tsar/Louis/religious-elite comparisons and the turn to fan-club versus Kazhagam workers. The authoritative canonical chapter gives the source date **29-05-1973**; the stale legacy translator-note claim about a 1978 metadata date is not carried into the canonical record.

### 0059 — `இதுதான் உலகம்!`

Preserves the movement's cadre history, references to Anna, Periyar, Navalar and Kamaraj, the *Ashok Kumar* / *Alibaba* comparison, welfare for freedom fighters and language/border activists, and the inset R. M. Veerappan letter dated **22 April 1972** praising Kalaignar for honouring veteran worker Rama Subbaiah.

### 0060 — `“கண்ணீர் மடல்!”`

Preserves the 31 May 1973 plane-crash account, the overnight telephone sequence, the wooden-horse recollection concerning Mohan Kumaramangalam, memories of Gurnam Singh and Baladhandayutham, the other named victims, and the source's grief-stricken closing on **02-06-1973**.
