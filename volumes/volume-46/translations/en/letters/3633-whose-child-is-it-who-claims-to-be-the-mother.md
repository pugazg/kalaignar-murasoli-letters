# 3633. Whose Child Is It; Who Claims to Be the Mother?

**Udanpirappē,**

Chief Minister Jayalalithaa left yesterday afternoon by a special aircraft for whirlwind campaigning in the Pudukkottai constituency, campaigned at nine places, and returned to Chennai last night. The Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam is not contesting that constituency. Yet it seems Jayalalithaa cannot find her speech unless she attacks the DMK and me!

At the beginning of her speech she did not forget to say that the by-election was being held because Brother Muthukumaran of the Indian Communist Party, who had won the constituency with AIADMK support, had died. But when the question arose of whom to field there, she seems to have forgotten that the person who had contested the constituency in the general election belonged to the Indian Communist Party. They unilaterally announced that the AIADMK would contest. Did it not occur to them that this was a seat earlier contested by their ally, the Indian Communist Party, and that, if only for political civility and courtesy, they might call that party’s leaders and ask them one word? Even if called and asked, is that leader one who would refuse? They could at least have invited him once for courtesy’s sake. They did not have the mind even for that.

But after putting up their own candidate, when the time came to seek votes they summoned the family of the late Muthukumaran to Fort St. George and Chief Minister Jayalalithaa granted them an audience. She called that party’s leaders for talks at the Fort and released the photograph in the newspapers; and now, even while beginning her Pudukkottai campaign, she carefully remembers to say that the by-election is being held because Brother Muthukumaran died an untimely death!

Speaking in Pudukkottai, Jayalalithaa mocked our decision not to contest, saying: “During the Sankarankovil by-election campaign, DMK leader Karunanidhi said that what was taking place was not an `idai`—a by-election—but an `edai`—an election weighing the performance of the Government headed by Jayalalithaa. Now, unable to face this `idai` election, he has tied up his `nadai` and walked away.”

In the same speech she herself said that the Government had granted ₹50 crore as a special assistance to improve Pudukkottai municipality’s basic infrastructure, and had also ordered the erection of a centenary memorial pillar and arches. The Election Commission does not seem even to have noticed whether it is proper for the Government to do such special things in a constituency where a by-election is under way, contrary to election rules. It was because we had already understood all this, and because even before the election was notified an army of ministers had been piled into that constituency and money poured out with police assistance, that the DMK announced it would not contest the Pudukkottai by-election.

Have there never been occasions when the AIADMK itself “packed up and walked away” from by-elections? When a by-election was held in the Cumbum Assembly constituency on 18 August 2009, the AIADMK did not contest. When DMK candidate Cumbum Ramakrishnan contested, it was the DMDK that stood against him.

When a by-election was held in Srivaikuntam on 18 August 2009, the DMK, with political civility, left the seat to the Congress Party, which had contested it in the preceding general election. When Congress candidate Sudalaiyandi contested, did the AIADMK not “pack up and walk away” without contesting?

Likewise, in the Thondamuthur by-election held the same day, did the AIADMK contest? No. The Congress, an ally of the DMK, contested on behalf of our front. Jayalalithaa packed up her walk then too and did not contest.

And in the Bargur by-election on that same day, did Jayalalithaa not pack up and walk away instead of contesting? Does she imagine that the people of Tamil Nadu have no memory when she goes to Pudukkottai and says the DMK has walked away?

Need more be said? What happened in the Ilayangudi by-election? The AIADMK did not contest at all. Why? Was that too an act of packing up the walk?

During DMK rule from 2006 to 2011, in all eleven Assembly by-elections the DMK and its ally the Congress alone won. Jayalalithaa’s AIADMK contested six seats and did not win even one. In particular, in the Pennagaram by-election held on 27 March 2010, DMK candidate P.N.P. Inbasekaran received 77,669 votes; PMK candidate G.K.M. Tamil Kumaran came second with 41,285; Jayalalithaa’s AIADMK came only third with 26,787—and not merely came third, it lost even its deposit.

With the thought that people may have forgotten all this, Jayalalithaa went to Pudukkottai and mockingly asked voters to make the parties opposing her lose their deposits.

Even when our dear friend M.G.R. was AIADMK leader and Chief Minister, the DMK won by-elections in Anna Nagar and Mayiladuthurai. Therefore, Ammaiyar—**guard your tongue!**

Speaking at Pudukkottai, Jayalalithaa said she knew people were suffering because of power cuts; blamed the previous DMK Government; said future power needs would be met; and spoke—or rather read out—a list of how much electricity would soon be available from various power projects. I have said many times in statements that every power station in the list she read was a project whose basic infrastructure work was begun during DMK rule.

For example, the project to generate 600 MW through the Ennore Thermal Power Station expansion was begun during DMK rule on 8 December 2006 at an estimated cost of ₹3,136 crore. Production from that project is to begin in August 2013. It is this DMK-initiated project that Jayalalithaa spoke about in Pudukkottai as though it were coming through her own Government and efforts.

At Mettur Thermal Power Station, a project capable of generating 600 MW at an estimated cost of ₹3,100 crore was begun on 2 May 2007. Production from that DMK-era project is now about to arrive.

At North Chennai Thermal Power Station, a 600 MW project was begun during DMK rule on 26 June 2007 at an estimated ₹2,475 crore; its production too is expected very soon.

At Udangudi, under a joint project of BHEL and the Tamil Nadu Electricity Board, two units each capable of producing 800 MW were begun during DMK rule on 15 October 2007 at an estimated ₹8,362 crore. It is this very project that Jayalalithaa has now announced will be begun again under the present Government.

After the DMK Government took office in 2006, various steps were taken with future demand in mind, providing a path for 1,400 MW in 2010–11, 3,316 MW in 2011–12, 1,222 MW in 2012–13 and 1,860 MW in 2013–14—a total of **7,798 MW**—to become available through various power projects.

After the DMK Government was formed in Tamil Nadu in 2006, many new factories arose in different places and electricity demand increased. Had Jayalalithaa, who led the AIADMK Government from 2001 to 2006, calculated future demand and undertaken efforts on this scale at that time, there would not now have been a power shortage and the people of Tamil Nadu would not have been made to suffer.

The voters of Pudukkottai know very well that, relying on the belief that they will not know these things, Chief Minister Jayalalithaa went there and spun the story that the DMK Government caused the electricity shortage and that she came and remedied it. **Some people may be deceived for some days; many may be deceived for many days; but everyone cannot be deceived forever!**

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**11 June 2012**

கோட்டையில் பேச்சு நடத்தி, புகைப்படத்தை ஏடுகளிலே வெளியிடுகிறார், தற்போது புதுக்கோட்டைக்குச் சென்று பிரச்சாரத்தைத் தொடங்கிய போதும் சகோதரர் முத்துக்குமரன் அகால மரணமடைந்ததையொட்டி நடைபெறும் இடைத்தேர்தல் என்றெல்லாம் ஞாபகத்தோடு சொல்லியிருக்கிறார்!

புதுக்கோட்டை தேர்தல் பிரச்சாரத்தில் ஜெயலலிதா பேசும்போது, “சங்கரன்கோவில் இடைத்தேர்தல் பிரச்சாரத்தின்போது நடைபெறுவது “இடைத்” தேர்தல் அல்ல; ஜெயலலிதா தலைமையிலான ஆட்சியின் செயல்பாடுகளை “எடை” போடும் தேர்தல் என்று சொன்ன தி.மு.க. தலைவர் கருணாநிதி, இந்த “இடைத்” தேர்தலை எதிர்கொள்ள முடியாமல் “நடை”யைக் கட்டி விட்டார்” என்று நாம் அந்தத் தொகுதியில் போட்டியிடாமல் புறக்கணித்ததைப் பற்றிக் கிண்டல் செய்திருக்கிறார்.

அவரே தனது பேச்சில் புதுக்கோட்டை நகராட்சியின் அடிப்படை கட்டமைப்பு வசதிகளை மேம்படுத்துவதற்காக அரசின் சார்பில் சிறப்பு உதவித் தொகையாக 50 கோடி ரூபாய் வழங்கப்பட்டதையும், மேலும் நூற்றாண்டு விழா நினைவுத் தூண் மற்றும் வளைவுகள் அமைக்க உத்தரவிட்டிருப்பதையும் சொல்லியிருக்கிறார். இடைத்தேர்தல் நடைபெறும் தொகுதியில் இவ்வாறு பிரத்தியேகமாக அரசின் சார்பில் தேர்தல் விதி முறைகளுக்கு மாறாக செய்வது சரிதானா என்பதை தேர்தல் ஆணையம் கண்டு கொண்டதாகவே தெரியவில்லை. அதையெல்லாம் ஏற்கனவே உணர்ந்த காரணத்தாலும், தேர்தல் அறிவிப்புக்கு முன்பேயே அமைச்சர்கள் பட்டாளத்தையே அந்தத் தொகுதியில் கொண்டு போய் குவித்து காவல் துறையினரின் துணையோடு பணத்தை வாரிவாரி இறைத்த காரணத்தாலும்தான் திராவிட முன்னேற்றக் கழகம் அந்தப் புதுக்கோட்டை இடைத்தேர்தலிலே போட்டியிடுவதில்லை என்று அறிவித்தது.

இதற்கு முன்பு அ.தி.மு.க. இடைத்தேர்தல்களில் “நடை”யைக் கட்டிய நிகழ்ச்சிகள் நடந்ததே கிடையாதா? 18-8-2009 அன்று கம்பம் சட்டமன்றத் தொகுதியில் இடைத்தேர்தல் நடைபெற்றபோது அ.தி.மு.க. அங்கே நிற்கவில்லை. தி.மு.க. சார்பில் கம்பம் ராமகிருஷ்ணன் அங்கே போட்டியிட்டபோது, அவரை எதிர்த்து தே.மு.தி.க. தான் போட்டியிட்டது.

18-8-2009 அன்று திருவைகுண்டம் தொகுதியில் இடைத்தேர்தல் நடைபெற்றபோது, தி.மு. கழகம் அரசியல் நாகரிகத்தோடு அதற்கு முன்பு நடைபெற்ற பொதுத் தேர்தலில் அந்தத் தொகுதியில் போட்டியிட்ட காங்கிரஸ் கட்சிக்கு விட்டுக் கொடுத்து - காங்கிரஸ் வேட்பாளராக திரு. சுடலையாண்டி போட்டியிட்ட போது, அந்தத் தொகுதியில் அ.தி.மு.க. போட்டியிடாமல் “நடை”யைக் கட்டியதை ஜெயலலிதா மறந்து விடலாமா?

அதுமாத்திரமல்ல; அதே நாளில் நடைபெற்ற பர்கூர் தொகுதி இடைத்தேர்தலிலும் ஜெயலலிதா “நடை”யைக் கட்டினாரே தவிர, போட்டியிடவில்லையே? தமிழ்நாட்டு மக்களுக்கு ஞாபகம் இருக்காது என்ற நம்பிக்கையில் புதுக்கோட்டையில் சென்று தி.மு.க. “நடை”யைக் கட்டியதாகச் சொன்னாரா?
