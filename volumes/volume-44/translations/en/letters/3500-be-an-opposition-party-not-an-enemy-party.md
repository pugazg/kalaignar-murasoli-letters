# 3500. Be an Opposition Party, Not an Enemy Party!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This translation follows the audited canonical Tamil and the locked Volume 44 conventions. The letter’s egg/Nutritious Meal policy argument, welfare figures, employment and allowance terms, and source-era designation `அரவாணிகள்` are retained without outside reconciliation or silent modernization. The complete audited Tamil is reproduced below and remains authoritative.

**Date:** 19 September 2010

**Udanpirappē,**

A bold-headline item in the 17-9-2010 issue of *Theekathir* caught my eye. Its title was, “Some Questions for the Chief Minister Who Provides Eggs Five Days a Week.” That paper is the official organ of the Communist Party of India (Marxist). At present, children from poor and simple households in Tamil Nadu are given three eggs a week. On the occasion of Perarignar Anna’s birthday, on behalf of the government I announced that this would be increased to five eggs. Unable to bear that announcement, reaching the very height of jealousy, that paper has put some questions to me. When I think of how far a paper that had resolved to work for people at the bottom has changed because of political animosity, I can only feel sorrow.

The report was published with an ulterior motive, saying poultry farmers had made a request and that the Chief Minister accepted it within twenty-four hours. Today’s Marxists may not know who it was that accepted the demands of `பண்ணையாளர்கள்`. My political life itself began in the Thanjavur soil by opposing such farm owners. Because of the bad influence of keeping company with those who did sinful things by accepting the demands of such interests, they have written this too with the same bad thought. The announcement was made not to benefit any poultry-farm owner but on the basic principle that children should truly receive nutritious food every day.

Is *Theekathir* prepared to publish evidence that anyone from the Namakkal Poultry Farmers Association mentioned by the Marxist paper met me before I made the announcement, gave me any request, and was told by me that I would accept it? Only after I made the announcement did members of that association seek an appointment through Union Minister of State Namakkal Gandhiselvan to thank me, and I met them yesterday. Even then, I was the one who told them something: the government has created conditions for egg production to increase; understanding the government’s generosity, you must come forward to reduce the price of eggs. Only then will this scheme continue. I told them that firmly.

The announcement I made on the evening of the 15th did not remain merely an announcement. The government issued an order on the 17th itself. That order states that students who do not eat eggs should be given bananas instead. Will the Marxist Communist paper immediately say that the demand of banana producers has been accepted?

There is a proverb: “Those who waited for it to be made will not wait for it to cool.” In that fashion, the very next day after the announcement, the paper hurriedly asked me many questions: how will the eggs be boiled, where will the eggshells be thrown, will chickpeas continue to be given, what will be done about all the related expenses? It was with such impatient people in mind that a detailed government order was issued this morning.

That order states clearly:

> “The 20 grams of boiled chickpeas or green gram already being provided on Tuesdays and the 20 grams of boiled potato on Fridays will continue to be provided. In addition, the 44 paise per child so far provided to Nutritious Meal centres for vegetables, firewood and related expenditure will be increased.”

In this situation, should *Theekathir* hurry and pour out its anger? There may be political opposition. But if you include every action needed by the people within that opposition, what will the children who are going to eat those eggs and their parents think about your party?

Along with the announcement on Anna’s birthday that students would receive eggs five days a week, I also announced that sanitation workers employed on a daily-wage or consolidated-pay basis in Tamil Nadu government offices, government-supported institutions and local bodies would receive an increase of about ₹1,000 each, so that the starting monthly pay would henceforth be ₹2,820. Whose demand was that? Which farm owner does the Marxist paper think placed that demand? Should not a paper that claims to work for people at the bottom have praised and written about that achievement announced for those people? If it has no heart to praise that, but invents an ulterior motive only for the provision of eggs, is that not the bad effect of the company it keeps — the source’s unusual `சேருவார் தோஷம்தானே?`

The government announced that swine-flu vaccine would be provided free to those below the poverty line. Should one immediately say that the scheme was announced for the sake of vaccine manufacturers?

Yesterday, on behalf of the government, I provided about ₹17 crore in incentive to milk producers. Should one immediately invent a meaning that the demand of milk producers has been accepted?

When a government that wishes to do good for the people is in office, it is normal for it to announce various welfare schemes and implement them. The Kalaignar Insurance Scheme was announced and poor and simple people welcome it. But if Opposition Leader Jayalalithaa alone issues a statement saying that ₹3,000 crore under that scheme has been given to somebody and that they are relatives of my family, should Marxist Communist friends speak and write in the same style — “was the mango sour, or was the mouth sour?”

I wish to point out one more example for them. After the DMK government was formed, when it decided to distribute colour television sets free to poor and simple people, they said it could not be done and wrote page after page that corruption would take place in purchasing the sets. But from the very beginning, for the purchase and distribution of the colour television sets, a committee comprising members of all parties in the Legislative Assembly was formed. Several times each year that committee met, open tenders were called for, those tenders were opened in the presence of all-party leaders, and each leader directly saw the prices quoted and gave approval before the purchase order was issued. Everyone knows this. Can anyone say that even a small corruption occurred in the purchase of more than one crore colour television sets?

Therefore, as far as this government is concerned, its concern is only to do good for the people.

Has this government merely increased the number of eggs for children eating Nutritious Meals through the Social Welfare Department? I shall mention here only a few achievements made through that department during the last four years.

Under the marriage financial-assistance scheme for poor women, ₹624 crore has been provided to 3,44,206 beneficiaries.

A scheme providing a monthly pension of ₹400 to poor women around the age of fifty who remain unmarried has been started, and up to 31-3-2010, 11,860 women had benefited.

Under the widow-remarriage financial-assistance scheme, ₹89 lakh has been provided during the last four years.

Under the inter-caste marriage financial-assistance scheme, ₹18 crore and 36 lakh has been provided to 9,545 couples during the last four years.

Under the marriage financial-assistance scheme for daughters of widows, about ₹18 crore has been provided to 11,197 beneficiaries.

Under the marriage financial-assistance scheme for destitute women, ₹199 lakh has been provided to 1,302 beneficiaries.

An Aravanis Welfare Board has been established through this department and various forms of assistance are being provided. So far, family cards have been provided to about one thousand Aravanis. House-site pattas have been provided to 451 Aravanis. In the 2009–2010 financial year, ₹1 crore was allocated for building houses for them.

To modernise Nutritious Meal centres, gas connections have been provided to 1,850 centres at a cost of ₹3 crore and 70 lakh.

Under the free-uniform scheme for children eating Nutritious Meals, ₹123 crore has been provided during the last four years for purchasing cloth.

Under DMK rule, Nutritious Meal and Anganwadi employees have received three revisions of their pay scale.

Under DMK rule, employees among Nutritious Meal and Anganwadi workers who completed ten years of service were given an increment.

Nutritious Meal cooks, helpers and Anganwadi helpers have all been receiving a special pay scale under DMK rule.

Under AIADMK rule, to which the Marxists are now allied, Nutritious Meal and Anganwadi employees received no allowances other than Dearness Allowance. Under DMK rule, for the first time, they are receiving House Rent Allowance, City Compensatory Allowance, medical allowance and festival advance.

Under AIADMK rule, Nutritious Meal and Anganwadi employees received no monthly pension. Under DMK rule, for the first time, all employees who have worked and retired since the Nutritious Meal Scheme began are receiving a pension.

Under DMK rule, their Pongal gift amount was raised to ₹825 from 28-12-2006 and again to ₹1,000 from 5-2-2008.

Under AIADMK rule they received no promotions. Under DMK rule, Nutritious Meal organisers are being promoted as record clerks, office assistants and second-level supervisors; Nutritious Meal assistants as cooks; and cooks and assistants as Nutritious Meal organisers.

Under DMK rule, the system of providing compassionate appointment to the heirs of Nutritious Meal cooks and assistants who die in service was introduced.

Under DMK rule, from 1-4-2010, the General Provident Fund scheme, a Special Savings Welfare Fund of ₹10,000, a Family Welfare Scheme of ₹1 lakh and 50 thousand, the Tamil Nadu government’s new Medical Insurance Scheme up to a maximum of ₹2 lakh, three months of maternity leave for women, and pay for Nutritious Meal workers during the summer vacation are all being provided.

In contrast to only 7,655 new appointments in vacant posts during the five-year AIADMK rule from 2001 to 2006, 57,000 posts have been newly filled during these four years of DMK rule.

Under AIADMK rule, Nutritious Meal organisers and Anganwadi workers received a minimum of ₹1,488 and a maximum of ₹2,158 per month. Under DMK rule they now receive a minimum monthly pay of ₹3,780 and a maximum monthly pay of ₹4,694.

When schemes are devised for the people, an opposition party should point out genuine faults and deficiencies if they exist. It should not become an enemy party that says something vague about something somewhere and spits irritation!

Udanpirappē, I have compiled the list of achievements being made through this department alone. I have also brought to your memory those who, unable to bear these achievements, write out of jealousy. Understand the truth!

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**19-9-2010**

## Letter-specific notes

1. The title’s `எதிர்க்கட்சி / எதிரிக்கட்சி` wordplay is preserved as **opposition party / enemy party**.
2. `அரவாணிகள்` is rendered **Aravanis**, preserving the source’s period-specific community designation rather than silently replacing it with a later preferred label.
3. All scheme counts, pay figures, allowances and dates are retained as stated in the audited source.
4. The unusual source expression `சேருவார் தோஷம்தானே?` is translated conservatively and left unchanged below.
