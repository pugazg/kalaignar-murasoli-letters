**Udanpirappē,**

A Government is truly functioning (?) in the State! In those days, it is said, a king would summon his minister and ask, “Minister, does rain fall three times a month in the great city?” Today, in place of the king, our Chief Minister summons ministers and officials all the way to Kodanadu and inquires whether rain is falling properly in the State. How much does it cost for the Chief Minister to stay in Kodanadu for so many days, and for officials and ministers to make their “pilgrimage” to see her? Is all that not the people’s tax money? I shall merely compile for you the reports appearing in today’s newspapers about what happened yesterday alone under the present AIADMK Government. You decide for yourself whether a Government is functioning or not!

- In Chennai’s Saidapet, two head constables, Sankar and Shahjahan, rolled about on the road fighting over the photographing of a bribe being received.
- In the Guindy Race Course premises in Chennai, a 45-year-old businessman named K. Ramesh was hacked to death.
- Arul, an auto driver from Korukkupet, Chennai, was beaten to death.
- College student Sakthikumar, who was staying in Avadi, died under mysterious circumstances.
- Traffic Sub-Inspector Karuppaiah quarrelled with a woman on Avinashi Road in Coimbatore while intoxicated.
- A High Court judge transferred to the CB-CID the investigation into the attack on the house of ruling AIADMK MLA V.P. Kalairajan.
- Ten huts in the Thousand Lights slum area of Chennai were destroyed by fire. This was the third fire accident in the same area within a month.
- At Pallavaram in Chennai, 23-year-old M. Subramani, a student of Anna University, died after being hit by a Government bus.
- On the same Pallavaram flyover, a private engineering-college bus suddenly caught fire and burned.
- Four IAS officers are on the waiting list because no postings have been allotted to them.
- Bhavanisagar Dam water level “a cause for concern” — agriculture becomes a question mark.
- The founder of the Indhiya Jananayaga Katchi has urged stern action against rice hoarders.
- Naam Tamilar Party members protested demanding the closure of the special refugee camps at Chengalpattu and Poonamallee; one thousand people were arrested and released.
- A newborn baby was abducted from Namakkal Government Hospital.
- At an Anganwadi centre near Cumbum, four-year-old Roshini was branded with heat.
- Nursing-school student Poomari died by suicide in the Madurai Government Hospital campus.
- Following a clash between two groups near Tiruchuli in Virudhunagar, five buses, including three Government buses, were damaged in stone-throwing.
- Near Ramanathapuram, the Melakottai village post-office official allegedly forged signatures and misappropriated ₹51,000.
- An undertrial prisoner named Mani died by suicide by hanging in Puzhal prison.
- Eighteen sovereigns of jewellery were stolen from the house of an official named Vivekumar in Thirumullaivoyal.
- Eleven flyover works across Tamil Nadu remain unfinished — Highways Department works left hanging in mid-air.
- Vice-Chancellor posts are vacant in six universities — many march upon Fort St. George seeking the posts.
- Paddy shortage — rice price rises — production halted in mills.
- Tamil Nadu Government fails to utilise Central funds — Union Minister Jairam writes to Jayalalithaa.
- No action against a teacher accused of “misbehaviour” — Education Department negligent in implementing the Chief Minister’s order.
- Near Madathukulam, an SI took a bribe from a sand-lorry driver.
- In Thoothukudi, people surrounded Minister Sengottaiyan.
- Theft on the police website — Anonymous group suspected.
- Irregularities in allotment of houses in Madurai.
- A prison official smuggled ganja into Salem Central Prison.
- Delay in appointing a Collector in Virudhunagar — development works affected.
- Near Gudalur, a lorry carrying 17 tonnes of explosives was seized.
- In Ambattur, nutritious meals are cooked in the open — a sorry state at a Government school.
- A chain was snatched from a woman named Meenakshi in Poonamallee.
- Encroaching shops spring up again in Koyambedu Market — administrative committee looks the other way.
- Will Chennai receive drinking water without shortage? — Krishna water inflow has fallen.
- A teacher slapped schoolgirl Madhumitha after demanding an additional fee.
- Irregularity in a liquor shop — TASMAC assistant manager suspended.
- Near Coimbatore, a car window was broken and ₹3.50 lakh stolen.
- Near Walajah, thirty people sold the goats given free to them.
- An *aimpon* idol at a worker’s house in Tiruppur.
- Misconduct toward a woman on the Pothigai Express.

Udanpirappē, I have reproduced here only the reports that appeared in the newspapers this morning. Is the Chief Minister, or are the other ministers, in the capital to attend to all these matters? But every day there is just one announcement in the Chief Minister’s name—appointment of sanitation workers, upgrading of revenue villages, appointment of guest lecturers—and these keep arriving by “fax” while she remains in Kodanadu. Should not all those announcements be properly discussed with ministers and officials before being issued? Are they being issued that way? Surely announcements meant actually to be implemented are discussed, decided upon and only then released. One may ask whether all these announcements are merely announcements and nothing more! Once something called a Government has been formed, whether or not that Government actually functions, should there not at least be the appearance that it functions?

If the Chief Minister wishes, she will take action against Sasikala, against her husband, and against people close to them such as Ravanan and Divakaran! She will register case after case. Now, if it is true that Ravanan, accused in those cases, has been summoned to Kodanadu itself and spoken to, whom are all these actions meant to deceive? What happened to all the cases filed against them? They said there was a complaint against Natarajan. Then they say the complaints that were given were withdrawn. If so, what action was taken against those who gave false complaints? Why did the police not conduct a proper preliminary inquiry into whether the complaint was false or true? What was the reason for that wrongful action by the police? After the person connected with that case came out of prison, he accused this Government of having attempted to “encounter” him; what is this Government’s answer to that? He said that he himself prepared the AIADMK election manifesto. He said the Chief Minister did not even know how to speak and that he was the one who wrote for her. Are all those things true? If this Government gives no answer, will not the people believe that everything he said was true? Why has he not opened his mouth since then?

What is the reason? Has he been restrained and told not to open his mouth? Has he been intimidated by this Government?

Is the newspaper report that Ravanan—who had been accused and imprisoned—came to Kodanadu to meet the Chief Minister true or not? If it is true, what did he come to discuss? Should not the country know all those truths? Only one thing seems to happen properly: taking revenge on the Opposition! What else is happening in the State? Can this be called *aatchi*—governance? Are they not merely changing *kaatchigal*—spectacles—staged one after another!

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**13 July 2012**

- கோவை அருகே கார் கண்ணாடியை உடைத்து 3.50 லட்சம் ரூபாய் திருட்டு.

- பொதிகை ரயிலில் பெண்ணிடம் முறைகேடு.

அமைச்சர்களோ தலைநகரிலே இருக்கிறார்களா? ஆனால் அன்றாடம் முதல் அமைச்சர் பெயரால் ஒரு அறிவிப்பு மட்டும்; துப்புரவுப் பணியாளர் நியமனம், வருவாய்க் கிராமங்கள் உயர்வு, கவுரவ விரிவுரையாளர் நியமனம் என்று மட்டும் கொடநாட்டிலே இருந்தவாறே “பேக்ஸ்” மூலமாகவே வந்து கொண்டிருக்கின்றன. அந்த அறிவிப்புகள் எல்லாம் முறையாக அமைச்சர்களுடனும் அதிகாரிகளுடனும் விவாதிக்கப்பட்டு, அதன் பிறகு வெளிவர வேண்டியவை அல்லவா? அவ்வாறு அந்த அறிவிப்புகள் வருகின்றவா? செயல்படுத்தப் போகின்ற அறிவிப்புகள்தானே விவாதித்து முடிவெடுக்கப்பட்டு அதன் பிறகு வெளியிடப்படும். இந்த அறிவிப்புகள் எல்லாம், வெறும் அறிவிப்புகளாக மட்டும்தானே என்று கேட்கலாம்! அரசு என்று ஒன்று அமைந்த பிறகு, அந்த அரசு செயல்படுகிறதோ இல்லையோ, செயல்படுவது போன்ற தோற்றமாவது இருக்க வேண்டாமா? முதலமைச்சர் நினைத்தால் சசிகலா மீதும், அவருடைய கணவர் மீதும், அவர்களுக்கு வேண்டிய ராவணன், திவாகரன் போன்றவர்கள் மீதும் நடவடிக்கை எடுப்பார்! அடுக்கடுக்கான வழக்குகளைப் பதிவு செய்வார். தற்போது அந்த வழக்கிலே குற்றம் சாட்டப்பட்ட ராவணனை கொடைநாட்டிற்கே அழைத்துப் பேசுகிறார் என்பது உண்மை என்றால் இவைகள் எல்லாம் யாரை ஏமாற்றுகின்ற செயல்கள்? அவர்கள் மீதெல்லாம் போடப்பட்ட வழக்குகள் என்னவாயிற்று? நடராஜன் மீது புகார் என்றார்கள். பிறகு கொடுக்கப்பட்ட புகார்கள் திரும்பப் பெறப்பட்டன என்கிறார்கள். அப்படி என்றால் பொய்ப் புகார் கொடுத்தவர்கள் மீது என்ன நடவடிக்கை? அந்தப் புகார் பொய்யானதா மெய்யானதா என்று காவல் துறை முறையாக முதல் நிலை விசாரணை நடத்தாதது ஏன்? காவல் துறையினரின் அந்தத் தவறான நடவடிக்கைக்குக் காரணம் என்ன? அந்த வழக்கிலே சம்பந்தப்பட்டவர் சிறையிலிருந்து வெளியே வந்ததும், அவரை “என்கவுண்டர்” செய்ய முயற்சி நடைபெற்றதாக இந்த ஆட்சியினர் மீது குற்றம் சாட்டினாரே; அதற்கு இந்த அரசின் பதில் என்ன? அ.தி.மு.க.வின் தேர்தல் அறிக்கையையே அவர்தான் தயாரித்துக் கொடுத்தேன் என்றார். முதல் அமைச்சருக்கு பேசவே தெரியாது, நான்தான் எழுதிக் கொடுத்தேன் என்றார். அவைகள் எல்லாம் உண்மைகளா? இந்த அரசு அதற்கு எந்தப் பதிலும் சொல்லாமல் இருப்பதிலிருந்து, அவர் சொன்னதெல்லாம் உண்மை என்றுதானே மக்கள் நம்புவார்கள். அதன்பிறகு அவர் வாய் திறக்கவில்லையே?
