**Date:** 25 July 2010

**Udanpirappē,**

I wrote two letters in *Murasoli* about the Supreme Court judgment delivered on 13-7-2010 on the reservation issue, explaining its implications and assembling in detail the events that had taken place on this question in the past. Jayalalithaa, in her own characteristic style, has now poured out that the Supreme Court case concerned a law enacted during her rule; that I remained silent and blacked out the judgment because writing about it might bring her credit; and that only after seeing newspaper reports that some backward-class organisations had decided to hold some kind of felicitation for her did I spit venom in these two articles.

Spitting venom — calling something a deceitful drama — speaking in an “I took it, I toppled it” manner — saying “fallen hair” — criticising someone as lacking devotion to her husband — speaking without respect about all other political-party leaders — shamelessly lying that the Governor grabbed her hand and pulled her — these are all expressions peculiar to one individual woman called Jayalalithaa.

She has said that I somehow concealed the fact that the law was enacted during her government. Is that true? In my *Murasoli* letter I wrote in detail about the resolution introduced during Jayalalithaa’s rule and about the law that was passed; I reminded readers that the draft resolution itself had been prepared by Dravidar Kazhagam General Secretary, Tamilar Thalaivar Veeramani; I said that he had not even been thanked during that regime; and I wrote that the draft law had been passed in the Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly with the support of all parties. As always, without reading anything properly, Jayalalithaa has issued a statement accusing me of blacking out the fact that the law was enacted during her rule.

In my own letter, with political civility and with the view that history should not be hidden, I specifically wrote: “On 18-7-1994 Jayalalithaa too wrote to the leaders of all parties in India seeking support on the reservation issue.” If she jumps about without understanding even that, what can I do?

Jayalalithaa has said that I accused her of playing a double role on reservation and has condemned me for it. Here too she has only skimmed the surface.

What I wrote was: “There was a view among journalists at the time that the Jayalalithaa government was playing a double role on the reservation issue. The reason was that in the affidavit submitted to the Supreme Court on 22-11-1993, the Jayalalithaa government accepted the Supreme Court judgment and consented to provide only 50 per cent reservation in Tamil Nadu for 1993–94.” I also said that the 15-7-1994 issue of *Frontline* had written a long article about it. I did not say that she had played a double role. It was *Frontline* that said so.

She may perhaps ask whether I can prove that *Frontline* wrote this. Here is what it printed:

> “If the AIADMK Government in Tamil Nadu finds itself isolated today on the sensitive reservation issue it has to blame itself. This isolation is in sharp contrast to the support it received from all Opposition Parties on December 31, 1993 when the State Assembly unanimously passed a bill.........But today, Jayalalitha finds herself caught in a cleft. The Chief Minister’s attempts to extricate herself have only worsened her plight. All the Major Opposition Parties rejected her invitation to join her to meet Prime Minister on June 25 to press the Centre for Presidential assent for the State Bill. They increasingly view her as being “insincere” and playing a “double game” on this issue, which is the bed rock of the Dravidian Movement.”

The source immediately follows this printed English passage with a parenthesised Tamil rendering of the same argument: if the AIADMK government found itself isolated on reservation, it had itself to blame; the unanimous opposition support it had received when the Assembly passed the Bill on 31-12-1993 had changed completely; Jayalalithaa had become caught in a trap; her efforts to escape had worsened the situation; the major opposition parties rejected her invitation to join the delegation meeting the Prime Minister on 25 June to seek Presidential assent; and they increasingly regarded her as insincere and as playing a double role on an issue that forms a foundation of the Dravidian movement.

Does this not make it clear that I did not say Jayalalithaa played a double role, but merely reported the view expressed by a publication? Yet if Jayalalithaa condemns me, it is enough for the people to understand that “woman of virtue”!

Next, Jayalalithaa has told a Himalayan lie in her statement. She has said that 18 per cent reservation for Scheduled Castes and 1 per cent for Scheduled Tribes had been followed in Tamil Nadu from time immemorial. After I became Chief Minister in 1969, a Backward Classes Welfare Committee under A. N. Sattanathan was constituted. Accepting its recommendation, I caused an order to be issued on 7-6-1971 raising the reservation that had until then been 16 per cent for Adi Dravidars and Scheduled Tribes to 18 per cent. In the same order I raised reservation for the Backward Classes from the previous 25 per cent to 31 per cent.

Later, during AIADMK rule, an annual-income ceiling of ₹9,000 was imposed for Backward Classes to receive concessions. After the AIADMK’s defeat in the Parliamentary election that followed, reservation for the Backward Classes was raised on 1-2-1980 from 31 per cent to 50 per cent. Then, when I was Chief Minister, after discussions with Dr. Ramadoss, I made a separate 20 per cent allocation for the Most Backward Classes within the 50 per cent reservation for the Backward Classes on 28-3-1989. It was when I was Chief Minister, on 22-6-1990, that the earlier combined 18 per cent for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes was changed to 18 per cent for Scheduled Castes alone and a separate 1 per cent for Scheduled Tribes.

But Jayalalithaa has hidden all of this — or, to use her own language, completely blacked it out — and has lied in her usual style that this reservation had been followed in Tamil Nadu from time immemorial.

There is another falsehood in Jayalalithaa’s statement. She writes that the Union Government issued its order accepting the Mandal Commission recommendations on 13-8-1990; that although I was in office from 13-8-1990 to 30-1-1991 I did not pass a resolution on the Mandal Commission; and that it was only on 30-9-1991 that Jayalalithaa brought such a resolution. Yet in the very same statement she herself says that on 21-8-1990, during DMK rule, a resolution was passed congratulating the government for issuing the order on the basis of the Mandal Commission recommendation.

It was I who moved a special resolution in the Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly on 12-5-1989 calling upon the Union Government to implement the Mandal Commission recommendations immediately. When I was in Delhi on 5-2-1990, I met Deputy Prime Minister Devi Lal and requested that the Mandal Commission recommendations be implemented quickly.

On 12-6-1990 Prime Minister V. P. Singh sought the views of all State Chief Ministers on the Mandal Commission recommendations. I replied before the end of that very month. Later V. P. Singh praised Tamil Nadu as the only State that had immediately replied to the Union Government’s letter. On 26-5-1990 a Social Justice Conference was held in Thanjavur city, attended by former President of India Zail Singh and Tamilar Thalaivar Veeramani. Zail Singh said:

“The people of Tamil Nadu are very fortunate and blessed. You are living in the time of a leader with a great historical distinction for ability, speed, affection, grace, good administrative skill and the capacity to approach problems. The name of that great leader is Kalaignar Karunanidhi. I now place a request before him. Today Kalaignar Karunanidhi’s rule is taking place in Delhi. Kalaignar Karunanidhi is a component of the National Front. At this moment I ask you to implement the Mandal Commission recommendations and establish them in law. You are not merely a component of the National Front. Without you, where is the National Front? Therefore I appeal to you as one who created the National Front. The country awaits the day when the Mandal Commission recommendations will be accepted and formally announced.”

Those words remain in the newspapers even today.

The very next week after the Union Government issued the order on the Mandal Commission recommendations, on 21-8-1990, it was I who moved the resolution in the Legislative Assembly welcoming it.

Jayalalithaa says in her statement that she introduced and passed the reservation Bill in the Legislative Assembly, but that when the Bill was put to a vote in the Rajya Sabha three DMK members neither voted nor participated. A “Brihaspati” from a party allied with her rushed ahead of Jayalalithaa and told reporters in Coimbatore the same thing. She says Jayalalithaa brought the reservation resolution on 9-11-1993 and that the Jayalalithaa government passed the Bill in the Assembly on 30-12-1993. If she were fair, should she not also have said whether the DMK supported that resolution or not? Likewise, when the matter was put to a vote in the Rajya Sabha, should she not have said that five DMK members took part and voted in support?

At a National Front–Left parties meeting in Delhi on 11-8-1994, attended by DMK Rajya Sabha member Viduthalai Virumbi, it was decided that they would vote to pass the constitutional amendment concerning Tamil Nadu’s reservation.

In a statement I issued on 21-8-1994, I said that if the Union Government delayed any further, there would be no alternative but to resume the struggle in accordance with the decision already taken at the meeting of party leaders in the front fighting for social justice.

Speaking for the DMK in the Rajya Sabha on 22-8-1994, Viduthalai Virumbi said: “Our leader Kalaignar has warned the Union Government that if the issue is delayed there will be no alternative but struggle.” He demanded a categorical assurance from the government that the constitutional-amendment Bill protecting the Tamil Nadu law would be introduced and passed in the current session itself. Without thinking, “This is a Tamil Nadu government law, so is it not a law passed by the AIADMK government?”, he raised his voice because of the importance of the issue. During his speech an AIADMK member interrupted to say that he should not say the Union Government had been “warned”.

On 24-8-1994, the constitutional-amendment Bill to include the Tamil Nadu reservation law in the Ninth Schedule was passed in the Rajya Sabha without debate. There were 148 votes in support and two against. Do you know who the two who voted against were? Chimanbhai Mehta and Satish Pradhan. The DMK Rajya Sabha members who participated and voted were S. Viduthalai Virumbi, MISA R. Ganesan, Pasumpon Tha. Kiruttinan, Mohamed Saki and Namakkal K. K. Veerappan.

Tindivanam Venkataraman, then a Member of Parliament, did not go to Delhi that day because his son’s wedding was being held under my leadership on the same day. Even at that wedding I said that, had the situation been such that one vote was crucial to passing the Bill, we would have conducted the wedding ourselves and sent Venkataraman to Delhi. That remains in the old *Murasoli* even today. Likewise, Murasoli Maran did not go to Delhi because of ill health. J. S. Raju, whose son was in a dangerous condition with kidney trouble, went a little late and participated. Hiding all of this and trying to stain the DMK by saying three members did not participate — is that not an attempt to deceive the town? When this amendment Bill came before the Lok Sabha on 26-8-1994, there was no opposition to it. Three hundred and forty-eight members voted in support.

Finally, one question. Jayalalithaa says in her statement that nobody can equal the AIADMK in protecting social justice. She says a State-wide general strike was held on 16-11-1993 and an all-party meeting was convened on 26-11-1993. But between those two dates, on 22-11-1993, did the Jayalalithaa government or did it not give an undertaking in the Supreme Court that it would not implement 69 per cent reservation and would implement only 50 per cent?

Did Jayalalithaa or did she not give an interview to the *Indian Express* saying caste should not be the basis of reservation and that the economic criterion alone should be used? In that interview, did she or did she not say:

“Some among the Backward Classes are well placed. If they receive reservation concessions, it will make a mockery of the very reservation policy. Therefore concessions should be made available to the economically weaker sections among the Backward Classes. Only then can it be called social justice. When the Union Government has decided on 27 per cent for the Backward Classes, there is nothing wrong in reserving 5 or 10 per cent even for the economically weaker sections among the forward classes.”

You played a double role on the Sri Lankan issue; on reservation too?

Finally, Jayalalithaa ends her statement by saying about me that I “fly like a demon at other people’s property.” The social-justice policy is not somebody else’s property. It is the policy of the Dravidian movement. But do I have to explain who it is that flies like a demon at other people’s property? Is that not a secret known to the whole town! The total embodiment of every description applied to ghost, goblin and demon! What else is there to say except: shut your mouth!

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**25-7-2010**

“ப்ரண்ட் லைன்” இதழ் அப்படி எழுதியதாக நிரூபிக்க முடியுமா என்று ஒருவேளை அவர் கேட்கக் கூடும்.

அவர்கள் இட ஒதுக்கீட்டுச் சலுகையைப் பெறுவது என்பது இட ஒதுக்கீட்டுக் கொள்கையையே கேலிக் கூத்தாக்கி விடும். எனவே பிற்படுத்தப்பட்ட வகுப்பில் பொருளாதாரத்தில் நலிந்த பிரிவினருக்குச் சலுகைகள் கிடைக்குமாறு செய்ய வேண்டும். அப்போதுதான் அதைச் சமூக நீதி என்று கூறலாம். பிற்படுத்தப்பட்டவர்களுக்கு 27 சதவிகிதம் என மத்திய அரசு முடிவெடுத்துள்ள நிலையில் முன்னேறிய வகுப்பினரில் கூட பொருளாதார ரீதியாக நலிந்த பிரிவினருக்கு 5 அல்லது 10 சதவிகிதம் இடஒதுக்கீடு செய்வது தவறில்லை” என்று கூறியது உண்டா? இல்லையா? இலங்கைப் பிரச்சினையில்தான் இரட்டை வேடம் போட்டீர்கள்; இட ஒதுக்கீட்டிலுமா?

இறுதியாக ஜெயலலிதா தனது அறிக்கையை முடிக்கும்போது “ஊரார் உடைமைக்கு பேயாய் பறப்பதாக” என்னைப் பற்றிச் சொல்லியிருக்கிறார். சமூக நீதிக் கொள்கை ஊரார் உடைமை அல்ல. திராவிட இயக்கத்தின் கொள்கை. ஆனால் ஊரார் உடைமைக்குப் பேயாய் பறப்பவர்கள் யார் என்பதைப் பற்றி நான் விளக்க வேண்டுமா என்ன? அது ஊரார் அறிந்த ரகசியமாயிற்றே! பேய், பூதம், பிசாசு இந்த மூன்றுக்கும் சொல்லப்படும் இலக்கணத்தின் மொத்த உருவமே! மூடிடுக வாயை என்பதைத் தவிர வேறென்ன சொல்ல?

அன்புள்ள,
