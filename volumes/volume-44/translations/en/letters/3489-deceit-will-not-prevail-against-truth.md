# 3489. Deceit Will Not Prevail Against Truth!

**Udanpirappē,**

Did you see them, Udanpirappē — these pitiable scenes!

Not only affection and compassion: any human being with culture and refinement would grow tearful, melt within and say, “God! You alone must save them,” leaving the burden upon the Lord. We are accustomed to seeing people do that. But if we alter that habit a little and use reason to ask what remedy there is for these cruelties, those who seek and find a remedy are not merely one or two people. When we realise that an entire government has accepted that responsibility, humane people will not begrudge the time, thought, labour and money spent for it. They are people whose minds are set on relieving the people’s distress. Those who sit in the Fort and govern as representatives of such a movement will come forward — must come forward — to remove the afflictions that befall the citizens. That is what a people’s-welfare government means.

We pledged to the Tamil people that we would be such a people’s-welfare government — that every atom of our thought was for you, for your good and for your future well-being. That pledge has not become a bubble on water. Like the finial atop a temple car visible in every direction, here before us stand human lives rescued from the very edge of death, bearing witness!

You may wonder: why this preface?

On this very day last year — 23-7-2009 — the Tamil Nadu government launched the **Kalaignar Insurance Scheme for Life-Saving High-End Treatment**.

One year has now been completed. What benefits has the scheme produced in this one year?

How many people have recovered from illness and are moving about again? Not one, not two! More than one lakh. To be precise, the statistics say that **1 lakh 53 thousand 257 patients** recovered. For their treatment, **₹415 crore 43 lakh** has so far been paid to hospitals through the insurance company. Over the past year, **1 crore 44 lakh 45 thousand 117 families** enrolled in the scheme, and identity cards have been issued to **1 crore 33 lakh 60 thousand 439 families**.

When the scheme began, there were those who asked:

Will it succeed?

Will it become complete year after year?

There were those who raised doubts —

those who mocked —

those who poured out irritation —

those who ridiculed it, asking why this useless work was needed.

We know their number too.

But now, as this scheme completes one year and steps into its next year, those minds that have become accustomed to condemning this government’s welfare schemes must confront the number of people who suffered last year, found a remedy, looked death in the face and received a new life. Does that count not immerse us in joy!

As the saying goes, one grain is enough to test a pot of rice. I shall explain just a few examples.

❖ **Ramadoss, 55, Mannivakkam near Chengalpattu.** On the morning of 24.01.2010 he suddenly became dizzy. That evening he fainted and lost consciousness. His relatives immediately admitted him to Billroth Hospital in Chennai. A CT scan showed a blood clot in the brain. Brain surgery was performed on 29.01.2010. The Kalaignar Insurance Scheme provided ₹60,000 toward the surgery. He has now returned to normal life and is well.

❖ **Mr. Veerappan of Padi, Vedasandur, Dindigul district.** He suffered greatly from cancer of the tongue and found it difficult even to eat. His family admitted him on 01.02.2010 to a treatment centre in Erode. ₹36,000 was provided through the Kalaignar Insurance Scheme for treatment; surgery was performed immediately, and on 24.02.2010 he returned in full health.

❖ **Mr. Saravana Iyer, 45, Lakshmi Nagar, Tambaram, Chennai.** For more than two months he had suffered severe abdominal pain. His abdomen became swollen; he vomited and struggled without being able to eat. He was also in a dangerous condition after suffering jaundice for more than two months. His poor family depended on him. When illness left the entire family not knowing what to do, they brought him to Lifeline Multi-Speciality Hospital on 10.05.2010. Doctors examined him and found cancer related to the gallbladder. He was immediately given ten bottles of blood and underwent surgery on 14.05.2010. He is now well. The Kalaignar Insurance Scheme bore the surgical cost and provided ₹1 lakh as insurance benefit.

❖ **Selvi Saranya, daughter of Mr. D. Thangavelu of K. Vallakundapuram, Udumalpet, a member of the Tamil Nadu Agricultural Labourers Welfare Board.** After fracturing a bone in her leg, she was admitted to Arun Hospital, Pollachi. Approval for ₹17,500 was obtained from the insurance company and she underwent surgery on 21.10.2009. Having received treatment under the Kalaignar Insurance Scheme, she is now well.

❖ **Muniyandi, a schoolboy and son of Mr. Pandian of Okkaraipatti, Andipatti, Theni district.** Heart disease prevented him from attending school and stunted his growth. His father, who was poor, learnt of the Kalaignar Insurance Scheme and admitted Muniyandi, in a severely weakened condition, to Saravana Hospital in Madurai district on 23.11.2009. Doctors examined him and immediately performed heart surgery. The full surgical cost of ₹60,000 was paid through the Kalaignar Insurance Scheme, and he returned home in good health.

❖ **Mrs. Dhanalakshmi of Magudanchavadi, Salem.** On 15-1-2010, after celebrating Pongal, she was returning home in a mini-auto when the vehicle lost balance and overturned on a bend near Magudanchavadi. Her forearm was severed. The severed forearm was preserved in ice, and within six hours of the accident she was taken to Ganga Hospital in Coimbatore. Through microsurgery, the severed hand was reattached. After two weeks of continuous observation she returned home with the reattached hand.

❖ **Mr. Sundarasekar of Dindigul, aged 52.** For two months he had been passing blood along with urine. He consulted several doctors but was not cured. After joining the Kalaignar Insurance Scheme, a scan was taken, the cause of the illness was identified, and on 23-5-2010 a six-hour operation was performed. He has now fully recovered.

❖ **Mrs. Madhina, aged 28, of Keezh Pennathur village in Tiruvannamalai district, a woman of the Kuravar community.** She had suffered a heart ailment for many years. Under the Kalaignar Insurance Scheme she was admitted to Chettinad Hospital, received cardiac treatment and is now well.

❖ **Selvan Murugan, aged 9, of Kulathur village, Vandavasi.** He had suffered from heart disease since birth. His poor parents, without the means to get him treated, had given up, believing that his life would simply end there. After the Kalaignar Insurance Scheme was announced, surgery was performed under the scheme and he is now in good health.

❖ **Mr. Moses, aged 60, in Dharmapuri district — a Sri Lankan Tamil living as a refugee.** Both his kidneys had been affected and he had suffered for a year. He joined the Kalaignar Insurance Scheme and underwent surgery under it. He is now well.

❖ **Mr. Vigneshwaran, aged 32, living in the refugee camp at Gummidipoondi.** He was involved in a road accident and suffered a fracture of the jaw, leaving him in great difficulty. Under the Kalaignar Insurance Scheme he was admitted to hospital and underwent surgery. He is now completely well.

I could go on writing. All those who benefited from the scheme met me at the Secretariat on the 23rd, marking the completion of its first year, and expressed their thanks. When they looked at me and said, with hearts melting, “If this scheme had not existed, we would not even be alive,” do you know how much happiness I felt? And when, with tears in their eyes, they spoke of how badly illness had affected them and how they could never themselves have spent such sums to obtain treatment, I also felt proud.

This is not the only such scheme. You know the **108** scheme. News about it keeps arriving every day. Rural people repeatedly tell how that scheme saved their lives and praise this government. Through the Health Department, this government has also introduced an insurance scheme for government employees, which has received a great welcome. Likewise, a heart-protection scheme for children has been launched; thousands of boys and girls have survived, and when they meet me and say, “You are the reason we are alive,” I forget myself. Through this department, with the cooperation of Minister Thambi M. R. K. Panneerselvam, Secretary Subburaj, I.A.S., officials and humane doctors, I have recalled here only the major schemes being carried out.

The scheme for persons with disabilities is similar. One morning, when I saw in the newspapers that some persons with disabilities — people with impaired limbs, people who could not see and people who could not hear — were on hunger strike at Chennai General Hospital, I immediately went directly to the General Hospital and met them. They had been fasting with demands, but they forgot their demands themselves. “It is enough that we have met you. We have faith that you will fulfil the demand. We end the hunger strike here,” they said, and rose and left.

So there must be a desire to help those who cannot manage for themselves. Because we have that desire, we act along that path.

If some people say this is a “corruption scheme” and that crores upon crores have been looted, the reason I do not take them seriously is this: truth will never be defeated; deceit will not prevail against truth. Those who have made betrayal a continuing feature of their lives cannot make even dust settle upon such clean schemes. From hamlet to seashore settlement across Tamil Nadu, the lives gained by poor, ordinary people who benefit from our schemes will shut the mouth of deceit and break its back. With that confidence, we shall walk forward!

There is no room in this letter to attach photographs of the thousand upon thousand — the one lakh — people whose restored health encourages us. Therefore, on behalf of all who were afflicted then and are well today, I offer heartfelt thanks to everyone who cooperated. I also appeal to them, and to others like them, to continue that cooperation.

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**26-07-2010**

## Letter-specific notes

1. The individual medical case histories are translated in the order printed; source hospital/place descriptions and treatment figures are not externally reconciled.
2. The unusual source form `எள் என்றால்` in the opening welfare-state passage is rendered for the immediate sense of responsiveness to public distress without altering the authoritative Tamil appendix.
3. `108` is retained as the source’s service-scheme name.
4. The closing imagery — truth, deceit, shutting deceit’s mouth and breaking its back — is preserved rather than softened.

இந்த ஒரு திட்டம் மாத்திரமல்ல; 108 என்ற திட்டத்தைப் பற்றி நீ அறிவாய். அந்தத் திட்டத்தைப் பற்றி நாள்தோறும் செய்திகள் வந்துகொண்டே இருக்கின்றன. கிராமப்புற மக்கள் எல்லாம் அந்தத் திட்டத்தினால் தாங்கள் உயிர் பிழைத்த செய்தியை தொடர்ந்து சொல்லி, இந்த அரசைப் பாராட்டுகிறார்கள். மக்கள் நல்வாழ்வுத் துறை மூலமாக அரசு அலுவலர் களுக்கான காப்பீட்டுத் திட்டம் ஒன்றும் இந்த அரசால் தொடங்கப்பட்டு பெரும் வரவேற்பினை பெற்றுள்ளது. அதுபோலவே இளஞ்சிறார்களுக்கான இதயப் பாதுகாப்பு திட்டம் ஒன்று தொடங்கப்பட்டு ஆயிரக்கணக்கான சிறுவர், சிறுமியர் உயிர்பிழைத்து அவர்கள் என்னைச் சந்தித்து தாங்கள் உயிரோடு இருப்பதற்குக் காரணம் நீங்கள்தான் என்று சொல்லும்போது நான் என்னையே மறக்கிறேன். இந்தத் துறையின் மூலமாக - அமைச்சர் தம்பி எம்.ஆர்.கே.பன்னீர்செல்வம், செயலாளர் சுப்பராஜ், ஐ.ஏ.எஸ்., மற்றும் அதிகாரிகள், மனித நேயமுள்ள மருத்துவர்கள் ஒத்துழைப்போடு செய்யப்படுகின்ற முக்கிய திட்டங்களை மட்டும் இங்கே நினைவுபடுத்தியிருக்கிறேன்.

மாற்றுத்திறனாளிகளுக்கான திட்டமும் அப்படித்தான். மாற்றுத் திறனாளிகள் கைகால் ஊனமுற்றோர், கண் தெரியாதோர், காது கேளாதோர் சிலர் சென்னை பொது மருத்துவமனையில் உண்ணா விரதம் இருக்கிறார்கள் என்ற செய்தியை காலையிலே ஏடுகளிலே கண்டதும், உடனடியாக நானே நேரடியாக பொது மருத்துவமனைக்கு விரைந்து சென்று அந்த மாற்றுத் திறனாளிகளைச் சந்தித்தேன். கோரிக்கைகளை முன் வைத்து உண்ணா விரதம் இருந்த அவர்கள், தங்கள் கோரிக்கைகளையே மறந்து விட்டார்கள். உங்களைச் சந்தித்ததே போதும், கோரிக்கையை நிறைவேற்றுவீர்கள் என்ற நம்பிக்கை உள்ளது, உண்ணா விரதத்தை இத்துடன் முடித்துக் கொள்கிறோம் என்று அங்கிருந்து எழுந்துச் சென்றார்கள்.

எனவே இயலாதோர்க்கு உதவிட வேண்டுமென்ற எண்ணம் வேண்டும். அந்த எண்ணம் எங்களுக்கு இருக்கின்ற காரணத்தால் அந்த வழியிலே செயல்படுகிறோம்.

இதை ஊழல்திட்டம் என்றும், கோடிக் கோடியாகக் கொள்ளைப் போய்விட்டது என்றும் சிலர் பேசுகிறார்கள் என்றால்; நான் அவர்களைப் பொருட்படுத்தாததற்குக் காரணம் - உண்மை ஒரு நாளும் தோற்காது - வாய்மையை எதிர்த்து வஞ்சகம் ஜெயிக்காது - துரோகத்தையே தங்கள் வாழ்வின் தொடர் நிகழ்ச்சிகளாக ஆக்கிக் கொண்டவர்களால் இதுபோன்ற தூய்மையான திட்டங்களின் மீது தூசி படவும் செய்திட இயலாது என்பதை தமிழகத்தின் பட்டிதொட்டி முதல் பட்டினக்கரை வரையில் நமது திட்டங்களால் பயன்பெறும் ஏழையெளிய சாதாரண மக்கள் பெறுகின்ற வாழ்வு வஞ்சகத்தின் வாயை அடைத்து அதன் இடுப்பை ஒடித்தே தீரும் என்ற நம்பிக்கையோடு நாம் நடை போடுவோம்! அதற்கு நம்மை ஊக்குவிக்கும் ஓராயிரம் பேர், ஒரு லட்சம் பேர் என்று உடல் நலம் பெற்றோரின் உருவப் படங்களை இணைத்து வழங்கிட இந்தக் கடிதத்தில் இடம் இல்லாத காரணத்தால் - அன்று நலிவடைந்து - இன்று நலம் பெற்றோர் அனைவரின் சார்பாகவும் ஒத்துழைப்போர் எல்லோருக்கும் உளமார்ந்த நன்றி கூறுகிறேன். தொடர்ந்து ஒத்துழைக்க வேண்டுமென்றும் அவர்களுக்கும், அவர் போன்றோருக்கும் வேண்டுகோள் விடுக்கின்றேன்.
