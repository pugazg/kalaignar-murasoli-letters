## Editorial method

This pass follows the completed Volume 49 release workflow while preserving Volume 48's already verified source decisions. It is a cross-letter consistency review, not a new translation or source-meaning review.

The pass checked:

1. metadata, English title and progress/index agreement;
2. the mandatory translator's note and bilingual document order;
3. names, place-names, honorifics and transliteration;
4. recurring institutional, legal, political and administrative terminology;
5. British/Indian English spelling and compound treatment;
6. punctuation and typography in English titles;
7. source links, PDF/printed page ranges, dates and verification status fields;
8. placement and preservation of letter-specific notes; and
9. preservation of the complete `Original Tamil — மூலத் தமிழ்` layer.

No argument was recast, no political language was softened, no source chronology or numerical tension was reconciled from outside information, and no exact source-English quotation was edited.

## Reviewed and deliberately retained

The pass also reviewed several apparent cross-volume variants and deliberately left them unchanged because they are source- or context-dependent rather than editorial errors:

- **`video conference / video conferencing`** remains an approved Volume 48 pair. The source itself uses the loan form `வீடியோ கான்பரன்சிங்`, and verified translations use the noun/gerund form according to sentence context rather than forcing one mechanical spelling.
- **`Navratna`** remains the Volume 48 treatment of source `நவரத்னா` in letter 3761. It is retained as an already aligned source-specific transliteration rather than being changed merely to match a different volume's spelling.
- Exact source-English passages remain byte-for-byte untouched in the English body where they were deliberately reproduced, including the Katchatheevu spelling variants, Supreme Court/N.O.C. material, *Hindu* passages and malformed **`By injectinga`** in 3763.
- Source-visible numerical, chronological and institutional tensions remain unreconciled where the alignment reports explicitly preserved them.
- All bilingual-alignment corrections remain in force; this editorial pass does not reopen or weaken them.
