## Review method

The review checked title/date, salutation/closing, paragraph and argument sequence, substantive claims and attribution, quotations and intentional source-English, names and honorifics, dates, figures, list completeness, rhetorical force, legal/institutional terminology, source-visible irregularities, and English wording that could broaden, narrow, soften or silently reconcile the Tamil.
