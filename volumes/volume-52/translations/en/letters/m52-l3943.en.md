# 3943. Panneer's Grace Lies in Keeping Silent!

## Translator's Note

This translation is intended to carry Kalaignar's voice into clear, contemporary English rather than recast the letter as literary or academic prose. It preserves the source's argument, political directness, rhetorical questions, repetition, irony, factual detail, and paragraph order. Names, dates, figures, quotations, and intentional English expressions are retained. Where Tamil idiom cannot be reproduced literally without sounding unnatural, the English follows its sense and rhetorical force without adding claims absent from the source. The Tamil below is a source-checked reading copy: the opening salutation omitted by OCR has been restored, clipped dates and newspaper headlines damaged at page breaks have been completed from the scans, and clear OCR character and word-break errors have been repaired. The canonical source file remains unchanged and authoritative.

`Udanpirappē` is retained in Tamil transliteration rather than flattened into “brother,” “sister,” or “comrade.” Literally evoking “one born alongside me,” Kalaignar uses it as a distinctive address of shared identity, equality, affection, and solidarity within the movement.

## English Translation

Udanpirappē,

Our Tamil Nadu Chief Minister O. Panneerselvam appears to have forgotten that he is the Chief Minister governing a State. Like the principal of some college, he has issued a statement trying to teach lessons on what a “benami Government” means, what a “minority Government” means, what an “adviser” means and what a “Deputy Chief Minister” means. Whether or not he attends each day to the responsibilities of the Tamil Nadu Government, he never fails to issue statements in “Amma's” way, in “Amma's” style and with “Amma's” approval. He has threatened that if DMK members behave in a manner that deprives members of their rights and brings disgrace upon the Assembly, they will receive the consequences they deserve. The DMK will not fear Panneerselvam's empty rustling. It will discharge its duty properly and according to law. But I have already described at length the many incidents in which AIADMK members behaved in ways that deprived members of their rights and brought disgrace upon the House.

Panneerselvam has also issued this challenge: “The entire country knows how Karunanidhi, unable to face the firm arguments of Revolutionary Leader Amma directly, hid behind files. If he has courage, is Karunanidhi ready to attend the Assembly on the fourth and speak?” Mr Panneerselvam, nothing prevents me from attending the Assembly on the fourth. It requires no particular courage. But what about your “Amma”? You are the one who has compelled me to ask this question! “Should someone living in a glass house try to throw stones?”

Panneerselvam further asks in his statement whether “Karunanidhi believes there is any subject on which to criticise the Tamil Nadu Government in the Assembly.” Not merely in the Assembly, but here before the people's forum, problems concerning the AIADMK Government have piled up like a mountain, one upon another, during these three and a half years. There is no room here to mention them all, so I give a compilation only of what appeared in the newspapers over the past week.

(தினமலர்) பன்னீர்செல்வம் அவர்களே, பேரவையில் பேசுவதற்கு ஏதாவது இந்த அரசின் மீது குறைபாடுகள் இருந்தால் சுட்டிக் காட்டுங்கள் என்று கேட்டதன் விளைவுதான் ஒரு வார காலத்தில்; இந்த ஆட்சியில் நடைபெற்ற இத்தனை முறைகேடுகளைத் தொகுத்துப் பட்டியலிட்டு எடுத்துக் காட்டியிருக்கிறேன். இந்தப் பட்டியல் போதுமா? இன்னும் மாத, ஆண்டு வாரியான விவரம் வேண்டுமா? அ.தி.மு.க. ஆட்சியின் அவலங்களைப் பற்றி நாள் கணக்கில் பேசுவதற்கு ஏராளமான பொருள்கள் தாராளமாகவே இருக்கின்றன. ஏன் நீங்கள் வாயைக் கொடுத்து வம்பை விலைக்கு வாங்கிக் கட்டிக் கொள்கிறீர்கள்? முதலமைச்சராகப் பொறுப்பேற்பதற்கு முன்பு வரை இருந்து வந்ததைப் போல, அமைதியாக இருப்பதுதான் பன்னீருக்கு அழகு; நாட்டிற்கும் நல்லது! எனவே பன்னீருக்கு அழகு, மெளனம் காத்தல்!

மு.க. 30-11-2014
