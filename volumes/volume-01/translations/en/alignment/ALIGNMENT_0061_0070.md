Status: **PASS — no English prose correction required.**

## Scope

This batch covers canonical English letters **0061–0070**, corresponding to source PDF / printed pages **250–276**.

The English records had already completed canonical migration/source checking. This gate compared each English translation directly with the complete canonical Tamil witness embedded in the record. The canonical Tamil layer had already passed the full scan-based visual/textual-fidelity gate; this batch does not claim a new independent scan transcription pass.

## Alignment method

For every record, the review checked argument and narrative sequence; substantive coverage and unsupported additions; paragraph, verse and quotation correspondence; names, dates, figures and political references; lists, metaphors, wordplay and rhetorical questions; repetition, irony, satire and political intensity; movement vocabulary and the established `Udanpirappē` convention; and closing/sign-off integrity.

Only demonstrable English/Tamil correspondence errors would be corrected at this gate. Stylistic polishing remains deferred to the later editorial-consistency gate.
