# Bilingual Alignment Review — Letters 3751–3755

## Scope

- Volume: **48**

## Review method

Each English translation was compared directly with the complete audited Tamil source reproduced in the bilingual letter and the canonical page sequence. The review checked title/date, salutation/closing, paragraph and argument sequence, substantive claims and attribution, quotations and intentional source-English, names, dates, figures, lists, rhetorical force, legal/institutional terminology, source-relative wording, and English wording that could broaden, narrow, soften or silently normalise the source.

The Tamil source had already passed the scan-based textual-fidelity audit. **No canonical Tamil page was modified in this alignment pass.**

## Results by letter

- The Rule 110/Budget argument, departmental allocations, foodgrain and sugarcane figures, cooperative-sector history, computerisation, veterinary-infrastructure figures and closing Assembly-procedure criticism were aligned against PDF 319–325.
- One source-structure correction was made: the closing sentence now says that the Chief Minister is reading under Rule 110 **“a statement that ought to be read under the Assembly's rules”**, restoring `சட்டப்பேரவை விதியின் கீழ்` rather than the earlier unsupported paraphrase “departmental procedure.”

- No Tamil-source change was made.

### 3752 — *A Roadblock for “Sethu”! An Act That Will Ruin Tamil Livelihoods!*

- The Kudankulam judgment framing, Jayalalithaa/NEERI material, exact N.O.C. passage, Madras High Court quotation, Omandurar/Police Commissioner/Maduravoyal examples, press argument and Ezhuchi Naal mobilisation were aligned against PDF 326–332.
- Three correction groups were required:
  1. `வாழும் உரிமைகளை உறுதிப்படுத்துவதற்காகவே` is now rendered as **“to guarantee the right to live”**, and the same source wording is carried into the Sethu comparison, replacing the looser “conditions for life / means of living.”
  2. The Tamil explanation following the exact English N.O.C. quotation now preserves `மாநில சுற்றுச் சூழல் வாரியம்` as **“State environmental board”** rather than silently normalising it to the English quotation's “Pollution Control Board.”
  3. The source word `மகராஜன்` at the start of the imagined police-personnel quotation has been restored as **Maharajan** and noted rather than omitted or interpreted.
- The exact printed N.O.C. English remains unchanged.
- No Tamil-source change was made.

### 3753 — *Industrial Growth in Tamil Nadu: Then and Now!*

- Rule 110/Assembly procedure, party and ministerial praise, industrial-project announcements, Thirumazhisai, DMK industrial policy, CSO rankings, *Wall Street Journal*, `ASSO CHAMP`/BIZCON, CMIE investment figures and SIPCOT history were aligned against PDF 333–339.

  2. `மேம்படுத்தப்பட்ட தொழில்மனைகளை` is now rendered **“developed industrial plots”** rather than the broader “industrial estates.”

### 3754 — *Must This Cruelty Still Continue in Eelam?*

- The Amnesty International framing, dissent/human-rights material, journalists/lawyers/civil-society cases, listed killings/abductions/expulsions, constitutional bodies, judicial material and closing demand for an international inquiry were aligned against PDF 340–346.
- Five source-alignment corrections were made:
  1. The report title is now translated directly from `கருத்து வேறுபாட்டின் மீது இலங்கை அரசின் தாக்குதல்` as **“The Sri Lankan Government's Attack on Dissent”**, rather than externally normalising the title.
  2. The January 2013 Chief Justice sentence now restores the source's causal wording: removal **for delivering judgments that protected human rights**, amid lawyers' united opposition.
  3. `ஐ.நா. மனித உரிமைகள் ஆணையம்` is retained as **“U.N. Human Rights Commission”** rather than silently changing the source's institutional term.
  4. The *Aguna* passage now follows the source: **a fabricated story was concocted that** the three named Sinhala trade-union leaders had disappeared; the earlier English incorrectly presented their disappearance first and the fabrication second.
  5. The later Chief Justice passage now says **charges were brought** even after the Sri Lankan Supreme Court ruled that bringing such charges was unconstitutional, rather than importing the more specific term “impeached.”
- Allegations and report claims remain explicitly source-attributed; no outside fact-checking was used to rewrite the source account.
- No Tamil-source change was made.
