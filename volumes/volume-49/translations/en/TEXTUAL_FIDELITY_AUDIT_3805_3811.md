# Textual-Fidelity Audit — Letters 3805–3811

## Scope

- Volume: **49**
- Letters: **3805–3811**
- Canonical Tamil Markdown pages: **PDF 314–364**
- Total pages visually compared with scans: **51/51**
- Method: every canonical page was compared with its corresponding rendered PDF scan before translation. Titles, letter numbers, paragraph order, numbered and bulleted lists, quotations, original English passages, figures, punctuation, signatures, dates and page boundaries were checked.

## Result

- All **51** pages were inspected.
- No paragraph or passage was missing.
- The canonical Markdown matched the visible scans closely enough that **no canonical correction was required** in this batch.
- Printed irregularities and historical source forms were retained rather than silently modernised or repaired.

Each English file contains:

1. the standard Translator’s note;

4. the full canonical Tamil text, divided by original PDF page.

This audit establishes **source-checked** status for letters **3805–3811**. Full bilingual line-by-line editorial review remains a later, separate stage.
