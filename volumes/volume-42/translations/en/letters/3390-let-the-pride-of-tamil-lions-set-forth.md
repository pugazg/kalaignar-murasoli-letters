# 3390. Let the Pride of Tamil Lions Set Forth!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This is an explicitly partisan election-campaign letter built around the DMK’s 2009 Parliamentary election manifesto and a retrospective list of welfare and infrastructure measures claimed by the author. Appeals to party workers and voters are preserved solely as historical source text, not as recommendations by the translator. The source-supplied English letter from Sonia Gandhi is reproduced verbatim. The complete audited Tamil is reproduced below and remains authoritative.

**Tamil source:** [Letter 3390](../../../chapters/3390-kilambattum-tamilach-singak-koottam.md)  
**Source pages:** [PDF 194](../../../pages/page-194.md)–[PDF 202](../../../pages/page-202.md)  
**Date:** 4 April 2009

**Udanpirappē,**

Yesterday morning, before beginning the scrutiny for selection of Kazhagam candidates, I released the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam’s election manifesto for India’s Fifteenth Parliament in the presence of journalists and in the presence of General Secretary Inamana Professor.

Those with me included Kazhagam Treasurer brother Thalapathi M. K. Stalin; Principal Secretary Arcot Veerasamy; Deputy General Secretaries Duraimurugan, Parithi Ilamvazhuthi and Sarguna Pandian; senior leaders Veerapandi Arumugam; Union Minister A. Raja; Minister Ponmudy; former Union Minister and Member of Parliament brother Dayanidhi Maran; Southern Zone Secretary M. K. Alagiri; Rajya Sabha Member M. K. Kanimozhi; Organisation Secretaries T. K. S. Elangovan and Kalyanasundaram; and Chief Election Work Committee Secretary L. Ganesan.

To prepare this 2009 election manifesto, a committee was formed under Dr. Nathan comprising Union Ministers T. R. Baalu and A. Raja, Sarguna Pandian and T. K. S. Elangovan.

Within a short time the committee prepared what the source describes as an excellent manifesto.

Professor and I reviewed it, it was finalised and printed, and it has now been released.

**Udanpirappē,**

I ask people like you not only to read this manifesto in full, but also to explain its programmes to the public and help gather their support.

Political parties contesting elections issue separate manifestos before every election.

But the DMK, the source argues, has earned a distinct place of importance in the preparation and publication of election manifestos.

Dear friend P. Chidambaram, then a Union Minister, read and praised the DMK’s 2006 Assembly-election manifesto and even declared during the campaign that the manifesto itself was the “hero” of that election.

That manifesto, the author says, set out in advance what programmes a DMK government would formulate and implement if elected.

In accordance with the phrase “We will do what we say; we will say what we do,” the source claims the Tamil Nadu Government subsequently implemented the promises contained in that manifesto.

During the election, AIADMK and its allies had campaigned strongly that the announced programmes were impossible to implement and had been designed merely to deceive the people.

But after winning the election and assuming office on 13.5.2006, I called for three files on the very stage where I took office and signed announcements for:

- waiver of ₹7,000 crore in cooperative loans to farmers;
- rice at ₹2 per kilogram;
- and three eggs a week for children receiving the nutritious meal.

You know this well.

The source says the Kazhagam government then continued implementing the election promises one after another and that the people of Tamil Nadu have experienced their benefits directly.

Similarly, the source says, the United Progressive Alliance government at the Centre has been implementing the promises made in the 2004 election manifesto.

One specific example concerns Tamil.

The 2004 manifesto said:

> “The DMK will continue pressing the Union Government to declare the Tamil language, which possesses all the required qualifications, a high and distinct Classical Language.”

Today, says the author, everyone knows that this promise has been fulfilled.

Tamil was declared a Classical Language.

In addition, the Central Institute of Classical Tamil was inaugurated in Chennai on 18.8.2007.

The United Progressive Alliance government also approved annual Presidential awards, with retrospective effect, in the name of Ayyan Thiruvalluvar for an internationally distinguished Tamil scholar and in the name of Tolkappiyar for a nationally distinguished Tamil scholar.

The Union Government did not stop with approving Classical Language status.

At that time, United Progressive Alliance Chairperson Mrs. Sonia Gandhi wrote me the following letter:

> **November 8, 2005.**
>
> **Dear Thiru Karunanidhiji,**
>
> **I have received your letter of 28th October. I am glad that all the formalities for declaring Tamil as a Classical Language have now been completed. This is an achievement for all the constituents of the UPA Government, but particular credit goes to you and your Party.**
>
> **With regards,**
>
> **Yours sincerely,**  
> **Sonia Gandhi.**

The 2004 manifesto also said we would press the Union Government to allocate the necessary funds and quickly begin implementation of the long-promised Sethusamudram Canal project.

The source says we did so; the Union Government announced the project at an estimated cost of ₹2,427 crore, allocated funds, and about sixty per cent of the work was completed before the project was halted pending a court decision, which the author attributes to religious groups and others who feared the government would receive credit.

We also promised in the 2004 manifesto that the DMK would press for immediate repeal of the POTA law.

The source says that pressure was applied and POTA was repealed.

The 2004 manifesto said we would press the Union Government to raise the income-tax exemption limit from ₹50,000 to ₹1 lakh.

The source says it was raised not only to ₹1 lakh but later to ₹1.5 lakh.

We had promised steps to reduce the suffering of weavers.

Accordingly, after the United Progressive Alliance government came to office, the CENVAT tax for weavers was removed.

We said the Chennai drinking-water problem would be addressed.

Accordingly, approval and funding were provided for the ₹908-crore seawater desalination project at Nemmeli near Chennai.

Having done, in the author’s framing, so many things as an example of “We will do what we say,” the United Progressive Alliance government also did more than it had promised.

The source lists the following:

- a National Automotive Testing and Research infrastructure centre at Oragadam near Chennai, with an investment of ₹470 crore;
- a National Institute of Siddha Research at Tambaram;
- expansion of the Salem Steel Plant to international standards, including a new cold-rolling mill at a cost of ₹1,553 crore;
- upgrading Salem Government Mohan Kumaramangalam Medical College Hospital into a “super-speciality” hospital at a cost of ₹120 crore;
- through the Shipping and Highways Ministry, works costing ₹56,664 crore involving 4,676 kilometres of National Highways, including upgrading 3,276 kilometres to four-lane roads, flyovers, port-expansion works, container terminals and inland-water transport facilities;
- a guarantee of one hundred days of employment under the National Rural Employment Guarantee Act;
- a ₹1,655-crore elevated road between Chennai Port and Maduravoyal, for which Prime Minister Manmohan Singh laid the foundation stone on 8.1.2009;
- a National Commission for the welfare of economically and socially backward sections among religious and minority communities;
- what the source describes as India’s first third-generation information-technology programme comparable with developed countries;
- introduction of a national e-governance plan aimed at full computerised administration in all villages;
- telephone calling across India at a cost of fifty paise;
- reduction in mobile-phone tariffs;
- approval and continuing conversion of metre-gauge railway lines in Tamil Nadu into broad gauge;
- ten new trains for the benefit of the people of Tamil Nadu;
- Union approval for ninety railway overbridges at an estimated cost of ₹1,859 crore, of which twelve, costing ₹137.31 crore, were said to have been completed;
- the Chennai Metro Rail project at an estimated cost of ₹16,400 crore;
- a new Salem Railway Division;
- expansion of the Tiruchirappalli, Coimbatore and Madurai airports;
- what the source describes as Asia’s first national institute near Chennai for people with multiple disabilities;
- a National Security Guard centre in Chennai;
- waiver of farmers’ loans and interest totalling ₹72,000 crore;
- education loans for students;
- a ninth berth at Thoothukudi Port, constructed at a cost of ₹57 crore and inaugurated on 14.7.2008;
- implementation of twenty-seven per cent reservation for Backward Classes in Union Government educational institutions;
- a world-class Central University in Coimbatore;
- an Indian Institute of Management in Tiruchirappalli;
- a Central University in Tiruvarur;
- and the Indian Maritime University, with an investment of ₹245 crore, inaugurated in Chennai on 31.12.2008.

**Udanpirappē,**

The list is long.

The source says the United Progressive Alliance is returning to the people to seek votes on the basis of these achievements.

Congress, the principal party in that alliance, has issued an election manifesto.

Yesterday the DMK too issued one.

The DMK manifesto I released yesterday has appeared in the newspapers.

It sets out what the Kazhagam government in the State and the United Progressive Alliance government at the Centre claim to have achieved, and what they say they will do if given another opportunity.

The source author calls on Kazhagam Udanpirappugal and Udanpirappugal in allied parties to explain these points to voters.

He says they should set out with evidence the claim that the governments worked in a manner that gave satisfaction to people who voted for them in the previous election.

He says he will continue writing about the achievements claimed by the Democratic Progressive Alliance government formed in Tamil Nadu in 2006, just as he has listed the United Progressive Alliance government’s schemes at the Centre.

And he tells his party worker:

do not set aside the responsibility of meeting voters;

work now with the resolve that food, sleep and rest can wait until votes have been counted and victory has been secured.

The names of alliance candidates for each constituency, he says, will be announced very soon.

Once a name is announced, regard that candidate as our candidate for victory, and work with affection and commitment.

Gather the fruits of victory.

Climb the hill and raise the flag.

The author then recalls his own early election work.

Before 1950, beginning with the Tiruvarur municipal election, he says he worked as a flag-carrying volunteer and a young lion spreading the movement’s principles, spinning like a top.

In those days there were no candidate symbols.

People voted using green, red or yellow boxes.

At election processions in Tiruvarur, opposing groups would challenge one another through slogans, but peacefully.

The opposition would shout:

> “Women put yellow on their faces — vote for the yellow box!”

I would immediately rouse our side to answer:

> “What is applied on that yellow face is red — so vote for our red box!”

Those were noisy contests, but, the source remembers, they were peaceful and non-violent.

Later came the Chennai Corporation election.

I worked hard for A. P. Arasu to become our Mayor, and Anna placed a **kanaiyazhi** ring on my hand.

Then, obeying Anna’s instruction, I contested and won the Kulithalai Assembly constituency in 1957.

Yes —

enough to celebrate a golden jubilee in the Legislative Assembly —

how many elections!

How much labour!

How much!

How much!

The author says he followed Anna’s path without collapsing in defeat or leaping excessively in victory.

That service, he says, is what brought the Kazhagam — and “this elder brother of yours” — into office for a fifth time.

From this journey I have from time to time shared with you the fruits of experience I gathered.

The strength and radiance gained from them, he says, belong not to you or me, but to our life-like Kazhagam.

The world knows this.

You and I rejoice in it.

That very joy irritates some people, he says, and they babble all kinds of things.

Journalists call it a “pounce.”

If a tiger pounces, we may be frightened.

If a cat pounces, only the pot will roll!

So, says the source author, cast all that aside.

My Udanpirappē, dearer than my own life —

rise at once and set forth.

Votes will be counted on May 16.

May every vote that comes to us, he says, be a vote earned by the sweat from your forehead falling to the soil.

And with the Revolutionary Poet’s line —

> **“Behold, the pride of Tamil lions has set forth!”**

let the youth force,  
the force of idealists,  
set forth.

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**4-4-2009**

உடன்பிறப்பே, இந்தப் பட்டியல் நீளமானது; இவ்வளவு சாதனைகளையும் மக்களுக்காக செய்து விட்டுத்தான் - அந்தச் சாதனைகளின் அடிப்படையில் திரும்பவும் மக்களிடம் வாக்குகளைக் கேட்க ஐக்கிய முற்போக்கு கூட்டணி முன் வந்துள்ளது. அந்த ஐக்கிய முற்போக்குக் கூட்டணியின் பிரதான கட்சியான காங்கிரஸ் கட்சி சார்பில் ஒரு தேர்தல் அறிக்கையும் - நேற்றைய தினம் தி.மு. கழகத்தின் சார்பில் ஒரு தேர்தல் அறிக்கையும் வெளியிடப்பட்டுள்ளன.

தி.மு.கழகத்தின் சார்பில் நேற்றைய தினம் நான் வெளியிட்டுள்ள தேர்தல் அறிக்கை ஏடுகளில் எல்லாம் வெளிவந்துள்ளது. இந்த தேர்தல் அறிக்கையில் மாநிலத்தில் கழக அரசும், மத்தியில் ஐக்கிய முற்போக்குக் கூட்டணி அரசும் செய்த சாதனைகள் எடுத்துக் காட்டப்பட்டுள்ளன. மேலும் நீங்கள் வாய்ப்பளித்தால் தொடர்ந்து என்னென்ன செய்வோம் என்பதும் எடுத்து விளக்கப்பட்டுள்ளது. இவற்றை கழக உடன்பிறப்புகளும், தோழமைக் கட்சிகளின் உடன்பிறப்புகளும் வாக்காளர் பெருமக்களிடம் எடுத்து விளக்கிட வேண்டும். கடந்த முறை வாக்களித்த மக்களின் மனம் நிறைவு பெறத்தக்க அளவிற்கு நாம் பணி யாற்றியிருக்கிறோம் என்பதை ஆதார பூர்வமாக எடுத்துக் கூற வேண்டும். மத்தியில் எவ்வாறு ஐக்கிய முற்போக்குக் கூட்டணி அரசு சாதனைகளையும், திட்டங்களையும் நிறைவேற்றியிருக்கிறதோ அதைப்போலவே தமிழகத்தில் 2006ஆம் ஆண்டு ஏற்பட்ட ஜனநாயக முற்போக்குக் கூட்டணி

அரசின் சார்பில் என்னென்ன சாதனைகளை செய்திருக்கிறோம் என்பதையும் தொடர்ந்து எழுதுகிறேன்.

நீயும் தொடர்ந்து வாக்காளர்களைச் சந்திக்கும் பொறுப்பைத் கட்டிக் கழித்திடாமல், வாக்குகள் எண்ணி முடித்து, வெற்றி வாகை சூடிய பிறகுதான் - உணவு, உறக்கம், ஓய்வு என்று முடிவெடுத்துக் கொண்டு உழைத்திட இக்கணமே புறப்படு - நமது அணியின் வேட்பாளர்கள் எந்தத் தொகுதியில் யார் என்று பெயர்கள் மிக விரைவில் அறிவிக்கப்பட இருக்கிறது.

அப்படி அறிவிக்கப்பட்ட பெயருக்குரிய வேட்பாளர்தான்; நமது வெற்றி வேட்பாளர் என்ற பற்று பாச உணர்வுடன்; வெற்றிக் கனிகள் பறித்துக் குவித்திடுக! குன்றேறி நின்று கொடி உயர்த்திடுக!

1950ஆம் ஆண்டுக்கு முன்பே திருவாரூர் நகராட்சித் தேர்தலில் இருந்து - கொடி பிடிக்கும் தொண்டனாக - கொள்கைப் பரப்பும் சிங்க இளைஞனாக - பணியாற்றிடப் பம்பரமெனச் சுழன்றவன் நான் - அப்போதெல்லாம் தேர்தல்களில் சின்னங்கள் இல்லை - பச்சைப்பெட்டி, சிகப்புப் பெட்டி, மஞ்சள் பெட்டி என்றுதான் மக்கள் வாக்களிப்பர் - திருவாரூரில் தேர்தல் பிரச்சார ஊர்வலங்கள்; முழக்கமிட்டு (அமைதியாக) மோதிக் கொள்வதுண்டு - எமக்கு எதிரணி ஊர்வலத்தில் “பெண்கள் முகத்திலிடுவது மஞ்சள் - அந்த மஞ்சள் பெட்டிக்கு ஓட்டுப் போடுக” என்பார்கள் எதிர் அணியினர். நான் உடனே என் அணியினரை ஆர்த்தெழச் செய்து “அந்த மஞ்சள் முகத்திலிடுவது சிகப்பு; ஆகவே எங்கள் சிகப்புப் பெட்டிக்கு ஓட்டுப் போடுக” என முழங்குவேன் - இப்படி ஆரவாரம் மிகுந்த போட்டிகளே தவிர, அனைத்தும் அப்போது அமைதியான அறவழிப் போட்டிகளே ஆகும்.

பின்னர் சென்னை மாநகராட்சி மன்றத் தேர்தல் - நமது மேயராக அ.பொ. அரசு வர அரும்பாடுபட்டு; அண்ணாவின் கரத்தினால் “கணையாழி” அணிவிக்கப் பெற்றேன். அதற்கடுத்து அண்ணாவின் ஆணையேற்று 1957இல் “குளித்தலை” சட்டமன்றத் தொகுதியில் நின்று வென்றேன்.

ஆம்; சட்டமன்றப் பொன்விழா காணும் அளவுக்கு; அப்பப்பா; எத்தனை தேர்தல்கள் - என்னே உழைப்பு - என்னே! என்னே!

இந்தப் பயணத்தில் நான் கண்டெடுத்த அனுபவக் கனிகளை - உனக்கும் உண்டிடத் தந்துள்ளேன் அவ்வப்போது!

அதனால் பெற்ற வலிவும் பொலிவும் உனக்கும் எனக்குமல்ல; உயிரனைய நமது கழகத்திற்கே என்பதை இந்த உலகறியும்.

அதில் உனக்கும் எனக்கும் உவகை! உவகை! இந்த உவகைதான், சில எரிச்சல்காரர்களின் உள்ளங்களை உறுத்துகிறது, உளறுகிறார்கள் ஏதேதோ! அதைப் “பாய்ச்சல்” என்கிறார்கள் பத்திரிகையாளர்கள்; புலி பாய்ந்தால் “கிலி” அடைவோம்; பூனை பாய்ந்தால் பானைதானே உருளும்!

எனவே அவற்றை உதறித் தள்ளிவிட்டு; என் உயிரினும் மேலான உடன்பிறப்பே; உடனே ஆர்த்தெழுந்து புறப்படு!
