## Review method

Each English translation was compared directly with the complete audited Tamil source reproduced in the bilingual letter and the canonical page sequence. The review checked:

1. title, salutation, closing and date;
2. paragraph and argumentative sequence;
3. every substantive claim and attribution;
4. quotations and reported speech;
5. names, dates, counts, monetary figures and institutional terms;
6. list completeness and item order;
7. rhetorical questions, irony, repetition, proverbs and political intensity; and
8. English wording that could accidentally broaden, narrow, normalise or change responsibility.

The Tamil source had already passed the scan-based textual-fidelity audit. **No canonical Tamil page was modified in this alignment pass.**
