# Textual-Fidelity Audit — Letters 3800–3804

## Scope

- Volume: **49**
- Letters: **3800–3804**
- Canonical Tamil Markdown pages: **PDF 277–313**
- Total pages visually compared with scans: **37/37**
- Method: every canonical page was compared against its corresponding rendered PDF scan before translation. Titles, letter numbers, paragraph order, quotations, English passages, figures, punctuation, signatures, dates and page boundaries were checked.

## Result

- All **37** pages were inspected.
- No paragraph or passage was missing.
- The canonical Markdown matched the visible scans closely enough that **no canonical correction was required** in this batch.
- Printed irregularities were retained rather than silently modernised or repaired.

## Source-exact features retained

- PDF page 278 visibly prints `இந்தப் பிரிவுகளின் ழ்`; the missing-looking syllable was not supplied editorially.
- PDF page 281 visibly divides `அனுப்பி / யிருப்பதாகவும்` and prints `முடிவெடுக்கப் படும்`; these source forms remain unchanged.
- PDF page 282 visibly prints `பாதிக்கப்படமாட்டா.`; it was not normalised to a different grammatical form.
- PDF page 301 visibly prints `நடைமுறைப்படுத்தப் பட்டதோடு`, and PDF page 306 carries the line division `மகளிர் முன்னேற்றத் / திற்க்காகச்`; both are preserved.
- PDF page 309 visibly prints the constitutional provision as `51-ஹ(ா)` and includes spacing before the semicolon in `எதிரானவை ;`; both remain source-exact.
- PDF pages 311–312 visibly retain spacing before semicolons in `நிறுவி இருப்பதாகவும் ;` and `மேற்கொள்ளப்பட வேண்டும் என்பதும் ;`.
- The original English passages in letters 3800, 3801, 3802 and 3804 are reproduced without silently correcting their grammar, punctuation, capitalisation or typography.
- Political descriptions, project costs, beneficiary counts, dates, percentages and attributed statements are translated as presented, without outside fact-correction or numerical reconciliation.

## Translation assembly

Each English file contains:

1. the standard Translator’s note;
2. a complete thought-preserving English translation;
3. letter-specific source notes where required; and
4. the full canonical Tamil text, divided by original PDF page.

This audit establishes **source-checked** status for letters **3800–3804**. Full bilingual line-by-line editorial review remains a later, separate stage.
