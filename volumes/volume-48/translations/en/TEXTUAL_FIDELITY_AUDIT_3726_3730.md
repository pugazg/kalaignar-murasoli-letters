## Scope and method

This audit covers five consecutive letters and **31/31 canonical pages**. Every page was visually compared with the rendered original scan. The review checked letter titles, salutations, page and paragraph continuity, quoted passages, names, dates, figures, list/bullet structures, intentional English text, punctuation affecting the printed reading, closing/signature/date pages, and possible omissions or duplicated text.

No source wording was modernised or silently regularised. Where the scan prints unusual spacing, punctuation, quotation behaviour or word forms, the scan remains authoritative.

## Source forms deliberately preserved

The audit deliberately retained source-specific forms and structures, including:

- letter 3726's repeated `“ரிமாண்ட்”` terminology, `ஜி.பி.ஆர்.எஸ்.`, the printed Italy/Kerala/Tamil-fishermen comparison and the quoted political wording;
- letter 3727's quoted allegations, company/tender figures and the printed company name `இன்ஜெனரி டெக்னாலஜீஸ் சொல்யூஷன்ஸ்`;
- letter 3728's statutory title, reservation percentages, education-beneficiary counts, welfare-board figures and the long 26.2.2009 letter quotation;
- letter 3729's `586.86` acres, `19,500` plots, 2006 satellite-town quotation, the `தமிழ் ஓசை` and `தீக்கதிர்` quoted passages, and the repeated rhetorical use of `“துணை”`;
- letter 3730's decorative bullet sequence, political quotations, date/number forms, the English sentence `The Kaveri River Authority headed by the Prime Minister is a very big fraud perpetrated on the farmers of the kauveri Delta in Tamil Nadu`, including the printed lowercase `kauveri`; and
- the source's inconsistent joined/separated forms where they visibly differ within the same letter.

No large passage was missing, no duplicated canonical passage was detected, and all five letter closing/date boundaries matched the scans.
