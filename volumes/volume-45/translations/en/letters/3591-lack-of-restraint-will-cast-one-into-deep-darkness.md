# 3591. Lack of Restraint Will Cast One into Deep Darkness!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This translation is intended to carry Kalaignar's voice into clear, contemporary English rather than recast the letter as literary or academic prose. It preserves the source's argument, political directness, rhetorical questions, repetition, irony, factual detail, and paragraph order. Names, dates, figures, quotations, and intentional English expressions are retained. Where Tamil idiom cannot be reproduced literally without sounding unnatural, the English follows its sense and rhetorical force without adding claims absent from the source. The original Tamil is reproduced in full below the translation and remains the authoritative text. `Udanpirappē` is retained in Tamil transliteration rather than flattened into “brother,” “sister,” or “comrade.” Literally evoking “one born alongside me,” Kalaignar uses it as a distinctive address of shared identity, equality, affection, and solidarity within the movement.

**Date:** 27 September 2011

**Udanpirappē,**

Another former minister who has now become the latest victim of this Government's cruelty of foisting false cases, arresting people and locking them in prison is Thambi K.P.P. Samy. He belongs to the fishing community and has rendered valuable service to that community across the State.

“Local-body elections are coming soon, aren't they? Can K.P.P. Samy be allowed to remain outside? If he remains free, won't he work in the election? Put a murder case on him immediately!”—as though this were the reasoning, the AIADMK Government has arrested him too.

He is a fisherman, after all. The police apparently tried to point out that there was no way to arrest him in a land-grab case, but without listening to them, a murder case itself has been filed against him. And what is the allegation? That he and others abducted and murdered two people belonging to the very community for whose welfare he had worked!

In that case, the police had already arrested nine people, including his younger brothers. When his brothers learnt that the police were looking for them, they themselves surrendered to the police on 23-8-2011. Shocked by this, K.P.P. Samy's wife—the sister-in-law who had brought up those younger brothers—suffered a sudden heart attack and died on 25-8-2011. Fortunately, perhaps, she did not live to hear of her husband's arrest. If the arrest of the younger brothers alone was more than she could bear and carried her to death's edge, what would have happened had she heard that her husband too had been arrested? Should K.P.P. Samy weep over his brothers' arrest, or shed tears over the death of his wife? Before his tears had even dried, Jayalalithaa's Government arrested him as well.

The case against him alleges that, a few days after the DMK Government came to office in 2006, Samy, his brothers and some others abducted two fishermen and killed them.

When Samy was arrested and taken away, he told reporters:

> “People know that the AIADMK Government has been targeting and arresting DMK members. The police have portrayed an incident that never happened as though it did happen and have filed a false case. My wife died from the shock caused by police harassment alleging two or three murders against me. Both my younger brothers have been arrested. We will face this legally and prove that we are innocent.”

A newspaper reporting on this action wrote:

> “An allegation has arisen that the Tiruvottiyur police failed to investigate this murder case properly and showed negligence. Following this, six officers—Tiruvottiyur Inspector Bhaskar, Sub-Inspectors Karpagamani, Kamalakannan and Rajarajan, and head constables Senthil Saravanan and Murugan—have all been transferred. The Inspector has been transferred to the North Zone. The others have been transferred to the Police Control Room. It is said that action may also be taken against the officers, including the Inspector, who investigated this case in 2006.”

The dates for the local-body elections have been announced and the election code of conduct is in force. At such a time, how did this Government transfer police officers wholesale in this manner? Whom are we to ask? Is there a State Election Commission? Are we really living in a democratic country? Is it a crime merely to have been a minister in a DMK Government? Why is this Government behaving with such frenzy?

A similar situation existed when the AIADMK came to power in 2001. Yes, on 21-3-2002 I wrote an Udanpirappu letter in *Murasoli* under the title, “Bala! What Is Happening in This Country?”

Who was that Bala? Here are a few lines from the letter I wrote then....

> “Bala! Tall as a temple flagstaff, with a body like diamond-grained teak, and with a heart as courageous as Bharathi who sang, ‘O Death! Come—I shall trample you beneath my foot!’ You bore the proud name Balan; with the majesty of a sword drawn from its sheath and given life to walk, were you not a young bull who laboured for the movement!
>
> “For three months we have not seen you. ‘Here! There! We will find him today! We will find him tomorrow!’ the police officers kept assuring us.
>
> “Your wife and children cried out and shed tears. Your eldest son, Selvan Manimaran, appealed to the court. Meanwhile the blame was placed upon the very movement in which you had moved with affection! It was not an accusation made by some passer-by on the street. It was a false accusation constructed by the elected head of the Government herself—saying that one might even be led to think this way!
>
> “Perhaps that is why, although word has now come that you have disappeared, as early as seven days ago the words ‘the late Balan’ somehow escaped in the middle of the Assembly—whether knowingly or unknowingly—and were then swallowed back!
>
> “Bala! What are we to do now?
>
> “Are we to believe the report that they burnt you and reduced you to ashes, and collapse in tears? Or are we to take comfort in your son Manimaran's firm insistence that you are still alive and continue the search?
>
> “Bala! With the belief that you are still alive, your wife's *mangalyam*—yes, that sister's marriage thread—still hangs around her neck! My heart has not the strength to conclude firmly that you are no more, that you have gone, that madmen have taken your life!
>
> “I too remain believing that you will come before me, that you will come and, as always, speak to me without lifting your bowed head. If wretches have burnt your body and reduced it to ashes, let that very ash rise like a Sahara desert storm and build a tomb over them and over those who set them upon you. Before there is any need for all these curses, will you not suddenly come? Will you not come and see us?”

That is what I wrote then.

DMK Executive Committee member M.K. Balan left his home for a walk at about 5.30 in the early morning. He was abducted by some people, and afterward no one even knew whether he had been killed or what had happened to him; his own son had to go to court and file a case. When the AIADMK came to power in 2001, party stalwarts like M.K. Balan were abducted and made to disappear. In 2011, after the AIADMK returned to power, party members and a former minister have been arrested and accused of murder in a case alleging that two fishermen were abducted and killed in 2006.

But where is Anna Nagar Jayakumar, who was made to disappear during AIADMK rule? What happened to Jothi Krishnan, who worked as a police officer in Erode district and was the brother of Rajya Sabha member K.P. Ramalingam? What became of those cases? Why is the AIADMK Government so eager to act against DMK members on the allegation that two missing fishermen were murdered, yet refuses to show the same eagerness when the accused belong to the AIADMK?

Even yesterday, in the Madras High Court, a case came up concerning a land-grab complaint lodged with the police last April against an AIADMK member. It was stated that the High Court had ordered the police to register the complaint and take appropriate action, but that the police had not implemented the order. Justice C.P. Selvam criticised the police, asking: What have the police been doing for the past five months? In land-grab complaints they register cases against others and send them to prison. Why has no action been taken against the AIADMK functionary? He imposed a final deadline for the police to reply by the coming Monday. He also noted that he had already directed the appointment of senior police officers and given the police a deadline to investigate the case, yet no action had been taken even then.

Therefore, the law is common to everyone. It must not be used only for taking action against members of the DMK. A Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam Government too was in office. Were false cases like these ever imposed on anyone and action taken then? Governments may come and governments may go. But when responsibility comes, there must be restraint.

That is why Ayyan Valluvar said:

> “Self-restraint raises one among the immortals; lack of restraint casts one into deep darkness.”

Let us hope that at least from now on the rulers will follow this Kural of Valluvar! But will those who think even Valluvar's portrait should not be in school textbooks remember the Kural he wrote?

**With affection, M.K.**  
27-9-2011
