# 3529. Government Employees and Madam Jayalalithaa!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This translation follows the audited canonical Tamil and the locked Volume 44 conventions. Accounts of strikes, arrests, dismissals, deaths, union negotiations, employee benefits and the disproportionate-assets case are retained as source-framed political and historical claims. The source’s “Neeli tears” image, ESMA/TESMA terminology and employment/pay figures are preserved. The complete audited Tamil is reproduced below and remains authoritative.

**Tamil source:** [Letter 3529](../../../chapters/3529-arasu-aluvalargalum-ammaiyaar-jayalalithaavum.md)  
**Source pages:** [PDF 326](../../../pages/page-326.md)–[PDF 331](../../../pages/page-331.md)  
**Date:** 27 February 2011

**Udanpirappē,**

Former Chief Minister Jayalalithaa has issued a statement shedding **Neeli tears** for government employees, Nutritious Meal workers, transport workers, teachers, honorary lecturers and other government employees. Can she so quickly forget what government employees went through during her rule?

She speaks even about transport workers. Has she forgotten how many workers voted for the AIADMK-affiliated union in the election held among transport workers only a few weeks ago? Does she not know the figures showing that the DMK-affiliated union came first, followed by the CITU union, while the AIADMK union came only third among transport workers?

Jayalalithaa now pretends in her statement to speak sympathetically on behalf of government employees. During her government, however, although she announced in the Legislative Assembly as Chief Minister that she would call and speak with striking government employees and teachers, she not only failed to call them for talks but, the letter says, unleashed repressive measures — applying emergency laws, arresting office-bearers and dismissing thousands from service.

On **25-10-2002**, brother Duraimurugan, then Deputy Leader of the Opposition, pressed for an adjournment motion on this issue to be taken up for debate in the Assembly. Jayalalithaa replied that representatives of **JACTO-GEO** had sought time to meet her and that the problem would then be resolved. But she refused even to meet them, saying talks would be held only if the protest committee first abandoned its agitation. Permission to continue speaking on the issue was denied and DMK members were removed from the House.

The source says that **Kasipandian**, headmaster of a school at Nambiyamuthur near Pollachi, who had participated in the teachers’ and government employees’ agitation and picketing, poured kerosene over himself and died by self-immolation. It also says that **Abdul Sattar**, a teacher working in the Manjalar Dam area of Devadanapatti in the Periyakulam area of Theni district, died by suicide in protest against what the letter describes as the AIADMK government’s authoritarian conduct. The DMK Trust gave financial assistance of **₹50,000 each** to the two families.

The government employees’ agitation, which had continued for ten days under AIADMK rule, could not continue further. The government, the letter says, refused to retreat from its rigid position. Union leaders repeatedly asked by telephone to be called for talks, but Jayalalithaa kept threatening that there was nothing more to discuss, that **ESMA** would be invoked if they did not return to work and that they would be dismissed.

The government first said there would be no salary for the strike days, then announced that those days would be treated as leave. The agitation was withdrawn after assurances that cases would be withdrawn, suspension orders revoked and there would be no retaliatory action.

In **1997**, during DMK rule, **10,000 road workers** were appointed. In **2001**, under AIADMK rule, they were dismissed on what the letter calls the false ground of financial crisis. When the DMK returned to office, those road workers were re-employed and are now working. They are the workers who held a hunger strike in Chennai yesterday asking that the **41 months** during which they were kept out of work under AIADMK rule be counted as service.

The principal reason for that hunger strike itself, the letter argues, was Jayalalithaa’s government. Yesterday, when the workers attempted to enter the new Secretariat despite the police stopping them, they were prevented from doing so.

When I was leaving the Secretariat for home in the afternoon and was informed about the road workers, I stopped my car and called the police officers accompanying me for security. I asked them to go directly to the place where the road workers and some other employees were fasting and arrange for their representatives to meet me. The officers did so, spoke to them and brought five or six representatives to my residence.

I then called senior government officials to my home and had them speak with those representatives. When the representatives met me afterward, I asked them, “You are educated people; should you act like this?” and then left to attend my scheduled functions.

Jayalalithaa, knowing nothing of this and seeing that an election is approaching, hurriedly issued a statement today saying that the government employees engaged in a hunger-strike **arapporattam** should be called for talks. The letter asks: will those employees themselves not laugh when a person who, during her rule, promised to talk to union representatives but refused, had government employees arrested at midnight from their homes and imprisoned, and took severe action against them even when they approached the courts, now issues a statement saying they should be called for talks without knowing that I had already invited representatives to my own home and spoken with them?

Only the day before yesterday, **25-2-2011**, office-bearers separately representing the **Tamil Nadu Government Officials Union**, the **Tamil Nadu Revenue Department Village Employees Association**, the **Tamil Nadu Village Administrative Officers Progressive Association**, and the federation of **Tamil Nadu Survey Department associations** came to the Secretariat and met me in person. They thanked the DMK government for the good measures it had continued to take for its employees and said that, on the assurance that the government would consider their remaining demands, they had decided immediately to withdraw their agitations and return to work. That news, too, appeared in the newspapers yesterday.

R. Shanmugarajan, the current president of the Government Officials Union, described by the letter as the largest association of government employees, and other office-bearers met me and thanked me. In a statement issued in the newspapers today on behalf of their association, they said:

> “Kalaignar, the Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu, who is governing the State for the fifth time, is conducting a golden-age government with the objective and foundation that all the people of Tamil Nadu should live in welfare and prosperity. He has fulfilled various demands to bring happiness and light into the lives of government employees and teachers working in the various departments of the Tamil Nadu government.”

The statement further said that they had resolved to hold a one-day hunger strike seeking fulfilment of a ten-point charter of demands in order to draw the government’s attention, but that after the association’s State office-bearers met me for talks, they accepted the Chief Minister’s assurance that the ten demands would be considered sympathetically and favourable orders issued soon. They therefore cancelled the one-day attention-drawing hunger strike and expressed thanks.

Concealing all this, Jayalalithaa issues a statement in the vain hope of winning at least a little affection from government employees. In doing so, the letter says, she merely reminds them of the difficulties they suffered under AIADMK rule — like the saying that even a frog is ruined by its own mouth.

Jayalalithaa’s statement also mentions Nutritious Meal workers. But under this government, with the aim of benefiting **2 lakh 12 thousand 981 Nutritious Meal workers**, we have provided time-scale pay and agreed to provide pensions.

We repealed the **ESMA** and **TESMA** laws brought in during Jayalalithaa’s rule and restored concessions taken away from government employees and teachers. From **1-1-2006**, the Sixth Pay Commission recommendations were implemented in Tamil Nadu at an additional annual cost of **₹5,155 crore 79 lakh**. As a result, the letter says, **₹11,093 crore** was sanctioned to government employees and teachers in arrears alone — a new history created under this DMK government.

When government employees’ associations pointed out certain anomalies in the Pay Commission recommendations, we accepted that concern and, based on the recommendations of a one-person committee, implemented corrections from **1-8-2010** at an additional cost of **₹200 crore**, benefiting **2 lakh government employees and teachers**.

By removing pay anomalies affecting teachers and providing additional concessions at a cost of **₹163 crore**, **2 lakh 73 thousand teachers** benefited. If one were to list all that the DMK government has done for government employees, teachers, Nutritious Meal workers, Anganwadi workers and road workers, one day’s letter would not be enough.

Concealing all of that, if Jayalalithaa now stages a statement as though she has suddenly developed concern for government employees, they are no longer prepared to believe it and be deceived.

Like the saying, **“Let everything else lie; lift the old woman and place her on the platform,”** Jayalalithaa, the letter says, ignores the actual situation and laments that I and members of my family should be arrested and investigated. The reason, it argues, is her fear that in only a few days the law may turn against her through proceedings and judgment in the **₹66-crore disproportionate-assets case** against her.

What can be done? Those who sow deeds must reap their consequences!

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
27 February 2011

கிடக்கட்டும், கிழவியைத் தூக்கி மனையிலேவை என்பதைப்
போல ஜெயலலிதா உண்மை நிலைமைகளை மறைத்து விட்டு
என்னையும் என் குடும்பத்தினரையும் கைது செய்ய வேண்டும்,
விசாரணை நடத்த வேண்டுமென்றெல்லாம் புலம்புகின்றார்
என்றால் அதற்குக் காரணம் இன்னும் சில நாட்களில் அவர்
மீதான 66 கோடி ரூபாய் சொத்துக் குவிப்பு வழக்கிலே விசாரணை,
தீர்ப்பு என்றெல்லாம் தன்மீது எங்கே சட்டத்தின் பாய்ச்சல்
நிகழ்ந்து விடுமோ என்கின்ற அச்சம்தான்! என்ன செய்வது?
வினை விதைத்தவர்கள் வினையை அறுவடை செய்துதானே
ஆகவேண்டும்!
