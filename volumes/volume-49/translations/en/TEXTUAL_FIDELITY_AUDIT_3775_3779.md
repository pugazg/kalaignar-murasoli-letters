# Textual-Fidelity Audit — Letters 3775–3779

## Scope

- Letters: **3775–3779**
- Canonical Markdown pages: **PDF 101–131**
- Total pages visually compared: **31**
- Source PDF: `TVA_BOK_0065839_கலைஞரின்_கடிதங்கள்_தொகுதி_49.pdf`

## Method

Before translation, every canonical Markdown page was visually compared with its corresponding PDF scan. The audit checked letter numbers and titles, salutations and closings, paragraph sequence, page boundaries, names, dates, figures, quotations, intentional English expressions, punctuation affecting meaning, omissions, duplicated text and OCR artefacts. OCR was not treated as authoritative; the scan controlled every decision.

## Source features preserved

- Letter 3775 is a broadcast tribute rather than a standard `Udanpirappē` letter and does not carry the usual `அன்புள்ள, மு.க.` sign-off.
- The poem in letter 3775 retains the unusual printed source form `“பெருந்தலைவ;` in the archival Tamil.
- Letter 3776 retains the organisation name printed as `“புரோடெக்”` without speculative correction.
- Letter 3778 retains its title wordplay between `கச்சத் தீவு` and `கச்சடா`, and reproduces the quoted English letter of 29 June 1974 as printed.
