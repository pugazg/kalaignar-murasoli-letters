# Volume 1 — Bilingual Alignment: Letters 0051–0060

Status: **PASS after two English/Tamil correspondence corrections.**

## Scope

This batch covers canonical English letters **0051–0060**, corresponding to source PDF / printed pages **214–249**.

The English records had already completed canonical migration/source checking. This gate compared each English translation directly with the complete canonical Tamil witness embedded in the record. The canonical Tamil layer had already passed the full scan-based visual/textual-fidelity gate; this batch does not claim a new independent scan transcription pass.

## Alignment method

For every record, the review checked argument and narrative sequence; substantive coverage and unsupported additions; paragraph, verse and quotation correspondence; names, dates, figures and political references; lists, metaphors, wordplay and rhetorical questions; repetition, irony and political intensity; movement vocabulary and the established `Udanpirappē` convention; and closing/sign-off integrity.

Only demonstrable English/Tamil correspondence errors were corrected. Stylistic polishing remains deferred to the later editorial-consistency gate.

**All ten records PASS bilingual alignment. Two English prose/quotation corrections were required, in 0058 and 0059.**

### 0058 — `“நடக்கின்ற ஆச்சரியம்!”`

PASS after quotation-sequence correction. The deliberately impossible Rasputin/Tsar/Louis/religious-establishment comparisons, worker-centred verse, wolf/lamb analogy and fan-club-versus-Kazhagam-worker turn all remain intact.

The opening lineated quotation had previously been reordered in English: the `நிறையுழைப்புத் தோள்கள்` / “shoulders of tireless labour” image was moved ahead of the source's `சிற்றூரும் வரப்பெடுத்த வயலும்` and `ஆறு தேக்கிய நல் வாய்க்காலும்` imagery. Because this gate requires quotation and thought-order correspondence, the English verse has been reordered to follow the Tamil sequence while retaining the already source-checked wording:

> the little village and its embanked fields,  
> the good canal that stores the river's water,  
> and the shoulders of tireless labour  
> that bring forth grain through long ploughing?

No Tamil text was changed. The source date **29-05-1973** remains intact.

### 0059 — `இதுதான் உலகம்!`

PASS after quotation-completeness correction. The cadre-to-leader argument, Anna/Periyar/Navalar/Kamaraj examples, *Ashok Kumar* / *Alibaba* analogy, welfare for freedom fighters and language/border activists, Karna/Vibhishana contrast and R. M. Veerappan letter dated **22 April 1972** remain aligned.

The Tamil excerpt from Veerappan's letter includes the closing `அன்புள்ள, / வீரப்பன்.`. The prior English retained `Veerappan.` but omitted `அன்புள்ள,`. The canonical English quotation now restores **“With affection,”** immediately before **“Veerappan.”**, preserving the complete source-supported inset sign-off.

### 0060 — `“கண்ணீர் மடல்!”`

PASS. The **31 May 1973, 11:30 p.m.** telephone opening, overnight search for information, more-than-forty-victim framing, Mohan Kumaramangalam wooden-horse recollection, Salem/Neyveli passage, Gurnam Singh and presidential-election memory, Baladhandayutham recollection, named victims and grief-stricken close on **02-06-1973** all remain aligned.
