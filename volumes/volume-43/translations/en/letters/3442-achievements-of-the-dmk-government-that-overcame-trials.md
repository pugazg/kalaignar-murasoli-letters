# 3442. Achievements of the DMK Government That Overcame Trials!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> The letter was written immediately before the December 2009 by-elections. It quotes Anna, repeats a 1992 statement attributed in the source to Jayalalithaa, and then sets out the author’s claims about DMK measures for Muslims and fishing communities. Those political and historical claims are preserved in source framing and are not independently reconciled here.

**Tamil source:** [Letter 3442](../../../chapters/3442-sothanaigalai-vendra-kazhaga-arasin-saadhanaigal.md)  
**Source pages:** [PDF 131](../../../pages/page-131.md)–[PDF 137](../../../pages/page-137.md)  
**Date:** 16 December 2009

**Udanpirappē,**

Today is 16-12-2009. The by-elections are on the 19th. Only 48 hours remain in between. Election campaigning is to end tomorrow. All the younger brothers are working night and day in such a way that no shortcoming of any kind can be alleged against anyone. Along with you, women too are continuously engaged in election work.

When Perarignar Anna concluded a letter written to a younger brother on 8-5-1966, he wrote as follows:

> “Each of you must ask yourselves, within yourselves:
>
> Have I applied the full force of my ability to the work?
>
> Am I continuing to work? Am I bringing comrades together and working collectively?
>
> Am I working without anger and with a sense of responsibility?
>
> Am I working with a sense of democratic duty?
>
> Am I working after setting aside every other desire and attachment?
>
> Am I working with the awareness that this is a great service to secure good government for the people?
>
> Younger brother! These questions are not for one person to ask another; each person must ask them of himself. Why do I say so much? Because, younger brother, you are capable of work that is higher both in quantity and quality than what you are doing now. You must not keep thinking ‘tomorrow, tomorrow.’ You must set out with the awakening of ‘today, today.’ A few people are spewing words of abuse! I tell you — I appeal to you — because I have confidence that their political arrogance can be subdued.”
>
> **Elder brother,  
> Annadurai.**

Udanpirappē, I too write in this letter the very words Anna wrote on 8-5-1966. Those on the opposition side, thinking this is their opportunity, are levelling all kinds of accusations against us. They say we have done nothing for fishermen. They say they alone are the representatives of minorities. They say we have not implemented the promises we gave to the Arundhathiyars. They have gone to Tiruchendur and spoken so.

During the kar seva of 1992, when Jayalalithaa spoke in support of the majority, did she not speak attacking the minorities?

Her speech itself appeared in the *Dinamani* dated 24-11-1992. What did she say then?

> “It is not proper for minorities to act in the background of political, historical and social structures in such a way as to advance their interests while affecting the ordinary rights and special rights available to the majority under the Constitution. Everyone will agree that the majority community in this country is the Hindus. If they try peacefully to achieve their religious objectives without violating the aims of the Constitution, they should be permitted to do so. The majority too should be permitted to enjoy their rights as minorities do.
>
> “To relate this specifically to the Ayodhya issue, Hindus should be permitted to carry out construction as they wish on the land acquired by the Uttar Pradesh government. All necessary steps should be taken to approach the courts for permission to conduct kar seva. Let me state my request very briefly. The conditions necessary for kar seva to take place on the land acquired by the Uttar Pradesh government should be created. The demand that a temple should be built at Ayodhya represents the wish of the majority of the people of this country. We should not ignore public opinion — the opinion of the majority of the people of this country.”

If someone who spoke in this manner against minorities now goes to Tiruchendur and speaks as though she is the one who cares for minorities, what a great act of deception is that?

Will Muslim people not know the closeness that exists between Muslims and the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam, and particularly between them and me? Shall I cite one or two examples?

Seventy years ago, while still a school student, I participated as a boy carrying the green crescent flag at an Indian Union Muslim League conference held in Tiruvarur. I recall that with pride.

In 1969, a government holiday was declared for Milad-un-Nabi for the first time. The previous AIADMK government cancelled that holiday in 2001; through an order dated 15.11.2006 it was restored as a government holiday. In 1973, Urdu-speaking Labbais and Deccani Muslims were included in the Backward Classes list. In 1974, the Government Women’s College on Anna Salai, Chennai, was named “Quaid-e-Millath Government College for Women.”

In 1989, so that Muslims and other minority communities could receive substantial benefit, a “Minorities Welfare Commission” was created on 13.2.1989. In 1999, for the first time, ₹40 lakh was provided for maintenance of Wakf Board properties. On 1.7.1999, an organisation named the “Tamil Nadu Minorities Development Corporation” was created. From 19.7.2000, the ulema pension scheme was extended to mujawars working in dargahs. Accepting a long-standing Muslim demand, the “Urdu Academy” was opened on 21.7.2000.

On 25.2.2001, a foundation stone was laid in Chennai for a “Quaid-e-Millath Memorial” at a cost of ₹58 lakh 69 thousand, and arrangements were made for its opening. An Islamic Studies Centre was established at the University of Madras. A separate directorate for minority welfare was established on 6.4.2007. A board named the “Ulema and Employees Welfare Board” was created in March 2009. The concessions and benefits the DMK government has provided to the Muslim community are thus beyond counting.

After India became independent in 1947, from 1947 to 1962 there was no minister from the Muslim community in Tamil Nadu. The first person to raise the demand that Muslims should be given a place in the Cabinet was our Perarignar Anna. Only after that did Kadaiyanallur Majid enter the Congress ministry as a minister in 1962.

The Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam was the first to nominate a Muslim member — Tiruchi Janibai — to the Tamil Nadu Legislative Council. It was the DMK that made Janab Abdul Samad a Rajya Sabha member. In 1967, Periyakulam Metha, Nellai Kathiravan and Sadiq Basha became Members of the Legislative Assembly on behalf of the Muslim community through the DMK.

When the AIADMK was in government from 2001 to 2006, Mr Anwar Raja represented the Muslim community for about the first six months. After that, the AIADMK Cabinet functioned without any Muslim representative.

In the present DMK government that assumed office in 2006, two Muslims — Mr Ubayadullah and Mr Mydeen Khan — are ministers. The Muslim community knows well the truth that there has never been a DMK Cabinet without representation for Muslims.

After obtaining a detailed inquiry, study and appropriate recommendation from the Tamil Nadu Backward Classes Commission headed by Justice Janarthanan, a separate 3.5 per cent reservation was provided for Muslims.

Likewise, I wish to remind you of a few of the achievements of the DMK government for fishermen.

Was it not I who named the Chennai District Collector’s office complex after Singaravelar? Was that not during a DMK government? If people who might ask who Singaravelar was say that they speak for fishermen, will fishermen believe them?

Was it not the DMK government that increased the diesel subsidy for fishermen?

The DMK government decided that when a fisherman who goes to sea for fishing is missing, his family should receive financial assistance of ₹50 a day until he returns; and that the family of a fisherman shot and killed by the Sri Lankan coast guard should receive ₹1 lakh as protective assistance. Those benefits are being provided.

During periods when fishing is not carried out, relief including the 50 per cent shares of the Union and State governments and an amount of ₹300 was provided: in 2006-2007, ₹21 crore 20 lakh to 1 lakh 79 thousand 853 members; and in 2007-2008, ₹20 crore 83 lakh 29 thousand to 1 lakh 76 thousand 17 members.

A new scheme was implemented in 2008-2009 to provide relief assistance of ₹500 each — ₹10 crore in all — to two lakh families unable to engage in fishing during the period regarded as the fish-breeding season.

There is a scheme to give cash prizes to students obtaining the highest marks at State and district level in Standards 10 and 12; a scheme to create artificial fish habitats; and a scheme to provide outboard motors to fishermen at subsidised prices.

For fishermen’s welfare, the Tamil Nadu Fishermen Welfare Board was constituted and ₹5 crore was provided by the DMK government.

Following damage in the December 2004 tsunami to 50 mechanised boats belonging to Tamil Nadu fishermen that had been detained by the Sri Lankan government, the Tamil Nadu government issued an order providing ₹2 crore 88 lakh in relief under the tsunami rehabilitation scheme.

A world-class fishermen’s training centre was established for fishermen’s benefit.

As additional relief for tsunami-affected fishermen in Tamil Nadu, nets, protective clothing, hand torches, bicycles and ice boxes and other items were distributed to 42,000 fishermen at a cost of ₹110 crore 36 lakh.

Through 147 primary fishermen’s cooperative societies, the DMK government waived the entire ₹19 crore 56 lakh in loans and interest owed by 5,635 fishermen.

Udanpirappē, as far as the DMK government is concerned, it does not work only for fishermen or minorities. It works with the single objective of providing every kind of benefit to people of all sections. All the people know this. But those who come to campaign for the opposition during a by-election speak of one thing or another merely because they have to say something to the people.

Even if there were no need to answer them, I have used their statements as an opportunity to remind the people of what we have done.

You too should know these details, tell the Udanpirappugal in the constituency, and arrange for all of them to vote for our Rising Sun symbol and pluck the fruit of victory. Do not forget that the achievements of the DMK government that overcame trials are our weapons of struggle in the democratic arena!

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**16-12-2009**

147 முதன்மை மீனவ கூட்டுறவு சங்கங்களின் மூலம் 5
ஆயிரத்து 635 மீனவர்களுக்கு வழங்கப்பட்ட 19 கோடியே 56
இலட்சம் ரூபாய் கடன் மற்றும் வட்டித் தொகை முழுவதையும்
கழக அரசு தள்ளுபடி செய்துள்ளது.

உடன்பிறப்பே, கழக அரசைப் பொறுத்த வரையில்
மீனவர்கள், சிறுபான்மையோர் என்றல்ல; அனைத்துத் தரப்பு
மக்களுக்கும் எல்லா வித நன்மைகளையும் செய்ய வேண்டுமென்ற
ஒரே நோக்கத்தோடுதான் பாடுபட்டு வருகின்றது. இதனை மக்கள்
அனைவரும் அறிவார்கள். ஆனால் எதிர்க்கட்சியின் சார்பில் இடைத்
தேர்தல் நேரத்தில் பிரச்சாரத்திற்காக வருபவர்கள் எதையாவது
மக்களிடம் சொல்லியாக வேண்டுமே என்பதற்காக சிலவற்றைப்
பேசுகின்றார்கள். அதற்குப் பதில் சொல்ல வேண்டுமென்று
இல்லாவிட்டாலும், அதைப் பயன்படுத்திக் கொண்டு நாம்
என்னென்ன செய்தோம் என்பதை மக்களுக்கு நினைவூட்டு
வதற்காக ஒரு வாய்ப்பாகக் கொண்டு இதனை எழுதியிருக்கிறேன்.

இந்த விவரங்களை நீயும் தெரிந்து கொள்வதோடு,
தொகுதியிலே உள்ள உடன்பிறப்புகளுக்கும் சொல்லி அவர்களை
யெல்லாம் நமது உதய சூரியன் சின்னத்திலே வாக்களித்து
வெற்றிக் கனியைப் பறித்திட ஆவன செய்திட வேண்டுமென்று
கேட்டுக் கொள்கிறேன். சோதனைகளை வென்ற கழக அரசின்
சாதனைகளே; ஜனநாயகக் களத்தில் நமது போர்க் கருவிகள்
என்பதை மறந்து விடாதே!
