# 3531. The Harvest Was Abundant; the Farmer's Tears Disappeared!

> **Translator’s note**
>

Every printed year, percentage, beneficiary count, rupee amount, acreage/hectare figure, procurement price, production figure and scheme-specific quantity represented in the source is retained in source order rather than reconciled from outside records. **Udanpirappē,**

Perarignar Anna said that the day the country attained freedom was the **“day the accounts were settled,”** and that the day on which that freedom is celebrated every year is the **“day the accounts are reviewed.”** Keeping that thought in its heart, the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam government has worked day after day. After assuming office for the fifth time in May 2006, for the past five years it has worked with the feeling that **service to the people is service to Mahesan**, discussing with officials each dawn what could be done for the people that day and what schemes could be implemented. The people of Tamil Nadu know this well.

At the time of the 2006 general election, we went before the people of Tamil Nadu and said: vote for us; if we assume office we will carry out these schemes for you; **“We shall do what we say; we shall say what we do.”** The people trusted us and seated us in office. Now, as we prepare to meet them again in 2011, I am in the position of having to render an account: did we fulfil the assurances given in 2006, and what additional schemes did we implement for the people through the various departments?

Whenever time permits, I will tell you what schemes and concessions were implemented through each department between 2006 and the end of 2010. I need hardly remind you that you and Udanpirappugal like you have the responsibility of taking that account to the people.

Ayyan Valluvar praised agriculture in lines whose immediate sense is that **the world turns behind the plough, and therefore, despite hardship, farming stands foremost**, and that **farmers are the sustaining axle of the world, bearing all those who cannot plough for themselves**.

In that spirit, I set out in this letter a list of the achievements of the DMK government in the Agriculture Department over the past four and a half years.

In the DMK manifesto placed before the people in the 2006 general election, we said: **“We shall waive in full all cooperative loans so far given to small and marginal farmers.”** On the very day I took office as Chief Minister after the DMK’s election victory, while still on the same stage, I signed the file granting that concession to farmers. Tamil Nadu’s farmers cannot have forgotten it. It was one of the three files I signed that day.

As a result, **₹7,000 crore** in cooperative agricultural loans was waived, benefiting **22 lakh 40 thousand 739 farming families**.

The interest rate on crop loans to farmers, which had been **9 per cent in 2005–2006**, was progressively reduced under the DMK government:

- in **2006–2007**, to **7 per cent**, with **6,31,283 farmers** receiving **₹1,250 crore 62 lakh**;
- in **2007–2008**, to **5 per cent**, with **6,48,397 farmers** receiving **₹1,393 crore 97 lakh**;
- in **2008–2009**, to **4 per cent**, with **6,91,192 farmers** receiving **₹1,570 crore 99 lakh**;
- in **2009–2010**, a revolutionary scheme, described by the source as the first of its kind in India, completely cancelled interest for farmers who repaid crop loans properly; **8,98,540 farmers** received **₹2,169 crore 48 lakh**;
- in the current financial year **2010–2011**, up to **15-2-2011**, **8 lakh 1 thousand 960 farmers** received **₹2,453 crore**.

Thus, since 2006, crop loans totalling **₹8,838 crore 6 lakh** were given to **36 lakh 71 thousand 372 farmers**.

Under the Social Security Scheme for farmers and agricultural labourers, **1 crore 73 lakh 92 thousand 556 people** were enrolled as members. So far, **8 lakh 61 thousand 614 member families** have received **₹632 crore 59 lakh 61 thousand 257** in assistance under heads such as old-age pension, education, marriage and accident relief.

For farmers, **talavari, talamelvari and water cess** were abolished. From 2009–2010, land tax has been collected only nominally: for dry land, the average **₹15 per acre** was reduced to **₹2**; for wet land, the average **₹50 per acre** was reduced to **₹5**.

Under the free-electricity scheme for agricultural pump sets introduced by the DMK government in **1990**, **19 lakh 13 thousand 697 farmers** are benefiting. Steps are being taken gradually to provide free electricity connections to another **2 lakh pump sets** now pending.

After the heavy rain and floods of **November 2008** damaged paddy and other crops cultivated over **5.77 lakh hectares**, the government removed the prescribed compensation ceiling of **2 hectares**, increased relief for paddy affected by natural calamity from **₹4,000 per hectare to ₹7,500 per hectare**, ordered relief for the entire affected agricultural area, and paid **₹404 crore 41 lakh** as flood relief to **9 lakh 10 thousand 200 farmers**.

Under the agricultural insurance scheme intended to protect farmers from crop losses caused by natural calamities, the Tamil Nadu government subsidised **50 per cent of the insurance premium**. The number of insured farmers consequently rose gradually from **1 lakh in 2006 to 9 lakh 88 thousand**. During the past four years, **9 lakh farmers** received **₹974 crore** as compensation under the scheme. In the current year, **₹40 crore** has been allocated for premium subsidy and **₹200 crore** as the Tamil Nadu government’s share toward compensation.

To further relieve farmers’ distress, **₹196 crore 37 lakh** in **Taccavi loans**, including soil-conservation loans collectible from farmers from **1-4-1980**, was waived.

For the Agriculture Department, the source compares budget allocations as follows:

- **2001–2006 AIADMK five-year rule:** **₹4,137 crore 34 lakh**;
- **2006–2011 DMK five-year rule:** **₹7,437 crore 23 lakh**.

### Record in food-grain production

- **2005–2006:** 61.17 lakh tonnes;
- **2006–2007:** 82.63 lakh tonnes;
- **2007–2008:** 65.82 lakh tonnes;
- **2008–2009:** 71.01 lakh tonnes;
- **2009–2010:** 84.29 lakh tonnes.

To improve the condition of Tamil Nadu’s paddy farmers, the State government added support over the price fixed by the Union Government.

### Paddy price — ordinary variety

- **2006–2007:** Union price **₹580 per quintal**; DMK government raised it to **₹620**.
- **2007–2008:** Union price **₹745**; DMK government raised it to **₹795**.
- **2008–2009:** Union price **₹900**; DMK government raised it to **₹1,000**.
- **2009–2010 and 2010–2011:** Union price **₹1,000**; DMK government raised it to **₹1,050**.
- The source notes that the AIADMK government’s procurement price in **2005–2006** had been **₹600 per quintal**, while the DMK government increased the price year by year, reaching **₹1,050 for ordinary paddy in 2009–2010**.

### Paddy price — fine variety

- **2006–2007:** Union price **₹610 per quintal**; DMK government raised it to **₹650**.
- **2007–2008:** Union price **₹775**; DMK government raised it to **₹825**.
- **2008–2009:** Union price **₹930**; DMK government raised it to **₹1,050**.
- **2009–2010 and 2010–2011:** Union price **₹1,030**; DMK government raised it to **₹1,100**.

### Sugarcane price

The DMK government, the letter says, likewise increased the price paid to sugarcane farmers year after year:

- **2005–2006:** AIADMK government price **₹1,014 per tonne**;
- **2006–2007:** after the DMK government assumed office, the Union-fixed **₹802.50** was raised to **₹1,024** per tonne;
- **2007–2008:** **₹811.80** was raised to **₹1,034**;
- **2008–2009:** **₹811.80** was raised to **₹1,100**;
- **2009–2010:** when the Union Government announced a fair and remunerative price of **₹1,077.60** for sugarcane with **9.5 per cent sugar recovery**, the DMK government paid **₹1,650 per tonne**, including transport charges and incentive;
- **2010–2011:** against the Union announcement of **₹1,391**, the DMK government raised payment to **₹2,000 per tonne**.

Loans obtained by Adi Dravidar farmers through **TAHDCO** up to **31-3-2006**, amounting with interest to **₹5 crore 25 lakh**, were waived.

At the 2006 Independence Day function, financial assistance of **₹50,000 each** was provided to the families of **19 farmers** who, the source says, had died because of drought during the 2001–2006 AIADMK period.

Under the free-land scheme for landless poor agricultural-labourer families, launched at Tiruvallur on **17-9-2006**, Periyar’s birthday, **2 lakh 12 thousand 853 acres** of developed land was distributed free to **1 lakh 78 thousand 796 landless poor agricultural-labourer and farmer families**.

Free house-site pattas were given to **7 lakh 74 thousand 386 poor families**.

Because Tamil Nadu stands first in coconut cultivation, a **Coconut Farmers Welfare Board** was constituted on **27-8-2010** under the chairmanship of **S. Rajkumar Mandradiyar**, for the welfare of farmers cultivating the long-lived coconut crop across the districts.

Under the **Uzhavar Sandhai (Farmers’ Market)** scheme introduced in **1999** for the direct benefit of farmers and consumers, **103 markets** were opened by May 2001 and won public appreciation. The letter says the subsequent AIADMK government allowed the scheme to deteriorate. After the DMK government returned in 2006, those markets were revitalised and **50 new markets** opened, bringing the total to **153 Uzhavar Sandhais**, benefiting farmers and the public.

A scheme to organise farmers into self-help groups and provide revolving funds was introduced in **2008**. So far, **35,924 groups** with **2 lakh 7 thousand farmers** as members have been formed; about **₹30 crore** has been provided as revolving fund and **₹442 crore 32 lakh** as crop loans.

Under the free-electricity scheme, to prevent waste of power, it was announced at the last Independence Day function that old motors of small and marginal farmers would be replaced with new motors free of cost, while old motors in the pump sets of larger farmers would be replaced with new ones at **50 per cent subsidy**, over five years. Preparatory work is under way for speedy implementation.

Under the already-existing scheme to provide new pump sets at 50 per cent subsidy in place of old ones, since 2006 farmers have received **₹39 crore 57 lakh 12 thousand** in subsidy to purchase new pump sets replacing **62,772 old pump sets**.

To increase the productivity of horticultural crops and give cultivating farmers greater income, a **precision farming scheme** has been implemented from **2007–2008**. It has so far covered **57,500 acres**, benefiting thousands of farmers. In **2010–2011**, it is being implemented over **12,500 acres** at an estimated cost of **₹38 crore 25 lakh**. Under the scheme, farmers receive **65 per cent subsidy** for drip irrigation, **₹15,000 per hectare** for water-soluble fertiliser and **₹1,000 per farmer** for training at Tamil Nadu Agricultural University.

### National Food Security Scheme

Under the National Food Security Scheme implemented from **2007–2008**, work is being carried out with an allocation of **₹47 crore 82 lakh**:

- to increase paddy production in **five districts** — Nagapattinam, Tiruvarur, Pudukkottai, Sivaganga and Ramanathapuram;
- to increase pulse production in **twelve districts** — Tiruvallur, Nagapattinam, Tiruvarur, Cuddalore, Vellore, Villupuram, Tiruvannamalai, Namakkal, Coimbatore, Erode, Virudhunagar and Thoothukudi.

Quality certified seeds, micronutrient fertilisers, crop-protection equipment and agricultural machinery have been supplied to farmers under the scheme.

### Seed distribution

From 2006 to February 2010, farmers were supplied:

- **70,175 metric tonnes of paddy seed** at a cost of **₹106 crore 25 lakh**;
- **1,493 metric tonnes of small-millet seed**;
- **11,362 metric tonnes of oilseeds** under the ISOPOM scheme at a cost of **₹11 crore 40 lakh**;
- **6,629 metric tonnes of pulse seed**, along with training.

### Fertiliser distribution

During the four years from **2006–2007 through 2009–2010**, average distribution was:

- **9.88 lakh metric tonnes of urea**;
- **3.43 lakh metric tonnes of DAP**;
- **4.99 lakh metric tonnes of potash**;
- **3.90 lakh metric tonnes of complex fertiliser**.

In **2010–2011**, up to November, distribution was:

- **6.63 lakh metric tonnes of urea**;
- **2.172 lakh metric tonnes of DAP**;
- **3.111 lakh metric tonnes of potash**;
- **4.284 lakh metric tonnes of complex fertiliser**.

When there was a nationwide shortage of DAP in **2007–2008**, the source says Tamil Nadu became the first State government in India to appoint **TANFED** as the State agency and provide it an interest-free advance:

- with **₹30 crore** in 2007–2008, **1.04 lakh metric tonnes of DAP** was procured;
- with **₹40 crore** in 2008–2009, **3.13 lakh metric tonnes** was procured and supplied to farmers without interruption;
- in **2009–2010**, with **₹90 crore**, **2.83 lakh metric tonnes** was procured through TANFED and distributed without shortage through Primary Agricultural Cooperative Banks;
- in **2010–2011**, again with **₹90 crore**, **2.17 lakh metric tonnes** had so far been procured and was being distributed without shortage through those banks.

### Rajarajan 1000 cultivation

At Thanjavur, while celebrating Rajaraja’s **1000th birth anniversary**, I named a new high-yield paddy technology **“Rajarajan 1000”** in memory of that great king.

The Rajarajan 1000 method was implemented over **4,688 acres in 2006–2007**. Because it was found to reduce water use and increase paddy productivity, it was extended to **10 lakh 52 thousand acres in 2007–2008**.

Because of drought in October 2007, floods in December 2007 and further flooding in March 2008, **5 lakh 95 thousand acres** of paddy area was affected. As a result, **₹282 crore 63 lakh** was paid as compensation under the National Crop Insurance Scheme and **₹213 crore 80 lakh** as relief under the National Calamity Relief Fund, totalling **₹496 crore 43 lakh**.

In **2008–2009**, the technology was followed over **13 lakh 45 thousand acres**. Flooding caused by Cyclone Nisha in November 2008 affected **11 lakh 78 thousand acres** of paddy area in **20 districts**. Consequently, **₹651 crore 66 lakh** was paid as compensation under the National Crop Insurance Scheme and **₹375 crore 48 lakh** as relief under the National Calamity Relief Fund, totalling **₹1,027 crore 14 lakh**.

Even so, because the Rajarajan 1000 technology was followed on **2 lakh 93 thousand acres more** than in the previous year, rice production did not decline in 2008–2009 and reached **51 lakh 83 thousand metric tonnes**, despite the greater area affected by natural calamity.

A study report by the team of the World Bank-funded Water Resources and Land Resources Project, the letter says, confirmed that the Rajarajan 1000 technology increased yield by **30 to 40 per cent**.

Under the Rajarajan 1000 scheme, **3 lakh 63 thousand cono weeders** and **2 lakh 43 thousand markers** have so far been provided to farmers and are in use.

**M.K.**

❖ தஞ்சையில் இராஜராஜனின் 1000வது பிறந்த நாள்
விழாவைக் கொண்டாடிய போது, அதிக மகசூல்
தரக்கூடிய புதிய நெல் தொழில் நுட்பத்திற்கு அந்த

மாமன்னன் நினைவாக “இராஜராஜன் 1000” என
என்னால் பெயர் சூட்டப்பட்டது.

❖ 2006-2007 ஆம் ஆண்டில் 4 ஆயிரத்து 688
ஏக்கரில் செயல்படுத்தப்பட்ட இராஜராஜன் 1000
தொழில்நுட்ப சாகுபடி முறை நீர்ப்பயன் பாட்டைக்
குறைத்து நெல் உற்பத்தித்திறனை அதிகரிக்கிறது என
கண்டறியப்பட்டதால் 2007-2008ஆம் ஆண்டில்
10 இலட்சத்து 52 ஆயிரம் ஏக்கர் பரப்பளவில்
இத்தொழில்நுட்பம் கடைப்பிடிக்கப்பட்டது.

2007ஆம் ஆண்டு அக்டோபர் மாதத்தில் நிலவிய
வறட்சி மற்றும் டிசம்பர் மாதத்தில் ஏற்பட்ட வெள்ளம்
காரணமாகவும், 2008ஆம் ஆண்டு மார்ச் மாதத்தில்
ஏற்பட்ட வெள்ளத்தாலும், 5 இலட்சத்து 95 ஆயிரம்
ஏக்கர் நெற்பரப்பு பாதிக்கப்பட்டது. இதன் காரணமாக
தேசிய பயிர்க் காப்பீட்டுத் திட்டத்தின்கீழ் 282 கோடியே
63 இலட்சம் ரூபாய் இழப்பீடாகவும், தேசிய பேரிடர்
நிவாரண நிதியின்கீழ் 213 கோடியே 80 இலட்சம் ரூபாய்
நிவாரணமாகவும், ஆக மொத்தம் 496 கோடியே 43
இலட்சம் ரூபாய் வழங்கப்பட்டது.

2008-2009 ஆம் ஆண்டில், 13 இலட்சத்து 45
ஆயிரம் ஏக்கர் பரப்பில் இத்தொழில் நுட்பம்
கடைப்பிடிக்கப்பட்டது. 2008ஆம் ஆண்டு நவம்பர்
மாதத்தில் ஏற்பட்ட நிஷா புயலினால், நிகழ்ந்த வெள்ள
பாதிப்பால் 20 மாவட்டங்களில் 11 இலட்சத்து 78
ஆயிரம் ஏக்கர் நெல் பரப்பு பாதிக்கப்பட்டது. இதன்
காரணமாக, தேசிய பயிர்க் காப்பீட்டுத் திட்டத்தின்
கீழ் 651 கோடியே 66 இலட்சம் ரூபாய் இழப்பீடாகவும்,
தேசிய பேரிடர் நிவாரண நிதியின்கீழ் 375 கோடியே
48 இலட்சம் ரூபாய் நிவாரணமாகவும் ஆக மொத்தம்
1027 கோடியே 14 இலட்சம் ரூபாய் வழங்கப்பட்டது.
இருப்பினும், கடந்த ஆண்டை விட 2008-2009ஆம்
ஆண்டில் கூடுதலாக 2 இலட்சத்து 93 ஆயிரம் ஏக்கரில்
இராஜராஜன் 1000 தொழில்நுட்பத்தைப் பின்பற்றிய
காரணத்தால் அரிசி உற்பத்தியில் பின்னடைவு
ஏதுமில்லாமல் 51 இலட்சத்து 83 ஆயிரம் மெட்ரிக் டன்

பெற முடிந்தது. இயற்கை சீற்றத்தினால் கூடுதலான
பரப்பு பாதிப்புக்கு உள்ளானபோதும் உற்பத்தி அதிகமாக
பெற முடிந்தது.

❖ இராஜராஜன் 1000 தொழில்நுட்பத்தினால் 30 முதல்
40 சதவீதம் மகசூல் அதிகரித்துள்ளதை உலக வங்கி
நிதியின்கீழ் செயல்படுத்தப்படும் நீர்வள, நிலவளத்
திட்டத்தின் குழு ஆய்வு அறிக்கை மூலம் உறுதி
செய்யப்பட்டுள்ளது.

❖ இராஜராஜன் 1000 திட்டத்தின்கீழ், இதுவரை 3
இலட்சத்து 63 ஆயிரம் கோனோகளையெடுக்கும்
கருவிகளும், 2 இலட்சத்து 43 ஆயிரம் மார்க்கர்களும்
விவசாயிகளுக்கு வழங்கப்பட்டு பயன்படுத்தப்பட்டு
வருகின்றன.
