# 3690. Recognise Those Staging a Deceptive Drama!

## English translation

Udanpirappē,

After attending the National Development Council meeting in Delhi, Chief Minister Jayalalithaa came out, announced that she had walked out, hurried back to Chennai and issued a long reply to my letter of the 27th, “The Water Karnataka Blocks and the Tears Tamil Nadu Sheds!” It would be more fitting to call her reply an abusive tirade than a statement. Whether in government or opposition, she seems unable to issue a statement with civility. That is her style. Still, I shall place what she wrote beside what I wrote and let the people of Tamil Nadu decide what is true.

At the start of her statement she suggests I falsely claimed that, when the Cauvery Tribunal's final award came, she had called it harmful to Tamil Nadu and that she now asks the Prime Minister to Gazette that same award. She presents my reference to the all-party meeting of 19 February 2007, after the award of 5 February, as though I had concealed something that happened in between. Let me state the truth.

The final award came on 5 February 2007. Without even taking time to understand its contents, Jayalalithaa issued a long statement on 6 February, published on the front page of the AIADMK's official Namadhu MGR on the 7th. It was filled with attacks on me. Let any number of defamation cases be filed; this is what her published statement said in substance: like a leaking ship with a thousand sailors, despite having power and ministers at both Centre and State, Karunanidhi had failed to secure through the Tribunal the full justice Tamil Nadu deserved in the Cauvery dispute.

What does that mean except that she considered the final award inadequate? Yet that is the award she now demands be Gazetted.

Her 7 February statement went further: she said that because of Karunanidhi's continuing betrayal the Tribunal's final award had come against Tamil Nadu's interests and that the “minority” DMK Government should accept moral responsibility and resign.

So the person who said in 2007 that the final award was harmful now writes as though she never said any such thing and as though I invented it. Does that not make clear what is true and what is false?

She also wrote then that I had welcomed the award and said I was happy with it, while in truth, she said, it was not an award to be welcomed. I still possess that issue of her party newspaper and am ready to show it anywhere. My present point was simply that the person who called the award unworthy of welcome now writes letter after letter asking the Prime Minister to publish it in the Gazette. Today she calls even that statement of mine false.

Although she had called the award unacceptable, the AIADMK participated in the all-party meetings convened by the DMK Government on 19 February and 15 April 2007. Today, with kuruvai destroyed and delta farmers fearing for samba, with Karnataka refusing water even after directions from the Supreme Court and Cauvery bodies and after repeatedly consulting its own all-party leaders, most parties in Tamil Nadu have asked for an all-party meeting here. Jayalalithaa ignores them and acts unilaterally. The people must decide whether she has any standing to condemn the DMK Government for having convened two all-party meetings and acted on their resolutions.

The award came on 5 February; within fifteen days, on 19 February, the DMK Government convened the all-party meeting and adopted a unanimous resolution. Yet Jayalalithaa's statement of 21 February 2007 criticised us even for taking fifteen days. What is the situation now?

I do not deny that after initially calling the award unworthy of welcome, she later repeatedly demanded through statements and letters to the Prime Minister that it be published in the Gazette.

She had also claimed that I said the final award gave “happiness” and that it provided less water than the interim award. In my reply of 8 February 2007 I pointed out the contradiction: I had said the award gave “consolation,” not “happiness.” And how could she say the final award reduced the quantity when the interim award was 205 TMC and the final award 210 TMC?

At the all-party meeting of 19 February 2007, AIADMK representatives themselves did not simply accept the final award. They said the Tribunal had gone so far as to recognise unlawfully created irrigated areas in Karnataka, that this was debatable and had to be opposed. Namadhu MGR of 20 February carried this and even headlined reports about “aspects harmful to Tamil Nadu in the Tribunal award.” It is that same award containing what they called harmful aspects that Jayalalithaa now presses to have Gazetted.

She then attacks the DMK MPs who met the Prime Minister on 17 December 2012 to seek immediate publication of the final award, asking in uncivil language whether they truly pressed the demand or merely staged a deceptive drama and secretly blocked publication. This does not merely malign DMK MPs; it casts a stain even on the Prime Minister's Office.

On that very 17 December I publicly appealed through all newspapers for publication of the award in the Central Gazette and announced that DMK MPs would immediately meet the Prime Minister. I contacted parliamentary party leader T.R. Baalu in Delhi and asked him to arrange the meeting. They met the Prime Minister that same day, made the request, and he assured them that he would look into it immediately. Newspapers of 18 December reported it. Yet Jayalalithaa, in her characteristic manner, makes a false accusation even about this.

Argument can be answered with argument. What answer can one give to the perverse argument of those staging a deceptive drama? The people of Tamil Nadu must understand.

With affection,
M.K.
31-12-2012

நடுவர் மன்றத்தின் இறுதித் தீர்ப்பு வெளிவந்தவுடன் அவசர

வரவேற்கத் தக்கது அல்ல என்று கூறிய போதிலும், அதன் பின்னர்
அந்த இறுதித் தீர்ப்பை அரசிதழில் வெளியிட வேண்டுமென்று

மேலும் ஜெயலலிதா அப்போது விடுத்த அறிக்கையில்,
நடுவர் மன்றத்தின் இறுதித் தீர்ப்பு மகிழ்ச்சி தருகிறது என்று நான்
சொன்னதாகவும், நடுவர் மன்றத்தின் இடைக்காலத் தீர்ப்பைவிட
இறுதித் தீர்ப்பில் தண்ணீரின் அளவு குறைவாகக் கிடைத்துள்ளது
என்றும் கூறியிருந்தார். இதற்கு அப்போதே 8-2-2007 அன்று
நான் அளித்த பதிலில், “காவேரி நடுவர் மன்ற இறுதித் தீர்ப்பு
குறித்து ஜெயலலிதா வெளியிட்டுள்ள அறிக்கை முன்னுக்குப்
பின் முரணாக இருக்கிறது. “ஆறுதல் தருகிறது” என்று நான்
சொன்னதை “மகிழ்ச்சி தருகிறது” என்பதாக அர்த்தப்படுத்திக்
கொண்டு அம்மையார் அறிக்கை விட்டது மாத்திரமல்லாமல்,
நடுவர் மன்றத்தின் இடைக்காலத் தீர்ப்பை விட தண்ணீர்
அளவு குறைவாக இறுதித் தீர்ப்பில் கிடைத்துள்ளது என்கிறார்.
ஜெயலலிதா. இடைக்காலத் தீர்ப்பு 205 டி.எம்.சி. இறுதித் தீர்ப்பு
210 டி.எம்.சி. இது எப்படி இடைக்காலத் தீர்ப்பை விட இறுதித்
தீர்ப்பிலே குறைந்து விட்டது என்று பொருள் ஆகும்?” என்று
தெளிவுபடுத்தியிருந்தேன்.

தலைவர்கள் கூட்டத்தில் அ.தி.மு.க. சார்பில் கலந்து
கொண்டவர்கள் அப்போது நடுவர் மன்றத்தின் இறுதித் தீர்ப்பை
ஏற்றுக் கொண்டார்களா என்றால், “நடுவர் மன்றம் அத்துமீறி
கர்நாடகத்தில் கட்டப்பட்ட ஆயக் கட்டுகளை அங்கீகரிக்கும்

உறுப்பினர்கள் பிரதமரைச் சந்தித்து நடுவர் மன்றத்தின்
