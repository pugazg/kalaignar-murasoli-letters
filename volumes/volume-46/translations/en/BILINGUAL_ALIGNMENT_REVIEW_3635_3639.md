### 3637 (second) — CORRECTION REQUIRED

The one-page proclamation is structurally faithful and correctly preserves the duplicate source number, actual page heading, lineation, repetition and handwritten-signature placeholder. One English phrase should be tightened for lexical fidelity:

- Source: `கிளம்பிற்று காண் தமிழச் சிங்கக் கூட்டம்`
- Current: `Behold! The pride of Tamil lions has set forth!`
- Required: **`Behold! The host of Tamil lions has set forth!`**

`கூட்டம்` is the gathered host/crowd. Although English `pride` can be a collective noun for lions, it introduces an additional semantic association with pride that is not present in the Tamil. `host` preserves the source's mass-mobilisation image more directly.

No other substantive correction is required in this record.
