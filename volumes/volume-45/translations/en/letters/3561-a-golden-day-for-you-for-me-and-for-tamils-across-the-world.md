# 3561. A Golden Day for You, for Me—and for Tamils Across the World!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This translation is intended to carry Kalaignar's voice into clear, contemporary English rather than recast the letter as literary or academic prose. It preserves the source's argument, political directness, rhetorical questions, repetition, irony, factual detail, and paragraph order. Names, dates, figures, quotations, and intentional English expressions are retained. Where Tamil idiom cannot be reproduced literally without sounding unnatural, the English follows its sense and rhetorical force without adding claims absent from the source. The original Tamil is reproduced in full below the translation and remains the authoritative text. `Udanpirappē` is retained in Tamil transliteration rather than flattened into “brother,” “sister,” or “comrade.” Literally evoking “one born alongside me,” Kalaignar uses it as a distinctive address of shared identity, equality, affection, and solidarity within the movement.

**Udanpirappē,**

At this moment, when my younger daughter Kanimozhi has been produced before the Delhi court, I am writing this letter to you.

Tamil—the language that rolled on the red tongues of poets and set out sometimes like a storm and sometimes like a flower-scented breeze; the sweet Tamil that echoed in the palaces of the three crowned kings, the Cheras, Cholas and Pandyas; the Tamil nurtured by the Sangams and made to sound like a conch; the Tamil that scattered seeds from Venkadam to Kumari and raised wondrous trees; the Tamil that crossed mountains and seas and moved through the world as an ancient, foremost language; the Tamil whose fragrance, once confined to mansions, was carried by Arignar Anna into every mud hut and spread rays of light like a lamp set upon a hill; the Tamil that excels other languages in **letter, word and meaning**—today, **6.5.2011**, enters with grandeur and **“royal honours”** into the Indian President's residence in Delhi, the **Rashtrapati Bhavan**.

Yes. The Tamil scholars selected to receive the Classical Tamil awards presented by the **Central Institute of Classical Tamil** in Chennai on behalf of the Union Government have been invited to Delhi.

Because the DMK repeatedly pressed the matter, the United Progressive Alliance government at the Centre, functioning under the guidance of **Sonia Gandhi** and the leadership of **Dr Manmohan Singh**, proclaimed Tamil a **Classical Language** and issued the notification on **12.10.2004**.

Until then, only scholars selected for awards in Sanskrit, Pali, Prakrit, Arabic and Persian had traditionally been invited to the President's residence and honoured by the President of India. Continuing that tradition, for the first time today, **6.5.2011 at 11.30 a.m.**, our Tamil scholars are being honoured at the Rashtrapati Bhavan. That news is flowing like sweet honey into the ears of Tamils spread across the world.

After our sustained efforts led the Union Government to proclaim Tamil a **Classical Language**, our request was accepted by my late dear friend, then Union Minister for Human Resource Development **Arjun Singh**. Through the Central Institute of Classical Tamil, he came forward to institute every year a **Tolkappiyar Award** carrying a special prize of **₹5 lakh**, a **Kural Peedam Award** carrying **₹5 lakh**, and **five Young Scholar Awards** carrying **₹1 lakh each**.

Accordingly, in my capacity as Chairman of the Central Institute of Classical Tamil, a committee consisting of **Dr Va.Se. Kuzhandaisamy, Dr Ma. Nannan and writer—and dear friend—Jayakanthan** was formed under my chairmanship to select the scholars deserving of these awards. Awards have been presented from **2005-2006** onward. At today's function in the President's residence, awards for **2005-2006, 2006-2007 and 2007-2008** are being presented.

The **2005-2006 Tolkappiyar Award** is being presented to **Professor Adigalasiriyar**, who is over one hundred years old; is from Villupuram district; began as a lecturer at Mailam Tamil College and later served as a professor at the University of Madras and Tamil University, Thanjavur; rendered distinguished service to Tamil; published more than **100 research articles** on Classical Tamil; wrote **65 books**; and received prizes and awards from various institutions.

Professor Adigalasiriyar travelled from Chennai to Delhi by **Air India** to receive the Tolkappiyar Award. This was the first flight of his life. At Chennai airport, he and the other Tamil scholars were seen off by Air India Deputy General Manager **R. Radhakrishnan** and Senior Manager **M.P. Menon**.

The **2006-2007 Kural Peedam Award** is being presented to **Professor George L. Hart** of the United States, who served for many years as Professor of Tamil at the University of California; studied Tamil and Sanskrit literatures systematically; studied in depth the classical European literatures of Latin and Greek; and studied Tamil and Malayalam literature extensively in the original languages. It has been announced that because of ill health he was unable to participate in today's function at the President's residence.

The **2005-2006 Young Scholar Awards** are being presented to **Professor R. Aravindan** of Villupuram district, **Dr Y. Mani Kandan** of Thanjavur district, **Dr S. Kalaimagal** of Thanjavur district, **Dr Va.Mu.Se. Muthuramalinga Andavar** of Ramanathapuram district, and **Dr K. Palanivelu** of Puducherry.

The **2006-2007 Young Scholar Awards** are being presented to **Dr S. Chandra** of Madurai district, **Dr Aranga. Pari** of Ariyalur district, **Dr Mu. Ilangovan** of Ariyalur district, **Dr M. Bhavani** of Tiruvarur district, and **Dr R. Kalaivani** of Nagai district.

The **2007-2008 Young Scholar Awards** are being presented to **Dr A. Selvarasu** of Tiruchirappalli district, **Dr P. Velmurugan** of Theni district, **Dr A. Manavazhagan** of Salem district, **Dr S. Chandrasekaran** of Sivaganga district, and **Dr S. Simon John** of Tirunelveli district.

The Tamil world has long awaited with great eagerness the function at which these **Classical Language Awards** would be presented. On this day, many events flash one after another through my memory.

The first person to demand classical-language status for Tamil was **Surya Narayana Sastri, known as Parithimar Kalaignar**. More than a hundred years ago, in his book **Tamil Mozhiyin Varalaru**, he declared firmly:

> “Tamil is certainly a classical language. From ancient times itself, good poets have spoken of Tamil as **Senthamizh**. Therefore, whichever way one examines the Tamil language—sweeter than nectar and shining with distinction in the South—it is certainly a high, independent classical language.”

The house in **Vilacheri, Madurai**, where Parithimar Kalaignar lived was taken into government ownership under the DMK government, renovated at a cost of **₹8 lakh**, and a bust of him was installed at the front. I opened it on **31.10.2007**. In the visitors' book there I wrote and signed: **“Long live the fame of Parithimar Kalaignar, the first Tamil to raise his voice demanding classical-language status for Tamil!”**

If Parithimar Kalaignar was the first Tamil to say that Tamil was a classical language, the first foreign scholar to say so was **Robert Caldwell**. In his great work on comparative Dravidian grammar, Caldwell established:

> “Tamil, which stands as a high and independent classical language among all the Dravidian languages, can live and flourish even after entirely removing the Sanskrit words that have found a place within it, without needing their assistance even to the slightest extent.”

The house in **Idaiyangudi, Tirunelveli district**, where the distinguished Robert Caldwell lived in Tamil Nadu was taken into government ownership at a cost of **₹20 lakh**, renovated, and his statue installed there. On **17.2.2011**, from Chennai, I opened the memorial house and statue through **video conferencing**.

At that function I said that it was my duty at that pleasant moment to remember the rare service rendered by Christian scholars to the growth and distinction of Tamil. I cited the words of Professor **R.P. Sethupillai**:

> “The work done by Christians for Tamil is of many kinds. Some showed the artistic riches of Tamil Nadu to Westerners. Some explained the qualities of Tamil literature through verse and prose. Some, through grammatical research, clarified the antiquity and refinement of Tamil. Some compiled Tamil dictionaries on Western lines. Some rendered service by writing works of knowledge in lucid Tamil prose.”

The Central Institute of Classical Tamil, which had been functioning in Mysore, was shifted to Chennai after the DMK government repeatedly pressed for it. In recognition of the goodwill of former Union Human Resource Development Minister **Arjun Singh**, and his affection for us and our language, the Tamil Nadu government held a felicitation for him in Chennai on **18.8.2007**, under my chairmanship, for shifting the Institute to Chennai.

The office of the Central Institute of Classical Tamil was inaugurated on **30.6.2008** at the Public Works Department's **Palaru Illam** on Kamarajar Salai, Chennai, at a function presided over by Finance Minister Perasiriyar. While opening the office I said:

> “The day the Central Institute of Classical Tamil takes root in Tamil Nadu is the day Parithimar Kalaignar's dream of 150 years ago—and the thought I held fifty years ago that the dream must come true—takes practical form today. On this occasion, from my own personal money, I am giving **₹1 crore** to the custody of this Institute to establish a **‘Kalaignar Karunanidhi Classical Language Trust.’** With the interest from this ₹1 crore, awards and gold purses will be presented on behalf of the Trust to those who research inscriptions of value to Tamil Nadu's history.”

Accordingly, the first **Kalaignar M. Karunanidhi Classical Tamil Award**, for **2009**, was presented to Finnish Professor **Asko Parpola** at the World Classical Tamil Conference in Coimbatore, together with a **₹10 lakh gold purse, citation and memento**, by the President of India.

Professor **George L. Hart**, selected for the 2006-2007 Kural Peedam Award, has immeasurable affection and deep friendship toward me. Whenever I hear his name, I cannot forget what he wrote in the foreword to **Kalaignar Kalanjiyam**, a volume containing my literary works, published on the tenth anniversary of the establishment of the **Tamil Chair** at the University of California:

> “Kalaignar has given literary works in many forms—novels, essays, screenplays, short stories, historical novels and the views of scholars. Kalaignar has made Tamils understand their ancient greatness in an excellent manner. Kalaignar has deep knowledge of both literature and politics. Throughout his life, he has worked for the advancement of the Tamil language and the lives of Tamils.”

After the DMK government decided to shift the Central Institute of Classical Tamil's **Pavendhar Classical Tamil Research Library** into the hall at **Fort St George, Chennai**, where the Legislative Assembly had earlier met, the library has functioned very well from **2.6.2010**, with many thousands of books useful for research.

So many more memories come one after another today and fill my heart. All of them circle around the Tamil scholars gathered in the President's residence. From here itself, I wholeheartedly congratulate them all and rejoice!

Tamils across the globe are celebrating the first occasion on which Tamil scholars enter the President's residence and receive these awards. A long-held dream is being fulfilled!

Udanpirappē, is this day not a **golden day** for you, for me, and for Tamils throughout the world?

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**7-5-2011**

கருணாநிதி செம்மொழி அறக்கட்டளை’ ஒன்றினை நிறுவிட
வழங்குகின்றேன். இந்த ஒரு கோடி ரூபாயிலிருந்து கிடைக்கும்
வட்டித் தொகையைக் கொண்டு, தமிழக வரலாற்றுப் பயன்மிக்க
கல்வெட்டுகளை ஆய்வு செய்பவர்களுக்கு அறக்கட்டளையின்
சார்பில் விருதுகளும், பொற்கிழிகளும் வழங்கப்படும்” என்று நான்
அறிவித்து; அதன்படி, 2009ஆம் ஆண்டுக்கான முதல் விருது -
“கலைஞர் மு.கருணாநிதி செம்மொழித் தமிழ் விருது” - பின்லாந்து

ஆகியவை, மேதகு இந்திய குடியரசுத் தலைவர் அவர்களால்
வழங்கிச் சிறப்பிக்கப்பட்டன.

2006-2007ஆம் ஆண்டுக்கான ‘குறள்பீடம் விருது’க்குத்
தேர்ந்தெடுக்கப்பட்டுள்ள அமெரிக்க நாட்டுப் பேராசிரியர்
முனைவர் ஜார்ஜ் எல்.ஹார்ட் அவர்கள் என்மீது அளவிடற்கரிய
அன்பும், ஆழமான நட்பும் பாராட்டுபவர். அவரது பெயரைக்
கேட்கும்போதெல்லாம்; கலிபோர்னியா பல்கலைக்கழகத்தில்
‘தமிழ் இருக்கை’ உருவாக்கப்பட்டதன் 10ஆவது ஆண்டு விழாவின்போது வெளியிடப்பட்ட; எனது இலக்கியப் படைப்புகள்
அடங்கிய ‘கலைஞர் களஞ்சியம்’ என்னும் நூலுக்கு அவர் வழங்கிய
முன்னுரையில், “கலைஞர் புதினம், கட்டுரைகள், திரைக்கதைகள்,
சிறுகதைகள், வரலாற்றுப் புதினங்கள், அறிஞர்கள் கூறும்
கருத்துகள் எனப் பலவிதங்களில் இலக்கியப் படைப்புகளை
அளித்துள்ளார். தமிழர்களிடம் அவர்களின் பழம் பெருமையைச்
சிறந்த முறையில் உணர்த்தியிருப்பவர் கலைஞர். கலைஞர்
இலக்கியத்திலும், அரசியலிலும் ஆழ்ந்த அறிவுடையவர். அவர்
வாழ்நாள் முழுவதும் தமிழ்மொழி, தமிழர் வாழ்வின் உயர்வுக்காகப்
பாடுபட்டுள்ளார்” என்று குறிப்பிட்டுள்ளதை என்னால் மறந்திட
இயலாது.
