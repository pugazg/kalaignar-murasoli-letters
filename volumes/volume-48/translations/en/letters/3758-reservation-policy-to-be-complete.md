# 3758. For the Reservation Policy to Become Complete...

>

It preserves the source's argument, political directness, rhetorical questions, repetition, irony, factual detail, and paragraph order. Names, dates, figures, quotations, and intentional English expressions are retained. **Udanpirappē,**

As a result of the DMK's persistent insistence, from the time of the National Front government in which the DMK participated and whose Prime Minister was the guardian of social justice, V.P. Singh, the Union Government has provided **27 per cent reservation for Other Backward Classes**. To exclude those among them considered advanced—the **creamy layer**—reservation is presently available only to those with annual income not exceeding ₹4.5 lakh.

The National Commission for Backward Classes recommended that this ceiling be raised to ₹12 lakh in urban areas and ₹9 lakh in rural areas. A Group of Ministers, however, recommended that such levels were not feasible and that a uniform limit of ₹6 lakh could be adopted. It is in this context that reports have appeared that a Union Cabinet meeting chaired by Prime Minister Dr Manmohan Singh decided to raise the income ceiling for OBC reservation from ₹4.5 lakh to ₹6 lakh.

When the DMK was in government, at the meeting of the DMK High-Level Action Committee held on 18 April 2008 to discuss **“Our Journey in Social Justice”**, there was a long discussion, after which I moved a resolution and it was adopted. The resolution was as follows:

> “The DMK High-Level Action Committee wholeheartedly welcomes and appreciates the United Progressive Alliance Government at the Centre, led by Dr Manmohan Singh and guided by Mrs Sonia Gandhi, for fulfilling the demand we had continuously pressed: amending the Constitution of India through the 93rd Amendment and, on that basis, enacting legislation providing 27 per cent reservation for Backward Classes in institutions of higher education run by the Union Government; and it also welcomes the historic judgment of the Constitution Bench of the Supreme Court of India upholding the **validity** of that law.
>
> “The Supreme Court judgment's statement that the economically advanced sections among the Backward Classes must be excluded from the 27 per cent reservation—**exclusion of creamy layer**—has become a matter of concern.
>
> “From the very beginning, the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam has never accepted either imposing an economic criterion within reservation or excluding economically advanced sections among the Backward Classes from reservation.
>
> “Because of the great agitation conducted in Tamil Nadu by Thanthai Periyar and Perarignar Anna, and because of the **pressure** exerted by Perunthalaivar Kamarajar, Pandit Jawaharlal Nehru came forward and, through the First Amendment to the Constitution of India enacted in 1951, inserted the new Article 15(4) using the expression **‘socially and educationally’**. Although some insisted that the word **‘economically’** should also be included, neither Pandit Nehru nor the then Law Minister Dr Ambedkar accepted it. Pandit Nehru explained: **‘socially’ is a much wider word including many things**.
>
> “From that day to this, the expression ‘socially and educationally’ has remained permanently in the Constitution without alteration. To use an expression—‘economically’—that does not occur in this constitutional formulation and say that economically advanced sections among Backward Classes should be excluded from reservation would dilute reservation for the Backward Classes.
>
> “Reservation is not an instrument for removing poverty. It is a constitutional right given to classes that were denied proper opportunities for centuries and are socially and educationally backward.
>
> “A family's annual income plainly varies from year to year for many reasons. Thus, someone certified in one year as ineligible for reservation because income exceeded the ceiling may in another year become eligible because income falls within the limit. Likewise, within one family, one person may qualify for a certificate in one year and another may fail to qualify in another year. This can create difference, and at times resentment and hostility, among children of the same mother. If Backward Classes are divided in this way, they may not even receive the full quantum of reservation available to them.
>
> “Therefore, the provision excluding economically advanced sections among Backward Classes from reservation should be removed, and all legal measures should be taken—without contradicting the principles explained by Pandit Nehru and Annal Ambedkar—to enable all Backward Classes, without discrimination, to enjoy the benefits of reservation.
>
> “Meanwhile, the DMK High-Level Action Committee urges the Union Government to implement fully and immediately, from the coming academic year itself, the 27 per cent reservation that Backward Classes have secured in Union Government institutions of higher education after a long period.”

We adopted this important resolution and sent it to the Union Government at that time itself.

The Supreme Court judgment itself stated:

> “Government can make a relaxation to some extent so that sufficient number of candidates may be available for the purpose of filling up the 27 per cent reservation. It is for the Union Government and the State Governments to issue appropriate guidelines to identify the ‘creamy layer’ so that SEBC are properly determined in accordance with the guidelines given by this Court. If, even by applying this principle, still the candidates are not available, the State can issue appropriate guidelines to effectuate the implementation of the reservation purposefully.”

That is, the government may relax the criteria for the creamy layer to the extent necessary to ensure that enough candidates are available to fill the entire 27 per cent reservation. The Union and State Governments may frame suitable guidelines, consistent with the Court's directions, for identifying the creamy layer and properly determining the socially and educationally backward classes. If even after applying that principle sufficient candidates are still not available, the State may issue suitable guidelines so that the purpose of reservation is effectively fulfilled.

Therefore, we cannot simply ignore that the judgment itself leaves room to revise the creamy-layer criteria so that, after economically weaker candidates have received the benefit, the remaining reserved seats may be filled by economically advanced persons within the Backward Classes.

When the Union Government earlier raised the income ceiling from ₹2 lakh to ₹4.5 lakh, it did so on the recommendation of the National Commission for Backward Classes. Because increasing the ceiling would enable more Backward Class people to gain access to Union Government education and employment, I welcomed the decision at the time and wrote to the Prime Minister. Later, I also wrote asking that the ₹4.5-lakh ceiling be increased to ₹9 lakh.

The National Commission for Backward Classes has now recommended raising the ceiling to ₹9 lakh for rural people and ₹12 lakh for urban people. The Union Cabinet has not accepted that recommendation in full, and has instead decided to raise the ceiling from ₹4.5 lakh to ₹6 lakh. Though this does not give us complete satisfaction, as one who has argued for this for a long time, it gives me some satisfaction.

While thanking the Union Government on behalf of the DMK for taking this decision, I once again urge it—just as it fully accepted the National Commission's recommendation on the previous occasion—to accept the present recommendation too and raise the income ceiling to ₹9 lakh for rural people and ₹12 lakh for urban people.

More fundamentally, the DMK's basic demand is that the very discrimination called the **“creamy layer”** should be removed entirely from reservation.

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**19 May 2013**

என்று சான்றளிக்கப்பட்ட ஒருவர், வேறு ஒரு ஆண்டில் வருமான
வரம்பிற்கு உட்பட்ட வருமானத்தின் காரணமாகச் சான்றிதழ்
பெறுவதற்கு வாய்ப்புண்டு. அதைப்போலவே, ஒரே குடும்பத்தில்
ஒருவர் ஒரு குறிப்பிட்ட ஆண்டில் சான்றிதழ் பெறுவதற்குத் தகுதி
பெற்றவராகவும், மற்றொருவர் மற்றொரு ஆண்டில் சான்றிதழ்
பெறுவதற்குத் தகுதியில்லாதவராகவும் ஆகிவிடுவதற்கும்
வாய்ப்புண்டு. இது ஒரு தாயின் வயிற்றுப் பிள்ளைகளுள்
வேறுபாட்டையும், சில நேரங்களில் வெறுப்பையும்,
பகைமையையும் உருவாக்குவதற்கு வழி வகுத்தது போல்
ஆகிவிடும். பிற்படுத்தப்பட்டோரைப் பேதப்படுத்தினால் முழு
இடஒதுக்கீட்டு அளவும் அவர்களுக்குக் கிட்டாமல் போய்விடக்
கூடும்.

எனவே, பிற்படுத்தப்பட்டோருள் பொருளாதார ரீதியாக
முன்னேறிய பிரிவினரை இடஒதுக்கீட்டிலிருந்து விலக்கி
வைத்திட வேண்டும் என்ற அம்சத்தை நீக்கி, பிற்படுத்தப்பட்டோர்
அனைவரும் பாகுபாடின்றி இடஒதுக்கீட்டின் பலன்களை
அனுபவிப்பதற்கு ஏதுவாக அனைத்து முயற்சிகளையும் பண்டித
நேருவும், அண்ணல் அம்பேத்கரும் எடுத்துரைத்த கருத்துக்கு
முரண் இல்லாமல், சட்டரீதியாக மேற்கொள்ள வேண்டுமென்றும்;
இதற்கிடையே -

அரிய வாய்ப்பாக மத்திய அரசின் உயர்கல்வி நிலையங்களில்
நீண்டகாலத்திற்குப் பிறகு, பிற்படுத்தப்பட்டோருக்குக்
கிடைத்துள்ள 27 சதவீத இடஒதுக்கீட்டினை முழுமையாகவும்,
உடனடியாகவும் வரும் கல்வியாண்டு முதலே
நடைமுறைப்படுத்திட வேண்டுமென்றும்; மத்திய அரசை
கழகத்தின் உயர்நிலை செயல்திட்டக் குழு வலியுறுத்திக் கேட்டுக்
கொள்கிறது.”

இந்த முக்கியமான தீர்மானத்தை நிறைவேற்றி மத்திய
அரசுக்கும் அப்போதே அனுப்பிவைத்தோம்.

உச்ச நீதிமன்றம் அளித்த தீர்ப்பிலேயே “Government can make
a relaxation to some extent so that sufficient number of candidates may
be available for the purpose of filling up the 27 per cent reservation.
It is for the Union Government and the State Governments to issue
appropriate guidelines to identify the ‘creamy layer’ so that SEBC are
properly determined in accordance with the guidelines given by this
Court. If, even by applying this principle, still the candidates are not
available, the State can issue appropriate guidelines to effectuate the
implementation of the reservation purposefully. - அதாவது;

படியாக நிறைவேற்றுவதற்குத் தேவையான அளவுக்கு
மாணவர்கள் கிடைத்திடத் தக்கவகையில், “கிரீமிலேயருக்கான”
அளவுகோல்களை ஓரளவுக்கு அரசு தளர்த்திக் கொள்ளலாம்.

அரசும், மாநில அரசுகளும் - “கிரீமிலேயரை” அடையாளப்படுத்துவதற்கு உரிய வழிகாட்டு நெறிமுறைகளை வழங்குவதைப்பற்றி

வழங்கலாம்” என்று குறிப்பிடப்பட்டுள்ளதால்; பொருளாதாரத்தில்

உள்ளோர் பெற்றிடத்தக்க வகையில் கிரீமிலேயருக்கான
அளவுகோல்களைத் திருத்திக்கொள்ள தீர்ப்பிலேயே இடம்
