# 3548. Five-Year Achievements of the Social Welfare and Nutritious Meal Programme Department!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This translation is intended to carry Kalaignar's voice into clear, contemporary English rather than recast the letter as literary or academic prose. It preserves the source's argument, political directness, rhetorical questions, repetition, irony, factual detail, and paragraph order. Names, dates, figures, quotations, and intentional English expressions are retained. Where Tamil idiom cannot be reproduced literally without sounding unnatural, the English follows its sense and rhetorical force without adding claims absent from the source. The original Tamil is reproduced in full below the translation and remains the authoritative text. `Udanpirappē` is retained in Tamil transliteration rather than flattened into “brother,” “sister,” or “comrade.” Literally evoking “one born alongside me,” Kalaignar uses it as a distinctive address of shared identity, equality, affection, and solidarity within the movement.

**Udanpirappē,**

One of the principal doctrines of the Dravidian movement is the advancement of women. On the basis of the resolutions formulated by Thanthai Periyar on women's advancement at the Chengalpattu Self-Respect Conference held in 1929, the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam government formed in Tamil Nadu in 1967 under Perarignar Anna, and then the DMK government formed in 1969 under my leadership, enacted many laws and implemented many schemes for women's advancement. They created opportunities for women to gain freedom in political, social and economic life, to progress step by step in their lives, to obtain degrees and positions, and to rise to higher standing in society.

I want to remind you of the many schemes through which women have advanced in different ways: women were appointed to the Tamil Nadu Police for the first time in 1973; the Women's Development Project, which created self-help groups that today have opened the path to economic freedom in the lives of lakhs of poor and ordinary women, was launched in Dharmapuri in 1989; a free degree-education scheme for poor women was introduced in the name of E. V. R. Nagammaiyar; the Dr. Muthulakshmi Reddy Memorial Maternity Financial Assistance Scheme now provides ₹6,000 to poor pregnant women so that they can obtain nutrition; 30 per cent reservation for women in government employment was introduced in 1990; a separate law granted property rights to women; 33 per cent reservation for women in local bodies was introduced in 1996; grant assistance was provided to first-generation women entrepreneurs; and women entrepreneurs were given priority in the allocation of industrial plots in industrial estates.

When Mrs. Pratibha Patil was announced as a candidate for the office of President of India, and when she was accordingly elected as the first woman President, we welcomed, honoured and praised her with that same feeling. As evidence of the priority the DMK government has given women, you know—and the country knows well—that two distinguished women today serve as the Chief Secretary of the Tamil Nadu Government and as the Director General of Police. In this situation, I continue this letter to remind you of the encouragement and assistance this government has continually provided for women's advancement.

### Marriage assistance schemes

With the objective of encouraging all women to obtain an education, the Moovalur Ramamirtham Ammaiyar Memorial Marriage Assistance Scheme, providing ₹5,000 for the marriage of poor women who had studied up to Class 8, was introduced under the DMK government in 1989. Later, to emphasise that every woman should study at least through Class 10, the assistance under this scheme was raised to ₹10,000 in 1996.

However, the AIADMK government that assumed office in 2001 suspended this scheme and prevented poor and ordinary women from benefiting. In that situation, this government, after assuming office in 2006, restored the Moovalur Ramamirtham Ammaiyar Memorial Marriage Assistance Scheme and raised the assistance from ₹10,000 to ₹15,000. It was then raised to ₹20,000 on 20 March 2008 and further to ₹25,000 from 1 April 2010.

The DMK election manifesto that has now been released has also announced that, if the new government is formed, this ₹25,000 will be increased to ₹30,000.

Since 2006, ₹880 crore 55 lakh 5 thousand has been provided as financial assistance for the marriages of 4,64,756 poor women, benefiting poor families over the past five years. Assistance under the other marriage schemes too has been raised to ₹25,000 from 1 April 2010.

### Anjugam Ammaiyar Memorial Inter-Caste Marriage Assistance Scheme

Under the Anjugam Ammaiyar Memorial Inter-Caste Marriage Assistance Scheme, the assistance was raised to ₹20,000 when one member of the marrying couple was an Adi Dravidar or belonged to a Scheduled Tribe and the other belonged to another community; and to ₹15,000 when one belonged to a Backward or Most Backward community and the other to a Forward community. From 1 April 2010, assistance under both categories was further raised to ₹25,000.

During the past five years, 11,383 couples have received ₹20 crore 89 lakh 60 thousand in assistance under this scheme.

### Dr. Dharmambal Ammaiyar Memorial Widow Remarriage Assistance Scheme

Under this scheme, during the past five years, ₹1 crore 12 lakh 71 thousand has been provided to enable 910 poor widowed women to remarry.

### E. V. R. Maniammaiyar Memorial Marriage Assistance Scheme for Daughters of Widowed Mothers

Under the E. V. R. Maniammaiyar Memorial Marriage Assistance Scheme for Daughters of Widowed Mothers, ₹30 crore 32 lakh 93 thousand has been provided as marriage assistance for the marriages of 16,365 women during five years.

### Mother Teresa Memorial Marriage Assistance Scheme for Destitute Women

Under the Mother Teresa Memorial Marriage Assistance Scheme for Destitute Women, ₹4 crore 58 thousand has been provided during five years for the marriages of 2,220 women.

Thus, under the various marriage-assistance schemes, a total of ₹937 crore 50 lakh 87 thousand—nearly ₹1,000 crore—has been provided over five years for the marriages of 4,95,634 poor women.

### Tamil Nadu Aravanis Welfare Board

Because transgender people—described in this source passage as `அரவாணிகள்` and `திருநங்கைகள்`—are also part of this society, the **Tamil Nadu Aravanis Welfare Board** was established under the DMK government on 15 April 2008 to ensure their welfare. This government has been providing various welfare-scheme benefits to improve their standard of living.

Through the Board, 3,878 Aravanis have so far been enumerated; identity cards have been issued to 2,328, family cards to 1,238, group houses to 133, sewing machines to 100, house-site pattas to 482, and medical cards under the Kalaignar Insurance Scheme to 585.

In 2010–11, ₹1 crore was allocated to the Board to provide welfare-scheme assistance to Aravanis.

### Government Children's Homes

₹4.33 crore was allocated to construct buildings for Government Children's Homes in Tirunelveli, Dharmapuri, Erode, the Nilgiris, Thoothukudi and Salem districts, and the buildings have been constructed.

During the past five years, ₹5 crore 23 lakh 64 thousand has been spent on food for 4,072 children benefiting from Government Children's Homes.

### Government Service Homes

This government runs Service Homes so that widows, women deserted by their husbands, and destitute poor women in society can improve their educational level, receive vocational education and training in tailoring, computers, shorthand and typing, and also obtain safe accommodation.

For the benefit of 670 residents, including women and children in all the Service Homes, ₹31.6 lakh has been allocated and computer training and infrastructure facilities have been provided.

So far, ₹1 crore 7 lakh 42 thousand has been spent on food for the 670 beneficiaries of Government Service Homes.

The monthly food expenditure for residents of Government Children's Homes and Service Homes has been increased from ₹225 to ₹450 for children and from ₹300 to ₹500 for adults.

### Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act

Under the Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, District Social Welfare Officers were appointed as temporary Protection Officers for women's protection.

Thirty-one Protection Officers and 31 Junior Assistants have been appointed as full-time officers in the 31 districts. Service providers have also been appointed in the 31 districts.

₹2 crore 61 lakh 32 thousand has been paid as salaries to Protection Officers and typists.

Ninety-eight short-stay protection homes have been notified to provide accommodation for affected women and children.

### Social-security schemes

The Tamil Nadu Government is implementing the scheme called the **Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension Scheme**. Persons living below the poverty line, aged between 18 and 64, with severe disabilities or multiple disabilities involving 80 per cent bodily impairment are eligible under this scheme. It has been implemented in our State from 31 March 2010. Beneficiaries receive ₹500 a month, of which ₹200 is the Union Government's share and ₹300 the State Government's share.

The State Government extended the Destitute Persons with Disabilities Pension Scheme to people unable to speak from 19 March 2010, and it is being implemented accordingly.

Alongside the Destitute Widows Pension Scheme that has been implemented since 1 June 1975, the Tamil Nadu Government has implemented from 31 March 2010 the **Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme** for widows aged between 40 and 64 who live below the poverty line. Beneficiaries receive ₹500 a month, of which ₹200 is the Union Government's share and ₹300 the State Government's share.

From 1 July 2008, the Tamil Nadu Government introduced a new pension scheme for poor women aged 50 and above who are unmarried and living in poverty. Under this scheme, the ₹400 monthly pension that had been paid to 14,270 poor women has now been raised by the State Government to ₹500.

Because the number of destitute elderly people living below the poverty line and eligible for old-age pension has been increasing, this government took steps to bring under the scheme people who were eligible but had been unable to receive the pension. As a result, in the 2010–11 financial year up to September, 9,78,677 people have benefited.

Under the Destitute Old Age Pension Scheme that the State Government had been implementing, this government raised the pension paid from State funds from ₹200 to ₹400 from 1 September 2006, and further raised it to ₹500 a month from 1 December 2010.

Previously, a destitute elderly person below the poverty line could not receive old-age pension if there was a male heir. To change that position, appropriate orders have been issued to all District Collectors that destitute elderly people below the poverty line may receive the Destitute Old Age Pension even if they have male heirs.

On 4 April 2008, the government ordered that destitute Aravanis who had reached the age of 65 should also be brought under the Destitute Old Age Pension Scheme and paid ₹400 a month as pension.

That pension is now being paid at ₹500 a month.

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**21-3-2011**

1-7-2008 முதல் 50 வயது மற்றும் அதற்கு
மேலாகியும் திருமணம் ஆகாத, வறுமையில் வாடும் ஏழைப்
பெண்களுக்குத் தமிழக அரசு புதிய ஓய்வூதியத் திட்டத்தை
அறிமுகப்படுத்தியுள்ளது. இத்திட்டத்தின்கீழ் 14 ஆயிரத்து
270 ஏழைப் பெண்களுக்கு மாதந்தோறும் வழங்கப்பட்ட 400
ரூபாய் தற்போது 500 ரூபாய் என மாநில அரசினால் உயர்த்தி
வழங்கப்படுகிறது.

வறுமைக் கோட்டுக்குக் கீழ் வாழும், முதியோர் ஓய்வூதியம்
பெறத் தகுதியான ஆதரவற்ற முதியோர்கள் எண்ணிக்கை
அதிகமாகி வருவதால், தகுதியிருந்தும் ஓய்வூதியம் பெற
இயலாதவர்களை இத்திட்டத்தின்கீழ் கொணர இவ்வரசு எடுத்த
முயற்சியின் காரணமாக, தற்போது 2010-2011ம் நிதி ஆண்டில்
செப்டம்பர் மாதம் வரை, 9 இலட்சத்து 78 ஆயிரத்து 677 பேர்
பயனடைந்துள்ளனர்.

இதுகாறும், மாநில அரசு நடைமுறைப்படுத்தி வந்த
ஆதரவற்ற முதியோர் ஓய்வூதியத்திட்டத்தின் கீழ் பயனாளிகளுக்கு
மாநில நிதியிலிருந்து வழங்கி வந்த ஓய்வூதியமான 200 ரூபாயை
1.9.2006 முதல் 400 ரூபாயாக உயர்த்தி வழங்கிய இந்த அரசு,
1.12.2010 முதல் இந்த ஓய்வூதியத்தை மாதம் 500 ரூபாய் என
மேலும் உயர்த்தி வழங்கி வருகிறது.

ஆதரவற்ற, வறுமைக் கோட்டுக்குக்கீழ் உள்ள, வயது
முதிர்ந்தோருக்கு ஆண் வாரிசு எவரேனும் இருப்பின் அவருக்கு
முதியோர் ஓய்வூதியம் கிட்டாத நிலை இருந்தது. அதை
மாற்றிடும் வகையில் ஆதரவற்ற, வறுமைக் கோட்டுக்குக்
கீழே உள்ள, ஆதரவற்ற முதியோர்களுக்கு ஆண் வாரிசுகள்
எவரேனும் இருந்தாலும் அம்முதியோர்களுக்கு ஆதரவற்ற
முதியோர் ஓய்வூதியம் வழங்கலாம் என்று அனைத்து மாவட்ட
ஆட்சியர்களுக்கும் உரிய ஆணை வெளியிடப்பட்டுள்ளது.

65 வயதினை எட்டிய, ஆதரவற்ற அரவாணிகளையும்
ஆதரவற்ற முதியோர் ஓய்வூதியத்திட்டத்தின்கீழ் சேர்த்து,
அவர்களுக்கு மாதமொன்றிற்கு 400 ரூபாயை ஓய்வூதியமாக
வழங்கிடலாம் என்று 4-4-2008இல் அரசு ஆணையிட்டது.

தற்போது இந்த ஓய்வூதியம் மாதம் 500 ரூபாய் என
வழங்கப்படுகிறது.
