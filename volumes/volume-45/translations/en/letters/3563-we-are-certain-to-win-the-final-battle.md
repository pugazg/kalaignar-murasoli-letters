# 3563. We Are Certain to Win the Final Battle!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This translation is intended to carry Kalaignar's voice into clear, contemporary English rather than recast the letter as literary or academic prose. It preserves the source's argument, political directness, rhetorical questions, repetition, irony, factual detail, and paragraph order. Names, dates, figures, quotations, and intentional English expressions are retained. Where Tamil idiom cannot be reproduced literally without sounding unnatural, the English follows its sense and rhetorical force without adding claims absent from the source. The original Tamil is reproduced in full below the translation and remains the authoritative text. `Udanpirappē` is retained in Tamil transliteration rather than flattened into “brother,” “sister,” or “comrade.” Literally evoking “one born alongside me,” Kalaignar uses it as a distinctive address of shared identity, equality, affection, and solidarity within the movement.

**Udanpirappē,**

Whenever the occasion arose, I have told you many times—and through you, told the people of the town—about myself, my family life and my public life. What I am about to say now is not another such **“self-chronicle.”** I have already written five volumes of my self-chronicle under the title **Nenjukku Neethi**!

You may regard what I am about to write now as the preface to the next volume; or you may regard it as a **cry of the mind** that has arisen in between. In the present situation, I must remind you of these things.

This is a period in which our DMK, which I regard as dearer than life itself, has suffered a major defeat and lost power. What caused that loss? Was it the number of seats given to alliance parties? Or the constituencies they insisted on taking? Was it the Election Commission—the Brahma-rakshasa-like monster that devised a dictatorial “song” for Tamil Nadu alone, using its own `ஜபர்தஸ்து` measures and anti-democratic actions as whips? I do not wish to enter into all those questions.

But those who, during the election, spread propaganda that **“Karunanidhi's family is a family that has increased its influence of power, earned a thousand crore, ten thousand crore, a lakh crore, and tied it all up in sacks”**—in India or in Tamil Nadu—continue that propaganda even now through newspapers and other media, like a drizzle that continues even after the rain has stopped.

To make my Tamil people understand that these are false statements, fabricated statements and deceptive clouds of dust, I wish to collect and state a few facts.

As I have said many times, I was not born into a wealthy, affluent family! In **Thirukkuvalai**, a prosperous village in the then Thanjavur district, now Nagai district, I was born as the third child of **Muthuvel Nadaswaram musician**, who lived in a tiled house and worked as a farmer as well as being one of the masters of music, using the crops, paddy and rice produced in the fields surrounding us.

I rolled, crawled, toddled, later studied at the school in Tiruvarur, and with the knowledge I gained there, even in youth accepted Anna and Periyar respectively as guides to the political movement and the movement of reason. With the still-burning spirit of Self-Respect, I began public life.

At the age of fourteen, after reading about the **Panagal Raja**, I entered the language struggle with the cry **“Compulsory Hindi cannot be studied”**, faced one arena after another, became Chief Minister five times and won election to the Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly twelve times. Even after golden jubilees and platinum jubilees, and even after receiving titles such as king of literature and king of art, I never wished to make myself the owner of agricultural estates, the proprietor of sky-touching mansions, or the master of stacked cash boxes. Nor did I engage in efforts to acquire them. I was not one who sought a crooked route to obtain them!

Then, one may ask: during all these years—as party treasurer, as party president for **42 years**, and as Chief Minister for **19 years**—did I earn nothing? My answer is: yes, I earned. I earned titles such as **“one who serves Tamil”** and **“one who is prepared to give even his head so that Tamil may live.”** I earned abundant words of praise.

I do not have the courage to falsely claim that I never earned money to conduct my simple life. I did earn. And I gave a large part of what I earned to those who lived in darkness.

The first play I wrote and acted in, **Shantha or Palaniappan**, was sold for **₹100** in the 1940s. I used that money not only for family expenses, but also for advance expenses for the next play and for the **Arur Actors' Association**.

After that, for **Rajakumari**, produced by Jupiter Pictures in Coimbatore, and for films such as **Manthiri Kumari** and **Devaki**, produced by Modern Theatres in Salem, what I received was a monthly salary; they paid me the balance after income tax.

Later, one after another, from **Parasakthi**, **Manohara**, **Malaikkallan**, **Iruvar Ullam**, **Marudha Nattu Ilavarasi**, **Thirumbi Paar**, **Panam**, **Neethikku Thandanai**, **Ilaignan** and others, up to the present **Ponnar-Sankar**, I have written story and dialogue for **76 films**. I have also written songs for some films.

For the films **Thaayilla Pillai** and **Iruvar Ullam**, directed by **Thiru Prasad**, he had promised that if the films ran for one hundred days he would give me an additional **₹10,000**. When the film did run for one hundred days, I used the ₹10,000 he gave me to build the **Muthuvelar–Anjugam Mother and Child Welfare Home** in my birthplace, Thirukkuvalai, and had it inaugurated by the then Chief Minister **Thiru Bhaktavatsalam**.

At that time, I was not merely a writer; I was also a Member of the Legislative Assembly—and Deputy Leader of the Opposition.

Similarly, from the remuneration I received for all the films I wrote, apart from buying a small extent of wet land at **Kattur**, near Tiruvarur, I gave the balance to the needy. At Kattur itself, I provided funds for a primary-school building and helped the growth of education in that village area.

When I was party treasurer, on Anna's orders, I travelled across Tamil Nadu—to towns, villages, hamlets and tiny settlements, to the point where there was scarcely a place I did not visit—charging amounts for hoisting the DMK flag and for dining in the homes of party members. At the DMK conference at **Virugambakkam, Chennai**, in the presence of Anna, **Rajaji, Quaid-e-Millath, M.P. Sivagnanam, P. Ramamurthi** and **P.K. Mookiah Thevar** of the Forward Bloc, I handed over **₹11 lakh** as election funds.

Flood-relief funds, cyclone-relief funds, funds for the welfare of weavers to wipe away the tears of handloom workers—I have rendered service by both contributing to and collecting so many funds.

In **2004-2005**, I received **₹11 lakh** for the film **Mannin Maindhan** and **₹10 lakh** for **Kannamma**. I had those amounts directly handed over as tsunami-relief contributions to the then Chief Minister Jayalalithaa through Thambi **M.K. Stalin**.

On **9-7-2008**, of the **₹25 lakh** paid to me for **Uliyin Osai**, after **₹7 lakh** in income tax, I directly distributed the remaining **₹18 lakh** as assistance to needy artists from the world of cinema at a function held in Kalaignar Arangam.

On **17-9-2009**, I was paid **₹50 lakh** for **Pen Singam**. When I announced that I would give that amount as assistance to students belonging to the Arunthathiyar community, and was told that the amount to be distributed would be **₹61 lakh**, I added **₹11 lakh** from my own cash reserve and gave the assistance on **29-10-2009**.

On **27-4-2010**, after income tax, I was paid **₹45 lakh** for **Ilaignan**. I added that amount to the Chief Minister's Relief Fund, and later that sum was given as assistance for the welfare of persons with disabilities.

For **Ponnar-Sankar**, I was paid **₹10 lakh on 8-9-2009** and **₹12.5 lakh on 6-6-2010**. Of the ₹25 lakh due for that film, after **₹2.5 lakh** in tax, the remaining amount was **₹22.5 lakh**. From that sum, I arranged assistance for police personnel who had participated in sports competitions and won medals.

At an Ambedkar birthday function held by the Viduthalai Chiruthaigal Katchi, **Thiru Thol. Thirumavalavan** presented me with **₹50,000**. I added that amount to the Chief Minister's Public Relief Fund.

To the leaders who founded the DMK, to leaders who remained unseen companions, and to the cadres—our Udanpirappugal—who are dearer than life, thousands upon thousands have been given as family assistance and welfare assistance. Even today such assistance continues to be distributed through trusts established in my name.

At every one of my birthday functions, money given in place of garlands and shawls has been added to the Chief Minister's Public Relief Fund.

When the Tamil Nadu government collected funds for Eelam Tamil relief, I personally contributed **₹10 lakh** from my own responsibility.

My wife **Dayalu Ammaiyar**, who had been a shareholder in **Sun Television**, received **₹100 crore** when she separated from it. Of the **₹10 crore** that came to me from that amount, I set aside **₹5 crore** and, on behalf of the DMK, established the **Kalaignar Karunanidhi Trust** to help needy people in the party.

Speaking at the **30th Book Fair on 11-1-2007**, I announced that from this ₹5 crore, **₹1 crore** would be donated to the **South Indian Booksellers and Publishers Association**, and that amount was accordingly given. I asked that, using the interest on the sum, every year five outstanding writers and publishers of good books carrying social-reform ideas be selected and each be given a **₹1 lakh gold purse**. With this ₹1 crore, the publishers' association established the **Kalaignar M. Karunanidhi Gold-Purse Trust**, and so far **17 scholars** have each received a ₹1 lakh gold purse.

From the monthly interest on the remaining **₹4 crore** of the amount I gave the DMK's Kalaignar Karunanidhi Trust, assistance is being distributed to needy people in the party. From **November 2005 through May of this year**, **2,337 people** have received a total of **₹2 crore 1 lakh 45 thousand**. The assistance continues to be distributed every month.

Of the **₹10 crore** that came to me from Sun Television, after the ₹5 crore given to the Kalaignar Karunanidhi Trust, the remaining **₹5 crore** has been kept as a fixed deposit in a bank. From the interest earned on that deposit, I donated **₹1 crore** to the Classical Tamil Institute on **26-7-2008**, to arrange awards for scholars researching **epigraphy, archaeology and numismatics**.

At the World Tamil Conference in Coimbatore, this award was presented for the first time to Finnish Tamil scholar **Asko Parpola**, together with a **₹10 lakh gold purse**.

Beyond all this, I have announced that even the house in **Gopalapuram, Chennai**, where I now live, will be converted into a hospital for the benefit of the poor, and the proper registration deeds for that have already been executed.

When Mrs Dayalu Ammal, who had been a shareholder in Sun Television, separated from it, the company paid **₹100 crore on 18-10-2005**. After **₹22.5 crore** was properly paid as income tax, the remaining **₹77.5 crore** was divided. My younger daughter **Kanimozhi** received **₹2 crore** as her share. I was the one who insisted that she pay it as share capital and become a shareholder in Kalaignar Television. Even though Kanimozhi did not want to, she committed no offence other than agreeing because her father asked her to.

It is common for people who are shareholders in any company to become participants in either its profit or its loss. But there is no rule that every shareholder must be responsible for each day's management actions.

Even after prominent lawyer **Ram Jethmalani**, arguing for Kanimozhi in the Delhi CBI court, clearly pointed out that shareholders of a company need not bear responsibility for every receipt, expenditure, payment and transaction in the company, Kalaignar Television administrator Thambi **Sarath Kumar** and my daughter Kanimozhi were denied bail and sent to prison.

Even after certain deceitful people joined together and successfully completed an episode of taking revenge upon me and my family, they are not satisfied. I am well aware that there are people from the tip of Kumari to the Himalayan summit praying that the places where we lived, the places where we live, and every place where our two-coloured flag flies should be levelled to the ground and become places where grass grows—indeed, places where **darbha grass** grows.

Udanpirappē,

I have written all this because you too must know these truths. Even if this is a **“self-chronicle”** from your elder brother, treat it as a **novel of Self-Respect**. If, from the beginning of this movement to its end, you read every line, think, understand and act, then this camp—the Self-Respect movement that seeks the welfare of Tamils—will send forth thousands upon thousands of arrows of righteous struggle in the form of young men, younger brothers and younger sisters. That righteous struggle will be the final battle. **We are certain to win.**

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**22-5-2011**

## Letter-specific notes

1. Every money amount, date, film title, trust figure and legal/political statement is retained from the audited source without outside reconciliation.
2. The source's `தர்ப்பைப் புல்` image is rendered conservatively as **darbha grass** rather than expanded with an external ritual explanation.
3. The final passage deliberately turns `சுயபுராணம்` into a `சுயமரியாதைப் புதினம்`; the English keeps the contrast between a self-chronicle and a Self-Respect narrative visible.

என் மீதும், என் குடும்பத்தினர் மீதும் வஞ்சம் தீர்த்துக் கொள்ளும் படலத்தை வஞ்சனையாளர்கள் சிலர் கூடி - வெற்றிகரமாக நடத்தி முடித்துள்ள போதிலுங்கூட - அத்துடன் நிம்மதி அடையாது, நாங்கள் வாழ்ந்த இடம், வாழும் இடம், நம் இரு வண்ணக் கொடி பறக்கும் இடம் அனைத்தும் தரை மட்டமாகி - புல் முளைத்த இடமாகப் போக வேண்டும் என்று - அதுவும் “தர்ப்பைப் புல்” முளைத்த இடமாகப் போக வேண்டும் என்று - குமரி முனையிலிருந்து இமயக் கொடி முடி வரையிலே உள்ளவர்கள் தவம் கிடக்கிறார்கள் என்பது எனக்குத் தெரியாமல் இல்லை.
