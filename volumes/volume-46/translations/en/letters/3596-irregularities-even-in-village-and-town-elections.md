# 3596. Irregularities Even in Village and Town Elections!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This translation follows the locked Volume 46 pilot conventions. Allegations, names, vote counts, dates, locations and reported acts are preserved as claims made in Kalaignar's letter; they are not independently verified or expanded. The complete audited Tamil follows and remains authoritative.

**Date:** 1 November 2011

**Udanpirappē,**

Details about how the local-body elections were conducted and how the counting of votes took place have appeared in a few daily newspapers and weekly magazines. They have reported that, here and there, victorious opposition candidates were declared defeated and defeated ruling-party candidates were declared victorious, and have published some of those irregularities. I wish to mention only a few of them.

In places where AIADMK candidates lost the local-body elections, unable to bear defeat, they have gone to the height of lawlessness—attacking those who won, ransacking their homes, smashing party offices, entering houses with the help of rowdies and attacking women inside. Police officials themselves are aiding them.

In the election for president of Seeyathamangai village panchayat in Thirumarugal Union, Nagapattinam district, DMK General Council member Anbazhagan won for the second consecutive time. Angered by this, AIADMK men entered the Seeyathamangai DMK election office on the night of the 21st, smashed tables, chairs and electric fans, and damaged more than eleven two-wheelers parked at the entrance. They also damaged the houses of DMK branch secretary Murugavel and his elder sister Indirani and attacked DMK members. More than fifteen people were injured. Former branch secretary Gunasekaran of Nadu Theru, Seeyathamangai, who was seriously injured, is undergoing treatment at Nagapattinam Government Hospital. The AIADMK violent group also set fire to the DMK election booth office. When no action was taken on the complaint lodged by DMK members at Thittacheri police station, they staged a road blockade led by General Council member and village panchayat president Anbazhagan, demanding immediate action against the AIADMK men. Meanwhile AIADMK men lodged a false complaint at Thittacheri police station alleging that DMK members had attacked them. The police, who had taken no action on the DMK complaint, then filed a false case against DMK members in support of the ruling party.

In Kuthalam village panchayat, Thirumarugal Union, Nagapattinam district, DMK candidate Thiruneelakandan won the panchayat president's post by 201 votes. DMK branch secretary and former panchayat president Suresh alias Govindaraj of Pulavanallur had worked for his victory. On the 21st, while votes were being counted, Suresh's wife Latha suddenly fell ill. He locked the house, admitted her to a private hospital for surgery, and left his two children with his father Ramalingam. At about 6 p.m. on the 22nd, Ravi alias Sivakumar, the AIADMK candidate who had lost to the DMK, allegedly came to Suresh's house with AIADMK rowdies carrying deadly weapons. They broke the windows and glass panes, smashed a bicycle and chairs at the entrance, broke the lock, entered the house, damaged the television, expensive chairs, sofa, fan and cot, entered the puja room and broke pictures and ritual articles, then broke two bureaus in the bedroom, threw out the contents, and allegedly took seven sovereigns of jewellery, ₹16,000 in cash and silver articles; kitchen utensils were also damaged.

They are also said to have attacked Suresh's father Ramalingam with a wooden club and beaten Suresh's children, and then damaged the neighbouring house of Suresh's brother. When villagers gathered, the group fled. A case was registered on Suresh's complaint at Thittacheri police station. No action followed.

In the election for president of Irukkai village panchayat in Keezhvelur Union, Nagapattinam district, the DMK won. Angered by this, opponents allegedly said they had lost because members of the Muslim community had voted for the DMK. On the early morning of the 22nd, after prayers, they waylaid Sathakathullah, aged 35, the ulema (Hazrat) of the Irukkai mosque, at the entrance to his house and assaulted him, saying that the Muslim community was responsible for the DMK capturing the panchayat presidency. Seriously injured, he was admitted to Nagapattinam Government Hospital.

In Pallivaramangalam panchayat of Thiruvarur Union, DMK's Sekar and AIADMK's Jeganathan contested for president. Though DMK candidate Sekar received more votes, Jeganathan of the AIADMK was declared elected. Alleging irregularities in counting and demanding a recount, residents staged a road blockade in the local bazaar.

In Mangudi panchayat, Thiruvarur Union, DMK's Durairaj won the president's election. Opponents who lost allegedly smashed and damaged the houses, shops and vehicles of DMK members. A person named Ayrasi, seriously injured in the attack, was admitted to Thiruvarur Hospital. Journalists and photographers who went to photograph the incident were also allegedly attacked and their cameras damaged.

DMK candidate Ramesh of Ward 12 of Walajabad Town Panchayat complained about the high-handed actions of Uthiramerur AIADMK MLA A. Ganesan, but the police took no action. Instead, allegedly at the instigation of AIADMK member Arikrishnan, AIADMK men led by Shankar and Divakar came in a group to Ramesh's house, abused his mother obscenely and created a disturbance. Ramesh again went to Walajabad police station to complain. Shocked that the police ignored it, he vomited blood there, collapsed and died.

In Salem district, Chetti in Ward 8 and Palani in Ward 4 of Muthampatti village panchayat were declared elected and given certificates. Yet notices were pasted on their houses stating that their victories were invalid.

For the presidency of Malla Mooppampatti village panchayat in Salem district, Rani was declared elected by a margin of 19 votes. But instead of giving her the certificate, officials later declared AIADMK's Kumar elected and handed the certificate to him.

When complaint petitions about these matters were submitted to the Election Commissioner, he reportedly refused to receive them and said they could be given to the District Collector or taken to court.

At Thummalapatti near Palani in Dindigul district, DMK's Kathiresan and AIADMK's Nattudurai contested for panchayat president. During counting, BDO Palaniappan announced that Kathiresan had won by 41 votes. Then phone calls began coming to him from above. The BDO delayed giving Kathiresan the victory certificate until 3 a.m. and secretly handed it instead to AIADMK's Nattudurai before leaving.

In Kovil Ammapatti panchayat, officials altered the result and declared defeated AIADMK candidate Sakthivel elected instead of DMK's Velavan, who had won by 16 votes.

In Eramalur panchayat in the Vandavasi area, DMK's Meeradevi was initially declared president. AIADMK functionary Ramesh then reportedly created a disturbance, saying, “It is our government that is in power, so my wife Rama must be declared elected.” Some votes polled for Meeradevi were allegedly torn up; a recount was announced, and AIADMK's Rama was then declared elected.

In Moondramsethi panchayat, Thiruvarur district, CPI comrade Asaithambi won by two votes. Told to collect his certificate in the morning, he went home. When he returned, he was told that, frightened by ruling-party threats, a certificate had already been given the previous night declaring AIADMK's Rajendran elected. When Asaithambi said he would protest, the BDO gave him a victory certificate too. With both men holding certificates and confusion over who would assume office, the BDO telephoned both and reportedly threatened suicide unless one returned his certificate. After a long struggle, Asaithambi of the CPI assumed office.

In Mannargudi Union Ward 5, DMK's Subbaiyan polled 945 votes and defeated AIADMK's Sekar. This was reported everywhere, including on the Election Commission website. But when Subbaiyan went the next day to collect his certificate, he found that Sekar, who had polled only 927 votes, had instead been declared elected. Friends reportedly stopped Subbaiyan from attempting self-immolation. Officials were said to have responded, “What can we do? Ruling-party men threatened us and took away the victory certificate.”

In Tharamangalam Union, Salem district, Mathes had sought the AIADMK nomination for Union Chairman. Minister Edappadi Palanisamy did not give him the ticket, instead announcing Kangeyan as the candidate. Mathes entered the contest as an independent with his supporters and proved that Kangeyan was a contractor in the same Union, getting his nomination rejected. Realising that the elected AIADMK Union Council members would not obey him, Minister Palanisamy allegedly ordered Tharamangalam AIADMK Union secretary Chinnasamy to abduct six AIADMK members who had come for the swearing-in, and directed police to make the necessary arrangements. Accordingly, on the morning of the 25th, police under Omalur Deputy Superintendent Muthukaruppan and Inspector Bhaskaran waited at the Tharamangalam Panchayat Union office to take away the AIADMK members. The public and journalists were kept at a distance. Police allowed only Chinnasamy's jeep and a Scorpio car into the premises.

After taking office, AIADMK women members Sumathi and Shanthi and councillor Manickam came out first. Police told them to get into the Union secretary's jeep; when they refused, police allegedly lifted them bodily and put them into it. Mathes came out behind them and shouted; police restrained, hit and kicked him. Mathes's supporters then surrounded the police. Taking advantage of this, the women councillors confined in the jeep pushed past the police and came out. The minister also had Kangeyan file a police complaint against Mathes. This is only a tiny drop from the “divine games” enacted in Salem!

These details show the manner in which the AIADMK government conducted the local-body elections!

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**1 November 2011**

சேலம் மாவட்டம், தாரமங்கலம் ஒன்றியத் தலைவர் பதவிக்கு
அ.தி.மு.க.வில் போட்டியிட மாதேஸ் விருப்ப மனு கொடுத்திருந்தார்.
அமைச்சர் எடப்பாடி பழனிசாமி அவருக்கு வாய்ப்பு வழங்காமல்
காங்கேயனை வேட்பாளராக அறிவித்தார். இதனால் மாதேஸ் தனது
ஆதரவாளர்களுடன் சுயேட்சையாக போட்டியிட்டார். காங்கேயன் அதே
ஒன்றியத்தில் ஒப்பந்ததாரராக இருப்பதை நிரூபித்து அவரது வேட்பு
மனுவை நிராகரிக்கச் செய்தார். தேர்ந்தெடுக்கப்பட்ட அ.தி.மு.க.
ஒன்றிய கவுன்சிலர்கள் தனக்குக் கட்டுப்பட மாட்டார்கள் என்பதை
உணர்ந்த அமைச்சர் எடப்பாடி பழனிசாமி, பதவியேற்புக்கு வந்திருந்த
6 அ.தி.மு.க. உறுப்பினர்களை கடத்துமாறு தாரமங்கலம் அ.தி.மு.க.
ஒன்றியச் செயலாளர் சின்னசாமிக்கு உத்தரவிட்டதாகவும், அதற்கான
ஏற்பாடுகளை செய்யுமாறு காவல் துறையினருக்கு உத்தரவிட்டதாகவும்
கூறப்படுகிறது. அதன்படி 25ந்தேதி காலை ஓமலூர் துணைக் கண்காணிப்பாளர்
முத்துக்கருப்பன், ஆய்வாளர் பாஸ்கரன் தலைமையில் காவல் துறையினர்
தாரமங்கலம் ஊராட்சி ஒன்றிய அலுவலகத்தில் அ.தி.மு.க. உறுப்பினர்களை
அழைத்துச் செல்ல காத்திருந்தனர். பொதுமக்கள், பத்திரிகையாளர்கள்
தூரத்தில் நிறுத்தப்பட்டனர். ஒன்றியச் செயலாளர் சின்னசாமியின் ஜீப்,
ஒரு ஸ்கார்பியோ கார் மட்டுமே வளாகத்திற்குள் செல்ல காவல் துறையினர்
அனுமதித்தனர்.

பதவியேற்ற பிறகு அ.தி.மு.க. பெண் உறுப்பினர்கள் சுமதி, சாந்தி,
கவுன்சிலர் மாணிக்கம் ஆகியோர் முதலில் வெளியே வந்தனர். அவர்களை
ஒன்றியச் செயலாளரின் ஜீப்பில் ஏறுமாறு காவல் துறையினர் கூறினர்.
அவர்கள் மறுத்ததால் காவல் துறையினர் அவர்களை குண்டுக்கட்டாக
தூக்கி ஜீப்பில் போட்டதாகக் கூறப்படுகிறது. அவர்களுக்குப் பின்னால்
வந்த மாதேஸ் கூச்சல் போட்டதால் காவல் துறையினர் அவரை தடுத்து,
அடித்து உதைத்ததாகக் கூறப்படுகிறது. மாதேஸின் ஆதரவாளர்கள் காவல்
துறையினரை சுற்றி வளைத்தனர். இதைப் பயன்படுத்திக் கொண்டு ஜீப்பில்
அடைத்து வைக்கப்பட்டிருந்த பெண் கவுன்சிலர்கள் காவல் துறையினரைத்
தள்ளிவிட்டு வெளியே வந்தனர். மாதேஸ் மீது காங்கேயன் மூலமாக
அமைச்சர் காவல் துறையில் புகார் கொடுக்கச் செய்ததாகவும் கூறப்படுகிறது.
சேலத்தில் நடைபெற்ற “திருவிளையாடல்”களில் இது ஒரு சிறு துளிதான்!
