# 3532. The Harvest Was Abundant; the Farmer's Tears Disappeared!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This translation continues the agriculture-achievements account from Letter 3531 and follows the audited canonical Tamil and locked Volume 44 conventions. **Udanpirappē,**

I continue the account of the achievements and schemes carried out by the DMK government in the Agriculture Department during the past five years.

## Ainthinai Genetic Gardens

As announced at the concluding function of the World Classical Tamil Conference in Coimbatore, traditional **Genetic Gardens** are to be established by the Tamil Nadu government for the five *thinai* landscape types:

- a **Kurinji Garden** on **75 acres** at Yercaud in Salem district;
- a **Mullai Garden** on **25 acres** in the Sirumalai area of Dindigul district;
- a **Marutham Garden** on **21.47 acres** at Sakkottai in Thanjavur district;
- a **Neithal Garden** on **14.61 acres** at Thirukadaiyur in Nagapattinam district; and
- a **Palai Garden** on **25 acres** at Achadipirambu village in Ramanathapuram district.

It has been decided to establish these Ainthinai Genetic Gardens, and **₹37 crore 391 lakh** has been allocated in the first phase. Preparatory work is under way.

## Botanical parks created by the DMK government

Because the Government Botanical Garden and Government Rose Garden at Udhagamandalam in the Nilgiris, Sim’s Park at Coonoor and Bryant Park at Kodaikanal attract large numbers of tourists, the DMK government is establishing more botanical parks in Tamil Nadu.

Along with the Classical Tamil Park to be established in Coimbatore, a world-class **Semmozhi Poonga** was established on Cathedral Road in Chennai at a cost of **₹7 crore 96 lakh** and was inaugurated by me on **24-11-2010**.

Likewise, work has been undertaken on:

- an ornamental garden and demonstration park over **28.16 acres** at Madhavaram near Chennai, at a cost of **₹5 crore 93 lakh**;
- an eco-park over **36.78 acres** at Courtallam in Tirunelveli district, at a cost of **₹5 crore 92 lakh**;
- a botanical park over **47.25 acres** at Yercaud in Salem district, at a cost of **₹11 crore 26 lakh**.

Bryant Park, Anna Park and Chettiar Park at Kodaikanal in Dindigul district are being improved at a cost of **₹1 crore 59 lakh**.

## Agricultural mechanisation scheme

By popularising agricultural machinery and implements among farmers, the government says agricultural production and productivity rise, labour shortages are reduced, agricultural operations can be carried out at the appropriate time, production costs decline, farmers’ net income rises and difficulties in agricultural work are reduced.

During the five years, **36,813 farmers** received **₹86 crore 71 lakh** in subsidy to purchase tractors, power tillers, harvesters and other agricultural machinery and implements.

For rental to farmers at low rates, **25 combine harvesters and 50 tractors** were purchased during the past five years at a cost of **₹5 crore 90 lakh 75 thousand**.

## Drying yards

To reduce post-harvest losses in grain produce, **200 drying yards** were established in villages from **2006–07 to 2009–10** at a cost of **₹4 crore 80 lakh**.

## Cashew export zone

An agricultural export zone for cashew was established at Panruti in Cuddalore district in **2006–07** by **Sattva Agro Exports**, at an estimated cost of **₹16 crore 54 lakh**. Through this company, cashew trade worth **₹1 crore 53 lakh** has been carried out.

## Flower auction centre

For the welfare of flower-growing farmers, a flower auction centre was established at Kaval Kinaru in Tirunelveli district at a cost of **₹1 crore 63 lakh 40 thousand**. In **2009–2010**, an average of **1,500 kilograms of flowers**, worth **₹85,756 per day**, was sold by **124 farmers**.

## New commercial complexes and marketing facilities

The following facilities are listed:

- an onion commercial complex with cold-storage facilities at Pongalur in Tiruppur district, costing **₹1 crore**;
- a mango commercial complex with cold-storage facilities at Krishnagiri, costing **₹1 crore**;
- a tomato commercial complex with cold-storage facilities at Palacode in Dharmapuri district, costing **₹1 crore**;
- a grape commercial complex with cold-storage godown facilities at Odaipatti in Theni district, costing **₹1 crore**;
- a coconut commercial complex at Ponnavarayan Kottai, Ukkadai village, near Pattukkottai in Thanjavur district, for the benefit of coconut farmers in Thanjavur, Tiruvarur, Nagapattinam and Pudukkottai districts, at a cost of **₹4 crore**;
- marketing terminals with modern infrastructure and collection-centre facilities in producing areas at Navalur near Chennai, Mukkampatti near Madurai and Perundurai near Coimbatore, for vegetables, fruits and other produce;
- a tomato cold-storage godown at Mecheri in Salem district costing **₹1 crore**;
- a coconut commercial complex at Pethappampatti in Tiruppur district costing **₹1 crore**;
- **10 rural business centres** at a cost of **₹1 crore 50 lakh**;
- four banana-ripening chambers at Tiruchi, Mohanur in Namakkal district, Srivaikuntam in Thoothukudi district and Chinnamanur in Theni district, each costing **₹50 lakh**;
- a marketing complex for hill vegetables at Karamadai in Coimbatore district, costing **₹1 crore**;
- additional shops in the paddy commercial complex at Madurai, costing **₹36 lakh**.

Together with these, infrastructure facilities have been created in regulated markets at a cost of **₹7 crore 81 lakh 25 thousand**.

A **Vegetable Commercial Complex** for the benefit of farmers and traders selling vegetables and fruits in Madurai is to be established at a cost of **₹85 crore**. **₹30 crore** has been released in the first phase.

## Tamil Nadu Small Farmers’ Agribusiness Consortium

Under the Tamil Nadu Small Farmers’ Agribusiness Consortium scheme, which works in association with government, the private sector, cooperative societies, service institutions, nationalised banks, State Bank of India and its associate banks to promote agribusiness in Tamil Nadu, **31 agribusiness enterprises** with a total investment of **₹132 crore 62 lakh 73 thousand** have received **₹12 crore 14 lakh 84 thousand** through the consortium as capital investment assistance for starting businesses.

## Agricultural development under the DMK government's Industrial Policy 2007

To secure fair prices for agricultural produce and raise agriculture to a level comparable with other industries, **Industrial Policy 2007** included several concessions for agriculture and agro-industries. Schemes are being implemented with the aim of achieving **4 per cent agricultural growth** during the Eleventh Five-Year Plan period.

## Water Resources and Land Resources Project

With World Bank assistance and the objective of increasing irrigated area under agricultural and horticultural crops, the Tamil Nadu Water Resources and Land Resources Project is being implemented in **63 sub-basins**, with an allocation of **₹578 crore 40 lakh**, over the six years **2007–2013**.

From **2007–2008 through October 2010–2011**, works under the project were completed in **55 sub-watershed areas**, covering **76,444 hectares**, at a cost of **₹112 crore 69 lakh**.

To help farmers in these sub-watersheds earn additional income, **936 commodity groups** were formed and **558 Memoranda of Understanding** were entered into with traders. So far, **50,925 metric tonnes** of agricultural produce worth **₹64 crore 3 lakh** have been sold, and **15,036 farmers** have benefited by earning **₹4 crore 58 lakh** in additional income. The letter says a World Bank review team examined and praised the marketing component.

## Group-based approach

From **2006–07 to 2008–09**, **3,432 farmers’ interest groups** with **51,285 farmers** as members were formed with an allocation of **₹3.36 crore**.

In **2006–2007 and 2007–2008**, **8,091 farm-women’s groups** received training and financial assistance to start self-employment activities at a cost of **₹5 crore 15 lakh**.

In **2008–2009**, **2,025 groups** received assistance at **₹10,000 per group** to start enterprises, under an allocation of **₹2 crore 19 lakh**. In all, about **1 lakh 50 thousand farm women** belonging to **10,116 groups** benefited.

In addition, **223 farm-women’s groups** were supplied farm implements at **25 per cent subsidy**, under an allocation of **₹1 crore**, so that they could benefit by renting agricultural machinery.

## Sugar sector

To develop the sugar industry, the Tamil Nadu government permitted **9 new sugar complexes** in the private sector. Of these, **8 sugar mills** completed construction and began sugar production.

From the **2006–2007 crushing season**, the government ordered that sugar mills themselves bear the full vehicle-transport charge from the sugarcane field to the factory.

Tamil Nadu is also described as a pioneer in establishing cogeneration plants in sugar mills. The State has cogeneration plants with a capacity of **404.19 MW** in **19 private sugar mills**, and small cogeneration plants in **3 cooperative sugar mills**. Steps are being taken through the Tamil Nadu Electricity Board to establish **183 MW** of cogeneration capacity in **12 cooperative and public-sector sugar mills** at a cost of **₹1,125 crore 63 lakh**.

The State has **9 ethanol-production plants** capable of producing **960 lakh litres a year**. Because of a shortage in rectified-spirit production, the source says it has not been possible to supply petrol blended with **5 per cent ethanol**. If molasses production increases and newly licensed rectified-spirit plants begin production, 5-per-cent ethanol-blended petrol can be supplied throughout the State.

Rectified-spirit and ethanol plants with a capacity of **45 kilolitres per day** are being established at the Cheyyar and M. R. Krishnamurthy cooperative sugar mills at a cost of **₹36 crore each**.

Molasses is a valuable by-product of sugar mills. At the Amaravathi and Salem cooperative sugar mills, molasses produced by the mills is being used in distilleries to make rectified spirit.

Through the government’s efforts, a **30-kilolitre rectified-spirit production scheme** was started at the Salem and Amaravathi cooperative sugar mills in **August 2008**.

The **Madhurantakam Cooperative Sugar Mill** was reopened on **15-10-2010** and is to function from the next crushing season.

### Sugar mills receiving Union Government awards

For the **2009–2010 sugar season**, the National Federation of Cooperative Sugar Factories awarded first prizes to:

- **Subramania Siva Cooperative Sugar Mill** for best technical performance;
- **Salem Cooperative Sugar Mill** for sugarcane development; and
- **Cheyyar Cooperative Sugar Mill** for financial management.

## Tamil Nadu Agricultural University

The Agricultural University established in Tamil Nadu in **1971**, when I was Chief Minister, with agricultural education, research and extension as its objectives, is serving through **10 colleges on 7 campuses, 36 research stations and 14 agricultural science centres**.

With the aim of benefiting students in Nagapattinam, Tiruvarur and Thanjavur districts, where many families depend on agriculture for their livelihood, a new agricultural college is to be started at **Keezhvelur in Nagapattinam district**, and a separate **Horticultural University** — horticulture being regarded as the future of agriculture — is to be started in **Krishnagiri district** this year.

According to the needs of Tamil Nadu farmers, during the five years the university developed and released **56 new crop varieties, 20 farm implements and 20 management technologies**, helping agricultural production increase.

## Appointment and advancement of agricultural officers

Whenever a DMK government has been formed in Tamil Nadu, the letter says, it has not only provided concessions and schemes to agricultural people but also successive benefits to agricultural officers. I was able to recognise this at their conference held in Chennai on **24-11-2010**.

It was under the DMK government in **1972** that agricultural officers were given gazetted status. In **1989**, agricultural officers were given pay on par with Assistant Veterinary Surgeons and Assistant Engineers.

At Tamil Nadu Agricultural University, **131 assistant teachers** working on consolidated pay were placed on time-scale pay and appointed as Assistant Professors.

In the **2007 departmental restructuring**:

- **509 Agricultural Officer posts** were upgraded to Assistant Directors of Agriculture;
- **512 Assistant Seed Officer posts** were upgraded to Deputy Agricultural Officers;
- **514 Assistant Agricultural Officer posts** were upgraded to Assistant Seed Officers;
- **399 Assistant Soil Conservation Officer posts** were upgraded to Junior Engineers; and
- **54 Assistant Soil Conservation Officer posts** held by engineering graduates were upgraded to Assistant Engineers.

Promotions were also granted.

After removal of the recruitment-ban order, direct recruitment was made to **2,632 posts**: **338 Assistant Professors** in Tamil Nadu Agricultural University, **540 Agricultural Officers**, **47 Horticultural Officers** and **1,707 Assistant Agricultural Officers** in the Agriculture and Horticulture Departments. In addition, **3,544 officers** in the Agriculture Department received promotions.

**Udanpirappē,** I have compiled and presented the achievements made by this department, under the responsibility of brother Veerapandi Arumugam and with the cooperation of its officers. Do not merely read them; do not fail to remind the great farming community that so many achievements have been carried out for them during these five years!

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**
