**Udanpirappē,**

Today’s newspapers carry, with much excitement, the report “Chief Minister Jayalalithaa Orders.” What has she ordered? She has announced an increase in the upper income limit for marriage-assistance schemes. She has also reportedly ordered the purchase and distribution of cooking utensils at a cost of ₹22,23,243. The State Government’s total revenue receipts for 2013–2014 alone are estimated at ₹1,18,580 crore. Yet today’s announcement by the Chief Minister is not worth even one crore rupees; it concerns only ₹22 lakh.

The Chief Minister has announced that the annual family-income ceiling for two marriage-assistance programmes—the Moovalur Ramamirtham Ammaiyar Memorial Marriage Financial Assistance Scheme and the E.V.R. Maniammaiyar Memorial Marriage Assistance Scheme for Daughters of Poor Widows—will rise from ₹24,000 to ₹72,000.

The Tamil Nadu Government operates five marriage-assistance programmes. They are the very same five that existed under the DMK Government: the Moovalur Ramamirtham Ammaiyar Memorial Marriage Financial Assistance Scheme; the Dr Dharmambal Ammaiyar Memorial Widow Remarriage Financial Assistance Scheme; the E.V.R. Maniammaiyar Memorial Marriage Assistance Scheme for Daughters of Poor Widows; the Mother Teresa Memorial Marriage Assistance Scheme for Orphaned Women; and the Anjugam Ammaiyar Memorial Inter-caste Marriage Financial Assistance Scheme.

All five continue under Jayalalithaa’s Government, with only one change. Do you know what it is? Because Anjugam Ammaiyar was my mother, they changed only the name of the Anjugam Ammaiyar Memorial Inter-caste Marriage Financial Assistance Scheme to the Dr Muthulakshmi Reddy Memorial Inter-caste Marriage Financial Assistance Scheme. What a high office—and what a small mind!

Let that pass. What is in a name? Three of the five schemes already had no income ceiling at all. Only the other two had a ceiling of ₹24,000. The Government has not even abolished that ceiling; it has merely raised it to ₹72,000. That is all.

One of those two is the Moovalur Ramamirtham Ammaiyar Memorial Marriage Financial Assistance Scheme. Do you know when it began? To encourage women’s education, the first DMK Government introduced it on 3 June 1989, granting ₹5,000 to the parents of women aged over eighteen who had completed the eighth standard or above.

Later, the educational qualification was raised to the tenth standard and the assistance to ₹10,000. But when the AIADMK Government headed by Jayalalithaa came to power in 2001, it stopped the scheme from 1 April 2002 because it had been introduced by a DMK Government.

When the DMK returned to office in 2006, the marriage-assistance scheme was restored and the amount was increased from ₹10,000 to ₹15,000. Under the DMK Government, it rose to ₹20,000 from 20 March 2008 and then to ₹25,000 from 1 April 2010. The annual family-income ceiling was also increased under the DMK Government from ₹12,000 to ₹24,000.

After 2006, during five years of DMK rule, ₹880,55,05,000 was provided for the marriages of 4,64,756 poor women, benefiting poor families. Had the DMK Government continued after 2011, it would have removed the income ceiling from all five schemes, just as three already had none. Instead, the present Government has merely raised it from ₹24,000 to ₹72,000.

Another programme, the Dr Dharmambal Ammaiyar Memorial Widow Remarriage Financial Assistance Scheme, was begun in 1975 under the DMK Government when I was Chief Minister, with the social-reform objective of encouraging young widows to remarry and begin a new life. The assistance of ₹15,000 was increased under the DMK Government to ₹20,000 from 20 November 2008 and later to ₹25,000. During five years of DMK rule, ₹1,72,71,000 was provided to help 910 poor widows remarry.

Under the E.V.R. Maniammaiyar Memorial Marriage Assistance Scheme for Daughters of Widowed Mothers, ₹30,32,93,000 was provided during five years of DMK rule for the marriages of 16,365 women.

Under the Mother Teresa Memorial Marriage Assistance Scheme for Orphaned Women, ₹4,00,58,000 was provided during those five years for the marriages of 2,220 women.

Across these five marriage-assistance schemes, the DMK Government provided a total of ₹937,50,87,000 during five years for the marriages of 4,95,634 poor women.

To remove caste distinctions in society and eradicate the cruelty of dowry, an inter-caste marriage financial-assistance programme has operated under DMK Governments since 1 July 1967, when Perarignar Anna was Chief Minister, encouraging couples entering inter-caste marriages. Between 2006 and 2011, ₹20,89,60,000 was provided under this programme to 11,383 couples.

It is the name of this scheme that was removed. The AIADMK Government replaced it with the name of Dr Muthulakshmi Reddy—a name the DMK Government had already given to its maternity-assistance scheme for pregnant women. The inter-caste marriage programme already had no upper income limit.

Women’s advancement is one of the central principles of the Dravidian movement. DMK Governments enacted many laws and implemented many programmes so that women could secure political, social and economic liberation; progress step by step in life; obtain education and office; and rise to higher status in society.

Among them were: the first recruitment of women into the Tamil Nadu Police in 1973; the Women’s Development Programme, begun through self-help groups in Dharmapuri in 1989 and now enabling lakhs of poor women to attain economic freedom; the E.V.R. Nagammaiyar Free Degree Education Scheme for poor women; the Dr Muthulakshmi Reddy Memorial Maternity Financial Assistance Scheme, providing ₹6,000 to poor pregnant women for nutrition; 30 per cent reservation for women in government employment in 1990; a separate law granting women equal property rights; 33 per cent reservation for women in local bodies in 1996; grants for first-generation women entrepreneurs; and priority for women entrepreneurs in the allotment of industrial plots. Through schemes such as these, introduced under DMK Governments, women advanced in many fields.

The Chief Minister’s announcement next says that Puratchi Thalaivar M.G.R. launched the nutritious-meal programme in 1982; that the same food had been served under it for thirty years; and that Jayalalithaa, having noticed this, ordered the introduction of new varieties.

I retained both the name “Puratchi Thalaivar M.G.R. Nutritious Meal Programme” and the programme itself under the DMK Government. I never thought that, because the scheme had begun under an AIADMK Government, it should be closed or obstructed. Tamil Nadu knows well that I instead planned and acted to improve it further.

The DMK Government introduced the provision of two eggs a week with nutritious meals for schoolchildren from 15 July 2006. From 15 July 2007, this was increased to three eggs a week, and from 17 September 2010 to five eggs a week. Through this, 57,74,673 school students benefited.

The DMK Government supplied bananas with nutritious meals to 37,249 children who did not eat eggs. From 15 July 2007, it also provided one egg a week to children aged one to two covered by the Integrated Child Development Services programme, benefiting 4,80,870 children.

From 25 October 2007, provision was made to offer compassionate appointments to the legal heirs of employees who died while in service. Women working in the nutritious-meal programme were granted three months of paid maternity leave.

During the DMK Government from 13 May 1996 to 13 May 2001, the Family Benefit Fund was extended to nutritious-meal employees. The heirs of employees who died in service received ₹1.5 lakh, as in the case of government employees.

The retirement age of cooks and assistants in the nutritious-meal programme was raised from 55 to 58. A special provident-welfare fund was introduced for nutritious-meal and anganwadi employees, with a government contribution of ₹5,000.

Under the previous AIADMK Government, the pay scale of nutritious-meal and anganwadi employees was not increased even once. Under the DMK Government, it was increased three times. Employees who completed ten years received one increment, and those who completed twenty years another.

Cooks, assistants and anganwadi helpers who had worked on consolidated pay under the AIADMK Government received special pay scales under the DMK Government.

Under the AIADMK Government, nutritious-meal and anganwadi workers received no allowance other than dearness allowance. Under the DMK Government, from 1 June 2009, they were granted house-rent allowance and city compensatory allowance every month, a ₹100 medical allowance and a ₹2,000 festival advance.

Under the AIADMK Government, they received no monthly pension. Under the DMK Government, for the first time, retired nutritious-meal organisers, cooks among anganwadi workers, first-grade anganwadi assistants and cooking assistants, and second-grade anganwadi assistants were granted monthly pensions of ₹700, ₹600 and ₹500 respectively.

The DMK Government undertook many more measures for the welfare of women and children. Whatever the present rulers claim to have done for women’s advancement, they cannot conceal that the roots of all those measures lie in schemes implemented by DMK Governments. Tamil Nadu’s administrative history records that programmes were devised in formation after formation, on every front, and implemented effectively for the advancement of women.

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**

8 September 2013

சத்துணவுப் பணியாளர்களின் வாரிசுதாரர்களுக்கு அரசுப்
பணியாளர்களுக்கு வழங்கப்படுவது போன்று ஒன்றரை இலட்சம்
ரூபாய் குடும்ப நல நிதி வழங்கப்பட்டது.

55 லிருந்து 58 ஆக உயர்த்தியது. சத்துணவு - அங்கன்வாடிப்
பணியாளர்களுக்கு சிறப்பு சேமநல நிதித் திட்டம் தொடங்கப்பட்டு;
அரசின் பங்குத் தொகை 5 ஆயிரம் ரூபாய் வழங்கப்பட்டது.
சத்துணவு மற்றும் அங்கன்வாடிப் பணியாளர்களுக்கு முன்பு
அ.தி.மு.க. ஆட்சியில் ஒருமுறைகூட ஊதிய விகிதம் உயர்த்தி
வழங்கப்படவில்லை. ஆனால் கழக ஆட்சியிலேதான் அவர்களுக்கு
மூன்று முறை ஊதிய விகிதம் உயர்த்தி வழங்கப்பட்டுள்ளது.
கழக ஆட்சியிலேதான், சத்துணவு மற்றும் அங்கன்வாடிப்
பணியாளர்களில் பத்தாண்டுப் பணி முடித்தவர்களுக்கு, ஒரு
ஊதிய உயர்வும், 20 ஆண்டு பணி முடித்தவர்களுக்கு மற்றொரு
ஊதிய உயர்வும் வழங்கப்பட்டது.
அ.தி.மு.க. ஆட்சியில் தொகுப்பூதியத்தில் பணியாற்றி
வந்த சத்துணவு சமையலர்கள், உதவியாளர்கள், அங்கன்வாடி
உதவியாளர்கள் ஆகிய அனைவருமே கழக ஆட்சியிலேதான்
சிறப்பு ஊதிய விகிதம் பெற்றுவருகின்றனர்.
சத்துணவு மற்றும் அங்கன்வாடிப் பணியாளர்களுக்கு
அ.தி.மு.க. ஆட்சியில் அகவிலைப் படி தவிர ஏனைய படிகள்
எதுவும் வழங்கப்படவில்லை. ஆனால் கழக ஆட்சியிலேதான்,
அவர்களுக்கு முதல்முறையாக 1.6.2009 முதல் மாதந்தோறும்
வீட்டு வாடகைப்படி, நகர ஈட்டுப்படி ஆகியவைகளும், 100 ரூபாய்
மருத்துவப்படியும், 2000 ரூபாய் பண்டிகைக் கால முன்பணமும்
வழங்கப்பட்டு வருகின்றன. சத்துணவு மற்றும் அங்கன்வாடிப்
பணியாளர்களுக்கு அ.தி.மு.க. ஆட்சியில் மாதாந்திர ஓய்வூதியம்
எதுவும் வழங்கப்படவில்லை. ஆனால் கழக ஆட்சியிலேதான்
ஓய்வு பெற்ற சத்துணவு அமைப்பாளர்களுக்கும், அங்கன்வாடிப்
பணியாளர்களில் சமையலர்களுக்கும், முதல் நிலை அங்கன்வாடி
உதவியாளர்களுக்கும் - சமையல் உதவியாளர்களுக்கும்,
இரண்டாம் நிலை அங்கன்வாடி உதவியாளர்களுக்கும் முறையே
