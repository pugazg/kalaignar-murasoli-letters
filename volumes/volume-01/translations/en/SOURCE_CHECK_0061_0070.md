# Volume 1 — English Source Check: Letters 0061–0070

Status: **PASS — migrated and source-checked; bilingual alignment remains a later gate.**

## Scope

This batch covers canonical Volume 1 letters **0061–0070**, spanning PDF / printed pages **250–276**.

The preserved legacy bilingual records under `volumes/volume-1/translations/en/letters/` were used only as migration drafts and evidence. The fully fidelity-verified canonical Tamil layer and canonical chapter/page structure under `volumes/volume-01/` remained authoritative.

## Method

For each of the ten letters:

1. compared the legacy English draft with the complete canonical Tamil and chapter metadata;
2. preserved source title, printed date or explicit absence of date, sign-off, names, figures, quotations, political terminology, rhetorical structure and thought order;
3. migrated the complete authoritative Tamil into the canonical English record;
4. recorded PDF/printed-page traceability;
5. marked the record `source-checked`, not `verified`;
6. deferred meaning-level bilingual alignment and volume-wide editorial consistency review to their dedicated later gates.

## Batch result

All ten records are now present under `translations/en/letters/` and source-checked. No substantive English omission comparable to the earlier letter 0019 gap was identified. The legacy bilingual tree remains unchanged.

## Letter-level notes

### 0061 — `“ஒழுங்கான கணக்கு!”`

Preserves the Kanyakumari correspondence, the 1972 Rajya Sabha letter beginning with “a thousand salutations,” the Gopalapuram temple/political-deity reversal, and the closing test of who remains during adversity. PDF 252 controls the Tamil reading **`என் நெஞ்சும் கல்லாகவில்லை!`** over the stale legacy form.

### 0062 — `உண்மை! உண்மை!!`

Preserves the entire deliberately impossible satirical news sequence — Kennedy, Nixon, Stalin, Edward VIII, Kissinger, Vijayalakshmi Pandit, Master Tara Singh, the air-crash reversal and the Puri Shankaracharya — without factual normalisation, because the absurdity is the mechanism of the satire.

### 0063 — `“கிலுகிலுப்பை!”`

Preserves the Harishchandra/Jambulingam parable, turkey/peacock, fireworks/lamp and mother/child/rattle images, the registered-letter evidence dated **19-4-71**, and the source-visible emphasis on `தொண்டர்களை மதிக்காதவர்களை மதிக்காதவன் நான் என்பதை நீ அறிவாய்!`. **No date is printed in the source; none is inferred.**

### 0064 — `பதவி-துறவி!`

Preserves the extended irony directed at C. Subramaniam, including prohibition, hereditary education, Rajaji/Kamaraj, the Krishnagiri by-election, Woodlands Hotel quotation and the closing `சாக்கடை` / Cooum argument.

### 0065 — `உன் பெயர் எங்கே?`

Preserves the membership-enrolment drive, Villupuram conference imagery, organisational structure, fallen-roof-tile and clipped-fingernail analogies, distinction between instruments and ideals, state-autonomy and social-justice goals, and the final demand that the Udanpirappu verify his own name on the membership form.

### 0066 — `“அண்ணன் எழுதிய மடல்!”`

Preserves Anna's last `தம்பிக்கு` letter of **15-1-1969**, the 1971 manifesto quotations on political and financial powers for states, the distinction between state autonomy and separatism, Anna's passage on Tamil Nadu's distinctive qualities, and the closing slogan `உறவுக்குக் கைகொடுப்போம் / உரிமைக்குக் குரல் கொடுப்போம்`.

### 0067 — `நடக்கட்டும் நாடகம்!`

Preserves the deliberately grotesque political allegory, land-ceiling accusation, Kannagi/chastity imagery and the source's unusual punctuation **`போடுபவர்கள்.-`** rather than silently regularising it.

### 0068 — `“டெல்லி லாயத்தில்!”`

Preserves the Bharathidasan commemorative-stamp argument and the poet's verse with scan-controlled lineation/hyphenation. Canonical Tamil follows PDF 271–272, including `உலகம்-சாதி`, `சுளையும்-முற்றல்`, `நனிபசு பொழியும்பாலும்-தென்னை`, `லாயம்-அதில்`, `உத்திரவுண்டு-வீட்டில்`, the joined form **`எழுச்சிமிகு`**, and source emphasis on **`“கனைக்கும் உரிமை”`**.

### 0069 — `படையெடுப்பு பலிக்குமா?`

Preserves the Veera Vanchi / Maniyachi naming request, Delhi's `Local`-leader objection, the compromise station-board wording, the paper-shortage joke and the National Development Council visit. PDF 274 controls the printed form **`இலாக்காக்களில்`**.

### 0070 — `உணரச் செய்வோம்!`

Preserves the anti-Hindi/student-history argument, Annamalai University/Udayakumar discussion, Palai and Clive Hostel references, and the judicial inquiry's printed English sentence **verbatim**, including its punctuation and syntax.
