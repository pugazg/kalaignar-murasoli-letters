# 3480. Tamil Will Now Grow to Prevail!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> Written after the World Classical Tamil Conference, this letter links the conference’s resolutions to Tamil-medium professional education and reservation outcomes. It preserves the source’s college-by-college engineering rollout, counselling numbers, compulsory-Tamil-law chronology, veterinary-seat distribution and Muslim/Arunthathiyar internal-reservation figures. `உள் ஒதுக்கீடு` follows the locked Volume 43 treatment **internal reservation**.

**Tamil source:** [Letter 3480](../../../chapters/3480-vellath-tamizh-ini-valarum.md)  
**Source pages:** [PDF 375](../../../pages/page-375.md)–[PDF 379](../../../pages/page-379.md)  
**Date:** 12 July 2010

**Udanpirappē,**

In the splendid city of Coimbatore, from 23 to 27 June, for five days, the World Classical Tamil Conference was conducted with such great success that everyone wondered and praised it as though the whole world had gathered there. Newspapers without distinction praised it as a vast confluence of people — “lakhs upon lakhs gathered! Countless human heads wherever one looked.”

At that conference, held under the name “World Classical Tamil Conference” with an added distinction beyond earlier conferences, thousands of Tamil scholars, elders, learned persons and researchers from more than fifty countries participated in research sessions. They spoke in many ways about the greatness and grace of the Tamil language; the antiquity and distinctiveness of Tamil civilisation; what is needed to carry Tamil into the twenty-first century; and the methods by which those needs may be fulfilled.

On the closing day of the conference, on the basis of the views offered by the Tamil scholars who attended, and keeping in mind the development of the Tamil language, the Tamil people and Tamil Nadu, I announced **15 useful declarations and resolutions**. One of them was: “Appropriate legislation will be enacted by the Tamil Nadu Government to give preference in Government employment to those who studied through Tamil.” That announcement received the wholehearted welcome of the vast flood of people gathered at the conference.

After the conference concluded and I returned to Chennai and continued, as usual, in public work, the news reaching me each day has been sweet to the ear and heart, like Bharathi’s line, “The honey of joy comes pouring into the ear.”

An incomparable scheme of engineering degree courses through Tamil has begun this year. In the first stage, Tamil-medium classes have been started only in the Civil and Mechanical branches in Anna University colleges.

For **Civil Engineering**, Tamil-medium classes have begun in a total of **11 colleges**: College of Engineering, Guindy; Anna University, Tiruchirappalli; and Anna University colleges at Tindivanam, Ariyalur, Ramanathapuram, Thirukkuvalai, Panruti, Pattukkottai, Dindigul, Nagercoil and Thoothukudi.

For **Mechanical Engineering**, Tamil-medium classes have begun in a total of **12 colleges**: College of Engineering, Guindy; Anna University, Tiruchirappalli; and Anna University colleges at Villupuram, Arani, Ariyalur, Ramanathapuram, Thirukkuvalai, Panruti, Pattukkottai, Dindigul, Nagercoil and Thoothukudi.

Counselling for general-category students has been taking place since the fifth of this month. The source says that even students with cut-off marks above **190** have chosen Tamil-medium engineering and that, within the first five days of engineering counselling, **22 students** had joined Tamil-medium engineering courses. This is truly sweet news for all of us.

On the 6th, 7th and 8th, two students joined each day; on the 10th, seven joined; and nine joined through vocational-study admissions — **22 in all** so far. Engineering counselling is to continue for more than another month, so we may confidently expect many more students to join Tamil-medium engineering.

Interest in Tamil-medium engineering has also produced a demand among students and parents that the courses, now begun only in Anna Universities, should also be introduced in Government and Government-aided colleges and in private self-financing colleges. The author sees this as further strength for the rising slogan, “Tamil everywhere — Tamil in everything.”

Udanpirappē, you will not have forgotten that immediately after the DMK Government assumed office in 2006, a law was enacted on **31.5.2006** making Tamil-language study compulsory from Standard I through Standard X. Because of that law, from this academic year students up to Standard V in all schools have entered a good situation in which they study Tamil as a compulsory subject.

Let me share another sweet piece of news. You know that Tamil Nadu Veterinary and Animal Sciences University is described by the source as the first such university in Asia, and that it was first started in Tamil Nadu under a DMK Government. Counselling for its B.V.Sc. degree began on **9.7.2010**. On that day alone, counselling was completed for **166 seats**.

Of those 166 seats:

- **51** were allotted to the General Turn;
- **44** to Backward Classes;
- **6** to Backward Class Muslims;
- **33** to Most Backward Classes;
- **25** to Adi Dravidars;
- **5** to Arunthathiyars;
- **2** to Scheduled Tribes.

Are not the **6 seats for Muslims** and **5 seats for Arunthathiyars** the result of the separate internal reservation provided to them by the DMK Government, the author asks?

Everyone knows, the source says, that the Muslim community has continued to benefit from the separate reservation provided by the DMK Government. In engineering degree courses, the total number of Muslim students admitted was **3,288 in 2008–09** and **3,655 in 2009–10**. Before separate internal reservation was provided, however, only **2,134 Muslim students** were admitted to engineering in **2007–08**.

Likewise, in **2008–09**, Arunthathiyars obtained **1,093 engineering seats**. After internal reservation was provided, they obtained **1,165 seats in 2009–10**.

Everyone knows, the author says, that after internal reservation was provided, both the Muslim community and Arunthathiyars have continued to obtain more seats in medical degree courses too.

Udanpirappē, at the 1967 Virugambakkam conference Arignar Anna proclaimed: “Tamil tradition, Tamil literature, Tamil history and the Tamil language must be protected; they must spread across the earth; they must be honoured and praised without fading or disappearing; the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam wishes to ascend the seat of government precisely so that this renaissance may come to Tamil and the Tamils.”

We have continued our journey with that call always in our thought and attention. In today’s letter I have offered only a few pieces of evidence that we are walking along that path and that the Kazhagam continues to stand as a protective fortress for Backward, Most Backward, oppressed, Scheduled Tribe and Arunthathiyar communities.

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**12-7-2010**
