## Review method

Each English draft was compared against the complete audited canonical Tamil reproduced in the bilingual file and, where needed, against the canonical page records. The review checked:

1. title, salutation, closing, date and source-page range;
2. paragraph and argumentative sequence;
3. every substantive claim, qualification and attribution;
4. quotations, reported speech and source-supplied English;
5. names, dates, counts, monetary figures, land measurements and institutional terms;
6. rhetorical questions, irony, repetition, proverbs and political intensity;
7. whether English wording broadened, narrowed or reassigned responsibility; and
8. whether duplicated/parenthetical source material was nevertheless fully represented rather than summarized away.

No canonical Tamil page was changed in this review.

### 3594 — *Then and Now, the Position Is the Same!*

**Result: ONE REQUIRED REVISION.**

The draft otherwise preserves the Article 161 argument, Assembly/Cabinet chronology, Vaiko passage, Jayalalithaa's 2008 statement, Thiyagu testimony, Chozhanambiyar testimony, closing and the parenthetical source note printed after the date.

However, after reproducing Colin Gonsalves's source-supplied English quotation, the Tamil source also prints a parenthetical Tamil rendering of that quotation. The current English draft introduces that second source passage with **`The Tamil explanation printed in the source says, in substance...`** and compresses it into a summary. Under the repository's no-summarising bilingual rule, duplicated source material still has to be represented completely.

Required replacement:

> **Tamil rendering printed immediately after the English quotation in the source:** “The Chief Minister has the authority to reduce the death sentence to life imprisonment, and this is the right time to convene the Cabinet and take a decision on it..... Jayalalithaa can exercise the authority under Article 161 of the Constitution to stay the death sentence..... said Mr. Colin Gonsalves, one of the three advocates who argued before the Madras High Court.”

This is an **English-file-only** correction. The canonical Tamil is already correct and requires no change.

## Pilot convention decisions supported by the review

The following conventions are suitable to lock once the 3594 correction above is applied:

- retain **`Udanpirappē`** and **`With affection, M.K.`**;
- translate thought/argument order before stylistic elegance;
- preserve source-supplied English verbatim, including unusual capitalization or punctuation when it is archival source text;
- if the source prints both an English passage and a Tamil rendering/explanation, represent **both**, even when substantively repetitive;
- keep quotations visibly attributed and do not replace them with outside versions;
- preserve Indian public-language units such as lakh/crore and source-specific legal/land measurements rather than silently converting them;
- preserve Kalaignar's rhetorical questions, sarcasm, proverbs and repeated argumentative turns;
- preserve source text printed after a formal closing/date when it is visibly part of the source record;
- do not repair source numbering anomalies in English.
