# 3716. Let Us See the Tamils Here Unite!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This translation is intended to carry Kalaignar's voice into clear, contemporary English rather than recast the letter as literary or academic prose. It preserves the source's argument, political directness, rhetorical questions, repetition, irony, factual detail, and paragraph order. Names, dates, figures, quotations, and intentional English expressions are retained. Where Tamil idiom cannot be reproduced literally without sounding unnatural, the English follows its sense and rhetorical force without adding claims absent from the source. The original Tamil is reproduced in full below the translation and remains the authoritative text. `Udanpirappē` is retained in Tamil transliteration rather than flattened into “brother,” “sister,” or “comrade.” Literally evoking “one born alongside me,” Kalaignar uses it as a distinctive address of shared identity, equality, affection, and solidarity within the movement.

**Date:** 9 March 2013

**Udanpirappē,**

A general strike in Tamil Nadu on 12 March 2013! This is the appeal of TESO.

Everything carried out in Sri Lanka by Sinhala-majoritarian President Rajapaksa, the letter argues, amounted to genocide of the Tamil people; the acts he committed one after another were war crimes; what he violated, after crushing his own conscience, were human rights. Therefore he must be declared an international war criminal.

There must be a credible, independent international investigation into the war crimes said to have been committed in Sri Lanka.

Rajapaksa must answer to the nations of the world under international law, the letter argues, for atrocities without precedent in history—including the shooting and killing of Balachandran, the twelve-year-old son of Liberation Tigers leader Prabhakaran, and the killing of many thousands of children, women and elderly people.

The Government of India must announce without even a moment's delay that it wholeheartedly supports the resolution being brought before the U.N. Human Rights Council condemning the genocide alleged to have taken place in Sri Lanka; and the Government of India itself must take forward an appropriate resolution for a referendum to protect the Eelam Tamils.

For these demands, I appeal to everyone to participate in a one-day strike—beyond party differences, without departing from democratic principles, peacefully and through the path of moral protest—with the understanding that this is a fundamental duty we must carry out for the Eelam Tamils, our own kin bound to us by the umbilical cord.

Some people may face practical inconvenience because of this strike. I ask them affectionately to bear with it.

Because this strike was announced on behalf of TESO, a few people have cast Tamil ethnic feeling out of their hearts and issued statements, with hostility, calling it some kind of diversionary act—perhaps fearing that someone might become angry if they failed to issue an opposing statement, perhaps fearing some resulting harm, or perhaps with some other motive. Yet this strike is wholly and entirely an expression of support by mother Tamil Nadu for Eelam Tamils who are suffering through unending hardship. I ask that it be made a success with that understanding.

Some “avaal” newspapers, anxious that Tamils here might unite over the Eelam Tamil issue and that the DMK might thereby receive credit, have already begun satisfying themselves by publishing stories of their own saying that the general strike will not succeed and that other parties do not support it!

If some shop remains open without joining the strike, or some autorickshaw driver drives his vehicle on the road, or an industrial establishment decides that its profit for a single day matters more and stays open, then all of them would be showing either that they have no deep concern for our Eelam Tamils or that they have not fully understood the sufferings and problems of the Eelam Tamils.

For the sole “crime” of having been born Prabhakaran's son, Balachandran—a tender child who should have been going to school, still at an age when he could not even understand that he was about to be killed—was struck in the chest by five bullets. After seeing that blood-chilling cruelty, can anyone still regard this general strike as a diversion or something unnecessary?

What are the terrible images filmed and produced amid so many dangers by the London-based Channel 4 television organisation? One image shows Balachandran with a piece of bread between hand and mouth; the next shows him lying dead after bullets struck his chest. A mother and child lie fallen while holding one another. Innocent women are stripped and shot dead while naked. Colonel Ramesh, one of the Liberation Tigers' commanders, lies dead with his face disfigured. The young woman Isaipriya, described as a female Tiger, is sexually abused and killed by the Sinhala military. Men are stripped, blindfolded, their hands tied behind their backs, and shot in the back. After seeing all these heart-wrenching images, if we do not even take part in this one-day strike, would we not be bowing our heads and accepting the cruelties of those Sinhala thugs?

After seeing Tamils killed in groups by bombs dropped even on areas declared safety zones and on hospitals; after seeing brutal attempts to destroy the roots of the Tamil people's cultural features and to erase their geographically rooted way of life; after seeing schools, temples, mosques and churches of Eelam Tamils demolished, Tamil linguistic identity damaged, hundreds of Tamil place names changed, and what the letter describes as Sinhalisation and occupation continuing in Sri Lanka—after seeing all this, is it not against one's conscience to say that a strike held to condemn these things is unnecessary or a diversion?

When Parliament discussed the Eelam Tamils yesterday, parties such as the BJP, joining their voices with that of Thambi T.R. Baalu, leader of the DMK parliamentary party, recognised the gravity of the issue and came forward on their own to speak for our people. Since the Union Government's reply did not give the degree of assurance we expected, and further pressure is necessary, is it not our duty to cooperate with a one-day strike?

For many years Tamil Nadu fishermen have repeatedly been subjected to cruelty by the Sri Lankan Navy. They are shot and killed; detained; their fishing equipment is thrown into the sea; the fish they catch are seized; and they are treated inhumanely. Even yesterday, Tamil Nadu fishermen were fired upon with guns at three places in a single day. Each time, fishermen are distressed and shed tears, undertake strikes, every political party in Tamil Nadu issues statements for them, the State government writes to the Union Government, and the Union Government immediately contacts Sri Lanka and tries to secure the fishermen's release. This has become a long-running serial. Should not everyone participate in this general strike with the feeling that supporting it is a duty and with the hope that a permanent solution may be found for their long-standing problem?

We wanted the Eelam Tamils to be protected; we did not want to display a fratricidal quarrel among ourselves on that issue; and we believed that the Sinhala government would tremble if there were unity among us. That is why, even when TESO passed the resolution, we announced that this would be a strike in which all Tamils here would come together for the Eelam Tamils, without party bias or political hostility.

Even before the AIADMK had uttered a single word opposing that resolution, a few people issued statements calling the strike an attempt at diversion, seemingly thinking that if only they raised a voice against our resolution, it would give someone a pleasant “cooling” satisfaction. What will Tamils around the world and Eelam Tamils think on reading such statements? Will they not remember how, in the past, these same people wore masks of affection and friendship toward us, and wonder how they now forget themselves and lower their standards merely because they want one or two electoral seats?

Let them go their way. We need not worry about them. As far as we are concerned, our position is clear. Except for a few, people from other organisations have been contacting us and saying that they will extend their full cooperation to the strike. I praise their feeling for the Tamil people and convey my thanks to them all.

To members of the public who may be affected for one day because of this strike, I ask you to accept it with the understanding that it is the smallest sacrifice we can make for people of our own community who are dying day after day in Sri Lanka.

During the strike, I request that everything from major industrial establishments to small businesses, large and small commercial establishments, and all shops remain closed. I ask that autorickshaws not run on the roads and that people in the film industry come forward to close their theatres and stop screenings for twelve hours that day.

Private and government buses too should stop. Why, I ask even the Government of India itself to come forward and announce that trains and aircraft will not operate in Tamil Nadu that day. I ask those responsible for the Tamil Nadu government to recognise that this strike is being held for our own people and declare a one-day, twelve-hour holiday for government offices in support of the general strike.

This is not merely a condemnation of the Sri Lankan government. It is a tearful tribute to Eelam Tamils who lived there and died, and an expression of support and embrace from mother Tamil Nadu for the Tamils still living there. With that understanding, I ask again and again that we come forward and firmly display our unity through this strike.

I make this appeal as the leader of the DMK, a movement accustomed since 1956 to conducting many forms of struggle for the Eelam Tamils, and as the leader of TESO—to all political-party leaders and members in Tamil Nadu, to all members of the public, to every section of society, to brothers and sisters in allied parties, and to DMK Udanpirappus.

March 12—from six in the morning until six in the evening! Twelve hours! If it becomes known that all Tamil Nadu came together and stood firmly in the strike, Rajapaksa will tremble. Treat this strike as an opportunity for that and take part with enthusiasm. As always, essential services such as hospitals and newspaper offices are exempt; and because this is examination season, schools and colleges are also exempt.

**Even after the death of Balachandran,**  
**the child sacrificed to the murderous frenzy of sinners,**

**those who seek to stain this strike—**

**those who seek to divert it and warm themselves by the fire—**

**whoever they may be, the Tamil people will not tolerate them!**

**The Tamil people will understand who they are!**

Let us carry to the suffering Eelam Tamils, as consolation, the news: **the general strike is a success, a success!** Let the conch resound! Let us sound the conch with the words: **“Our enemies hid themselves somewhere, having seen the Tamils here unite.”**

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**9 March 2013**

## Letter-specific notes

1. **TESO** is retained for the Tamil Eelam Supporters Organisation. `அறவழி / அறப்போராட்டம்` is rendered as moral or peaceful political protest according to context.
2. The source's allegations concerning wartime killings, sexual violence, Sinhalisation and occupation remain attributed to Kalaignar and the material he cites; this archival translation does not independently verify them.
3. `அவாள்` is retained as **“avaal”** in quotation marks. It is a source-specific colloquial political label whose force is better preserved than normalised into an added sociological explanation.
4. The concluding line about enemies disappearing on seeing Tamils unite is translated as the source presents the slogan/song-like quotation; no external attribution is added.

இந்த வேலை நிறுத்தம் காரணமாக ஒரு நாள் பாதிப்புக்கு ஆளாகக் கூடிய பொதுமக்களிடம், நம்மினத்தவர் இலங்கையிலே நாளும் நாளும் செத்துக் கொண்டிருக்கிறார்களே, அவர்களுக்காக நாம் செய்கின்ற எள்ளளவு தியாகம் என்ற உணர்வோடு, அதனை ஏற்றுக் கொள்ள முன்வர வேண்டுமென்று கேட்டுக்கொள்கிறேன்.

இந்த வேலை நிறுத்தத்தின்போது, பெரிய தொழில் நிறுவனங்கள் முதல் சிறிய சிறிய நிறுவனங்கள் - பெரிய, சிறிய வர்த்தக நிறுவனங்கள் - கடைகள் அனைத்தும் மூடப்பட்டிருக்க வேண்டுமெனக் கேட்டுக்கொள்கிறேன். சாலைகளிலே ஆட்டோக்கள் ஓடக் கூடாது என்றும், திரைப்படத் துறையைச் சார்ந்தவர்கள் அன்று ஒரு நாள் பன்னிரண்டு மணிநேரம் தங்கள் தியேட்டர்களை மூடி, காட்சிகளை நிறுத்த முன்வரவும் வேண்டுகிறேன்.

தனியார் மற்றும் அரசுப் பேருந்துகள்; ஏன் இந்திய அரசே முன் வந்து அன்றையதினம் புகைவண்டிகள் மற்றும் விமானங்கள் தமிழகத்திலே ஓடாது என்று அறிவித்திட வேண்டும்; தமிழக அரசின் பொறுப்பிலே இருப்போரும், இந்த வேலை நிறுத்தம் என்பது நம்முடைய இன மக்களுக்காக நடைபெறுகின்ற ஒன்று என்ற உணர்வோடு அரசு அலுவலகங்களையெல்லாம் அன்று ஒரு நாள்-12 மணி நேரம், இந்தப் பொது வேலை நிறுத்தத்திற்காக விடுமுறைவிட வேண்டுமென்று கேட்டுக்கொள்கிறேன்.

இது இலங்கை அரசுக்கான கண்டனம் மாத்திரமல்ல; அங்கே வாழ்ந்து மறைந்த ஈழத் தமிழர்களுக்காகக் கண்ணீர் அஞ்சலியுடன், இன்னமும் வாழ்ந்து கொண்டிருக்கின்ற தமிழர்களுக்காக தாய்த் தமிழகம் காட்டுகின்ற ஆதரவு - அரவணைப்பு என்ற உணர்வோடு, இந்த வேலை நிறுத்தத்தில் நம்முடைய ஒற்றுமையை உறுதியாக வெளிப்படுத்திட முன்வர வேண்டுமென்று மீண்டும் மீண்டும் கேட்டுக் கொள்கிறேன்.

ஈழத் தமிழர்களுக்காக எத்தனையோ வகைப் போராட்டங்களை 1956ஆம் ஆண்டிலிருந்து நடத்திப் பழக்கப்பட்ட தி.மு. கழகத்தின் தலைவன் என்ற முறையிலும், “டெசோ” இயக்கத்தின் தலைவன் என்ற முறையிலும், தமிழகத்திலே உள்ள அனைத்துக் கட்சித் தலைவர்களுக்கும், கட்சியினருக்கும், அனைத்துப் பொதுமக்களுக்கும், சமுதாயத்தின்

எல்லாப் பிரிவினர்க்கும், தோழமைக் கட்சிகளின் சோதரர்க்கும், கழக உடன்பிறப்புக்களுக்கும் நான் விடுக்கின்ற வேண்டுகோள் இது!

மார்ச் 12 - காலை 6 மணி முதல் மாலை 6 மணி வரை! 12 மணிநேரம்! தமிழகமே ஒன்று திரண்டு வேலை நிறுத்தத்தில் அழுத்தமாக நின்றது என்று தெரிந்தாலே ராஜபக்சே நடுங்குவார். அதற்கொரு வாய்ப்பு என்ற முறையில் இந்த வேலை நிறுத்தத்தில் அனைவரும் ஆர்வத்தோடு பங்கேற்பீர்! எப்போதும்போல இந்த வேலை நிறுத்தத்தில் மருத்துவமனைகள், பத்திரிகை அலுவலகங்கள் போன்ற அத்தியாவசியச் சேவைகளுக்கும்; இது தேர்வு நேரம் என்பதால் பள்ளிகள், கல்லூரிகளுக்கும்; விதி விலக்கு உண்டு.

**பாவிகளின் கொலைவெறிக்கு பலியான பாலகன்**  
**பாலச்சந்திரனின் மரணத்திற்குப் பிறகும்,**

**இந்த வேலை நிறுத்தத்தைக் களங்கப்படுத்த**  
**நினைப்போர் -**

**திசை திருப்பிக் குளிர்காய எண்ணுவோர் -**

**எவராயினும், தமிழ் இனம் சகித்துக் கொள்ளாது!**

**அவர்கள் யார் என்று புரிந்துகொள்ளும்!**

பொது வேலை நிறுத்தம் வெற்றி வெற்றி என்ற செய்தியை, இன்னல்களுக்கு ஆளாகியிருக்கும் ஈழத் தமிழர்களுக்கு ஆறுதலாக வழங்குவோம்! சங்கம் முழங்கிடுவோம்! “எங்கள் பகைவர் எங்கோ மறைந்தார்; இங்குள்ள தமிழர் ஒன்றாதல் கண்டே” என்று சங்கம் முழங்கிடுவோம்!
