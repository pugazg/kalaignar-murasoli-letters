# 3735. Couldn’t a Statement Be Read for This Too?

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This translation is intended to carry Kalaignar's voice into clear, contemporary English rather than recast the letter as literary or academic prose. It preserves the source's argument, political directness, rhetorical questions, repetition, irony, factual detail, and paragraph order. Names, dates, figures, quotations, and intentional English expressions are retained. Where Tamil idiom cannot be reproduced literally without sounding unnatural, the English follows its sense and rhetorical force without adding claims absent from the source. The original Tamil is reproduced in full below the translation and remains the authoritative text. `Udanpirappē` is retained in Tamil transliteration rather than flattened into “brother,” “sister,” or “comrade.” Literally evoking “one born alongside me,” Kalaignar uses it as a distinctive address of shared identity, equality, affection, and solidarity within the movement.

**Udanpirappē,**

In Pudukkottai district, there are mechanised-boat fishing bases at Kottaipattinam and Jegathapattinam. Just as thousands of fishermen go out to sea from these bases in hundreds of mechanised boats and return after fishing, the day before yesterday 184 mechanised boats set out from there for fishing.

The fishermen who had gone in 182 of those boats returned to shore yesterday morning, but two mechanised boats belonging to Veeran and Vadivazhagan did not return. Since the nine fishermen who had gone in those boats also failed to return, all the fishermen of that area were plunged into distress. When they returned yesterday evening, the fishermen tearfully said that the Sri Lankan Navy had intercepted them and beaten them indiscriminately with rods, pipes, bolts, spanners and other objects; that the glass panes, GPS equipment and wireless phones in the two boats had been smashed; that all their mobile phones had been seized; and that fish worth one and a half lakh rupees which they had caught and kept in the boats had been taken and thrown into the sea.

They also said that five fishing nets had been cut and thrown into the sea, that those nets were worth two and a half lakh rupees, and that only after subjecting them to this suffering were they released. This is not merely a one-day incident.

Fishermen from the Karaikal area were fishing near Velankanni, within Indian waters, when a clash occurred in mid-sea between our fishermen and Sri Lankan fishermen. The Sri Lankan fishermen immediately informed the Sri Lankan Navy. The Sri Lankan Navy rushed there, arrested 46 Indian fishermen and took them to Sri Lanka along with five boats. When fishermen from Nagapattinam district informed the Indian Navy, the Navy intervened and brought the Indian fishermen back.

On 12 April 2013, photographs appeared in the newspapers showing eight Nagapattinam fishermen who had suffered serious injuries in an attack by the Sri Lankan Navy. In recent times, attacks and arrests of Tamil Nadu fishermen by Sri Lankan fishermen and the Sri Lankan Navy have thus become a continuing story. Day after day, the situation continues as though there is no dawn at all for the suffering of Tamil Nadu's fishermen.

From 1 January 1991 to 1996, 38 fishermen died and 99 were injured in 79 firing incidents carried out by the Sri Lankan Navy. From 1996 to 2001, 34 fishermen died and 68 were injured in 58 firing incidents. From 2001 to 2006, seven fishermen died and 21 were injured in 17 firing incidents. In the 26 firing incidents that took place from 13 May 2006 up to the present, eight fishermen died and 22 were injured.

Thus, from 1991 to 21 December 2006, in a total of 180 firing incidents carried out by the Sri Lankan Navy against Tamil Nadu fishermen, 87 Tamil Nadu fishermen lost their lives and 210 were injured.

In various incidents, 175 fishing boats belonging to Tamil Nadu fishermen were seized and taken away by the Sri Lankan Navy.

Statistics say that, so far, more than 600 Tamil Nadu fishermen have been killed by the Sri Lankan Navy. The cruelties continuously inflicted on fishermen, and the anguish that their families continue to endure even today, are beyond what one can finish writing about.

During the DMK government, the Tamil Nadu Fishermen Welfare Board was established in 2007 to look after the welfare of fishermen. According to statistics taken up to 1 March 2011, 385,756 fishermen had been enrolled as members of the Board, and by then relief amounting to ₹4 crore 52 lakh had been provided to 9,452 members through various welfare schemes.

A scheme to build free houses for fishermen was decided upon during the DMK government, and ₹7 crore 40 lakh was sanctioned in the first phase to build 2,000 houses. Again, in 2010–2011, under the “M. Singaravelar Memorial Housing Scheme”, a total of 2,949 houses were sanctioned by the government at a unit cost of ₹75,000, amounting to ₹22 crore 12 lakh, and work orders were also issued to the beneficiaries to begin construction of the houses.

Taking a special interest in fishermen's welfare, the DMK government also introduced and implemented a scheme to provide financial assistance to the families of fishermen who go missing while at sea, as well as a scheme to provide financial assistance to fishing families during periods when fishing is prohibited at sea.

That fishing-ban period begins from today. A study conducted by the Union Ministry of Environment found that the use of prohibited nets and mechanised boats was reducing marine resources. Taking this into account, breeding seasons were identified, and it was decided that fish stocks would increase if fishing were prohibited during those days. Accordingly, the period from 15 April to 29 May—45 days—has been declared the “fishing-ban season” and observed for the past several years. In continuation of that practice, this year's fishing-ban season begins from today.

With the intention of providing relief to fishermen during the fishing-ban season, a scheme was begun during the DMK government. The relief amount has gradually been increased and is now ₹2,000. Fishermen have made a deeply felt appeal to this government that, given today's cost of living, this is a very small amount and should be increased.

That demand is entirely reasonable. In present living conditions, for 45 days—taking even a minimum of ₹100 a day as the basis—this government should come forward to provide ₹4,500 to every fisherman. It is difficult even to imagine running a family on ₹4,500. Even so, recognising this reality, I strongly urge, on behalf of the DMK, that the Tamil Nadu government immediately provide this assistance to fishermen.

The Tamil Nadu Chief Minister, who reads statements under Rule 110 for all manner of things, may perhaps tomorrow read an announcement—and a statement—for this too, to help wipe away the tears of fishermen. Let us hope so.

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**16 April 2013**

## Letter-specific notes

1. **Udanpirappē** is retained as Kalaignar's characteristic political-familial address. See the [glossary](../GLOSSARY.md).
2. The source itself gives `13-5-2006 முதல் இதுவரை` (“from 13 May 2006 up to the present”) for one set of firing figures, but then gives the aggregate period as `1991 முதல் 21-12-2006 வரை` (“from 1991 to 21 December 2006”). The translation preserves both source forms without reconciling them.
3. **Rule 110** is retained as the Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly procedural reference used in the source.
4. `lakh` and `crore` are retained as Indian public-language numbering units.

செயல்படுத்தி வந்தது.

தொடங்குகிறது. மத்திய சுற்றுச்சூழல் அமைச்சகம் ஆய்வு
நடத்தியதில், தடை செய்யப்பட்ட வலைகள், இயந்திரம்
பொருத்திய படகுகள் பயன்பாடு காரணமாகக் கடல் உயிரின

அதிகரிக்கும் என்பதால் இந்த ஏப்ரல் 15 முதல் மே 29ஆம்
தேதிவரை 45 நாட்களுக்கு “மீன்பிடித் தடைக் காலம்” என்று
அறிவித்து, ஒருசில ஆண்டுகளாக அது கடைப்பிடிக்கப்பட்டு
வருவதின் தொடர்ச்சியாக இந்த ஆண்டு இன்று முதல் மீன் பிடித்
தடைக்காலம்.
இந்த மீன் பிடித் தடைக்காலத்தில் மீனவர்களுக்கு
நிவாரணத் தொகை வழங்க வேண்டுமென்ற எண்ணத்தோடு
கழக ஆட்சிக் காலத்தில் ஒரு திட்டம் தொடங்கப்பட்டு, அந்த
நிவாரணத் தொகை படிப்படியாக உயர்ந்து தற்போது 2000 ரூபாய்
அளவிற்கு வழங்கப்படுவது தற்போதுள்ள விலைவாசி நிலையில்
மிகவும் குறைவான தொகை என்றும், இந்தத் தொகையை
உயர்த்தித்தர வேண்டுமென்றும் மீனவர்கள் மிகவும் உருக்கமாக ஒரு கோரிக்கையினை இந்த அரசுக்கு விடுத்திருக்கிறார்கள்.
இந்தக் கோரிக்கை மிகவும் நியாயமான கோரிக்கை. தற்போதுள்ள
வாழ்க்கை நிலையில் 45 நாட்களுக்கு - நாள் ஒன்றுக்குக் குறைந்த
பட்சம் 100 ரூபாய் என்ற அளவிலாவது கணக்கில் எடுத்துக்
கொண்டு - ஒவ்வொரு மீனவருக்கும் 4,500 ரூபாயினை -
வழங்கிட இந்த அரசு முன்வர வேண்டும். இந்த 4,500 ரூபாயைக்
கொண்டு குடும்பம் நடத்துவதென்பதை எண்ணிக்கூடப் பார்க்க
இயலாது. இருந்தாலும், இந்த உண்மை நிலையை உணர்ந்து
தமிழக அரசு உடனடியாக மீனவர்களுக்கு இந்த உதவியினை
நிச்சயம் நிறைவேற்றித்தர வேண்டுமென்று நான் கழகத்தின்
சார்பில் வலியுறுத்துகின்றேன்.
