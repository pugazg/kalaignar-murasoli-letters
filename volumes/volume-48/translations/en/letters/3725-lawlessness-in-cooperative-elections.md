# 3725. Lawlessness in the Cooperative Elections!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This translation is intended to carry Kalaignar's voice into clear, contemporary English rather than recast the letter as literary or academic prose. It preserves the source's argument, political directness, rhetorical questions, repetition, irony, factual detail, and paragraph order. Names, dates, figures, quotations, and intentional English expressions are retained. Where Tamil idiom cannot be reproduced literally without sounding unnatural, the English follows its sense and rhetorical force without adding claims absent from the source. The original Tamil is reproduced in full below the translation and remains the authoritative text. `Udanpirappē` is retained in Tamil transliteration rather than flattened into “brother,” “sister,” or “comrade.” Literally evoking “one born alongside me,” Kalaignar uses it as a distinctive address of shared identity, equality, affection, and solidarity within the movement.

**Date:** 31 March 2013

**Udanpirappē,**

In Tamil Nadu, all thirty-one districts apart from Chennai have been declared drought-affected. Yet in the Delta districts relief has been announced only for farmers whose yield loss exceeds 50 per cent, and even that at only ₹15,000 per acre—an amount the Chief Minister appears to regard as generous. Delta farmers have been protesting that this is inadequate. They expected the full relief package to be announced when the Chief Minister replied to the Budget debate and were waiting because even that concession had to be announced personally by the Chief Minister. Without concern for any of this, the Tamil Nadu government has announced elections to cooperative societies in five phases beginning on 5 April.

The AIADMK government brought an amendment to the cooperative law with the underlying objective, the letter argues, of enrolling AIADMK members in the State's cooperative societies. Under that amendment, when cooperative societies are under the administration of special officers, new members can be admitted through the “general body.” Without thinking deeply about anything, those in power planned to enrol only their own party members and conduct cooperative elections like an internal party election. The very haste with which they moved to hold cooperative-society elections in Tamil Nadu made it clear, the letter argues, how extensively they intended to commit irregularities and capture the cooperative institutions in violation of democratic norms.

The DMK therefore decided and announced that it would boycott cooperative-society elections conducted in a manner hostile to democracy and leaving no room for fairness or justice. In proof, the letter says, of how justified that announcement was, here are some examples from newspaper reports over the past few days showing how the Tamil Nadu government has been conducting these elections:

❖ In the election for the Salem District Women Child-Welfare Organisers and Assistants Cooperative Thrift and Credit Society, seven people filed nominations on behalf of the AIADMK. Nine filed nominations on behalf of the Tamil Nadu Anganwadi Workers and Assistants Association, affiliated with CITU. Yet a notice was pasted on the society's notice board saying that all those who had filed nominations on behalf of the AIADMK had been elected unanimously and unopposed.

❖ In Theni district, Marxist party area secretary G.M. Nagarajan went to complain after nomination forms were not provided for contesting the K. Puthupatti Primary Agricultural Cooperative Bank election. He was brutally attacked, the source reports, by AIADMK members. Seriously injured, he was receiving treatment at a government hospital.

❖ In a statement, CPI(M) Thanjavur district secretary Ko. Neelamegam said that although 29 March had been announced as nomination day for the first phase of elections to Primary Agricultural Cooperative Banks in rural areas, most of those bank offices were not opened at all that day. Even where some offices opened, officials refused to show the electoral rolls, refused to provide nomination forms, and said there were oral instructions from above to accept only those nomination papers they were told to receive.

❖ At the society in Vikramam village in Madukkur Union, a person who went to file a nomination was allegedly told to go and see the ruling party's union secretary.

❖ Opportunities to contest were denied at societies in Madukkur, Mandalakottai, Irangadu and Veppankulam.

❖ At the Devanancheri, Cholapuram and Tiruppurambiyam societies in Kumbakonam Union, officials refused to issue nomination forms, while ruling-party members gathered in groups and created disputes. Cooperative officials said they would give nomination forms after noon and then locked the office.

❖ At the Patharankottai society in Thiruvonam Union too, nomination forms were refused.

❖ The letter reports allegations that ruling-party members, with the assistance of officials, were attempting to conduct fraudulent elections in Primary Agricultural Cooperative Banks.

❖ Nutrition-meal workers accused Karur district AIADMK functionaries of lawlessness in the election to the Karur District All Nutrition-Meal Workers Cooperative Thrift and Credit Society.

❖ At the society office in Thanthonimalai, when people asked for nomination forms, two AIADMK functionaries sitting there allegedly said that nobody should be given a form unless they themselves first checked by phone and gave permission. Officials too reportedly refused to issue the forms.

❖ At the Samayanallur Primary Agricultural Cooperative Society in Madurai district, nomination forms were reportedly not issued until 11 a.m. on Friday. When CPI(M) members asked for forms, officials refused. A road-blockade protest followed. Police arrived and said they would obtain the forms, after which the blockade was withdrawn. But when candidates then went to submit nominations, more than 300 AIADMK members gathered at the cooperative-society office and prevented them from filing. A nomination submitted by T. Ramamoorthy was allegedly snatched away by the younger brother of a ruling-party member named Malayalam. The police, the source says, simply watched these acts.

❖ A protest took place in the Vellore CMC cooperative-society election after 240 workers were deprived of voting rights.

❖ P. Saravanan works as a bookbinder at the Government Public Library in Dharmapuri. He is visually impaired, serves as a district executive leader of his association and is a leading worker in the Tamil Nadu Government Employees Association. He has been a member of the local Public Libraries Department Cooperative Thrift and Credit Society for the past twenty years and was elected a director in the 2008 cooperative election. He filed a nomination on 29 March 2013 to contest again as a director. Election officer Loganathan initially refused to receive his nomination and allegedly threatened that because he was blind and could not read or write, he could not contest and his nomination would be rejected. After strong protests, the nomination was eventually accepted reluctantly and after delay.

Udanpirappē, these are only the reports published in the newspapers about incidents across Tamil Nadu on the very first day nominations began.

It was precisely because the DMK foresaw and understood that events of this kind would take place that it boycotted the cooperative-society elections being conducted by the AIADMK government in Tamil Nadu. Tamil Nadu is yet to see what further spectacles the AIADMK government and ruling party will stage in these elections!

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**31 March 2013**

## Letter-specific notes

1. The specific allegations of assault, obstruction, nomination denial and official collusion are translated as newspaper reports and political allegations collected by Kalaignar; this archival translation does not independently establish them.
2. **Primary Agricultural Cooperative Bank/Society**, **Cooperative Thrift and Credit Society**, **CITU**, and the quoted institutional names follow the level of specificity in the source.
3. `அராஜகம்` is rendered **lawlessness** in the title and body, preserving the source's political accusation without converting it into a legal finding.
4. The source's description of P. Saravanan's visual impairment and the alleged reason given for rejecting his nomination is preserved without normalising or rewriting it.

❖ கரூர் மாவட்ட அனைத்துச் சத்துணவுப் பணியாளர்கள் கூட்டுறவு சிக்கன மற்றும் நாணய சங்கத் தேர்தலில் கரூர் மாவட்ட அ.தி.மு.க. நிர்வாகிகள் அராஜகத்தில் ஈடுபடுவதாகச் சத்துணவுப் பணியாளர்கள் குற்றஞ்சாட்டி உள்ளனர்.

❖ மதுரை மாவட்டம், சமயநல்லூர் தொடக்க வேளாண்மைக் கூட்டுறவு சங்கத்தில் வெள்ளியன்று காலை 11 மணிவரை, வேட்புமனு கொடுக்கப்படவில்லையாம். மார்க்சிஸ்ட் கம்யூனிஸ்ட் கட்சியின் சார்பில் வேட்பு மனுக்களைக் கேட்டதற்கு அதிகாரிகள் தர மறுத்து விட்டார்களாம். இதைத் தொடர்ந்து கட்சியின் சார்பில் சாலை மறியல் போராட்டமும் நடைபெற்றுள்ளது. காவல் துறையினர் அங்கே வந்து, தாங்கள் வேட்பு மனுக்களைப் பெற்றுத் தருவதாகக் கூறியதைத் தொடர்ந்து மறியல் கைவிடப்பட்டுள்ளது. ஆனால் மனுக்களைத் தாக்கல் செய்யச் சென்றபோது, கூட்டுறவுச் சங்க அலுவலகத்தில் கூடியிருந்த 300க்கும் மேற்பட்ட அ.தி.மு.க.வினர் மனுக்களைத் தாக்கல் செய்ய விடாமல் தடுத்திருக்கிறார்கள். து.ராமமூர்த்தி என்பவர் கொடுத்த மனுவை, ஆளுங்கட்சியைச் சேர்ந்த மலையாளம் என்பவரின் தம்பி பறித்துக்கொண்டு போய்விட்டாராம். இந்த அராஜகங்களையெல்லாம் காவல் துறையினர் வேடிக்கை பார்த்துக் கொண்டிருந்தார்களாம்.

விளங்குபவர். பல்வேறு பொதுச்சேவைகளிலும் ஈடுபட்டு வரும் இவர், அங்குள்ள பொது நூலகத் துறை கூட்டுறவு சிக்கனம் மற்றும் கடன் சங்கத்தில் கடந்த இருபதாண்டுகளாக உறுப்பினராக இருந்து வருகிறார். கடந்த 2008ஆம் ஆண்டு நடைபெற்ற கூட்டுறவு சங்கத் தேர்தலில் இயக்குநராகவும் தேர்ந்தெடுக்கப்பட்டவர். தற்போது நடைபெறவுள்ள கூட்டுறவு சங்கத் தேர்தலில் இயக்குநராகப் போட்டியிட 29-3-2013 அன்று வேட்பு மனுதாக்கல் செய்துள்ளார். தேர்தல் அதிகாரி, லோகநாதன் என்பவர் இவருடைய வேட்பு மனுவைப்பெற முதலில் மறுத்ததோடு, பார்வையற்றவர் எழுதப் படிக்க முடியாது என்பதால், போட்டியிட முடியாது எனவும் கூறி வேட்பு மனு தள்ளுபடி செய்யப்படும் என்றும் மிரட்டியிருக்கிறார். கடும் எதிர்ப்பிற்குப் பிறகு வேண்டா வெறுப்பாகக் காலதாமதம் செய்து வேட்பு மனுவை வாங்கிக்கொண்டிருக்கிறார்.
