# 3769. Some of the Charitable Work Carried Out by the DMK!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This translation is intended to carry Kalaignar's voice into clear, contemporary English rather than recast the letter as literary or academic prose. It preserves the source's argument, political directness, rhetorical questions, repetition, irony, factual detail, and paragraph order. Names, dates, figures, quotations, and intentional English expressions are retained. Where Tamil idiom cannot be reproduced literally without sounding unnatural, the English follows its sense and rhetorical force without adding claims absent from the source. The original Tamil is reproduced in full below the translation and remains the authoritative text. `Udanpirappē` is retained in Tamil transliteration rather than flattened into “brother,” “sister,” or “comrade.” Literally evoking “one born alongside me,” Kalaignar uses it as a distinctive address of shared identity, equality, affection, and solidarity within the movement.

**Udanpirappē,**

For the growth of the movement and for contesting elections, we raise party funds on occasions such as my birthdays, through conference donations and entrance fees, and through contributions from the district units, the Youth Wing, the Labour Wing and other bodies. Apart from using those funds for elections, we have made it a regular practice to assist the Government in providing relief to people affected whenever natural disasters occur in India or within the State. We also provide financial assistance for urgent needs such as education and medical treatment to Udanpirappugal in the party who have fallen into hardship. Though some may expect larger sums, we provide as much as possible on the principle that assistance must reach everyone broadly.

There are accounts for the money raised by the DMK. There are also accounts of who receives it and for what purpose. We publish these details from time to time through the newspapers.

On behalf of the DMK, assistance continues to be given to many groups: those in financial distress; those whose health is seriously affected; accident victims; and those harmed by repression or by violence committed by rival parties. Beyond this, the movement encourages students' education; gives awards to workers, particularly autorickshaw drivers; encourages those who recite the poems of the Revolutionary Poet Bharathidasan; presents awards and gold purses in the names of Periyar, Anna, the Revolutionary Poet and the President of the movement; and provides substantial sums through the *Murasoli* awards. It also continuously maintains the historical archive of the movement at Anna Arivalayam, efficiently runs the Vetriselvi Anbazhagan Eye Hospital, and operates the Perasiriyar Library as a major library and reading room. All these activities, together with the connected social work, continue to be carried out on behalf of the movement.

I have previously given some examples of the assistance provided by the movement, but I wish to recall them once again.

During the Emergency in 1976, the DMK provided monthly assistance to the families of those arrested under MISA who required support. Among those families was that of Sattur Balakrishnan, a MISA detainee who died in prison. The monthly assistance of ₹200 paid to his family at that time continued from 1976 until 2010. It was then raised to ₹1,000 and continues to be sent by money order.

From 1988 to the present, the DMK Charitable Trust has spent ₹44,95,525—about ₹45 lakh—on medicines, equipment and staff salaries at the Vetriselvi Anbazhagan Free Eye Hospital. Under the supervision of Dr Vasanthi, 70,656 people—about seventy thousand—have received free treatment there during this period, including eye surgery.

On 28 December 2004, one crore rupees was given to the Prime Minister's Relief Fund through Madam Sonia Gandhi as assistance for those affected by the tsunami that struck Tamil Nadu.

In response to my appeal, former and serving DMK Members of Parliament and Members of the Legislative Assembly, together with leading functionaries of the movement, contributed tsunami relief. A total of ₹1,97,51,000 was added to the Prime Minister's Fund as assistance during 2004–2005.

The DMK Charitable Trust also provided:

- ₹9,44,885 to 222 students who had secured high marks or wished to pursue higher education;
- ₹50,300 towards the medical expenses of eleven people;
- ₹1,56,650 as relief to families affected by fire and flood;
- ₹1,00,000 on 4 June 1987 as assistance to Eelam Tamils who had lost their homes and suffered without food because of military atrocities in Sri Lanka; and
- ₹25,00,000 on 3 November 1995 for medical assistance to affected Eelam Tamils.

A further total of ₹58,66,853 was provided towards the Little Flower Convent School in Chennai; the Sirkazhi Govindarajan Memorial Trust; Sivananda Gurukulam at Kattankulathur; assistance to the people of Cuba; Maharashtra earthquake relief; the Udavum Karangal service organisation; flood relief following damage in Tamil Nadu; assistance to the families of eleven soldiers who died in the Kargil war; and assistance to the families of two people who died after participating in the government employees' and teachers' agitation.

Apart from the DMK Charitable Trust, the DMK headquarters itself provided ₹1.39 crore in assistance during the twenty years from 1985 to 2005. This included ₹1,06,09,432 as family assistance to 370 comrades of the movement; ₹8,97,983 as medical assistance to 205 comrades; ₹6,97,500 as assistance to 358 speakers of the movement; ₹4,60,000 as gold purses to thirty-four recipients of the Mupperum Vizha awards; and ₹12,50,000 as assistance to the Tamil people of Eelam and as Gujarat earthquake relief.

From 2005 to the present, the DMK Charitable Trust has provided ₹98,79,005 for purposes including ₹10 lakh to relieve the suffering of people affected by rain and floods in Maharashtra; ₹10 lakh for Pakistan–Jammu and Kashmir earthquake relief; ₹25 lakh to assist people affected by floods in Tamil Nadu; and ₹50 lakh for those affected by Cyclone Thane in 2011.

From 2005 to the present, the headquarters of the movement has provided assistance to comrades and has also given ₹40 lakh to Anna's heirs to mark Perarignar Anna's centenary; ₹5 lakh to the Periyar Self-Respect Propaganda Institution in Delhi; ₹55 lakh to the legal heirs of Silambu Selvar M. P. Sivagnanam; and, most recently, ₹25 lakh to assist those affected by heavy rain in Uttarakhand. In all, ₹2,12,63,000 has been provided as assistance.

Just as aid is provided on behalf of the movement and the DMK Charitable Trust, the DMK Youth Wing Charitable Trust has, for the past several years, marked my birthday by giving awards each year to students who secure the first three places at State and district level in the Class 10 and Class 12 examinations. At State level, the first-place students receive ₹25,000 each, the second-place students ₹15,000 each, and the third-place students ₹10,000 each. At district level, the corresponding awards are ₹10,000, ₹5,000 and ₹3,000. This has continued for four years.

During those four years, the DMK Youth Wing Charitable Trust has provided ₹97,60,000 in assistance to 1,646 students. In the fifth year, the awards for the present year are to be distributed in Madurai on 29 June 2013 by Treasurer of the movement Thalapathi M. K. Stalin: ₹97,05,000 in total to 211 State-level high achievers and 1,364 district-level high achievers. Youth Wing members themselves take responsibility for bringing the students to the function, ensuring that they receive the awards and taking them back safely.

On behalf of the Tamil Nadu Government, cash prizes and certificates are given to those who study Tamil as a language subject and secure the first three places at State level in the Class 12 public examination, and likewise to the first three State-level students in the Class 10 public examination. The DMK Youth Wing Charitable Trust, however, gives cash prizes not only to the first three students at State level in both examinations, but also to the first three at district level.

Even the Government's distribution of these awards fell into confusion. After *Dinamalar* reported it and I too wrote about it, the Chief Minister directly intervened, called back to Chennai the students who had returned home without receiving their prizes, and personally presented the awards. This year, the Government has given awards to only about two hundred students. The DMK Youth Wing, however, will give cash awards to about 1,500 students in Madurai on the 29th. We must therefore allow no confusion of any kind and ensure that the students receive their awards and return with happiness.

The reason so many students are receiving these prizes this year is the Samacheer Kalvi system—a Uniform System of School Education with a long history, upheld through court judgments. In the 2013 Class 10 examination, large numbers of students from areas inhabited by people who had been denied educational opportunity for many years succeeded. Since Samacheer Kalvi was implemented in Class 10 in government schools, a greater number of students have passed.

The pass percentage in 2013 was 89 per cent, 2.8 percentage points higher than in 2012. Eighty-six per cent of male students and 92 per cent of female students passed. Nine students achieved 498 marks out of 500. Fifty-two students scored 497 and 137 scored 496. In this way, the Samacheer Kalvi system introduced by the DMK Government has begun to produce fundamental changes in society. That is why, in this single year, the DMK Youth Wing is going to distribute to the students a sum equal to the entire amount of awards distributed during the previous four years.

The DMK Youth Wing Charitable Trust also carries out another praiseworthy activity. Beginning with Perarignar Anna's centenary and continuing every year on his birthday, it conducts speech, essay-writing and poetry-recitation competitions for high-school and higher-secondary students and gives prizes at State and district level. Through these events, held continuously for five years, ₹1,53,60,000 has been given to 7,098 students.

This letter is written to explain that every rupee collected—whether on behalf of the movement or the Youth Wing—is properly spent on good causes. I congratulate the 1,500 students who are to receive prizes at the function in Madurai on the 29th. I ask other students too to apply themselves to education with dedication, so that they may become eligible to receive these awards in the years ahead.

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**28 June 2013**

## Letter-specific notes

1. **MISA** refers to India's Maintenance of Internal Security Act, used for detention during the Emergency.
2. **Mupperum Vizha** is the DMK's annual “three great celebrations” observance associated with Periyar's birthday, Anna's birthday and the founding of the DMK.
3. **Samacheer Kalvi** is Tamil Nadu's Uniform System of School Education. The Tamil name is retained because it identifies a specific policy and political history.
4. The Indian-numbering forms `lakh` and `crore` and the source's rupee figures are retained rather than converted into rounded international-numbering estimates.
