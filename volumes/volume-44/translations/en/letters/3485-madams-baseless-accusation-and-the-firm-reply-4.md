# 3485. Madam's Baseless Accusation and the Firm Reply! (4)

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This pilot translation follows the audited canonical Tamil directly. It preserves Kalaignar's argument order, political directness, sarcasm, rhetorical questions, repetitions, figures, names, dates, quotations, and intentionally printed English. **Udanpirappē,**

People say Jayalalithaa is always in a tearing hurry. That seems entirely right. Without asking whether a reply has come to the accusation she made, what that reply says if it has come, or whether what she herself said was correct — while my replies had been appearing continuously for two or three days — and even after I gave a lengthy explanation on the 16th itself to what she said in Coimbatore about the rise in prices, she has gone on lamenting that Karunanidhi did not open his mouth about prices and that therefore what she said had become true.

As examples of Tamil Nadu's development, I did not merely show how allocations to various departments had grown; I also pointed out how greatly the number of universities and colleges had increased. It seems Jayalalithaa, who only skims the surface, and the newspapers that support her have not read any of that! Jayalalithaa says that establishing new colleges, schools and districts is not an achievement. If she had started such institutions during her own rule, they could have listed them school by school and said, “We did more under the AIADMK government than under the DMK government.” When they have no such “masala,” what harm is there in issuing a statement?

The entire final part of the statement I issued on the 16th was about fertiliser. Yet yesterday Jayalalithaa said in her statement that I had not opened my mouth about fertiliser. What are we to think of her?

In her Coimbatore speech, Jayalalithaa also thundered about rice hoarding and smuggling under the DMK government. Under Jayalalithaa's rule, under the Essential Commodities Act, 1955, in the five years from 2001 to April 2006, there were a total of 34,308 cases, 3,313 arrests, 1,492 vehicle seizures, and action under preventive-detention law against 67 persons. In 2004, among all the cases registered, 251 people were arrested, but only one person was proceeded against under preventive detention for black-marketing. That was the extent to which Jayalalithaa's government failed to act against hoarders and smugglers and instead garlanded them.

But under the present DMK government, from 2006 through June 2010, there have been 58,807 cases, 13,311 arrests and 4,728 vehicle seizures. Action has also been taken to cancel the licences of 453 of those vehicles and to cancel 270 driving licences.

Speaking about online trading, Jayalalithaa has said that commodities such as rice, pulses and sugar are traded through it, that the DMK government has taken no action to control that danger, and that there are many reasons for protecting those traders. She does not even know the basic fact of which commodities are currently permitted for online trading. None of the three commodities she named is presently permitted to be sold through online trading. Not only that, Jayalalithaa has lied that I am somehow protecting those trades and that there must be some reason for it. The truth is that, objecting to online trading in essential food commodities, I officially informed the Honourable Prime Minister of India on 14.03.2007 itself. Only after that were all varieties of rice other than basmati rice, wheat, toor dal and urad dal removed, from 2007, from the list of commodities permitted for futures trading. Without knowing any of these details, Madam went to Coimbatore and lied in the style of “whether the mouth is sour or the mango is sour,” as if merely because someone had written it down and handed her the sheet.

Jayalalithaa also spoke in Coimbatore about expired medicines. Detailed replies on expired medicines had already been given in the Legislative Assembly itself; a case had been registered; the accused had been arrested; and the case was proceeding. It was this DMK government that discovered a gang which, from the period of Jayalalithaa's rule itself, had been changing the labels on expired medicines and sending them back for sale, and took action against them. Forty persons involved in this activity were arrested and imprisoned. Of them, 19 were detained under the Goondas Act. The case has been transferred to the Crime Branch-CID for further investigation. The drug licences of eight wholesale drug firms involved in the offence have been cancelled. The Director of Drug Control is taking action to cancel the licences of some more firms. In this situation, she has given the false information that many thousands of people died because of counterfeit and expired medicines.

In her speech, Jayalalithaa said that Tamil Nadu has 42,000 pharmacies and that the government has only 52 drug inspectors to inspect them. How many drug inspectors were there during Jayalalithaa's rule? Is she prepared to say whether this government has reduced their number?

Jayalalithaa says that I did not reply to the accusation she made against Stalin's son-in-law and that therefore I have accepted it. Amma is in a hurry again! Stalin's son-in-law himself has sent that lady a lawyer's notice over the allegation she made against him.

Jayalalithaa says I did not answer her on the electricity issue either. If she keeps grinding the same flour again and again, how many times are we to reply? The government has already given an explanation on the electricity issue. Her principal claim is that there was surplus electricity during her rule and there is a shortage now. It has already been stated categorically that the reason is that, under the present DMK government, industrial growth has expanded through the establishment of new factories, increasing electricity demand, and that steps have been taken to make up the resulting shortage.

Jayalalithaa spent a long time in her speech alleging sand robbery. It was during Jayalalithaa's own rule, in 2003, that the Tamil Nadu government announced and implemented the policy that the government itself would take over and operate sand quarries. The same system continues now. Forgetting how the Arumugasamys paid tribute through the sand trade during her own rule, she spoke in Coimbatore on the basis of an unsupported assumption that the same must be happening now!

Jayalalithaa said that this is the right moment to reckon up the government's failures and achievements, and that there are very few things that can be called achievements of this government. Let us list here only a few of the achievements of these four years. Even after that, will Jayalalithaa say that all these achievements are “illusions”?

Co-operative loan waiver; reduced crop-loan interest for farmers; crop-insurance scheme; 31 welfare boards for unorganised workers; free land for landless poor farmers; free house-site pattas; eggs three times a week with the nutritious meal for schoolchildren, and bananas; abolition of tuition fees for school and college students; establishment in Chennai of the Central Institute of Classical Tamil; increase of marriage-assistance funding from ₹10,000 to ₹25,000; ₹6,000 each for poor pregnant women; the “Kalaignar Insurance Scheme for Life-Saving High-End Treatment”; the 108 service scheme; 3.5 per cent separate internal reservation for the advancement of the Muslim community; 3 per cent separate internal reservation to address the deprivation of the Arunthathiyar community; the Samathuvapuram scheme; the Metro Rail project; the Hogenakkal drinking-water project; the Ramanathapuram combined drinking-water project; the Maduravoyal–Chennai Port elevated-road project; the “Kalaignar Housing Scheme” — are none of these schemes achievements in Jayalalithaa's dictionary? And was it not also an achievement that, after letting the looting demon loose for a little while, this demon, with cage and company, was made to remain shut inside the Kodanadu cave?

Jayalalithaa also spoke in Coimbatore about the World Classical Tamil Conference held there. She alleged that the conference was conducted only to collect money and that DMK members had collected ₹2,000 crore across Tamil Nadu for the conference. A decision had been taken that no money should be collected from any private person or institution for the conference held in Coimbatore, and the entire expenditure was met only through the government.

Jayalalithaa also says that, using the name of Tamil, I have filled my family's treasury. Whatever the subject, her mind runs only to collecting money, corruption and filling a treasury. She thinks as though I had filled a treasury with the 3 crore dollars that came from America — and as though I had somehow seized a Kodanadu Estate that belonged to someone else. If ₹2,000 crore was collected, and if Jayalalithaa is truly the Leader of the Opposition, is she prepared responsibly to prove from whom I collected that money and what evidence she has for it?

Jayalalithaa has said that I did not lift even a straw to implement the final award of the Cauvery Water Disputes Tribunal. After the Tribunal delivered its final report on 05.02.2007, and in accordance with the decisions taken at the all-party meetings held on 19.02.2007 and 05.04.2007, the Tamil Nadu government filed a petition before the Cauvery Tribunal seeking further clarification under Section 5(3) of the Inter-State River Water Disputes Act. It also filed a Special Leave Petition in the Supreme Court on 05.05.2007. The governments of Kerala and Karnataka too filed Special Leave Petitions in the Supreme Court challenging the Tribunal's final order.

Further, all the State governments and the Union Government filed petitions before the Tribunal under Section 5(3) of the Inter-State River Water Disputes Act seeking clarifications in the final order. On 10.07.2007, the Cauvery Tribunal said that, because the Cauvery case was then pending in the Supreme Court through Special Leave Petitions (SLPs), and because the Supreme Court had admitted those petitions and they remained pending there, the Section 5(3) clarification petitions filed before the Tribunal could be taken up only after the Supreme Court heard those petitions and passed orders.

Only after the Supreme Court petitions are heard and orders passed will the Tribunal hear the petitions filed under Section 5(3) of the Inter-State River Water Disputes Act and send a further report to the Union Government. Only after all these steps are completed will the Union Government publish the Tribunal's final order in the Gazette under Section 6(1) of the Inter-State River Water Disputes Act. That order will bind the basin States. This is the law presently in force. Until then, the Tribunal's interim order already obtained on 25.6.1991 remains in operation. When this case came up on 29.9.2009, the Supreme Court adjourned it to the first week of December 2009. But the case has not yet been taken up for hearing in the Supreme Court. For arguing the Cauvery Special Leave Petitions before the Supreme Court, Karnataka filed its documents in 25 volumes and Kerala in 15 volumes. After consultation with counsel, Tamil Nadu too filed 13 volumes containing the additional documents it required.

On 06.5.2010, when Tamil Nadu's lawyers brought the matter to the attention of the Chief Justice's Bench, the Court adjourned the case to the first week of August. Concealing all of this, Jayalalithaa says that I did not lift even a straw. The people of Tamil Nadu must weigh for themselves what kind of liar that makes her.

I wish to conclude by saying one final thing to show what kind of person Jayalalithaa is. Speaking about me — a man who has been Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu five times, who to this day continues to live in a street house (Street House), that is, a house joined to ten other houses, and in the very same house that I bought before becoming Chief Minister — she has said: “A man who himself said that he boarded a train from Tiruvarur without a ticket and without money in his shirt pocket, travelling as a stowaway, has earned all this through politics.” The people of Tamil Nadu must understand what that tells them about her.

I have never said that I came by boarding a train as a stowaway. Before I ever came to Chennai, I worked in Erode as an assistant editor in Thanthai Periyar's *Kudiyarasu* office; later, in Coimbatore and Salem, I entered the film world and earned by writing stories and dialogues for many films; only after that did I come to Chennai. Through the many films I wrote with my pen, and through the many books I produced, I earned money. From those earnings I have also continued to give funds for public causes. But just as Jayalalithaa asked me, if someone else were to look at Jayalalithaa and ask, “How did you earn so many assets? Could you have earned all these assets through acting alone?”, what answer would she give?

My final warning to her is this: “Heroine of *Vairam*, do not say everything that comes into your mouth!”

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**19-7-2010**

குற்றச்சாட்டும், ஆணித்தரமான பதிலும்! (4)

உடன்பிறப்பே,

ஜெயலலிதா எப்போதுமே ஒரு “அவசரக் குடுக்கை” என்பார்கள். அது சரியாகத்தான் இருக்கிறது. தான் சொன்ன குற்றச்சாட்டுக்கு பதில் வந்திருக்கிறதா? அப்படி பதில் வந்தால் என்ன பதில் வந்திருக்கிறது? தான் சொன்னது சரிதானா என்றெல்லாம் பார்க்காமல் - என்னுடைய பதில்கள் தொடர்ந்து இரண்டு மூன்று நாட்களாக வந்து கொண்டிருக்கும் நிலையில்; விலைவாசி உயர்வு பற்றி அவர் கோவையிலே பேசியதற்கு நீண்ட விளக்கத்தை நான் 16ஆம் தேதியே அளித்த பிறகும் - விலைவாசி பற்றி பேசியதற்கு கருணாநிதி வாய் திறக்கவில்லை, ஆகவே நான் கூறியது உண்மையாகி விட்டது என்றெல்லாம் புலம்பித் தள்ளியிருக்கிறார்.

தமிழ்நாடு வளர்ச்சி அடைந்தது என்பதற்கு உதாரணமாக பல்வேறு துறைகளுக்கான நிதி ஒதுக்கீடுகள் பெருகியிருப்பதை மட்டுமே நான் காட்டிடாமல், எந்த அளவிற்கு பல்கலைக் கழகங்கள், கல்லூரிகள் பெருகியிருக்கின்றன என்பதையும் எடுத்துக்காட்டியிருக்கிறேன். நுனிப்புல் மேயும் ஜெயலலிதாவும், அவரது ஆதரவு ஏடுகளும் அவைகளை யெல்லாம் படிக்கவில்லை போலும்! புதிய கல்லூரிகள், பள்ளிகள், மாவட்டங்கள் ஏற்படுத்துவது தெல்லாம் சாதனை அல்ல என்று சொல்லுகிறார் ஜெயலலிதா. அவரது ஆட்சிக் காலத்தில் அவர் இவ்வாறு தொடங்கியிருந்தால், அதனைப் பள்ளி விவரத்தோடு குறிப்பிட்டு, கழக ஆட்சியைவிட அ.தி.மு.க. ஆட்சியிலே அதிகமாகச் செய்தோம் என்று சொல்வதற்கு அவர்களிடம் “மசாலா” இல்லாதபோது அறிக்கை என்ன கேடு?

16ஆம் தேதி நான் வெளியிட்ட அறிக்கையின் இறுதிப் பகுதி முழுவதும் உரம் பற்றித்தான் எழுதியிருக்கிறேன். ஆனால் உரம் பற்றி நான் வாய் திறக்கவில்லை என்று ஜெயலலிதா தனது

அறிக்கையிலே நேற்று சொல்லியிருக்கிறார் என்றால் அவரைப் பற்றி என்ன நினைப்பது?

ஜெயலலிதா மேலும் தனது கோவை உரையிலே கழக ஆட்சியில் அரிசி பதுக்கல், கடத்தல் என்றெல்லாம் முழங்கியிருக்கிறார். ஜெயலலிதாவின் ஆட்சியில் அத்தியாவசியப் பண்டங்கள் சட்டம், 1955-ன் கீழ், 2001 முதல் 2006 ஏப்ரல் மாதம் வரை ஐந்தாண்டுகளில் மொத்தம் 34,308 வழக்குகளும், 3,313 கைது நடவடிக்கைகளும், 1,492 வாகனக் கைப்பற்றுதலும், 67 நபர்கள் மீது தடுப்புக் காவல் சட்டப்படி நடவடிக்கை எடுக்கப்பட்டுள்ளது. 2004 ஆம் ஆண்டில் பதிவு செய்யப்பட்ட மொத்த வழக்குகளிலிருந்து 251 நபர்கள் கைது செய்யப்பட்டதில் ஒருவர் மீது மட்டும் கள்ளச்சந்தை தடுப்புக் காவலின் கீழ் நடவடிக்கை எடுக்கப்பட்டுள்ளது. அந்த அளவிற்கு ஜெயலலிதா ஆட்சியிலே பதுக்கல்காரர்கள் மீதும், கடத்தல்காரர்கள் மீதும் நடவடிக்கை எடுக்கப்படாமல் அவர்களுக்கு மாலை அணிவிக்கப்பட்டது.

ஆனால் தற்போது கழக ஆட்சியில் 2006 முதல் 2010 ஜூன் மாதம் முடிய மொத்தம் 58,807 வழக்குகளும், 13,311 கைது நடவடிக்கைகளும், 4,728 வாகனக் கைப்பற்றுதலும், அதில் 453 வாகனங்களின் உரிமத்தை ரத்து செய்யவும், 270 ஓட்டுநர் உரிமங்களை ரத்து செய்யவும் நடவடிக்கை எடுக்கப்பட்டுள்ளது.

மூலம் இவ்வளவையும் சம்பாதித்து இருக்கிறார்” என்று பேசியிருக்கிறார் என்றால் அவரைப் பற்றி தமிழ்நாட்டு மக்கள்தான் புரிந்து கொள்ளவேண்டும். திருட்டு ரயில் ஏறி வந்தவன் நான் என்று எப்போதும் நான் கூறிக் கொண்டதில்லை. நான் சென்னைக்கு வருவதற்கு முன்பே ஈ.ரோட்டில் தந்தை பெரியாரின் “குடியரசு” அலுவலகத்தில் துணை ஆசிரியனாகப் பணியாற்றி, பின்னர் கோவையிலும், சேலத்திலும் திரையுலகிலே சேர்ந்து பல திரைப்படங்களுக்கு கதை வசனம் எழுதி, சம்பாதித்து அதன் பின்னர்தான் சென்னைக்கே வந்தேன். என்னுடைய பேனாவினால் நான் எழுதிக் குவித்த திரைப்படங்கள் மூலமாக, ஏராளமான நூல்கள் வாயிலாக நான் சம்பாதித்தேன். அவற்றிலிருந்து பொது நலன்களுக்காக நிதியும் வழங்கி வருகிறேன். ஆனால் ஜெயலலிதா என்னைக் கேட்டதைப் போல; வேறு யாராவது ஜெயலலிதாவைப் பார்த்து நீ எப்படி இவ்வளவு சொத்துக்களையும் சம்பாதித்தாய், நடிப்பின் மூலமாக மட்டும் இத்தனை சொத்துக்களையும் சம்பாதிக்க முடியுமா என்று கேட்டுவிட்டால் அவர் என்ன பதில் சொல்வார்? அவருக்கு இறுதி எச்சரிக்கை: “வைரம்” படத்து நாயகியே, வாயில் வந்ததையெல்லாம் பேசாதே! என்பது தான்!
