# 3536. Five-Year Achievements of the Rural Development and Panchayat Raj Department! (1)

**Udanpirappē,**

Like the veins and nerves of the human body, local-government institutions stand as the roots and supporting shoots through which democracy flourishes. To choose the leadership and working arrangements needed for them to function properly, this government conducted elections in **October 2006** for Tamil Nadu’s **12,618 village panchayats, 385 panchayat unions and 29 district panchayats**.

In rural areas, an unprecedented **76 per cent** of the people voted. The source lists the elected representatives as:

- **29 district panchayat presidents**;
- **385 panchayat union committee presidents**;
- **12,618 village panchayat presidents**;
- **656 district panchayat ward members**;
- **6,570 panchayat union ward members**;
- **97,458 village panchayat ward members**;
- printed total: **1,07,716 people’s representatives**.

The component figures and the printed total are preserved as given in the source.

As promised by the DMK in its **1996 election manifesto**, Tamil Nadu for the first time implemented **33 per cent reservation for women** and conducted elections to local bodies.

On the same basis, in the **2006 local-body elections**, nearly **40,000 women** assumed office as heads and members of local bodies, including **four corporation mayoral posts**, standing, the letter says, as marks of democracy’s success.

- Elections were successfully conducted in **October 2006** for the village panchayats of **Pappapatti, Keeripatti, Nattarmangalam and Kottakachiyendal**, where elections had not been held for many years. The elected village panchayat presidents and vice-presidents and village community leaders were invited to Chennai and honoured at a **“Samathuva Peruvizha”** on **13-11-2006**.

- To give local bodies additional responsibilities and powers, two high-level committees were formed under the chairmanship of the Rural Development and Local Administration Minister. Of the committees’ **99 recommendations on 39 subjects**, submitted on **10-12-2007**, Government Orders were issued on **61 recommendations** and implemented.

- On delimitation of rural local bodies — upgrading rural local bodies, altering their boundaries and dividing them — **397 recommendations** were received from all districts on the basis of public views, and decisions are being taken on them.

- To reduce farmers’ tax burden, legislation was enacted in **December 2009**, abolishing collection of **head tax and head surcharge**.

- Multi-member wards in village panchayats were abolished and replaced by single-member wards through a **2009 legislative amendment**.

- Traditionally, small tanks had been maintained by panchayat unions. A **2009 legislative amendment** was made to entrust maintenance of ponds legally to village panchayats and maintenance of tanks to panchayat unions, as the source states.

- Accepting a recommendation in the seventh report of the **National Commission for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes**, a legislative amendment was made in **2009** providing that those found to have obtained false caste certificates in relation to Parliament, Legislative Assemblies, corporations and panchayats should be removed immediately from office or position, subjected to punishment provided by law, and barred from contesting elections for at least **six years**.

- The election rule under which postal ballot papers could be received only until **5 p.m. on the evening before polling day** was amended, and on **2-1-2009** it was ordered that postal ballots could be received until **8 a.m. on counting day**.

- To strengthen procedures for licensing hoardings in rural areas and monitor their installation, powers to issue licences and levy advertisement tax were vested in District Collectors, on a footing corresponding to urban law, by Government Order dated **18-5-2009**.

## Recognition for Panchayat administration

The letter says Tamil Nadu’s rural local bodies have functioned efficiently and responsibly and that, after 2006, the Rural Development and Panchayat Raj Department has been repeatedly commended by the Union Government’s Panchayat Ministry:

- **2006–2007:** fourth place — **₹82.64 lakh** prize;
- **2007–2008:** first place — **₹1 crore**;
- **2008–2009:** third place;
- **2009–2010:** second place;
- for the latter two years, **₹1.5 crore each**, totalling **₹3 crore**.

**Udanpirappē,** from 2008 onward, CNN-IBN has assessed all States through appropriate judges on factors such as development, law and order and infrastructure, and has presented **Diamond State Awards** to selected States. For **2010**, Tamil Nadu received:

- the special award for **Best State among large States in India**; and
- Diamond State Awards in **citizen safety, drinking water and sanitation, and women’s development**.

These were presented on **23-2-2011**.

From **2006**, a scheme was introduced to select **15 village panchayats each year** for exceptional and innovative initiatives and present the **Uthamar Gandhi Panchayat Award**. Each winning village panchayat receives a shield and **₹5 lakh cash prize**, while its president receives a medal and certificate.

From **2007–2008**, to encourage industrial, service and commercial establishments to carry out socio-economic development work as part of their social responsibility, **five establishments each year** are selected and given **₹5 lakh each** and certificates.

## Travel allowances for local-body representatives

The source says a fixed travel allowance was ordered in **1997** for village panchayat presidents, panchayat union committee presidents, district panchayat presidents, panchayat union committee members and district panchayat members, with a further **10 per cent increase in 1999**.

Under the order dated **28-9-2007**, monthly travel allowances were increased as follows:

- village panchayat presidents: **₹330 → ₹500**;
- panchayat union committee presidents: **₹550 → ₹750**;
- panchayat union committee members: **₹220 → ₹400**;
- district panchayat presidents: **₹1,100 → ₹1,500**;
- district panchayat members: **₹330 → ₹500**.

To strengthen links between people and local institutions, an order dated **26-10-2007** instituted **Local Bodies Day** on **1 November every year**, and it is being observed.

## Higher administrative sanction limits and engineering powers

The government ordered on **20-12-2007** that administrative-sanction limits be raised:

- village panchayat: from up to **₹1 lakh** per work to up to **₹2 lakh**;
- panchayat union: from up to **₹5 lakh** to up to **₹10 lakh**;
- district panchayat: from up to **₹10 lakh** to up to **₹20 lakh**.

The same order increased engineers’ powers for technical sanction and rationalised measurement and check-measurement powers of engineering-wing officers for works under the National Rural Employment Guarantee Scheme.

## Communication and office facilities

To improve communication facilities in village panchayats, telephone facilities were provided to all village panchayat offices.

Using scheme funds provided in **2006–2007 and 2007–2008** to encourage efficient and responsible panchayat functioning, computers and printers were provided for office use by the presidents of **29 district panchayats and 385 panchayat unions**.

Using the **2008–2009** funds, the government supplied `(Invertors)` for the use of **29 district panchayats and 385 panchayat unions**.

Using the **2009–2010** funds, **385 computers and printers** were supplied to **385 panchayat unions**.

The source prints that wireless communication devices had been supplied to all districts in Tamil Nadu by **2007-09**; that printed year form is retained.

## No-confidence motions

For a no-confidence motion against a village panchayat president/vice-president, or panchayat union/district panchayat president/vice-president, the minimum support required to bring the motion was raised from **one-half** of the total membership to **three-fifths**, and the share required to pass it from **two-thirds to four-fifths**.

The amendment also provides that such a no-confidence motion cannot be brought within the **first year after assuming office** or during the **final year of the term**.

## Devolution of State revenue

The share of the State government’s own tax revenue distributed to local bodies was raised:

- **2007–2008:** from **8 per cent to 9 per cent**;
- **2009–2010:** to **9.5 per cent**;
- **2010–2011:** to **10 per cent**.

Funds allotted to rural local bodies rose from **₹1,225 crore in 2006–2007** to **₹2,674.13 crore in 2010–2011**.

Village panchayats are responsible for basic services such as drinking water, sanitation, roads and streetlights. Because many small village panchayats lacked sufficient funds, the following measures were taken:

- from **2007–2008**, the village-panchayat share in State Finance Commission devolution was increased from **47 per cent to 60 per cent**;
- because population-only distribution left many small panchayats with inadequate funds, every panchayat was ordered to receive a minimum **₹3 lakh equally** from State Finance Commission grants, with the balance distributed by population;
- from **2008–2009**, every panchayat union was ordered to receive a minimum **₹30 lakh equally** from State Finance Commission grants;
- the entire **Twelfth Finance Commission grant** received from the Union Government was ordered to be given to village panchayats from the **second half of 2006–2007**;
- similarly, **₹286 crore** in Thirteenth Finance Commission grant funds for **2010–2011** was released to village panchayats.

Revenue from head tax, head surcharge, stamp duty and entertainment tax had previously been adjusted by District Collectors at district level to the accounts of the relevant rural local bodies.

The source then refers to a **special scheme for pooled assigned revenue**, using the internally opaque printed wording `வருவாய்க்குமுள்ள`; under this passage it records vehicle provision as follows:

- **385 vehicles** for panchayat union committee presidents; and
- **664 vehicles** for Block Development Officers, Executive Engineers, Assistant Executive Engineers and Assistant Executive Engineers of newly created sub-divisions.

No outside wording is used to repair the source phrase.

## Panchayat Union office buildings and essential works

In place of **87 badly dilapidated panchayat union office buildings**, new buildings are being constructed at an estimated **₹1.5 crore each**.

Essential road works, panchayat office buildings, streetlights and bridges are being undertaken from this funding.

## Kalaignar Housing Scheme

With the objective of creating a Tamil Nadu with hut-free rural areas and slum-free towns, the **Kalaignar Housing Scheme** is being implemented as a six-year programme from **2010–2011 to 2015–2016** to convert all thatched huts in rural Tamil Nadu into permanent houses with concrete roofs.

In the first year, the target was to convert **3 lakh huts** into concrete houses. The State government provides **₹75,000 subsidy per beneficiary**.

By **28-2-2011**, **75,000 concrete houses** had been completed. The source states that:

- **1 lakh 50 thousand houses** would be completed by **31-3-2011**;
- **2 lakh houses** by **15-4-2011**.

The government had spent **₹1,082 crore** on the scheme to that point, and eligibility cards had been issued to **12 lakh beneficiaries**.

In the second phase, another **3 lakh huts** were taken up under the scheme and work orders were issued to beneficiaries in **February 2011**.

## All-Village Anna Renaissance Scheme

The **All-Village Anna Renaissance Scheme**, launched in **January 2007**, is described as the largest State-government scheme.

Under it, each panchayat takes up mandatory works such as:

- pond;
- library;
- playground;
- cremation/burial ground;
- drinking-water scheme works.

Depending on local needs, works also include streetlights, fish-rearing ponds, cement roads and village markets.

For the **12,618 village panchayats**, a total of **₹2,548 crore 84 lakh** was provided under this scheme. Works of other major State departments — including Agriculture, Education, the Water Supply and Drainage Board and Highways — were coordinated with it. Over five years, the source says **31 lakh 20 thousand 320 works** were completed across all village panchayats at a cost of **₹12,746 crore 19 lakh 73 thousand**.

Every village panchayat has been provided with a playground, pond, cremation/burial ground, drinking-water-source works and cement-concrete streets. The source says Tamil Nadu achieved the distinction of being the only State with **a library in every village panchayat**.

கிராம ஊராட்சித் தலைவர் மற்றும் துணைத் தலைவர்
அல்லது ஊராட்சி ஒன்றிய, மாவட்ட ஊராட்சித் தலைவர் மற்றும்
துணைத் தலைவர்கள் மீது நம்பிக்கையில்லாத் தீர்மானம்
கொண்டு வருவதற்கு சம்பந்தப்பட்ட உள்ளாட்சி அமைப்பின்
மொத்த உறுப்பினர்களின் எண்ணிக்கையில் சரிபாதி என்பதைக்
குறைந்தபட்சம் ஐந்தில் மூன்று பங்கு எனவும், தீர்மானத்தை
நிறைவேற்ற தற்போது உள்ள மூன்றில் இரண்டு பங்கு என்பதை
ஐந்தில் நான்கு பங்கு எனவும் உயர்த்தி ஆணையிடப்பட்டுள்ளது.
மேலும், மேற்படி சட்ட திருத்தப்படி, கிராம ஊராட்சிகளின் துணைத்
தலைவர்கள் அல்லது தலைவர்கள், ஊராட்சி ஒன்றியம் மற்றும்
மாவட்ட ஊராட்சிகளின் தலைவர் மற்றும் துணைத் தலைவர்கள்
பொறுப்பேற்ற ஓராண்டுக்குள்ளாகவோ, பதவிக்காலம் முடியும்
இறுதி ஆண்டிலோ நம்பிக்கையில்லாத் தீர்மானம் கொண்டுவர
இயலாது என்றும் ஆணையிடப்பட்டுள்ளது.

மாநில அரசின் சொந்த வரி வருவாயிலிருந்து உள்ளாட்சி
அமைப்புகளுக்கு பகிர்ந்தளிக்கப்படும் நிதி 2007-2008-ம்
ஆண்டில் 8 சதவீதத்திலிருந்து 9 சதவீதமாகவும் மேலும் இதுநடப்பு
நிதியாண்டில் 2009-2010-ல் 9.5 சதவீதமாகவும் உயர்த்தப்பட்டு
2010-2011ஆம் ஆண்டில் 10 சதவீதமாக உயர்த்தப்பட்டுள்ளது.

ஊரக உள்ளாட்சி அமைப்புகளுக்கு ஒதுக்கீடு செய்யப்படும்
நிதி 2006-07-ம் ஆண்டில் ரூ.1,225 கோடியிலிருந்து 2010-11
ஆம் ஆண்டிற்கு ரூபாய் 2,674.13 கோடி ஒதுக்கீடு செய்யப்பட்டது.

குடிநீர் வழங்குதல், சுகாதாரம், சாலைகள் மற்றும்
தெருவிளக்குகளை பராமரித்தல் போன்ற அடிப்படை வசதிகளை

ஆனால் பல கிராம ஊராட்சிகள் போதிய நிதி வசதியின்றி
அடிப்படை வசதிகளை நிறைவு செய்ய சிரமப்படுவதால்,

2007-08-ம் ஆண்டிலிருந்து மாநிலநிதி ஆணைய நிதிப்

சதவீதமாக உயர்த்தப்பட்டுள்ளது.

மக்கள் தொகை அடிப்படையில் மட்டுமே நிதிப் பங்கீடு
செய்யப்படும்போது, பல சிறிய ஊராட்சிகளுக்கு போதுமான
நிதி கிடைப்பதில்லை. இந்நிலையினை மாற்ற மாநில நிதிக் குழு
மானியத்திலிருந்து அனைத்து ஊராட்சிகளுக்கும் குறைந்தபட்ச
நிதியாக தலா ரூபாய் 3 இலட்சம் வீதம் சமமாக வழங்கவும்,
மீதமுள்ள தொகையை மக்கள் தொகை அடிப்படையில் வழங்கவும்
ஆணையிடப்பட்டு நடைமுறைப்படுத்தப்பட்டு வருகிறது.

அனைத்து ஊராட்சி ஒன்றியங்களுக்கும் சமச்சீராக நிதி
கிடைக்க வேண்டும் என்ற நோக்கில், 2008-09-ம் ஆண்டு முதல்
மாநில நிதிக்குழு மானியத்திலிருந்து விடுவிக்கப்படும் நிதியில்
குறைந்தபட்ச நிதியாக தலா ரூ.30 இலட்சம் வீதம் சமமாக
வழங்க ஆணை வெளியிடப்பட்டு அதன்படி நிதி விடுவிக்கப்
படுகிறது.

மத்திய அரசிடமிருந்து பெறப்படும் பன்னிரெண்டாவது
நிதிக் குழு மானியம் முழுமையையும் கிராம ஊராட்சிகளுக்கு
வழங்க அரசு ஆணையிட்டுள்ளது. இது 2006-07 ஆம் ஆண்டு
இரண்டாவது அரையாண்டு முதல் ஒதுக்கீடு செய்யப்பட்டு
வருகிறது. இதேபோல், 2010-11-ம் ஆண்டிற்கான பதிமூன்றாவது
நிதிக் குழு மானிய நிதி 286 கோடி ரூபாய் கிராம ஊராட்சிகளுக்கு
விடுவிக்கப்பட்டுள்ளது.

தலவரி, தலமேல் வரி, முத்திரைத்தாள் கட்டணம் மற்றும்
கேளிக்கை வரி இவை மூலமான வருவாய் இதற்கு முன்னர் மாவட்ட

உள்ளாட்சி அமைப்புகளின் கணக்குகளுக்கு ஈடு செய்யப்பட்டது.

மிகவும் அவசியமான சாலைப் பணிகள், ஊராட்சி அலுவலகக்
