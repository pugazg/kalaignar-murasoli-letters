# 3614. Poor Panneerselvam!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This draft follows the locked Volume 46 conventions. Housing Board allotment figures, names, official designations, plot measurements, legal references, the long recipient list, and Kalaignar’s closing sarcasm toward Panneerselvam are preserved in source order. **Date:** 14 April 2012

**Udanpirappē,**

One accusation that AIADMK members have repeatedly made whenever they get an opportunity, both before and after coming to power in May 2011, is that under DMK rule houses or plots belonging to the Housing Board were improperly allotted to DMK members, to people working at the DMK headquarters, and to a few of those who had served as security personnel for the Chief Minister.

I have repeatedly demonstrated, both in the Legislative Assembly and outside it, with supporting evidence, that those houses and plots were allotted properly and that no wrongdoing occurred. I have also written in detail about which AIADMK members received such houses or plots during AIADMK rule, which officials received them, and how even an AIADMK trade-union organisation received an allotment. Yet the minister responsible for the department, apparently having nothing else to say except repeating this accusation in the Assembly in order to delight their “Amma,” has repeated the same charge, and it has appeared in the newspapers.

One allegation is that land with a market value of ₹5 crore was given to employees of the Anna Arivalayam office for ₹1.48 crore. Housing Board land was allotted to those working at Anna Arivalayam at exactly the same price at which it was allotted to others.

Moreover, those houses or plots were given only after the Board—which includes the Government’s Finance Secretary—decided and fixed the price at which the Housing Board plots could be sold. A house worth ₹5 crore was not deliberately undervalued merely because the recipients worked at Arivalayam. The allotments were made on precedent, properly and within the rules. No special concession was shown to them because they worked at Arivalayam.

A few people who worked as my security personnel when I was Chief Minister were also allotted Housing Board plots. What is wrong in Housing Board plots being given to them? Is there any rule saying they must not be given plots? Were the plots allotted without collecting the proper price? Were they given at a lower price than to others? Or were people serving in comparable positions during AIADMK rule never allotted such houses or plots? Were Housing Board plots never given to Government Secretaries, people working in the Chief Minister’s residence, ministers, Members of the Legislative Assembly, or their relatives? And when they were allotted plots, were they charged more than others?

A case was even filed in the High Court regarding Housing Board plots allotted to people who had served as the Chief Minister’s security personnel, and the judge dismissed that case. Even so, the AIADMK Government keeps grinding the same flour again and again.

Under the Government’s discretionary quota, fifteen per cent of Housing Board houses and plots were allotted not only during DMK rule but during AIADMK rule as well. The quota had originally been ten per cent; it was during AIADMK rule, when Jayalalithaa was Chief Minister, that it was increased to fifteen per cent. Indeed, during DMK rule, when some people criticised this discretionary system, the DMK Government decided and announced that the Government’s discretionary quota itself would henceforth be abolished.

It was during AIADMK rule, when Jayalalithaa was Chief Minister, that the following allotments were made: to then Chief Secretary Mr. N. Narayanan, I.A.S., 4,115 square feet in 1993; to K. S. Karthesan, son of Minister K. A. Sengottaiyan, 4,535 square feet in Besant Nagar in 1995; to Noor Jameela, wife of former minister Nagore Meeran, 2,559 square feet at Kottivakkam in 1993; to Dr. Bhanumathi Thambidurai, wife of Member of Parliament Thambidurai, seven grounds in Anna Nagar; to the AIADMK Labour Federation, three grounds in Anna Nagar; in 2004, to several I.P.S. officers including Devaram, K. Vijayakumar and R. Nataraj, 4,800 square feet each at Sholinganallur; and to T. Natarajan, I.A.S., who served as Secretary to the Chief Minister, 6,784 square feet at Thiruvanmiyur in 1995. There is a long list.

I am not saying that giving those allotments was wrong. Housing Board plots have been allotted as a matter of continuing practice under every Government; the DMK Government did the same. Nothing in those allotments was contrary to the system or in violation of rules. Because today’s rulers repeatedly make accusations about DMK-era allotments, I am compelled to state who received such Housing Board plots during AIADMK rule.

To mention some more names of those who received houses or plots: C. R. Saraswathi; P. Kalimuthu, I.P.S.; Malik Feroz Khan, I.A.S.; C. R. Rajesh; Thanga Tamilselvan; P. A. Ramaiah, I.A.S.; Anuradha Kathi Rajivan, I.A.S.; S. Kapilan, I.A.S.; Dr. Deepak, son of H. P. N. Shetty, I.A.S.; K. Selvaraj; Markabandhu; Kuruvikkarambai Shanmugam; Kovai Thambi; Raghavan, I.A.S.; Ramadas, I.A.S.; today’s Mayor Saidai Duraisamy; Shyam Sundar, son of Varadarajulu, I.A.S.; Srinivasan, I.A.S.; A. R. Venkatesan; L. Sudhakar Reddy; Aadhi Rajaram; Iqbal Mohammed, I.P.S.; Lal Raina Silo, I.A.S.; Saraswathi, M.L.A.; Chandrika, wife of Balkanga; Mrs. Mallika, M.L.A.; Arumugam, M.L.A.; Mohan Rao; T. N. Renganathan; Inspector Rajendran; M.G.R.’s driver Boopathy; A. P. Mohammed Ali, I.P.S.; P. Poongavanam, wife of former Member of Parliament P. Mohan; Philomina, wife of S. Andik Thevar; V. S. Panchavarnam; R. Kannan, I.A.S.; P. Uma, wife of Namakkal District AIADMK Secretary K. P. P. Baskaran; V. Tamilarasi, wife of former AIADMK Member of Parliament S. Anbazhagan; S. S. Jawahar Babu, I.A.S., Secretary to the Chief Minister; V. Ramu, I.A.S.; and S. Bose, I.R.S. The list goes on.

If one looks at those who received more than one plot during AIADMK rule, there are P. Senthil, Geetha, K. Sundararaj, P. Vinoth Kumar, T. Balamurugan, T. Balasubramanian, T. Sakthivel, M. Lakshmikanthan, P. Manickavasagam and S. Sundaresan, and the list continues. A long list of those who served in the Special Task Force were given Housing Board plots free of cost during AIADMK rule. Not only that: security officers who served Jayalalithaa—Ramakrishnan, Rajendran, Thirumalachamy, Perumalsamy and Veeraperumal—and police personnel who were in her residence itself, such as Krishnamaraj, Ashokan, Balaji, Vadivel and Subramaniam, were allotted these housing plots under the Government discretionary quota.

When so many Housing Board plots were distributed during AIADMK rule, why did the minister responsible for that department declare in the Assembly that irregularities had taken place? Why deny the opposition an opportunity to explain? And now, is it fair for Vigilance and Anti-Corruption police to go to the homes of people who received those plots and conduct searches, while some newspapers deliberately give the matter enormous publicity in order to fling dust and abuse at them? That is our question.

It is not only Housing Board plot allotments. In Coimbatore, when defects were found in some houses built by the Slum Clearance Board during DMK rule, all proper corrective action was taken during DMK rule itself. The minister concerned personally went there, consulted technical professors of Anna University and obtained their views, and maintenance work was carried out. When the AIADMK, then in opposition, raised the issue in the Legislative Assembly, the minister gave a detailed explanation. But now all of that is concealed, and they indulge in alliterative slander such as “robbery in the hut, robbery in the Fort”; when DMK members rise to reply, permission is denied and the entire group is expelled. What kind of civility is this?

Who is sitting in the Fort and looting today? Who looted Kodanadu? Who looted Siruthavur? Who accumulated assets worth ₹66 crore and has dragged out the trial in the Bengaluru court for years by seeking adjournment after adjournment? Every day complaints arrive alleging that those surrounding them committed looting. Do they imagine the town knows none of this? Having somehow won and come to power, and in the arrogance of that victory, the Leader of the House himself, instead of maintaining order in the Assembly, stretches his tongue at every opportunity about “2G” and spectrum allocation. Is that the standard by which the House is to be run?

The spectrum case is still being tried; no judgment has been delivered. Kalaignar Television received a loan from those who had obtained spectrum licences; that loan was properly received through banks by cheque and later repaid with interest, again by cheque. Even though I had no connection whatsoever with the television company’s day-to-day operations and was only a shareholder, Panneerselvam speaks in the Assembly as though some great wrongdoing had been committed, comparing it to the “Bengaluru” case.

When cases concerning spectrum are pending before courts including the Supreme Court of India, even the convention that such matters should not be discussed in the Assembly is ignored by the Leader of the House himself. Poor man! The situation there seems to be that only by behaving so aggressively can he earn the Chief Minister’s confidence and continue as a minister.

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**14 April 2012**
