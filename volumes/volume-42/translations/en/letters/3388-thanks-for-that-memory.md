# 3388. Thanks for That Memory!!
## (Kalaignar Poetic Letter)

> **Translator’s note**
>
> The actual source prints the main title and the subtitle **(கலைஞர் கவிதைக் கடிதம்)** as two separate title layers; that structure is preserved. The letter deliberately leaves one participant unnamed. No identity is inferred. The poem’s political memories and accusations remain the author’s source framing. The complete audited Tamil is reproduced below and remains authoritative.

**Tamil source:** [Letter 3388](../../../chapters/3388-antha-ninaivukku-oru-nandri.md)  
**Source pages:** [PDF 187](../../../pages/page-187.md)–[PDF 190](../../../pages/page-190.md)  
**Date:** 29 March 2009

*(Preface: When Udanpirappugal of the Kazhagam family — affectionate younger brothers L.G. and Gingee, followed by brothers M. Kannappan, Cumbum Ramakrishnan and T. K. Subbu — came with thousands of Kazhagam followers and rejoined the parent organisation, I spoke privately with those five. The waves of memory that arose during that conversation became this letter.)*

**Udanpirappē,**

At the guest house  
on the Kanyakumari seashore,

I am told I was sleeping soundly.

At that midnight hour,  
our younger brothers then —

Arcot,  
Duraimurugan,  
and one more —

a person whose name  
I do not now wish to speak —

the three of them,  
on the upstairs veranda,

talked until the sky grew pale  
about the growth of the DMK —

that Dravidian movement  
rarely found,  
yet found by Dravidians —

about its strength,  
its inexhaustible affection,  
and its future.

Then that younger brother —  
the distinguished man  
whose name I do not speak —

looked at my brothers  
Arcot and Duraimurugan  
and, rising in agitation, asked:

“So then, after Kalaignar,  
will Stalin be the leader  
of the Kazhagam?”

When the other two asked,  
“How do you say that?”

that dear younger brother  
is said to have sighed:

“At meetings and conferences,  
Professor’s praises of Stalin  
pour down like rain!”

Immediately Arcot and Duraimurugan replied:

“What is wrong  
with Anbazhagan speaking so?

That young man’s sacrifice,  
his unmatched ability  
to build the Youth Wing —

what is wrong  
with Professor praising those things?

Professor accepts anyone  
only after weighing them.

He tested even Kalaignar,  
understood his true capacity,  
and only then accepted him  
as his own leader.

So what urgency is there now  
to talk about Stalin  
being leader after Kalaignar?

Why do you jump  
as though you touched fire?

Kalaignar himself  
now calls you his ‘sword’ —

praises you —

and who else has he repeatedly favoured  
as he did you  
with a Rajya Sabha seat?

Do not worry.

Kalaignar will make you,  
after himself,  
the leader of the movement.”

With such reassurance,  
even at that midnight hour,  
they brought light  
to the face of our illustrious  
unnamed younger brother.

On that smiling face  
another thought was born.

It turned into a question:

“Duraimurugan,  
you say I will become leader  
after Kalaignar.

Suppose our Kalaignar lives,  
like Periyar,  
like Rajaji,  
beyond ninety years —

how many years  
must I wait  
to become leader?”

The other two brothers  
did not treat it merely as a question.

They were shaken by it,  
as though it carried  
the poison of a scorpion.

They are said to have secretly sworn  
that neither of them  
would ever tell the leader  
about this conversation.

But —

Duraimurugan is an iron chest  
when it comes to guarding a secret.

Arcot, on the other hand,  
is truly a glass case!

Even so,  
only after several years had passed  
did he tell me  
about that night at Kanyakumari —

about the eruption  
of the narrow ambition  
inside that gathered volcano.

Then came to mind  
the nectar-like words of the Kural:

> **“Though joined outwardly like lid and vessel,  
> a house afflicted by inner enmity is not truly united.”**

*(The lid of a container may appear to fit it perfectly from outside. In the same way, those who harbour inner hostility do not truly unite in heart.)*

**Udanpirappē,**

This Kural,  
which I happened not to include  
in my *Kuraloviyam* collection,

has come to mind  
at this very moment.

Thanks  
for that memory!

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**29-03-2009**

# 3388. அந்த நினைவுக்கு ஒரு நன்றி!!
## (கலைஞர் கவிதைக் கடிதம்)

வருங்காலம் பற்றியும் வானம் வெளுக்கும் வரையில்

உரையாடினார்களாம்.

என் தம்பிகள் ஆற்காட்டாரையும், துரைமுருகனையும்

பார்த்து;
