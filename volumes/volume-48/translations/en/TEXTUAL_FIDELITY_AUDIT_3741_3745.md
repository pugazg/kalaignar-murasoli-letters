## Scope and method

This audit covers five consecutive letters and **31/31 canonical pages**. Every page was visually compared with the rendered original scan. The review checked titles, salutations, page and paragraph continuity, Tamil and English quotations, names, dates, figures, welfare-board statistics, punctuation, source word spacing, closing/signature/date pages, omissions, duplicated text and page-boundary fidelity.

The scan remained authoritative. Source-specific spelling, spacing, punctuation and line/page continuation were preserved rather than silently normalised.

## Source forms and material deliberately preserved

The audit retained the printed source rather than copy-editing it, including:

- the refugee testimonies, institutional names and Commonwealth terminology in letters 3741–3742;
- the existing scan-proven letter-3743 title `கழுதையே` and the source's rhetorical and numerical forms;
- source punctuation, dates, statistics and political terminology in letter 3744;
- the extensive welfare-board names, membership counts, assistance figures and source spacing across letter 3745; and
- source-specific compounds even where contemporary editorial practice might choose different spacing.

No external fact-checking or stylistic normalisation was used to alter the canonical text.
