## Volume 43 conventions locked

1. **Udanpirappē** is retained without flattening it to “brother”, “sister” or “comrade”.
2. Standard `அன்புள்ள, / மு.க.` closes as **With affection, M.K.**; the printed date remains visible.
3. **lakh** and **crore** are retained as Indian public-language units; source numerical forms are not silently converted to million/billion.
4. Ordinary political references use **DMK** where `தி.மு.க.` / `கழக அரசு` clearly identifies the party; the full **Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam** may be used where source framing benefits from it.
5. `மத்திய அரசு` is rendered **Union Government** in institutional/legal prose and `மாநில அரசு` as **State government**.
6. `காவல் துறை` is rendered contextually as **Police Department**, **police** or the named police unit without changing institutional meaning.
7. `கன்னக்களவு` is **housebreaking**; `ஆதாயக்கொலை` is **murder for gain**; `தடுப்புக் காவல்` is **preventive detention**; `குண்டர் தடைக் சட்டம்` is **Goondas Act**.
8. `பணக்கொடை` in the refugee-camp welfare context is **cash dole**.
9. `முல்லைப் பெரியாறு அணை` is **Mullai Periyar Dam**. Dam-level, technical and judicial claims remain source-framed rather than externally reconciled.
10. Source-supplied English is preserved verbatim when it functions as printed source wording, including judicial passages, institutional parentheses and correspondence extracts.
11. Political accusation, irony, repetition, rhetorical questions and image-bearing idioms are preserved rather than neutralised.
12. The Letter 3428 lullaby is translated sense-first while retaining its satirical image sequence; the complete Tamil appendix remains the authority for its colloquial wording and ellipses.
13. Every bilingual record includes the complete audited Tamil under **Original Tamil — மூலத் தமிழ்**, with physical source-page markers retained.
