# 3522. Employment Opportunities: Then and Now!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This translation follows the audited canonical Tamil and the locked Volume 44 conventions. Employment, government-recruitment, unemployment-assistance, investment and job-camp figures are preserved exactly as source claims and are not reconciled against outside records or against one another. In particular, the source first gives **45,987** teachers appointed on consolidated pay and then says **47,200** teachers were placed on time-scale pay from 1-6-2006; both figures are retained. The complete audited Tamil is reproduced below and remains authoritative.

**Tamil source:** [Letter 3522](../../../chapters/3522-velai-vaaippu-anrum-inrum.md)  
**Source pages:** [PDF 278](../../../pages/page-278.md)–[PDF 283](../../../pages/page-283.md)  
**Date:** 23 December 2010

**Udanpirappē,**

Before the 2006 general election, the DMK manifesto placed before the people gave this assurance:

> Young people who, even after completing their studies and registering at the Employment Exchange, have not obtained any employment will be given assistance until they find work: ₹150 a month to those who have completed the Tenth Standard, ₹200 a month to those who have completed Higher Secondary School, and ₹300 a month to graduates. We will relax the upper age limit for young people who, because of the recruitment-ban order under AIADMK rule, were unable to enter service and were affected by the age ceiling during the previous four years. We will fill the roughly three lakh posts left vacant as a result of the “recruitment-ban order” introduced under AIADMK rule.

During the previous AIADMK government, government employees and teachers who asked for their rights were dismissed in a single day, and there were tragic incidents in which, the letter says, many died of shock. After the DMK government took office in 2006, however, **4 lakh 98 thousand 40 people** have so far been given government employment in accordance with the manifesto promise.

The letter lists the appointments department by department:

- Social Welfare and Nutritious Meal Department — **1 lakh 61 thousand 625**;
- School Education Department — **1 lakh 5 thousand 67**;
- Transport Department — **41 thousand 916**;
- Energy Department — **26 thousand 154**;
- Rural Development and Panchayat Raj Department — **33 thousand 766**;
- Home, Prohibition and Excise — **27 thousand 189**;
- Municipal Administration and Water Supply — **21 thousand 172**;
- Health and Family Welfare — **30 thousand 832**;
- Revenue — **9 thousand 972**;
- Higher Education — **8 thousand 422**;
- Agriculture — **4 thousand 966**;
- Co-operation, Food and Consumer Protection — **4 thousand 265**;
- Public Works — **3 thousand 388**;
- Animal Husbandry, Dairying and Fisheries — **3 thousand 555**; and
- hundreds more through other departments.

In the first week of this month, Deputy Chief Minister M. K. Stalin, on behalf of the School Education Department, issued appointment orders to **851** special teachers, physical-education teachers, sewing teachers and music teachers; **62** persons on compassionate grounds; and **190** typists selected through the Tamil Nadu Public Service Commission — **1,103 persons in all**.

Of the **1 lakh 5 thousand 67** persons who received employment through School Education, the letter says **57 thousand 127** were newly appointed. Among them, **27 thousand 915** teachers were appointed through the Directorate of School Education as secondary-grade teachers, graduate teachers, teacher trainers, postgraduate teachers, special teachers, vocational teachers, computer teachers and agricultural teachers. Another **26 thousand 451** were appointed through the Directorate of Elementary Education, **248** as lecturers in District Institutes of Education and Training, **740** as assistants and typists on compassionate grounds, and **741** through the Public Service Commission.

Besides these appointments, the previous government had appointed **45 thousand 987 teachers** on consolidated pay — roughly one-third of the salary they should have received. After the DMK government took office in 2006, from **1-6-2006** time-scale pay was extended to **47 thousand 200 teachers**, enabling each teacher, the letter says, to receive about three times the pay he or she had been receiving. The two printed teacher counts are retained here exactly as the source gives them.

Even while the government was working on this scale to secure employment for the jobless, Rajya Sabha member and poet Kanimozhi, with the support of public-service organisations and in coordination with district ministers, district collectors and industrial establishments, organised employment camps at Kariyapatti, Nagercoil, Vellore, Udhagamandalam, Virudhunagar, Cuddalore, Tiruchi, Tirunelveli and Thoothukudi.

Across these places, **2 lakh 33 thousand 712** unemployed young people were called for interviews, and **1 lakh 33 thousand 998** of them obtained employment. The source gives the district figures as:

- Tiruchi — **36 thousand 297**;
- Tirunelveli — **22 thousand 408**;
- Cuddalore — **19 thousand 98**;
- Vellore — **17 thousand 2**;
- Thoothukudi — **16 thousand 663**;
- Kanniyakumari — **5 thousand 77**;
- the Nilgiris — **2 thousand 165**; and
- Kariyapatti — **1 thousand 196**.

Apart from those who had already found employment, the employment prospects of **55 thousand 656** persons were still under examination, and the letter says they too had opportunities to obtain work.

Next, in accordance with the election-manifesto promise and the 2006–2007 Budget announcement, the unemployment-assistance scheme was launched at Tiruchi on **11-11-2006** and has continued: ₹150 a month for those who passed the Tenth Standard, ₹200 for those who passed the Twelfth Standard, and ₹300 for graduates and postgraduates.

Under that scheme, up to **31-3-2010**, **3 lakh 53 thousand 801** educated unemployed young people had received **₹261 crore** in assistance. Of them, **1 lakh 11 thousand 774** were Adi Dravidars and Scheduled Tribes. The annual-income ceiling for receiving unemployment assistance was increased from **₹24,000 to ₹50,000**.

The letter further says that, with the aim of creating employment for approximately **2 lakh 32 thousand 169** people, **28 Memoranda of Understanding** involving investment of **₹60 thousand 55 crore** were entered into, **13 Government Orders** were issued, and steps were taken to establish **41 new factories**. Of these, **13 factories** had already opened, providing **10 thousand 683 jobs**.

Following the principle, “We will do what we say, and say what we do,” the DMK government, after fulfilling what it had stated in the manifesto, also acted in the spirit of “we will do even what we did not promise.” On Independence Day, **15-8-2010**, while hoisting the national flag at Fort St. George in Chennai, I announced a new programme in view of the United Nations having declared the year the “International Year of Youth.”

The programme, I said, would intensify employability training for unemployed youth. Young people who had studied the Tenth and Twelfth Standards, as well as engineering graduates, undergraduates and postgraduates, would receive skill-development training for employment. The scheme would help trained youth obtain suitable jobs in various industrial establishments. In the current year, **one lakh youth** would be trained and employment arranged, and a Government Order allocating **₹50 crore** for the first phase was being issued on that auspicious day.

Accordingly, employment camps under the training-and-placement programme were first conducted in six districts — Tiruvarur, Tiruvallur, Tirunelveli, Nagapattinam, Dharmapuri and Krishnagiri.

At the Tiruvallur camp, **13 thousand 441** young people registered for employment. **83 industrial establishments** interviewed them and selected **5 thousand 216** for training and employment.

At the Tirunelveli camp, **7 thousand 286** registered; **60 industrial establishments** interviewed them and selected **2 thousand 335** for training and employment.

At the combined Tiruvarur–Nagapattinam camp, **12 thousand 550** young people registered; **54 industrial establishments** interviewed them and selected **4 thousand 703** for training and employment.

At the combined Krishnagiri–Dharmapuri camp, **16 thousand 218** registered; **77 industrial establishments** interviewed them and selected **5 thousand 346** for training and employment.

Thus, of **49 thousand 495** young people registered at employment camps in the six districts, a total of **17 thousand 600** were being given training and employment opportunities.

I will bring all of them to Chennai on **5 January 2011** and personally give them the appointment orders for receiving job training. The Tamil Nadu government has undertaken the training expenditure and provided **₹50 crore** for it. On the basis of the orders I issue, the young people will go to the concerned industrial establishments, receive training and obtain employment there.

Therefore, to create a situation in which boards saying **“No vacancies”** will themselves have **“no place anywhere”**, I have not the slightest doubt that young people and women alike will extend their hands of support to this government as it continues its work!

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
23 December 2010

திருவாரூர், நாகப்பட்டினம் ஆகிய இரண்டு மாவட்டங்களுக்கும் சேர்த்து நடத்தப்பட்ட வேலைவாய்ப்பு முகாமில், மொத்தம் 12 ஆயிரத்து 550 இளைஞர்கள் வேலைவாய்ப்புக்காக முன்பதிவு செய்யப்பட்டனர். அவர்களை 54 தொழில் நிறுவனங்கள் அழைத்து நேர்முகத் தேர்வு நடத்தி, 4 ஆயிரத்து 703 இளைஞர்களுக்குப் பயிற்சி அளித்து, வேலைவாய்ப்புகள் அளித்திட தேர்வு செய்தன. கிருஷ்ணகிரி, தருமபுரி ஆகிய இரண்டு மாவட்டங்களுக்கும் சேர்த்து நடத்தப்பட்ட வேலைவாய்ப்பு முகாமில், 16 ஆயிரத்து 218 இளைஞர்கள் வேலைவாய்ப்புக்காக முன்பதிவு செய்யப்பட்டனர். அவர்களை 77 தொழில் நிறுவனங்கள் அழைத்து நேர்முகத் தேர்வு நடத்தி, 5 ஆயிரத்து 346 இளைஞர்களுக்குப் பயிற்சி அளித்து வேலைவாய்ப்புகள் அளித்திட தேர்வு செய்தன. எனவே இந்த ஆறு மாவட்டங்களிலும், வேலைவாய்ப்பு முகாம்களில் பதிவு செய்யப்பட்ட 49 ஆயிரத்து 495 இளைஞர்களில், மொத்தம் 17 ஆயிரத்து 600 இளைஞர்களுக்குப் பயிற்சி அளித்து, வேலை வாய்ப்புகள் அளிக்கப்படுகின்றன. இவர்கள் அனைவரையும் 2011, ஜனவரி 5ஆம் தேதியன்று சென்னைக்கு அழைத்து, அவர்கள் அனைவருக்கும் வேலைக்கான பயிற்சி பெறுவதற்கான நியமன ஆணைகளை நானே நேரடியாக வழங்கிடவிருக்கிறேன். இவர்களுக்குப் பயிற்சி அளிப்பதற்குரிய செலவினங்களை தமிழக அரசு ஏற்றுக் கொண்டுள்ளது. அதற்காக 50 கோடி ரூபாய் நிதியை தமிழக அரசு வழங்கியுள்ளது. நான் வழங்கும் ஆணைகளின் அடிப்படையில், இளைஞர்கள் சம்மந்தப்பட்ட தொழில் நிறுவனங்களுக்குச் சென்று, பயிற்சிகளைப் பெற்று, அந்த நிறுவனங்களில் வேலைவாய்ப்புகளைப் பெறுவார்கள். எனவே “வேலை காலி இல்லை” என்று விரட்டும் பலகைகளுக்கு “இனி எங்கும் இடம் இல்லை” என்ற நிலையை உருவாக்க; இந்த அரசு தொடர்ந்து பாடுபட இளைஞர், மகளிர் அனைவரும் - ஆதரவுக்கரம் நீட்டுவர் என்பதில் அணுவளவும் அய்யமில்லை! அன்புள்ள, மு.க. 23-12-2010
