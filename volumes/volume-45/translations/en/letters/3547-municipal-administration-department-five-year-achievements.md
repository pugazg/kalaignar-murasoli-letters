# 3547. Five-Year Achievements of the Municipal Administration Department!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This translation is intended to carry Kalaignar's voice into clear, contemporary English rather than recast the letter as literary or academic prose. It preserves the source's argument, political directness, rhetorical questions, repetition, irony, factual detail, and paragraph order. Names, dates, figures, quotations, and intentional English expressions are retained. Where Tamil idiom cannot be reproduced literally without sounding unnatural, the English follows its sense and rhetorical force without adding claims absent from the source. The original Tamil is reproduced in full below the translation and remains the authoritative text. `Udanpirappē` is retained in Tamil transliteration rather than flattened into “brother,” “sister,” or “comrade.” Literally evoking “one born alongside me,” Kalaignar uses it as a distinctive address of shared identity, equality, affection, and solidarity within the movement.

**Udanpirappē,**

I continue to write about the achievements and schemes carried out by the DMK government in the Municipal Administration Department during the past five years.

### Hogenakkal Water Supply and Fluorosis Mitigation Project

Drinking-water sources in Dharmapuri and Krishnagiri districts are affected by fluoride salts. To provide protected and quality drinking water to the people affected by this, the Tamil Nadu Government is implementing the Hogenakkal Water Supply and Fluorosis Mitigation Project at an estimated cost of ₹1,928.80 crore. For effective implementation, the project has been divided into five packages and the works are progressing rapidly. The target is to complete the works in December 2012 and bring the project into public use. When completed, approximately 30 lakh people living in three Municipalities, 17 Town Panchayats and 6,755 rural habitations in 18 Panchayat Unions in the two districts will benefit.

### Major combined water-supply schemes that have been sanctioned

Apart from this, some of the drinking-water schemes sanctioned for important towns requiring drinking-water facilities are as follows.

For 295 habitations affected by poor drinking-water quality and 315 wayside habitations in the Sirkazhi, Kollidam, Sembanarkoil and Mayiladuthurai Panchayat Unions of Nagapattinam district, a combined water-supply scheme has been designed at an estimated cost of ₹1.06 crore to benefit 3.08 lakh people, and administrative sanction has been granted.

For 755 habitations in the Sattur, Vembakottai, Sivakasi, Virudhunagar, Vathirairuppu (part), Srivilliputhur (part) and Aruppukottai (part) Panchayat Unions of Virudhunagar district, a combined water-supply scheme has been designed at an estimated cost of ₹234 crore to benefit 5.43 lakh people, and administrative sanction has been granted.

For seven Town Panchayats and 395 rural habitations in the Rajapalayam, Srivilliputhur, Vathirairuppu and Sivakasi (part) Panchayat Unions of Virudhunagar district, a combined water-supply scheme has been designed at an estimated cost of ₹173 crore to benefit 3.84 lakh people, and administrative sanction has been granted.

For 637 habitations in the Aruppukottai, Kariapatti, Tiruchuli, Narikudi and Virudhunagar (part) Panchayat Unions of Virudhunagar district, a combined water-supply scheme has been designed at an estimated cost of ₹190 crore to benefit 3.53 lakh people, and administrative sanction has been granted.

For Palladam Municipality, 23 Town Panchayats and 965 habitations in six Panchayat Unions in Coimbatore and Tiruppur districts, a drinking-water improvement scheme has been designed at an estimated cost of ₹225 crore to benefit 9.88 lakh people, and administrative sanction has been granted.

For Melur, Avaniyapuram and Thirumangalam Municipalities; A. Vellalapatti, Vilangudi, Paravai, Thirunagar, Alanganallur and Palamedu Town Panchayats; 1,430 rural habitations in eight Panchayat Unions in Madurai district; and Singampunari Town Panchayat in Sivaganga district, a combined water-supply scheme has been designed at an estimated cost of ₹784 crore to benefit 12.15 lakh people, and administrative sanction has been granted.

For Vellore Municipal Corporation, 11 Municipalities, five Town Panchayats and 944 rural habitations in Vellore district, a combined water-supply scheme has been designed at an estimated cost of ₹1,295 crore to benefit 18.68 lakh people, and administrative sanction has been granted. The foundation stone for this scheme was laid by the Deputy Chief Minister on 25 January 2011.

### Combined water-supply schemes completed between 2006 and 2010

The Tamil Nadu Water Supply and Drainage Board is a pioneer in the country in creating and completing combined water-supply schemes.

Since 2006, 201 combined water-supply schemes with an estimated value of ₹1,734.68 crore have benefited 89.35 lakh people living in 16 Municipalities, 87 Town Panchayats and 13,469 rural habitations.

Among the various completed schemes are:

- a ₹98.85-crore combined water-supply scheme benefiting 1.34 lakh people in Nagapattinam Municipality, Kilvelur and Velankanni Town Panchayats and 890 rural habitations in Nagapattinam district;
- a ₹68-crore combined water-supply scheme benefiting 1.31 lakh people in 239 coastal and rural habitations in Thoothukudi district, and Aruppukottai and Virudhunagar Municipalities, Mallankinaru and Kariapatti Town Panchayats and 67 rural habitations in Virudhunagar district;
- a ₹49.93-crore combined water-supply scheme benefiting 1,41,849 people in Kurichi and Kuniyamuthur Municipalities and Kinathukadavu Town Panchayat in Coimbatore district;
- a ₹29.92-crore combined water-supply scheme benefiting 1.86 lakh people in Ayakudi, Achankuttam, Panmoli, Vadakarai and Keezhapavur Town Panchayats and 151 habitations in the Keezhapavur, Shencottah and Tenkasi Panchayat Unions of Tirunelveli district;
- a ₹23.04-crore combined water-supply scheme benefiting 92,553 people in Chennimalai, K. C. Palayam, Chithode and Nasiyanur Town Panchayats and 69 habitations in the Erode, Chennimalai and Perundurai Panchayat Unions of Erode district; and
- a ₹13.57-crore combined water-supply scheme benefiting 1,23,082 people in Arakkonam and Tiruttani Municipalities in Vellore and Tiruvallur districts.

### Urban drinking-water schemes

Across Tamil Nadu, 134 urban drinking-water schemes valued at ₹201.77 crore have been completed.

These include the Tambaram Municipality drinking-water improvement scheme, valued at ₹32.61 crore and benefiting 1,37,933 people; the Srirangam drinking-water improvement scheme in Tiruchirappalli Municipal Corporation, valued at ₹7.90 crore and benefiting 82,689 people; the Tirupattur Municipality drinking-water improvement scheme in Vellore district, valued at ₹6.48 crore and benefiting 60,876 people; and the Sivakasi Municipality drinking-water improvement scheme in Virudhunagar district, valued at ₹4.89 crore and benefiting 72,168.

### Drinking-water scheme for small and medium towns

Of the 32 drinking-water scheme works sanctioned in six phases at an estimated value of ₹493.54 crore under the Urban Infrastructure Development Scheme for Small and Medium Towns, works have been completed in Tirupattur, Valparai, Karur, Devakottai, Arakkonam, Tiruttani, Ramanathapuram, Paramakudi, Keelakarai, Rameswaram, Aranthangi, Maraimalai Nagar, Sivaganga, Namakkal, Gudalur and Vikramasingapuram Municipalities. In Palladam Municipality, infrastructure works have been completed and revised administrative sanction has been obtained for the combined water-supply works.

Works are in progress in Srivilliputhur, Rasipuram, Thanjavur and Villupuram Municipalities and in Erode Municipal Corporation. Works in Vellakoil, Mettur and Chidambaram Municipalities are at the initial stage.

### New appointments

In the office of the Commissioner of Municipal Administration, 24 people—from one Superintending Engineer through three drivers—have been appointed. In the seven offices of Regional Directors of Municipal Administration, 19 people—from one Executive Engineer through two drivers—have been appointed. Seven Assistant Executive Engineers and seven Second Grade Draftsmen, a total of 14 people, have also been appointed.

Further, in Municipal Corporations and Municipalities, 2,799 people have received permanent appointments through direct recruitment; 1,701 on compassionate grounds; 3,074 daily-wage workers through regularisation of service and placement on time-scale pay; and 134 people who lost employment while working in the Government public sector through re-employment—a total of 7,708 permanent appointments.

The 170 community organisers appointed in Municipalities on consolidated-pay scales were brought from 1 September 2006 under the time-scale of ₹3,200–85–4,900. A further 133 community organisers have been newly appointed.

### Cemetery Assistants

Orders were issued that the workers serving in Municipalities and Municipal Corporations under the source designation `வெட்டியான்கள்` should be called **“Cemetery Assistants”**, and 55 such workers have been appointed as Cemetery Assistants on time-scale pay.

In Chennai Corporation, 1,266 people have been appointed on compassionate grounds and 1,042 directly.

Orders have been issued regularising the service of 328 workers and placing them on time-scale pay. Permanent-service orders have been issued to 2,025 temporary sanitation workers who had served for more than ten years.

Thus, from 2006 to 2010, a total of 4,661 workers benefited in Chennai Corporation.

Through the compassionate-appointment scheme in the Chennai Metropolitan Water Supply and Sewerage Board, appointment orders were issued to 416 legal heirs between 13 May 2006 and 30 September 2010. During the same period, a total of 223 officers and employees were directly recruited through district employment offices: one Deputy Financial Controller, 128 Assistant Engineers, three laboratory technical employees, three draftsmen, 21 drivers, 33 Junior Assistants and 34 Workshop Managers.

In the Tamil Nadu Water Supply and Drainage Board, 48 Junior Assistants / Typists / Junior Drafting Officers / Record Clerks / Office Assistants / Watchmen / Sanitation Workers were appointed on compassionate grounds. Eight hundred and fifty-eight consolidated-pay workers were appointed as time-scale employees. One hundred and seventy-eight honorarium-paid workers were shifted to consolidated-pay employment. Three hundred and ten Assistant Engineers / Junior Engineers / Junior Assistants / Junior Water Analysts / Electrical Supervisors / Drivers were appointed from lower-level service.

Fifty-two Assistant Engineers / Assistant Groundwater Experts were directly recruited.

Udanpirappē, I feel immense happiness on seeing good schemes and works being carried out every day in the Municipal Administration Department. The reason for that happiness is the benefits the country receives through them. You know, just as I do, that the root cause behind all the excellent work of the Municipal Administration Department is the energetic initiative of younger brother Deputy Chief Minister M. K. Stalin! Let your work continue so that those works may continue!

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**20-03-2011**

### புதிய நியமனங்கள்

நகராட்சி நிர்வாக ஆணையர் அலுவலகத்தில் ஒரு கண்காணிப்புப் பொறியாளர் முதற்கொண்டு 3 ஓட்டுநர்கள் வரை 24 பேரும், 7 நகராட்சி நிர்வாக மண்டல இயக்குநர் அலுவலகங்களில் ஒரு செயற்பொறியாளர் முதல் 2 ஓட்டுநர் வரை 19 பேரும், 7 உதவி செயற் பொறியாளர்களும், 7 இரண்டாம்நிலை பட்ட வரைவாளர்களுமாக 14 பேரும் நியமிக்கப்பட்டுள்ளனர்.

மேலும், மாநகராட்சி மற்றும் நகராட்சிகளில், நேரடி நியமனம் மூலம் 2799 பேரும், கருணை அடிப்படையில் 1701 பேரும், தினக்கூலிப் பணியாளர்களுக்குக் காலமுறை ஊதியம் வழங்கி பணி வரன்முறை செய்து 3074 பேரும், அரசு பொதுத்துறையில் பணிபுரிந்து பணியிழந்தவர்களுக்கு மீண்டும் வேலை வாய்ப்பு வழங்கல் மூலம் 134 பேரும் ஆக 7708 பேருக்கு நிரந்தரப் பணி நியமனம் வழங்கப்பட்டுள்ளது.

நகராட்சிகளில் தொகுப்பூதிய விகிதத்தின் கீழ் பணி நியமனம் செய்யப்பட்ட 170 சமுதாய அமைப்பாளர்கள் 1.9.2006 முதல் ரூ.3200-85-4900 என்ற காலமுறை ஊதிய விகிதத்தின்கீழ் கொண்டு வரப்பட்டுள்ளனர். மேலும், 133 சமுதாய அமைப்பாளர்கள் புதிதாக நியமனம் செய்யப்பட்டுள்ளனர்.

### மயான உதவியாளர்கள்

நகராட்சி, மாநகராட்சிகளில் பணிபுரியும் வெட்டியான்கள் ‘மயான உதவியாளர்கள்’ என அழைக்கப்பட வேண்டுமென ஆணையிடப்பட்டு, 55 வெட்டியான்கள் மயான உதவியாளர்களாகக் காலமுறை ஊதியத்தில் நியமனம் செய்யப்பட்டுள்ளனர்.

சென்னை மாநகராட்சியில் 1266 பேருக்குக் கருணை அடிப்படையிலும், 1042 பேருக்கு நேரடியாகவும் பணி நியமனங்கள் வழங்கப்பட்டுள்ளன.

328 பணியாளர்களுக்கு அவர்களது பணிவரன்முறைப்படுத்தப்பட்டு காலமுறை ஊதியம் வழங்க ஆணையிடப்பட்டுள்ளது. 10 ஆண்டு காலத்திற்கு மேல் பணிபுரிந்த 2025 தற்காலிக துப்புரவு பணியாளர்களுக்குப் பணி நிரந்தர ஆணைகள் வழங்கப்பட்டுள்ளன.

இவ்வகையாக 2006 முதல் 2010 வரை சென்னை மாநகராட்சியில் மொத்தம் 4661 பணியாளர்கள் பயன்பெற்றுள்ளனர்.

சென்னைப் பெருநகர் குடிநீர் வழங்கல் மற்றும் கழிவுநீர் அகற்று வாரியத்தில் கருணை அடிப்படையில் பணி வழங்கும் திட்டம் மூலம் 13.05.2006 முதல் 30.9.2010 வரை 416 வாரிசுதாரர்களுக்குப் பணி நியமன ஆணை வழங்கப்பட்டுள்ளது. மேலும், இதே காலகட்டத்தில் ஒரு துணை நிதிக்கட்டுப்பாட்டு அலுவலர், 128 உதவிப் பொறியாளர்கள், 3 ஆய்வக தொழில்நுட்ப பணியாளர்கள், 3 வரைவாளர்கள், 21 ஓட்டுநர்கள், 33 இளநிலை உதவியாளர்கள் மற்றும் 34 பணிமனை மேலாளர் ஆக மொத்தம் 223 அலுவலர் மற்றும் பணியாளர்கள், மாவட்ட வேலைவாய்ப்பு அலுவலகம் மூலம் நேரடியாக நியமனம் செய்யப்பட்டுள்ளனர்.

தமிழ்நாடு குடிநீர் வடிகால் வாரியத்தில் கருணை அடிப்படையில் 48 இளநிலை உதவியாளர்கள்/தட்டச்சர் / இளநிலை வரைவு அலுவலர்/பதிவுறு எழுத்தர்/அலுவலக உதவியாளர்/காவலர்/ துப்புரவாளர் ஆகியோர் பணி நியமனம் செய்யப்பட்டனர். 858 தொகுப்பூதியப் பணியாளர்கள் காலமுறை பணியாளர்களாக நியமிக்கப்பட்டனர். 178 மதிப்பூதிய பணியாளர்கள் தொகுப்பூதியப் பணியாளர்களாகப் பணிமாற்றம் செய்யப்பட்டனர். 310 உதவிப் பொறியாளர்கள் / இளநிலை பொறியாளர்கள்/ இளநிலை உதவியாளர்கள் / இளநிலை நீராய்வாளர்கள்/ மின் கண்காணிப்பாளர்கள்/ ஓட்டுநர்கள் ஆகியோர் கீழ்நிலைப் பணியில் இருந்து பணியமர்வு வழங்கப்பட்டது.
