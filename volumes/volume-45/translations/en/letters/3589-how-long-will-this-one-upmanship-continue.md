# 3589. How Long Will This One-Upmanship Continue?

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This translation is intended to carry Kalaignar's voice into clear, contemporary English rather than recast the letter as literary or academic prose. It preserves the source's argument, political directness, rhetorical questions, repetition, irony, factual detail, and paragraph order. Names, dates, figures, quotations, and intentional English expressions are retained. Where Tamil idiom cannot be reproduced literally without sounding unnatural, the English follows its sense and rhetorical force without adding claims absent from the source. The original Tamil is reproduced in full below the translation and remains the authoritative text. `Udanpirappē` is retained in Tamil transliteration rather than flattened into “brother,” “sister,” or “comrade.” Literally evoking “one born alongside me,” Kalaignar uses it as a distinctive address of shared identity, equality, affection, and solidarity within the movement.

**Udanpirappē,**

When some free schemes were announced in the DMK's 2006 election manifesto, Jayalalithaa was the one who strongly opposed them. She said that free schemes were measures that would make people even poorer. But what was the AIADMK's 2011 election manifesto like? In its editorial of 25-3-2011, *Dinamani* said that it was rather like someone shouting, “Buy one, get one free.”

Following what the AIADMK had announced in its election manifesto—and by slightly expanding schemes of the DMK Government—the scheme under which the DMK Government provided 20 kilograms of rice every month at ₹1 per kilogram has been expanded. They have said: you need not even pay those ₹20 a month; we will give that too free—and they have implemented it. Thus this is a scheme that slightly expands the DMK Government's scheme.

The marriage-assistance scheme for women from poor families was introduced under the DMK Government. In the last five years alone, ₹922 crore was provided as assistance under that scheme. The scheme providing pregnant women ₹1,000 a month for six months, a total of ₹6,000, was also introduced under the DMK Government. The AIADMK Government has announced that it will continue these schemes after slightly expanding them—that is, by increasing the amount of assistance.

The scheme providing free colour television sets to the poor was begun under the DMK Government. Ninety-eight per cent of the beneficiaries had received them, and only about ten lakh people remained. Orders had already been issued to provide sets to those people too. Yet after assuming office, the AIADMK Government announced not only that it would not continue the scheme, but also that it would not provide sets to those ten lakh people who were waiting for them, leaving all of them disappointed. This is an example of holding a closing ceremony for a DMK Government scheme. Another such scheme is the free cooking-gas scheme. The AIADMK Government has not continued that scheme either.

Following the path of the DMK Government in providing free schemes, the Jayalalithaa Government too has come forward to distribute mixers, grinders, electric fans, laptops, goats and cattle free of cost. As time goes on, the people of Tamil Nadu will see and understand how far all these are actually distributed.

The life-saving advanced-treatment scheme that benefited about 2 lakh 62 thousand poor people was introduced by the DMK Government, and village people received treatment available in very large private hospitals and survived. Although I repeatedly said no, the minister concerned under the DMK Government insisted on giving that scheme my name. Could Jayalalithaa's mind tolerate that? She has held a closing ceremony for that scheme and said the same scheme will continue under another name. What does the name matter? It is enough if that good scheme of the DMK Government continues!

The same is true of another DMK Government scheme—the 108 ambulance service! Whenever I travel outside Chennai, one or two people from each village meet me and say that it is only because of that scheme that they are alive, and they bless me to remain well. That scheme too is now being run under this Government without speed or vigour, as though unwillingly.

The first scheme the AIADMK Government tried to close down was Samacheer Kalvi. Jayalalithaa struggled in every possible way to abolish it, particularly by hurriedly introducing an amending law in the Legislative Assembly and passing it with the strength of their majority. Yet because of the courts' principled judgments, the scheme continues. But the irritation and anger this caused the present rulers have produced a continuing lack of concern for the students' future education.

All the changes the AIADMK Government made to the Samacheer Kalvi textbooks in the name of making corrections merely revealed its resentment towards the DMK.

Next, another object of the AIADMK rulers' intense anger was the Secretariat building and the new Legislative Assembly complex built during the DMK Government. Because of the resentment of Amma Jayalalithaa alone, the staff and officials of the Secretariat today suffer from lack of space. Even ruling-party MLAs privately lament that, because of this one woman's stubbornness, they are unable to continue their work in the splendid building that was built for the purpose and stands there majestically, and that officers instead have to work in cramped conditions in the old building.

Why, even the ministers are simmering, unable either to say anything or to swallow it. Amma Jayalalithaa has humiliated the very building that was inaugurated by India's Prime Minister Dr. Manmohan Singh in the presence of United Progressive Alliance guiding leader Sonia Gandhi.

That great complex, designed with the facilities needed by each person in mind, stands magnificently without any opportunity for use because of Amma Jayalalithaa's stubbornness. What will happen if buildings like this are simply left unused for several years?

Because many people have criticised what she has done, she has offered some reason and appointed an inquiry commission under a judge of her choosing. In explaining the Paramakudi police firing in the Assembly, Jayalalithaa said that once an inquiry commission had been appointed, nothing could be done until its report came. Yet after announcing an inquiry commission regarding the Secretariat, she herself has announced that a hospital for advanced treatment will be established in that same building. Why did she not wait for the inquiry commission's recommendation in this case?

Can a high-level hospital be established in a building constructed for the Secretariat, Legislative Assembly complex, Legislative Council and library? If a hospital was to be created, should not all the necessary facilities have been incorporated from the very beginning when the building was constructed? After committing one mistake, must nine more mistakes be made to conceal it?

Another casualty of Amma's insistence that the Secretariat created during the DMK Government must not be used is the Classical Tamil central office and the Pavendhar Classical Tamil Research Library. Without even convening the Cabinet and taking a decision, Jayalalithaa decided overnight to conduct the Legislative Assembly in the place where the Pavendhar Classical Tamil Research Library had been functioning. Because of that decision, the rare research books and old manuscript repositories kept there were removed and thrown out. Even today, under AIADMK rule, no one knows what has become of that Pavendhar Classical Tamil Research Library!

Likewise, with the assistance of the Union Government and with the aim of reducing traffic congestion in Chennai city, the DMK Government announced the “Metro Rail Project,” and work is proceeding at several places. Jayalalithaa, however, has announced that the “Monorail Project” is superior and that she will proceed with that instead. Is this not another scheme born of one-upmanship?

Another excellent DMK Government scheme for which Jayalalithaa held a closing ceremony was the Kalaignar Housing Scheme. It had been introduced with the great objective of creating a Tamil Nadu without huts, and hut-dwelling people had celebrated it with joy. Amma has now held a closing ceremony for it. One thing is certain: the anguish and tears of the 21 lakh hut dwellers of Tamil Nadu who are going to be affected by this will not leave the AIADMK rulers in peace!

Another scheme the AIADMK Government closed was the Legislative Council. Perarignar Anna, Rajaji, Makkal Thilagam M.G.R., Silambu Selvar, Dr. Muthulakshmi Reddy, R. Venkataraman, Dr. P.V. Cherian, Kundrakudi Adigalar, Dr. Lakshmanasamy Mudaliar and many others had served in that Council. The DMK Government made many efforts to restore it, and Parliament had accepted the proposal. The distinction of holding the closing ceremony for that Council belongs to Jayalalithaa.

Similarly, when Rajaraja's thousandth-anniversary celebration was conducted with great distinction at Thanjavur, I named the System of Rice Intensification cultivation method “Rajarajan 1000.” Was it not I who gave it that name? Therefore that name too has now been changed.

Under the DMK Government, twelve new Government engineering colleges were started with the aim of having Government engineering colleges in all districts. As the number of Government engineering colleges in Tamil Nadu increased, the administrative workload of Anna University of Technology in Chennai also increased. With the additional objective that research in engineering technology should be decentralised, five new Anna Universities of Technology were established at Tiruchi, Coimbatore, Tirunelveli, Madurai and Chennai. But with the harmful intention of changing everything done by the DMK Government, the present Government has introduced and passed a Bill in the Assembly to return Chennai Anna University to its previous structure. This is an AIADMK Government effort that will, in the source's argument, damage students' future, the quality of higher education and the necessity of research.

Jayalalithaa announced in the Assembly, as usual under Rule 110, that a satellite town would be established at Thirumazhisai. I have already written in detail about that scheme, the earlier efforts under the DMK Government to establish a satellite town, and the indecent statement Jayalalithaa issued against it at that time. After reading what I wrote, they called the department's officials and asked whether everything I had written was true. Having found out, they simply left the matter without giving any reply.

Five kinds of marriage-assistance schemes are being implemented on behalf of the Tamil Nadu Government: the Moovalur Ramamirtham Ammaiyar Memorial Marriage Assistance Scheme; the Dr. Dharmambal Ammaiyar Memorial Widow Remarriage Assistance Scheme; the E.V.R. Maniammaiyar Memorial Marriage Assistance Scheme for daughters of poor widows; the Mother Teresa Memorial Marriage Assistance Scheme for orphan women; and the Anjugam Ammaiyar Memorial Inter-Caste Marriage Assistance Scheme.

Of these five schemes, Jayalalithaa hurriedly changed only the “Anjugam Ammaiyar Inter-Caste Marriage Assistance Scheme,” which bears my mother's name, and merged it with the Dr. Muthulakshmi Reddy Memorial Inter-Caste Marriage Assistance Scheme.

To implement resolutions adopted at the World Classical Tamil Conference in Coimbatore, such as the “Tolkappiyar Peravai,” the DMK Government allocated ₹100 crore and a Government Order had already been issued. But the present rulers, instead of accepting that scheme, are engaged in an effort to give it another name.

Another decision taken after the Jayalalithaa Government assumed office was to introduce a Bill in the Assembly to shift Tamil New Year Day from the first day of Thai back to the first day of Chithirai and pass it by voice vote.

She has held a closing ceremony for the super-specialty hospital established at Salem at a cost of ₹100 crore.

Udanpirappē, this is how things have been proceeding during these four months of AIADMK rule. The ruler has no thought of thinking and acting independently. The reason is that all their thought is directed towards how to trap DMK leaders in false cases, and how to arrest and imprison them whether evidence exists or not.

They file false cases in every possible way. If a court grants bail in one case, they file some other case against the same people and, with the help of the police, try to keep them in prison continuously. One truth remains: those who sow deeds today will one day have to reap those deeds. I say this to the police too!

**With affection, M.K.**  
19-9-2011
