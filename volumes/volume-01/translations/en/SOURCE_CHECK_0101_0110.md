# Volume 1 — English Source Check: Letters 0101–0110

Status: **PASS — final source-check batch migrated; Volume 1 canonical English source-check gate reaches 110 / 110.**

## Scope

This final Volume 1 source-check batch covers canonical letters **0101–0110**, spanning PDF / printed pages **359–400**.

The preserved legacy bilingual records under `volumes/volume-1/translations/en/letters/` were used only as migration drafts/evidence. The verified canonical Tamil page layer under `volumes/volume-01/pages/` remained authoritative. The controlling scan comparison for the relevant source range is already closed as PASS: PDF **351–375** and **376–400** were visually checked page by page, and letter 0110 closes on PDF 400.

## Method

For every letter in this batch:

1. checked the legacy English draft against the complete canonical Tamil witness;
2. checked for omission, mistranslation, OCR-derived drift, title/date error, altered metaphor, rhetorical flattening and political-language drift;
3. preserved Kalaignar's thought order, repetition, imagery, figures, quotations and movement vocabulary;
4. retained `Udanpirappē` under the established project convention;
5. included the complete canonical Tamil witness under `Original Tamil — மூலத் தமிழ்`;
6. set `translation_status: source-checked` with `source_checked: true` and `full_tamil_included: true`;
7. left `bilingual_alignment_checked: false` and `editorial_consistency_checked: false`.

No record is promoted to `verified` at this gate.

## Batch result

All ten canonical English records **0101–0110** are present and source-checked. The legacy bilingual tree remains unchanged.

### 0101 — `உயிரே! உடன்பிறப்பே! வா! வா!`

Preserves the cadre-as-medicine sequence after Kalaignar's Nilgiris–Coimbatore tour, the peacock/raincloud image, Anna's `கடமை / கண்ணியம் / கட்டுப்பாடு`, the five great slogans, and the contrast between affirmative `வாழ்க` and hurtful `ஒழிக`.

### 0102 — `என் காணிக்கை!`

Preserves the ten-mile Chennai procession, N. V. N.'s emotional appeal against betrayal, the *Ravana Kaviyam* verse, the religious/Congress festival comparison, the sun and rain images, and Kalaignar's closing offering of himself to the cadre's principle-filled slogans.

### 0103 — `பார், பார்-இதோ பார்! இந்தியா பார்!`

Preserves the opening starvation report and delayed Bihar identification, the sequence of newspaper reports from several states, the explicit non-partisan appeal to relieve suffering, and the Chengalpattu / `செங்கை` closing wordplay. The source-printed reports and figures are carried without modernisation.

### 0104 — `‘கல்கி’யின் கண்ணோட்டம்!`

Preserves Kalaignar's distinction between disagreement and deliberate defamation; his defence of Cooum, Valluvar Kottam, Kattabomman memorial and Poompuhar expenditure; fisher welfare, employment and memorial arguments; and the 1967/1971 political comparison culminating in the Assembly-strength rise from 138 to 184.

### 0105 — `‘கல்கி’யின் கண்ணோட்டம் (2)`

Preserves the many-pots-of-milk analogy, Madras Corporation action, industrial-finance sequence, TIDCO/SIPCOT/SIDCO material, source abbreviations and `Grey Iron castings`, industry counts, Industrial Production Index figures **148.1 → 195**, Tamil Nadu growth-index figures **215.18 → 379.15**, and the closing tortoise/goat/rabbit metaphor.

### 0106 — `‘கல்கி’யின் கண்ணோட்டம்(3)`

Preserves the distinction between duty-performing cadres and leaders who lose discipline, the shade-giving-tree quotation from Anna, the distinction between principled realignment and office-seeking defection, and *Kalki*'s own 19 March 1967 federalist/state-autonomy argument against its 1974 position.

### 0107 — `வாழ்க-உன் புன்னகை!`

Preserves the longer Dravidian-movement lineage behind the DMK's twenty-five years, the central `ஆட்சி / மாட்சி` distinction, the roll of earlier leaders and cadres, the private elder-brother/younger-brother bond, N. V. N.'s sacrifice, and the closing transition from tears to the Udanpirappē's smile.

### 0108 — `வாண வேடிக்கை!`

Preserves Kalaignar's stated constitutional position on elected governments, the direct-engagement answer regarding Jayaprakash Narayan, the satire of the `சனநாயகப் பாதுகாப்புப் பேரணி`, Jayaprakash's sacrifice/history sequence, the Bihar/Kerala/Tamil Nadu comparison, and the Deepavali political-fireworks close.

### 0109 — `அவள் ஒரு தொடர்கதை!`

Preserves the actual PDF-392 heading `அவள் ஒரு தொடர்கதை!` rather than the contents-page variant `அவள் ஒரு தொடற்கதை!`; the Kavitha/family/DMK allegory; both repeated epithet lists; and the opaque source phrase `நமப்பார்வதி பதேக்கள்!` without speculative reconstruction.

### 0110 — `கயிற்றில் தொங்கிய கணபதி!`

Preserves the 1974 Sambasivam meeting, the 1949 publication history, the long quoted passage from *கயிற்றில் தொங்கிய கணபதி*, the flower/fragrance argument against fatalism, the critique of hunger-driven migration to Malaya and Ceylon, the source's four-exclamation form `தமிழகமே!!!!`, and the final Tamil-rights statement. Letter 0110 closes on PDF 400 with `(01-12-1974)`; PDF 401 is non-letter back-cover material.
