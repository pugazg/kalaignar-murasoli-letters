# Textual-Fidelity Audit — Letters 3780–3784

## Scope

- Letters: **3780–3784**
- Canonical Markdown pages: **PDF 132–168**
- Total pages visually compared: **37**
- Source PDF: `TVA_BOK_0065839_கலைஞரின்_கடிதங்கள்_தொகுதி_49.pdf`

## Method

Before translation, every canonical Markdown page was visually compared with its corresponding PDF scan. The audit checked letter numbers and titles, salutations and closings, paragraph sequence, page boundaries, names, dates, figures, quotations, intentional English expressions, punctuation affecting meaning, omissions, duplicated text and OCR artefacts. OCR was not treated as authoritative; the scan controlled every decision.

## Source features preserved

- Letter 3780 does not introduce the later acronym **NEET**; the translation discusses the nationwide common medical entrance examination in the terminology of the 2013 source.
- Letter 3781 retains the complete chronological argument built from 2006 newspaper reports and quoted political responses, as well as the concluding *Tirukkural*.
- Letter 3782 preserves the two long English newspaper quotations from *The Hindu*, including the original wording and capitalization.
- Letter 3783 reproduces Clause 2.14 of the Rajiv–Jayewardene Accord in English exactly as printed and preserves the distinction between TESO’s preferred referendum-based solution and the Thirteenth Amendment as an interim measure.
- Letter 3784 retains both English quotations attributed to Jayalalithaa and the source’s double opening quotation mark before `இந்தியாவிற்கே` on PDF page 168.
