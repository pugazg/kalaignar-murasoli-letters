### 3560 — *Those Above Praise; Those Below Revile!*

**Result: ALIGNED AFTER ONE ENGLISH-ONLY CORRECTION.**

The five-year achievements inventory, welfare and infrastructure figures, foodgrain/per-capita-income comparisons, quoted Manmohan Singh and Pranab Mukherjee passages, awards, World Bank material, Wall Street Journal passage, Oxford Analytica / `India Deconstructed` passage and final argument align with the audited Tamil. The scan-proven PDF 187 restoration is preserved exactly through the physical transition into PDF 188.

One English semantic/causal error was corrected in the Pranab Mukherjee quotation. Tamil says the Tamil Nadu government had not obtained an overdraft from the Reserve Bank even for one day **because of having no money in its account** (`ஒருநாள் கூட தன் கணக்கில் பணம் இல்லாமல், கூடுதல் வரைவுத் தொகையைத் ... பெற்றதில்லை`). The earlier English, “has not taken an overdraft ... because there was no money in its account,” wrongly made the absence of money the reason an overdraft was not taken. The aligned English now reads: **“The Tamil Nadu government has not had to obtain an overdraft from the Reserve Bank of India even for a single day because of having no money in its account.”** Canonical Tamil is unchanged.
