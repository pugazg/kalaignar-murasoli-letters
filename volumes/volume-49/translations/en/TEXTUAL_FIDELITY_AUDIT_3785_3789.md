# Textual-Fidelity Audit — Letters 3785–3789

## Scope

- Volume: **49**
- Letters: **3785–3789**
- Canonical Tamil Markdown pages: **PDF 169–202**
- Total pages visually compared with scans: **34/34**
- Method: each canonical page was compared against its corresponding rendered PDF scan before translation. Titles, letter numbers, paragraph order, quotations, English passages, figures, punctuation, signatures, dates and page boundaries were checked.

## Result

- All **34** pages were inspected.
- No paragraph or passage was missing.
- Ten scan-verified transcription artefacts were corrected in the canonical Tamil pages before the bilingual files were assembled.
- The corrections are limited to visible spacing, word-boundary, quotation-mark and grammatical-sandhi transcription errors. They do not modernise or rewrite the source.

## Source-exact features retained

- The English passages quoted in letters 3785, 3786, 3788 and 3789 remain as printed, including their original grammar and punctuation.
- The visibly printed string `Goverflouisleiro` on PDF page 189 is preserved without speculative correction.
- The printed parenthetical form `Jean D Reze` is preserved in the Tamil source; the English translation notes its treatment.
- Dates, percentages, financial amounts, institutional titles and cited statistics are retained as presented in the volume and are not externally reconciled in this source-fidelity pass.

## Translation assembly

Each English file contains:

1. the standard Translator’s note;
2. a complete thought-preserving English translation;
3. letter-specific notes where needed; and
4. the full corrected canonical Tamil text, divided by original PDF page.

This audit establishes **source-checked** status for letters **3785–3789**. Full bilingual line-by-line editorial review remains a later, separate stage.
