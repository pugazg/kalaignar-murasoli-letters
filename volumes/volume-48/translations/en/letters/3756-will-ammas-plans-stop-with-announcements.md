# 3756. Will Amma’s Plans Stop at Announcements!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This translation is intended to carry Kalaignar's voice into clear, contemporary English rather than recast the letter as literary or academic prose. It preserves the source's argument, political directness, rhetorical questions, repetition, irony, factual detail, and paragraph order. Names, dates, figures, quotations, and intentional English expressions are retained. Where Tamil idiom cannot be reproduced literally without sounding unnatural, the English follows its sense and rhetorical force without adding claims absent from the source. The original Tamil is reproduced in full below the translation and remains the authoritative text. `Udanpirappē` is retained in Tamil transliteration rather than flattened into “brother,” “sister,” or “comrade.” Literally evoking “one born alongside me,” Kalaignar uses it as a distinctive address of shared identity, equality, affection, and solidarity within the movement.

**Date:** 15 May 2013

**Udanpirappē,**

In the statement that Chief Minister Jayalalithaa read in the Assembly under Rule 110 on 30 April 2013, she announced:

> “Taking into account the traffic congestion prevailing at the Koyambedu suburban bus terminus, and also the fact that more than half the buses leaving from here are bound for the southern districts, I am happy to inform the House that a suburban bus terminus exclusively for buses arriving from and departing to the southern districts will be established near Vandalur railway station, adjoining the Vandalur–Walajabad State Highway, between the Southern Trunk Road and the Outer Ring Road.”

News has now appeared that farmers in the area have opposed the Chief Minister's announcement that a suburban bus terminus would be established at Vandalur. According to that report, the farmers opposing the new bus terminus said:

> “Many families are cultivating about 100 acres of wet agricultural land adjoining the Vandalur–Walajabad road. The government has already taken many acres of land for road widening and for establishing the Vandalur zoo. When the then Chief Minister M.G.R. opened the Vandalur zoo in 1985, he said that a great deal of land had already been acquired in Vandalur and that not one more piece of land would be taken. But today's Chief Minister Jayalalithaa has now announced that a suburban bus terminus will be established on agricultural land. To protect the livelihood of the farmers, the suburban bus terminus must be shifted elsewhere.”

This is what *Dinamalar* reported.

The moment the Chief Minister read the statement in the Assembly announcing a new bus terminus at Vandalur, I had asked: **“What happened to the schemes already drawn up to establish new bus termini at Velachery and Madhavaram?”** Although no reply came from the government, today's edition of the same *Dinamalar* (14 May 2013) carries a report under the headline **“Velachery outstation bus terminus being abandoned — CMDA's new decision.”** The report says:

> “The Chennai Metropolitan Development Authority (CMDA) is learnt to have decided to abandon altogether the plan to establish an outstation bus terminus at Velachery in Chennai. When measures were studied to reduce vehicular congestion at the suburban bus terminus in Koyambedu, it was decided to establish terminals for outstation buses at Velachery and Madhavaram. An announcement to this effect was made in the Tamil Nadu Budget speech for 2011–12. Accordingly, ₹48 crore was allotted to establish a bus terminus on 12 acres of vacant land in the elevated railway station complex at Velachery, and ₹32 crore to establish a bus terminus on eight acres at Madhavaram.
>
> “Along with allocating funds for these two projects, administrative approval was also granted and a Government Order was issued on 31 October 2011, during the AIADMK government. Of the two, the land selected for the bus terminal at Velachery would be required for the ‘monorail’ project proposed by the Transport Department. The Transport Department therefore stated that the land for the bus terminal could be finalised after determining how much land would be needed for the monorail project. Until then, the CMDA had decided to keep this project on hold.”

The report continues:

> “Recently, the Tamil Nadu government announced a scheme to establish a new suburban bus terminus on 65 acres at Vandalur to serve buses to the southern districts. In this situation, reports have emerged that the scheme to establish an outstation bus terminus at Velachery is to be abandoned altogether.”

Along with this report, a senior CMDA official is quoted as saying:

> “It has been decided to abandon the scheme to establish a terminus for outstation buses at Velachery. A letter on this has been written to the Secretary of the Housing and Urban Development Department.”

Now the Chief Minister has read a statement under Rule 110 announcing a suburban bus terminus at Vandalur. Farmers in that area, citing M.G.R.'s assurance, have opposed the announcement. In this situation, what is the Tamil Nadu government going to do? Will the suburban bus terminus come up at Vandalur despite the farmers' opposition? What is to become of the suburban bus termini proposed for Velachery and Madhavaram? When will the Chief Minister read an explanation on all this under yet another Rule 110 statement?

Oh! Perhaps Rule 110 does not permit explanations to be given?

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**15 May 2013**

## Letter-specific notes

1. **Amma** is retained in the English title because the Tamil title itself uses `அம்மா` as the political-honorific reference around which the letter's irony is framed.
2. **CMDA** is expanded on first reference as the Chennai Metropolitan Development Authority, matching the source's parenthetical identification.
3. The source presents different land extents in different reported contexts: farmers refer to **100 acres** of wet agricultural land, while the later newspaper report describes the newly announced Vandalur terminus as occupying **65 acres**. Both figures are preserved without reconciliation.
4. **Rule 110** is retained as the Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly procedural reference used throughout the letter.
