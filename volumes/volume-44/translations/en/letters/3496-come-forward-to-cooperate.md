# 3496. Come Forward to Cooperate!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This translation follows the audited canonical Tamil and the locked Volume 44 conventions. The source’s period terminology `மாற்றுத் திறனாளிகள்` is rendered **differently abled persons** because the letter itself contrasts that designation with the older `ஊனமுற்றோர்`; it is not modernised to a different label. Budget paragraphs, benefit figures, quoted hunger-strikers’ response and the final tender argument are retained without outside reconciliation. The complete audited Tamil is reproduced below and remains authoritative.

**Tamil source:** [Letter 3496](../../../chapters/3496-oththuzhaiththida-mun-varuveer.md)  
**Source pages:** [PDF 112](../../../pages/page-112.md)–[PDF 117](../../../pages/page-117.md)  
**Date:** 3 September 2010

**Udanpirappē,**

Some newspapers carry reports that differently abled persons are conducting agitations here and there, holding demonstrations and going on hunger strikes. One association representing them announced yesterday that differently abled persons would conduct protests throughout Tamil Nadu from September 3 to September 10, pressing demands such as employment for all differently abled persons or a monthly unemployment relief payment of ₹3,000.

At a time when no other side showed concern for differently abled persons, it was I who took a great interest. In the Budget for 2010–2011, paragraphs 87, 88, 89, 90, 91, 92 and 93 — seven paragraphs in all — were written about them. Never before had seven paragraphs in any year’s Budget been devoted to the welfare of differently abled persons. And the return for that is that a few among them engage in such agitations against the government. Even so, when this government spoke in the Budget about differently abled persons, it said:

> “The income ceiling prescribed for these ‘differently abled persons’ to receive assistance under the various welfare schemes of the State government will be completely removed. This government, which exempted differently abled persons from payment of tuition fees to enable them to pursue higher education, will from the coming academic year also exempt them from payment of special fees. For students residing and studying in homes for ‘differently abled’ students run by the government and voluntary organisations, the present monthly food assistance of ₹200 will be increased to ₹450. For the first time in India, a separate welfare board has been established in Tamil Nadu to provide social security and welfare assistance to differently abled persons.
>
> “Because this government is providing a monthly assistance of ₹500, without any income ceiling, to persons with intellectual disabilities who cannot care for themselves, 50,000 people are benefiting. Various assistance payments for the visually impaired and other differently abled persons have been doubled. Special employment opportunities have been provided for them to work as teachers. Schools to identify hearing impairment in early childhood and provide special education have been started in every district. Along with travel concessions for persons with disabilities of the arms and legs, for the first time in the country motor-fitted three-wheeled vehicles and various other assistive devices have been provided.
>
> “The unemployment-relief scheme for visually impaired persons without employment has been extended to all categories of differently abled persons, and 60,000 people have benefited from it. For the first time in India, from 2008–2009, 1,000 persons affected by muscular dystrophy have been receiving monthly assistance of ₹900. The amount spent on the welfare of differently abled persons, which was about ₹49 crore in 2005–2006, has been increased to ₹176 crore for the coming financial year, and a separate department for differently abled persons will function under my direct supervision.”

Going further, it was under this government that it was announced that, instead of calling them `ஊனமுற்றோர்`, they should, in recognition of their individual abilities, be called `மாற்றுத் திறனாளிகள்` — differently abled persons.

Just as relief is given to the visually impaired, unemployment relief is being provided to all other categories of unemployed differently abled persons: ₹450 for graduates, ₹375 for those who have studied through Standard XII, and ₹300 for those who have studied through Standard X or below. Under the previous government they were not given even one rupee of this kind of assistance.

In accordance with the announcement in the 2010–2011 demand for grants, a “Relief Fund for Differently Abled Persons Who Suffer Distress” was established. Under this scheme, a relief fund was created to assist differently abled persons affected by severe acts of violence, and up to ₹1 lakh has been allotted to each district.

This year, 1,500 differently abled persons with more than one disability and those severely affected are to be provided folding wheelchairs free of cost. Since differently abled persons severely affected by cerebral palsy have stiffness in the muscles of their arms and legs, newly designed special chairs suited to their individual needs are proposed to be provided by the government to 500 persons in the first phase. The government has allotted ₹5 lakh to each Member of the Legislative Assembly for purchasing and providing assistive devices to differently abled persons in the respective constituency.

The achievements made by the DMK government for differently abled persons have been explained many times, so I do not now wish to explain them all over again.

When the Tamil Nadu government is doing so much for differently abled persons, I once went personally to the General Hospital when they were on hunger strike there and asked them: why do you conduct protests against it, why do you go on hunger strike? Their reply to me was: “You do not have to do anything for us. When we had been on hunger strike for two days and nobody was concerned about us, it is enough that you, the Chief Minister yourself, came this morning without even having taken food and met us.”

I then asked them whether, when this government was successively granting concessions to them, it was fair to go directly on hunger strike without making a representation to anyone. They replied: “It is true that under the previous government we never made demands and agitated like this. It is under this government that we agitate and go on hunger strike. The reason is that only if we ask now will something happen and deliverance come to us. If it were AIADMK rule, they would send the police, chase us away, and there would be nobody even to listen. The very reason we agitate now is that if somehow the news reaches your ears, relief will come immediately. We make these demands even though we know that what we are asking is at the maximum — because we believe relief will come.”

As far as I am concerned, it is not only differently abled persons: I want everyone to live well without any kind of difficulty. That is why, even at this age, I work and think day and night. I keep thinking about what can be done for whom so that they may have a good life. But when there is a government, do we not also have to see how much that government can spend? My thought is that, with the funds available to the government, every section should be satisfied to the greatest extent possible.

And with a general election due in only a few months, every section thinks its needs should be fulfilled before then. The method they choose for that is agitation! In truth, their purpose is not to wage a struggle against this government. At the same time, a few politicians, with the ulterior motive of growing their own associations, incite these innocent people and seek to warm themselves at the fire. I know very well that, even though those employees understand this too, they sometimes become caught up in it without realising.

To those who are trying to agitate because the general election is approaching, I say this: you need not fear that this government will change and that your needs will then go unfulfilled. The government certainly will not change! Why should it change? Have your needs not been attended to under this government? Has any section been neglected? Have there been corruptions? Do you not know the concessions announced for you by this government? Are you receiving those concessions or not? Is there any deficiency or corruption in the schemes being implemented through those announcements?

Colour television sets have been purchased and supplied to about one and a half crore families. Has any complaint arisen in that? Under this government, however, a committee containing members of all parties was formed, that committee was invited, and the “tenders” themselves were opened and decisions taken in their presence. Will the people of Tamil Nadu not know all those details?

Therefore, there is no need to think that the government will change and your demands will remain unfulfilled. Give up agitation, demonstrations and hunger strikes against this government, and come forward to cooperate with the efforts of this government — come forward, I call upon you!

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**3-9-2010**

## Letter-specific notes

1. `மாற்றுத் திறனாளிகள்` is rendered **differently abled persons** to preserve the source’s own 2010-era naming contrast with `ஊனமுற்றோர்`; the terminology is not silently modernised.
2. The source phrase `மாற்றுத் திறனாளிகளுக்கான துயர் உறுவோர் நிவாரண நிதி` is unusual as printed; the English gives its immediate functional sense while the exact Tamil remains unchanged below.
3. Figures for monthly relief, beneficiaries, welfare expenditure, assistive devices and constituency allocations are retained exactly as stated in the audited source.
4. The quoted account of why protesters said they agitated under this government is preserved without softening its political contrast.
