# 3438. The DMK Government as a Walking Stick for Persons with Disabilities!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This translation follows the actual letter-start title on PDF 099, where `ஊன்றுகோலாகக்` is joined; the printed contents independently has `ஊன்று கோலாகக்`. The source’s welfare terminology, scheme sequence, beneficiary counts, percentages and rupee figures are preserved. English uses “persons with disabilities” for the source category `ஊனமுற்றோர்` without importing a later legal classification.

**Tamil source:** [Letter 3438](../../../chapters/3438-oonamutrorin-oonrukolaaga-kazhaga-arasu.md)  
**Source pages:** [PDF 99](../../../pages/page-099.md)–[PDF 103](../../../pages/page-103.md)  
**Date:** 4 December 2009

**Udanpirappē,**

Today is World Day of Persons with Disabilities. Everyone knows that the DMK government has been continuously working with comprehensive rehabilitation as its principal objective — special education to build self-confidence, vocational training, placement in employment, assistance for self-employment and the free provision of assistive devices — so that persons with disabilities may reach a position in which they have self-confidence and are free of anxiety about their economic condition.

Persons with disabilities themselves know well that, when it comes to the need to help them, the DMK government is second to none. What is required is genuine assistance to persons with disabilities. Merely writing statements saying “I did this for them, I did that for them” cannot by itself bring them benefits.

On this occasion, I wish briefly to set out the welfare schemes formulated under the DMK government to improve the lives of persons with disabilities.

When the DMK assumed office in 2006–2007, the amount allocated to this sector was about ₹39 crore. Under DMK rule, this allocation was raised to ₹78 crore in 2007–2008. If the allocation for this sector was raised to ₹107 crore in 2009–2010, one can understand the genuine concern the DMK government has for persons with disabilities.

For the first time in India, under a model scheme for persons affected by “muscular dystrophy,” ₹500 per month is being provided as assistance, benefiting 1,000 persons a year.

Under the maintenance-assistance scheme for persons with severe disabilities, ₹500 per month is currently being provided to 10,000 persons.

For persons with intellectual disabilities of 60 per cent and above who are unable to care for themselves, monthly maintenance assistance of ₹500 is being provided without upper limits based on age, income or number of beneficiaries; 50,600 persons are benefiting.

In the 2007–2008 financial year, maintenance assistance had been provided to 20,000 beneficiaries. Last year, a further 30,600 persons with intellectual disabilities were added and funds were allocated so that a total of 50,600 persons could benefit. Across these three schemes, ₹38.00 crore a year has been allocated so that 61,600 persons may benefit.

Because the DMK government is a government concerned about persons with disabilities, Government Order (Ms.) No. 59, Social Welfare and Nutritious Meal Programme Department, dated 24-4-2007, ordered the constitution of the Tamil Nadu Welfare Board for Persons with Disabilities so as to provide them comprehensive social security. ₹100 lakh is allocated every year for schemes operated through the Welfare Board. So far, a total of ₹2.00 crore has been spent from this fund for the benefit of 3,471 persons.

Assistance has been provided to those 3,471 persons as follows: educational assistance to 450; marriage assistance to 115; maternity assistance to 17; assistance to 57 for purchasing spectacles; funeral assistance to 180; accident relief to 2; motorised three-wheelers to 100; assistance to 150 for setting up petty shops in public places; gloves and knee pads to 2,250; and assistance to 150 under the self-employment scheme.

When assistance has been provided to this extent through the Board, and expenditure has even exceeded the amount allocated, some people have issued statements concealing this and claiming that adequate funds were not allocated and that people with disabilities were being deceived in the name of a board. The people of Tamil Nadu will understand very well what kind of fraudulent politics that is.

Under the free bus-travel concession scheme for persons with disabilities, assistance was provided to 19,982 persons in 2006–2007, 20,818 persons in 2007–2008 and 31,243 persons in 2008–2009. Over these three years, the government spent ₹28.35 crore on this scheme.

Under the marriage-assistance scheme for persons with disabilities, ₹10,000 is given for wedding expenses and, keeping their future needs in view, another ₹10,000 is given as a National Savings Certificate, making a total of ₹20,000. The ₹10,000 National Savings Certificate doubles after seven and a half years to ₹20,000. This method is followed because it gives greater benefit to persons with disabilities.

The scheme to provide motorised three-wheelers (scooters) free to persons with disabilities was introduced by the DMK government from 2007–2008. During the two years 2007–2008 and 2008–2009, they were provided free to 120 students with disabilities and with a subsidy of ₹10,000 each to 580 employed persons with disabilities; ₹1 crore was spent for this purpose. Further, through the Tamil Nadu Welfare Board for Persons with Disabilities, ₹34.50 lakh has been spent this year to provide motorised three-wheelers (scooters) to 100 persons with disabilities.

I personally met representatives of associations of persons with disabilities on 19-08-2007 and immediately announced the following rehabilitation measures:

- A government order was issued announcing that 300 graduate-teacher posts would be filled by visually impaired persons as a special measure.
- A high-level committee was constituted to ensure 3 per cent reservation in employment for persons with disabilities.
- Persons with disabilities were given a 75 per cent fare concession on government buses.
- Under the Prime Minister’s Employment Scheme, an order was issued for the Tamil Nadu government itself to bear the 5 per cent margin money payable by persons with disabilities.
- Students with disabilities pursuing higher education were exempted from tuition fees.
- Just as unemployment relief was being provided to visually impaired persons, unemployment relief is also being provided to persons with other types of disabilities.
- A State policy document for persons with disabilities was issued.

For the rehabilitation of persons with disabilities, the following schemes were announced in this year’s 2009–10 Demand for Grants and are being implemented:

- Orders have been issued to provide motorised sewing machines free to persons with disabilities.
- Under the maintenance-assistance scheme for persons with intellectual disabilities, the number of beneficiaries has been raised from 20,000 to 50,600.
- For physically disabled students studying from Classes 1 to 8 to receive educational assistance, for subsidies under the self-employment scheme for persons with disabilities, for maintenance assistance to persons with severe disabilities, and for assistive devices, the previously fixed income ceiling of ₹12,000 has been raised to ₹24,000.
- An order is to be issued within a few days to provide hearing aids and solar-powered batteries to 6,000 persons with hearing impairment.
- An order has been issued creating 77 secondary-grade teacher posts for visually impaired persons.
- An order has been issued to provide ₹20,000 assistance when a person with a disability marries another person with a disability.

Thus, without waiting for anyone’s recommendation, the DMK government has been providing these benefits and concessions to persons with disabilities. Since today is World Day of Persons with Disabilities, I am happy to use the occasion to set out all these details. On this day I assure you that the DMK government will continue to extend assistance to persons with disabilities in every further way possible.

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**4-12-2009**

3. `தசை சிதைவு` is rendered **muscular dystrophy**, while the source’s scheme figures and eligibility wording remain unchanged.
4. `மனவளர்ச்சி குன்றியோர்` is rendered **persons with intellectual disabilities**; no outside diagnostic standard is imported.
