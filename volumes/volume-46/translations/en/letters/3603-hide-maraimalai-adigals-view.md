# 3603. Are They Hiding Maraimalai Adigal's View?

**Udanpirappē,**

From the time the AIADMK won the general election held last May and assumed office, the people of Tamil Nadu know very well that, whether or not they are doing the good things people need, they have continuously been engaged in putting an end to, and diverting, all the important schemes carried out under the DMK government. To what extent has it gone? Even the Legislative Assembly offices and Parliament offices allotted to Members of the Legislative Assembly and Members of Parliament have been taken back. Long live their broad and noble hearts!

In that same line, at yesterday's AIADMK General Council meeting, a special resolution was passed thanking the Chief Minister for having introduced through the Hindu Religious and Charitable Endowments Minister a Bill in the Assembly to change the law enacted during the DMK government declaring the first day of the month of Thai as Tamil New Year, and to restore the first day of Chithirai as Tamil New Year, and for having passed it by voice vote. Does that bring disgrace upon the DMK or the government it conducted? No.

During the DMK government, in the announcement made in his address to the Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly on 23 January 2008, His Excellency the Governor said:

> “Under the leadership of the great scholar Maraimalai Adigal, founder of the Pure Tamil Movement, more than five hundred eminent Tamil scholars assembled in 1921 at Pachaiyappa's College in Chennai. Considering that Tamils needed a distinct era of their own, they decided to follow a continuous year count in the name of Ayyan Thiruvalluvar, to take that as the ‘Tamil Year,’ and to fix the year of Thiruvalluvar's birth as 31 BCE. Honourable Chief Minister Kalaignar accepted this view thirty-seven years ago and ordered it to be implemented in the Tamil Nadu Government diary from 1971 and in the Tamil Nadu Government Gazette from 1972. Since all Tamil scholars as a whole have accepted that the first day of the month of Thai, when the Thiruvalluvar year begins, is the beginning of the Tamil New Year, the Kazhagam government has decided to declare and implement the first day of Thai as the beginning of the Tamil New Year. Therefore, the people of Tamil Nadu, who celebrate Pongal as the festival of Tamils, will henceforth combine with it the birth of the Tamil New Year and celebrate the day with double joy—planting the three fruit trees, banana, mango and jackfruit; drawing colourful kolams; illuminating homes with rows of lamps so that they shine with new beauty; wearing new clothes; singing and dancing in praise of Tamil honour and self-respect; spreading the feeling of equality; and pouring out love in leaping joy that this is the Tamil New Year.”

This announcement was made amid loud applause. Thus even that announcement, as an announcement of the Kazhagam government, was made by the Governor in his address.

At that meeting held under the leadership of Maraimalai Adigal, eminent scholars including Tamil Thendral Thiru. Vi. Kalyanasundaranar, Tamil guardian Ka. Subramania Pillai, Saivite elder Sachidananda Pillai, Navalar N. M. Venkatasamy Nattar, Navalar Somasundara Bharathiar and Muthamizh guardian Ki. Aa. Pe. Viswanatham participated. The three decisions they took were:

1. To follow a continuous year count in the name of Thiruvalluvar;
2. To celebrate that itself as the Tamil Year;
3. To adopt the method by which adding 31 BCE, the accepted period of Thiruvalluvar, to the current year gives the Thiruvalluvar Year.

After that, in 1939, the All India Tamils Conference met in Tiruchi under the leadership of Somasundara Bharathiar. Thanthai Periyar, Karanthai Tamil Sangam president Umamaheswaranar, Professor Ka. Subramaniam, Te. Po. Meenakshisundaranar, Thiru. Vi. Ka., Maraimalai Adigal, P. T. Rajan, Arcot Ramasamy Mudaliar, Revolutionary Poet Bharathidasan, Pattukkottai Azhagiri and many others participated. That conference too resolved that the first day of Thai was the Tamil New Year and that the Pongal festival itself was the festival of the Tamils.

Revolutionary Poet Bharathidasan wrote:

> “O Tamil, sleeping in slumber!  
> Chithirai is not your Tamil New Year!  
> The sixty years shown by the Aryan crowd  
> that came here to live off us  
> do not accord with reason.  
> For the Tamil who ruled the earth,  
> the first day of Thai alone is Tamil New Year.”

If that is what he wrote, it is enough if Tamils understand that the resolution now passed by the AIADMK General Council is an insult inflicted upon all those Tamil scholars who lived and moved in Tamil Nadu!

The very next day after the Governor's announcement was read in the Assembly, Thavathiru Kundrakudi Ponnambala Adigalar said in a statement:

> “From ancient times, the first festival day of the month of Thai, connected with religion, natural life, the soil and its people, and agricultural life, has been a festival that gives rise and joy to Tamil life. Thanks and appreciation to Muthamizh scholar and Chief Minister Kalaignar for implementing the declaration by eminent senior Tamil scholars such as Maraimalai Adigal that, beginning from the first day of Thai, the Tamil New Year should be observed in the name of Ayyan Thiruvalluvar.”

Dravidar Kazhagam president and Tamil leader, younger brother Veeramani, said:

> “Our long-standing demand that the first day of the month of Thai should be the beginning of the Tamil New Year has succeeded. This is a new renaissance chapter in the direction of Tamil cultural history. We appreciate it and rejoice.”

Kaviperarasu Vairamuthu said in his statement:

> “Sentiment-filled Tamils are celebrating with joy on seeing the Kalaignar government announce in the Governor's address that the first day of Thai is the beginning of the Tamil New Year. Every nationality has certain indestructible identities. Tamils have a territorial identity; they have an ethnic identity; but only their identity of time remained confused. Today that darkness of confusion has broken and light has dawned. It was the Dravidian movement that presented Ayyan Thiruvalluvar as the measure of the world of ideas. Today the Kalaignar government has also made Thiruvalluvar the measure of time. This is history that sets history right.”

Many schemes implemented during the Kazhagam government now face great danger. In the history of Tamil Nadu, there does exist a contrary-minded group that is hostile even to Tamil itself—one that hates, rejects and ignores even the very idea of Classical Tamil. Even in earlier times, there were one or two poets who hated Tamil in this way. Here is a story! It is one I have written before; still, I will remind you of it once more.

In the time of Nakkiran, a man called Kuyakkondan, at a debate held in the Tamil Sangam, is said to have declared, “Aryam is good; Tamil is bad.” Nakkiran immediately became angry and is said to have sung an *aram* verse: “You who said Tamil is bad and the northern language is good—may you die.” Kuyakkondan at once fell down and died. Some of those present then asked Nakkiran to bring Kuyakkondan back to life. Hearing this, Nakkiran sang:

> “For the deed of saying Aryam is good and Tamil is bad,  
> he fell into the snare of Yama—  
> by the command of Agastya of the noble sacred Podhigai,  
> O classical Tamil, restore him—swaha!”

And Kuyakkondan is said to have come back to life. Such an imaginary tale! A story that circulated in ancient times!

At the very time the Tamil Nadu Government's announcement was made, the distinguished Tamil researcher Iravatham Mahadevan said:

> “If we do not have the courage to examine and correct today's almanacs scientifically in accordance with astronomy and the present cycle of the seasons, what is wrong in celebrating the sixty-day Pongal festival as the New Year? Even historically, can we not accept this as one among the many New Years observed in India today?”

Following the announcement in the Governor's address, I introduced the Tamil Nadu Tamil New Year Bill in the Assembly on 29 January 2008. Debate on the Bill took place on 1 February 2008, and it was passed.

Today, Tamils in Malaysia celebrate the first day of Thai itself as New Year. Dr. Mu. Varadarasanar explained:

> “In ancient times the beginning of the year was not the first day of Chithirai. Elders celebrated the first day of Thai itself as the beginning of the year. On that day they would buy and wear new clothes. They would spread fresh sand and red earth on the streets and put them in order. Festivals would be held throughout the town. In this way Pongal was celebrated in towns as the birth of the New Year.”

Because it was something enacted under the DMK government, could it be allowed to remain? Under the AIADMK government, a Bill was introduced and passed in the Assembly to put an end to it, and now the AIADMK General Council has passed a resolution thanking the government even for that.

PMK founder Dr. Ramadoss said in a statement that this decision of the AIADMK government was one that would cause pain to those with Tamil feeling; that it was not proper suddenly to shift Tamil New Year back to the month of Chithirai; and that it would become a very great historical error.

Further, in her Assembly speech Jayalalithaa said that the law enacted by the Kazhagam government benefited no one; that at the felicitation function held in Chennai after the law was enacted, all of Karunanidhi's “praise-singers” had extolled it; and that the declaration of the first day of Thai as Tamil New Year had been made only to publicise me.

Do you know who the eminent people described by that lady as “praise-singers” were? Our Professor Anbazhagan; Va. Se. Kulandaisamy, who won great distinction as Vice-Chancellor of Anna University and Indira Gandhi Open University; Dr. Tamilannal; Dr. V. I. Subramaniam; Dr. Agathiyalingam; Dr. Avvai Natarajan; Dr. R. Nagaswamy; Thavathiru Ooran Adigal; Professor Kana. Chirsabesan; Dr. A. Arivoli; Dr. Sudha Seshayyan; Sri Lankan E. Jayaraj; Dr. Saraswathi Ramanathan; advocate T. Ramalingam; Professor Thi. Rajagopalan; Dr. Rama. Soundaravalli; Dr. R. Selvaganapathy; and Pulavar Ko. Sarangapani!

Let them do whatever they wish and go their way. As far as we are concerned, the first day of the month of Thai is the day on which the Tamil New Year begins. At the same time, we had announced even then that we would not say no to, or prevent, those who celebrate a festival on the first day of Chithirai. As far as we are concerned, we will celebrate the first day of Thai itself as Tamil New Year.

**With affection,**  
**M.K.**  
**1 January 2012**

மறைமலை அடிகள் தலைமையில் தமிழ்த் தென்றல் திரு.வி. கல்யாண சுந்தரனார், தமிழ்க் காவலர் கா. சுப்பிரமணியப் பிள்ளை, சைவப் பெரியார் சச்சிதானந்தப் பிள்ளை, நாவலர் ந.மு. வேங்கடசாமி நாட்டார், நாவலர் சோமசுந்தர பாரதியார், முத்தமிழ்க் காவலர் கி.ஆ.பெ. விசுவநாதம் உள்ளிட்ட அறிஞர் பெருமக்கள் எல்லாம் கலந்து கொண்ட அந்தக் கூட்டத்தில் அவர்கள் எடுத்த முடிவுகள் மூன்று :

1. திருவள்ளுவர் பெயரில் தொடர் ஆண்டாகப் பின்பற்றுவது;

மேலும் ஜெயலலிதா தனது பேரவை உரையிலே கழக ஆட்சி நிறைவேற்றிய சட்டம் யாருக்கும் பயன் அளிக்காத ஒன்று என்றும், அந்தச் சட்டம் இயற்றப்பட்டதை அடுத்து சென்னையில் நடைபெற்ற பாராட்டு விழாவில் கருணாநிதியின் “துதிபாடிகள்” அனைவரும் அதனைப் போற்றினர் என்றும் தன்னை விளம்பரப்படுத்திக் கொள்வதற்காகத்தான் தமிழ்ப் புத்தாண்டு தை முதல் நாள் என்று அறிவிக்கப்பட்டதென்றும் பேசியிருக்கிறார். “துதிபாடிகள்” என்று அம்மையாரால் வர்ணிக்கப்படும் பேரு பெற்றவர்கள் யார் யார் தெரியுமா? நமது பேராசிரியர் அன்பழகனார், அண்ணா பல்கலைக் கழகத்திலும், இந்திரா காந்தி திறந்தவெளி பல்கலைக்கழகத்திலும் துணை வேந்தராக இருந்து பெரும்புகழ் பெற்ற வா.செ. குழந்தைசாமி, முனைவர் தமிழண்ணல், முனைவர் வ.அய். சுப்பிரமணியம், முனைவர் அகத்தியலிங்கம், முனைவர் அவ்வை நடராசன், முனைவர் இரா. நாகசாமி, தவத்திரு ஊரன் அடிகள், பேராசிரியர் கண. சிற்சபேசன், முனைவர் அ. அறிவொளி, முனைவர் சுதா சேஷயன், இலங்கை இ. ஜெயராஜ், முனைவர் சரசுவதி ராமநாதன், வழக்கறிஞர் த. ராமலிங்கம், பேராசிரியர் தி. ராசகோபாலன், முனைவர் இராம. சவுந்தரவல்லி, முனைவர் இரா. செல்வ கணபதி, புலவர் கோ. சாரங்கபாணி ஆகியோர்தான்!
