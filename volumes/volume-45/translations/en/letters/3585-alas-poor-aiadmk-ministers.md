# 3585. Alas! Poor AIADMK Ministers!!

> **Translator’s note**
>
> This translation is intended to carry Kalaignar's voice into clear, contemporary English rather than recast the letter as literary or academic prose. It preserves the source's argument, political directness, rhetorical questions, repetition, irony, factual detail, and paragraph order. Names, dates, figures, quotations, and intentional English expressions are retained. Where Tamil idiom cannot be reproduced literally without sounding unnatural, the English follows its sense and rhetorical force without adding claims absent from the source. The original Tamil is reproduced in full below the translation and remains the authoritative text. `Udanpirappē` is retained in Tamil transliteration rather than flattened into “brother,” “sister,” or “comrade.” Literally evoking “one born alongside me,” Kalaignar uses it as a distinctive address of shared identity, equality, affection, and solidarity within the movement.

**Udanpirappē,**

Every day in the Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly, demands for grants relating to different departments are debated. At the end, the ministers concerned reply to the debate and make announcements on behalf of their departments. The debate on the Youth Welfare and Sports Development Department took place on 25-8-2011, and the minister in charge gave his reply. Yet several important announcements for that department were read out in the Assembly by Chief Minister Jayalalithaa on 6-9-2011 under Rule 110. Likewise, although the Housing Minister had completed the debate on his department's grant as early as 25-8-2011, Chief Minister Jayalalithaa read the announcements for that department in the Assembly on 8-9-2011 under Rule 110.

Why did the ministers concerned not make these announcements when the demands for grants relating to their own departments were debated? It is not only in these departments. During the grants debate for every department, a few insipid announcements and the task of abusing the DMK are left to the poor ministers concerned, while all the important announcements are read out in the House by the Chief Minister herself under Rule 110. And because the Chief Minister makes these announcements under Rule 110, the moment she finishes reading, one member from each party in the House rises in turn to shower praise on the announcements. Perhaps this new arrangement exists precisely for those praises!

In earlier times, announcements under Rule 110 were not followed by this kind of praise. But a few days ago Chief Minister Jayalalithaa herself said in the Assembly that when a statement is read under Rule 110, no question may be asked; one may offer praise and express thanks. Ever since then, each time she makes announcements under Rule 110, members from all parties seem to treat it as a great opportunity and rise one after another to deliver speeches of praise.

The “Explanatory Note on the Demand for Grants” of the department on which the Chief Minister read a Rule 110 statement on 6-9-2011 was prepared by the department's minister, C.V. Shanmugam, and distributed to all members of the Legislative Assembly.

The first paragraph of that policy explanatory note for 2011-12 reads:

> “Youth welfare and sports development, which form an important component of human-resource development, create a clear impact in improving the personality skills of young people by developing health, fellowship and a spirit of friendly competition among them.”

The policy explanatory note for this department for 2010-2011 was placed before the Assembly and debated by the DMK Government on 13-5-2010. Its first paragraph read:

> “Youth welfare and sports development, which form an important component of human-resource development, create a clear impact in improving the personality skills of young people by developing health, fellowship and a spirit of friendly competition among them.”

In other words, whatever was written in the policy explanatory notes during the previous years of DMK rule has been copied word for word into this year's policy explanatory note under the AIADMK Government—what people call an “Eeyadichaan copy,” a slavish copy without even a letter changed.

It is not merely this first sentence. The next sentence in the 2011-12 policy note, beginning “among young people of all sections” and ending “enable them to blossom,” has also been taken exactly from what was written in the 2010-11 policy note under the DMK Government. The next paragraph begins “our State” and ends “attention will be given”; the very same paragraph appeared in the DMK Government's notes in previous years. The paragraph after that is even more amusing. The DMK Government's policy note stated: “Tamil Nadu has the largest number of volunteers in the National Service Scheme in India (3,70,018 persons). In this State, 98,790 male and female students are in the National Cadet Corps.” This year's policy note presented by Minister C.V. Shanmugam says exactly the same thing: “Tamil Nadu has the largest number of volunteers in the National Service Scheme in India (3,70,018 persons). In this State, 98,790 male and female students are in the National Cadet Corps”—without even changing the figures.

Many more paragraphs in this year's policy note are likewise exactly the same as those in the policy notes issued during the previous DMK Government. Is this the result of the minister's laziness—of not even looking at his own policy note—or is it a trick played by the officials?

In the statement she read under Rule 110, Chief Minister Jayalalithaa listed what the Government proposed to do in the sports sector. Because the minister for this department had said in his reply that “nothing happened in this department during Karunanidhi's rule; only poetry gatherings and Sangamam took place,” I wish to underline just a few things that were in fact done by this department under the DMK Government.

During the past four years of DMK rule, the amount distributed merely as incentive awards to sportspersons was ₹3 crore 71 lakh 67 thousand 655.

Apart from this, the synthetic athletics track at Chennai's Jawaharlal Nehru Stadium was renovated at a cost of ₹240 lakh. An annual grant of ₹1 crore has been provided to conduct international-level tennis tournaments in Chennai. A new modern swimming pool was constructed in Krishnagiri district with Government participation at a cost of ₹60 lakh. Indoor badminton halls were established at three places—the South Asian Games Village at Koyambedu in Chennai, the Kanchipuram District Stadium, and the Shenoy Nagar Swimming Pool Complex in Chennai—at ₹20 lakh each, a total cost of ₹60 lakh.

Sports hostels for athletes were established and brought into use at the Tiruchi and Madurai district sports complexes at ₹70 lakh each. Two modern swimming pools were established in Tiruvannamalai and Thiruvarur districts at ₹70 lakh each. Modern floodlighting was installed at the Race Course Stadium in Madurai district with an allocation of ₹50 lakh, and sporting events are being conducted there. The roof of the Chennai Indoor Stadium was modernised and renovated at a cost of ₹131 lakh. A new sports hostel was built in Tirunelveli at a cost of ₹70 lakh and is in use. Permission was granted to build a new sports hostel at Chennai's Jawaharlal Nehru Stadium at a cost of ₹70 lakh, and construction is under way.

Allocations were made to construct indoor stadiums in Tirunelveli and Tiruppur districts at a project cost of ₹1 crore each, and construction in Tirunelveli district is proceeding rapidly. Work is under way to build mini-stadiums at Harur in Dharmapuri district and Arani in Tiruvannamalai district at a project cost of ₹60 lakh. Construction is under way for a modern swimming pool in the District Collectorate complex in Villupuram district at a project cost of ₹70 lakh. A sum of ₹300 lakh was sanctioned to lay an artificial-turf hockey field at the Anna Stadium in Tiruchi, and work is in progress.

Sports hostels for economically very disadvantaged sportspersons were established in Tiruvannamalai and Krishnagiri districts at a cost of ₹310 lakh. Seventy-five male students are benefiting from the Krishnagiri sports hostel and seventy-five female students from the Tiruvannamalai sports hostel. Permission was granted to lay a synthetic athletics track at the Anna Stadium in Tirunelveli district at a cost of ₹3 crore 50 lakh; a Government grant of ₹134 lakh was obtained, and steps are being expedited to begin the work. The Government sanctioned an international-standard swimming pool in Coimbatore district at a cost of ₹175 lakh, and action to construct it is being expedited.

Steps were taken to establish natural-grass football grounds at twelve places—Tirunelveli, Thoothukudi, Thanjavur, Sivaganga, Nagapattinam, Erode, Krishnagiri, Tiruvannamalai, Dindigul, Karur, Ariyalur and Chennai—at a cost of ₹120 lakh. Work has been completed and the grounds are in use at Krishnagiri, Erode, Tiruvannamalai, Nagapattinam and Sivaganga; work is proceeding rapidly at the other places.

Preliminary work is under way to establish mini-stadiums at Veppanthattai in Perambalur district, Nilakkottai in Dindigul district, Nenmenipatti-Sattur in Virudhunagar district, Tiruchendur in Thoothukudi district, and Namakkal district, at a cost of ₹50 lakh. A total grant of ₹1 crore, at ₹50 lakh each, was sanctioned for swimming pools at the Ariyalur and Perambalur district sports complexes, and work is under way.

A total grant of ₹1 crore, at ₹50 lakh each, was sanctioned for multipurpose indoor stadiums at the Tiruvannamalai and Thiruvarur district sports complexes, and the halls are being established.

Approval was given to provide ₹1 crore every year for five consecutive years from 2010-2011 to conduct World Squash Championships at the modern squash stadium in Chennai; ₹1 crore was provided for 2010-2011.

After the Indian cricket team led by M.S. Dhoni defeated Sri Lanka and won the 2011 World Cup, it was announced that the team would receive a special prize of ₹3 crore and Tamil Nadu off-spinner R. Ashwin, who was part of the team, ₹1 crore. Accordingly, on 5-5-2011, while I was Chief Minister, the ₹3 crore due to the team was distributed among its fourteen players, and ₹1 crore was given to Ashwin.

Therefore, Udanpirappē, these figures make it clear that, contrary to what this department's minister said in the House, it was not merely poetry gatherings and Sangamam that took place under the DMK Government. Those took place too, and innumerable achievements such as these were also accomplished. Classical Tamil, poetry gatherings and Sangamam—all these are things they dislike. I had thought somewhat highly of this minister, imagining that among AIADMK ministers he was different. I now understand that he too is just the same.

**With affection, M.K.**  
10-9-2011

2011இல் நடைபெற்ற உலகக் கோப்பைக்கான கிரிக்கெட் போட்டியில் இலங்கையை எதிர்த்து கோப்பையை வென்ற திரு.எம். எஸ். தோனி தலைமையிலான இந்தியக் கிரிக்கெட் அணிக்கு 3 கோடி ரூபாயும், அணியில் இடம் பெற்ற தமிழக சுழற்பந்து வீச்சாளர் ஆர். அஸ்வின் அவர்களுக்கு 1 கோடி ரூபாயும் சிறப்புப் பரிசாக வழங்கப்படும் என அறிவித்ததற்கேற்ப; 5.5.2011 அன்று முதலமைச்சராக இருந்த என்னால் அணிக்கு உரிய 3 கோடி ரூபாய் 14 வீரர்களுக்கு பகிர்ந்து வழங்கப்பட்டது. திரு.அஸ்வினுக்கு 1 கோடி ரூபாய் வழங்கப்பட்டது.
