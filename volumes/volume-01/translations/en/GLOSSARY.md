# Volume 1 — English Migration Glossary

Status: **active**.

Canonical English migration is underway. Recurrent decisions are recorded here and may be expanded as later letters introduce new source terms.

| Tamil / source form | Canonical English handling | Note |
|---|---|---|
| `கழகம்` | `Kazhagam` | Retain movement self-designation rather than flattening every occurrence to a generic “party”. |
| `உடன்பிறப்பே` | `Udanpirappē` | Distinctive Kalaignar address; retain in transliteration when it occurs in the source. |
| `அண்ணா` | `Anna` | Retain established movement/personal address. |
| `மறவன்` | `Maravan` | Retain the early-volume sign-off as printed; do not substitute a later signature. |
| `ஆதிதிராவிட` | `Adi-Dravidar` | Retain historical/source terminology in these letters. |
| `லட்சம்` | `lakh` | Preserve Indian numerical vocabulary. |
| `கோடி` | `crore` | Preserve Indian numerical vocabulary. |
| `பராக்! பராக்!` | `Parāk! Parāk!` | Retain the theatrical herald's acclamation rather than reducing it to “make way”. |

The controlling scan and verified canonical Tamil always override glossary convenience when a source-specific form differs.
