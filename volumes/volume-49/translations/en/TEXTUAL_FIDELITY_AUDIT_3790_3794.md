# Textual-Fidelity Audit — Letters 3790–3794

## Scope

- Volume: **49**
- Letters: **3790–3794**
- Canonical Tamil Markdown pages: **PDF 203–241**
- Total pages visually compared with scans: **39/39**
- Method: each canonical page was compared against its corresponding rendered PDF scan before translation. Titles, letter numbers, paragraph order, quotations, English passages, figures, punctuation, signatures, dates and page boundaries were checked.

## Result

- All **39** pages were inspected.
- No paragraph or passage was missing.
- Fifteen scan-verified transcription artefacts were corrected in the canonical Tamil pages before the bilingual files were assembled.
- The corrections are limited to visible word boundaries, broken words, quotation marks, grammatical consonants or paragraph-boundary errors. They do not modernise, fact-check or rewrite the source.

## Source-exact features retained

- All English passages printed in letter 3793 are reproduced without silently correcting their original grammar, punctuation or typography.
- The visibly printed double full stop on PDF page 230 is preserved in the canonical Tamil source.
- The figures and internally differing teacher-post counts in letter 3794 are retained exactly as printed and are not externally reconciled.
- The source's political descriptions, institutional titles, dates, financial amounts, percentages and historical claims are translated as presented, without outside fact-correction.
- Unusual source spacing that is visibly present in the scan, including selected numerical and phrase breaks, is retained where it reflects the printed page rather than a transcription artefact.

## Translation assembly

Each English file contains:

1. the standard Translator’s note;
2. a complete thought-preserving English translation;
3. letter-specific notes where required; and
4. the full corrected canonical Tamil text, divided by original PDF page.

This audit establishes **source-checked** status for letters **3790–3794**. Full bilingual line-by-line editorial review remains a later, separate stage.
