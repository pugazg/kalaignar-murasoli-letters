## Scope and method

This audit covers five consecutive letters and **39/39 canonical pages**. Every page was visually compared with the rendered original scan. The review checked titles, salutations, page and paragraph continuity, Tamil and English quotations, names and initials, dates, figures, historical/project material, punctuation, source word forms, closing/signature/date pages, omissions, duplicated text and page-boundary fidelity.

The scan remained authoritative. Source-specific spelling, punctuation, English passages, quotation behaviour, numerical forms and unusual typography were preserved rather than silently normalised.

## Source forms and material deliberately preserved

The audit retained the printed source rather than copy-editing it, including:

- the source English passages concerning the Sethu Samuthiram project, Katchathivu correspondence and NASA disclaimer;
- the source-visible standalone `2` at the bottom of PDF 289, which continues as `2 ஆயிரத்து 427 கோடி` on PDF 290;
- source quotation and punctuation irregularities, including malformed or asymmetrical quotation marks;
- the letter-3749 start-page subtitle form `கலைஞர் தொடர் கடிதம்-3`, distinct from the printed-contents form already recorded elsewhere;
- historical names, initials, dates, committee/project names and monetary figures as printed; and
- source-specific word spacing and compounds even where modern copy-editing might prefer another form.

No external fact-checking or stylistic normalisation was used to alter the canonical text.
